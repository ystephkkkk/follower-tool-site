TL;DR

Meta Chief AI Officer and Scale AI co-founder Alexandr Wang details the seven-month creation of Muse—the fastest-growing consumer AI product since ChatGPT—following Mark Zuckerberg’s recruitment of Wang in early 2026 after Meta's Llama 4 setback. By embracing a flat organizational structure, extreme talent density under 200 people, and a laser focus on personal superintelligence rather than coding agents, Meta built an AI product designed to amplify human agency for billions.

1. The Genesis and Vision of Muse

  • Founding Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL):
    • Personal Superintelligence Memo: Published by Mark Zuckerberg in June 2025 (co-authored by Alexandr Wang, Nat Friedman, and others) to focus AI on lifting the human experience and building personal agents.
    • Strategic Shift: While the industry conventional wisdom focused heavily on coding agents (dominated by Anthropic's Opus 4.5 and Claude), Meta leaned into personal agents because Meta’s ecosystem encapsulates what humans *want* to do (interests, hobbies, friends/family) rather than just what they *have* to do.
  • The OpenClaw Inspiration:
    • In February 2026, Nat Friedman experimented extensively with OpenClaw, experiencing a mix of terror and euphoria (including a deep psychological audit prompt akin to three years of therapy).
    • This early-adopter signal convinced Wang and Friedman that personal superintelligence was the final consumer product endpoint.
  • Rapid Prototyping:
    • The team built a functional prototype within 1 to 2 weeks, showcasing early versions of JollyBot and the Muse charm directly to the Meta board.

2. Product Refinement and the Seven-Month Build

  • The Unglamorous Grinding Phase:
    • Moving from a prototype that frequently broke to a reliable, beautifully crafted consumer product took seven meticulous months of engineering.
  • Rigorous Evals:
    • The team tracked over 100 specific model behaviors and capabilities on detailed spreadsheets, establishing launch-blocking vs. good thresholds.
    • The final green-light checkpoint required a specially trained version of *MuseSpark 1.3* to be green across the board.
  • Product-Model Synergy:
    • Muse's success relies heavily on model reliability, non-deterministic agent task handling, and graduating users from a chatbot mindset to an agentic "trust fall" workflow (recording voice notes, brain dumping, and trusting the AI to organize plans).
  • Lean Organizational Discipline:
    • Despite being Meta's most important product of the year, the combined model and product team was kept under 200 people to preserve a single, cohesive point of view (heavily shaped by Nat Friedman's tastes).

3. Recruitment, Refounding, and Mark Zuckerberg's Leadership

  • The Transition from Scale AI:
    • After 9 years building Scale AI, Wang was recruited by Mark Zuckerberg following Meta's internal disappointment with *Llama 4*.
    • The resulting deal structure left Meta owning 49% of Scale AI while Wang and key personnel transitioned to Meta.
  • Zuckerberg's Superpowers:
    • Wang highlights Zuckerberg's unmatched patience, restraint, long-term focus, and ability to ruthlessly prioritize while maintaining family commitments and team cohesion.
    • Zuckerberg views failure as immaterial compared to the scale of winning, operating without fear and aggressively emptying the clip once a strategic decision is locked in.
  • Refounding the AI Lab:
    • Wang approached MSL as a "refounding" of Meta's AI lab, moving away from bureaucratic corporate structures toward a flat, high-talent-density model.

4. Viral Marketing and the "Unhinged" X Strategy

  • Breaking Through the Noise:
    • Faced with a torrential stream of AI product releases, the team needed a cultural breakthrough beyond standard Meta app distribution.
  • Meme-Driven Growth:
    • Wang leaned into posting racy and surprising memes featuring the Muse mascot (the little jolly guy holding a briefcase), which drove massive internet intrigue and real-world app growth.
  • Advertising Philosophy:
    • Criticizing the poor, inward-facing ads of competitors like Anthropic and OpenAI, Wang emphasized that consumers do not care about companies or research—they only care about self-interest and what a product does for them, a principle rooted in classic advertising literature like Claude Hopkins' *Scientific Advertising*.

5. Management Philosophy: Diamond Mining vs. Skyscrapers

  • Scale AI (Skyscraper Model):
    • Built on operational efficiency, precision, and the founder acting as the ultimate bottleneck who understands and touches every job.
  • Meta Superintelligence Labs (Diamond Mining Model):
    • Built for power-law payoffs where rare, brilliant ideas justify all investments.
    • Direct Report Structure: Wang manages over 200 direct reports (primarily brilliant researchers in flat "pods" with no traditional managers or bureaucracy) to act as a steward of an environment where exceptional talent has the freedom to cook.
  • Empowering Human Agency:
    • The ultimate promise of abundant intelligence is shifting humanity from corporate "zombie hood" to a world where billions of people have the resources and agency to make their biggest dreams and wants real.

TL;DR

Global trade is facing unprecedented disruption and fragmentation reminiscent of the 1930s, driven by geopolitical tensions, shifting subsidies, and protectionism. Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala, Director-General of the World Trade Organization, advocates for "re-globalization" to decentralize supply chains and reform multilateral rules while maintaining resilience.

The State of Global Trade and WTO Pressures

  • Unprecedented Stress: Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala notes that global trade is experiencing fragmentation and stress unseen in 80 years, with dissatisfaction leading some members toward unilateral and bilateral arrangements.
  • The 1930s Parallel: Turning inward mirrors the protectionist era of a century ago; however, modern supply chains, financial linkages, and digital trade make any collapse far more damaging today.
  • Resilience of the Core: Despite strains, about 75% of world trade continues to function under WTO rules, demonstrating a solid core.
  • The 25% Friction Points: Contentious areas include outdated 1990s subsidy rules, state-directed economic models, and disputes over developing country status (such as China's ongoing classification alongside positions held by India and Brazil).

Subsidies, Industrial Policy, and China

  • Rising Subsidies: Government support via tax breaks, grants, and cheap loans distorts trade by lowering company costs artificially rather than relying strictly on efficiency.
  • Semiconductor Investments: Citing estimates from the Semiconductor Industry Association, China is on track to invest $142 billion into chip making.
  • Evolution of Rules: Okonjo-Iweala emphasizes the need to update subsidy and trade frameworks established before major developing economies like China joined the WTO.

Personal Cost and the Fight Against Corruption

  • Nigeria Finance Minister Tenure: Okonjo-Iweala served two non-consecutive terms as Nigeria's finance minister, waging a central life's work against corruption that diverted public funds from education, health, and infrastructure.
  • Gas Subsidy Fraud: An audit initiated by Okonjo-Iweala uncovered $2.4 billion worth of fraud in fuel import subsidy claims, leading to her refusal to pay fraudulent marketers.
  • Abduction Ordeal: The crackdown triggered death threats and the harrowing 5-day kidnapping of her 96-year-old mother (then younger), during which kidnappers demanded Okonjo-Iweala's resignation and departure from the country.
  • Resilience Under Fire: Despite immense fear and guilt, she continued her governmental duties following her father's firm insistence that the family would not submit to blackmail.

African Trade and Structural Barriers

  • Low Intra-Continental Trade: Africa accounts for only 3% of global exports, with roughly 15% of the continent's formal trade occurring between African nations.
  • Structural Hurdles: High trade barriers, shipments of raw materials abroad followed by reimportation of finished goods, dysfunctional transport, numerous currencies, and red tape hinder economic growth.
  • Mobility Obstacles: Okonjo-Iweala highlighted needing 35 different visas on her passport as an investor and noted personal incidents where the African Union's experimental African passport was initially rejected at borders.
  • AfCFTA Initiative: Launched in 2021, the African Continental Free Trade Area groups 54 countries into a single market to stimulate manufacturing and intra-African commerce.

Re-Imagining Globalization and Digital Trade

  • Re-Globalization Concept: Okonjo-Iweala champions decentralizing and de-concentrating production away from single geographies to multiple locations with comparative advantages to build global resilience.
  • Digital Trade Growth: Total world trade stands at $33 trillion, with digitally delivered services (movies, music, education, health) growing the fastest at about 6% per year, reaching roughly $4 trillion.
  • Digital Moratorium Stalemate: A longstanding moratorium on electronic transmissions faced resistance at the latest WTO ministerial conference—pitting the US and EU against Brazil, India, and developing nations—raising fears of national rules and internet taxation.
