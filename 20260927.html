Bill Gates says it’s ‘not enough to have a kill switch’ for AI: Full interview

NBC News(12.1M)·32:05·2026-09-27
Bill GatesCo-founder, Microsoft Kristen WelkerHost, Meet the Press
TL;DRBill Gates warns that AI represents a profound evolutionary event carrying severe near-term risks from bad actors—such as bioterrorism and advanced cyberattacks—requiring mandatory global safeguards and government oversight rather than reliance on voluntary industry measures.

TL;DR

Bill Gates warns that AI represents a profound evolutionary event carrying severe near-term risks from bad actors—such as bioterrorism and advanced cyberattacks—requiring mandatory global safeguards and government oversight rather than reliance on voluntary industry measures. Simultaneously, the Gates Foundation is launching a 60-organization partnership to bridge the global AI equity gap by bringing low-resource languages up to English-language parity within five years.

AI Equity and Language Accessibility Initiative

  • Global Language Disparity:
    • Current Error Rates: AI models are highly accurate in English and about a dozen mainstream languages, but African languages like Yoruba (spoken by 3 million people in Western Africa) suffer from an error rate roughly 10 times higher.
    • Exclusion of Populations: Nearly half the world (over 3 billion people) is currently represented by very poor AI quality due to pure capitalistic incentives failing to prioritize these languages.
  • Gates Foundation Partnership:
    • Coalition Scope: The foundation partnered with 60 organizations, including AI companies, to improve language accessibility and benchmarking.
    • Five-Year Timeline: The initiative aims to take every language spoken by 1 million or more people and bring it close to English language quality within 5 years.
    • Practical Impact: Enable patients, farmers, and students to use audio interfaces for health interviews and agricultural challenges in their native languages, with capabilities ultimately built into every major AI model.

Existential Threat, Whistleblower Warnings, and Near-Term Risks

  • Evolutionary Nature of AI:
    • Intelligence Superiority: Gates describes AI not as a mere new technology, but as an intelligent species already smarter than humans in many respects, improving at an exponentially increasing level.
    • Need for Intentional Shaping: Society, policymakers, voters, and think tanks must engage actively to accelerate good potential and minimize horrific outcomes.
  • Near-Term Threats from Bad Actors:
    • Empowered Malicious Actors: The most immediate danger is people with bad intent using AI to defraud individuals, shut down electric networks, scramble bank accounts, or execute sophisticated cyberattacks.
    • Democratization of Bioterrorism: Small groups using AI tools can now execute capabilities previously restricted to the biggest nation-states, including designing bioterrorism weapons capable of driving events that cause a billion deaths.
  • Whistleblower Claims:
    • Plausibility of Severe Harm: Gates notes that while total human extinction is hard to reach 100%, AI is powerful enough to drive events causing 20 million to a billion deaths if left unmonitored.
    • Distraction from Immediate Dangers: While loss-of-control scenarios and existential doom get significant attention, the urgent priority is stopping bad actors from weaponizing AI today.

Regulation, Global Guardrails, and Government Oversight

  • Inadequacy of Self-Regulation:
    • Need for Legislation: Self-regulation by companies is insufficient; Washington must pass legislation requiring mandatory safeguards and monitoring.
    • Industry and Government Collaboration: Because AI emerged from the private sector with limited government expertise, authorities must draw on responsible industry members to establish rules without causing dramatic delays.
  • Rejection of Ineffective Solutions:
    • Kill Switches: Gates dismisses relying solely on a "kill switch" as non-technical, noting that AIS are not yet autonomously seizing computers beyond shutdown, and a kill switch alone provides no insight or records of what was done.
    • Voluntary Reviews: Merely having a couple of companies engage in voluntary review "doesn't do anything."
  • Global Cooperation:
    • International Standards: Common-sense monitoring and safeguards must be established globally, with Gates arguing that even China would want to prevent non-state actors from launching bioweapons or major network attacks.
    • Cold War Precedent: Comparing the situation to nuclear weapons limitations during the Cold War, Gates emphasizes that the U.S. must lead a benign, global coalition to address shared humanity challenges.

Personal Background, Education, and the Future of Work

  • Jeffrey Epstein Relationship:
    • Regretted Meetings: Gates acknowledges regretting every minute spent with Jeffrey Epstein across roughly 10 meetings over a couple of years, noting that Epstein falsely claimed he could help raise billions of dollars for global health.
    • Congressional Testimony: Gates reiterated that he had no knowledge or participation in Epstein's criminal conduct, never visited Epstein's island, and had no financial or personal involvement in his crimes.
  • AI in Education and Daily Life:
    • Social Development Risks: Using AI as a sycophantic companion or friend for children risks retarding social development by eliminating the friction of dealing with non-sycophantic people (noting China's ban on such uses).
    • Foundation Utilization: The Gates Foundation is actively using AI to guide and improve the deployment of its $200+ billion endowment over its remaining 19 years of operations.
  • Long-Term Era of Abundance:
    • 20-Year Transition: Society faces a roughly 20-year adjustment period involving job market shifts and criminal empowerment risks before reaching a potential "era of abundance" powered by humanoid robots and advanced AI.

Background & Evolution of GLM

  • Origin of the Name: Explained by Zixuan Li, the name GLM stands for general language model pre-training with auto-regressive blank filling, originating from a paper published in 2021.
  • Pioneering Lab Status: Z.ai was one of the earliest labs to explore large language models alongside OpenAI, Anthropic, and DeepMind.
  • Branding Shift: While the underlying architecture has evolved past the original GLM design, the name has been retained as the core product brand (moving from GLM-5.1 to GLM-5.2).

Performance & Benchmarking

  • Coding and Agentic Capabilities: On hard long-horizon tasks and benchmarks—specifically SWE-bench Terminal Bench 211—GLM-5.2 lands between Claude Opus 4.7 and 4.8, marking a significant improvement over version 5.1.
  • Thinking Budget Efficiency: GLM-5.2 introduces a new "high" thinking budget level to manage token consumption on difficult tasks. Notably, the non-thinking mode outperforms the GLM-5.1 thinking mode, delivering major efficiency gains for an open-weight model.
  • Beyond Coding: While heavily used in coding tools (like Cursor, Claude Code, and OpenCode), GLM-5.2 has been extensively trained across general chat, math problems, and GDP-eval. Strategic Rationale for Open Weights

  • Enterprise Security & Control: Opening the weights allows enterprises and governments (particularly in the Western world) to run the model on-premise on their own servers for maximum security and trust.

TL;DR

Software engineering is rapidly evolving into factory engineering, where developers build and manage automated systems to handle the entire software development lifecycle. Zach Lloyd argues that while coding volume will decrease, overall shipping velocity will surge, requiring engineers to focus on meta-engineering, product taste, and process optimization.

The Software Factory Thesis & Lifecycle

  • Shift to Factory Engineering:
    • Core Paradigm: Zach Lloyd shares that he has not written a line of code in the last six months despite shipping frequently, operating instead as a builder of automated software factories.
    • Industry Inevitability: Every significant project and company will soon run a software factory, mirroring the universal adoption of CI/CD over the past decade.
  • The Factory Floor Workflow:
    • Inputs: Work enters via task trackers, Slack, communication channels, the terminal/IDE, or monitoring systems.
    • Triage: Topic Label: Automated Triage: An agent assesses incoming issues; easy/unambiguous tasks are implemented automatically, while hard issues are routed to spec generation.
    • Specs: Topic Label: Spec-Driven Development: Agents generate a *Product Spec* (describing product invariants) and a *Tech Spec* (describing architecture and code shape), which human engineers review.
    • Implementation & Review: Topic Label: Layered Code Review: Cloud coding agents produce diffs, followed by agentic code review before human intervention is introduced as a risk-management exercise.
    • Verification & Monitoring: Topic Label: Continuous Observation: Computer-use agents test UIs by producing videos/screenshots alongside CI/CD, while post-ship monitoring feeds crash and usage data back into the top of the factory.

Open Source in the Age of Agentic Development

  • The Open Source Strategy:
    • Motivation: Warp spent five years building closed source before open-sourcing its agentic development environment (now at 60,000+ GitHub stars, hundreds of contributors, and 800,000+ active developers) largely to build a public factory at scale (`build.warp.dev`).
    • Ecosystem over Product: Because software is increasingly cheap to build and trivial to clone, a great product is no longer enough; startups must build in the open to foster ecosystems, build brand, and create community.
  • Overcoming Traditional OS Pain Points:
    • Automation at Scale: Agents now handle the historical pains of noisy issues, sloppy PRs, code review hell, and manual verification.

Factory Architecture, Data, and Self-Improvement Loops

  • System Components:
    • Control & Data Planes: Factories require ingestion channels, a control plane to distribute work, cloud sandboxes with specialized harnesses/models, and a data plane below to give agents memory, learning capabilities, and historical context.
  • Self-Improving Loops:
    • Topic Label: Skill Loops: Observer agents monitor how factory agents apply skills and are corrected by humans (such as senior engineers overriding code review comments), automatically updating and improving the skills for subsequent runs.

The Evolving Role of Software Engineers

  • Meta-Engineering Mindset:
    • Process Engineering: Engineers must shift from directly building the product to building the systems that build the product.
    • Joy of Shipping: While engineers will write less code, they will ship significantly more product.
  • Desirable Skills for New Graduates:
    • Topic Label: Core Competencies: Zach Lloyd emphasizes adaptability, critical thinking, rapid learning, and a deep understanding of underlying systems, architecture, and code reasoning.
    • Human Taste: Underlying all automation, human product sense, taste, and empathy remain essential to ensure the factory produces genuinely useful products rather than mechanized noise.
