## What Actually Makes A Startup Durable

- Channel: Y Combinator

### TL;DR
AI is rapidly reducing the cost and complexity of writing software, meaning the traditional "hard bit" of building pure software is gone; founders must now focus on distribution, hard regulatory/physical barriers, and deep customer engagement. At Y Combinator, partners stress that durable startups rely on obsessing over building something people want, launching early and often, and maintaining strong co-founder relationships rather than overly relying on AI or solo execution.

### The Economics and Reality of AI Development
* **Cost Trends:** **Intelligence Is Getting 10x Cheaper:** The cost of intelligence drops by roughly a factor of 10 every year for the same level of capability, making token costs a non-issue over a 6 to 12-month horizon.
  * YC provides accepted founders with $1M in free tokens, and student founders with $25,000 in credits through partner labs and hyperscalers.
* **Engineering Impact:** **Models Surpass Humans in Coding:** There is essentially no programming task today where a human is more cost-effective or efficient than the absolute best available AI model.
* **The Death of Pure Software:** **Low-Moat Software is Dead:** Products like Calendly, DocuSign, or basic SaaS wrappers lack durability because replication has become trivial.
  * Startups must identify their "hard bit"—such as brutal B2B sales, complex hardware, or regulatory hurdles like obtaining a banking license.

### Startup Ecosystems and Hubs
* **Startup Hubs:** **SF Remains the Global Epicenter:** While regional hubs exist (like London or Paris), San Francisco remains the primary global hub attracting the most ambitious people and biggest visions.
  * Replicating a community from scratch is extremely difficult; founders should instead join an existing high-culture startup or move to an established hub.
* **Ecosystem Origins:** **The GoCardless Family Tree:** Founded in 2011, GoCardless and a handful of other companies formed the kernel of the London startup ecosystem, later spawning dozens of major companies including TransferWise and Monzo.

### Founders, Teams, and Co-Founder Dynamics
* **The Solo Founder Myth:** **Co-Founders Provide Emotional Resilience:** While a one-person billion-dollar company is theoretically possible, the marginal benefit of adding a co-founder far outweighs the coordination cost.
  * Solo founders face higher statistical failure rates and often suffer severe crises of confidence without a partner to pull them out of the gutter.
* **Co-Founder Selection Criteria:** **Prioritize Integrity and Resilience:** Look for the smartest, most determined, highest-integrity person you have worked with—someone who shares your values and whose absence on a competing team would scare you.
  * Technical co-founders should not waste time hunting for a "business" co-founder; business skills are easily learned, and technical alignment is more valuable.
* **High Agency:** **Building Agency as a Muscle:** High agency is the core belief that your actions produce tangible outcomes; it is developed by tackling progressively harder side projects and seeing them through to execution.

### Research vs. Sales and the Startup Playbook
* **The Build Trap:** **Launch Early and Often:** Founders suffer from loss aversion and prefer building or researching over talking to customers because rejection is terrifying.
  * Successful startups treat their business as an empirical hypothesis-testing exercise, launching early and constantly cycling between building and talking to users.
* **YC Success Patterns:** **Overwhelming Bottlenecks:** Top-ranking startups set ambitious goals, reassess every two weeks, and relentlessly throw all their focus at their single biggest bottleneck.
* **Pivoting in the AI Era:** **Hypothesis Testing vs. Getting Sad:** Pivoting should be driven by hard empirical data showing your core hypothesis is wrong, rather than running out of enthusiasm because sales are hard.

### AI-Native Operations and the Future of B2B
* **AI Integration:** **Start with Narrow Loops:** To build an AI-native company, make internal information queryable (via tools like Obsidian or Gbrain) and automate hyper-specific, narrow processes (such as automated sales follow-up drafts).
* **What Not to Automate:** **Never Outsource Customers or Co-Founders:** Founders must retain direct exposure to customer conversations and maintain authentic communication with their co-founders.
* **Model Routing:** **Automatic Model Intermediation:** Within roughly 12 months, queries will be automatically routed behind the scenes to the optimal intelligence tier without human intervention or concern over specific underlying models.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Cost Reductions:** The cost of intelligence will continue a 10x cost reduction per year for the same level of capability — Speakers (Y Combinator Partners).
* **Model Routing and Abstraction:** Within approximately 12 months, AI model selection will be entirely automated behind the scenes, routing queries to the appropriate intelligence tier without user intervention — Y Combinator Partners.
