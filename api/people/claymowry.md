- **TL;DR:** Palmer Luckey, founder of Anduril Industries, discusses his vision for defense technology, moving away from cost-plus contracting toward high-speed product manufacturing modeled after the automotive industry, and highlights future battle spaces including subterranean operations and autonomous air systems.

### Speaker Roster & Event Context
- **Clay Mowry:** CEO of AIAA and host of *Up Next*
- **Jessica “STING” Peterson:** Instructor Flight Test Engineer at the U.S. Air Force Test Pilot School, serving as co-host ("wingman")
- **Palmer Luckey:** Founder of Anduril Industries (and former founder of Oculus VR), guest on the program
- **Setting:** Recorded on location at Anduril headquarters in Costa Mesa, California.

### Journey from Consumer Tech to Defense
- **Founding Oculus:** **Consumer Virtual Reality:** Started working on VR as a gamer and hobbyist to push past the limits of multi-monitor setups, ultimately selling Oculus to Facebook for $2.3 billion before turning 21.
- **Pivoting to National Security:** **Impact Over Fun:** After being fired from Facebook 3 years post-acquisition, evaluated solving obesity via zero-calorie foods and prison reform before choosing national security because the US tech community was reluctant to work with the defense apparatus.
- **Early Defense Exposure:** **Bravemind Program:** Worked for a few months before Oculus at the ICT Mixed Reality Lab on virtual reality exposure therapy (Bravemind) to treat veterans with PTSD.

### Manufacturing Strategy & Arsenal 1
- **Product Company vs. Contractor:** **Speculative Development:** Anduril operates as a product company using its own capital to build and test systems rather than relying on time-and-materials cost-plus contracts.
- **Automotive Model for Aerospace:** **Car Factory Manufacturing:** Luckey asserts that the US must design weapons (like missiles and aircraft) that can be built in existing car and tractor factories, emulating World War II manufacturing scaling.
- **Arsenal 1 Facility:** **5 Million Square Feet:** Anduril's massive new Ohio facility utilizes automotive-style tools and processes rather than bespoke aerospace manufacturing.
- **Intellectual Property & Patents:** **Trade Secrets Over Patents:** Luckey avoids patents, calling them "Chinese instruction manuals" that block Western allies while adversaries freely copy them, preferring trade secrets and government-owned interoperability standards.

### Unmanned Platforms & Autonomy
- **Fury and Collaborative Combat Aircraft (CCA):** **FQ44 Designation:** The prototype YFQ-44A has moved to serial production as the FQ44, fulfilling Luckey's goal of securing an official unmanned fighter ("Q") designation.
- **Thunder Hybrid-Electric VTOL:** **Jet Fuel Reliance:** Thunder is a high-speed, long-range tilt-rotor aircraft powered primarily by jet fuel (partnering with Archer for composite structures and drivetrains) serving as a "loyal wingman" for attack helicopters.
- **Autonomous Deterrence:** **Human Safety in Conflict:** Luckey argues that adversaries only believe the US will deploy systems that do not put human lives at risk, giving autonomous systems a massive deterrence advantage.
- **Counter-UAS & Micro-Defense:** **Helicopter Protection:** Suggests that adapting technologies like the Trophy active protection system to aircraft will enable helicopters to easily shoot down incoming drones using small-caliber guns.

### Interoperability, AI, & Testing
- **Lattis Ecosystem:** **Open Integration:** Lattis integrates with over 100 existing DoD platforms and has approximately 700 companies in its partner program.
- **Enforcing Standards:** **Testing Interoperability:** Luckey stresses that the government must actively test and enforce interoperability standards rather than accepting contractors' claims at face value.
- **Rigorous Edge-Case Testing:** **Worst-Case Scenarios:** Testing must simulate high-workload, degraded conditions (such as hydraulics-out or GPS jamming) during early design phases.
- **The "Steve Jobs" Critique:** **High Skill Ceilings:** Luckey laments the "iPhone-ization" of technology, arguing that touch screens and simplified UIs sacrifice long-term efficiency and skill ceilings for ease of first-minute use.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
- **Subterranean Warfare:** Palmer Luckey predicts that the subterranean domain will become a major new warfare domain, with autonomous vehicles utilizing the Earth's crust as a 3D maneuver and battle space.
- **Government Nationalization of Designs:** Palmer Luckey anticipates that in a major shooting war, the US government will nationalize Anduril's designs and farm them out to mass-production partners because Arsenal 1 alone will be insufficient.
- **Bullet-on-Bullet Aerial Defense:** Palmer Luckey predicts that on a not-too-distant timeframe, radar will enable aircraft to track bullets and shoot incoming projectiles and quadcopters out of the air using other projectiles.
