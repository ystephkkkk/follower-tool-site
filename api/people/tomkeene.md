  * Recommends intermediate duration, defining the belly of the curve between **5 and 10 years** (specifically **4 to 6** duration), avoiding the excessive price volatility of 30-year bonds.
* **Municipal Bonds:** 
  * Bory favors municipal bonds for their tax-equivalent yields, robust credit fundamentals, and steady tax-base backstops independent of geopolitical shocks.

### Consumer Affordability and the K-Shaped Economy
* **Household Cost Pressures:** 
  * Meredith Whitney (Meredith Whitney Advisory Group) notes that property insurance costs are up **73%** since the pandemic.
  * Citing a Brown University Watson Group tracker, the cost of the Iran war equates to a **$900** increase per household, forcing lower-income consumers to cut grocery spending (flat to negative year-to-date) to afford gasoline as prices cross **$4.00**.
* **Banking Sector Dynamics:** 
  * Whitney states US banks are lending primarily to non-revolvers (prime/superprime) and non-deposit financial institutions (private credit) rather than struggling retail consumers, making major US banks less attractive than cheaper European alternatives.

### Wealth Transfer and Private Wealth Management
* **Evolution of Advisory Services:** 
  * Onur Erzan (Alliance Bernstein) emphasizes that younger generations inheriting baby boomer wealth bypass traditional 60/40 asset allocations in favor of holistic, multi-generational planning covering taxes, philanthropy, and family governance.
* **Role of AI in Wealth Management:** 
  * Erzan views AI as an enabler and operational scaler for advisors rather than a replacement for human judgment and trust in handling ultra-high-net-worth family needs.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Inflation and Bond Horizon** — George Bory predicts that inflation will drop back down to target and normal levels over the next **5 to 10 years**.
