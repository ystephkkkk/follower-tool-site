- Speakers: Kimberly Powell (Vice President of Healthcare, NVIDIA)

**TL;DR**  
NVIDIA has launched the BioNeMo Agent Toolkit, transitioning AI in life sciences from generative assistants to autonomous agents capable of performing complex biological and chemical computations. Supported by an ecosystem of partners including Benchling, Chai Discovery, and Edison Scientific, this new agentic paradigm leverages persistent, code-writing sandboxes to drastically accelerate molecular design and wet-lab integration.

---

### The Shift to Agentic AI in Life Sciences
* **From Generative to Agentic:** NVIDIA has introduced a new computing pattern where AI agents do hands-on scientific work rather than just summarizing text. Agentic AI relies on a two-part system: the large language model (LLM) acts as the reasoning "brain," while a software "harness" (such as NVIDIA NeMo Claw) serves as the "body," connecting the model to tools, databases, sandboxes, and security policies.
* **The BioNeMo Agent Toolkit:** This toolkit acts as the "arms and legs" of the agent, providing a decade of NVIDIA’s accelerated computing libraries as ready-to-use scientific skills. It is completely agent-agnostic and integrates with frontier models from Anthropic and OpenAI. Key pre-integrated tools include:
    * **MONAI:** An open-source medical imaging framework with over 9 million downloads.
    * **Parabricks:** A suite of genomic analysis libraries that holds two Guinness World Records for processing speed.
    * **cuEquivariance:** A specialized library designed to accelerate structural protein prediction.
    * **ProteinaComplexa:** An open-source model designed to predict complex protein structure interactions.
* **Ecosystem Adoption:** More than 50 partners are integrating or adopting the BioNeMo Agent Toolkit. 
    * **Dassault Systèmes** has introduced "Marie," a virtual scientific companion powered by the Nemotron LLM family and LangChain.
    * **Schrödinger** has announced "Bunsen," an autonomous co-scientist agent.
    * **Seqera** is building agentic systems using Nextflow to assemble custom bioinformatics workflows on the fly.
    * **Databricks and Snowflake** are integrating the toolkit to let users deploy autonomous scientific agents directly onto their secure, governed data.

### Frontier Breakthroughs in Molecular and Antibody Design
* **Exponential Success Rates:** Over the last two years, AI models have achieved order-of-magnitude improvements in molecular binding and design accuracy.
    * **Antibody Binding:** Prediction success rates for how an antibody binds to a target antigen (3D structure) have improved by roughly 10x, climbing from a baseline of ~5% to 50%.
    * **Antibody Design:** In the physical laboratory, the success rate for antibody design has jumped from a historical baseline of 1-in-1,000 (0.1%) to 10–15% within the last year—representing a two-orders-of-magnitude leap.
* **Navigating Infinite Search Spaces:** **Joshua Meier (Co-founder & CEO, Chai Discovery)** emphasized that the search space for potential antibodies is virtually limitless, containing more possibilities than there are atoms in the universe. High-accuracy AI models allow researchers to test a massive volume of hypotheses computationally, finding viable drug candidates that would be statistically impossible to discover via traditional wet-lab screening.
* **The High Value of Deep Inference:** Unlike consumer applications, life science companies are willing to invest massive compute budgets (upwards of $100,000 for a single complex inference run) because the financial and therapeutic return on finding a successful drug candidate is exceptionally high.

### Redefining Lab Workflows: From Records to Action
* **The Evolution of the Electronic Lab Notebook (ELN):** Traditional ELNs functioned strictly as retrospective archives for data. **Sajith Wickramasekara (Co-founder & CEO, Benchling)** noted that ELNs are shifting into proactive "systems of action" where AI co-edits notebooks, drafts experimental protocols, and automates records based on ongoing laboratory activities.
* **Case Study (Prime Medicine):** Using Benchling’s AI-powered data harness to prepare for FDA validation of a complex cell and gene therapy (encompassing 35 different validated products), Prime Medicine parsed four years of historical, unstructured experimental data. The system automatically identified data gaps, drafted follow-up studies, and compressed months of administrative and scientific labor into just a few days.
* **Persistent "Sandbox" Agents:** Edison Scientific’s "Cosmos" platform showcases how letting code-writing, sandbox-isolated agents run autonomously for several days can complete months of human intellectual labor. 
* **The Wet-Lab Verification Bottleneck:** Unlike software engineering, where written code can be verified instantly via software compilers, biological agents must be validated in physical "wet" labs. Testing benchmarks have outgrown human ability; when creating the *LabBench 2* evaluation suite, **Andrew White (Co-founder & CTO, Edison Scientific)** discovered that human question-writers made more errors than the testing AI agents, proving that physical lab experimentation is now the ultimate bottleneck for validation.

### The Critical Role of Forward Deployed Scientists (FDS)
* **Translating AI into Science:** Because implementing frontier AI in pharmaceuticals requires navigating complex data pipelines, compute orchestration, and change management, companies are relying heavily on Forward Deployed Scientists (FDS) or Forward Deployed Engineers (FDE).
* **Bridging the Integration Gap:** To ensure AI platforms are actually utilized by bench scientists:
    * **Benchling** maintains a team of 120 to 150 former scientists on staff to hand-hold laboratory clients through the process of workflow transformation.
    * **Chai Discovery** deploys scientists with decades of experience at the frontier of AI and drug discovery directly to pharmaceutical partners to restructure laboratory workflows for agentic integration.
    * **Edison Scientific** uses FDS teams to construct multi-page onboarding checklists to resolve server orchestration, LLM access, and secure database connections before research partnerships begin.

---

**TL;DR** 
NVIDIA is driving an AI-native paradigm shift in healthcare by integrating agentic AI, biology reasoning models, and physical AI platforms. By open-sourcing massive domain-specific datasets and foundation models (such as GR00T-H and Proteina-Complexa), NVIDIA and its global partners are automating dry and wet labs, accelerating drug discovery, and scaling global access to clinical care.

---

### Agentic AI & Digital Health: Scaling Clinical Operations
* **The Shift to Agentic AI:** Healthcare software is transitioning from monolithic applications to a "mosaic" of specialized, collaborative AI agents capable of orchestrating other agents and interacting with legacy medical systems.
* **NVIDIA Open Source Commitment:** NVIDIA provides over 650 to 700 open foundation models (including Nemotron for language, BioNeMo for biology, Cosmos for world models, and GR00T for robotics) to allow secure, cost-effective domain customization.
* **NVIDIA Agent Toolkit & Blueprint:** 
    * **Frontier Models:** Features the newly announced Nemotron Super and Ultra models, delivering frontier-level multimodal capabilities.
    * **The "IQ" Blueprint:** A reference application allowing enterprises to perform deep AI-driven research directly against their proprietary, sovereign data.
* **Rapid Startup Scaling:** High-growth startups in the NVIDIA Inception program (which has over 2,000 digital health members) are deploying agentic solutions at massive scale:
    * **Heidi Health:** A clinical documentation platform processing 2.4 million conversations weekly across 190 countries.
    * **OpenEvidence:** Used by approximately 50% of US clinicians for real-time medical knowledge synthesis.
    * **Sword Health:** Delivers AI-powered physical and mental therapy to over 700,000 members.
* **IQVIA.AI Platform:** Powered by the NVIDIA Agent Toolkit, IQVIA's new unified platform hosts 150 specialized agents. It reduces complex pharmaceutical workflows, such as clinical trial site selection, from several weeks to just a few days.

### The Biology Reasoning Era: BioNeMo & AI-Native R&D
* **AI Scientists:** Edison Scientific built *Kosmos*, an AI-driven scientific research system. Kosmos coordinates multiple agents (Literature, Analysis, and Molecules) to compress a traditional six-month drug research cycle into a single day.
* **"Compute is Data" Paradigm:** When AI models achieve experimental-grade accuracy, they can be utilized as synthetic data generators to post-train other models, bridging the historical biological data bottleneck.
* **AlphaFold Protein Complexes Dataset:** NVIDIA, in collaboration with EMBL-EBI, Google DeepMind, and Seoul National University, has released a dataset of 30 million protein complexes. Computing these interactive complexes required 100x more compute than single-molecule structures.
* **Proteina-Complexa Reasoning Model:** A third-generation protein design model utilizing "Test Time Compute" (allowing the AI to think longer to generate optimized structures down to the atomic level).
    * **Experimental Validation:** The VIVA team used it to generate 16 binders for a muscle-wasting target, and the University of Cambridge achieved the first computational design of a protein capable of binding to a carbohydrate antigen.
* **Context Parallelism:** A new BioNeMo framework that shards massive molecular complexes across multiple GPUs, giving researchers an unlimited context window to compute larger protein interactions than ever before.

### Autonomous Wet Labs & Enterprise AI Factories
* **Bridging Dry and Wet Labs:** Discoveries simulated in silico must be tested in physical wet labs, creating a demand for lab automation powered by both agentic and physical AI.
* **Lab Automation Pioneers:**
    * **HighRes:** Uses advanced perception AI to dynamically adapt to varying laboratory layouts.
    * **Tecan:** Leverages Nemotron to orchestrate and optimize lab operations.
    * **Opentrons:** Employs vision-language-action brains to bring adaptive physical intelligence to robotic benchtop pipetting and handling.
* **Roche's Enterprise AI Factory Expansion:** 
    * **Scale:** Roche is tripling its AI factory infrastructure by deploying over 3,500 NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs across US and European sites.
    * **Integration:** AI is being integrated into Genentech’s "lab-in-the-loop" R&D pipelines.
    * **Digital Twins:** Roche's manufacturing division is using NVIDIA Omniverse libraries to build high-fidelity digital twins to simulate and optimize factories before construction.
    * **Genomics:** Roche Diagnostics utilizes NVIDIA Parabricks to process high-throughput, large-scale genomic analysis workloads.

### Physical AI & Surgical Robotics: Project Rheo & GR00T-H
* **Project Rheo (Hospital Automation):** An automated workflow blueprint built with Peritas AI, Lightwheel, and AdventHealth. It uses Isaac Sim and Cosmos Transfer to reconstruct physical hospitals into physics-accurate digital twins, allowing service robots to train via reinforcement learning in simulation before physical deployment.
* **Open-H Dataset:** The world's first and largest surgical robotics dataset, containing over 750 hours of paired kinematics and surgical video across 11 robotic embodiments. It was developed in collaboration with over 35 global institutions (including CMR Surgical, Moon Surgical, and Johns Hopkins).
* **GR00T-H Surgical Brain:** A first-of-its-kind, generalizable vision-language-action (VLA) foundation model post-trained specifically for surgical robotics.
* **Cosmos-H World Models:** Specialized surgical world foundation models designed for synthetic data generation:
    * **Cosmos-H Surgical Transfer:** Simulates a single robotic skill across multiple clinical environments.
    * **Surgical Simulator:** Allows researchers to evaluate robotic control policies in silico, introducing surgical edge cases (such as smoke and tissue deformation) before physical deployment.
