## 'They Do Not Get To Negotiate': Mike Waltz Calls For Full, 'Irreversible' Demilitarization Of Hamas

**TL;DR** 
U.S. Ambassador to the UN Mike Waltz demanded the complete, irreversible demilitarization of Hamas as the non-negotiable foundation of the Gaza peace plan endorsed under UN Resolution 2803. Highlighting progress on an international stabilization force and new Palestinian police training, Waltz condemned Hamas's documented human rights abuses against Gazan civilians and criticized global silence on these war crimes.

### Non-Negotiable Demilitarization and Bipartisan Consensus
* **Irreversible Demilitarization:** Ambassador Waltz emphasized that Hamas cannot remain armed and must not be allowed to negotiate its way into keeping a terrorist army.
    * Demilitarization requires the complete, phased, and internationally verified destruction of all military infrastructure, weapon production facilities, and Gaza’s sophisticated tunnel network.
* **Bipartisan U.S. Support:** Waltz highlighted a significant domestic political alignment, noting that former Secretary of State Hillary Clinton recently published an op-ed endorsing President Trump's Gaza peace plan as the "only game in town," agreeing that peace depends entirely on Hamas disarming.
* **Active Mediation:** Ongoing negotiations led by Turkey, Egypt, and Qatar are actively working to convince Hamas that there is no armed future for its rule in Gaza.

### Documented Hamas War Crimes and Local Suppression
* **UN Inquiry Findings:** Waltz cited a report by the UN Independent International Commission of Inquiry detailing systematic war crimes committed by Hamas-affiliated forces against Palestinian civilians between 2024 and 2026.
    * Documented abuses include summary extrajudicial executions, public torture (including knee-capping with metal pipes and cement bricks), beatings, and rape.
    * The report highlighted specific incidents, such as public executions and torture conducted in front of crowds (including children) at the Nasser Medical Complex.
* **Suppression of Civil Protests:** When hundreds of Gazans attempted to protest Hamas’s authoritarian rule, the Al-Qassam Brigades launched an industrial-scale campaign of intimidation, blackmail, house arrests, and interrogation, while utilizing schools and hospitals as police stations.
* **Critique of Global Silence:** Waltz sharply criticized the UN, major NGOs, and mainstream media for their silence regarding both Hamas's internal atrocities and the courage of Gazans attempting to protest against their rulers.

### Transition to International Stabilization and New Governance
* **Stabilization Force Deployment:** Under the authority of Resolution 2803, international forces from Morocco, Kosovo, and Albania have begun deploying boots on the ground in Gaza.
* **New Palestinian Police Force:** Egypt and Jordan are actively training the first recruits for a new Palestinian police force, supported by a $100 million funding commitment from the United Arab Emirates (UAE).
* **Governance and Rebuilding:** Ongoing meetings are taking place with the New Governance Authority (N-CAG) to draft concrete plans for restoring public services, housing, and education.

## UN Ambassador Mike Waltz Touts Gaza Plan, Board Of Peace At Munich Security Conference

- Channel: Forbes Breaking News
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c-EpfjRti5U
- Published: 2026-02-13
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260213.html#video-c-EpfjRti5U
- Speakers: Mike Waltz (UN Ambassador, USA)

---

**TL;DR**
US Ambassador Mike Waltz detailed a paradigm shift in American foreign policy characterized by aggressive UN budgetary reforms and the "Donroe Doctrine," prioritizing Western Hemisphere security and results-oriented "focused multilateralism" over traditional, symbolism-heavy diplomacy.

### UN Reform and "Make the UN Great Again" (MUNGA)
*   **Budgetary Reductions:** Ambassador Mike Waltz announced the first-ever budget cut in UN history, emphasizing a "back to basics" approach to peacekeeping and peacemaking.
    *   **15% overall budget reduction** approved in December.
    *   **3,000 headquarters bureaucrats** and 18% of total personnel to be eliminated.
    *   **25% cut to peacekeeping** funds to ensure the organization is "fit for purpose."
*   **Fiscal Responsibility:** Waltz argued that US leadership in reform is mandatory because the US pays more in dues than 180 other countries combined, making the "status quo" unsustainable for American taxpayers.
*   **Operational Focus:** The US is pushing the UN to abandon its attempt to "be everything to every nation" and return to its core founding mandates.

### The "Donroe Doctrine" and Hemispheric Security
*   **Strategic Shift:** The US has articulated a "Trump corollary" to the Monroe Doctrine (termed the "Donroe Doctrine"), asserting explicit US leadership and power projection within the Western Hemisphere.
*   **Colombia’s Role:** Defense Minister Sanchez emphasized that the drug trade is a global economic scourge rather than just a local military issue.
    *   **Human Cost:** Over 2,000 Colombian military and police personnel were killed or injured in the last 3.5 years fighting the drug trade.
    *   **"Americas First":** President Petro adjusted the "America First" slogan to "Americas" (plural), signaling a desire for a continental partnership rather than US isolationism.
*   **Combatting Disinformation:** Both Waltz and Sanchez highlighted disinformation as a primary threat to regional security, noting it nearly derailed US-Colombia relations by misrepresenting Colombia’s efforts against drug cartels.

### The Gaza Plan and the "Board of Peace"
*   **Focused Multilateralism:** Waltz touted the creation of a "Board of Peace" to bypass the perceived stagnation of the UN Security Council regarding the conflict in Gaza.
    *   **Regional Inclusion:** The board involves Gulf Arab nations and Muslim-majority states, including Turkey, Pakistan, and Indonesia.
    *   **Troop Contributions:** Indonesia has publicly committed 8,000 troops to the peace efforts.
*   **Alternative to Hamas:** The strategy seeks a "third way" between Hamas rule and endless war, utilizing a Palestinian Technocratic Committee and a specialized funding mechanism.
*   **Diplomatic Wins:** Waltz cited the first direct flight between Azerbaijan and Armenia and new wheat shipments through the Caucasus as evidence that this focused approach is yielding results.

### Challenges to the New World Order
*   **Equality of States:** Estonian PM Kaja Kallas argued that while reform is necessary, the multilateral system must maintain the "equality of states" and ensure no nation is above the law.
    *   **The "Allies" Advantage:** Kallas noted that the primary difference between the US and other superpowers (like Russia or China) is the US's network of allies who choose to cooperate.
*   **Transition Risks:** Prince Faisal warned that the world is in a dangerous transition period between the old Eurocentric rules-based order and a new, yet-to-be-defined system.
*   **Shift from Symbolism:** There was a general consensus among the panel that the previous system was often an obstacle to addressing the actual drivers of conflict, favoring "symbolism over reality."
