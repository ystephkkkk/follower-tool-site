    * The NFL allows up to 6 team investments per institutional partner (Arctos holds stakes in the Bills, Chargers, Browns, and recently the Atlanta Falcons).
    * Institutional ownership rules are evolving: the NBA increased its limit from 5 to 8 teams, while Major League Baseball lifted its ownership percentage cap from 15% to 20%.
* **European Football vs. North American Sports:** European football features severe "relegation risk" (compounded over a 25-hour hold, a 98% non-relegation probability equals a 3,540% chance of relegation), uncontrolled player expenses, and less media rights competition outside the English Premier League.
* **Valuation Drivers:** Sports valuations are protected by strict league leverage caps (league average loan-to-value is roughly 15-16%), control premiums, civic leadership value, and safe-haven status against obsolescence.

* **The "Twin Scarcities":** For non-elite firms, capital is scarce and easy deals are hard to come by, creating an environment where middle-market leadership hoards assets and hunkers down.

* **The Four Ingredients of PE Health:** A healthy private equity environment requires:
