**TL;DR**
Vice President JD Vance and Prime Minister Nikol Pashinyan formalized a historic shift in U.S.-Armenia relations in Yerevan, headlined by a landmark civil nuclear cooperation agreement, the first major U.S. military technology sale to Armenia, and a massive high-tech partnership involving NVIDIA.

### Energy and Nuclear Cooperation
*   **The "123 Agreement":** The leaders signed a joint statement concluding negotiations on a civil nuclear cooperation agreement, opening a new chapter for Armenian energy independence.
    *   **Economic Impact:** The deal paves the way for up to $5 billion in initial U.S. exports and an estimated $4 billion in long-term maintenance and fuel contracts.
    *   **Technology Transfer:** The U.S. plans to deploy Small Modular Reactors (SMRs) to diversify Armenia’s energy resources and enhance regional security.
*   **Energy Security:** VP Vance characterized the deal as a "classic win-win" that strengthens energy security for both nations while creating high-paying jobs in the American energy sector.

### High-Tech and Artificial Intelligence
*   **NVIDIA Partnership:** Prime Minister Pashinyan announced that export permits have been approved for a new batch of "superpowerful" NVIDIA chips to Armenia.
    *   **AI Infrastructure:** The U.S. government is supporting a "mega-project" to build an AI plant and a dedicated data center within Armenia.
*   **Strategic Tech Corridor:** Vance noted these chips represent technology that "does not exist in most countries," signaling a high level of trust and a desire to make Armenia a regional tech hub.

### Regional Peace and the "TRIP" Project
*   **TRIP Implementation:** The leaders reviewed progress on the "Trump Route for International Peace and Prosperity" (TRIP), a framework signed in early 2025 to develop trade and transit corridors between the East and West.
    *   **Infrastructure:** The project focuses on private capital investment for new railroads and pipelines to create "interconnected economies" as a deterrent to future conflict.
    *   **Turkey Normalization:** Pashinyan emphasized the importance of finalizing the Armenia-Turkey normalization process, specifically the reopening of the Gyumri-Kars railway.
*   **Nobel Peace Prize Nomination:** Pashinyan revealed that he and Azerbaijani President Aliyev have jointly nominated President Donald Trump for the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize following his mediation efforts at the Washington Peace Summit.

### Defense and Security Partnerships
*   **Military Technology Sale:** The U.S. announced its first major military technology transaction with Armenia: an $11 million sale of V-BAT surveillance drones.
    *   **Deterrence Strategy:** VP Vance stated that the best way to ensure lasting peace is to establish "real deterrence" through superior American military technology.
*   **Ongoing Exercises:** The leaders reaffirmed the continuation of the "Eagle Partnership" military exercises, which have now run for three consecutive years.
*   **Critical Minerals:** Both nations committed to a partnership focused on securing the extraction and supply chains for critical minerals and rare earth elements.
