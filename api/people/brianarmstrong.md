### TL;DR
Coinbase co-founder and CEO Brian Armstrong details Coinbase's evolution into the "Everything Exchange" via tokenized real-world assets, explosive stablecoin payments, and agentic finance (AIFI) tools for AI agents. Additionally, Armstrong discusses his longevity startup, New Limit, and its AI-driven epigenetic reprogramming platform working toward upcoming human clinical trials.

### Coinbase & The Everything Exchange
* **Core Business Shift:** 88% of Coinbase's revenue now comes from non-Bitcoin trading, pointing to broader asset class expansion on-chain.
* **Tokenized Stocks:** Coinbase launched a true tokenized stock product (held 1-to-1 against actual securities in custody), targeting the roughly 4 billion people globally lacking access to US brokerage accounts (currently available outside the US while pursuing SEC pathways).
* **Prediction Markets:** Grew rapidly within the Coinbase app, hitting a $100M revenue run rate within months of launch and compounding at over 100% quarter-over-quarter. Armstrong sees long-term societal utility for prediction markets beyond sports and politics, envisioning use cases for government policy forecasting and complex "big life questions."
* **Global Economic Footprint:** Crypto currently powers about 0.5% of global GDP, with an estimated 700 million people having used or held crypto (50M–100M monthly active users), a footprint Coinbase aims to scale past 1 billion users.

### Agentic Finance (AIFI) & AI Integration
* **Banking the AIs:** Coinbase created tools allowing any AI agent to get its own self-custodial financial account via a single prompt, avoiding traditional KYC bottlenecks.
* **Micro-Transactions & Rails:** ~76% of agentic commerce transactions are under $0.30 (used for tasks like scraping data, paywalled information, and specialist agent tool calls), rendering traditional credit card flat fees ($0.30 + percentage) economically unviable and driving adoption of low-cost stablecoin rails and the Linux Foundation-housed **X42** protocols incubated by Coinbase.
* **Internal Software Factory (Toshi):** Coinbase uses internal AI agent harnesses ("Toshi") linked to team and repository "brains" (recording past incidents, financial controls, and pull-request histories) to build toward recursive self-improvement and higher acceptance rates on PRs.

### New Limit & Biotech Ventures
* **Founding Thesis:** Inspired by cellular reprogramming discoveries from Shinya Yamanaka's lab, Armstrong co-founded **New Limit** with Jacob Kimmel, Blake Buyers, and Greg Johnson to focus on epigenetic reprogramming to restore cellular function to a younger state.
* **Platform & Scale:** Headquartered in South San Francisco with 50-60 employees, the company uses a proprietary AI frontier model for epigenetic reprogramming to run large-scale pooled screens testing millions of transcription factor hypotheses.
* **Therapeutic Roadmap:**
    * **Initial Indications:** The website roadmap targets three initial cell types: hepatocytes (liver), vascular cells, and T-cells (immune system).
    * **Alcoholic Liver Disease (ALD):** The first phase 1 clinical trial is slated to launch next year targeting ALD, addressing a condition with an initial market estimate near $20B due to high unmet medical needs.
    * **Future Pipeline:** 3 to 10 additional drug candidates are slated to come off the platform in coming years, with ultimate long-term ambitions targeting chronic age-related cell degradation across skin, muscles, and the brain.

### Civilizational Progress & Frontier Ideas
* **Special Economic Zones:** Armstrong supports experimentation via freedom cities and special economic zones on federal land (similar to Shenzhen, Singapore, or Dubai) to bypass red tape for high-priority technology development like nuclear power and drone delivery.
* **Cognitive Enhancement & Embryo Editing:** Armstrong invests in ventures like *Preventive* (doing embryo disease-screening and editing) and contemplates future meta-problems around biological and digital cognitive enhancement to raise human baselines.

**TL;DR** 
Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong defends the crypto industry's push for clear regulatory frameworks under the Clarity Act, emphasizing that crypto's massive bipartisan grassroots base and highly funded PACs are successfully reshaping Washington's legislative and political landscape.

***

### The Clash Over the Clarity Act and Traditional Banking

* **The Regulatory Debate:** The debate surrounding the Clarity Act centers on whether cryptocurrency platforms and classical banking should operate under the same regulatory rules, particularly regarding stablecoin rewards.
    * **Stablecoin Rewards:** Armstrong argues that stablecoin rewards (backed 100% by short-term US Treasuries) are low-risk and should be accessible to consumers. Traditional banks oppose this, citing concerns over "deposit flight" from traditional accounts to high-yielding stablecoins.
    * **Legislative Compromise:** In negotiations with the Senate Banking Committee, Coinbase conceded on a key bank lobby demand, agreeing that rewards would not be paid on idle consumer balances in order to keep the bill moving forward.
* **Bank Licenses vs. Trust Charters:** Armstrong rejects the notion that Coinbase is lobbying for lighter regulations than traditional banks.
    * **Fractional Reserve Lending:** Traditional banks face heavy regulations because they engage in fractional reserve lending, which presents risks like bank runs.
    * **100% Reserves:** Coinbase operates under different licenses (such as an OCC trust charter, CFTC, and SEC regulations) where they maintain 100% of customer reserves and do not perform fractional lending, rendering bank-specific rules unnecessary.
* **The Jamie Dimon Conflict:** Addressing JP Morgan CEO Jamie Dimon’s public criticism of him, Armstrong called for direct communication over media hostility and emphasized that clear rules in America are ultimately beneficial for both banks and crypto firms.

### Bipartisanship and the Grassroots Crypto Movement

* **Bipartisan Policy Motivations:** Armstrong asserts that crypto has become a highly bipartisan issue in Washington, attracting lawmakers for different reasons:
    * **Democratic Appeal:** Focuses heavily on financial inclusion, aiming to democratize financial services for the estimated 80% of Americans who feel the current system does not work for them.
    * **Republican Appeal:** Emphasizes personal freedom, national security, and ensuring the U.S. dollar remains the global reserve currency in competition with China.
* **Grassroots and Generational Support:** The political power of crypto is fueled by a rapidly growing demographic base.
    * **User Base:** Approximately 50 million Americans have used cryptocurrency.
    * **Stand with Crypto:** Over 3 million advocates have registered with StandwithCrypto.org to elect pro-crypto candidates, sending over 1 million direct messages to legislators.
    * **Generational Divide:** Crypto adoption is heavily concentrated in the 15-to-35 age demographic, with younger congressional staffers and family members frequently driving the education of older lawmakers.

### Crypto's Expanding Political and Lobbying Influence

* **The Fairshake Super PAC:** Coinbase and other crypto-backed entities have heavily funded Fairshake, an independent, bipartisan super PAC. 
    * **Financial War Chest:** Fairshake entered the election cycle with more than $193 million in cash on hand, making it one of the most influential outside spending groups in politics.
* **The Political Cost of Anti-Crypto Stances:** Armstrong highlighted how opposition to crypto has directly impacted major political races.
    * **Sherrod Brown's Defeat:** Armstrong noted that former Senate Banking Chairman Sherrod Brown consistently refused to meet with him or Ohio-based crypto entrepreneurs. Armstrong believes this resistance contributed significantly to Brown losing his Senate seat to Bernie Moreno.
    * **New Committee Progress:** Under new Senate Banking Chair Tim Scott, the committee has achieved more legislative progress in months than it had in several years prior.
* **Key Congressional Allies:** Armstrong highlighted strong, collaborative relationships with lawmakers on both sides of the aisle, including Senators Kirsten Gillibrand, Angela Alsobrooks, and Cynthia Lummis.

### Future Horizons: AI Regulation and Human-AI Integration

* **AI Regulation and Startups:** Regarding President Trump’s recent executive order establishing a voluntary 30-day government review process for new AI models, Armstrong warned against excessive federal hurdles.
    * **Stifling Innovation:** Armstrong cautioned that mandatory registrations could stall the industry, drawing a parallel to how the creation of the Nuclear Regulatory Commission virtually halted the expansion of domestic nuclear energy.
* **The Convergence of Humanity and AI:** Armstrong shared a highly contrarian, philosophical view on the long-term future of artificial intelligence.
    * **Incremental Uploads:** He argues humans are already incrementally merging with AI by outsourcing memory, organization, and communication to smartphones and cloud systems.
    * **The 50-Year Vision:** Armstrong envisions a future where brain-machine interfaces allow humans to control humanoid robotic bodies, potentially preserving a continuity of self even after the biological body ceases to function.

* **Internal AI Efficiency:** Coinbase has adopted an "AI-first" mentality to maintain competitiveness.
    * **Engineering:** Over 50% of the company’s code is now written by AI.
    * **Customer Support:** Roughly 60% of support tickets are resolved via AI.
    * **Leadership:** Armstrong famously mandated that all engineers adopt AI tools within a week, hosting a "Saturday meeting" for those who failed to comply to ensure a "tone from the top" regarding technical relevance.

### Regulatory Strategy and Geopolitics
* **From Apolitical to Policy-Focused:** While Coinbase declared itself "mission-focused" (avoiding broad social issues) in 2020, it has become a powerful political actor to fight "lawfare" from the SEC and specific US senators.
    * **Legislative Priorities:** The goal is passing "market structure" legislation to clarify the distinction between commodities and securities, which Armstrong believes will be a "huge unlock" for the industry.
* **US Competitiveness:** With President Trump vowing to make the US the "crypto capital of the world," Armstrong notes that crypto talent is beginning to move back onshore as regulatory clarity emerges.

### Longevity and the Future of Humanity
* **New Limit and Cellular Programming:** Armstrong co-founded **New Limit**, a biotech firm focused on "epigenetic reprogramming"—restoring cellular function to a younger state.
    * **Clinical Progress:** Within three years, the company has demonstrated successful reprogramming of human cells and aims to begin clinical trials next year.
* **Longevity Escape Velocity:** Armstrong believes there is a 50% chance humanity will reach "longevity escape velocity" by 2030–2035. This is the point where science adds more than one year to a person's life expectancy for every year they live, potentially extending lifespans to hundreds of years.
