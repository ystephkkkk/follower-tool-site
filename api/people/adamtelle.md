### TL;DR
The Department of War leadership announced the creation of Autonomous Warfare Command (Auto WARCOM)—a new 4-star combatant command targeted to stand up by October 1, 2027—to rapidly field millions of autonomous systems and scale industrial production. The panel detailed sweeping acquisition reforms, a push toward a $1.5 trillion defense budget through congressional reconciliation, and decentralizing technology directly to frontline warfighters.

### Autonomous Warfare Command & Organizational Reform
* **Establishment of Auto WARCOM:** 
  * Will be led by a 4-star combatant commander fully empowered with service-like authorities to run autonomous operations and drive institutional change across the joint force — Pete Hegseth
  * Targeted deadline to stand up the command is October 1, 2027 — Pete Hegseth
  * SouthCom is the first command with an autonomous "auto warcom" operationalizing capabilities under General Donovan — Pete Hegseth
* **Service-Level Integration:**
  * **Army:** Announced the Futures and Autonomous Systems Command (Fast Com) to centralize autonomy while keeping it distributed to units; expanding the 390 Alpha robotics expert MOS across the force — Adam Telle
  * **Navy / Marine Corps:** Deployed the Teddy Roosevelt Carrier Strike Group on October 8th with a medium-sized and large-sized unmanned surface vessel; utilized unmanned underwater vehicles (UUVs) and Corsair IIs in the Strait of Hormuz to demine and recover pilots — Hung Cao
  * **Air Force / Space Force:** Integrating autonomous fighters into experimental ops units at Creech Air Force Base just one year after first flight; Space Force sensing platforms went from contract award to launch in large numbers in 6 months — Troy Meink
  * **Special Operations (SOCOM):** Utilizing Task Force Autonomous Warfare to support DIRTCOM and Auto WARCOM, building on the Defense Autonomous War Group (DOG) — Derrick Anderson

### Industrial Base & Acquisition Overhaul
* **The Arsenal of Freedom & Prime Expansion:** 
  * Aiming to expand the number of prime defense contractors from 5 to 10 or 15 by the end of the term — Pete Hegseth
  * Rebuilding the defense industrial base and manufacturing base at a scale not seen since World War II — Pete Hegseth
* **Procurement Speed & Scale:**
  * Fielded hundreds of thousands of one-way attack systems this year, moving into the millions in future years — Pete Hegseth
  * Deputy Secretary Steve Feinberg partnering with industry to secure tens of billions of private investment in new manufacturing plants in exchange for 5-to-7-year munitions contracts locking in pricing — Pete Hegseth
* **The Drone Marketplace:**
  * Operating an Amazon-like marketplace where soldiers buy drones directly via points (5-15% of average BCT budget) and leave 3-star or 5-star reviews to iterate in real time; already processed $800 million in purchases and signed 9 agreements with partner militaries — Adam Telle
* **Booking.com for Test Ranges:**
  * Opening up Department of War test ranges to industry innovators to accelerate the feedback loop — Adam Telle

### Budget & Financial Strategy
* **Funding and Reconciliation:**

* **Tail-to-Tooth Shifts:**
