## This CEO Raised $400 Million To "Poison" Your Data (And Protect Your Privacy) | The Under 30 Podcast

- Channel: Forbes
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u3xipu_sz3w
- Published: 2026-07-06
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260706.html#video-u3xipu_sz3w
- Speakers: Alex York (Host, Forbes Under 30 Podcast); Zoya Hasan (Host, Forbes Under 30 Podcast); Arjun Bhatnagar (Co-founder and CEO, Cloaked)

---

**TL;DR:** Arjun Bhatnagar, CEO of Cloaked, has raised $400 million to pioneer "data poisoning," a privacy strategy that uses masked, unique credentials for every transaction to disrupt the ability of AI and data brokers to track individual identities.

### The Core Philosophy: Data Poisoning
* **The Vulnerability of a Single Number:** Bhatnagar highlights that a single phone number can reveal an individual's Social Security number, address, family members, and credit card history because it acts as a universal identifier.
* **The "Poisoning" Strategy:** Rather than just deleting old data, Cloaked "poisons the well" by providing users with thousands of unique phone numbers, emails, and credit cards.
    * **Disrupting Identity:** By giving different information to every merchant (e.g., Domino’s vs. Sephora), there is no clear consensus for surveillance systems to build a profile.
    * **Contextual Relevance:** Data brokers value current, contextual data; Cloaked renders this data useless by ensuring it doesn't map back to a central identity.
* **The Privacy Paradox:** Bhatnagar notes that "people like to feel known, but they don't want to feel surveilled," aiming to balance personalization with safety.

### Product Capabilities and Security Infrastructure
* **Three-Pillar Protection:** The platform is designed to manage the full lifecycle of personal data:
    1. **Clean up the Past:** Scanning the dark web and removing existing data.
    2. **Defend the Present:** Using AI agents to screen calls; 40% of the millions of calls Cloaked handles daily are identified as AI scams.
    3. **Protect the Future:** Generating real-time masked credentials (emails, phone numbers, credit cards, and soon physical addresses).
* **Technical Architecture:** To prevent a mass breach, Cloaked does not use a single "multi-tenant" database.
    * **Unique Infrastructure:** Every user has their own unique database and infrastructure.
    * **Zero-Knowledge Encryption:** The company cannot access user data; even if the system is breached, an attacker would have to hack every individual user's encrypted database separately.
* **Financial Services Integration:** Cloaked allows users to create merchant-specific credit cards with hard spending limits (e.g., a card exclusively for Spotify capped at $15/month).

### Explosive Business Growth and Market Scale
* **Funding Milestones:** The company has raised over **$400 million**, including a $375 million Series B in 2024.
* **User Adoption:** 
    * **Consumer Base:** Grown from 20,000 to nearly 400,000 paying subscribers in approximately one year.
    * **Pricing Model:** Primarily B2C, with users paying roughly $10/month or $100/year.
    * **Enterprise Shift:** Expanded from zero to over 50 enterprise accounts in four months, driven by employees wanting the tool for business use.
* **Strategic Partnerships:** 
    * **NFLPA Partnership:** Cloaked has a deal to provide privacy services to every NFL athlete to protect them from doxing, physical threats, and financial fraud.
    * **Sports as a Standard:** After working with the Chicago Cubs, Cloaked views professional sports as the "gold standard" for testing high-stakes security.

### Founder Insights: High-Stakes Leadership
* **The $1.5M Marketing Gamble:** In late 2023/early 2024, Cloaked was nearly out of cash. Bhatnagar decided to spend the final $1.5 million on performance marketing to prove the "story" of their growth. The gamble worked, leading to their massive Series B.
* **Storytelling Over Decks:** Bhatnagar advises young founders to "ditch the deck." He practiced by sending voice memos to investors while driving, refining his ability to explain the AI privacy crisis without visual aids.
* **AI Origin Story:** The idea for Cloaked began in 2020 when Bhatnagar built a personal AI that accidentally began "automating" his relationship, sending "I love you" texts and memes to his girlfriend without his knowledge. This made him realize the terrifying lack of control individuals have over personal data used by AI.

> "I realized the question of security and privacy and personal data will be the biggest question of our lifetime when AI comes to life. Now, surprise, we're exactly in that world." — **Arjun Bhatnagar**

> "People like to feel known, but they don't want to feel surveilled... what we really want to accomplish is to make those statements true, so you can keep living your life, but there's Cloaked in the middle saying, 'Let’s figure out how this data should look.'" — **Arjun Bhatnagar**

> "One investor gave me this advice: ditch the deck and tell the story." — **Arjun Bhatnagar**


## Bootstrapped To $1 Billion: How Arizona-Based Lectric eBikes Is Dominating The D2C Market

- Channel: Forbes
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZURQmnymdio
- Published: 2026-04-16
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260416.html#video-ZURQmnymdio
- Speakers: Levi Conlow (Co-founder and CEO, Lectric eBikes); Alex York (Reporter, Forbes)

---

**TL;DR**
By prioritizing immediate profitability and operational leverage over the traditional venture capital "growth-at-all-costs" model, Levi Conlow has scaled Lectric eBikes to over $1 billion in sales and 700,000 units, securing its position as North America's largest e-bike brand.

### The Bootstrapped Business Model vs. VC Failure
*   **Profitable Growth:** Unlike competitors that raised hundreds of millions at billion-dollar valuations and subsequently failed, Lectric has been profitable every year since its 2019 launch.
*   **The "Venture Capital Formula" Trap:** Conlow attributes the failure of many e-bike brands to a reliance on achieving future "operational leverage" to become profitable. Lectric, conversely, ensures every bike designed and sold is profitable from day one.
*   **Margin Management:** The company operates with a strict "margin ceiling" and "margin floor." When operational efficiencies or component prices improve, the savings are reinvested into product quality or price reductions for the consumer rather than padding bottom-line profits.
*   **Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) Advantage:** While expanded into 300 retail locations like Best Buy, the core D2C model provides a wealth of data used to identify market gaps and customer performance needs.

### Supply Chain Complexity and the Tariff Landscape
*   **Global Sourcing:** Lectric manages a complex supply chain utilizing components from China, Cambodia, Vietnam, Taiwan, Thailand, Germany, and the US (Kentucky).
*   **The Tariffs Challenge:** The company has faced significant friction from Section 301, 122, and 232 tariffs. Recently, Lectric moved manufacturing from China to Cambodia, only to face new trade headwinds shortly after.
*   **Absorbing Costs:** Rather than passing price fluctuations to customers, Lectric has "taken it on the chin," absorbing short-term tariff costs to maintain market stability while waiting for a clearer long-term trade climate.
*   **The Onshoring Reality:** Conlow states there are currently no plans to reshore manufacturing to the US, noting that onshoring would increase costs four to five times and introduce high quality-control risks compared to established Asian manufacturing partners with decades of expertise.

### Market Expansion and Strategic Leverage
*   **Aggressive Acquisitions:** Lectric is deploying $10 million toward two non-Lectric e-bike businesses, including the acquisition of a bankrupt competitor. These brands will target market segments (performance and aesthetics) that the core Lectric brand does not currently serve.
*   **Negotiation Power:** Due to its scale (selling nearly 100,000 units of a single model annually), Lectric commands significant leverage with suppliers like LG. This allows them to include premium components—such as two-piston hydraulic brakes—at price points that competitors cannot match.
*   **Customer Service as Marketing:** Lectric maintains an in-house customer service team of over 75 people—a force larger than the entire US operations of some of its largest competitors. Conlow views high-quality service as a primary driver for lowering Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) through referrals.

### Corporate Culture and Philanthropy
*   **Philanthropy as a Core Value:** The company has funded desalination systems in Kenya, schools in Cameroon, and donated over $600,000 to St. Jude. 
*   **Community Impact:** In 2025, the founders announced a program to provide 30 full-ride college scholarships (including housing and meals) for Arizona foster children at Grand Canyon University.
*   **High-Touch Leadership:** Conlow prioritizes employee retention through unconventional methods, such as taking every employee to high-end dinners and hosting "Banner Ceremonies" (modeled after the NBA) to celebrate becoming the industry’s #1 seller.

### Future Outlook: The US E-Bike Market
*   **Market Penetration:** US e-bike adoption has grown from 7% in 2023 to approximately 12% today. However, this remains far behind European markets like Germany and the Netherlands, where penetration exceeds 50%.
*   **Correction vs. Shrinkage:** While the broader market experienced a "right-sizing" after the 2020-2021 boom, Conlow remains bullish, believing that the exit of "undeserving" competitors has created a massive opportunity for market leaders to capture share.

> "A lot of our competitors... their whole US team is smaller than just my customer service team. Why not apply pressure on everyone to have such a high standard placed in the industry?" — **Levi Conlow**

> "If the customer says your product doesn't sell well, the worst thing you could do is try to focus on getting people to believe in that version of the product. You should listen to every single customer... even if that means completely ripping away everything that you cared so much about." — **Levi Conlow**

> "It would be incredibly naive for me to think that I could do it better than [partners in Asia] by onshoring it here. It’s a very, very difficult product." — **Levi Conlow**
