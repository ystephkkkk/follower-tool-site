### TL;DR
Saronic, an autonomous maritime company founded in 2022 by four co-founders, has raised approximately $2.6 billion at a $9.25 billion valuation by scaling autonomous surface vessel production to address America's severe shipbuilding gap with China.

### Macro Fleet Statistics & The Shipbuilding Deficit
* **China's Industrial Output:** **230 to 1 shipbuilding ratio** between China and the U.S., with China controlling 57% of total global shipbuilding capacity via heavily subsidized raw materials, labor, and free shipyard financing — building over 1,000 commercial ships in 2024 compared to just 5 large oceangoing commercial vessels in the U.S. (**Dino Mavrookas**)
* **U.S. Navy Fleet Decline:** The U.S. naval fleet currently sits at around **290 ships**, going in the wrong direction despite Congress setting a statutory minimum of **355 ships** in 2018 (which the Navy revised to **381** in 2024), while the 30-year shipbuilding plan carries a projected cost of **$1.2 trillion** if executable. (**Dino Mavrookas**)
* **Budget Allocations & Exquisite Cost Overruns:** Aircraft carriers cost **$13 billion to $19 billion** and take over 10 years to manufacture, while the Department of War's nearly **$1 trillion to $1.5 trillion budget request** allocated only **1% toward autonomy**, highlighting a massive institutional gap. (**Dino Mavrookas**)
* **The Military-Commercial Discrepancy:** The U.S. launches about **5 to 10 military warships** annually compared to China's **30** (a 6:1 or 7:1 ratio), though allies like South Korea and Japan increasingly rely on Chinese commercial modules and labor. (**Dino Mavrookas**)

### Company Scale, Partnerships & Manufacturing Infrastructure
* **Total Capital & Valuation:** Raised **~$2.6 billion** at a **$9.25 billion valuation** just four years after founding in 2022. (**Dino Mavrookas / Doug Lambert**)
* **Port Alpha Investment:** Investing **$3+ billion into Port Alpha** to revolutionize final outfitting, move traditional shipboard work into subassemblies, and scale production capacity. (**Dino Mavrookas / Doug Lambert**)
* **Palantir Integration:** Utilizes Palantir’s **Foundry program** as core backbone software for internal manufacturing execution systems, material resource planning (MRP), and logistics planning across the business. (**Doug Lambert / Vibhav Altekar**)
* **Path Robotics Collaboration:** Partnering with Path Robotics at the Franklin facility and Gulfcraft shipyard in Louisiana to automate **aluminum welding** capable of passing ABS, IMO, and Coast Guard certifications. (**Doug Lambert / Vibhav Altekar**)
* **Workforce & Supply Chain Scale:** Employs **1,600 people** under operations; previously bought up the vast majority of aluminum in the Louisiana area for Marauder production and is proactively establishing domestic steel pipelines for Port Alpha. (**Doug Lambert**)

### Product Evolution & Autonomous Maritime Technology
* **Product Lineage:** Started with early, lesser-known products including *Spy Glass* and *Cutless*, before scaling to *Corsair*, *Marauder* (a **180-foot ship** analogous to stepping stones toward 400-foot, 800-foot, or Panamanian-sized 1,200-person ships), and Port Alpha manufacturing systems. (**Doug Lambert**)
* **Extreme Operating Stress:** Commercial boats are typically designed for human G-force limits of **4 to 6 Gs**, whereas Saronic autonomous boats have recorded accelerations **north of 20 Gs**. (**Dino Mavrookas / Vibhav Altekar**)
* **Software-Hardware Co-Design:** Built around **Modular Open Systems** with easy-to-use software and hardware APIs, utilizing Nvidia GPU compute, redundant power distribution modules for remote power cycling, and deep simulation testing prior to physical sea trials. (**Vibhav Altekar**)
* **Communications & Resilience:** Implements a redundant **pace plan** (including Starlink and Starshield) to handle tumultuous sea states, signal degradation, bio-fouling, and contested RF environments. (**Vibhav Altekar**)

### Defense Contracting & Acquisition Reform
* **Rapid Initial Contracting:** Secured a **Classified/Cooperative Research and Development Agreement (CRADA)** within the first **90 days** of founding through direct fleet-level operator feedback rather than top-down bureaucratic channels. (**Rob Lehman**)
* **Bureaucratic Friction & Accountability:** Highlighting the need to replace rigid risk-averse guidebooks with AI-augmented tools for contracting officers and placing clear operational risk and authority back on program managers. (**Rob Lehman**)
