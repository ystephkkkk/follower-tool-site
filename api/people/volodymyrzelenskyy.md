### TL;DR
Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy criticized the Trump administration's pressure to halt strikes on Russian energy infrastructure while U.S. intelligence sharing and Starlink speeds were restricted, warning that deals with Moscow only fund continued aggression. He also asserted that Donald Trump harbors an instinctive affinity for autocrats and the super-wealthy.

### U.S. Pressure and Starlink Restrictions
* **Intelligence and Communications Interference:** 
  * **Starlink Slowdowns:** **Volodymyr Zelenskyy** stated that Starlink speeds were deliberately throttled by SpaceX/American will across Ukrainian territory and the capital during critical military moments.
  * **Logistical Impact:** Supply drops of food and water to soldiers in gray zones had to shift entirely to high-speed domestic drones because slowed Starlink-dependent operations risked destruction.
  * **Military-Level Threats:** **Volodymyr Zelenskyy** noted that senior U.S. military officials threatened to block intelligence sharing if Ukraine did not stop attacking Russian oil refineries.

* **The Energy Ceasefire Breakdown:**
  * **Unilateral Halt:** **Volodymyr Zelenskyy** agreed to a 3-day pause on striking Russian energy and diesel systems to support President Trump's proposed energy ceasefire.
  * **Russian Retaliation:** Despite the Ukrainian pause, Russian forces launched massive ballistic and drone attacks hitting civilian and energy infrastructure across Kyiv, Kharkiv, and Lutsk.
  * **Resumption of Strikes:** Following civilian deaths and renewed Russian bombardment, **Volodymyr Zelenskyy** instructed the Ukrainian military to resume responses against Russian energy assets.

### Geopolitical Strategy and the Trump-Putin Relationship
* **Autocratic Affinity:** **Volodymyr Zelenskyy** asserted that Donald Trump has an instinctive affinity for autocrats and the super-wealthy, noting Trump's public respect for leaders who hold power for decades.
* **Economic Motivations:** **Volodymyr Zelenskyy** warned that U.S. eagerness to strike business and energy deals with Moscow generates funding that Russia converts directly into military production capacity.
* **The "Diesel Deal" Risk:** **Volodymyr Zelenskyy** rejected deals that silence Ukrainian battlefield responses, comparing them to slippery slopes that could next lead to gas deals and increased funding for Russian weapon manufacturing.

### European Security and Technology Sharing
* **Military Tech Exchange:** **Volodymyr Zelenskyy** argued that European nations—specifically France, the UK, and the US—benefit heavily from co-production and real-world Ukrainian combat testing of advanced systems like Sampi air defense, Scalps missiles, and anti-drone defenses.
* **Budget Savings for Europe:** **Volodymyr Zelenskyy** noted that Ukraine's battlefield experience saved European budgets billions of dollars on conventional tanks and armaments ill-suited for modern technological warfare.
* **Skepticism Toward Far-Right Figures:** Addressing Marine Le Pen's proposal to halt funding for Ukraine after six months, **Volodymyr Zelenskyy** countered that European security relies on Ukrainian technological cooperation, and claimed Le Pen is not deeply versed in the details of reciprocal security benefits.
