## T-Mobile Q2 2026 Earnings Call Livestream

- Channel: T-Mobile

- Speakers: Srini Gopalan (Senior Leadership, T-Mobile)

---

### TL;DR
T-Mobile delivered an extraordinary Q2 2026, highlighted by a record-high Net Promoter Score (NPS) of 46—the highest ever in wireless across the Big Three carriers—alongside 13% growth in postpaid service revenue and 12% growth in core adjusted EBITDA. Driven by strong underlying business momentum and disciplined execution, management raised full-year adjusted free cash flow guidance to between $18.4 billion and $18.8 billion.

---

### Financial Results & Guidance
* **Postpaid Service Revenue:** Grew **13% year-over-year** in Q2.
* **Total Service Revenue:** Reached **9% growth year-over-year** in Q2, with full-year 2026 service revenues expected to be approximately **$77 billion** (representing 8% growth), and Q3 expected to be approximately **$19.3 billion** (up 6% year-over-year).
* **Core Adjusted EBITDA:** Grew **12% year-over-year** in Q2, with full-year expectations between **$37.1 billion and $37.5 billion** (10% growth at the midpoint), and Q3 expected to be approximately **$9.4 billion** (up 8% year-over-year).
* **Free Cash Flow & CapEx:** Full-year 2026 cash CapEx remains unchanged at approximately **$10 billion**. Adjusted free cash flow guidance was increased to **$18.4 billion to $18.8 billion** (an increase of $200 million at the midpoint), driven primarily by lower cash income taxes.
* **Shareholder Returns:** Repurchased an incremental **$2.5 billion** in Q2 and through July 17. Since late 2022, T-Mobile has repurchased **253 million shares**, reducing total shares outstanding to **1.07 billion**.
* **ARPA & Churn:** Postpaid ARPA grew **2% year-over-year** (and **3.7% excluding M&A**). Postpaid phone churn dropped significantly to **0.85** in Q2. Postpaid net account additions reached **277,000**, with full-year net account additions expected between **950,000 and 1,050,000** (anticipating a temporary Q3 churn elevation due to rate plan modernizations, resulting in an expected ~250,000 net additions for Q3).

### Network Leadership & AI Innovation
* **Third-Party Accolades:** Won "Best Mobile Network" by Ookla for the third straight time; swept every subcategory in OpenSignal's Quality of Experience and Network Performance Awards (making them the most awarded mobile network over the last 5 years); and was named U.S. Test Champion by P3 across all 13 categories tested, including AI Services Champion.
* **AI & Network-Native Apps:** Rolled out live translation on beta in Q2 as T-Mobile's first network-native AI application embedded directly into the core. Long-term, the network is positioned to become connective tissue for physical AI with edge inferencing, partnering with industry leaders such as **Figure AI**.
* **High-Profile Events & Slicing:** Utilized network slicing for live broadcast and wireless terminal ticket entry at major events (PGA, US Open Golf). Achieved the fastest network in all 11 U.S. host locations for the World Cup, utilizing AI-driven **Dynamic CX** and self-organizing networks to autonomously adjust cell sites near crowds.

### Broadband & FWA Growth
* **Industry-Leading Broadband:** Continued as the fastest-growing ISP in the country, adding upper-400,000s in total broadband subscribers in Q2 with strong ARPUs and winning J.D. Power #1 in customer satisfaction.
* **Fiber Performance:** JV fiber rollout is achieving close to **20% penetration** over the first 12 months in deployed areas. Fiber and Fixed Wireless Access (FWA) are treated as complementary, because winning a fiber customer frees up cellular fallow capacity elsewhere.
* **Bundling Metrics:** Approximately **70%** of T-Mobile's broadband customers also bundle mobile with the company.
* **Fallow Capacity Model:** FWA traffic consumption is entirely by design under the fallow capacity model, with total network capacity remaining several times larger than current traffic utilization.

### Customer Experience & Value Propositions
* **NPS Record:** Achieved an all-time high NPS of **46** in Q2—the highest in wireless history across the Big Three.
* **CLV and Port-in Advantage:** Customer Lifetime Values (CLVs) grew by healthy double digits year-over-year. Port-in ARPAs continue to exceed port-out ARPAs by approximately **20%**, with over **60%** of customers on new accounts selecting premium rate plans.
* **Member Month & T-Life:** Celebrated 10 years of T-Mobile Tuesdays with "Member Month" in June, ending the quarter with over **30 million monthly active users** on T-Life.
* **Financial Services & T-Ads:** The T-Mobile Visa credit card launch with Capital One was one of Capital One's most successful co-branded launches and is on track to rank among its leading co-brand programs for new accounts.

### Strategic Capital Allocation & Spectrum
* **Spectrum Pipeline:** Thoughtfully maintaining a capital envelope considerate of upcoming spectrum opportunities in **2027 and 2028**, including **C-band 2.0** and **2.7 GHz** (unlocked via Chairman Carr's commitment to American 6G leadership).
* **Direct-to-Device (D2D):** Starlink D2D usage remains a very small percentage of network usage (**0.0002%** in regular months, **0.0003%** in peak summer months). Conversations regarding the D2D joint venture are progressing rapidly toward a long-form agreement.

**TL;DR**
T-Mobile delivered a strong Q1 2026 performance, raising full-year guidance for EBITDA and free cash flow while emphasizing a "widening differentiation" strategy that leverages network superiority to drive industry-leading growth in mobile, broadband, and emerging AI sectors.

### Financial Performance and Guidance Raise
* **Updated 2026 Guidance:** On the strength of Q1 momentum, leadership raised several full-year targets:
    * **Core Adjusted EBITDA:** Now expected to be between $37.1 billion and $37.5 billion.
    * **Adjusted Free Cash Flow:** Raised to between $18.1 billion and $18.7 billion.
    * **Postpaid Net Account Additions:** Increased to a range of 950,000 to 1,050,000.
* **Service Revenue Growth:** Postpaid service revenue grew 15% year-over-year, with total service revenue up 11%—a rate CFO Peter Osvaldik noted is more than four times that of the nearest competitor.
* **ARPA Expansion:** Postpaid Average Revenue Per Account (ARPA) grew 3.9% in Q1.
    * **Premium Mix:** Over 60% of new accounts are selecting premium-tier rate plans.
* **Capital Allocation:** The company increased its 2026 stockholder return authorization by $3.6 billion, bringing the total authorization to $18.2 billion.

### Broadband and Fiber Expansion
* **Market Leadership:** T-Mobile remains the fastest-growing ISP in America, adding over 500,000 total broadband customers in Q1.
* **New Fiber Joint Ventures:** CEO Srini Gopalan announced two new JVs with infrastructure partners to acquire **GoNetSpeed, Greenlight Networks, and i3 Broadband**.
    * **Strategic Focus:** The company is prioritizing "first to fiber" assets and local scale over a specific "homes passed" target to ensure double-digit IRRs.
    * **Integration:** T-Mobile will manage all retail consumer operations for these assets, using a common IT platform to ensure a unified customer experience.
* **Business Super Broadband:** In partnership with SpaceX/Starlink, T-Mobile is launching a "Super Broadband" product for B2B customers, providing satellite-based redundancy and nationwide coverage for businesses.

### Network Innovation and Physical AI
* **5G Advanced and Robotics:** T-Mobile is connecting its 5G Advanced network to **Figure AI’s F3 humanoid robots**.
    * **Edge Inferencing:** CTO Dr. John Saw highlighted that the network's low latency and "fallow compute" capabilities at the edge are being designed specifically to support the evolution of physical AI.
* **Network-Native AI:** The company is beta-testing a "live translation" application that translates voices into 80 languages directly through the network core, requiring no third-party apps.
* **Business Applications:** The "automated ball-strike system" recently rolled out by Major League Baseball (MLB) was cited as a primary example of 5G Advanced being used for real-time challenge systems.

### Market Differentiation and Governance
* **Customer Loyalty:** T-Mobile reported an industry-leading Net Promoter Score (NPS) of 45, which is 20% higher than its closest competitor.
* **Postpaid Account Churn:** Leadership addressed a slight rise in account churn, attributing it to the mathematical weighting of the high-growth broadband business, which structurally has higher churn than wireless.
* **Merger Speculation:** Addressing rumors regarding a merger with Deutsche Telekom, Srini Gopalan clarified that such a transaction would require a "majority of the minority" approval process by disinterested shareholders.
* **Rural and Business Growth:** T-Mobile currently holds only a 24% share in smaller markets and rural areas, representing what Gopalan called a "substantial growth runway" as network perception improves.

> "We didn't build a 5G advanced network just for faster phones; we actually built it for physical AI... we believe that we have a multi-year advantage over the competition for this." — **Dr. John Saw, CTO**
