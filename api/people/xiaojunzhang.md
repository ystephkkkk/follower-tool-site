### Background & AI Journey
- **Speaker Introduction:** 
  - **Zhang Xiaojun (Host):** Host of the *Zhang Xiaojun Podcast*, exploring the intersection of AI, education, and human society.
  - **Jonathan (Su Tinghao):** 17-year-old high school student (born in 2009, rising to Grade 12) at Chinese International School in Hong Kong, born in Shanghai, and self-taught AI researcher.
- **Spark of Interest:** 
  - **Inspiration from OpenAI & Karpathy:** Sparked by early OpenAI YouTube demos and Andrej Karpathy's 20-hour zero-to-hero video series on building GPT and Transformers.
  - **30-Day Reading Challenge:** Challenged himself to read one machine learning paper every day for 30 days to build foundational research intuition.
- **Accepted ICML 2026 Paper:**
  - **Title:** *Attention Projection Mixing with Exogenous Anchors*
  - **Core Mechanism:** Explores Value Residual Learning by applying RMSNorm on residuals, separating the dual functions of first-layer attention values by doubling projection width.
  - **Resource Constraints:** Burned **3,000 RMB** and **1,500 RMB** on failed un-saved checkpoints due to hyperparameter mistakes, scaling up models on a **0.5B** parameter size using datasets like **FineWeb-Edu** (up to **20B** datasets).
