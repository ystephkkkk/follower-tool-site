## Mukesh Ambani & Larry Fink In Fireside Chat | The Future Of Global Finance & Business

- Channel: CNBC-TV18

- Speakers: Mukesh Ambani (Chairman and Managing Director, Reliance Industries); Larry Fink (Chairman and CEO, BlackRock)

---

**TL;DR:** India is entering a 20- to 30-year "Era of India" characterized by 8–10% GDP growth; the JioBlackRock partnership aims to capitalize on this by using technology to convert India’s massive "unproductive" savings (gold and bank deposits) into compoundable capital market investments.

### The "Era of India" and Macroeconomic Outlook
*   **Sustained High Growth:** Both leaders project India will remain the world's fastest-growing large economy, with GDP growth of 8–10% likely and double-digit growth possible. 
    *   Ambani projects India’s GDP could reach $25–30 trillion within the next 30 years.
*   **Stability and Policy Continuity:** Ambani credits the last 15 years of stable leadership for providing the predictability necessary for long-term investment.
    *   **Viksit Bharat 2047:** The national goal of becoming a developed nation by 2047 has created a unified aspirational mindset across the population.
    *   **Fiscal Responsibility:** India maintains a conservative debt-to-GDP ratio of approximately 50%, significantly lower than the global norm of 100%.
*   **Infrastructure Execution:** India has built more roads in the last 15 years than many developed nations at their peak and has become a global leader in renewable energy and 5G rollout.

### The JioBlackRock Partnership: Democratizing Wealth
*   **From Savers to Investors:** A core objective of the partnership is shifting the Indian mindset from "saving" (often in unproductive assets like gold or silver) to "investing."
    *   **Unproductive Assets:** Ambani noted that India imported $60 billion in gold and $10–15 billion in silver last year, mostly for domestic savings rather than industrial use.
*   **The Power of Compounding:** Fink emphasized that keeping money in bank accounts fails to capture the growth of the country. Investing in equity allows citizens to grow their personal wealth alongside the national economy.
*   **The "5-Minute" Deal:** The partnership between Reliance and BlackRock was famously struck during a five-minute car ride, based on a shared philosophy of using profit for societal betterment.
*   **Market Ambition:** Ambani envisions the startup ecosystem creating "100 new Reliances," suggesting a massive expansion of the private sector.

### AI and Technology: Productivity over "Bubbles"
*   **Not an AI Bubble:** Larry Fink dismissed fears of an AI bubble, stating the greater risk is *under-investing*, which would allow competitors like China to lead the next era of technology.
*   **Solving Social Challenges:** Ambani views AI as the only way to scale critical services for 1.4 billion people:
    *   **Education:** Delivering quality education to 200 million children in remote villages via AI-driven digital tools.
    *   **Healthcare:** Creating a digital-physical health system at a fraction of global costs.
*   **Acceptance of Change:** Fink noted that India is more accepting of technological disruption (e.g., the digitization of the Rupee) than most developed economies, positioning it as a prime laboratory for AI deployment.
*   **Demographic Dividend:** While some fear AI will displace jobs, Fink argues it will increase productivity in aging nations (Japan, Italy) and act as a growth engine for high-population nations like India.

### Investment Strategy: Long-Termism vs. Market Noise
*   **"Honest" Markets:** Fink argues that while media focuses on daily "velocity of money" and interest rate trajectories, markets are long-term mirrors of economic reality that eventually self-correct.
*   **Global Risks:** Fink is less concerned with "front-page" issues like interest rates and more concerned with ignored long-term problems, specifically the excessive deficits in developed economies like the U.S.
*   **Patient Capital:** Ambani highlighted that India's needs—energy self-sufficiency, physical infrastructure, and country-scale intelligence—require 20- to 30-year "patient capital."
    *   The challenge is not a lack of global capital, but rather the capacity for execution and creating transparent frameworks for investors.

> "The reality is for most people, they need to be in a position to grow with each and every country's growth. If you believe in the 'Era of India,' we need to compel hundreds of millions of Indians to invest alongside that growth." — **Larry Fink**

> "A tree only attracts attention if it bears fruit. Today, the tree of the Indian economy is bearing fruit with great visibility, sustainability, and abundance." — **Mukesh Ambani**

> "It's better to be an optimist and being wrong than being a pessimist who's right... long-term optimists generally win." — **Larry Fink** (referencing Elon Musk)

## Mukesh Ambani Speech at AI Summit: Will Prove AI Doesn't Take Away Jobs, Creates New Ones | N18V

- Channel: CNBC-TV18
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_bNMvPqPktU
- Published: 2026-02-19
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260219.html#video-_bNMvPqPktU
- Speakers: Mukesh Ambani (Chairman & Managing Director, Reliance Industries Limited)

---

**TL;DR:** Mukesh Ambani, Chairman of Reliance Industries, announced a ₹10 lakh crore investment over seven years to transition India from the "internet era" to the "intelligence era," pledging to make AI as affordable and ubiquitous as mobile data while driving national development toward a "Viksit Bharat" by 2047.

### Strategic Announcements and Investments
*   **Massive Capital Commitment:** Reliance and Jio will invest ₹10 lakh crore over the next seven years to build durable economic value and strategic AI resilience.
*   **Shift to the Intelligence Era:** Ambani aims to replicate Jio’s disruption of the telecom market by making "intelligence" extremely affordable and accessible to every citizen, sector, and government service.
*   **National Sovereignty:** A core tenet of the strategy is that "India cannot afford to rent intelligence," necessitating the development of homegrown, sovereign AI capabilities.

### Sovereign Compute Infrastructure
*   **Gigawatt-Scale Data Centers:** Construction has begun on multi-gigawatt, AI-ready data centers in Jamnagar.
    *   The first 120 megawatts are scheduled to come online in the second half of 2026.
*   **Green Energy Advantage:** The infrastructure will be powered by an in-house surplus of 10 gigawatts of green energy, primarily solar-anchored, from Kutch and Andhra Pradesh.
*   **Edge Compute Integration:** To ensure low latency and affordability, intelligence will be deployed at the "edge" of Jio’s network, bringing AI tools directly to local clinics, classrooms, and small businesses (kirana stores).

### Sector-Specific AI Applications (Jio Intelligence)
*   **Education (Jio Shikshak):** An adaptive AI teaching assistant supporting 22 languages, aimed at empowering 250 million school children and 50 million higher education students.
*   **Healthcare (Jio Arogya AI):** A platform designed to deliver preliminary medical guidance in local languages in under five minutes via any mobile phone.
*   **Agriculture (Jio Krishi):** Uses satellite imagery and precision weather data to provide voice-first advice to 140 million farmers to optimize yields and income.
*   **Consumer Tech & Inclusion:** 
    *   **Jio Bharat IQ:** A voice-first AI companion for accessing government services.
    *   **Jio Frames:** AI-powered glasses designed to make intelligence "ambient" and natural.

### Economic Impact and Guarantees
*   **Job Creation vs. Displacement:** Ambani countered fears of automation by asserting that AI will not take away jobs but will create new, high-skill work opportunities.
*   **Linguistic Inclusion:** A commitment that "Jio AI" will speak Indian languages and be rooted in Indian culture, ensuring that AI isn't just a tool for the English-speaking elite.
*   **Ecosystem Partnerships:** Reliance plans to work "shoulder to shoulder" with startups, IITs, and other industrial groups to embed AI across manufacturing, finance, and retail rather than just acting as an importer of foreign technology.

### Notable Quotes
> "I see AI as a modern-day Akshay Patra, the legendary vessel in Mahabharata that provided endless nourishment to all. Likewise, AI offers limitless augmentation in knowledge, efficiency and productivity." — **Mukesh Ambani**

> "India cannot afford to rent intelligence. Therefore, we will reduce the cost of intelligence as dramatically as we did the cost of data." — **Mukesh Ambani**

> "We will prove that AI does not take away jobs, rather it will create new high-skill work opportunities." — **Mukesh Ambani**

> "The AI story has shifted from who has the best model to who can build the strongest ecosystem for speed and scale of usage." — **Mukesh Ambani**

BlackRock CEO Larry Fink and Reliance Chairman Mukesh Ambani affirmed that India is entering a multi-decade "era of India" driven by sustained economic growth, stable leadership, and rapid technological adoption, emphasizing the critical need for domestic capital market investment to unlock its full potential.

### India's Economic Ascendance: The "Era of India"

*   **Long-Term Growth Opportunity:** BlackRock CEO Larry Fink highlights India's growth as a long-term horizon (20-25 years), not a short-term phenomenon, dubbing it "the era for India" and personally desiring to invest in it.

        *   **Stable Leadership:** 15 years of consistent economic leadership under PM Modi.
        *   **Clear Goals:** Aspirational target of a developed "Viksit Bharat" by 2047.
        *   **Execution at Scale:** Building extensive infrastructure (roads, power capacity, leading renewable energy production), and deploying superior, affordable 5G across every village.
        *   **Policy Continuity & Fiscal Prudence:** Sustainable policies and conservative debt management (50% of GDP vs. global norm of 100%).
        *   **Startup Ecosystem:** A vibrant startup scene fostering ambition and confidence among young entrepreneurs.
        *   **Societal Confidence:** A prevailing belief among parents and youth that tomorrow will be better than today.
        *   **Global Attention:** India is attracting significant global investment and partnerships.

### Jio BlackRock's Vision: Transforming Savings into Investment

*   **Compelling Domestic Investment:** Larry Fink argues that hundreds of millions of Indians need to be compelled to invest in capital markets alongside India's growth.
    *   This domestic investment will reduce India's need for foreign capital and build the economy on a stronger foundation of local savings.
*   **Purpose-Driven Partnership:** Mukesh Ambani emphasizes the alignment of Reliance's and BlackRock's philosophies, focusing on putting money to work for the betterment of society, with returns as a byproduct.
*   **Converting Unproductive Savings:** Ambani notes that while Indians are natural savers, a significant portion (e.g., $60-75 billion in gold/silver imports annually for domestic savings) is unproductive.
    *   **Jio BlackRock's Role:** The opportunity is to encourage Indian savers to invest in capital markets in a "safe, transparent, and consistent way" to convert savings into compounding earnings.
    *   **Accessibility & Technology:** The ambition is to disrupt the financial sector by making investment access simple, affordable, and available to all, leveraging technology and broad distribution, akin to the Jio telecom revolution.
*   **Patient Capital for Long-Term Opportunities:** Ambani highlights India's need for patient capital over 10-30 year horizons for critical investments.
    *   **Key Sectors:** Energy self-sufficiency, physical infrastructure for 1.4 billion people, and country-scale intelligence infrastructure.
    *   These opportunities are in the hundreds of billions of dollars and are limited by execution, not capital.

### AI as a Catalyst for Growth and Societal Progress

*   **No AI Bubble, but a Necessity:** Larry Fink dismisses the notion of an "AI bubble," stating the greatest risk is *not* investing in AI, potentially ceding leadership to competitors like China.
    *   AI will be profoundly disruptive and advancing across industries, from drug discovery to new power sources.
*   **Broadening Economic Success:** A critical challenge for AI is ensuring it broadens economic success globally, rather than narrowing it, by increasing productivity and GDP, especially for countries with diminishing populations.
*   **India's Competitive Edge in AI:** India's large, youthful population, combined with its high acceptance and rapid deployment of new technologies (evidenced by the digitization of the rupee), positions it uniquely to leverage AI for growth.
*   **Solving India's Grand Challenges:** Mukesh Ambani sees AI as "hugely advantageous" for India, enabling solutions for large-scale societal problems:
    *   **Education:** Delivering quality education to 200 million children, even in remote villages, at a fraction of global costs.
    *   **Healthcare:** Providing digital-physical healthcare for 1.4 billion people at a fraction of global costs.
    *   **Security & Taxation:** Enhancing law enforcement and simplifying transactional transparency for citizens and businesses.
*   **Productivity & Catch-Up:** India must embrace AI as a competitive advantage to drive productivity, increase per capita income (from $4,000 to potentially $40-50,000 like the US), and redefine its global position within the next 20-30 years.

### Long-Term Optimism and Global Investment Strategy

*   **Beyond Short-Term Volatility:** Larry Fink emphasizes focusing on long-term market trends rather than short-term fluctuations, political pressures, or daily news cycles. Markets are an honest reflection of global economies over time and tend to self-correct.
    *   He sees discussions of global problems as a positive sign, indicating mitigation efforts.
*   **Optimism as a Winning Strategy:** Fink advocates for long-term optimism, echoing Elon Musk's sentiment: "better to be an optimist and being wrong than being a pessimist who's right."
    *   He highlights the importance of ambition, imagination, and guts in driving growth and opportunity, exemplified by figures like Musk and Ambani.
*   **Multigenerational Opportunity:** Mukesh Ambani describes the current period as a "multigenerational opportunity" and a "once in tens of centuries opportunity" for India.
    *   He projects India's GDP to outgrow the world, potentially reaching $25-30 trillion in 20-30 years, contributing significantly to a global economy that could reach $300 trillion with AI productivity.
    *   This creates enormous new market value for young Indians to participate in and benefit from.
*   **Message to Young Indians:** Both leaders urge young Indians to believe in their country's growth, embrace technology, and channel savings into productive investments in the capital markets to build wealth and financial security.
