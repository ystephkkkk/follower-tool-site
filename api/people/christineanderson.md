### Technology Evolution, Scale, and Superintelligence
- **Eric Schmidt’s Tech Tenure:** Eric Schmidt reflects on 55 years in technology, starting with mainframes and noting that as people get older, they should take *more* risk because they have less to lose in service of democracy and freedom.

- **Recent Mathematical Breakthroughs:** Computers recently solved **10** major math problems in the last month, including Navier-Stokes equations governing fluid flows, which improve algorithmic answers for aviation, gas efficiency, and rocket travel.
- **Long Reasoning Capability:** Schmidt identifies the arrival of long reasoning (chains of **1,000 to 2,000 steps** or up to **8 hours** of reasoning) as the most important recent advancement, allowing AIs to function as 24-hour agent thought partners.

- **Revenue Determination:** Eric Schmidt points out that AI company revenues are completely determined by their data centers, shifting software economics by requiring heavy hardware investments.
- **Electricity Demand:** AI data center demand is projected to reach **11%** of U.S. electricity demand by **2030**, up historically from **2% to 3%**.
- **Megawatt Economics:** Jaz Khaira explains that companies can generate **$50 to $70 million** in revenue per megawatt against all-in costs of **$12 to 15 million** (including chips and depreciating cycles), driving firms to plow today's cash into purchasing tomorrow's AI factories.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
- **Fusion Energy Timeline:** Eric Schmidt predicts that fusion energy will likely be achieved within **2 to 3 years**, with commercial realization in **3 to 5 years**.
- **Superintelligence Consensus Timeline:** Schmidt notes the "San Francisco consensus" among developers expects superintelligence leaps in **2 years**, though he personally believes it will take at least a decade or happen within his lifetime.
