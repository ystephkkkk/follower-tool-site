## Ares CEO on Access & Risks in Private Markets

- Channel: Bloomberg Live
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4TzrJ41vAFU
- Published: 2026-03-03
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260303.html#video-4TzrJ41vAFU
- Speakers: Michael Arougheti (Co-Founder & CEO, Ares Management Corporation); Dani Burger (Anchor, Bloomberg)

---

**TL;DR**
Ares CEO Michael Arougheti argues that the private markets are entering a "winner-take-most" era where scale drives performance, dismisses "irresponsible" predictions of high default rates, and views persistent inflation as a net positive for private credit returns.

### Long-Term Resilience and Macro Volatility
*   **Insulation from Daily Volatility:** Ares operates on 5- to 15-year investment horizons, making the firm less reactive to daily geopolitical headlines or short-term market fluctuations.
*   **Focus on Secular Themes:** Investment strategies are built around long-term global shifts, including:
    *   Re-aligned global supply chains and U.S. re-industrialization.
    *   Digital infrastructure and the "digitization" of the economy.
*   **The "Rates-Higher-for-Longer" Benefit:** Contrary to market anxiety, persistent inflation and elevated base rates are theoretically positive for private credit, as they drive higher total returns for lenders.
*   **Structural Hedging:** Large platforms balance risks internally; for example, if AI disrupts a traditional software portfolio, the firm’s data center and digital infrastructure business typically benefits.

### The Consolidation of Private Markets
*   **Scale as a Performance Driver:** In private markets, unlike public markets, size is an asset rather than an "enemy of performance." Larger firms have more resources for local origination, product development, and global distribution.
*   **The "Middle-Market" Squeeze:** Arougheti predicts a shakeout where the industry consolidates into a handful of giant platforms (capturing up to 25% market share each) and small, niche "alpha" generators.
    *   **Manager Rationalization:** Global Limited Partners (LPs) are looking to allocate more capital to fewer managers to gain better economics, information analytics, and access.
*   **Deployment Advantage:** Last year, Ares raised $115 billion but deployed $145 billion, illustrating how market leadership compounds through the ability to put massive amounts of capital to work.

### Growth Strategy and Private Equity Expansion
*   **Organic vs. Inorganic Growth:** Historically, 75-80% of Ares' growth is organic. Acquisitions are reserved for "transformational shifts" where the firm lacks specific scale or distribution (e.g., data centers or wealth distribution).
*   **Targeting Private Equity:** Arougheti identified Private Equity as an area for potential inorganic growth, noting it has not yet matched the scale of Ares’ credit or real estate platforms.
*   **Alpha vs. Beta:** The era of "beta players"—managers who relied on low interest rates and market tailwinds—is over. Ares is looking for managers who can demonstrate true "alpha" by fundamentally improving the companies they own.

### The Retail "Wealth" Channel and Redemption Noise
*   **Indiscriminate Headlines:** Redemptions in retail-focused funds (like those at Blackstone or Blue Owl) are often "noise" rather than a reflection of bad performance.
*   **The 5% Redemption Math:** Arougheti clarified that a 5% redemption queue often represents only a small fraction of investors seeking liquidity for outside reasons, while 97% of capital remains committed.
*   **Pro-Cyclical Risks:** Firms over-indexed to wealth distribution can be pro-cyclical. To counter this, Ares maintains a massive "institutional fund complex" to ensure dry powder is available when markets are dislocated.

### Default Rates and Specialized Asset Classes
*   **Rebutting UBS Default Predictions:** Arougheti labeled predictions of 15% default rates in private credit as "irresponsible," noting that private credit defaults have historically lagged behind those of bank loans and bonds.
*   **The Sports Ecosystem:** Ares is "all in" on sports as a new asset class.
    *   **Investment Value:** Sports assets are almost completely uncorrelated to the S&P 500 or traditional credit, offering unique inflation protection and a "store of value."

### Notable Quotes
> "In the private markets, size is not the enemy of performance... it’s quite the opposite, because the larger you get, the more resources you have... the more relevant you are to global investors." — **Michael Arougheti**

> "If you find ourselves in an environment where we still have inflation and rates are elevated, that's actually theoretically good for total returns in private credit." — **Michael Arougheti**

> "We have a simple saying at Ares that assets follow performance. If you perform, you grow, and if you do well for your investors, they do well by you." — **Michael Arougheti**

> "If you're talking about 15% default rates in private credit... everything else in your portfolio I assure you is going to be completely torched." — **Michael Arougheti**


## CEO Michael Arougheti on Ares Record Q4 '25 Earnings, Scale in Private Markets, Retaining Top Talent

---

**TL;DR**
Ares Management reported record AUM of $622 billion for Q4 2025 (29% annual growth), signaling a massive disconnect between public market volatility and the robust institutional demand for private credit despite concerns over AI disruption and tightening spreads.

### Record Financial Growth and Market Disconnect
*   **Record AUM:** Total assets under management reached **$622 billion** in Q4 2025.
    *   The firm’s assets grew by **29% in 2025**, driven by record inflows for its US direct lending business.
*   **Fundamental Divergence:** CEO Michael Arougheti highlights a "huge disconnect" between public equity markets (software indices down 20-22%) and the fundamentals of private credit.
*   **Guidance and Dividends:** 
    *   Declared a **20% forward increase** in the dividend.
    *   Five-year guidance projects earnings growth of **16-20%** and dividend growth of **20%+**.
*   **Yield Performance:** Private credit continues to generate **150 to 200 basis points of excess return** over broadly syndicated loans, even as spreads tighten across the broader market (high yield, high grade, and loans).

### Defensive Credit Profile and AI Exposure
*   **Software Exposure:** Just **9% of the private credit book** (6% of total AUM) is tied to software, countering market fears of AI-driven sector collapse.
*   **Seniority and Safety:** These loans are largely senior secured with a **30% loan-to-value (LTV)** ratio.
    *   Portfolios are comprised of mature enterprise software businesses with **40%+ EBITDA margins**.
    *   Weighted average maturity is short, at **3 to 4 years**, with stream yields between **10-12%**.
*   **AI as a Catalyst:** Rather than just a risk, Arougheti views AI as a growth driver for Ares’ digital infrastructure and renewable energy practices.
    *   **Portfolio De-leveraging:** AI implementation is expected to improve margins and free cash flow across general portfolios, naturally de-leveraging the book.

### The Strategy of Scale and Industry Consolidation
*   **Scale Advantage:** Unlike public markets where size can diminish performance, Arougheti argues that **size drives performance in private markets** due to the resource-intensive nature of sourcing and managing "esoteric" investments.
*   **M&A Activity:** Recent consolidation (e.g., KKR/Arctos, CVC/Marathon, EQT/Coller) is driven by institutional investors wanting to consolidate capital with fewer global managers.
    *   **Succession Planning:** Much of the current M&A wave is driven by founders in their 60s and 70s seeking to solidify their legacies.
    *   **Ares M&A:** Approximately **25% of Ares’ historical growth** has come from acquisitions, which remains a key part of their future strategy.
*   **Fee Consistency:** Despite "retail" expansion, Ares is not experiencing fee compression in high-alpha products; fee rates have remained steady at approximately **1%**.

### Talent Retention and the AI-Driven Shift in Labor
*   **Leadership Stability:** Ares maintains low turnover among senior staff; all co-founders remain in management roles.
*   **Succession Execution:** Arougheti recently promoted two long-standing partners to **Co-Presidents**, following his own 10-year tenure as CEO and a smooth transition from predecessor Tony Ressler.
*   **Evolution of Junior Roles:** AI is expected to replace the "mundane" aspects of junior analyst work (e.g., 24-hour model grinding).
    *   **Future Talent Profile:** The firm will pivot toward hiring talent capable of **systemic and critical thinking** rather than pure manual data synthesis.

> "There's a huge, huge disconnect... I think what the market is missing is how attractive these loans have been and how difficult it will be to actually displace them." — **Michael Arougheti**

> "Value is driven through your ability to source, develop, structure and manage esoteric investments... the larger you get the higher the likelihood that you can actually capture whatever excess return and alpha's in the market." — **Michael Arougheti**

> "Everything that we're deploying [in AI] now is being deployed to enhance the work that's already getting done... we hope that we're hiring young people that know how to think systemically." — **Michael Arougheti**
