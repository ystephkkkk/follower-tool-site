*   **Diplomatic Resolution:** Both leaders emphasized that the U.S. is uniquely positioned to bring both sides of the Russian-Ukrainian war to the table.

*   **National Interest as a Standard:** Rubio defended the "America First" and "Hungary First" approaches, stating that a leader who does not act in their own nation's interest "won't be leader for long."
