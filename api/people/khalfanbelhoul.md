---

**TL;DR:** The inaugural Annual Meeting of Global Future Leaders concluded in Dubai with over 500 leaders committing to intergenerational collaboration, strategic foresight, and action-driven frameworks to navigate AI, climate, and economic shifts. Key announcements included the launch of the Center for Global Future Opportunities and the upcoming Annual Meeting scheduled for October 12–14, 2027.

### Event Overview & Context
* **Convening Scale:** 
  * Brought together over 500 leaders across generations, including more than 150 CEOs and chairs, Young Global Leaders (YGLs), Global Shapers, and Future 20 Honorees.
  * Hosted in Dubai in partnership with the World Economic Forum and the UAE Government (Dubai Future Foundation / Prime Minister's Office).
* **Core Themes Explored:** 
  * AI safety, governance, and economic workforce transitions.
  * Climate risks as financial realities requiring national and corporate resilience.
  * Intergenerational dialogue bridging the 27-year leadership gap between veteran executives and young innovators.

### Key Insights by Speaker
* **Ida Jeng Christensen** *(Opening & Moderation)*
  * Highlighted the event's ambition to create a human-centric, future-oriented, and solution-focused bridge between emerging and established leaders.
  * Noted that slido poll results identified **empathy, adaptability, listening, critical thinking, emotional intelligence, and humility** as essential leadership skills for the next generation.
* **Elisabeth Burda Furtwängler** *(Shareholder, Burda Media / Co-founder, Elisabeth Foundation / YGL)*
  * **Listening as Leadership:** Emphasized Esther Perel’s quote: *"The quality of listening shapes what is being said."*
  * **Slowing Down to Speed Up:** Advised leaders facing the acceleration of AI and climate change to step back and trust internal instincts rather than succumbing to demands for immediate action.
  * **Cathedral Thinking:** Encouraged accepting humility and recognizing that leaders are part of a greater web of life, contributing small pieces to long-term generational goals.
* **Benjamin Pfeffer** *(Strategy & Sales Director, Meridian / Global Shaper / Future 20 Honoree)*
  * **Global Shapers Community:** Noted that 450 global hubs drive action under-30, citing Natalie Pierce's opening plenary statistic that **two-thirds of young people are excited about the future**.
  * **Intergenerational Foresight:** Stressed the value of pairing the 30-year industry veteran's vision with the ambition of the recent university graduate across a 27-year gap.
* **Doone Roisin Hansen** *(Founder & CEO, Imagine If / YGL)*
  * **Action Framework:** Summarized the mindset of over 750 interviewed successful entrepreneurs as *"imagine if, and then they do."*
  * **Listening to Signals:** Urged leaders to heed signals from marginalized groups, women, and young people by actively inviting them into executive rooms and pipelines.
* **Katie Knight** *(President & CEO, Siegel Family Endowment / WEF Executive Fellow / YGL)*
  * **Shattering Paradigms:** Stated that in the next 12 months, education, work paradigms, and regional economies must shift away from top-down decrees toward bottom-up collaboration.
  * **Human-Centric AI:** Warned against a *"Jurassic Park style model"* of AI development (*"just because you can doesn't mean you should"*), insisting humans must define technological utility.
* **Khalfan Belhoul** *(CEO, Dubai Future Foundation)*
  * **New UAE Initiatives:** Announced the release of *The Fourth Industrial Revolution in the UAE: 100 Use Cases* report by the UAE Center for the 4IR and WEF.
  * **Global Center:** Highlighted His Highness Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum's announcement of the **Center for Global Future Opportunities**, connecting global foresight with concrete execution.
* **Ohood Bint Khalfan Al Roumi** *(Minister of State for Development and the Future, UAE)*
  * **Optimism as Commitment:** Affirmed the UAE's philosophy that challenges unlock new possibilities and that optimism provides the courage to begin and the humility to learn.
  * **Future Metrics:** Announced the upcoming **Future Leaders Outlook** report and confirmed dates for the next Annual Meeting of Global Future Leaders: **October 12–14, 2027**.
* **Sebastian Buckup** *(World Economic Forum)*
  * **Closing Reflection:** Summarized the foundational ethos of the inaugural meeting, reiterating that dialogue is a necessity in fragile times and foresight is an active tool for shaping shared futures.

### Strategic Announcements & Deliverables
* **New Center:** **Center for Global Future Opportunities** launched as a partnership between the UAE government and the World Economic Forum, based in Dubai.
* **New Reports & Frameworks:** 
  * *The Fourth Industrial Revolution in the UAE: 100 Use Cases* report.
  * The newly launched **Leadership Commitment Framework**.
  * The upcoming **Future Leaders Outlook** report (in partnership with the UAE).
* **Future Convening:** The next Annual Meeting of Global Future Leaders is officially scheduled for **October 12–14, 2027** in the UAE.
