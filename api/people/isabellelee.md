### TL;DR
White House National Economic Council Director Kevin Hassett defended the administration's economic record, dismissing high September inflation figures as a temporary energy-driven blip while maintaining that real incomes and consumption are robust. Hassett also addressed Federal Reserve policy, geopolitical energy impacts, and a White House panel reviewing Fed Governor Lisa Cook.

### Economic Data & Inflation Outlook
* **Inflation Assessment:**
  * **September Spike:** Acknowledge PCE inflation rose 0.5% in September—the highest in six months—largely driven by energy prices.
  * **The "Blip" Argument:** Argue that September was an outlier, noting that the three preceding months aligned with the Fed's target, and pointing to Cleveland Fed October nowcasts indicating a return to ~2% inflation.
* **Consumer Health & Real Incomes:**
  * **Consumption Growth:** Note that real consumption since President Trump took office is up $1 trillion, or roughly $5,000 per family, after adjusting for inflation.
  * **Income Projections:** Cite upcoming Census data showing real incomes projected to rise by about $2,500 by the end of the year.
  * **Labor Market Strength:** Point to initial jobless claims remaining at historic lows and strong consumer confidence as evidence that underlying economic fundamentals remain healthy.

### Energy Markets & Geopolitical Factors
* **Energy Price Drivers:**
  * **Middle East Actions:** State that energy prices rose because President Trump took decisive action to prevent Iran from obtaining a nuclear weapon.
  * **Supply Mitigations:** Highlight efforts by the US Navy protecting strait traffic, Gulf countries building backup pipelines, domestic refineries increasing capacity, and European nations releasing strategic reserves of refined products to stabilize energy supply.

### Federal Reserve Policy & Independence
* **Monetary Policy & Dissent:**
  * **Data-Dependent Approach:** Maintain that Chairman Warsh is leading a data-driven committee, noting that if October inflation data comes in high, the Fed will "probably appropriately act" with rate hikes.
  * **Long-Term Expectations:** Point out that 30-year Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS) indicate markets expect long-term inflation to settle around 2.1%.
* **Federal Reserve Governance & Lisa Cook Review:**
  * **White House Panel:** Confirm participation in a newly formed three-person presidential commission evaluating Fed Governor Lisa Cook following her recent rate-hike dissent.
  * **Fair Process:** Characterize the investigation as operating under carefully scrutinized instructions aligned with Supreme Court rulings, with a collegial meeting scheduled between White House and Cook's representatives in December.
