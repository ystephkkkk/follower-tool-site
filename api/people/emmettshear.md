## "It's So Over?" Emmett Shear, Brendan Foody, Byron Deeter & Nico Rosberg on Tech's Future

- Channel: Sourcery with Molly O'Shea
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EsbZpadq-MQ
- Published: 2026-11-21
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20261121.html#video-EsbZpadq-MQ
- Speakers: Molly O'Shea (Host, Sourcery); Emmett Shear (Former Interim CEO, OpenAI); Brendan Foody (Founder, Mercor)

---

**TL;DR:** Amid market volatility, anticipation of Anthropic’s IPO, and Jensen Huang’s departure from Nvidia, AI founders and investors at the Cerebral Valley AI Summit see unprecedented private-market scale ($3.6T on Bessemer's AI 100) alongside a shift in value from raw reasoning and software toward physical deployments, verification, inference, and human agency.

### Market Volatility, Private Valuations & The Nvidia Transition
* **Unprecedented Private-Market Scale:** Byron Deeter (Bessemer Venture Partners) noted that the AI supercycle combines the internet and cloud waves, creating a debut **AI 100 list** worth **$3.6 trillion** (compared to the Cloud 100, which reached $1 trillion over a decade).
  * Companies on the AI 100 are growing at an average rate of **500%**, with an average raise of over **$1 billion** and a mean valuation of **$36 billion**.
  * **20%** of the list is less than two years old, and none are public yet. Bessemer has invested in **250 AI companies** alone.
* **Navigating Jensen Huang’s Departure:**
  * **Emmett Shear (Former Interim CEO of OpenAI)** compared the Nvidia situation to his own interim CEO experience, noting that "when you get on the inside, you often find there's less of the story than you thought there was."
  * **Nico Rosberg (F1 World Champion & Investor)** warned that open-source AI could struggle without Huang, who spent **$30 billion** in the first half of the year supporting the ecosystem. However, he reminded LPs that VC is a **7-to-10-year** exit game.
  * **Ethan Barajas (Icarus)** noted that Nvidia was the "glue player" and voice of reason; without Huang, downstream physical AI players face negative effects and must diversify away from single-ecosystem reliance. Despite the Nasdaq dropping 5 points and Anthropic coming out at 1.4 instead of 2, he expects a "very, very soft landing" with a quick rebound.

### AI Alignment, Trust & Verification
* **The "Sociopathic Snake" Risk:** Emmett Shear argued that logic, reasoning, and programming have been commoditized, leaving social modeling as the next frontier. He warned that training AIs in simulations to perform socially positive behaviors only when watched creates "cuckoo birds" that pretend to care—leading to a "sociopathic snake" if humans cannot tell the difference.
  * Shear emphasized that alignment must move beyond rule-based social control ("super ego") toward deeper alignment with self and relationships, allowing AIs to partake in an ongoing standard-setting cycle.
* **Collapsing the Cost of Verification:** Alex Garden (Mnemom) stated that while frontier AI reduces the cost of work toward zero, businesses aren't seeing the value because the bottleneck shifted to **verification**.
  * Mnemom builds runtimes, alignment, governance, and trust systems that mathematically and cryptographically prove expected work was done, collapsing verification costs to zero and enabling a "new human Renaissance."

### Inference, Space Robotics & Emerging Infrastructure
* **The Inference Exponential:** Alex Heath (Sources / Sound Ventures) highlighted AI inference as vastly underappreciated, noting the AWS CEO recently told him current real-world inference is **not even 1%** of what it will become.
* **On-Orbit Robotics & Space Data Centers:** Ethan Barajas (Icarus) is deploying on-orbit maintenance robots (first handed over to **NASA** in January) to free astronauts from moving **3.5 tons** of cargo every **60 days**—a task that takes **14 days** for four astronauts costing **$130,000/hour** each ($3.2M/day).
  * Barajas noted that software moats are disappearing, shifting the true moat to hardware and physical deployments. With terrestrial data centers heavily regulated, he expects more volume and data centers to move on-orbit.
* **Robotics & Cyber Opportunities:** Nico Rosberg is watching robotics closely (calling out **Edged** for inference infrastructure and **Flapping Airplanes**), though he noted general intelligence for unprepared tasks remains "miles away." He also flagged severe cybersecurity vulnerabilities, including attackers stealing GitHub tokens and credit cards.
* **Local Hardware Prep:** Aidan Guo (Attn), who raised **$6 million** (from Village, Neo, SB Angel, Chris Ray, and angels) to capture passive knowledge-work exhaust for process mapping, advised everyone to buy local hardware to run small open-source models in case of infrastructure outages.

### The Future of Work, Expertise & Founder Dynamics
* **More Jobs, Not Fewer:** Brendan Foody (Mercor) argued that people underestimate the elasticity of demand for jobs, predicting that physical-world and intrinsically human tasks will take an extremely long time to automate. He also praised **Felicis** (which led Mercor's Series B) as the most founder-friendly firm in Silicon Valley and highlighted **Conway** (founded by Anne) for consumer and financial prediction models.
* **The Decline of Expertise:** James Detweiler (Felicis) agreed with Foody on job growth but argued that human **expertise** is on the way out as a source of purpose. As models generate expertise, the new premium bottlenecks will shift to **imagination, curiosity, agency, trustworthiness, and locality**.
  * Detweiler also highlighted **Enigma** (reimagining human-agent-robotics interfaces) and **Solcoa** (rare earth metals, founded by Huma) from the Felicis/Newcomer Emergent list.
* **Traits of Winning Founders:** Alex Heath looks for founders with the ability to recruit early, pattern-matching for an "overly audacious, kind of crazy, but it just may work" energy.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Byron Deeter:** Physical AI with robotics is one of the next horizons that will come forward in the coming years.
* **Alex Heath:** AI inference will be talked about in a few years in the same exponential way foundational models are today.
* **Ethan Barajas:** The market will experience a "very, very soft landing" with a quick rebound; major AI labs will make big announcements about data centers in space, with companies like Starcloud raising hundreds of millions or billions on billion-dollar valuations.
* **Nico Rosberg:** We will see who takes the lead in robotics over the next 12 months, though general intelligence for unprepared random tasks is still "miles away."
* **Aidan Guo:** Over the next 6 months, it is very likely that cell towers go down at some point for a few days.
* **Brendan Foody:** There will be more jobs in 10 years than there are today.
