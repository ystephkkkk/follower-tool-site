    * **Safety Concerns:** She argued that current military actions and the blockade are driving up domestic prices and making Americans less safe.

    * **Economic Alignment:** She justified her support by focusing on Platner's stance on the 2008 financial crisis, specifically his frustration that "not one banker went to jail."
