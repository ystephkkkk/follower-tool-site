## Nvidia GPUs in Orbit: Inside Starcloud’s $2B Factory

- Channel: Sourcery with Molly O'Shea

#### TL;DR
Starcloud is building AI data centers in space, having raised $450 million and gone from founding to orbit in 21 months with Starcloud 1. The company is preparing for its January launch of Starcloud 2 (featuring 100x the power) and developing Starcloud 3 to deploy up to 50 satellites per SpaceX Starship launch.

### Company Overview & Mission
* **Founding & Capital:** 
  * Total funding raised stands at **$450 million**, with the latest **$250 million round** led by Manhattan West, alongside participation from Nvidia, Cisco Investments, Benchmark, and EQT — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)
  * The team went from founding to orbit in **21 months**, setting a record previously held by a 4-year timeline — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)
* **Location Strategy:** 
  * Headquartered in Redmond, Washington, because 90% of all satellites launched by mass in recent years were designed, built, and tested there — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)
  * Team composition is split evenly between former SpaceX engineers and former hyperscaler engineers from AWS and Azure — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)

### Satellite Fleet & Technical Specifications
* **Starcloud 1:** 
  * Launched in November of the prior year carrying the first NVIDIA H100s in space, moving at roughly 17,000 miles per hour and circumnavigating the globe every hour and a half — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)
  * Currently equipped with 5 total chips: 3 from Nvidia, 2 from Arm, and some CPUs from AMD — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)
* **Starcloud 2:** 
  * Features a 120-foot wingspan deployment, dual top lasers, and has 100x the power of Starcloud 1 — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder), Molly O'Shea (Host)
  * Features the largest commercial deployable radiator in space, boasting at least 10 times less mass per watt of dissipation and 500 times less cost per watt than the ISS radiator — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)
  * Underwent a 3-month burn-in period running chips hot to weed out infancy-period failures before flight — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)
  * Utilizes fully fueled electric propulsion systems and Ion thrusters tested for leaks following vibration tests — Bailey Montaño (Engineering Manager)
* **Starcloud 3:** 
  * Designed as a 2-ton vehicle (fully loaded) measuring approximately 235 kW, built to fit through the payload dispenser door of a SpaceX Starship — Ezra Feilden (CTO & Co-Founder)
  * Capable of deploying up to **50 satellites per Starship launch**, stacking in a 2x2 array across 25 layers inside Starship — Ezra Feilden (CTO & Co-Founder)
  * Manufacturing footprint is scaling into an expanded ~100,000 sq ft facility designed to ultimately produce up to 100 units per week — Bailey Montaño (Engineering Manager), Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)

### Economics of Orbital vs. Terrestrial Data Centers
* **Cost Threshold:** 
  * Orbital data centers become economically superior to terrestrial ones when launch costs drop below approximately **$500 per kilogram** — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)
* **Terrestrial vs. Space Cost Drivers:** 
  * Space eliminates the need for expensive perimeter land and massive battery/backup power storage (due to 24/7 solar exposure) — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)
  * Solar efficiency is significantly higher in space, requiring roughly 8 times fewer solar cells per square meter — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)
* **Launch Options & Transportation:** 
  * Past missions utilized Falcon 9 rideshare (booked roughly 21 months in advance) — Ezra Feilden (CTO & Co-Founder)
  * Future capacity relies on SpaceX Starship, which targets internal launch costs of **$20 to $40 per kilogram** — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)
  * Additional payload reservations are booked with Firefly, Stoic, and other providers — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)

### Chip Reliability & Security
* **Radiation & Failure Prevention:** 
  * Chips undergo rigorous particle accelerator testing and radiation shielding to prevent failure rates that would otherwise break unit economics — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)
  * Critical systems incorporate mechanical redundancy, such as redundant pumps, and overprovisioned solar panels to account for a few percentage points of degradation per year — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)
* **Current Workloads & Edge Inference:** 
  * Satellites process models like Andrej Karpathy's NanoGPT and early Gemini versions, and execute inference on synthetic aperture radar and hyperspectral satellite imagery for government customers — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)
  * Edge processing reduces data-link bottlenecks from hours or days down to minutes, allowing rapid down-linking of specific target coordinates (e.g., vessel border crossings) — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)
* **National Security & Defense:** 
  * Satellites travel at 17,000 mph, making kinetic interception vastly more difficult than attacking a terrestrial data center in Virginia — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)
  * Constellations use advanced cryptography similar to Starlink, and objects larger than one cubic inch are actively tracked in space, meaning tampering would violate the Outer Space Treaty and be considered an act of war against the US — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* Starcloud 2 will launch in **January** with 100x the power of Starcloud 1 — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)
* SpaceX Starship will achieve upper-stage reusability (including capture of the upper stage) **within a year**, with commercial capacity potentially opening up to customers from **2028 onwards** — Philip Johnston (CEO & Co-Founder)
* Rocket Lab's Neutron will execute its first flight in **2027** — Ezra Feilden (CTO & Co-Founder)
