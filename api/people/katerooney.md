## ‘We Have To Do It’: Amazon CEO Andy Jassy On Racing China In The AI Buildout

- Channel: Semafor
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AcFE-uESAy8
- Published: 2026-11-18
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20261118.html#video-AcFE-uESAy8
- Speakers: Andy Jassy (CEO, Amazon); Kate Rooney (Technology Correspondent, CNBC)

---

**TL;DR**
Amazon CEO Andy Jassy defended the company's $220 billion CapEx plan by highlighting that AWS's generative AI revenue has surpassed $25 billion in its first three years—growing 440 times faster than early AWS—and argued that the U.S. cannot afford to slow down data center construction and cede AI leadership to China.

### AWS AI Growth, CapEx, and Demand Signals
* **Unprecedented AI Revenue Velocity:** In response to CNBC's Kate Rooney on Wall Street's reaction to Amazon's $220 billion CapEx plan, Jassy noted that AWS's generative AI business has reached over $25 billion in annual revenue within its first three years, compared to a $57 million annual revenue run rate in AWS's first three years (~440x faster).
* **Long-Term Capacity Reservations & Backlog:**
  * Big chunks of AWS capacity are already largely reserved for 2027, much of it for 2028, and a significant portion for 2029.
  * Amazon's backlog stands at ~$580 billion, with no single company representing even 25% of it.
* **Enterprise Adoption & Barbell Demand:**
  * Over 80% of the Fortune 100 are using Bedrock, Amazon's inference service.
  * Jassy described current demand as "barbelled": AI labs consuming massive compute (e.g., ChatGPT, coding) on one end, and enterprises using AI for cost avoidance and productivity (customer service, business process automation, fraud) on the other, with core enterprise production applications in the middle still ramping up.
* **Capital Cycle & Free Cash Flow:**
  * Data centers require capital outlays ~2 years ahead of monetization, whereas servers and networking gear (the largest capital expense) are purchased just a few months ahead, giving Amazon real visibility into demand before pulling the trigger.
  * While heavy CapEx creates short-term free cash flow headwinds, Jassy expects strong long-term return on invested capital.

### Data Center Buildout, Community Impact, and US-China Competition
* **Historic Infrastructure Scale:** Jassy compared the current AI infrastructure buildout to the 1950s U.S. highway and bridge construction, noting that the country and the world had underinvested in upgrading the power grid.
* **Geopolitical Imperative:** Addressing state-level moratoriums and public backlash, Jassy warned that stopping U.S. data center construction and allowing China to establish global AI norms would be a "historic mistake" that the U.S. would regret for a long time.
* **Community Partnerships & Tax Contributions:**
  * In St. Joseph County, Indiana, Amazon will contribute over $3 billion in taxes over the next few years, compared to ~$1 million from previous landowners.
  * Through its Build Together program, Amazon has contributed another $1 billion to local neighborhood priorities.
* **Hyperscaler Capacity:** Amazon added significant capacity in 2026 and has substantially larger capacity in progress for 2027 and 2028.

### Custom Silicon, Nvidia, and AI Model Partnerships
* **Custom Silicon Scale:** Amazon's chips business—comprising Trainium (AI accelerator) and Graviton (CPU)—has surpassed a $25 billion annual revenue run rate (as of Q2). The majority of Bedrock inference runs on Trainium chips.
* **Nvidia & "Coopetition":** Jassy downplayed any impact on Amazon from recent Nvidia news, calling Nvidia and Jensen Huang a long-term, important partner alongside Amazon's own custom silicon. On export controls, Jassy stated Amazon will abide by all U.S. laws.
* **Multi-Model Strategy (Anthropic & OpenAI):**
  * Anthropic, which is set to price and list on the Nasdaq, trains models and runs inference on Trainium. Jassy dismissed "circular financing" criticisms, noting that interconnected customer-vendor relationships (like Amazon using Adobe, which runs on AWS) are standard.
  * Driven by customer demand for choice, Amazon also partnered with OpenAI to offer its models on AWS.

### Agentic Shopping and Consumer/Enterprise Health
* **Third-Party Shopping Agents:** Jassy explained that Amazon has not permanently closed amazon.com to third-party agents (like Instinct or Dots), but requires a good customer experience and a fair exchange of value.
* **Alexa for Shopping:** Over 400 million people have used Alexa for shopping in the last 12 months, driving significant downstream revenue. Jassy believes most agentic shopping will happen natively within the retailer's own app, though Amazon is in talks with horizontal agent companies.
* **Resilient Consumer & Enterprise Spending:**
  * Consumer spending has been consistent over the last 18 months, with shoppers remaining price-conscious and deal-seeking ahead of events like Amazon's "Turkey 12."
  * On the enterprise side, Jassy has seen "no attenuation" in demand, with companies resuming cloud migrations over the past 18 months.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **AWS $1 Trillion Run Rate:** AWS can become an over $1 trillion annual revenue run rate business within the next 5 to 10 years (Andy Jassy).
* **Frontier Model Landscape:** In 3 to 5 years, there will be 6 to 8 frontier models that are roughly comparable in quality—each excelling at different tasks like coding, math, reasoning, visual arts, or lower cost/latency—and one of them will be Amazon's own model (Andy Jassy).
* **Cloud Migration Flip:** Today, 80% to 85% of global IT spend is still on-premises; that equation will flip to majority cloud over the next 10 to 20 years (Andy Jassy).

### Notable Quotes
> "I cannot fathom any scenario where the United States stops building data centers and allows another country like China to establish all the norms. Like, I think that would be a historic mistake by the United States that we would all regret for a very long time." — Andy Jassy

> "We felt like when we were building AWS the last number of years, we felt like AWS could be a few hundred billion dollar revenue run rate business. We now believe it can be over a trillion dollars in the next five to 10 years." — Andy Jassy

> "Customers want choice. They don't believe in one tool to rule the world... I think three to five years from now, there are going to be six to eight models that are about as good as each other, and they're all going to be a little bit better at something." — Andy Jassy

### TL;DR
Glean introduced new proactive AI capabilities, its enterprise AI platform features (including AI Gateway and Glean Tau), and advanced governance/threat detection tools to help enterprises move from simple question-answering to automated, multi-team workflows.

### Context Engine, AI Platform, Memory, and Model Choice
* **Retrieval and Enterprise Graph:** **Arvind Jain** explained that Glean's context engine combines advanced retrieval (surfacing current, high-quality information) with an enterprise graph that automatically maps people, customers, teams, projects, and relationships across structured and unstructured data.
* **AI Gateway:** **Arvind Jain** and **Allen** noted that the gateway makes disparate AI tools and front doors (like Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, and Cursor) think alike and work consistently while providing access to over 40 open and frontier models.
* **Memory Platform:** Glean automatically learns employee working preferences and agent improvement over time, making this memory portable and shareable across all enterprise AI tools via the AI Gateway.
* **Token Efficiency and Auto-Routing:** **Tony** and **Eddie** detailed Glean's auto-routing technology, which uses evaluation frameworks and specialist models (like Glean Waldo) to match tasks with the optimal model and reasoning level, saving 81% on token costs compared to Claude Cowork.
* **Glean Tau:** **Arvind Jain** and **Allen** introduced Glean Tau, a desktop app combining an open-source coding harness with Glean's context platform and AI gateway for code generation and coworking.

### Proactive AI, Independent Agents, and Teamchat
* **Proactive AI Features:** **Emrecan** announced proactive AI capabilities that prioritize/execute task management, run email triage, power meeting coaches, and provide interactive dashboards.
* **Multiplayer AI (Teamchat):** Enables teammates and Glean to collaborate inside a shared workspace based on shared context rather than single-player prompt loops.
* **Independent Agents:** Long-running agents with distinct identities, memories, and governance that operate autonomously across teams (e.g., automatically detecting and fixing API failures in Slack or handling customer support).
* **Glean Transform:** **Emrecan** presented this tool for mapping how work actually gets done, identifying transformation opportunities, building agent blueprints in one click, and measuring workflow improvements (such as a 46% increase in customer escalation speed).

### Security, Governance, and Threat Detection
* **AI Protect and Guardrails:** **Allen** and **Blair** discussed how Glean Protect handles sensitive content detection (at 95% accuracy), restricted topic policies, and agent governance policies at the individual agent level.
* **Context-Aware AI Threat Detection:** **Allen** introduced this new feature to spot high-risk agentic behavior and data exfiltration across sessions by utilizing enterprise context to distinguish legitimate activity from insider threats.

### Enterprise Transformations and Customer Case Studies
* **General Motors:** **Amar Maletira** and **Phil Lutka** shared that GM scaled Glean adoption from zero to 60,000 users in 90 days, maintaining an 80%+ 5D30 sustained usage metric, and deployed design release engineer (DRE) agents to help engineers design cars faster.
* **Snap:** **Evan Spiegel** noted that Snap uses Glean agents for quarterly performance reviews, IT self-service (Tech Lounge), and R&D tax credit documentation, saving thousands of hours and increasing automated advertising image reviews to nearly 90%.
* **Nvidia:** **Carrie** stated that 90% of Nvidia's 42,000 employees use Glean daily for IT search and run over 400 custom Slack agents backed by Glean's enterprise-ready platform and Nvidia NemoTron models.
* **Snowflake:** **Vivek** shared that Snowflake improved internal engineering NPS by a 16x swing in 18 months by treating engineers like customers and letting agentic chaos reign before reining it in.
* **DaVita:** **Madhusudan Narasimhan** and **Aaron Groh** reported that 40,000 out of 70,000 clinic and office employees actively use Glean, generating 5.4 million queries and saving approximately 19–20 minutes per person to refocus on patient care.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Year of Open Source:** "2026 is the year of open source in AI... open-source harnesses often beat close ones in benchmarks, and they're allowing us to create amazing AI products very quickly." — Arvind Jain
