## Democratizing Security for the Agentic Web with Cloudflare CTO Dane Knecht

- Channel: No Priors: AI, Machine Learning, Tech, & Startups

- Published: 2026-10-23

### TL;DR
As the agentic web drives an exponential surge in AI-powered security vulnerabilities and shifts the attacker landscape toward automated high school actors, Cloudflare CTO Dane Knecht predicts that engineering teams will spend the next 18 months heavily prioritizing security and zero-trust infrastructure to patch 70 years of legacy software.

### AI Security Landscape and the Evolving Threat Vector
* **Explosion of Vulnerabilities:** Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures (CVEs) and Log4J-scale incidents are shifting from annual occurrences to weekly events, driven by AI models and harnesses that do not sleep.
* **Democratization of Offense:** **Dane Knecht** noted that recent high-profile vulnerabilities are not just the work of nation-state actors, but increasingly of high school students deploying agents in capture-the-flag contests and leveraging bug bounties.
* **The "Messy Middle" of the Web:** **Dane Knecht** described the current internet as a messy middle where humans and agents ride the same rails, forcing websites into inefficient scraping and parsing cycles because agents have a thirst to explore the human-designed web.

### Shifting Engineering Resources and Zero-Trust Infrastructure
* **Resource Realignment:** **Dane Knecht** expects that over the next 18 months, the majority of engineering team time will be dedicated to security, with some team keynotes indicating over 50% of engineering time going toward securing the internet during this transition.
* **Zero-Trust Advantage:** Organizations that transitioned away from castle-and-moat security toward Zero Trust over the past 5 to 10 years are in a significantly better position to secure devices and traffic flows.
* **Forcing Automation Through Crisis:** **Dane Knecht** explained that Cloudflare plans to scale its internal red teams from one to four or five by the end of next year, forcing engineering teams to automate away vulnerabilities by pushing them out of crisis.

### AI-Driven Workflows, Cloudflare OS, and Internal Experimentation
* **Cloudflare OS:** **Dane Knecht** detailed Cloudflare's internal platform, which allows employees—particularly go-to-market and G&A teams—to safely deploy AI tools, build internal applications, and maintain data governance.
* **Bottom-Up Experimentation:** Instead of hiring consulting firms to catalog workflows, Cloudflare fostered a bottom-up experimentation culture, utilizing intern energy and high-agency employees (including an early `agents@cloudflare.com` initiative powered by interns) to build hundreds of internal productivity tools.
* **Intelligent Tooling & Hiring:** **Dane Knecht** emphasized that hiring at Cloudflare heavily prioritizes curiosity, drive, and empathy, noting that a lack of personal AI use at home is a major red flag.

### The Agentic Web and New Economic Models
* **Cloudflare Monetization Gateway:** **Dane Knecht** highlighted Cloudflare's new gateway designed to facilitate microtransactions for content creators, providing wallets for agents and stablecoin rails to handle millions of transactions per second.
* **Project Signals:** **Dane Knecht** mentioned a collaboration with OpenAI called Project Signals, which allows agents to proactively discover new content rather than repeatedly crawling and paying for infrastructure across the entire internet.
* **Reviving HTTP 402:** **Dane Knecht** expressed excitement that agents are finally pushing the internet toward solving the 30-year-old HTTP 402 payment protocol challenge, creating a new economic model that can increase the number of content creators.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Dane Knecht** predicts that over the next 18 months, the majority of a team's time will be spent on security, after which software productivity will rise significantly as resources return to standard development.
* **Greg Brockman (cited by Sarah Guo)** predicted that by 2027, people will spend as much money on tokens for security as they do for engineering.
* **Dane Knecht** predicts a world in 18 months where new software productivity increases dramatically once security resources have surveyed and patched legacy bugs.
* **Dane Knecht** claims that over time, cyber security models and tooling *all have to be open* because malicious actors will gain access to them regardless, and closing them excludes the mass population that needs defense.
