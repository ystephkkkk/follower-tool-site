### TL;DR
Tuhin Srivastava, CEO and co-founder of AI cloud startup Baseten, discusses the explosive demand for AI inference, the shift toward enterprise-owned intelligence, and how autonomous agents running in virtual machines are reshaping the software stack and infrastructure markets.

### AI Inference and the Baseten Architecture
* **Definition of AI Inference:** Serving post-trained, open-weight, or custom models in production, requiring complex underlying infrastructure, orchestration, and chip-level optimization.
* **The Three-Layer Problem:** 
  * **Infrastructure/Compute:** Procuring chips (like B200s and G300s) across a heterogeneous network of 20 different clouds and 90 regions.
  * **Core Inference Layer:** Providing performance, reliability, numerics, and software tooling behind an API.
  * **Adjacent Primitives:** Incorporating post-training (RL), sandboxes for code execution, evals, and routing.
* **Differentiation from Hyperscalers:** Baseten focuses specifically on inference and production reliability, moving faster and offering more customer alignment than traditional cloud providers (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud).
* **Current Scale:** 
  * Processing **40 to 50 trillion tokens a day**.
  * Token volume has grown **40x year-on-year**.
  * Revenue has **10xed in the last 12 months** with thousands of customers.
  * Valued at **$5 billion in January 2026** and **$13 billion in June**.

### The Rise of "Owned Intelligence" and Open Weight Models
* **Concept of Owned Intelligence:** Enterprises want to control their own models, data sovereignty, and user feedback loops to protect margins and avoid reliance on closed frontier providers.
* **Market Shift in Open Source:** 
  * 18 months ago (around Deepseek V3's release in December 2024), enterprises were skeptical and fearful of open weights.
  * Today, capability gains have driven widespread enterprise adoption, and the origin debate has largely evaporated as open weight usage surges.
* **Base Labs:** Baseten's internal research lab, established via a team acquisition, dedicated to pushing the open weight ecosystem and continual learning loops.

### Vertical Integration and Market Growth
* **Driving Factors for Going Vertical:** Moving up and down the stack is driven by the need to deepen customer value (learning loops) and secure raw compute capacity for future scale.
* **Future Compute Demand:** Srivastava projects Baseten will be one of the largest individual compute consumers on the planet within 12 to 24 months.
* **The Agentic Shift & Blacksel Acquisition:** 
  * Baseten acquired Blacksel to integrate agent runtimes, virtual machine sandboxes, and browser-use environments directly with inference layers.
  * Agents (such as coding agents utilizing Baseten's MCP server and skills) are increasingly deploying and debugging models autonomously on the platform.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Global AI Spend:** Projected by industry figures to reach **$5 trillion** in 5 years, generating roughly **$1 to $5 trillion** in inference spend (Tuhin Srivastava).
* **American Open Weight Models:** Truly competitive American open weight models will emerge in **weeks, not months or quarters** (Tuhin Srivastava).
* **Compute Volume Growth:** Inference demand will scale **100x or 1,000x** as consumer adoption of agents catches up to enterprise usage (Tuhin Srivastava).
* **Future of AI Markets:** Post-AGI (or in a high-AI world), inference will become the primary or **only market left** because all models will run continuously in execution loops (Tuhin Srivastava).
