### TL;DR
Datasite global CTO Raj Bakhru and Reducto CEO Adit Abraham discuss scaling AI across virtual data rooms handling 60% of $100M+ deals, explaining why outsourcing document parsing to Reducto allowed Datasite's 250 engineers to focus on high-value GraphRAG and agentic products.

### Background & Scale of Datasite
- **Company Leadership:** Raj Bakhru serves as Global CTO at Datasite; Adit Abraham is Co-founder and CEO at Reducto.
- **Transaction Volume:** Datasite handles approximately 60% of all global corporate transactions over $100 million.
- **Platform Scale:** 
  - Over 500,000 monthly active users accessing content.
  - Generates a trillion-plus tokens per year.
  - Processes 55,000 virtual data rooms (VDRs) a year.
  - Experienced 40% top-line growth over the 18 months following the BlueFlame acquisition.

### The BlueFlame Acquisition Journey
- **Founding & Bootstrap:** Raj Bakhru co-founded the vertical AI M&A startup BlueFlame after running M&A/strategy for a security firm he previously sold. BlueFlame bootstrapped initially to save costs.
- **The Acquisition:** Approached Datasite for a $5 million investment round during VC fundraising, which resulted in Datasite acquiring the 50-person company instead over a dinner meeting.
- **Competitive Pressure:** BlueFlame realized that competing against venture-backed vertical AI rivals (who raised hundreds of millions) and well-funded frontier model labs would require massive dilution, prompting the acquisition.
- **Proprietary Data Advantage:** The acquisition combined BlueFlame with Datasite's VDR ecosystem and Grata (a private company database with 20 million companies), creating an isolated, highly differentiated proprietary data moat.

### Parsing Challenges & The Build vs. Buy Strategy
- **The Endless Edge Cases:** Scrapily built ingestion pipelines failed on complex financial documents. Edge cases included:
  - Pie charts failing because a legend's shade of blue didn't correlate perfectly with the LLM's interpretation.
  - Complex Excel spreadsheets featuring merged cells, XLSB macro-enabled files, and templates.
- **Engineering Allocation:** Datasite employs 250 engineers divided across 5 major revenue-generating initiatives. 
- **Outsourcing Logic:** Bakhru determined that supporting document extraction mechanics was a solved problem not worth consuming valuable engineering bandwidth, leading Datasite to partner with Reducto.

### Architecture & Evaluation (VDR Bench)
- **Data Ingestion Pipeline:** Large bursts of transaction data (up to a terabyte dropped instantly by bankers) pass through Reducto within Datasite's secure environment.
- **Vector Storage & GraphRAG:** Processed data feeds into Turbo Buffer as the vector database, powering GraphRAG queries executed via Python code.
- **VDR Bench:** Datasite's internal evaluation benchmark consisting of hundreds of documents and hundreds of questions with known answers.
- **Evaluation Results:** Upgrading parsing with Reducto improved VDR Bench recall by **22%**, outperforming direct off-the-shelf frontier lab tools (such as Claude Projects or OpenAI Projects).
