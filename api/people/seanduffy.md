### TL;DR
Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy briefed Senate Republicans on the Trump administration's updated $50.1 billion emergency request to modernize FAA air traffic control systems following a nationwide ground stop of over 9,000 flights, amid a broader wave of tech outages at the NSA, major banks, and Apple. Meanwhile, President Donald Trump opened his Asia trip in Manila ahead of a trilateral meeting with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin, while Senate committees held confirmation hearings for Navy Secretary nominee Hung Cao and two Federal Election Commission nominees.

### Aviation & Tech Outages: $50.1B FAA Request
* **Updated Funding Request:** The Trump administration formally updated its request to lawmakers from $30.1 billion to **$50.1 billion** to modernize air traffic control (ATC) and improve airports, less than 24 hours after the initial ask.
* **Duffy's Capitol Hill Briefing:** Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy, alongside FAA Administrator Brian Bedford, briefed Senate Republicans in a closed-door lunch.
  * Duffy stated the root cause of Friday's nationwide ground stop—which grounded **over 9,000 flights**—is not yet known, though a faulty router monitored by **L3 Harris** was isolated.
  * Duffy defended shutting down the airspace, referencing the January 2025 collision of **American Airlines Flight 5342** with an Army helicopter near Reagan National Airport.
* **Congressional Pushback & Legislation:**
  * **Senate Majority Leader John Thune (R-SD):** Noted GOP concerns over the price tag and lack of offsets, pointing out Congress already provided **$12.5 billion** last year in the working families tax cuts legislation. Thune emphasized the Senate's immediate priority is confirming conservative judges and executive nominees.
  * **Rep. Suhas Subramanyam (D-VA):** Criticized Duffy on the House floor, noting the FAA has spent only **$1 billion** of the **$12.5 billion** package (which included **$5 billion** for FAA telecom modernization). He called Friday's outage a configuration issue and demanded a moratorium on non-vital network maintenance through **2026**.
  * **Sen. Ted Cruz (R-TX):** Announced a bipartisan House-Senate agreement in principle to mandate **ADS-B In** ("ED SB in") cockpit alert technology in crowded airspace, a feature recommended by the NTSB since **2008**.
* **Broader Tech & Cyber Outages:**
  * Outages hit the **Apple Store** and **Apple Music**, a highly classified **National Security Agency (NSA)** internal network, **Chase**, **Microsoft**, and **United Airlines**.
  * **DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin:** Ruled out cyberattacks on CNBC, blaming AI and new software pushed onto outdated systems; he urged companies to schedule upgrades between **1:00 AM and 4:00 AM**.
  * **Rep. Delia Ramirez (D-IL):** Blamed Trump administration cuts at **CISA**, noting a separate cyberattack targeted payment data across **180 websites**.
* **Market Impact:** Wall Street fell on the day, with the **Dow down 848**, **NASDAQ down 181**, and **S&P 500 down 76**.

### President Trump's Asia Trip & Global Affairs
* **Manila & Quad Summit:** President Donald Trump landed in Manila for the ASEAN summit—arriving 30 minutes before it ended, according to critics—and posed with Quad leaders from **Japan, Australia, and India**.
* **Upcoming China Trilateral:** Trump heads to China for the APEC forum and an unprecedented trilateral meeting with Chinese President **Xi Jinping** and Russian President **Vladimir Putin** to discuss nuclear disarmament and the Iran war (which Trump claimed is costing **25,000 lives a month**, including **32,000** last month).
* **Thailand Trade Deal:** Trump signed a joint agreement with Thailand's Prime Minister to eliminate all tariffs on U.S. exports, remove non-tariff barriers on vehicles, medical devices, pharmaceuticals, ethanol, and poultry, and purchase an estimated **$2.6 billion** in additional U.S. agricultural products.
* **Nvidia CEO News:** Former Nvidia CEO **Jensen Huang** posted on X that he would be honored to return if the Board asks. Trump praised Nvidia's new National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee.
* **Democratic Criticism:** Senate Minority Leader **Chuck Schumer (D-NY)** slammed the trip as "adversaries over allies," urging Trump to enforce AI chip restrictions with ASEAN partners rather than catering to Xi and Putin.
* **UK-EU Summit Preview:** British Prime Minister **Andy Burnham** argued at the Lord Mayor's banquet that the UK has lost more than it gained from Brexit—citing an OBR estimate of a **4%** hit to the economy (and others up to **8% of GDP**)—ahead of Friday's UK-EU summit in Brussels on food, energy, and youth mobility deals that could add **0.4% to GDP**.

### Confirmation Hearings, Child Safety & Local Policing
* **Navy Secretary Confirmation:** Acting Navy Secretary **Hung Cao** (who requires a congressional waiver because he left active duty less than **7 years** ago) was grilled by Sen. **Mark Kelly (D-AZ)** over poor warship conditions.
  * Kelly cited reports of a sailor losing **45+ pounds in 6 months** aboard the *USS George Washington* and *Boxer* Amphibious Ready Group, and families shipping food care packages.
  * Cao noted the destroyer *USS Gonzalez* is down to **1 week** of rations (below his preferred 2-week minimum) but insisted supplies are adequate, comparing complaints to kids looking in a refrigerator.
  * Cao also defended not publicly announcing the firings of commanders, after the Navy failed to inform Congress it fired the leadership of a nuclear attack submarine and a Middle East destroyer.
* **FEC Quorum Hearing:** The Senate Rules Committee (Chair **Bill Hagerty, R-TN**; Ranking Member **Alex Padilla, D-CA**) questioned Trump nominees **Ashley Stoe** and **Andrew Woodson**.

  * Both nominees said Congress must amend FICA to regulate AI in campaigns beyond the FEC's September 2024 fraudulent misrepresentation rule, and declined to comment on pending complaints regarding Trump's **MAGA Inc.** hybrid PAC.
* **Roblox Child Safety Hearing:** A Senate Judiciary Subcommittee heard testimony from survivor **"Woody Doe"** on a bipartisan bill by Sens. **Josh Hawley** and **Dick Durbin** banning forced arbitration for child sexual exploitation victims.

  * *Ro Monitor Stats* cited **138 million to 144 million** daily active users on Roblox (**2.6M–2.7M** concurrent players), split roughly into thirds: under 13, 13–17, and over 18.
* **NYC Policing:** Former NYC Mayor **Eric Adams (D)** criticized current Mayor **Zohran Mamdani (D)** for disbanding the NYPD's Strategic Response Group (SRG). Mamdani defended replacing it with a First Amendment support team within **90 days** and a civilian-led Community Dialogue and De-escalation division launching in **2028**.
