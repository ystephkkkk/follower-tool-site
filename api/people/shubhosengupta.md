### TL;DR
While the initial phase of enterprise AI focused on proof-of-concept demos, 2026 demands reliable production deployment, shifting the focus toward robust governance, traceability, deterministic guardrails, and managing enterprise token costs.

### POC-to-Production Pitfalls & Governance
*   **Auditability & Trust:** 
    *   **Financial & Regulatory Risk:** High-stress environments like financial services face regulatory scrutiny, requiring agents to be easily auditable and deterministic where possible.
    *   **Autonomous Actions:** As agents move from recommendations to taking direct actions (e.g., healing broken enterprise systems), security teams demand proof that actions won't crash systems.
*   **Traceability Challenges:** Unlike traditional software that fails loudly with a 500 error code, agents fail quietly with incorrect outcomes (akin to a 200 error code), making end-to-end traceability of agentic traffic mandatory.
*   **Accountability Dilemma:** Enterprises are stuck on edge cases because CIOs and CTOs remain unclear on who is legally and operationally accountable when an agent (e.g., granting a loan outside risk profiles) makes a core business error.

### Technical Architecture & Data Layers
*   **Deterministic Layers:** 
    *   **Code and Math:** Shubho Sengupta and Anish Agarwal emphasize that the safest way to ground probabilistic LLMs is to have them output deterministic intermediate steps like code or math before interacting with production systems.
    *   **Multimodal Complexity:** Soyoung Lee notes that video data is inherently undeterministic (combining sound, visuals, time, and narrative), requiring automated compliance and technical defect-checking before human review.
*   **Defensible Moats Beyond the Harness:** 
    *   **Proprietary Data Infrastructure:** Anish Agarwal warns that companies building solely at the harness/API level risk having their work absorbed by frontier model weights. Lasting moats require rebuilding underlying data structures, indexing, and caching to support parallel agent queries.
    *   **Formalized Knowledge:** Axiom Math focuses on building massive indexes of formalized mathematical knowledge to serve as an uncopyable structural foundation.

### Human-in-the-Loop & Organizational Roles
*   **Two-Lens Approach to Automation:** Eleonore Crespo advises dividing workflows into low-risk tasks (where human-in-the-loop can be entirely removed and audited via logs) versus strategic tasks (e.g., hiring plans, budget allocations) that require human checkpoints and context.
*   **Human Bottlenecks and Fatigue:** Carl Mattsson warns that relying on humans to manually review 100% of agent actions fails because human monitors get distracted or bored; systems must intelligently flag only the crucial 1% to 5% of anomalies.
*   **Organizational Shift:** While short-term transformation roles are emerging to accelerate AI adoption, panelists predict these roles will disappear within 3 to 5 years as end users and business functions naturally become "agent managers."

### Pricing, ROI, and Token Management
*   **The ROI Reality Check:** Panelists highlight massive enterprise spend juxtaposed against slow bottom-line (EBITDA) returns, citing examples like Uber burning $3.4 billion on AI spend in a single quarter without clear value capture.
*   **Evolving Pricing Models:**
    *   **Outcome & Consumption Splits:** Eleonore Crespo notes that Pigment prices based on productivity gains and decision quality, while offering a free token-management app paired with Fireworks AI for open-weight model flexibility.
    *   **Predictable and Seat-Based Options:** Soyoung Lee predicts a movement toward fixed, baseline pricing for standard predictable outcomes, with consumption added for extra work.
    *   **AWS-Style Commitments:** Carl Mattsson predicts enterprise pricing will consolidate around large upfront utility commitments drawn down across multiple use cases.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
