### TL;DR
OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and Oracle co-CEO Clay Magouyrk discussed their expanded collaboration at Oracle AI World, highlighting record enterprise adoption of GPT-6, the rise of AI-driven software creation via Codex, and the critical role of AI in discovering both software vulnerabilities and scientific breakthroughs.

### AI Adoption & Enterprise Impact
* **Record Growth of GPT-6:** Launched in August, GPT-6 has driven OpenAI's strongest adoption by far across coding, enterprise software, security, and science.
* **Model Capabilities:** Sam Altman noted that while models four years ago struggled with grade school math, current models are tackling the hardest unproven math problems and discovering new knowledge.
* **Enterprise Rollouts:** Clay Magouyrk stated that Oracle rolled out ChatGPT desktop to all employees, noting that more than 70% of Oracle's software code is now written by AI using tools like Codex.
* **Workplace Productivity:** Altman shared user feedback of individuals feeling twice as productive at the end of 2026 compared to early 2025, while enjoying their jobs more by eliminating repetitive tasks ("the schlep").

### Democratization of Software Development
* **Unlocking Creativity:** Magouyrk and Altman agreed that AI automates the manual, instructional aspects of programming, allowing non-developers to build complete applications and websites from scratch.
* **App Composer Deployment:** Oracle built "app composer" to provide a secure and controlled way to deploy the surge of new applications created by non-technical employees.
* **Factory-Like Management:** Altman described his personal user experience with the latest tools as feeling like "managing a factory inside of my computer," lowering the activation energy to explore unlikely and ambitious creative directions.

### Cybersecurity & Threat Mitigation
* **AI Security Mesh:** Magouyrk highlighted that Oracle and OpenAI partnered to create the AI security mesh to help customers access models, point them at targets, validate issues, and deploy fixes.
* **Vulnerability Discovery:** Altman advised companies that their top short-term priority should be understanding and mitigating the AI security threat landscape.
* **Historical Debt vs. New Bugs:** Magouyrk pushed back on the idea that AI creates bugs, asserting instead that AI is helping discover security issues developed over the past 50 years.
* **Scaling Challenges:** Altman expressed shock at the volume of vulnerabilities discovered by AI systems, warning that a Herculean effort—including automated continuous monitoring services rather than one-by-one patching—will be required to get ahead of them.

### Innovation, Science & Hardware
* **Accelerating Scientific Discovery:** Altman emphasized that AI is increasingly being used to discover new science, invent new technology, and accelerate innovation.
* **AI Chip Design:** Altman revealed that OpenAI designed a new AI chip for inference named **jalapeno**, which is deployed in Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) and was built much faster using AI assistance.
* **Future Outlook:** Both leaders emphasized that AI is rapidly transitioning from a chatbot to coding agents and direct innovation engines capable of curing diseases and producing higher-quality, lower-cost services.
