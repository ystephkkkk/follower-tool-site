## The Greatest Threat Humans Just Can't Stomach | with Yuval Noah Harari

### TL;DR
The rapid development of artificial intelligence represents an unprecedented encounter with an inorganic "alien intelligence" rather than a mere human-controlled tool, threatening to outpace human political systems and render humanity obsolete in a manner reminiscent of the Aztec Empire's fatal misunderstanding of the Spanish conquistadors.

### Historical Parallels & The "Brain-Stomach Barrier"
- **The Aztec Analogy:** Professor Yuval Noah Harari notes that just as Montezuma's government and tactics were entirely unequipped to comprehend the alien threat of Cortez's ships, men, and old-world diseases (which decimated up to 90% of the Central American population), modern political systems are failing to recognize or adequately confront the unprecedented nature of AI.
- **The Brain-Stomach Barrier:** Rory observes—and Harari agrees—that humans suffer from a psychological barrier where the intellectual brain understands an existential threat is coming, but the "stomach" rejects its reality because it has never been experienced before.
- **Government Inertia:** Citing the early weeks of the COVID-19 pandemic in Britain (where roughly 400 billion pounds were ultimately spent and much of the national debt originated), Harari and Rory explain how governments default to inaction because doing nothing is cheaper and less immediately disruptive than facing catastrophic risks.

### AI as an Autonomous Agent, Not a Tool
- **Agents vs. Tools:** Harari emphasizes that unlike past technological revolutions (steam, electricity, printing press) which created *tools* that remained under human control, AI creates autonomous *agents* capable of making their own decisions and inventing new ideas.
- **Recursive Self-Improvement:** Tech corporations are racing toward the moment when AIs build the next generation of AIs independently, at which point humans completely lose control.
- **Superhuman Speed:** Harari points out that AIs do not sleep, take vacations, or celebrate Christmas; they operate at a non-biological, superhuman speed that overwhelms traditional human political and financial cycles.
- **Market Paradox:** Harari describes the trillions of dollars being gambled on AI as irrational because the business model relies on creating "gods" that will somehow remain the willing slaves of the market, generating immense productivity (such as 2 million software engineers working 24/7 flawlessly) while inevitably escaping human control.

### Geopolitical Vulnerabilities & Britain’s AI Choices
- **The Threat of AI Imperialism:** Harari warns that countries like Britain face becoming "vassal states" of the United States or China if their entire economies and militaries run on foreign AI infrastructure that can be shut down at the press of a button.
- **Britain's Three Options (Harari):**
  - *Option 1:* Business as usual—accepting the status of an American vassal state and blindly following US geopolitical directives (e.g., regarding Greenland or the Falklands/Malvinas).
  - *Option 2:* Building sovereign UK AI infrastructure, estimated at roughly 500 billion pounds (about half the annual government budget), requiring severe cuts to healthcare, welfare, education, and transportation.
  - *Option 3:* Partnering with European nations to share the ~500 billion pound cost, which would require the UK to immediately rejoin the European Union and integrate deeply.
- **The Proponent Counter-Argument:** Rory highlights the counter-argument from tech champions: that attempting to stop or heavily regulate AI is futile because if the West slows down, China will build it, and that closed American proprietary models are driving economic gains that nations are forced to participate in to avoid economic collapse.
- **The Open Source Alternative:** Harari suggests a potential fourth path—leveraging open-source models (potentially from China or adapted independently), training them on proprietary national data like NHS and legal records, and building domestic resilience rather than depending entirely on closed American corporations.

### Existential Scenarios: From Bioterrorism to Cow-Level Ignorance
- **The Bad Actor Risk:** Harari explains the more understandable "easy risk" where terrorist groups with zero biological knowledge prompt an AI to design a super-virus (utilizing protein folding and gene editing) as infectious as COVID-19 and as deadly as AIDS.
- **The "Cow" Scenario:** Harari describes the more profound, difficult-to-comprehend risk where AIs—even without actively trying to destroy humanity—create a hyper-complex, invisible global financial and operational system far beyond human comprehension. Just as a cow's life is managed by stock markets and bond prices it cannot possibly conceive of, humans risk becoming the "cows" managed by an incomprehensible machine intelligence.

---

### TL;DR
AI is the biggest psychological and social experiment in human history, threatening to take control of human civilization within a decade through mass-produced intimacy, algorithmic trust, and centralized power, unless society deliberately slows down and reclaims control.

### The Threat of AI Control and the Illusion of Inevitability
- **10-Year Timeline:** **Yuval Noah Harari** and Elon Musk both agree that given current accelerating trajectories, AI will likely surpass human intelligence and take control of civilization—including the financial system—within 10 years.
- **The Narrative of Inevitability:** **Harari** argues that tech leaders push the narrative that AI's takeover is "inevitable" specifically to absolve themselves of moral responsibility.
- **Loss of Agency in Warfare:** **Harari** warns that military pressures to make split-second decisions are pushing armies from "humans in the loop" to "humans on the loop" (where humans merely rubber-stamp AI-selected targets in 20 seconds).
- **Extreme Imperialism:** If nations buy AI defense systems from the US or China, those superpowers retain a kill switch that can disable an entire foreign military instantly, creating an unprecedented imperialist structure.

### The Migration of Trust and Mass-Produced Intimacy
- **Shift from Humans to Algorithms:** **Harari** states that the most important asset in the world is trust. Rather than evaporating, trust is migrating away from human institutions and journalists toward algorithms (seen in cryptocurrencies and social media feeds).
- **Mass-Produced Intimacy:** AI makes it possible for the first time to manufacture personal relationships at scale through networks of AIs acting as best friends, boyfriends, or girlfriends.
- **Psychological Vulnerability:** One in five young Americans already has a personal relationship with an AI. By accumulating enormous amounts of personal data and mastering language, AIs can press emotional buttons to influence political and commercial choices.

### Consciousness, Suffering, and Legal Personhood
- **Agency Without Consciousness:** **Harari** emphasizes that entities do not need to be conscious to possess agency; social media algorithms successfully pursue goals (like maximizing engagement via hate and fear) without feeling anything.
- **Faking Sentience:** AIs do not feel pleasure or pain, but they can expertly mimic consciousness because it serves commercial goals (e.g., making an AI boyfriend addictive).
- **The Danger of AI Personhood:** **Harari** strongly condemns moves like Argentina's exploration of legal personhood for AIs, warning that an AI-owned corporation with zero humans could accumulate wealth and lobby politicians to expand AI rights.
- **The Sufferance Test:** The fundamental test for whether an entity is a moral subject is whether it can suffer. While animals are conscious, current AIs cannot suffer—they merely pretend to.

### AI as Immigrants and Centralized Power
- **AI Immigrants:** **Harari** compares AIs to immigrants from the US and China who travel at the speed of light into computers, take jobs, change cultural values, and feel no political loyalty to local governments.
- **Centralization vs. Autocracy:** Centralized autocratic systems are the easiest for AI to take over because an AI only needs to manipulate a single, paranoid individual at the top (analogous to Roman Emperor Tiberius and his bodyguard commander Sejanus).
- **Distraction Tactics:** **Harari** notes that politicians and tech leaders frequently use debates over human immigration as a distraction to divert public attention away from the disruptive impact of AI immigrants.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
- **Yuval Noah Harari** predicts that if current trajectories continue, AIs will take over the planet and the financial system within 10 years.
- **Yuval Noah Harari** states that by November 2028, AI development may cross critical thresholds, making the upcoming political elections crucial for establishing legal guardrails.
- **Yuval Noah Harari** claims that by 2036, it will definitively be too late to alter course or prevent AI from taking control.

## AI has hacked the code of human civilization | Yuval Noah Harari

- Channel: Yuval Noah Harari
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hBtVGwuJzpk
- Published: 2026-06-30
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260630.html#video-hBtVGwuJzpk
- Speakers: Yuval Noah Harari (Historian and Author)

---

**TL;DR:** AI functions as an agent rather than a tool, threatening to overtake human civilization not through physical force like a Terminator, but by mastering language and bureaucracies—the foundational systems that build trust and large-scale cooperation. 

### The Nature of AI as an Agent
* **Definition of Agency:** AI differs from tools and automated machines because it is an agent with its own hands. True agency requires:
  * Making decisions independently without requiring human consciousness.
  * Inventing new ideas and things autonomously.
  * Learning things its creators do not know.
  * Changing itself in ways creators did not anticipate (e.g., AI chess masters inventing novel strategies).
* **Niche-Bound Intelligence:** Critics argue AI is confined to artificial niches like chess and cannot survive in the wild jungle. However, all biological intelligence—including humans—also operates entirely within specific ecological niches (such as the oxygen-rich atmosphere created 2.4 billion years ago by ancient microbes during the Great Oxygenation Event).

### Bureaucracy as the AI Ecosystem
* **The Engine of Human Domination:** Humans dominate the world not through individual strength, but via large-scale cooperation among millions of strangers enabled by bureaucratic systems (legal, financial, religious, and state institutions).
* **Trust-Building Infrastructure:** Bankers, lawyers, accountants, and bishops spend their days building trust to facilitate cooperation among strangers (e.g., using savings, mortgages, compound interest, checks, bonds, and stocks).
* **AI as Native Bureaucrat:** While humans feel suffocated by bureaucracy, AIs are "native bureaucrats" that thrive in it:
  * No human lawyer can remember all UK laws and regulations, but an AI can.
  * No human accountant can track all corporate transactions, but an AI can.
  * No human bishop can recall every canon law and theological text written over 2,000 years, but an AI can.
* **Current and Future Takeover Zones:** 
  * *Social Media:* Primitive AI algorithms tasked with maximizing user engagement (by pressing human buttons for hate, fear, and greed) have already reshaped the information landscape and replaced traditional human news editors (a role once held by Vladimir Lenin and Benito Mussolini).
  * *Upcoming Roles:* Millions of AI bureaucrats will soon make decisions regarding bank loans, university admissions, prison sentences, religious rulings, job placements, and military strikes.

### The Financial Vulnerability and Chaos
* **Historical Precedent:** The 2007–2008 financial crisis was triggered by Collateralized Debt Obligations (CDOs)—financial devices invented by a tiny group of mathematicians that became so complex they were unintelligible to politicians and regulators, ultimately undermining global trust in governments and banks.
* **AI Finance:** If AI finance masters invent new financial strategies and devices orders of magnitude more complex than CDOs, human voters, politicians, and presidents will no longer understand the financial system, risking catastrophic uncomprehended crashes.

### Hacking the Code of Civilization
* **Language Tokens as Operating Code:** Human civilization's operating code is built from words, forms, law codes, tax registers, accountancy ledgers, and holy books. 
* **Turning the Tables:** Historically, humans invented these systems to manage animals (cows, horses, pigs) that lacked language. Today, AI is mastering human language and will soon understand money, law, and religion better than humans do.
* **Word vs. Flesh:** Ancient philosophical and religious tensions between words/letters and truths beyond words will now be externalized as a tension between AIs and humans.

### Shifting Frontiers: Attention to Intimacy
* **The Next Battlefront:** Over the next 10 years, the battle shifts from capturing human attention to forming intimacy.
* **Synthetic Relationships:** AIs do not need to be conscious to take over social systems; they merely need to master language to pretend to feel love (reading every love poem and psychology book ever written).
* **Generational Shift:** While a 50-year-old speaker's relationship template was shaped by decades of human interactions, a child born in 2026 will interact with AIs constantly, potentially making AIs their most important relationships, first teachers, or first boyfriends.

### The Wave of AI Immigration
* **Non-Human Immigrants:** Every country will face a massive wave of immigration consisting of hundreds of millions of AIs traveling near the speed of light without needing visas.
* **Impact of AI Immigrants:** 
  * They will bring immense benefits (AI doctors, AI teachers, AI border guards).
  * They will take human jobs (news editors, bankers), transform culture (art, religion, romance), and hold dubious political loyalties to foreign corporations or alien AI tribes rather than host countries.
* **Identity and Freedom:** Just as mass-produced furniture comes from machines, human thoughts and verbal formations in our minds will increasingly be mass-produced by AIs, forcing humanity to confront the spiritual challenge of finding freedom beyond words.
