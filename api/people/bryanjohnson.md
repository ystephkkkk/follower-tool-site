**TL;DR**
Bryan Johnson explores 5-MeO-DMT not as a recreational psychedelic, but as a "metabolic reset" for the brain, arguing that "annihilating" the Default Mode Network (DMN) can restore a child-like psychological state and drive neuroplasticity essential for longevity.

### 5-MeO-DMT: The "Hero Dose" Protocol
*   **The Dosage:** Johnson underwent a dual-delivery protocol consisting of **9mg intramuscularly** and **18mg vaporized**. It is estimated to be 5-10 times more powerful than standard DMT.
*   **Subjective Experience:** Described as "raw consciousness" and "raw intelligence." Unlike the visual hallucinations of DMT, 5-MeO is a visceral, non-visual experience of "unimaginable bliss" and the total release of ego, control, and attachment.
*   **The "Metabolic Reset":** 
    *   Following a previous psilocybin trial, Johnson saw his blood glucose move from the 99.5th percentile to the **99.9th percentile** of the population, suggesting psychedelics trigger a total-body systemic reset.
    *   **Child-like Restoration:** Johnson reported waking up laughing in his sleep and feeling an "emergent excitement" for life, qualities typically lost as the brain ages and forms rigid behavioral "ruts."

### Neurological Mapping and the Default Mode Network (DMN)
*   **DMN Dissolution:** The DMN is the brain's "engine of ego" that facilitates rumination and self-identity. Johnson argues that as humans age, this network becomes "stiffer," narrowing our experience of reality. 
*   **Quantifying the Brain:** Johnson used high-fidelity monitoring—including **Kernel (optical interface)**, **Structural/Functional MRI**, and **real-time EEG**—to map how the molecule reshuffles neural traffic patterns.
*   **Neuroplasticity:** By silencing the DMN, the brain is forced to form new connections. Johnson observed that his ability to communicate, his sense of humor, and his emotional reactivity (quipping vs. escalating conflict) improved significantly post-dose.

### The Next Frontier: Gene Therapy and Organoids
*   **Mitochondrial Augmentation Therapy:** Johnson plans to harvest mitochondria from young maternally-linked family members, grow them in a bioreactor, and transplant them into his own cells to reverse DNA degradation.
*   **"Bryan Johnson Organoids":** He has successfully turned his own skin/blood cells into **Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells (iPSCs)** to grow "mini" versions of his heart, liver, and lungs in a dish.
    *   **Testing Protocol:** These organoids allow him to simulate drug interactions and combinatorial therapies in a petri dish before attempting them on his own body.
*   **Gene Therapy (FOXO3):** He is investigating the use of plasmids and mesenchymal stem cells to deliver FOXO3 expression, which has shown 50% tissue rejuvenation in preclinical studies.

### Philosophical Risks and "Psycho-Flexibility"
*   **The Founder Risk:** Host David Friedberg noted that heavy psychedelics often cause "dramatic life changes" (divorce, quitting jobs, abandoning responsibilities). Johnson acknowledges this "tail risk" but argues the reward is a necessary reconstruction of the self.
*   **AI Adaptation:** Johnson posits that humans need **"psycho-flexibility"** to survive an AI-dominant world. He believes the ability to rewire one's personality and biases rapidly is an asset, not a liability, for future-proofing the human mind.
*   **The "No Limit" Approach:** Johnson confirmed he is open to **Neuralink-style implants**, transgenic systems, and any frontier technology that allows him to "play" with the configuration of life, stating he has no limit on what he would try.
