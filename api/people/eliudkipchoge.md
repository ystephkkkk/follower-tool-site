### Global Politics: Israel National Elections
* **Referendum on Netanyahu:** Voting closes in Israel's first national election since the October 7th attack, serving as a high-turnout referendum on Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's political career. 
* **Political Landscape & Coalition Dynamics:** Bloomberg Israel Bureau Chief Ethan Bronner reports a razor-thin race between Netanyahu's bloc (allied with ultra-orthodox and national religious parties) and the center-right opposition led by Gadi Eisenkot. No party wins an outright majority, requiring post-election coalition horse-trading to reach a 61-seat threshold.
* **Policy Differences:** While security and troop deployments in regional conflicts will remain largely unchanged, an opposition win could see a crackdown on settler violence, a more deliberate and limited approach regarding Iran (avoiding regime change), greater transparency, and repaired U.S. relations.
* **Economic & Legal Impacts:** The Israeli economy has shown resilience, but faces major drags including a high-earning brain drain and heavy defense spending. Netanyahu's ongoing trial on bribery, fraud, and breach of trust charges will proceed, carrying potential prison time if he loses power.

### Alternative Real Estate, Senior Housing & Data Centers
* **Harrison Street Asset Management Strategy:** Global CEO Chris Merrill highlighted a focus on need-based, essential services and social infrastructure that perform across economic cycles.
* **Senior Housing Demographics:** The firm targets the aging population—noting nearly 2 million people turning 80 annually and high post-85 memory care needs—focusing on private-pay, rental-based continuums of care (independent, assisted, memory care) across 35 to 40 states.
* **Data Center Expansion:** Data center demand is "off the charts," but growth is severely constrained by access to power. Harrison Street manages risk via long-term net leases with hyper-scale tenants and essential off-takers (universities, health systems) in high-power nodes like Northern Virginia.
* **Capital Return & Sales:** The firm anticipates selling $6 billion to $7 billion in assets this year, prioritizing DPI (Distributed to Paid-In capital) as a core metric to return capital to investors amid strong buyer demand for non-traditional alternative segments.

### Sports & Endurance: The Eliud Kipchoge World Tour
* **World Tour Mission:** Two-time Olympic marathon gold medalist and Nike partner Eliud Kipchoge discussed his multi-year world tour running marathons across all seven continents over the next two years to spread the spirit of running, unity, and health.
* **Philosophy & Mental Resilience:** Kipchoge emphasized his guiding principle that "no human is limited," asserting that perceived barriers exist purely in the mind and apply to any profession (engineering, medicine, law). He cited breaking the two-hour marathon barrier as his toughest mental hurdle, overcome through trust in himself and his team.
* **Nutrition & Gear Evolution:** Kipchoge highlighted structured fueling every 5 kilometers as a game-changer for marathon recovery and energy. He credited ongoing technological advancements in Nike footwear—focused on muscle stability and rapid recovery—as key drivers of endurance sport progression.
