**TL;DR** U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent joined SMU President Jay Hartzell and moderator Ray Washburne for a fireside chat discussing economic policy, global market dynamics, artificial intelligence, and his journey from global finance to public service.

### Background & Personal History
* **Early Hardships:** **Scott Bessent** grew up in South Carolina where his father, a successful real estate developer, suffered severe financial setbacks due to over-leverage and poor decisions, forcing Bessent to work as a busboy at age 9.
* **Risk Management & Perspective:** This early experience deeply ingrained in Bessent a respect for risk management, avoiding over-leverage, and maintaining long-term perspective across economic cycles (such as the Texas oil bust, Asian financial crisis, and dot-com bust).
* **Education & Influences:** Bessent attended Yale University, studying history, philosophy, and psychology rather than traditional economics, describing himself as an "economic historian" rather than an economist.

### Economic Policy, Debt, and Growth
* **Spending vs. Revenue:** **Scott Bessent** stated that the U.S. does not have a revenue problem but a spending problem, aiming to contain spending and achieve 3% GDP growth to grow out of national debt.
* **Economic Stimulus & Tax Policy:** Cited the "Working Families Tax Cut" (one big beautiful bill) featuring full expensing for equipment, factories, and farm structures alongside working-class tax relief (no tax on tips or overtime, and social security tax exemptions for 85% of seniors).
* **Foreign Policy & Currency Interventions:** **Scott Bessent** explained using the U.S. balance sheet and Exchange Stabilization Fund for foreign policy goals (such as bridging Argentina during elections) to foster pro-market Western Hemisphere allies and counter opposition panics.
* **Energy Markets:** Discussed the tight correlation between interest rates and energy prices, predicting future oil over-supply driven by U.S., Venezuelan, and eventual Middle Eastern/Ukrainian normalization.

### Global Competitiveness & Artificial Intelligence
* **Competing Economic Ideologies:** **Scott Bessent** contrasted the U.S. growth agenda with European economic sclerosis and the Chinese state-planning model, predicting the U.S. model will prevail.
* **AI Ecosystem & Compute Power:** Highlighted America's organic AI boom, noting the U.S. will hold 80% of global computing power by 2028 (compared to China's projected 15%), while criticizing European risk-aversion and heavy regulation.
* **Impact on Jobs and Small Business:** Advised students that AI will not replace them, but someone who knows how to use AI will; noted a record surge in small business formations enabled by AI tools.

### Financial Literacy & Local Impact
* **Trump Accounts:** **Scott Bessent** highlighted Treasury's financial literacy initiatives and "Trump Accounts," seeded with $1,000 for children born during the term, augmented by a $6.25 billion donation from Susan and Michael Dell ($250 per child outside top 20 zip codes).
* **Texas Economy:** **Ray Washburne** and **Scott Bessent** praised Texas and Dallas ("Y'all Street") as high-pressure, pro-growth labs for the Trump economy, drawing massive domestic migration away from over-regulated states like California and New York.
