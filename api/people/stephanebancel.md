## Inside Moderna’s Biggest mRNA Test Since COVID

- Channel: a16z
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yTNMEYeCgWw
- Published: 2026-09-02
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260902.html#video-yTNMEYeCgWw
- Speakers: Jorge Conde (General Partner, a16z); Stéphane Bancel (CEO, Moderna)

---

### TL;DR
Moderna and Merck achieved positive Phase 3 results for their individualized mRNA cancer treatment (mRNA-4157/V940) combined with Keytruda in melanoma, marking the first time a personalized cancer vaccine has met its primary endpoint across a major clinical trial.

### Clinical Trial Results & Phase 3 Milestones
* **Primary Endpoint Success:** Stéphane Bancel (CEO, Moderna) noted that the Phase 3 clinical trial in melanoma met its primary endpoint of recurrence-free survival (RFS), significantly reducing cancer return or death compared to Keytruda alone.
* **Secondary Endpoint Achievement:** The trial also met its secondary endpoint of distant metastasis-free survival (DMFS) during the first interim analysis, which was an unexpected and positive surprise given that it requires more time to mature.
* **Phase 2 Long-Term Durability:** At ASCO in spring 2026, Moderna showed that Phase 2 data at five years post-treatment demonstrated an ~50% recurrence-free survival advantage for combination therapy vs. Keytruda alone, with ~80% of patients remaining disease-free at five years (considered a clinical cure in oncology).

### Mechanism of Action: mRNA vs. Traditional Checkpoint Inhibitors
* **Complementary Mechanisms:** Stéphane Bancel explained that Keytruda (a checkpoint inhibitor) acts like "opening the gates to let the dogs out" of the immune system, but only works for about 60% of patients while carrying severe autoimmune side effects (e.g., type 1 diabetes, lupus, Crohn's disease).
* **Targeted T-Cell Training:** Rather than unleashing the immune system blindly, Moderna's mRNA treatment encodes the patient's specific tumor mutations so that T-cells are taught at a molecular level exactly which signature to recognize and attack.
* **Intracellular Presentation:** Injected intramuscularly, Moderna's mRNA travels to lymph nodes and enters Antigen-Presenting Cells (APCs), translating the message from within, differing from older protein/peptide vaccines that circulate openly in the blood.

### Personalization & Manufacturing Scale
* **High Individualization Rate:** Roughly 90% of neoantigens identified across patients are completely unique, proving that personalized, end-to-end individualized manufacturing is essential.
* **The 42-Day Process:** From the initial tumor biopsy to the final vaccine ready at the hospital, the needle-to-needle turnaround time is currently roughly 42 days.
* **Manufacturing Engineering & Footprint:** Stéphane Bancel highlighted that manufacturing is fully synthetic and enzymatic (taking place in liquid/water with tiny reactors), allowing for a much smaller clean-room footprint compared to CAR-T cell therapy's large bioreactor volumes. The Marboro, Massachusetts facility is currently capable of producing tens of thousands of doses.

### Expansion Vectors Beyond Melanoma
* **PD-1 Combo Studies:** Moderna and Merck are actively running Phase 3 trials in lung cancer and Phase 2 trials in kidney and bladder cancers where Keytruda is already standard of care.
* **Early-Stage Monotherapy:** Moderna announced a Phase 3 study in spring 2026 for stage 1 lung cancer patients using the mRNA therapy as a monotherapy without checkpoint inhibitors, targeting former smokers screened via X-ray.
* **Cold Tumors:** Trials are underway in notoriously difficult, checkpoint-resistant cancers like pancreatic and gastric cancers, with potential future combinations with targeted therapies like Revolution Medicines' KRAS inhibitors.
* **Platform Horizons:** Stéphane Bancel shared that Moderna is preparing pivotal late-stage data for rare liver genetic diseases in children and is directing its mRNA platform toward treating the root causes of autoimmune diseases.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Stéphane Bancel** predicts that Moderna will file with regulators so the melanoma treatment can be available to patients as soon as possible, with a target of **2027**.
* **Stéphane Bancel** states that the current version of Moderna's individualized cancer mRNA technology (version 1.0) is "the worst version of inisurana you're going to see for the rest of medical history," anticipating algorithmic and technological improvements from mining Phase 3 patient data.
* **Stéphane Bancel** claims that before the end of the year (**2026**), Moderna will have pivotal late-stage study data for rare genetic liver diseases in children.
