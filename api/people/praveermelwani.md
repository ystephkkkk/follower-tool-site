### TL;DR
AI is transforming Figma’s finance team from a reactive, reporting-heavy function into operational builders who automate workflows, prototype products, and shape strategy. CFO Praveer Melwani emphasizes that while AI eliminates tedious tasks like SQL queries and data reconciliation, finance professionals must remain rigorous thinkers rather than outsourcing their judgment.

### Evolution of Finance & Automation
* **Shift from Manual to Automated:** 
  * Early-career finance required writing 400-500 line SQL queries and waiting hours for data tables to map product usage to Stripe.
  * Today, teams use AI to automate complex workflows like invoice tracking, cash collections, and reconciliation with NetSuite.
* **Real-Time Data Access:** 
  * Data cadence has shifted from monthly/quarterly reviews to daily and hourly metric tracking.
  * Praveer regularly reviews hourly dashboards to spot anomalies and operational trends before they impact the business.
* **The "Messy Middle" of Systems:** 
  * Decentralized AI experimentation has led teams to build dozens of independent agents, necessitating a centralized systems layer led by a CIO to harmonize tools.

### AI Use Cases at Figma
* **Credit & Collections:** 
  * Kajal on the collections team rebuilt invoice tracking, cash collections, and credit check data stitching using home-grown systems to drive down Days Sales Outstanding (DSO).
* **M&A Diligence:** 
  * The corporate development team uses "vibe coding" to prototype products and test technological feasibility during M&A diligence.
* **Simulated Investment Committee:** 
  * Corp dev created unique AI personas representing Figma executives (including Praveer, CEO Dylan Field, and CRO Sean) to vet investment memos and highlight potential pushback before meetings.
* **T&E and Portfolio Analysis:** 
  * The corporate finance team stitched together unstructured data from Navan, Zip, and Ramp using fuzzy matching to analyze T&E policy compliance across 2027 planning.

### Hiring and the "Slop" Risk
* **Intellectual Curiosity Over Outsourced Thinking:** 
  * Praveer looks for intellectually curious, data-fluent candidates who use AI as an empowering tool rather than a replacement for critical thinking.
* **The Danger of AI "Slop":** 
  * Unchecked AI usage can generate unlimited, low-quality documents that waste consuming time and energy.
  * Figma maintains strict internal policies on branding AI-generated content to ensure rigorous human review.
* **Case Study Rigor:** 
  * During VP of Finance interviews, Praveer uses a sacred case study to test whether candidates truly understand their model's sensitivity and underlying structure.
