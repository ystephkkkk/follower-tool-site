### TL;DR
AMD opened IFA 2026 by unveiling its "Era of Personal AI" vision, introducing high-performance local AI hardware—including Strix Halo, Gorgon Halo, and the Threadripper Halo Station—designed to run massive frontier-class models locally, maintain user privacy and context, and scale seamlessly from individual developer workstations to enterprise infrastructure.

### AMD Computing & AI Vision
* **Expanding Human Capability:** **Global impact of AMD technology:** Ranging from deep space exploration (Artemis II and Orion spacecraft) and precision medical robotics (Intuitive surgical systems) to climate research supercomputers (LUMI in Finland) and global entertainment infrastructure (EPYC, Threadripper, PlayStation 5 Pro).
* **The AI Paradigm Shift:** **Transition to agentic intelligence:** AI has progressed from reading and conversing to reasoning and acting via autonomous agents that can plan, use tools, and work in parallel.
* **The Token Consumption Crisis:** **Exploding enterprise compute demand:** 
  * **93% of companies** are currently exceeding their AI budgets.
  * **Monthly AI token processing** jumped from **0.7 quadrillion to 1.7 quadrillion** in just one year, with estimates reaching **120 quadrillion tokens per month by 2030**.
  * **Cloud cost disparity:** A single active user running agentic workflows can consume millions of tokens daily, translating to cloud costs near **€300 per day** (~€100,000 annually) for 15 million output tokens.

### Next-Generation AI PCs & Personal AI
* **The Three Pillars of Personal AI:** **Core design principles:** 
  * **Local compute:** Running powerful intelligence directly on personal hardware.
  * **Privacy and control:** Keeping sensitive data local and portable under user direction.
  * **Personal context:** Integrating a contextual layer so AI understands individual goals and workflows rather than just answering generic questions.
* **Strix Halo Hardware & Performance:** **Local frontier-class capability:** 
  * Features **120GB of unified memory**, enabling local execution of models up to **200 billion parameters**.
  * **Benchmark comparison:** The open-source model *Laguna S2.1* running locally on Strix Halo outperforms leading cloud models like *Cloud SANA 5* (which scores 70.3) on software engineering benchmarks without per-token cloud charges.
  * **Multitasking:** Capable of running **10 AI agents locally in parallel**.
* **Gorgon Halo & Gorgon Halo Box:** **Desktop and workstation power:** 
  * Boosts unified memory to **192 gigabytes**, supporting models with **200 to 300 billion parameters**.
  * *GLM 5.3 Flash* running locally on Gorgon Halo beats the cloud model *Cloud Fable 5* on benchmark tests.
  * Lenovo introduced the **ThinkCentre X** in Berlin powered by Gorgon Halo, designed to run 300 billion parameter models locally.
* **Project Sunday (Next-Gen HP ZBook):** **Mobile personal AI:** A new class of laptop featuring **190GB of unified memory** capable of generating real-time 3D worlds from a single prompt and playing demanding games like Flight Sim locally.

### Microsoft Partnership & Windows Integration
* **Pavan Davuluri (Microsoft) on Agentic Windows:** **OS-level transformation:** 
  * Windows is evolving from a tool to an active partner that can act across apps, services, and files via secure isolation boundaries (Microsoft Execution Containers).
  * **Hardware specs:** Acknowledged joint engineering on Gorgon Halo featuring **16 Zen 5 CPU cores**, an RDNA GPU, an efficient NPU, and **192GB of memory** running a **125 billion parameter model** locally.
* **Project Zenith:** **Developer-optimized experience:** 
  * A ready-to-code Windows experience coming pre-installed with VS Code, WSL, GitHub Copilot CLI, and PowerShell on hardware with a minimum of **64GB of UMA**.
  * Will be available out of the box on **AMD Ryzen AI Halo**.

### A New Class of AI System: Threadripper Halo Station
* **Threadripper Halo Station Architecture:** **Personal supercomputer workstation:** 
  * Designed for models with more than **one trillion parameters**.
  * Specs include **96 cores of Threadripper Pro**, **2 MI-350P Instinct accelerators** (with a path to four), up to **2 terabytes of system memory**, up to **576 gigabytes of HBM3E**, and full liquid cooling.

### Enterprise AI & Open Infrastructure
* **Dirk-Peter van Leeuwen (SUSE) on Scaling & Openness:** **Bridging local development to enterprise production:** 
  * **SUSE AI Factory and Rancher:** Provide a secure, supported path from local Ryzen AI Halo development workstations to enterprise-wide production scaling without discarding existing builds.
  * **Open Ecosystem:** Emphasized that developers and businesses need freedom of choice across models, frameworks, and infrastructure (local, private data center, edge, or cloud) without vendor lock-in, celebrating SUSE's **34th anniversary**.
  * **Unified Pool of Compute:** The long-term vision connects workstations, servers, data centers, and edge devices into a single open platform for AI.
