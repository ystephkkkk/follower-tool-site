### TL;DR
Morgan Stanley CEO Ted Pick projects cumulative AI infrastructure spending will reach $10–$15 trillion over the next decade, while PIMCO CEO Manny Roman asserts that global sovereign debt markets remain "well anchored" and that high interest rates are driven primarily by Middle East developments and the energy complex rather than AI financing.

### AI Infrastructure Boom & Capital Expenditure (Ted Pick)
* **Scale of Investment:** 
  * Cumulative AI CapEx is projected to reach **$10–$15 trillion** over the next 7 to 12 years.
  * Current AI spending is in the **"second or third inning,"** running at roughly **$1 trillion a year** for the next several years.
  * By comparison, total Asian CapEx is **$11 trillion** currently and is projected to reach **$16 trillion** by **2030**.
* **ROI & Urgency:** 
  * Enterprise demand currently outstrips supply, forcing companies to pay up to secure compute power rather than fall behind competitors.
  * While not every project will succeed, efficiency and effectiveness gains are expected to bring **50 to 70 basis points of real GDP growth** to the global economy.
* **Financing & Execution Risk:** 
  * Local-level resistance (NIMBYism) creates localized completion and location risks that will command risk premiums in capital structures.
  * Morgan Stanley acts as a trusted advisor to help structure deals, determining the appropriate mix of debt, equity, and private securities (such as through their recent acquisition of **Equity Zen**).

### Sovereign Debt, Central Banks, & Macro Economics (Manny Roman)
* **Sovereign Markets & Interest Rates:** 
  * Sovereign debt markets are **"well anchored,"** evidenced by stable swap spreads and TIPS.
  * High interest rates are driven by the Middle East and the energy complex, not the **$500 billion** of AI financing to date.
* **Central Bank Trajectory:** 
  * In the US, the Federal Reserve is expected to raise rates by **a quarter point** at the end of the year and then pause.
  * In Europe, central banks likely need to raise rates **twice** due to worse macroeconomic conditions, characterized by low growth, high debt-to-GDP, and heavy taxation.
* **Currency & Global Reserves:** 
  * The US dollar remains the primary reserve currency due to a lack of attractive currency diversification alternatives, ensuring the strength of the dollar is likely to persist.
  * For China, the single most important macro decision over the next 20 years will be whether to let the yuan float.

### Fixed Income & Investment Strategy (Manny Roman)
* **Yield Attractiveness:** 
  * Fixed income offers a blended return of **7% to 8%**, which looks highly attractive on a risk-adjusted basis compared to expensive S&P 500 equity valuations.
  * PIMCO is seeing strong rebalancing trends toward fixed income across the US, Europe, and Asia.
* **Specific Opportunities:** 
  * **US and Global Markets:** US sovereign debt and mortgages look very attractive.
  * **Emerging Markets & Alternatives:** Emerging markets offer numerous opportunities, and private structured finance/asset-backed assets are generating **12% to 15%** returns (including building data centers in Europe).
* **Inflation & Long-Term Drivers:** 
  * AI is CapEx-intensive upfront from strong hands, but **long-term deflationary**, aided by future labor market shifts driven by robotics.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* Cumulative AI infrastructure spending will reach **$10–$15 trillion** over the next decade (Ted Pick).
* The AI investment timeline will span **7 to 12 years** (Ted Pick).
* AI higher intelligence and productivity will add **50 to 70 basis points** of real GDP growth to the global economy (Ted Pick).
* The Federal Reserve will raise rates by **a quarter point** at the end of the year and stop (Manny Roman).
