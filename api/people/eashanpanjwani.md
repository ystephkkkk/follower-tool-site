### TL;DR
Regna AI is a new sovereign AI joint venture between Palantir and Ondas designed to provide mission-specific, post-trained open-weights models for defense, intelligence, and edge operations while safeguarding sensitive data.

### Origins and Vision of Regna AI
* **Founding Context:** 
  * Formed after six months of close collaboration between Ondas and Palantir, tackling mission autonomy capabilities like *Sky Weaver*.
  * The name *Regna* translates to "Sovereign Kingdom," reflecting the commitment to delivering sovereign AI solutions for defense and government customers.
* **The Sovereign AI Thesis:** 
  * Inspired by Palantir CEO Dr. Alex Karp’s vision for sovereign AI across industries.
  * Focuses on post-training models for specific missions (such as intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance, or ISR) to compound operational "alpha"—measured in lives saved rather than commercial metrics.
* **Spin-Out Structure:** 
  * Initially bootstrapped by Ondas with Ondas serving as the first customer.
  * Structured to operate as an independent company serving the broader defense ecosystem rather than just a proprietary internal tool.

### Technical Architecture & Edge Constraints
* **Rejection of General-Purpose AI:** 
  * Rather than relying on cloud-hosted frontier models designed for general tasks, Regna AI builds specialized, fine-tuned models tailored to specific operational constraints.
* **Edge Deployment:** 
  * Models are engineered to run on limited form factors and compute footprints found on edge platforms, including stratospheric balloons, long-endurance UAVs, and ground robotics.
* **Disconnected Operations:** 
  * Designed for environments with limited bandwidth and compute, enabling distributed inferencing directly on platforms while maintaining situational awareness.

### Human-in-the-Loop Validation and Trust
* **Forward-Deployed Engineering Model:** 
  * Utilizes Palantir-style Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) working shoulder-to-shoulder with customers to map data inputs directly to operational decisions.
* **"Mini-Labs" for Customers:** 
  * Commanders maintain continuous control over model training, reviewing end-to-end data pipelines and recommended actions.
  * Operators can iteratively accept or reject outputs, ensuring high trust before deploying models to the battlefield or simulated exercises.

### Complex Multi-Domain Use Cases
* **Stratospheric Balloon Routing:** 
  * Managing infinitely complex operations for stratospheric balloons—including dynamic weather, wind patterns, exclusion zones, and inclement weather routing.
* **Multi-Domain Orchestration:** 
  * Connecting assets across the stratosphere, air, space, ground, and surface domains to distill infinite data streams into actionable commander decisions.
* **Building on Existing Frameworks:** 
  * Leverages prior operational learnings from *Sky Weaver*, mission control, and orbit systems to shape the architecture of the new models.
