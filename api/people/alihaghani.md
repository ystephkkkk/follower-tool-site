### TL;DR
Ali Haghani, CEO of YC Winter '24 startup Circleback, explains how his company uses AI note-takers and multi-agent workflows to automate operations, manage engineering, and serve as a centralized "company brain."

### Hardware & Daily Gear Setup
* **Typing Equipment:** Uses a Topre keyboard (Tokyo Precision brand) with Japanese instruction manuals; chosen for its membrane typing feel, early key press registration, and quiet operation in open-plan offices over noisy mechanical blue/brown switches.
* **Fidget Gadget:** Features a Temu-branded toy car on his desk, acquired after almost completing a $400 cash-back onboarding funnel; used for fidgeting during discussions or meeting dictations.
* **Status Signaling:** Wears AirPods Max infrequently as a signal of being "locked in"—akin to an MSN or AIM status indicator.

### Meeting Intelligence & Unusual Use Cases
* **ATS & Candidate Tracking:** Uses Circleback as an Applicant Tracking System (ATS), filtering incomplete interview action items and checking full touch-point timelines across emails and meetings to monitor candidate progress.
* **CRM & Relationship Management:** Tracks all communications and action items linked to external partners, such as Y Combinator company pages and post-batch office hours.
* **Meeting Prep:** Used Circleback the night before the interview to aggregate past calendar events, emails, and previous conversations to generate customized prep notes.
* **Hardware Capture:** Utilizes the action button on his device for quick captures without reaching for a phone, and is currently beta testing a watch app for meeting recording via watch complications.

### AI Agents & Telegram Workflows
* **Agent Architecture:** Runs a main OpenClaw agent alongside specialized agents for finance, customer service, and people operations via Telegram (10-20% of his day).
* **Migration to Slack:** Transitioning operational processes from Telegram to Slack channels (e.g., customer emails and inbound sales threads) to provide company-wide visibility.
* **Support Rotation:** Maintains a weekly engineer run rotation where team members handle incoming customer support, feeding real-world customer feedback and fireside chat recordings directly into Circleback.
* **Task Boundaries:** Enforces strict guardrails where agents are never allowed to independently send emails, push copy straight to production without human review, or architect security/data systems.

### Engineering, Coding, & Prompt Engineering
* **Coding & Orchestration:** Spends time on code review rather than writing raw code; uses orchestrating agents to fire off tasks and review diffs using lightweight tools instead of traditional editors.
* **No-Meeting Days:** Allocates Thursdays as a dedicated no-meeting day to focus end-to-end on shipping specific features.
* **Rigorous Evals:** Employs evals to test prompts for quantifiable correctness (such as filtering out action items that were already completed during the meeting) and writing style (explicitly banning words like "discussed" to make notes sound human).
* **Token Culture:** Operates with an unlimited AI budget and a "tokenmaxxing" culture where teams automate ops tasks and share new tool workflows during show-and-tells.
