### TL;DR
US Secretary of State Marco Rubio discusses upcoming India-US trade agreements, energy cooperation, and global security issues during an exclusive interview with Network18's Rahul Joshi in Mumbai.

* **Status of the Bilateral Trade Deal:**

### Geopolitics, Russian Oil, & Iran
* **Russian Oil Sanctions & Waivers:** 
  * **Balancing Act:** Acknowledged the tension between applying economic pressure on Russia and avoiding severe negative spillover effects on global partner nations.
