### TL;DR
Google DeepMind introduced Genie 4—its latest world model featuring native audio, Gemini-in-the-loop control, and advanced photorealism built on the Omni Foundation—marking its first-ever release through both Google products and the API.

### Panelists & Speakers
* **Logan Kilpatrick** (Host, Google for Developers)
* **Raia Hadsell** (Google DeepMind)
* **Diego Rivas** (Google DeepMind)
* **Hang Qi** (Google DeepMind)

### What Are World Models & The Arc to Genie 4
* **Definition of World Models:** Unlike language models like Gemini (which handle knowledge and language), a world model understands a grounded environment, its internal state, and how interactions, collisions, and actions change the world through physics prediction, even in fantastical settings (**Raia Hadsell**).
* **The Evolution from Genie 1 to Genie 4:**
  * **Genie 1:** A high-risk research proof-of-concept that generated basic 2D platformer environments where agents could run and jump (**Raia Hadsell**).
  * **Genie 2:** Expanded generation into 3D environments, though still gamified and not fully real-time or interactive (**Raia Hadsell**).
  * **Genie 3:** Released in January, this was the first model released to the public, offering a new level of real-time, interactive 3D realism (**Raia Hadsell**).
  * **Genie 4:** Introduces a massive leap in photorealism (backed by Omni), native audio prediction, dialogue capabilities, and real-time text steering via Gemini (**Raia Hadsell**, **Diego Rivas**, **Hang Qi**).

### Live Demos & Technical Capabilities
* **Audio Modality:** Genie 4 predicts video and audio together from the same model, supporting both ambient environmental sound/music and character dialogue (**Diego Rivas**, **Hang Qi**).
* **Interaction Paradigms:** Shifts focus from third-party characters (seen in Genie 3) to egocentric first-person views (e.g., making coffee) and precise camera controls (**Diego Rivas**).
* **Gemini in the Loop:** Vanilla **3.8 Flash** runs in the background as an intelligence layer, observing previous frames, tracking user intent, and driving character dialogue and scene logic in real time (**Hang Qi**, **Diego Rivas**).
* **Robotics Integration:** Robotic partners used **Argon** as a text station alongside Genie 4 to generate interleaved actions and visualize simulation tasks (like arranging Scrabble letters) to generate scalable training data (**Hang Qi**, **Diego Rivas**).

### Key Differentiators of Genie 4
* **Enhanced Realism:** Addresses Genie 3 feedback regarding static or unrealistic characters, delivering highly detailed and dynamic environments (e.g., busy restaurant kitchens) (**Diego Rivas**).
* **Freeform Controls:** Moves beyond simple navigation into full simulation, allowing users to execute complex actions and steer the world freely (**Diego Rivas**).
* **Native Audio Integration:** Seamlessly pairs sound and video for immersive dialogue and environmental feedback (**Diego Rivas**).

### Availability, Safety, and SynthID
* **Access Channels:** Available via **Project Genie** surfaces and—for the first time—available in the **API** (**Diego Rivas**).
* **Watermarking:** All outputs include **SynthID** real-time watermarking (**Logan Kilpatrick**).
* **Real-Time Safety:** Pioneering real-time filtering systems and guardrails developed with safety teams to manage photorealism safely while maintaining low latency (**Diego Rivas**).
* **User-Context Guardrails:** Safety constraints adapt based on user personas (e.g., filmmakers or educators simulating complex or historical disaster scenarios versus general public use) (**Raia Hadsell**).

> "Genie 4, we're just making further leaps on those fronts [absorbing world varieties and real-time responsiveness]." — Hang Qi

> "You can think about the language models, Gemini, as a thinking model... versus a world model is about understanding a grounded environment, a world, and how do interactions happen within that world." — Raia Hadsell
