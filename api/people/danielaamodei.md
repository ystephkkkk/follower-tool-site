* **Complementary vs. Replacement:** Anthropic’s economic index suggests AI is currently an "enabler" rather than a replacement for work, with the notable exception of customer service roles.
* **Shift in Professional Scopes:**
    * **Software Engineering:** Developers may write less code but will spend more time on product management, customer interaction, and high-level architecture.
    * **Medicine:** AI will excel at diagnosis, making "bedside manner" and human empathy the most prized skills for future physicians.
* **"Learning Mode" vs. "Cheating Mode":** In education, the goal is for AI to act as a "patient tutor" that gets students unstuck rather than a tool that simply provides the answer.
* **Managerial Coaching:** Amodei uses Claude to spot long-term patterns in employee performance and to receive "upward feedback" on her own leadership blind spots.

### Global Adoption and Future Risks
* **The "Bubble" Disconnect:** While Silicon Valley is saturated with AI, adoption is not equally distributed. It currently skews toward college-educated men in high-income countries.
* **Geographic Optimism:** Interestingly, the "Global South" and developing countries show higher optimism for AI as an equalizing force, whereas the US and Europe exhibit higher anxiety about disruption to the status quo.
* **The Risk of "Brain Atrophy":** A major qualitative study by Anthropic (81,000 participants) identified a fear that users may stop "engaging their brains" because delegating thought to AI is too easy, potentially stifling human curiosity.
* **Capital Expenditure Risks:** Amodei acknowledges the industry is in a high-CAPEX cycle. Companies must bet billions on future compute needs (GPUs) long before they see the return, making the current era a "calculated bet" on sustained progress.
