### MGM Resorts: The Asset-Backed Asian Casino Play
Presented by Aaron Cowen (Suvretta Capital Management), this thesis positions MGM not as a standard Las Vegas recovery play, but as a deeply mispriced asset with massive regional growth optionality and strong downside protection.

*   **The Valuation Floor:** 
    *   **Corporate Actions:** MGM has aggressively bought back half of its outstanding stock float over the last six years.
    *   **The Diller Floor:** IAC's Barry Diller owns 26% of MGM and recently submitted an acquisition bid at $48 per share (up from the stock's ~$37 trading price when the pitch was prepared). 
    *   **Sum-of-the-Parts:** Cowen values the core Las Vegas properties and Macau operations at roughly $60 per share, establishing a strong margin of safety.
*   **Hidden Asset 1 (Osaka, Japan):** MGM secured a sole license to open a massive casino resort in Osaka, Japan by 2030. 
    *   **Market Scale:** Japan’s domestic gambling market (pachinko and racing) is already $40 billion, compared to Macau’s $30 billion and Las Vegas's $10 billion. 
    *   **Geographic Moat:** Osaka is physically closer to Shanghai and Beijing than Macau or Singapore, making it highly attractive to Chinese VIP gamblers.
    *   **Financial Impact:** The project is projected to yield $2 billion in EBITDA, of which MGM owns 40% alongside receiving ongoing management fees. Cowen values this option at ~$50 per share.
*   **Hidden Asset 2 (Dubai "Free Option"):** MGM has developed a massive non-gaming resort footprint in Dubai that includes 300,000 square feet of undeclared empty space. If Dubai legalizes gambling—accelerated by Wynn's upcoming rival casino in nearby Al Marjan—MGM is perfectly positioned to instantly capture the market, adding an estimated $40–$50 per share in value.

### Talen Energy: Underpriced Baseload Power for the AI Era
Presented by Dan Dreyfus (Bornite Capital), this thesis argues that the market drastically underestimates the multi-decade structural deficit in US electrical grid capacity, making baseload power producers highly asymmetric investments.

*   **The Power Supercycle:** Dreyfus notes that after two decades of flat US power demand driven by efficiency gains, a new technological expansion phase is underway. AI data centers act as energy refineries, converting electricity into intelligence.
*   **Discount to Replacement Value:** Talen Energy operates 2 gigawatts (GW) of nuclear power and 6 GW of natural gas baseload power.
    *   **The Arbitrage:** At a $25 billion enterprise value, the company trades at a steep discount to its physical replacement cost of $45 billion.
*   **Severe Grid Tightness:** The PJM grid operator (serving Pennsylvania, New Jersey, and Maryland) forecasts a requirement of 106 GW of new power over the next decade. Supply chain shortages in copper, superalloys, and equipment mean this capacity cannot be built fast enough, keeping power prices elevated.
*   **Three Valuation Scenarios:**
    1.  **Status Quo:** If Talen does nothing and runs its existing portfolio, it will generate $50/share in free cash flow (FCF), implying a cheap ~7x multiple on its high-$300s stock price.
    2.  **Premium Contracts:** Signing long-term power purchase agreements (PPAs) with hyperscalers (similar to Microsoft’s 20-year deal to restart Constellation's Three Mile Island reactor) would push FCF to $70/share, commanding a 15x multiple (~$1,050 stock price).
    3.  **New Buildouts:** Actively constructing 4 GW of new capacity under regulator-backed partnerships could yield over $100/share in FCF.

### Aktis Oncology: Safe, Non-Replicable Precision Cancer Warfare
Presented by Oleg Nodelman (EcoR1 Capital), this pitch details the investment potential of Aktis Oncology (AKTS), a newly public biotech firm pioneering targeted radiopharmaceuticals.

*   **The Modality:** Targeted radiopharmaceuticals work like "molecular micro-drones." They navigate the bloodstream using mini-proteins to find tumor-specific biomarkers (such as Nectin-4 or B7-H3) and detonate an isotope payload with a precise, single-cell blast radius (100 microns), minimizing collateral damage.
*   **Strong Financial Position:** Aktis went public with a $300 million IPO that was 18 times oversubscribed and anchored by a $100 million order from Eli Lilly. The firm sports a $1 billion market cap, $500 million enterprise value, and more than three years of cash runway.
*   **The Manufacturing and Geopolitical Moat:** Unlike standard small-molecule biologics, radiopharmaceuticals cannot easily be copied or reverse-engineered by generic manufacturers.
    *   **The China Shield:** The core isotope used (Actinium) is derived from Radium-233, a legacy byproduct of US nuclear defense programs. Because China's nuclear history utilized a different fuel cycle, they lack the raw materials to compete, creating a secure regulatory and supply-chain moat in the US.
*   **Catalyst Path:** Initial clinical data for the lead Nectin-4 program is expected as early as Q1 2027. If successful, Nodelman estimates the platform could achieve a $10 billion valuation ($200 per share).

### GEODNET: Decentralized High-Precision Localization
Presented by Kyle Samani (Multicoin Capital), this pitch highlights GEODNET, a Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network (DePIN) establishing a global, ultra-precise alternative to traditional GPS.

*   **The Technology:** Traditional GPS is accurate to roughly 2 meters. GEODNET utilizes Real-Time Kinematics (RTK) to narrow location tracking down to 2 centimeters (a 100x improvement). This level of precision is critical for autonomous tractors, drones, consumer robotics, and mapping.
*   **The DePIN Growth Engine:** Legacy operators took 30 years to deploy 12,000 RTK base stations globally. Using a token incentive model, GEODNET crowdsourced 22,000 active nodes across 150 countries in under three years.
    *   **Bootstrap Mechanics:** Individual homeowners or businesses buy a $300-400 base station, mount it to their roof, and earn GEOD tokens for broadcasting local satellite data.
*   **Commercial Adoption:** Major industry players are already customers, including DJI (drones), TomTom (navigation), and John Deere’s GUSS autonomous spraying platforms.
*   **Tokenomics & Capital Return:** GEODNET has a fully diluted valuation of roughly $150 million and generates $11 million in annualized run-rate revenue (growing 3x YoY). Programmatically, 80% of all network revenue is used to purchase GEOD tokens on the open market and burn/retire them, functioning as a direct buyback yield for token holders.

### Post-Pitch Panel Review and Verdict
The panel of hosts (Chamath Palihapitiya, David Friedberg, David Sacks, and guest investor Gavin Baker) weighed the pitches based on risk-reward, liquidity, and structural trends.

*   **Sizing and Liquidity Constraints:** Chamath noted that while he loved the asymmetric upside of Aktis (AKTS) and GEODNET, they are highly volatile "lottery tickets" with limited capacity to absorb institutional capital. In contrast, MGM and Talen (TLN) can easily accommodate tens of millions of dollars in position size.
*   **Macro and Competitive Risks:**
    *   **Talen:** Sacks and Gavin flagged political and regulatory risks, noting that the federal government could step in to cap utility prices if consumer rates spike due to AI demand.
    *   **GEODNET:** Sacks questioned whether a dense constellation of low-Earth orbit (LEO) micro-satellites (like SpaceX Starlink) would eventually render terrestrial RTK stations obsolete. Samani countered that ground-based networks are vastly more cost-effective and energy-efficient for battery-sensitive devices like drones.
*   **Final Results:**
    *   **Audience Vote:** Dan Dreyfus / Talen Energy won by a landslide with 50% of the vote.
    *   **The Besties' Vote:** Aaron Cowen / MGM Resorts took first place due to its immediate downside floor (the Barry Diller bid) and massive, overlooked international options.
