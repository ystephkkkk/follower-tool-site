### AI Impact & Overexposure Risks
* **Ubiquitous Disruption:** **Sarah H. Ketterer** notes that AI affects all nine global sectors, creating a risk where companies could be permanently undermined if their software is not mission-critical (such as enterprise resource planning by Oracle or SAP).
* **The "SaaS Apocalypse":** **Sarah H. Ketterer** points out that software-as-a-service companies have suffered share price destruction due to expectations that users can "vibe code" what they do.
* **Contrarian AI Opportunities:** **Sarah H. Ketterer** identifies market-perceived AI "losers"—companies lagging in agent deployment with strong financial resources—as prime contrarian buys, analogous to Meta prior to its equity offering.

### Quantitative Strategies & Emerging Markets
* **Controlling Top-Down Risk:** **Sarah H. Ketterer** explains that Causeway's quantitative models avoid big macro bets in emerging markets—such as the recent unexpected Brazilian election win for Lula after 16 years of heavy-handed socialist leadership, or Russia's 2022 invasion of Ukraine that drove Russian stocks to zero.
