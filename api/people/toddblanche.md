**TL;DR**
Deputy Attorney General Todd Blanche detailed a massive restructuring of the Department of Justice and FBI, including the departure of over 200 personnel, while prioritizing the reversal of "weaponized" prosecutions and aggressive new enforcement against narco-terrorism and election fraud.

### DOJ and FBI Personnel Overhaul
* **"Cleaning House":** Blanche reported that more than 200 employees have left the Department of Justice—via firing, early retirement, or resignation—since the administration took office.
* **FBI Purge:** Under Director Patel, the FBI has removed every agent involved in the prior prosecutions of President Trump. Blanche emphasized that federal agents now clearly understand they work for the President under the Executive Branch's constitutional authority.
* **Institutional Shift:** The administration is actively moving to end what Blanche described as the "weaponization" of the DOJ and FBI seen over the past four years, specifically targeting the influence of partisan actors within the civil service.

### Policy Reversals and Constitutional Priorities
* **January 6th Actions:** Blanche noted that as of 5:00 PM on Inauguration Day, every individual convicted in relation to January 6th had their sentence commuted or received a pardon.
* **Religious Liberty:** The DOJ has reversed policies targeting Christian organizations and non-profits. This includes investigating and stopping the "de-banking" of religious groups by financial institutions.
* **Second Amendment Rights:** The department is actively working to restore gun rights, withdrawing from several lawsuits, and removing taxes on specific firearm types that the administration deems unconstitutional.
* **Ending Consent Decrees:** The DOJ has moved to withdraw from federal consent decrees nationwide that placed local police departments under judicial or law firm oversight, unless specifically blocked by a judge.

### Law Enforcement and Border Security
* **Narco-Terrorism:** The administration has reclassified major drug dealers as "narco-terrorists." Blanche highlighted a recent surge in arrests and raids, including a major fentanyl and cocaine seizure in Los Angeles.
* **Human Trafficking and Deportation:** Priorities have shifted toward aggressive prosecution of human traffickers and the immediate deportation of criminal illegal immigrants.
* **Election Integrity:**
    * **Prosecutions:** The DOJ has charged 20 times more cases of illegal voting by non-citizens in one year than the previous administration did in four.
    * **Voter ID:** While acknowledging state-level jurisdictional hurdles, Blanche affirmed that universal photo voter ID is a primary goal of the administration’s election integrity agenda.

### Judicial Strategy and Venue Reform
* **Jurisdictional Strategy:** Blanche confirmed a tactical shift toward investigating and bringing federal cases in "fair" jurisdictions (red areas) where possible, rather than blue cities like Washington D.C. or New York, to ensure more balanced juries and judges.
* **Judicial Appointments:** A top priority remains the appointment of conservative, originalist judges. Blanche cited the reversal of *Roe v. Wade* as the blueprint for the long-term impact of President Trump's judicial legacy.
* **Combatting "Lawfare":** The department is seeking ways to make "whole" individuals targeted by previous political prosecutions. Blanche highlighted a recent settlement with General Michael Flynn as a step toward compensating those impacted by legal fees and professional damage.
