## Novo Nordisk CEO: Open to deals, we need the broadest pipeline in the world

- Channel: CNBC Television

---

**TL;DR**
Novo Nordisk is leveraging a successful "peptide-in-a-pill" Wegovy launch and strategic rebranding of Rybelsus to maintain a 65% share of new prescriptions, while aggressively pursuing M&A to build the world’s broadest metabolic pipeline.

### Wegovy Pill Launch and Market Performance
* **Clinical Efficacy:** The Wegovy pill demonstrates up to 17% weight loss with daily use, which CEO Mike Doustdar notes is approximately 30% higher efficacy than current oral competitors.
* **The "Peptide" Advantage:** While competitors often use small molecules for oral drugs, Novo Nordisk intentionally chose a peptide formulation.
    * **Potency:** Peptides are characterized as more powerful and potent than small molecules.
    * **Safety Profile:** The pill is backed by 15 million patient years of safety data collected via the Wegovy injectable franchise.
* **Commercial Strategy:** Currently, 90% of sales are through the cash-pay channel.
    * **Affordability:** The pill was priced to be accessible to a large group of patients without immediate reimbursement.
    * **Expansion:** International launches are scheduled to begin in the second half of the year as production scales.

### Competitive Positioning and Demographics
* **Market Share:** Novo Nordisk currently captures 65% of new prescriptions in the anti-obesity segment, compared to 35% for its primary competitor (Eli Lilly).
* **Patient Demographics:** 60–65% of the patient base is female. 
    * **Drug Interactions:** Doustdar highlights a competitive edge in safety, noting that Novo’s pill does not require patients to stop oral birth control, a restriction seen in some competitor clinical trials.
    * **Male Adoption:** The pill format is successfully reaching more male patients than the injectable versions previously did.
* **The "1-0" Lead:** Acknowledging Eli Lilly CEO Dave Ricks’ comment that the "pill war" will take quarters to play out, Doustdar stated he is happy to be "1-0" after the first quarter of the launch.

### Portfolio Strategy and Rebranding
* **Leveraging Brand Equity:** Novo Nordisk plans to rebrand the oral GLP-1 **Rybelsus** as the **"Ozempic pill."** 
    * **Rationale:** The goal is to capitalize on the massive global brand recognition of Ozempic to drive sales for both the pill and the injectable formats.
* **Franchise Growth:** The Wegovy franchise is being bolstered by the introduction of **Wegovy HD (High Dose)**, which has further strengthened brand recognition alongside the oral launch.

### Pipeline Innovation and M&A Outlook
* **Near-Term Assets:** The company is preparing for the launch of **CagriSema** (an amylin/GLP-1 combination therapy) early next year, following an expected approval at the end of this year.
* **Early-Stage Assets:** 
    * **Zegalatide:** A follow-on product currently in development in both injectable and pill formats.
    * **UBT251:** A triple agonist that has shown superior early Phase 2 results compared to competitors.
* **Aggressive Deal-Making:** Doustdar emphasized that the company is "looking more than ever before" at external acquisitions.
    * **Mindset:** The company is actively fighting internal complacency or "not invented here" syndrome to ensure they possess the broadest pipeline globally.
* **Diversification:** Following successful Phase 3 data, Novo Nordisk is seeking approval for a **Sickle Cell** treatment, an asset originally acquired through a business development deal.

## Novo Nordisk CEO on medicare coverage, new obesity pill, U.S. pricing pressure

- Channel: CNBC Television
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UT3YKDb4WFQ
- Published: 2026-02-13
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260213.html#video-UT3YKDb4WFQ
- Speakers: Mike Doustdar (CEO, Novo Nordisk); Annika Kim Constantino (Reporter, CNBC)

---

**TL;DR**
Novo Nordisk expects 2026 to face sales growth headwinds due to U.S. pricing pressures, but aims to offset lower margins through massive volume increases driven by the Wegovy pill launch and expanded Medicare access. The company is also taking aggressive legal action against compounders to protect its intellectual property and patient safety as supply shortages resolve.

### 2026 Strategic Outlook & Pricing Pressures
*   **Financial Headwinds:** CEO Mike Doustdar acknowledges that 2026 will likely see negative sales growth due to significant pricing pressures in the U.S. market.
*   **Volume-Based Recovery:** The company plans to compensate for lower prices by scaling production and increasing the total patient base.
    *   **Patient Reach:** Novo Nordisk expects to serve more patients in 2026 than ever before.
    *   **Pipeline Investment:** Continued growth is expected from new product launches and expanding the current clinical pipeline.

### The Wegovy Pill Launch & Competitive Edge
*   **Rapid Adoption:** Within the first five weeks of launch, 246,000 patients have started on the oral Wegovy pill.
*   **Market Strategy:** The pill is designed to attract patients with "needle phobia" or those who view injections as a social taboo.
*   **Efficacy vs. Competitors:** 
    *   **Weight Loss Performance:** Clinical data shows Novo’s pill achieves 16.6% weight loss compared to a competitor's 12.4% (a 40% efficacy advantage).
    *   **Tolerability:** Novo reports a 3.6% discontinuation rate due to gastrointestinal issues, significantly lower than the 10.4% seen in competitor trials.
*   **Multi-Channel Distribution:** To maximize accessibility, Novo is partnering with telehealth and digital pharmacies including Ro, WeightWatchers, and Amazon Pharmacy.

### Market Access and Medicare Expansion
*   **Medicare Potential:** Lowering prices has opened access to 67 million Medicare beneficiaries.
    *   **Initial Targets:** Novo is initially targeting approximately 15 million eligible patients within the Medicare system.
    *   **Gradual Rollout:** Doustdar warns that access will be gradual rather than "overnight," as the company must navigate plan-by-plan negotiations and insurance pre-authorizations.
*   **Supply Chain Resilience:** Following past shortages, the company has adopted a "never again" policy regarding supply gaps. European expansion is planned only once U.S. supply is fully stabilized.

### Legal Actions and Pipeline Innovation
*   **Cracking Down on Compounding:** Novo Nordisk has filed over 120 lawsuits, including a recent case against Hims & Hers, to stop the "mass compounding" of GLP-1s.
    *   **Safety Concerns:** Doustdar cites risks from unknown raw materials (often from China) and argues that compounding is no longer justified now that the "shortage is over."
*   **Next-Generation Treatments:**
    *   **High-Dose Injectables:** A 7.2mg dose of Wegovy is in development, which clinical data suggests can achieve 21% weight loss, matching competitor Eli Lilly’s highest doses.
    *   **CagriSema:** This amylin-based combination therapy is a major pillar for future growth, with REDEFINE-1 trials showing 23% weight loss.
*   **Diversification:** The company is moving toward "precision" obesity care, targeting specific comorbidities like fatty liver (MASH) rather than treating obesity as a single condition.

### Notable Quotes
> "We have for a while now been speaking that 2026 will be a different year... we are projecting a negative sales growth on the back of pricing headwinds... but it's also a year of growth in many ways. We will have more patients this year than ever before." — **Mike Doustdar**

> "We really don't understand why [compounding] continued, and I certainly don't understand why it's still here when the prices of the original product and the knockoff version has become very, very similar." — **Mike Doustdar**

> "Our industry lives and dies from intellectual property. If we could not safeguard the years of investment that we have put into these products... it really puts a question mark how much innovation will come tomorrow." — **Mike Doustdar**
