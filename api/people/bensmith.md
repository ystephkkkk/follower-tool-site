### TL;DR
Perplexity CEO Aravind Srinivas revealed that the company just crossed $1 billion in annual run rate (ARR) with 5x lower burn than its major IPO-bound competitor, driven by a capital-efficient, model-neutral "agent orchestration" strategy. To maintain this momentum, Perplexity is committing at least 10,000 GPUs to a new frontier research program while expanding into air-gapped, on-premises local AI for regulated enterprises.

### Financial Milestones & Capital Efficiency
* **Crossing $1 Billion ARR:** Srinivas announced on stage that Perplexity crossed $1 billion in annual run rate on the day of the interview, noting their cash burn to reach that milestone was 5x lower than "the company that's going to IPO this week."
* **Gross Margin Expansion:**
  * **Paid Users:** Currently at 56% gross margins on the $1 billion ARR.
  * **Blended (Including Free Users):** Flipped from -219% in Q4 2025 to 17% in Q3 2026, with expectations to climb further as Perplexity trains more of its own models.
* **In-House Infrastructure Savings:** Perplexity built its own model, compute clusters, post-training models, and inference stack for quick, fast search—currently serving 80% to 90% of its traffic and saving at least $100 million to $200 million annually.
* **Lean Headcount:** The company operates with roughly 500 employees, which Srinivas described as a "sweet spot" that provides researchers ample compute per person without requiring trillion-dollar CAPEX commitments.

### Agent Orchestration & The Frontier Research Program
* **The "Mode-Neutral" Moat:** In response to moderator Ben Smith's question about competing against hyperscalers in the agent space, Srinivas argued that Perplexity's moat is accuracy and model-neutral orchestration. Unlike hyperscalers—who are incentivized to lock users into their own clouds or equity-backed models to recoup massive CAPEX—Perplexity is strictly user-aligned.
* **10,000 GPU Commitment:** Perplexity announced a new frontier research program committing at least 10,000 GPUs to post-train models specifically for multi-agent orchestration and continual learning.
* **Open-Weight vs. Frontier Models:** 
  * Srinivas noted Perplexity's thinking on Chinese open-weight models has evolved beyond its earlier "1776" uncensored DeepSeek R1 project, shifting focus to agent sandbox security, accuracy, and reducing tool-call error rates.
  * **Benchmarking GLM vs. Opus:** Currently, post-trained GLM models (GLM 53 / GLM 31) take about 15 minutes to complete a task that Opus 55 finishes in 3 minutes, largely because GLM wastes time on erroneous tool calls.

### Local AI, On-Prem Agents & The Compute Debate
* **Air-Gapped Enterprise Security:** Perplexity is partnering with companies like NVIDIA to deploy fully local, air-gapped, on-premises agents for regulated institutions—including sovereign wealth funds, banks, credit card companies, exchanges, insurance firms, and pharma/bio companies—that cannot risk sending proprietary data or IP to frontier labs for post-training.
* **Escalation & Hybrid Frameworks:**
  * **Local-to-Cloud Escalation:** Local models can request user permission to orchestrate up to cloud frontier models when a task requires higher intelligence.
  * **Hybrid Mode:** Released on Apple Silicon, allowing users to default to frontier cloud models and orchestrate down to local hardware for sensitive data.
* **Jevons Paradox & AI Compute:** Addressing Smith's question on whether the industry is overbuilding compute, Srinivas argued that local AI will not pop the AI bubble. Instead, Jevons Paradox will take effect: lowering the cost of AI will drive massive adoption among small businesses and expand overall usage of frontier AI as a gateway.

### Media Relations & Ecosystem Integration
* **Pre-Training vs. Summarization:** Pressed by Ben Smith on media lawsuits and Perplexity's 2025 revenue-share program, Srinivas emphasized that Perplexity does not pre-train on or distill books and publisher content. Instead, it provides answers and summaries with citations, arguing that while answer engines naturally have lower click-through rates than link-based search, they create a positive flywheel of curiosity and reading.
* **Premium Connectors:** Perplexity is expanding its role as an orchestrator across all data sources—including the web, CRMs, Slack, and premium connectors like the *New England Journal of Medicine*.

### Leadership Lessons
* **Jensen Huang's Grit:** Reflecting on NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang's advice, Srinivas praised Huang's relentlessness and "day-one" paranoia—operating even the world's most valuable company as if it could go out of business in 30 days—calling him one of the greatest founders in American history.

### Predictions & Forward Calls

## Silicon Valley & The World: Brad Gerstner, Founder & CEO, Altimeter Capital | Semafor

---

**TL;DR:** Altimeter Capital CEO Brad Gerstner urged the Nvidia board to resist political pressure and take its time investigating CEO Jensen Huang, revealed Altimeter requested a firm-record $5 billion allocation in Anthropic's 10–20x oversubscribed IPO, and detailed how universal "Trump Accounts" are catalyzing tens of billions of dollars in direct philanthropy from tech leaders like Elon Musk and Michael Dell.

### Nvidia, Jensen Huang, and US-China Export Controls
* **Clarifying the Board Letter:** Gerstner stated that his Friday letter (co-written with Gavin Baker) was widely misreported—it did not call for Jensen Huang's immediate return, but rather urged the board to take whatever time it needs to "turn over the cards" and avoid a rush to judgment.
  * **Resisting Political Pressure:** Gerstner criticized external pressure from Washington, including President Trump suggesting Nvidia hire Tim Cook, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent demanding answers within 10 days, and members of Congress weighing in.
  * **Economic Stakes:** With AI driving 75% of US GDP growth this year, Gerstner emphasized that Nvidia is critical to national economic and national security.
  * **Governance & Accountability:** A corporate and securities lawyer by training, Gerstner said he takes allegations of Huang misleading the board very seriously ("nobody is above the law"). While he prefers founder-led tech companies over his 26 years of investing, it is "not at any cost."
  * **Public Petition & Trump's Remarks:** Gerstner did not sign the online petition (150,000+ signatures) calling for Huang's immediate return, and he said "pretty confidently" that Huang was not the AI executive Trump threatened with jail.
* **Legality of Chip Exports:** Gerstner drew a sharp distinction between cloud access and smuggling:
  * **Cloud Training:** Sending chips to Singapore, Malaysia, or Indonesia for Chinese firms to use via cloud computing was not illegal under existing law, though Treasury and Commerce just rushed through a new rule tightening limitations and requiring more export control licenses.
  * **Sham Companies:** Selling chips to a sham company ("mega speed") knowing they were bound for China in violation of export controls *is* illegal; if that occurred, Gerstner said Huang should not run Nvidia.
* **Administration Camps on China:** Gerstner noted Trump's administration is split between a hawkish/decoupling camp (led by Scott Bessent) and an engagement/constructivist camp (Howard Lutnick, Michael Dell, Jensen Huang, Tim Cook, Elon Musk, and Trump himself, who is meeting the Chinese president four times this year).

### AI Market Positioning and the Anthropic IPO
* **Public Market Exposure:** While Altimeter's 10-year venture and late-stage growth funds remain unchanged, Gerstner lowered public exposure in his long-only and hedge funds—first warning on CNBC on May 28 that AI was 10–20% overvalued and could see a 20% pullback, and confirming on July 2 that exposure was cut from large to small (remaining at some of its lowest levels).
* **Shift to Intelligence & Applications:** Gerstner believes the market is transitioning from a 3.5-year AI infrastructure phase into an intelligence and applications phase, with OpenAI and Anthropic (both major Altimeter holdings) as the two principal ways to play intelligence.
* **Record IPO Request:** Altimeter submitted a **$5 billion** request for the Anthropic IPO—the largest allocation request in the firm's 20-year history—noting the offering is **10 to 20 times oversubscribed**.
* **Supermajority Governance:** Gerstner defended Anthropic's structure (a single share voted by seven co-founders), arguing it shields leadership from short-term activist pressure (citing Tesla in 2019–2020) and intense political pressure on Dario Amodei and Sam Altman.
* **Navigating Political Risk:** Despite friction with the Department of War over using models for "all lawful purposes"—which led to consequences like Mythos being export-controlled—Gerstner noted bipartisan recognition of the US's two national champion labs and pointed out that Amodei still dines with the President at the White House.

### Invest America ("Trump Accounts") and Direct Philanthropy
* **Bipartisan Momentum:** Gerstner, the principal architect of Invest America (now called Trump Accounts), said the program enjoys 85–90% popularity across both parties. Since election night, he has spoken with 25 Democrats—including Bill Clinton, Barack Obama, Mark Warner, Cory Booker, and Hakeem Jeffries—who support the concept.
* **Core Mechanics:** Every day, 10,000 babies born in America receive an account seeded with **$1,000** in the S&P 500 from the federal government, creating a "universal capital fabric" comparable to Social Security.
* **Corporate & Philanthropic Scale ("Giving Pledge 2.0"):** Using a simple one-page donor agreement with the US Treasury, corporations and philanthropists are wiring funds directly to children's accounts:
  * **Altimeter Capital:** Added $1,000 to the accounts of all 85 kids of its employees (on a look-through basis).
  * **Brad Gerstner:** Adopted all children under age five in his home state of Indiana.
  * **Michael & Susan Dell:** Committed **$6.25 billion** to **25 million kids**.
  * **Anthropic:** Committed 5% of its public benefit plan (on a $2 trillion valuation, **$100 billion**) to charities addressing AI downsides and citizen accounts.
  * **SpaceX & Elon Musk:** Gwynne Shotwell already gave **2 million shares** to **2 million kids** ages 10–18. Catalyzed by Anthropic's pledge, Elon Musk announced he will give **one share of SpaceX** to every child in America (**70 million kids**), an **$11.5 billion** gift.

### Predictions & Forward Calls

* **Long-Term Commitment:** "Michael Dell and I are going to commit the next decade of our life to this [Invest America / Trump Accounts]." — Brad Gerstner

## Social Media Broke The Internet. Can Reddit Save It? | Mixed Signals

- Channel: Semafor
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ExTgRBzqu2g
- Published: 2026-07-03
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260703.html#video-ExTgRBzqu2g
- Speakers: Steve Huffman (Co-founder and CEO, Reddit); Max Tani (Host, Mixed Signals); Ben Smith (Host, Mixed Signals)

---

**TL;DR**  
Reddit CEO Steve Huffman outlines the platform’s transformation from a niche, hands-off forum into a major public company, detailing its commercial battles with AI firms, its firm stance against the "influencer economy," and why maintaining pseudonymity is crucial to preserving human authenticity on the modern internet.

---

### AI Data Licensing and the Battle Against "Laundering"

*   **Training Set Dominance:** **High LLM reliance on Reddit:** Huffman notes that Reddit content constitutes a massive portion of LLM training data, historically accounting for roughly one-third of OpenAI’s GPT training sets. 
*   **Commercial Demands:** **Shifting from free to paid access:** While Reddit remains supportive of academic research and public-good data sharing, Huffman states that commercial use now requires strict commercial terms. The company has established partnerships with Google and OpenAI to prevent platforms from "laundering" Reddit’s data to enrich themselves.
*   **Targeted Litigation:** **Suing Anthropic:** Reddit is actively suing AI firm Anthropic for scraping its data without permission.
*   **Preventing AI Monopolies:** **Fostering a competitive ecosystem:** Huffman prefers licensing data to multiple players rather than establishing an exclusive relationship with one firm, arguing that a single dominant AI or search winner would be fundamentally bad for the internet.

### The Evolution of Safety, Moderation, and "Spez"

*   **From No Policies to Brand Safety:** **Building an ad-safe platform:** For its first decade, Reddit had no dedicated safety team or policies. Over the last several years, the platform built rigorous safety frameworks, successfully transforming brand safety from a major advertiser concern into a core business asset.
*   **The "Spez" Moderation Standard:** **Allowing personal criticism:** As CEO, Huffman (username u/Spez) is frequently the target of community anger. Huffman instructs his safety team to take a light touch regarding insults directed at him—such as the ubiquitous "F*** Spez" memes—to avoid creating an empty website and a censorship loop, provided the content does not cross into severe harassment.
*   **Handling the API Backlash:** **Extracting truth from outrage:** Reflecting on the intense moderator protests following Reddit's API pricing changes three years ago, Huffman emphasizes the importance of looking past emotional delivery to find the "rational truth" in user anger to help guide platform decisions.

### Growth, Changing Demographics, and the "Anti-Influencer" Model

*   **Demographic Broadening:** **Expanding past the gaming niche:** Reddit’s user base has transitioned from a narrow demographic of young, male gamers (who tolerated a historically terrible UI) to a diverse, gender-balanced population ranging in age up to 80 years old. 
*   **The Anti-Influencer Ethos:** **Preventing corrupted incentives:** Unlike YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram, Reddit is designed so that users cannot get rich or famous directly through the platform. Huffman believes this structural limitation keeps the user experience authentic.
*   **Rejecting the Social Graph:** **Prioritizing user privacy:** Reddit deliberately chose not to build a "social graph" (connecting real-world friends and family). Huffman argues that knowing what your friends are doing—or having them know what you are doing—would break Reddit's core utility.
*   **Three Chapters of Reddit:** **The platform's historical identity:** 
    *   *Chapter 1:* Not traditional media (users decide the news, not editors).
    *   *Chapter 2:* Not social media (no social graphs or influencers).
    *   *Chapter 3:* Not AI (remaining messy, human, and authentic while the rest of the web becomes automated).

### Navigating AI Slop and Protecting Pseudonymity

*   **The Community Immune System:** **Downvoting AI content:** To combat AI-generated "slop," Reddit relies on its technological detection tools alongside the community's natural aversion to AI text. Users quickly downvote and reject unhelpful, obviously AI-generated write-ups.
*   **Verifying Humanness over Identity:** **Rejecting government IDs:** Huffman strongly rejects real-name identity verification, noting that government IDs are easily bypassed. Instead, Reddit focuses on verifying "humanness" via passkeys, phone numbers, or decentralized technologies (such as Worldcoin's concept).
*   **Preserving Pseudonymity:** **Enabling honest discussions:** Pseudonymity remains critical to Reddit's value, allowing users to speak honestly about sensitive topics like medical maladies or addiction without real-world professional or personal repercussions.
