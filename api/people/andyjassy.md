## Amazon CEO Andy Jassy on the Infrastructure the AI Era Needs

- Channel: a16z
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-YMMRQ1qscI

- Speakers: Andy Jassy (CEO, Amazon); Ben Horowitz (Co-founder, a16z); Erik Torenberg (Host, a16z)

---

### TL;DR
Amazon CEO Andy Jassy explains how massive AI demand is driving the company's unprecedented $220 billion 2026 CapEx buildout, why lowering the cost of inference is critical for broad enterprise adoption, and how generative AI is fundamentally reshaping Amazon's internal development and product lineup.

### Amazon's AI Infrastructure & Capacity Demand
* **Massive CapEx Scale:** Amazon expects to spend roughly $220 billion in CapEx in 2026, a staggering sum that reflects the unprecedented pace of infrastructure expansion.
* **Sold-Out Capacity:** **Andy Jassy** notes that 2026 infrastructure capacity is sold out, 2027 is largely sold out, and a good chunk of 2028 is already committed.
* **The Cash Cycle & Visibility:** **Andy Jassy** explains that data centers require capital investment roughly two years in advance with a 30-year useful life, while server and networking gear are purchased just months in advance based on clear demand visibility with an average 5-year deal structure.
* **The Barbell Demand:** **Andy Jassy** describes AI demand as a barbell, noting that while massive frontier model labs are buying up massive capacity, mainstream enterprise production apps are still waiting for lower inference costs to scale broadly.

### Custom Silicon & Annapurna Labs
* **Acquisition Genesis:** **Andy Jassy** highlights the 2015 acquisition of Annapurna Labs—initially intended to offload virtualization, networking, security, and storage onto NICs to free up 100% of EC2 servers for compute—as one of Amazon's best-ever acquisitions.
* **Graviton Success:** **Andy Jassy** shares that 99% of Amazon's top 1,000 EC2 customers use Graviton CPUs, which deliver about 40% better price performance than comparable X86 processors.
* **Trainium & Inference Costs:** **Andy Jassy** explains that Annapurna's Trainium AI accelerators provide 30% to 40% better price performance, aiming to drive down the cost of inference, which he believes is currently an order of magnitude too expensive for pervasive enterprise rewriting of CPU applications.

### High-Profile Investments & Partnerships
* **Strategic Investments:** **Andy Jassy** addresses Wall Street's skepticism over Amazon's large investments in Anthropic ($8 billion) and OpenAI, framing them as key partnerships with two of the most successful category-defining companies.
* **Ecosystem Integration:** **Andy Jassy** notes that Anthropic runs extensively on Bedrock and builds on Trainium, while partnerships with OpenAI allow customers to run OpenAI models on Bedrock via a unique stateful runtime environment designed for agentic workflows.

### AI-Driven Organizational & Product Transformation
* **Radical Productivity Gains:** **Andy Jassy** cites the Bedrock team rebuilding their inference engine with just 6 engineers in 76 days (a task that previously would have taken 40 people a year), proving that AI enables drastically smaller, cross-functional teams.
* **Workforce Shift:** **Andy Jassy** predicts that organizations will employ fewer humans for routine coding, customer service, business intelligence, and legal work, shifting toward smaller, highly agile teams starting from much more advanced stages.
* **Alexa & Core Reinvention:** **Andy Jassy** explains that Alexa's brains are being completely rewired from scratch with generative AI, and similar deep reinventions are underway across Prime Video, retail, Fire TV, and music rather than relying on incremental updates.

### Cybersecurity Realities & Future Skills
* **Immediate Cybersecurity Threats:** **Andy Jassy** warns that while media focuses on fantastical sci-fi AI risks, the immediate danger is cybersecurity—models uncovering vast new vulnerabilities that require applications to deploy patches in minutes rather than months.
* **Education & Critical Thinking:** **Andy Jassy** advises young people (including his own children) to embrace AI tools rather than fight them, but stresses that human judgment, ingenuity, critical reasoning, and strategic thinking remain essential for synthesis and invention.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Andy Jassy** predicts that supply constraints for various data center and chip components will persist for several years.
* **Andy Jassy** predicts that companies will have to rewrite almost all of their code to be agentically capable and capable of responding to security threats in minutes.
* **Andy Jassy** predicts that organizations in the future will employ fewer humans doing traditional tasks like coding, legal work, and research, shifting to smaller cross-functional teams.

## AI Will Replace Some Jobs, But Create New Ones Too, Says Amazon CEO Andy Jassy | Exclusive

- Channel: CNBC-TV18
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MduXTRc1Y_g
- Published: 2026-06-28
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260628.html#video-MduXTRc1Y_g
- Speakers: Andy Jassy (CEO, Amazon)

---

**TL;DR** 
Amazon is expanding its investment commitment in India by adding $13 billion to its 2030 target (bringing the total future commitment to $48 billion), primarily to fund AWS cloud and AI infrastructure. CEO Andy Jassy highlighted the rapid, global-first expansion of India's "Amazon Now" quick commerce playbook and maintained that massive AI capital expenditures will yield substantial, long-term free cash flow despite short-term headwinds.

---

### Escalating India Investments & AWS AI Infrastructure
* **Expanded Capital Commitment:** Amazon has invested approximately $40 billion in India since 2010. It is now raising its 2030 future investment target by $13 billion—bringing the total additional commitment to $48 billion by 2030.
* **AWS & AI Allocation:** This incremental $13 billion will be directed almost entirely toward AWS, cloud services, and artificial intelligence capabilities to capture India's rapid AI growth.
* **Local Capacity Building:** AWS is deploying significant amounts of GPUs and custom AI accelerators in India to serve local startups, developers, enterprises, and government entities.

### AI Monetization, CapEx Cycles, and Custom Silicon
* **Unprecedented Growth Run Rate:** During the first three years of the current generative AI wave, AWS has reached a $15 billion annual run rate. This is 260 times larger than AWS's run rate ($57 million) during its first three years of operation 20 years ago.
* **Supply Constraints:** AWS grew 28% year-over-year on a $150 billion annual revenue run rate, but demand is so high that Amazon is still "dropping demand on the floor" due to supply constraints.
* **The Cash Cycle Dynamics:** Massive upfront capital expenditures for data centers, hardware, and networking gear will temporarily impact free cash flow. Jassy expects revenue growth to outpace CapEx growth in a few years, resulting in strong return on invested capital (ROIC) and free cash flow.
* **Reducing Inference Costs:** To make enterprise AI economically viable, Amazon is aggressively lowering inference costs. 
    * **Trainium Silicon:** Amazon's custom chips provide roughly 30% more price performance than comparable market solutions.
    * **Amazon Bedrock:** This inference service is designed to host frontier models cost-effectively and at high performance.

### The India Quick Commerce Playbook & Global Replication
* **Hyper-Local Delivery Expansion:** Amazon is expanding its "Amazon Now" quick commerce service to over 300 cities in India. 
* **Customer Behavior Shift:** Prime members who try quick commerce buy products three times more frequently than Prime members who do not.
* **The Global Playbook:** The quick commerce model, pioneered and incubated by Amazon’s team in India, is currently being trialed and expanded in the United States and Europe.
* **Back-End Optimization:** Quick commerce is powered by hyper-local micro-fulfillment centers. In a live test during his visit, Jassy received an order in under four minutes.
* **Employee Social Initiatives:** Amazon is scaling local social programs alongside this rapid expansion, including:
    * **Sambhav:** Academic scholarships and health insurance for employees' children.
    * **Ashray:** Mobile, air-conditioned relief shelters where delivery drivers can rest, charge phones, and access restrooms.

### Geopolitical Sovereignty, Trade, and the Future of Labor
* **Aggressive Export Targets:** Amazon is utilizing its network of 1.7 million Indian third-party selling partners to target $80 billion in exports by 2030, having already surpassed $20 billion.
* **Data Sovereignty Architecture:** To ease government concerns over data privacy, AWS ensures physical infrastructure is bound by local laws. Customers can fully encrypt data in transit and at rest, retaining exclusive ownership of encryption keys.
* **Labor Market Dislocation vs. Job Creation:** Jassy acknowledged that AI will automate certain repetitive tasks in coding and customer service, but argues it will create entirely new categories of employment. 
    * **Subject Matter Experts:** Amazon is heavily hiring legal, financial, and medical experts to train frontier AI models—roles that did not exist a few years ago.
