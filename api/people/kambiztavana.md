### TL;DR
US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent detailed the ongoing "Operation Economic Outcast" against Iran, predicting a 25%+ contraction in Iran's economy by March 2027 and advising Iranian citizens to hoard fuel and withhold taxes. Bessent stated that the US naval blockade is airtight, regional financial nodes are being systematically severed, and the Trump administration remains resolute regardless of US midterm elections.

### Campaign Status & Economic Impact
- **Operation Outcast Progress:** Launched roughly 8 weeks ago as an escalation of the maximum pressure campaign initiated in March 2025.
- **Inflation & Currency:** Inflation has surpassed 100%, and the regime has reportedly stopped publishing the true value of the rial, which Bessent estimates is trading around 3 million to the dollar.
- **Global Compliance:** 
  - Several Gulf countries have severed financial ties.
  - Mahan Air has halted all flights except to Russia and China; other airlines defying restrictions face imminent shutdown by Treasury.
- **Humanitarian Channels:** 
  - Treasury maintains open humanitarian channels for food and medicine, but Bessent noted the regime is too broke to pay for international imports due to lack of dollars.
  - The central bank continues printing money, driving inflation while failing to pay government salaries.

### Cracking Down on International Financial Networks
- **Targeting Trustees & Brokers:** 
  - Treasury is targeting trade and financial facilitators operating from Dubai to Hong Kong.
  - Many trustees are refusing to return funds to Tehran, viewing the regime as a bankrupt entity.
- **International Banking Interventions:** 
  - Treasury has subpoenaed a Greek bank, sent information requests to six European banks, and targeted institutions across Pakistan, Dubai, Egypt, Turkey, and Russia.
  - Specific actions include shutting Dubai branches of an Egyptian bank, closing a Turkish bank, and targeting VTB (the second-largest Russian bank) for aiding the regime.
- **Cryptocurrency & Asset Tracking:** 
  - The regime's use of digital currencies is viewed as an act of desperation.
  - Treasury is tracking and escrowing illicit crypto and luxury assets (such as expensive apartments) to recover funds intended for the Iranian people.
- **Whistleblower Rewards:** 
  - Treasury offers a whistleblower program where citizens, diaspora members, and embassy employees can claim up to 30% of recovered regime funds by reporting illicit channels via the Treasury website.
  - Sanitization and un-sanctioning are offered to brokers who come forward and cooperate.

### Naval Blockade & Energy Sector Collapse
- **Strait of Hormuz Control:** 
  - The US Navy controls the strait, refuting regime claims. 
  - Energy is flowing out nightly at rates exceeding pre-war levels.
- **Oil & Gasoline Shortages:** 
  - Iran has effectively "no oil on the water" and zero loadings at Kharg Island.
  - Domestic gasoline production is faltering in a country with the world's third-largest energy assets.
- **Military Neutralization:** 
  - Iran's navy and air force have been neutralized.
  - Substantial missile and drone stockpiles were destroyed, alongside 85% of Iranian missile factories.

### Political Isolation & Regime Dynamics
- **Internal Fractures:** 
  - Economic pressure is causing visible infighting among politicians, the IRGC, and the Supreme Leader.
  - Public figures (such as President, Speaker Ghalibaf, and the head of the central bank) have issued economic warnings over the summer, followed by some IRGC members.
- **Leadership Isolation:** 
  - Bessent characterized IRGC commanders as "rats in a sewer pipe" hiding in deep locations and remaining blind to the suffering of ordinary Iranians.
- **Midterm Election Calculus:** 
  - Bessent dismissed regime hopes that upcoming US midterm elections would shift Washington's calculus, stating President Trump's resolute blockade and policies remain entirely unchanged.

### Predictions & Forward Calls

- **Campaign Intensity:** Scott Bessent claims that the next eight weeks of Operation Economic Outcast will become "even more intense."
