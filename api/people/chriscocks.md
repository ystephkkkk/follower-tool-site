### TL;DR
Hasbro CEO Chris Cocks explains how the 160+ year-old toy maker transformed into an IP powerhouse where 65% of sales go to consumers 13 and older, driven by massive fan communities like *Magic: The Gathering* and innovative uses of AI like Character OS.

### The Evolution of Toys and the Rise of Adult Fandom
* **Demographic Shift:** The majority of toys and games are now sold to households without kids, with **65%** of all Hasbro sales going to consumers aged **13 and older** (most being **18+**).
* **Community-Centric Marketing:** 
  * **Community Building:** Marketing has shifted away from traditional 30-second ad spots toward iterative, collaborative community engagement across social chatter, pre-orders, and influencer feedback.
  * **Fandom Connection:** Properties like *Star Wars*, *My Little Pony*, and hobby-store communities provide adults with a sense of belonging and tribe connection.
* **Expanding Traditional Brands:**
  * **Blooms for Play-Doh:** A new product line allowing adults to build flowers and plants out of Play-Doh.
  * **My Little Pony Relaunch:** Scheduled for next year with a bold new look targeting the 18–35 demographic who loved it in the 1980s/90s.

### The *Magic: The Gathering* Juggernaut and Licensing Economics
* **Explosive Growth:** When Chris Cocks started as president of Wizards of the Coast in **2016**, *Magic* was a **$300 million** business; it is projected to be north of **$2 billion** in revenue this year.
* **Trading Card Dominance:** Trading card games have grown to become the biggest overall category in the toy and game industry, outpacing building blocks.
* **The Licensing Flywheel:** 
  * Hasbro’s combined licensing business approaches **$700 million** a year, part of a **$17 to $18 billion** total merchandising universe.
  * **Margin Advantage:** Licensing carries operating profit margins of **40% to 50%**, compared to **5 to 15%** for typical toy businesses.
  * **Licensing Growth:** The licensing business is up nearly **60%** over the last five years.
* **Retail Strategy:** **60% to 70%** of *Magic* sales flow through mom-and-pop hobby shops in the Wizards Play Network (WPN), avoiding heavy reliance on live social selling platforms like Whatnot or TikTok.

### AI Integration and Character OS
* **Character OS Platform:** Developed by Hasbro's AI studio **Six Wall**, this talent management platform brings characters to life with lifelike expressions for licensing out to voice platforms (like **11 Labs**), theme park animatronics, and customer service bots.
* **Named Voice Collaborations:** 
  * **Mr. Potato Head:** Utilizes early recordings from **Don Rickles' estate** to give the character a distinctly adult edge.
  * **Trivial Pursuit:** **LeVar Burton** is onboarded as the host of *Trivial Pursuit* on the platform.
* **Workflow Acceleration:** Generative AI tools (like Claude and ChatGPT) reduce the time required to get a 3D prototype from **3 or 4 months down to 3 or 4 days**, massively expanding internal concept generation.

### Hollywood, Cinematic Universes, and the Vault
* **Historical Firsts:** Hasbro produced the first-ever toy-to-movie translation with *Clue* in **1984**.
* **Current Pipeline:** Hasbro currently has about **65 projects** in development with roughly **a dozen different studios**, including a *Monopoly* movie executive produced by **Margot Robbie**, D&D TV series at Netflix (with **Sean Levy**), a *Magic* movie at Legendary, and a *Magic* animated series coming to Netflix next year.
* **Deep Catalog Strategy:** Hasbro manages **1,900 brands**, actively resting and reviving vault properties like *My Little Pony*, *Littlest Pet Shop*, *Jem and the Holograms*, *Mask*, and *G.I. Joe*.
