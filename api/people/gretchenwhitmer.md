## JPMorgan, Ford to Invest Billions in Michigan Manufacturing

### TL;DR
JPMorgan Chase, Ford Motor Company, and Michigan Governor Gretchen Whitmer launched "Michigan Lift," an industrial innovation program backed by a $1 billion purchasing commitment from Ford to onshore advanced manufacturing and supply chains.

### Michigan Lift Initiative & Industrial Onshoring
* **Program Overview:** Launched in Detroit at Michigan Central, "Michigan Lift" aims to fix vulnerabilities caused by past offshoring by connecting local innovators with capital and customers.
* **Ford's Commitment:** **Ford Motor Co.** CEO Jim Farley committed $1 billion in purchasing for new companies building advanced manufacturing capabilities, rare earth magnets, and aluminum supply chain components.
* **Cross-Sector Collaboration:** **JPMorgan CEO Jamie Dimon** emphasized that rebuilding domestic manufacturing requires cooperation between business, state government, and financial institutions to provide reliable orders and production scale.
* **Geographic Scope:** While primarily a Michigan-focused initiative leveraging the state's engineering talent and industrial DNA, leaders noted that trusted allies like Canadian suppliers heavily integrated into the auto industry can participate.

### U.S. Economic Preeminence & Global Trade
* **National Security & Economy:** **Jamie Dimon** argued in a *Wall Street Journal* op-ed that a dominant military is predicated on having the world's best economy and the U.S. dollar as the reserve currency.
* **Alliances & Trade:** Dimon advocated for strengthening trade relationships with Western allies (Europe, UK, Japan, Australia, Mexico, Canada, South Korea), noting they comprise 60% of global GDP and share 40 military alliances and 20 free trade agreements.
* **Capital Market Advantages:** Dimon contrasted the vibrant, multi-layered U.S. capital markets (venture capital, private equity, debt, and preferred markets) with stagnant European markets that lack comparable risk-taking capital and R&D funding.

### Skilled Trades Deficit & Education
* **The Skilled Labor Crisis:** **Jim Farley** stated the U.S. faces a crisis with 1.7 million open skilled trade jobs, noting that high school and college advisers overwhelmingly recommend four-year degrees while only 2% recommend trade schools.
* **Ford's Fact-Finding Report:** Ford is publishing its first report on the facts surrounding the U.S. skilled trade deficit to raise awareness and help solve national supply chain resilience.
* **State Workforce Efforts:** **Governor Gretchen Whitmer** highlighted Michigan's investments in upskilling, including free community college, accessible apprenticeships, and a workforce ranked number four in the country by *Site Selector* magazine, supported by the highest per-capita concentration of engineers in the nation.

### Macroeconomic Pressures & Capital Allocation
* **Inflation and Interest Rates:** **Jamie Dimon** acknowledged that higher interest costs and rising bond yields hit small and medium-sized businesses disproportionately, but argued that long-term national safety and getting inflation down transcend short-term market fluctuations.
* **Crowding Out Capital:** Addressing capital availability amid massive AI investments, Dimon stated that capital crowding out is primarily driven by governments around the world borrowing record, inflationary sums of money rather than private productive investments.
* **Security & Resiliency Initiative:** Dimon noted that JPMorgan's broader security and resiliency initiative (approaching its one-year anniversary of a targeted $1.5 trillion goal) has already invested $5 billion directly into pre-public companies in sectors like drones, nuclear power, rare earths, and semiconductors.

### Automotive Competitiveness & Innovation
* **Global Speed and Competition:** **Jim Farley** addressed the intense global competition from Chinese automakers moving at "light speed," stating that Ford developed a small U.S. skunkworks team in California and Michigan to out-innovate rivals.
* **New EV Development:** Ford is engineering a new $30,000 electric vehicle—built in Louisville, Kentucky, and engineered in California and Michigan—designed to be fully competitive with global low-cost producers like BYD.

### TL;DR
JPMorgan Chase, Ford, and the State of Michigan have launched **Michigan LIFT** (Launchpad for Industrial Innovation and Transformation) to onshore advanced manufacturing supply chains, secure critical components, and boost domestic industrial independence.

### Michigan LIFT Initiative & Supply Chain Onshoring
* **Program Purpose:** **Jim Farley** explained that Michigan LIFT is designed to fix supply chain vulnerabilities by onshoring capabilities—such as advanced manufacturing, new technology, magnets, and rare earth elements—that were previously offshored for lower costs.
* **Ford's Commitment:** **Jim Farley** stated that Ford is committing $1 billion in purchasing power for new Michigan-based companies building advanced manufacturing capabilities.
* **Role of Startups & Small Businesses:** **Jamie Dimon** emphasized that innovative startups need actual orders and production-scale plants (not just experimental ones) to succeed, making initiatives like LIFT essential for bridging that gap.
* **Cross-Border Partnerships:** **Governor Gretchen Whitmer** acknowledged Canada as a vital ally and noted that Canadian companies in the industrial and aluminum supply chains can apply to the program, highlighting that all Ford truck aluminum ingots cross the Detroit border.

### Skilled Trades & Workforce Development
* **The Trades Deficit:** **Jim Farley** characterized the U.S. skilled trades deficit as a "crisis" with 1.7 million open skilled trade jobs, noting we are currently in the "second inning" of addressing it. 
* **Educational Bias:** **Jim Farley** pointed out that high school advisors recommend 4-year degrees while only 2% recommend trade schools, despite high-quality trade schools boasting nearly 100% graduation rates.
* **State Workforce Rankings:** **Governor Gretchen Whitmer** highlighted that Michigan is ranked #4 in the country for workforce efforts by *Site Selector Magazine* and noted the state has the highest concentration of engineers per capita in the country.
* **Debt-Free Training:** **Governor Gretchen Whitmer** detailed Michigan's investments in free community college and accessible apprenticeships that allow workers to earn a good living without carrying student debt—jobs she noted will not be replaced by AI.

### National Economic Resilience & Capital Markets
* **Connection to Military Power:** **Jamie Dimon** argued that a powerful military is predicated on having a pre-eminent U.S. economy, which anchors the dollar as the global reserve currency.
* **JP Morgan's Broader Initiative:** **Jamie Dimon** reported that the one-year anniversary of JPMorgan's security and resiliency initiative is approaching, noting the firm has invested $5 billion directly into non-venture, pre-public companies focused on security and resiliency.
* **Advantage of U.S. Capital Markets:** **Jamie Dimon** contrasted vibrant U.S. venture capital, private equity, debt markets, and risk-taking capital with a stagnated Europe, while identifying government borrowing—not AI—as the primary source of capital crowding out.
* **Global Competition:** **Jim Farley** discussed the intense speed of global competition, specifically noting that Ford developed a small group in the U.S. and California to out-innovate competitors and is rolling out a $30,000 vehicle engineered in California and Michigan and built in Louisville, Kentucky.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Jamie Dimon** predicted that JPMorgan may exceed its initial $1.5 trillion goal for its security and resiliency initiative by its one-year anniversary.
* **Jim Farley** claimed that Ford's upcoming $30,000 vehicle—engineered in California and Michigan and built in Louisville, Kentucky—will be fully competitive with Chinese EV giant BYD.
