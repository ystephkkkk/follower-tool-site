## Citi CEO Jane Fraser Talks Outages, Banking Industry | Bloomberg Talks

- Channel: Bloomberg Podcasts
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hhoLKzR08Ak
- Published: 2026-11-18
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20261118.html#video-hhoLKzR08Ak
- Speakers: Jane Fraser (CEO, Citigroup); Lisa Abramowicz (Host, Bloomberg Talks); Elizabeth Warren (Senator)

---

### TL;DR
Speaking with Bloomberg’s Lisa Abramowicz in Washington, D.C., Citigroup CEO Jane Fraser attributed recent high-profile tech outages to an unprecedented 5x to 10x surge in simultaneous tech stack patches, while arguing that the record-breaking Anthropic IPO signals a healthy 2027 capital markets pipeline. Fraser also urged incoming Senate Banking Committee leadership to judge Citi by its simplified five-business structure and warned that potential interest rate hikes may not solve supply-driven inflation in diesel and housing.

### Capital Markets, IPOs, and the AI Ecosystem
* **Anthropic IPO Barometer:** The world’s largest IPO just priced (ahead of its market debut tomorrow) with strong institutional, retail, North American, and international demand. Fraser views it as a positive barometer for both capital markets and the AI ecosystem.
* **2027 Pipeline Outlook:** Because year-end markets are typically thin, Fraser expects the Anthropic deal to primarily catalyze the 2027 IPO pipeline rather than 2026, providing a boost to a tech IPO market that has been quiet since SpaceX.
* **Global AI Demand & Financing Risk:** Following better-than-expected Nvidia earnings and strong forward guidance, Fraser noted that compute demand is global (citing Turkey building its first data center to remain competitive). However, Citigroup flagged AI financing risk as a key uncertainty in its Q3 earnings for the first time, noting that the current "picks and shovels" phase (energy, chips, data centers, frontier models) is capital-intensive and will inevitably produce both winners and losers.
* **The US Capital Markets Advantage:** Dismissing ECB warnings of a systemic, subprime-style AI bubble as "jealousy," Fraser highlighted the US's unique ability to pair innovation with deep capital markets—holding **4%** of the world’s population but over **50%** of global market capitalization. She noted that top French and UK entrepreneurs frequently relocate to the US West Coast to scale.
* **Community "Win-Wins":** Following an election centered on affordability three months ago, Fraser observed that tech firms are increasingly recognizing the need to share benefits with local communities—such as protecting local energy pricing, funding free school lunches, and raising teacher salaries—to maintain public trust.

### Tech Outages, Cybersecurity, and AI at Citi
* **Surge in Tech Outages:** Addressing recent outages at the FAA, Apple, and JPMorgan, Fraser said she is not surprised given that the volume of patches and upgrades moving through the global tech stack is **5 to 10-fold** higher than ever before—a wave anticipated since powerful AI models emerged six months ago.
* **Payment System Resiliency:** While parts of the payment system could experience temporary downtime during upgrades, banks have spent years building recovery plans to get systems back online quickly. 
* **Private Sector Cyber Collaboration:** Fraser emphasized that banks do not compete on cybersecurity ("we will compete on the death for pitching the Anthropic IPO," she joked). She spent the morning meeting with smaller banks to share cyber-hardening and downtime-recovery strategies.
* **Citi’s Internal AI Deployment:** AI is currently a mix of revenue generation, cost savings (notably driving a big productivity uplift in coding), and defensive spending on cyber and fraud protection. Fraser declined to predict whether Citi’s headcount will shrink in five years, noting only that the workforce composition will change.
* **No AI Agents in Bank Accounts:** Fraser flatly rejected allowing personal AI agents (such as "Muse") to access Citi customer bank accounts, stating the necessary risk and control frameworks do not yet exist to prevent unauthorized transactions.

### Geopolitics, Regulation, and Bank Consolidation
* **US-China Engagement:** Fraser recently joined President Trump’s delegation to China. As Trump and Xi Jinping meet, she noted an encouraging focus on stabilizing the relationship and maintaining de-escalation channels despite clear areas of competition.
* **Keeping Multinationals on "US Rails":** Citi serves **70%** of the Fortune 500 operating in China (handling FX, cash management, trade, and payroll). Fraser argued that keeping this activity on US banking rails is vital for American economic security.
* **Response to Senator Elizabeth Warren:** With Senator Warren likely to chair the Senate Banking Committee next year—and having warned Citi against acquisitions in July—Fraser asked Warren to judge Citi on its current state rather than its 1985–2007 history. Citi has simplified into **five business lines**, made massive investments in risk, controls, and infrastructure, and wants to collaborate constructively.
* **Healthy Bank Consolidation:** With over **4,000 banks** in the US, Fraser believes some consolidation is healthy given the high cost of cyber, fraud, and tech investments, though Citi itself is focused on organic growth.

### Macroeconomic Outlook & Risks
* **Uneven Economic Performance:** The US labor market remains stable ("low fire, low hire," near full employment) with strong consumer spending. Lower-income households are flat rather than declining, resembling an "alligator mouth" rather than a K-shaped recovery.
* **Inflation & Rate Hike Concerns:** Questioned on whether the economy can handle a **50 basis point** rate hike next month, Fraser expressed concern that higher rates will not fix the root causes of current inflation: diesel prices (refineries take years to build) and housing supply (the US needs **7 million** more affordable housing units). 
* **Converging Tail Risks:** While a slowdown is not imminent, tail risks are larger than before. Fraser noted that the biggest danger is if these risks converge and feed off each other, though she welcomed "some of the air coming out of the tires" in a market previously priced to perfection.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **2027 IPO Pipeline:** The Anthropic IPO will serve as positive momentum for the 2027 IPO market rather than 2026, given thin year-end calendars — *Jane Fraser*
* **Continued Tech Outages:** Expect more temporary downtime and outages across different sectors for a while as companies continue upgrading tech stacks and patching vulnerabilities — *Jane Fraser*
* **Anthropic Debut:** Anthropic is set to have a "very successful IPO" when it begins trading tomorrow — *Jane Fraser*

## Qatar, Citi and PIMCO on Capital Discipline and Resilience

- Channel: Bloomberg Live
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HcMDY6mwYNk
- Published: 2026-09-20
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260920.html#video-HcMDY6mwYNk
- Speakers: Ali bin Ahmed Al-Kuwari (Minister of Finance, Qatar); Jane Fraser (Chair & CEO, Citi); Emmanuel Roman (CEO, PIMCO)

---

**TL;DR:** Leaders from Qatar, Citi, and PIMCO discuss navigating the economic shocks of the regional conflict that began in February 2026, highlighting Qatar's fiscal discipline, a global surge in AI-driven energy demand, and structural shifts in international trade and capital allocation.

### Qatar’s Fiscal Resilience and Economic Strategy
* **Financial Impact of the Conflict:** 
  * **H.E. Ali bin Ahmed Al-Kuwari:** Noted that halted oil and gas exports (representing nearly 70% of revenues) caused a first-half budget deficit of **$36 billion** and a Q1 GDP contraction of **7%** (with oil and gas down **25%**, though non-oil grew **3.5%**).
* **Fiscal Tightening and Debt Management:**
  * **Operational Efficiency:** Second-quarter expenditures were reduced by **25%** compared to the same period last year through operational tightening.
  * **Debt Reduction:** Despite the crisis, Qatar settled **$5.5 billion** in debt, including a **$3 million** private placement. Debt-to-GDP was successfully reduced from **70%** post-COVID to **42%**.
  * **Credit Ratings:** Qatar's credit ratings were affirmed by all three major rating agencies, maintaining a **double-A** status.
* **Sovereign Investment Restructuring:**
  * **Mandate Split:** The Qatar Investment Authority (QIA) is splitting its focus: QIA will continue international investments and diversification, while a newly established platform will focus on the local economy, startups, new sectors, and the National Development Strategy 3 (expiring in **2030**).

### Global Energy Pressures, AI Infrastructure, and Capital Demands
* **Energy Bottlenecks and Inflationary Risks:**
  * **Jane Fraser:** Highlighted that rising diesel and refined product prices, compounded by Ukrainian actions reducing Russian capacity, point toward a bumpier road for inflation.
  * **Emmanuel Roman:** Noted that AI compute demand has shifted the primary technology bottleneck from chips into energy, creating real, multi-year global demand.
* **AI Financing and Structural Shifts:**
  * **PIMCO Deals:** Emmanuel Roman highlighted PIMCO’s involvement in massive data center financing deals, including with Oracle in Michigan and Meta in Louisiana.
  * **Exporting Computational Power:** H.E. Ali bin Ahmed Al-Kuwari emphasized that Qatar views AI as a way to diversify by exporting computational power rather than solely oil and gas, anchored by national AI champion **‘Call’**.
* **Corporate Risk and Regulation:**
  * **Jane Fraser:** Described running three simultaneous races at Citi: offense (applying AI to coding and revenue generation), defense (patching infrastructure against advanced threats like the *mythos* model), and managing security perimeters against AI-enabled cyberattacks.
  * **H.E. Ali bin Ahmed Al-Kuwari:** Stressed the urgent need for AI regulation to prevent severe risks, noting recent AI-enabled cyberattacks in London.

### Sovereign Debt, Fiscal Prudence, and Global Divergence
* **Developed Market Fiscal Pressures:**
  * **Emmanuel Roman:** Pointed to looming defining election and bond market cycles in Europe (notably **2027 in France**) and heavy debt loads in the UK and Europe, contrasting them favorably with growth opportunities in the Middle East and Far East.
  * **Jane Fraser:** Noted that while there is marginal diversification away from U.S. assets as global fragmentation increases, the U.S. dollar and Treasuries still lack strong alternatives. She also highlighted the growing global competitiveness of Chinese companies.
* **Fiscal Philosophy:**
  * **H.E. Ali bin Ahmed Al-Kuwari:** Argued that fiscal discipline must be the absolute #1 rule for any government to avoid being crushed by debt-servicing costs during periods of high inflation and interest rates.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **H.E. Ali bin Ahmed Al-Kuwari:** Qatar’s economic contraction will be a V-shaped recovery, compensating for all current GDP losses in **next year**, and positioning Qatar as the fastest-growing economy in the region for the **following 50 years**.
* **Emmanuel Roman:** The US 10-year Treasury yield will experience a release and rates will go down once a better outcome or deal is reached in the Middle East.
