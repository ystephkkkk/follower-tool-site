## Crusoe CEO on AI Energy Demand and Building in Texas

- Channel: Bloomberg Live
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tFvOfPJsWK8
- Published: 2026-10-27
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20261027.html#video-tFvOfPJsWK8
- Speakers: Chase Lochmiller (Co-Founder & CEO, Crusoe); Dina Bass (Host, Bloomberg)

---

**TL;DR:** Crusoe CEO Chase Lochmiller argues that recent market anxiety over AI infrastructure debt is overblown given overwhelming, deep demand, and details the company's energy-first approach to building massive AI data centers like the Abilene Stargate Project and international facilities powered by stranded renewable energy.

### Market Sentiment and Debt Financing
- **Assessment of AI Infrastructure "Bubble":** 
  - Chase Lochmiller states that recent neo-cloud debt and risk angst is an overreaction driven by a focus on public companies like CoreWeave.
  - Crusoe sees an "overwhelming tidal wave of demand" that is both deep and broad across major buyers and long-tail customers.
  - Existing customer deals are strong; while unsecured bond interest rates are rising (e.g., CoreWeave paying ~9% to 9.75%), it may slow growth rather than break the model.
- **Crusoe’s Debt Approach:**
  - Recently issued a public bond/delayed term loan trading above par at an ~8.5% interest rate.
  - Debt is secured against high-credit offtakes, making it structurally distinct from riskier unsecured debt.

### Data Center Opposition, Energy, and Community Impact
- **Texas "Moratorium" Clarification:**
  - The Texas pause on data center applications is actually an audit of large load interconnection agreements by officials checking if the state has enough power to energize massive builds.
- **Public Perception and Marketing:**
  - Lochmiller describes data center opposition as a marketing and misinformation issue rather than a structural flaw.
  - **Closed-Loop Cooling:** Large AI factories use closed-loop water recycling; a very large building uses roughly the same amount of water as 10 single-family homes (mostly employee use).
  - **Local Economic Transformation:** 
    - In **Abilene**, Crusoe accounts for over a third of the combined city and county tax budget.
    - In **Armstrong County**, Crusoe's investment more than doubles county tax receipts and funds community services.
- **Armstrong County Energy Strategy:**
  - Armstrong County has zero oil and gas revenue and is one of the windiest areas in the US.
  - Crusoe partnered with **Serena Energy**, which built the **Good Night One** 265 MW wind farm, with a second farm catalyzed and a third on the way.
  - Integrates on-site renewable generation, a firm grid interconnection (sharing a substation with Serena), planned gas peaking capacity for baseload power, and large-scale battery storage.
  - Excess energy production feeds back into the local grid to drive down utility costs for local ratepayers.

### Business Models and Vertically Integrated Infrastructure
- **Cloud vs. Data Center Strategy:**
  - Crusoe operates both a neo-cloud business (serving customers like **Perplexity**, **Jane Street**, and **Bloomberg**) and builds large-scale data center infrastructure.
  - Being vertically integrated allows Crusoe to understand exact workloads at the frontier, leading to better-designed data centers and cloud platforms.
- **Abilene "Stargate" Project (OpenAI):**
  - Designed as a gigawatt-scale computer interconnecting a massive cluster of GPUs on a single backend high-performance networking fabric.
  - Enabled the creation of OpenAI’s **Astro** model and unreleased models capable of solving complex math and science problems like Navier-Stokes.
- **Crusoe Spark (Inference Infrastructure):**
  - Small, modular, manufactured AI data centers built for rapid deployment and token serving rather than giant pre-training workloads.
  - Manufactured in a large factory in Denver, creating industrial jobs for electricians, technicians, painters, and welders.

### International Expansion and Energy-First Strategy
- **Paraguay Expansion:**
  - Announced a deal in Paraguay powered by the **Itaipu Dam** (a 15-gigawatt hydroelectric dam shared by Paraguay and Brazil).
  - Utilizes stranded energy that Paraguay was previously dumping onto the Brazilian grid at ~$0.02 per kilowatt-hour due to a lack of domestic heavy load. Supported by local partner **Arico** and the US and Paraguayan governments.
- **Global Sovereign and Renewable Playbook:**
  - **Iceland:** Leverages abundant geothermal and hydropower (similar to long-standing aluminum smelter models), moving data across the ocean instead of shipping heavy raw materials.
  - **Norway:** Utilizes local hydropower resources.
  - **Sovereign AI Markets:** Looking at regions wanting localized data storage and control over their own AI models.

### Operational Resilience and Supply Chain Solutions
- **The "Think Like a Mountaineer" Mindset:**
  - Derived from Chase Lochmiller climbing Mount Everest prior to starting Crusoe; bakes cultural resilience and contingency planning into execution and staffing.
- **Supply Chain Override Example (Abilene Project):**
  - When told medium-voltage power distribution centers had an industry supply chain delivery timeline of **100 weeks** against a **52-week** project deadline, Crusoe utilized its in-house manufacturing team.
  - Crusoe successfully manufactured the required medium-voltage distribution centers themselves in **28 weeks**.

## The Infrastructure of Intelligence: NVIDIA and OpenAI on a Decade of Building Frontier AI

- Channel: NVIDIA
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KT89PEe9d7U
- Published: 2026-09-16
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260916.html#video-KT89PEe9d7U
- Speakers: Ian Buck (VP of Hyperscale and High-Performance Computing, NVIDIA); Sachin Katti (VP of Infrastructure, OpenAI); Dina Bass (Reporter, Bloomberg)

---

#### TL;DR
AI infrastructure has evolved rapidly from single-GPU nodes to gigawatt-scale data centers, driven by continuous scaling laws and deep co-design partnerships like the decade-long collaboration between NVIDIA and OpenAI.

---

### 1. Evolution of AI Scale and Architecture
* **The Shift to Data Center-Scale Compute:** 
  * **Broader Footprint:** Models no longer fit on a single GPU or server; compute has expanded from GPU and rack scale to row and data center scale.
  * **Holistic System Design:** Ian Buck noted that modern architecture requires end-to-end design encompassing power distribution, cooling systems, warm shells, CPUs, storage, and networking.
* **Historical Collaboration Milestones:** 
  * **The Hopper-to-NVLink Transition:** Moving from single-box Hopper nodes (eight GPUs per server) to disaggregated rack architectures (such as NVLink 72-GPU systems) was an existential engineering moment required to reach trillion-parameter models.
  * **Astra on Vera Rubin:** Sachin Katti shared that OpenAI tested an Astra model checkpoint on a Vera Rubin rack over a weekend; out-of-the-box hardware yielded a 3x throughput improvement, and 72 hours of agent-driven recursive loop optimization produced an additional 2x throughput gain.

### 2. Infrastructure Innovations and the Ohio Data Center Project
* **Greenfield Optimization at Gigawatt Scale:** 
  * **Massive Footprint:** The Ohio data center project (a partnership between OpenAI, NVIDIA, and SB Energy) is designed to reach an 8-gigawatt capacity when fully built out, representing a substantial portion of the local grid.
  * **Dynamic Power Scheduling:** Workloads are categorized by power and latency sensitivity (e.g., maximizing user token rates versus running offline/batch pre-fill jobs). Scheduling jobs across different nodes and rows enables dynamic load balancing.
  * **Hardware-Level Power Management:** The design relies on smart GPUs that understand and manage their own power, racks built to handle transients and electrical spikes, and intelligent power controllers (PDUs) to achieve upwards of 30–40% more GPU density.
* **Grid Stabilization:** 
  * **Adaptive Load Sharing:** By co-designing workloads with the power system, the data center can scale down usage during grid spikes and scale up during gaps. 
  * **Economic Benefit:** This adaptive load balancing helps stabilize the local grid, modernize infrastructure, and reduce electricity bills.

### 3. AI in Chip Design and Software Engineering
* **Internal AI Deployment at NVIDIA:** 
  * **Bug Analysis and Yields:** NVIDIA utilizes open-source models (including its own NeMo-Tron models) and third-party options (like GPT) to analyze historical bug databases dating back decades, understand hardware issues, and optimize silicon yields.
  * **Code Review Agents:** An agentic pre-check tool runs on Cuda driver check-ins to flag common bugs and known issues. Developers settled on a **15-minute** processing window as the acceptable threshold for pre-checks.
* **Token Efficiency:** GPT models are leveraged for their strong token efficiency, which speeds up pre-check tests and keeps developer friction low.

### 4. Software, Heterogeneity, and Safety Compute
* **Heterogeneous Orchestration:** 
  * **Complex Ecosystems:** Modern data centers require stitching together GPUs, CPUs, networking, and storage. 
  * **Agentic Optimization:** With OpenAI operating as a relatively small team, they rely on AI models and tools like Astra to handle end-to-end system optimization and inference serving across diverse hardware.
* **Compute Impact of Safety and Alignment:** 
  * **Increased Demand:** Discussions around safety pauses do not slow down buildouts; instead, Sachin Katti emphasized that achieving safety and alignment for capable models requires *more* compute investment dedicated specifically to safety and alignment models.
