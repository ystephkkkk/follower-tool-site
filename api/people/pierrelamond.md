### TL;DR
Eclipse has reached ~$12.5B in AUM and closed $1.3B in April 2026 for Fund VI and Early Growth III, driven by a thesis that physical industries comprise 85% of global GDP ($100 trillion) and require hands-on operators rather than passive capital. Founder & CEO Lior Susan leads the firm alongside 96-year-old co-founder Pierre Lamond, utilizing an intensive incubation model that has built 30 of its 90 total portfolio companies.

### Firm Origins & The "Operators with Capital" Model
* **Background & Founding:** 
  * Founded in 2015 by Lior Susan (who left Flex) and Pierre Lamond (who started at Fairchild, co-founded National Semiconductor, and spent 35+ years at Sequoia Capital).
  * Lior Susan recounts meeting Lamond in early 2015 when Susan was 31 and Lamond was 85; Lamond told him "we're going to start a firm together" four months later.
  * Lamond still mentors the team and recently celebrated his 96th birthday.
* **Operating Philosophy:** 
  * Positioned as "operators with capital" rather than traditional venture capitalists.
  * Emphasizes discipline, institutional knowledge, and hands-on crisis management—such as Susan spending 72 hours in the Midwest handling a severe water-cooling leakage in a portfolio data center.

### Fund Performance & Milestone Investments
* **AUM and Fundraising:** 
  * Manages approximately $12.5B in AUM.
  * Closed $1.3B in April 2026 across Eclipse Fund VI and Early Growth III (the firm's largest raise to date).
  * Portfolio companies have raised $15.9B in 2026 alone.
* **Major Portfolio Outcomes:** 
  * **Cerebras:** 2016 Series A investment; returned roughly $2.5B at the $185 IPO price in May 2026 on about $147M total invested.
  * **SpaceX:** Named alongside Cerebras as recent successful IPOs in physical industries.
  * **Other Noteworthy Portfolio Companies:** Bright Machines, Bedrock, Maitra, Mind, Ursa Major, Vulcan forms, Auggury, Skyrise, Oxide, Wave, True Anomaly, Redwood Materials, Arc (The Nuclear Company), Verata, Anduril, and Peak Energy.

### Incubation Strategy & The "Eclipse Economy"
* **Incubation Model:** 
  * 30 out of Eclipse's 90 total portfolio companies were built/incubated internally.
  * Keeps the team's operating skills fresh by actively carving out and recruiting teams (e.g., spinning out **Mind** from Rivian with RJ Scaringe after three years of discussions).
* **The "Eclipse Economy" (Full-Stack Infrastructure):** 
  * Integrates investments across the entire physical AI and compute stack: chips (Cerebras), manufacturing racks and hardware, server infrastructure (Oxide), nuclear/sodium-ion/gas turbine energy sources, and physical robotics (Maitra, Mind).
  * Leverages internal portfolio cross-collaboration on multi-billion dollar commercial deals.

### Macro Thesis: Physical vs. Software
* **The $100 Trillion Opportunity:** 
  * 85% of world GDP (~$100 trillion) resides in the physical world. 
  * Metal manufacturing alone is a $3 trillion industry that historically received near-zero venture dollars compared to crowded enterprise software markets.
* **Re-evaluating Software Metrics:** 
  * Traditional Silicon Valley obsession with 85% SaaS gross margins often masks cash burn hidden in R&D accounting tricks.
  * Free cash flow and solving massive, difficult physical markets (like energy, manufacturing, and defense) dictate true long-term value, comparing favorably to modern giants like Tesla.
