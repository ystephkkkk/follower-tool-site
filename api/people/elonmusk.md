* **Top Speed:** Capable of exceeding 230 mph, with engineering targets aimed at reaching Mach 0.5 (halfway to the speed of sound).
* **Stunt Demos:** Live demonstrations included a rocket-powered ramp jump ("flying car") and a "kickflip" maneuver utilizing attitude control thrusters for aerial rolls.
