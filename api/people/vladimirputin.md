### Historic Asia Trip & US-Russia-China Trilateral Summit
* **First-Ever Trilateral Summit:** President Trump, Russian President Vladimir Putin, and Chinese President Xi Jinping held a historic trilateral meeting in Shenzhen, China, signing a first-of-its-kind peace declaration stating that nuclear conflict must never occur.
  * **Nuclear Balance:** Trump noted the three nations hold 92% of the world's nuclear power, estimating the U.S. has 4,000 nuclear weapons and China has 600. China agreed for the first time to conduct trilateral discussions on reducing nuclear risk.
  * **Energy & ICC:** The three leaders agreed to work together to increase oil, gasoline, and diesel production, and China joined in condemning the International Criminal Court (ICC).
  * **Diplomatic Rationale:** Secretary of State Marco Rubio emphasized that regular engagement among the three largest nuclear powers is essential, arguing it sets a positive foundation for trade negotiations and global safety.
* **Skeptical Assessment of China-Russia Ties:** Prior to the press conference, former National Security Adviser John Bolton warned that Xi and Putin have formed a "partnership without limits" where China is the dominant partner, creating a tri-polar nuclear world for which the U.S. lacks a strategy.
* **Ukraine & Bilateral Meetings:** Trump stated that Putin expressed a desire to end the Russia-Ukraine war diplomatically, noting that 25,000 soldiers are being killed every month. Trump also met with Xi on economic progress, noting fentanyl coming into the U.S. by sea is down 97%.

### Trade Deals, Tariffs & Economic Metrics
* **Southeast Asia & ASEAN Deals:** At the ASEAN summit in Manila, Trump secured more than $17 billion in commercial deals, including:
  * **Philippines:** Philippine Airlines will purchase 20 Boeing 787 Dreamliners with General Electric engines (supporting 47,000 U.S. jobs). The Philippines also agreed to buy 100% of its agricultural products (starting January 1) and 100% of its energy from the U.S., alongside more than $2 billion in U.S. military equipment.
  * **Thailand:** Signed an agreement to eliminate all tariffs and non-tariff barriers on U.S. products and purchase $2.6 billion per year in U.S. agricultural goods.
* **North American Trade:** Trump noted he is close to a deal with Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum on trade surpluses and cartels, while criticizing Canada over an $80 billion U.S. trade deficit.
* **Domestic Economic Claims:** Trump highlighted 10 straight months of manufacturing growth, a 21% increase in new equipment investment, $25 trillion in new wealth created since taking office (though $4 trillion was lost after the election), and record-low poverty.
  * **Auto Manufacturing:** Trump cited new U.S. investments driven by tariffs, including a $4 billion Audi plant, a $5 billion Toyota plant, and the expansion of a Detroit Ford plant originally built in 1906.
  * **Energy Prices:** Crude oil has dropped 20% since early November to nearly $80 a barrel, and gasoline is down 60 cents a gallon from a month ago.

### Super Intelligence (SI), Data Centers & Domestic Permitting
* **Global AI/SI Competition:** Trump stated the U.S. leads China in "Super Intelligence" (SI) and agreed with Xi to establish joint guardrails. The U.S. also launched a Quad SI Accelerator with India, Australia, and Japan.
* **Ban on Overseas Data Centers:** Trump announced that the U.S. will not allow American companies to build data centers in other countries (citing a proposed facility in Finland), threatening "tremendous" taxes and penalties to keep construction—and energy infrastructure—domestic.

* **Permitting Reform:** The Senate passed a bipartisan permitting reform bill that Vice President JD Vance said will enable unprecedented domestic construction of homes, factories, and power plants. Trump projected the bill will put at least $1,300 a year back into Americans' pockets.

### Iran Conflict, Oil Blockade & War Powers Debate
* **Senate War Powers Vote:** For the 15th time, Senate Republicans blocked a War Powers resolution to end hostilities in Iran, failing 48–51. GOP Senators Susan Collins, Lisa Murkowski, and Rand Paul joined most Democrats in favor, while Democrat John Fetterman and Republican Tom Tillis voted against.
  * **Opposition View:** Sen. Tim Kaine (D-VA) argued the war is unconstitutional and violates the 1973 War Powers Act's 60-day limit, calling the November 3 election results a repudiation of the conflict.
* **Administration Stance:** Trump stated Iran "will never have a nuclear weapon, ever," and noted the U.S. Navy's blockade gives the U.S. total control over the Strait of Hormuz, allowing record amounts of oil to move out. Vice President Vance argued on Fox News that the U.S. has destroyed Iran's nuclear program and conventional military.
* **Bolton's Critique:** John Bolton argued that while the U.S.-Israeli strikes and oil blockade have devastated Iran's economy and leadership, Trump lacks a clear, long-term strategy for regime change and failed to coordinate with NATO, Gulf Arab states, or Asian allies before the strikes began.

### Domestic Issues & Tech Outages
* **System Outages:** Addressing public concern over recent banking, credit card, and air traffic control outages, Trump said the incidents—such as Chase and other companies going down—are separate events caused by thousands of companies simultaneously upgrading their systems, not a coordinated crisis.

### Historic Trilateral Summit & Major Power Relations
* **First-Ever Trilateral Format:** Chinese President Xi Jinping hosted Russian President Vladimir Putin and U.S. President Donald Trump in Shenzhen, marking the first time the top leaders of the three nations have held formal trilateral talks.

  * **Shenzhen & APEC Praise:** Trump congratulated Xi on hosting a successful APEC summit and noted this was his second visit to China this year, following a state visit to Beijing in May. ### Global Security, International Law, and Sovereignty
* **UN Security Council Responsibilities:** President Putin emphasized that Russia, China, and the United States are victors of World War II and permanent members of the UN Security Council, giving them a joint responsibility for international peace and security.

  * **Conditions for Cooperation:** Putin expressed conviction that the three countries can strengthen global stability if they cooperate constructively based on "mutual respect, equality, and a balance of interests."

  * **Critique of the ICC:** Trump specifically named the International Criminal Court (ICC), stating it has "gotten out of control" and strayed far beyond its jurisdiction.

* **Trilateral Arms Reduction:** President Trump noted that as the world's three largest nuclear powers "by far," the U.S., Russia, and China are uniquely positioned to discuss reducing the number of nuclear weapons on Earth and lessening the risk of their use.

* **Combating Proliferation:** Trump stressed the vital need for trilateral cooperation to stop nuclear proliferation in other countries, specifically stating regarding Iran: "We're not going to let Iran have a nuclear weapon."
