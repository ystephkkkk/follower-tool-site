### TL;DR
Peregrine provides a data-connecting, privacy-first software and AI platform that empowers law enforcement, emergency services, and city institutions in over 400 global communities without collecting new sensor data. Co-founders Nick Noone and Ben Rudolph built a business rooted in forward-deployed engineering, data sovereignty, and strict data governance to help cities solve complex public safety challenges.

### Company Origins & The Public Safety Thesis
* **Founding Context & Persistence:** 
  * Founded by Nick Noone and Ben Rudolph on February 26, 2018 (incorporated slightly earlier), following careers spanning national security (Palantir SOCOM unit) and humanitarian/refugee work (UNHCR, Dimagi).
  * Secured access after over two dozen rejections by cold-calling Commander Brian Bouar in Contra Costa County, leading to their first deployment at the San Pablo Police Department in February 2018.
* **Navigating Social Turmoil:** 
  * Launched during a period of high polarization (COVID-19 protests and "defund the police" movements), operating out of a Honda Accord while holding the complex realities of both community activists and gang homicide investigators.
* **The "Anti-Surveillance State" Business Inversion:** 
  * Unlike companies like Flock or Axon that grow via hardware/sensor data collection, Peregrine inverts the model by installing zero new sensors and bringing no new data.
  * The core business focuses on joining disparate pre-existing city data to provide secure, properly governed solutions that enhance human precision and accuracy.

### Forward-Deployed Engineering (FDE) & Cultural Philosophy
* **Co-Owning the Problem:** 
  * FDE means psychologically co-owning the customer’s problem and driving to the outcome at all costs (ideally 3–5x faster than others), while taking deep institutional pride in the customer's win rather than seeking personal glory.
* **High-Empathy Implementation:** 
  * Rejecting Silicon Valley ego, the team spends extensive time on ride-alongs (e.g., overnight with Oakland PD) and deeply learning 30 years of human and institutional context before attempting to move fast.
* **Empowering the Underdog:** 
  * Built to serve municipal agencies (police, fire, EMT, health) that historically lacked access to advanced software due to prohibitive cost and complexity (unlike traditional enterprise vendors that require eight-figure contracts).
* **Talent & The "Dark Cave" Model:** 
  * Forward-deployed engineers are treated as R&D engines who operate with high institutional trust, stepping into complex customer environments ("dark caves") to build custom solutions and rapid prototypes.

### Data Sovereignty & Governance
* **Customer Data Ownership:** 
  * Operating on the principle that data belongs to the community and the institution, not Peregrine; the company explicitly rejects centralized panopticons and unamerican data amalgamation.
* **Strict Permissioning Logic:** 
  * Prioritizes robust data governance, access controls, and adherence to departmental standard operating procedures to prevent oversharing or unauthorized access (e.g., pulling unvetted open-source data).
* **Policy on Facial Recognition:** 
  * Refuses to impose top-down Silicon Valley mandates; instead, helps customers navigate local laws and institutional norms, noting that most public safety agencies and communities choose not to use facial recognition.

### AI Agents, Use Cases, & Platform Capabilities
* **Data Integration & Agentic Workflows:** 
  * 50% of engineers work on the data platform, having integrated tens of thousands of data sets across customers.
  * Uses Python notebooks where agents—overseen by deployment strategists—write ~90% of integration code, running for hours through sub-agents to analyze databases and ontologies.
* **Cold Case & Investigative Agents:** 
  * **Exoneration Replication:** Built a cold case agent using 200–300 GB of evidence (video, audio, PDF files) that successfully reproduced an exoneration detectives had previously reached by hand.
  * **Suspect Placement in Wisconsin:** A county in Wisconsin used a cold case agent to parse 300 GB of evidence and identify/place a suspect at a crime scene using cell call detail records scattered across massive datasets.
* **Semantic Search & Threat Detection:** 
  * Allowed detectives to use semantic search (beyond basic keywords) to identify a pattern of anti-Semitic threats made against local synagogues.
* **Emergency Response & Weather Patterns:** 
  * In a Florida county dealing with over 100 water rescues, an AI agent uncovered that three consecutive days of specific weather patterns created sand channels and rip currents, providing actionable predictive intelligence.
* **Rapid Custom Software Creation:** 
  * Deployment strategists rapidly spin up custom applications for specific customer needs, such as a hurricane simulator or a fire department placement/budget impact calculator built by a team member with just one month at the company.
