### TL;DR
Oracle and OpenAI announced an expanded partnership at Oracle AI World integrating ChatGPT and Codex into Oracle's software and infrastructure, while showcasing new products across media, security, infrastructure, and data platforms.

### Media Fabric & Adaptive Platform
* **Oracle Media Fabric:** Designed to fundamentally change media creation, delivery, and experience through a unified suite of tools.
    * **Creative Studio:** Reimagines production workflows using Oracle's AI media models (supporting still images, video generation, and IP rights management) alongside other leading third-party models.
    * **Video Edge:** Content delivery system featuring edge compute for content optimization and generation, optimized by the emerging "media over quick" open standard.
    * **Adaptive Media Platform:** Combines Creative Studio and Video Edge to deliver real-time ad personalization and interactive live viewing experiences at scale.
* **Availability:** Currently in limited availability with key customers, with general launch scheduled for early next year.

### Security, Authentication & AI Security Mesh
* **Evolution of Security Risks:** 
    * Initial LLM deployments faced prompt injection and data disclosure risks.
    * AI-written code introduced supply chain risks, including malicious packages targeting build systems.
    * Strict sandboxing models introduced too much developer friction until vendor-backed enterprise controls were adopted.
* **Biometric Authentication:** Added facial, voice, and fingerprint recognition to Oracle Identity and Access Management to verify human-in-the-loop approvals.
* **Agentic Identity & Access Control:** Introduced agent identity management supporting rapid creation/deletion, minimal just-in-time access, and specific approval workflows.
* **Oracle AI Security Mesh:** A new product enabling companies to use advanced cyber models to continuously discover system vulnerabilities, test attack paths, and deploy automated fixes.
* **Rob Clark's Perspective:** Noted that AI is not creating bugs but rather helping uncover decades-old security issues, predicting fewer patches and more secure software over the long term.

### Infrastructure, Accelerators & Power Management
* **AI Accelerators & Hardware Integration:**
    * **Nvidia Systems:** Spans from P100 (2017) and H100 (2023) to Grace Blackwell rack-scale compute with NVLink, alongside delivery of the first Vera Rubin systems to customers.
    * **Alternative Processors:** Bringing Google TPUs (starting with TPU V8) to OCI, AMD MI300 and 455 rack-scale offerings, Positron Atlas (power-efficient inferencing), Broadcom-OpenAI co-designed Jalapeno chips, and Cerebus/Samonova low-latency inferencing.
* **Acceleron Networking:** Features multi-planar front-end and back-end networks that provide full redundancy, higher resiliency, and RDMA over front-end networks.
* **Storage Partnerships:** First-party NetApp integration in OCI alongside a partner program for AI-accelerated storage (Weta, Vast, DDN) connected via Acceleron and RDMA.
* **Power Infrastructure & Grid Stability:** 
    * Praep Vincent highlighted that AI clusters act as massive, highly coordinated electrical loads.
    * Utilizes large energy stores (batteries and supercapacitors) combined with sophisticated power electronics as a "giant shock absorber" to smooth out power ramps and prevent grid destabilization.

### Data Ecosystem, Apex & Enterprise Workflows
* **Deep Trust AI Architecture:** Pushes data controls down into the database to individual tables, rows, and columns for automated policy enforcement regardless of user or agent.
* **Oracle Apex & Apex Lang:** Distills enterprise applications into a high-level language ("Apex Lang") that allows AI coding agents to build applications within a safe, governed, Oracle-maintained runtime.
* **Oracle App Composer:** Combines Codex code generation with IT governance, profiles, and deployment controls to turn business ideas into enterprise applications.
* **Oracle AI Workspace:** Developed in partnership with OpenAI, it unifies Oracle and third-party data (Slack, Office 365, wikis) to answer questions and drive complex workflows across the enterprise.
* **Startup Bundle:** Combines NetSuite QuickStart financial software, OCI credits, and OpenAI credits for new businesses.

### Partnership Insights: Clay Magourk & Sam Altman
* **Model Adoption & Productivity:** 
    * Sam Altman noted that GPT-6 adoption has been OpenAI's strongest ever, advancing rapidly from grade school math to hard unproven math problems and enterprise software generation.
    * Clay Magourk shared that more than 70% of Oracle's software is now written by AI via Codex.
    * Altman described the modern developer experience as managing a factory inside a computer, unlocking software creation for non-technical workers.
* **AI-Driven Innovation:** Altman highlighted that AI is accelerating scientific discovery and hardware design, noting that OpenAI used AI to design the Jalapeno chip faster than previously imaginable.
