## Qualcomm Q2 FY26 earnings call

- Channel: Qualcomm
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9unmr7CgEcg
- Published: 2026-05-01
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260501.html#video-9unmr7CgEcg
- Speakers: Cristiano Amon (President & CEO, Qualcomm); Akash Palkhiwala (CFO & COO, Qualcomm)

---

**TL;DR**
Qualcomm delivered solid Q2 results with record automotive growth and is pivoting toward "agentic AI" as its primary growth engine, despite temporary handset headwinds caused by memory industry dynamics. The company announced its first major custom silicon win with a leading hyperscaler, signaling a significant expansion into the data center market.

### Financial Performance & Handset Market Dynamics
* **Q2 Results:** Qualcomm reported revenues of $10.6 billion and non-GAAP EPS of $2.65, hitting the high end of guidance.
    * **QCT Revenue:** $9.1 billion, driven by record automotive and IoT growth.
    * **QTL (Licensing) Revenue:** $1.4 billion with a 72% EBT margin.
* **Handset Recovery Outlook:** While QCT handset revenues ($6 billion) were impacted by OEMs drawing down inventory due to high memory costs, management expects a recovery soon.
    * **China Bottoming:** China Android revenue is expected to bottom out in fiscal Q3 and return to sequential growth in Q4.
    * **Apple Partnership:** Qualcomm maintains a 20% share assumption for the 2026 iPhone launch, with roughly $2 billion in product revenue modeled for fiscal 2027.
* **Capital Return:** The company returned $3.7 billion to stockholders during the quarter, including $2.8 billion in share repurchases.

### The Paradigm Shift: Agentic AI & PC Leadership
* **The "Agentic" Opportunity:** CEO Cristiano Amon highlighted that "agentic AI" (AI agents running continuously in the background) is fundamentally reshaping the product roadmap.
    * **CPU Focus:** Unlike early AI which was GPU-heavy, agent orchestration is predominantly CPU-bound, playing to the strengths of Qualcomm’s Oryon CPU.
    * **Upgrade Cycle:** Management believes current devices are not built for background agents, creating a massive multi-year upgrade opportunity across smartphones and PCs.
* **Snapdragon X2 PC Platform:** Now in production, the X2 platform is positioned as a market leader.
    * **Performance Lead:** The platform reportedly outperforms Intel’s Panther Lake by nearly 30% in end-to-end agentic experiences.
    * **Industry Reception:** Management cited third-party reviews stating Qualcomm is no longer just catching up but is now "setting the pace" in the PC space.

### Automotive & Industrial Growth
* **Record Automotive Revenue:** The segment reached a $5 billion annualized revenue run rate for the first time, with a target to exceed $6 billion by the end of fiscal 2026.
    * **Content Increase:** The upcoming 5th Gen Snapdragon Digital Chassis represents the largest content increase in company history, featuring 12x NPU performance for Level 3 and Level 4 autonomous driving.
    * **ADAS Adoption:** Over 1 million cars are now operating ADAS and autonomy on Snapdragon Ride processors, with significant traction from BMW, Bosch, and Vay.
* **Industrial & Physical AI:**
    * **DragonWing IQ10:** A new platform for industrial robotics featuring 700 TOPS of AI performance and support for over 20 camera sensors.
    * **Robotics Partnerships:** Building on its win with Figure AI, Qualcomm announced a new multi-year agreement with Neura.

### Strategic Diversification: Data Center & 6G
* **Data Center Custom Silicon:** Qualcomm has entered the custom silicon space for a "large hyperscaler," with initial shipments expected in the December quarter. 
    * **AlphaWave Integration:** The integration of AlphaWave is accelerating execution in the custom ASIC market.
* **6G Strategy:** Qualcomm is positioning itself as the architect of 6G, which it defines as an "AI-native" network.
    * **Timeline:** Prototype demonstrations are expected in 2028, with commercial scale-up by 2030.
    * **Use Cases:** Beyond connectivity, 6G will support "wide area real-time sensing" for digital twins, drone tracking, and autonomous traffic management.
