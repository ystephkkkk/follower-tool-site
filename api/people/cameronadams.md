## Canva Create 2026

- Channel: Canva
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9HkO8masPT0

**TL;DR**
Canva has unveiled **Canva AI 2.0**, a generational leap that transforms the platform into an agentic creative partner capable of automating complex workflows, maintaining brand intelligence, and bridging the gap between design and productivity through deep integrations.

### Canva AI 2.0: From Design Tool to Creative Partner
The center of the "Canva Create 2026" event is the evolution of Canva’s AI into a holistic "orchestra" of tools that move beyond one-shot prompting to full-scale creation management.

*   **Agentic Orchestration:** Canva AI can now take direct actions on a user's behalf, such as selecting fonts, adjusting layouts, and tweaking elements based on the specific goals of a campaign.
*   **Memory Library:** A new personalization feature where the AI builds an "About Me" knowledge base. It learns individual styles, preferences, and project histories to ensure future designs are personalized to the user or team.
*   **Layered Object Intelligence:** Unlike standard AI image generators, Canva's in-house model creates fully layered, editable designs. Every generated element can be moved, modified, or replaced.
*   **Conversational Design:** Users can now initiate projects through natural dialogue rather than starting from a blank page or a fixed template.

### Enterprise Efficiency & Brand Intelligence
Canva is targeting the enterprise friction caused by fragmented tools and data silos with new connectivity features.

*   **AI Connectors:** Deep integration with daily-use apps like **Slack, Gmail, Google Drive, and Google Calendar**.
    *   *Example:* The AI can scan Slack threads to automatically generate a team newsletter or summarize a meeting transcript into a strategy document.
*   **Brand Intelligence:** AI now applies brand guidelines to every draft by default. This allows non-designers (e.g., sales teams) to generate content that is automatically compliant with corporate identity.
*   **Affinity Integration:** Pro-level assets created in Affinity (now part of the Canva family) flow seamlessly into Canva Brand Kits, allowing for high-end craft to be scaled across an entire organization.
*   **Automated Scheduling:** Users can now put repetitive tasks on "autopilot," such as overnight market research pulls or recurring social media posts.

### Interactive Design & Canva Code 2.0
One of the most significant technical updates is the ability to handle code and interactive elements without traditional programming.

*   **HTML & Code Import:** In collaboration with **Anthropic and OpenAI**, Canva now allows users to import HTML files. These files become instantly editable via a drag-and-drop interface.
*   **Interactive Components:** Users can build interactive websites, RSVPs with data collection, and "vibe-coded" interactive elements (like polls or games) that feed directly into **Canva Sheets**.
*   **Integrated Web Research:** An AI-powered research tool that pulls data and insights from the web directly into the design editor, eliminating the need to switch tabs or copy-paste external data.

### Global Accessibility and "Step Two" Initiatives
CEO Melanie Perkins highlighted the company's "Two-Step Plan": building a valuable company and using that value to "do the most good."

*   **Canva Education & Learn Grid:** A new all-in-one destination for teachers to mix and match curriculum-mapped resources (worksheets, games, presentations) across 16 languages. It is free for K-12 educators and students.
*   **Canva Offline:** A highly requested feature allowing users to edit designs without an internet connection. Changes sync automatically once the user returns online, targeting "creative flow" in areas with poor connectivity.
*   **Canva Print Shop:** A redesigned, built-in marketplace for 60+ physical products.
    *   **Sustainability Impact:** Through "One Print, One Tree," Canva has planted 16 million trees and is now backing solar farms to match the energy used for print orders in the US and Canada.
