#### TL;DR
SpaceX Chief Financial Officer Bret Johnsen and BlackRock Global Technology Team Head Tony Kim discussed SpaceX's rapid scaling into AI infrastructure, projecting over $100 billion in annualized run rate (ARR) by the end of 2026, driven by a 1-year terrestrial compute payback and upcoming orbital compute deployments starting next year.

### SpaceX's AI Infrastructure & Financial Scale
* **Rapid Revenue Growth:** 
  * SpaceX went from **$0** in AI revenue a year ago to an annualized run rate (ARR) projection of over **$100 billion** by the end of December 2026 (inclusive of total company revenue, with AI comprising the majority).
* **Terrestrial Compute Expansion:** 
  * SpaceX is on track to exit 2026 with **a little over 2 gigawatts** of terrestrial compute capacity.
  * Guidance targets scaling to **5 to 10 gigawatts** by the end of next year.
* **Vertical Integration Advantage:** 
  * Leveraging in-house engineering for data centers, power systems, cooling, and networking allows SpaceX to avoid margin stacking and move rapidly.
  * Former aerospace talent (accustomed to pressurized fuels and gases) is directly applying engineering discipline to data center design and high-power systems.
* **Hosting and Monetization Strategy:** 
  * Operates a flexible hosting model for tier-one customers like Anthropic and Google, using short-term commitments (3-month contracts with 3-month options, effectively 6-month terms).
  * Achieves a **dramatically less than 1-year payback** on cutting-edge terrestrial compute.

### Software, Frontier Models, & Products
* **Catching the Frontier:** 
  * While OpenAI and Anthropic currently sit at the bleeding edge of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), SpaceX (via xAI) is closing the gap rapidly.
  * Five major players currently form the front tier of the industry, with weekly releases of new model functionality.
* **Ecosystem and Content Integration:** 
  * Integrating **X** provides a massive content advantage with **500M to nearly 600M monthly active users**.
  * **Grokbot** is experiencing viral growth faster than any product the company has previously seen.
  * **Cursor** integration is driving internal design efficiencies and external developer tooling improvements.

### Starship and Orbital Compute
* **Starship 14 and V3 Satellites:** 
  * The upcoming Starship 14 launch (later this month) marks the deployment of commercial **Version 3 broadband satellites** into orbit.
  * A single Starship launch with V3 satellites delivers **20 times the capacity** of previous Falcon 9 flights, yielding **50 terabits** of downlink bandwidth per launch.
* **Reusability Timeline:** 
  * Flight 15 (the flight following Starship 14) will target full first- and second-stage reusability, aiming for meaningful re-flights next year.
* **Orbital Compute Economics:** 
  * Starship drives a **10x improvement in cost per kilogram to orbit**.
  * By the end of the decade, SpaceX aims to deploy **100 gigawatts per year** in space-based compute.
  * Orbital compute benefits from falling chip costs (Moore's Law) and **free, clean energy** from solar/space fusion, avoiding terrestrial power grid, cooling, and regulatory bottlenecks.

### Manufacturing & Supply Chain Constraints
* **Power and Turbine Bottlenecks:** 
  * Gas turbine supply chains are heavily constrained (18 to 24-month lead times for blades and vanes) due to sudden multi-fold demand from the AI industry.
  * SpaceX is standing up an in-house investment casting facility to pour metal and build necessary turbine components itself.
* **Terafab Concept:** 
  * To bypass long-term semiconductor and memory fab capacity constraints (where memory is projected to grow ~30% annually against triple-digit AI demand), SpaceX is pursuing the "Terafab" concept.
  * The goal is to see real volume benefits from development versions (partnering with Tesla next year, moving to 2028/2029 production) to support the orbital AI hockey-stick ramp.
