### ## The TGH and Palantir Sepsis Breakthrough
* **Strategic Collaboration:** TGH and Palantir have partnered for over six years, utilizing Palantir's Foundry and AIP (Artificial Intelligence Platform) as the core analytical operating system for the hospital network.

* **Workforce Augmentation:** Panelists rejected the narrative that AI will cause mass unemployment, arguing instead that AI "turbocharges" human operators rather than replacing them.
* **Agentic AI in TGH Call Centers:** TGH deployed an agentic AI system in its call center within an 8-to-10-week window.
    * **Efficiency Gains:** The system has processed approximately 1.5 million calls, reduced call abandonment rates by 58%, and increased scheduled appointments by 21%.

* **Applying Defense Insights to Commerce:** Palantir's commercial approach is rooted in its highly rigorous work with military organizations (such as SOCOM and CENTCOM). 
* **Addressing Global Inefficiencies:** Beyond healthcare, Palantir is applying cognitive AI to massive supply chain and industrial bottlenecks:
