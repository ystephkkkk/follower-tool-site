## AI, Security & Economic Power: A New Playbook for Democracies | Sebastian Kurz | RAISE Summit 2026

- Channel: RAISE Summit
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3mZ30Yq0_HE
- Published: 2026-07-20
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260720.html#video-3mZ30Yq0_HE
- Speakers: Sebastian Kurz (Former Chancellor of Austria); Jacob Helberg (U.S. Department of State); Sebastian Matthes (Editor-in-Chief, Handelsblatt)

---

**TL;DR**
To maintain global relevance, democracies must transition from a regulatory-first mindset to an "ecosystem-based" sovereignty that prioritizes massive capex investment, energy abundance, and localized control over security-critical AI infrastructure.

### Redefining AI Sovereignty: Ecosystems vs. Isolation
* **The Ecosystem Model:** Jacob Helberg argues that true sovereignty is not about vertical integration or "rebuilding yesterday's technology" in-house, but being a net contributor to a global innovation ecosystem.
    * **Historical Precedent:** When the US missed the "5G boat," it did not attempt to build a domestic Nokia or Ericsson; instead, it became a massive buyer of allied technology while banning national security threats like Huawei.
* **The European Perspective:** Sebastian Kurz emphasizes that while 100% independence is impossible, the COVID-19 pandemic revealed the dangers of total dependency on China for basic goods. 
    * **The "Mythos" Moment:** Recent US restrictions on the "Mythos" AI model served as a wake-up call for Europe, highlighting that a foreign government can unilaterally decide whether European companies remain customers of foundational technology.
* **Strategic Autonomy:** Kurz suggests Europe should focus sovereignty efforts on "on-prem" solutions for sensitive sectors, including government data, financial systems, and national security, rather than trying to compete in every consumer-facing foundational model.

### Security, Intelligence, and "Dream"
* **Hybrid Warfare:** Sebastian Kurz’s new venture, **Dream**, focuses on AI-based cybersecurity and data analysis for nations and critical infrastructure. 
    * **The New Superpower Metrics:** Kurz posits that while nuclear capabilities defined superpowers in the past, modern power is derived from AI, Cyber, and eventually Quantum capabilities.
    * **Current Scale:** Dream is roughly three and a half years old and has reached a valuation/funding level of $3 billion, aiming to help non-superpowers achieve "superpower" status in digital defense.
* **Responsible Release:** Helberg clarified the US government’s stance on restricting powerful models, noting the goal is a "30-day window" for security evaluation to prevent mass cyberattacks on critical infrastructure before a general release.

### Economic Security and Industrial Reality
* **The "Facebook Ads" Fallacy:** Helberg warns that digital sovereignty is meaningless without industrial sovereignty, noting that the war in Ukraine proves that "you can't win a war with Facebook ads; you need drones, bullets, and factories."
* **China's Dominance:** The discussion highlighted alarming statistics regarding dependencies on China:
    * **Rare Earths:** China controls 90% to 99% of the market.
    * **Smartphones:** China controls 50% to 85% of the global market.
    * **Automotive:** Chinese manufacturers are increasingly "crushing" the traditional European and American automobile industries.
* **The AI Economic Engine:** In the US, the AI boom is driving 2–3% GDP growth and hundreds of billions in capex, keeping unemployment below 5% despite energy market volatility.

### A Policy Playbook for Democracies
* **US Strategic Pillars:** Helberg outlines the current US "playbook" for AI leadership:
    * **Capex Expensing:** Allowing companies to benefit from accelerated depreciation on massive technology investments.
    * **Energy Abundance:** Lowering the cost of AI compute by expanding domestic energy production, including drilling in Anoir and the Gulf of Mexico.
    * **Permitting Reform:** Streamlining the NEPA process to allow builders to move faster.
* **The "Draghi" Warning for Europe:** Helberg cites Mario Draghi’s report, noting that the EU has gone "way too far" on regulation. 
    * **Regulatory Friction:** The **Digital Markets Act (DMA)** is cited as the source of 80% of friction between the US and EU, with US officials viewing it as a "non-tariff trade barrier" designed to extract money from wealthy American firms.
* **Mindset Shift:** Kurz argues that Europe’s primary obstacle is its mindset. He asserts that Europe has lost its focus on competitiveness and must realize that without being an economic leader in AI, it will be unable to finance its social welfare systems in the future.
