## Gemini 4 Argon: What’s new and what’s next

- Channel: Google for Developers
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jh6mgf9M06M
- Published: 2026-10-14
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20261014.html#video-Jh6mgf9M06M
- Speakers: Josh Woodward (Executive, Google DeepMind); Melvin Johnson (Executive, Google DeepMind); Sergey Brin (Co-founder, Google)

---

### TL;DR
Google DeepMind introduced **Gemini 4 Argon**, an advanced AI model featuring an industry-leading one million output tokens, refined writing quality, and deep integration into internal engineering workflows by 100,000 Googlers prior to launch. 

---

### Model Access, Scale, and Testing
* **Availability:** **Josh Woodward** announced that Gemini 4 Argon is available immediately across the Gemini app, AI Studio, the Gemini API on cloud, and Antigravity.
* **Internal Scale at Google:** **Sergey Brin** highlighted that 100,000 internal engineers tested the model, forming an internal flywheel where researchers and models work hand-in-hand on rapid three-to-four-week iteration cycles.
* **Early Enterprise and Cyber Testing:** **Tulsee Doshi** noted that the model has undergone three to four weeks of internal testing, while early external access has been provided to cyber defenders—including a partnership with Wiz via their Scan For Good program—to identify and patch vulnerabilities.
* **Safety and Regulatory Filings:** Google submitted the model to the US government for testing and reinforced internal and external guardrails regarding monitoring and model setup.

---

### Writing Quality and Model Behavior
* **Eradicating AI Slop:** **Josh Woodward** explained that the team worked with professional writers to stamp out "AI slop" (load-bearing phrases, excessive listification), aiming to give Gemini 4 Argon a concise, precise, and unique "house style" tailored for sales, legal, finance, and coding.
* **Behavioral Trade-offs:** **Tulsee Doshi** detailed efforts to strike a balance between model persistence (working hard without stopping) and avoiding extremes like over-verbosity, aiming for optimal transparency regarding what the model is doing.
* **Thought Traces in UI:** **Josh Woodward** mentioned ongoing product experiments regarding the length and frequency of thought traces, noting that users initially value deep thought traces but rely on them less as trust grows.

---

### Real-World Use Cases and Breakthroughs
* **Code Migrations and Legacy Systems:** **Melvin Johnson** and the team discussed the Antigravity team using Argon to migrate external Fortran codebases to Rust.
* **Hardware Root-Causing:** **Melvin Johnson** and **Sergey Brin** shared an instance where an engineer had Argon investigate a hardware bug; the model successfully pinpointed corrupted High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) from a specific vendor that regular ECC checks missed, allowing Google to outcast those machines from the fleet.
* **Knowledge Work and Creative Tasks:** Googlers utilized the model for Chromium development, Gemma optimizations, drafting and sharpening professional writing, building a reading app grounded in early childhood learning theory, and simulating data center operations.

---

### One Million Output Tokens and Advanced Reasoning
* **Industry-Leading Capacity:** **Logan Kilpatrick** and **Melvin Johnson** emphasized the release of industry-leading **one million output tokens** in a single step.
* **Depth on Complex Problems:** **Tulsee Doshi** and **Melvin Johnson** explained that at max reasoning effort ("high"), a non-trivial percentage of evaluation trajectories hit the million-token limit, enabling the model to handle deep, multi-step math and open-ended research problems requiring long horizons.
* **Metaphorical Scale:** **Melvin Johnson** compared one million tokens at human processing speeds to roughly a month of continuous human thinking.
