* **High-Capital Risk Profile:** **Complex Financial Modeling:** While Ackman calls Anthropic perhaps the most amazing business story of the century, its requirement for massive capital investments makes valuation and future cash flows difficult to nail down with precision.

## Intel CEO: AI is buying almost as many CPUs as GPUs now | Invest 2026

- Speakers: Lip-Bu Tan (CEO, Intel); Brian Sozzi (Executive Editor, Yahoo Finance)

---

### TL;DR
Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan told Yahoo Finance’s Brian Sozzi that the GPU-to-CPU ratio has shifted from 8:1 to parity (or even higher for certain inference and agent workloads), while emphasizing that Intel will not commit capital without verified customer backing.

### AI Infrastructure and Compute Demand
* **GPU-to-CPU Ratio Shift:** 
  * The historical 8:1 GPU-to-CPU ratio has shifted toward parity as the industry transitions from training to inference, generative AI reinforcement, and orchestration.
  * In certain inference and agent workloads, CPU demand has actually surpassed GPU demand.
* **AI Market Dynamics and Corrections:** 
  * AI is a permanent structural shift comparable to the internet, but rapid over-expansion has led to early corrections and potential market casualties.
  * Security has become critical as millions of autonomous agents operate without proper guidance.
* **Capital and Resource Constraints:** 
  * Memory constraints have driven prices up 6x to 7x, with extreme scarcity meaning buyers may not secure supply even at inflated prices.
  * Raw materials, packaging, and power availability present significant operational hurdles.
  * Total AI capital requirements reach into the trillions of dollars, creating a widening gap between heavy capex spending and realized revenue generation that requires investor patience.

### Intel Corporate Culture and Turnaround Strategy
* **Cultural Re-Engineering:** 
  * Lip-Bu Tan stepped in to lead a multi-year turnaround for an iconic company facing bureaucratic bloat after 50 years of success.
  * The focus has shifted back to first principles: speed, risk-taking, listening to customers, and obsessing over execution details like quality, yield, and reliability.
* **Engineering Rigor:** 
  * Emphasizing that engineering must focus past the initial 90% of "cool stuff" to nail the final 10%—specifically defect density, variation control, and yield.

### Partnerships, Foundry Strategy, and US Manufacturing
* **Nvidia Collaboration and Investment:** 
  * Intel partnered with Nvidia for engineering co-development, embracing high-speed interconnect standards like NVLink.
  * Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang invested $5 billion into Intel as a major shareholder.
* **Foundry Discipline and Trust:** 
  * Lip-Bu Tan convinced the board to maintain the foundry business, anchored by advanced packaging (EMIT technology delivering 14x to 15x reticle integration).
  * Recruited former SK Hynix CEO Shil Lee to lead packaging operations.
  * **Strict Capex Rule:** Intel maintains strict financial discipline, committing capital and pulling in equipment from Applied Materials, Lam Research, and KLA only when backed by confirmed customer commitments.
* **US-Centric R&D:** 
  * Intel remains the only major chipmaker performing its core research and development on US soil, while maintaining a pragmatic, collaborative posture with ecosystem partners like TSMC, Samsung, AMD, and Nvidia.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Industry Server CPU Growth:** Industry server CPU units will grow more than 30% in both 2026 and 2027 — Lip-Bu Tan.
* **Memory Scarcity Escalation:** Memory constraints will become even worse next year — Lip-Bu Tan.
* **Market Corrections:** Further market corrections and industry casualties are coming following the hyper-growth phase — Lip-Bu Tan.

### Notable Quotes
> "Used to be the ratio GPU to CPU is 8 to 1 and now it's kind of parity now and in some cases actually more CPU than GPU... depend on different workload."
> — Lip-Bu Tan

> "Memory become from a constraint next year going to be even worse... The prices have gone through the roof... six seven time and even that you're willing to pay you may not get it."
> — Lip-Bu Tan

* **Long-Term Strategic Bets:** **Decade-Long Evolution:** Dr. Su noted that technology decisions require three to five years to come to fruition, with AMD making systematic, multi-year bets on high-performance computing.
  * **Product Generations:** The company is now advancing its sixth generation of EPYC Italian-city-named CPU products.
* **Scale and Visibility:** **Rising Profile:** AMD has transitioned from being considered one of tech's "best kept secrets" between 2015 and 2018 into a prominent industry leader with frequent White House engagements.

### 2. Market Dynamics and the Rise of AI Agents
* **The AI Inflection Point:** **Compute as Intelligence:** Dr. Su asserted that computing is the foundation of AI and that "compute equates to intelligence," making investments ahead of the curve essential for long-term progress.
  * **Ecosystem Collaboration:** Rejecting the idea of a single winning semiconductor model, Su stressed that success relies on deep partnerships across the ecosystem between chipmakers, frontier model labs, and hyperscalers.
* **The Impact of Personal Agents:** **Rapid Adoption:** The conversation highlighted the emergence of personal agents (such as Muse, which reached 10 million activations in 45 days) as a massive new demand driver.

### 3. Financial Outlook and Supply-Demand Realities
* **Guidance and Projections:** **Revenue and Data Center Scale:** AMD's Q3 into Q4 guidance targets over $50 billion in revenue for the year, with the data center business projected to grow over 100% (more than doubling) compared to the prior year.

* **Supply Constraints:** **Exceeding Capacity:** Dr. Su stated that demand currently outpaces what AMD can supply, allowing the company to quickly backfill any isolated project delays by reallocating capacity to customers who need more.
  * **Customer Partnerships:** AMD maintains deep, ongoing relationships with top hyperscalers and model developers—including OpenAI, Meta, Anthropic, Microsoft, Oracle, Amazon, and Google—all of which are continuously asking for more computing capacity.

### 4. Public Perception and Data Center Infrastructure
* **Community Engagement:** **Proactive Storytelling:** Addressing public concerns over data center expansion, Dr. Su emphasized that the tech industry must be front-footed and proactive at the local level.
  * **Local Benefits:** She stressed the importance of communicating how data centers contribute to energy infrastructure, local economic growth, job creation, and ensuring that communities directly share in the benefits of American technological leadership.
