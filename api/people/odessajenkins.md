**TL;DR**
The Women’s National Football Conference (WNFC) is a 16-team professional tackle football league transitioning from a bootstrapped startup to a $50 million Series A funding round, utilizing lean SaaS business strategies to achieve profitability and financial equity for female athletes.

### Financial Strategy and Scaling
* **SaaS Methodology:** Founder Odessa Jenkins applied mechanisms from her tech background to grow the league leanly and efficiently, prioritizing market building over early external investment.
* **Funding Rounds:**
    * **Bootstrapping:** The league operated for its first 4–5 years using internal revenue from apparel and ticket sales.
    * **Seed Round:** Raised $1.5 million at a $20 million valuation in late 2024, led by investor and CEO Dot Sherman.
    * **Series A:** The league is currently launching a Series A round with a target valuation of $50 million.
* **Profitability:** The business is currently profitable due to a lean operating model, with revenue quadrupling annually in key areas.

### League Structure and Valuation
* **Equity Model:** The WNFC is a private corporation where the 16 individual team owners also hold equity in the league. This "team-owned entity" structure allows investors to buy in at the league level or the team level.
* **Franchise Values:** Individual team valuations currently range between $1 million and $3 million.
* **Market Strategy:**
    * **Current Footprint:** Operating in major markets including Los Angeles, Las Vegas, Chicago, Dallas, and New Jersey.
    * **Expansion:** Jenkins plans to cap the league at 16–20 teams to avoid the pitfalls of over-expansion, with interest in North Carolina and Arizona.
    * **Selection Criteria:** Focuses on the quality and longevity of ownership groups rather than just geographic location.

### Revenue Diversification and Media
* **The Revenue Pie:** Financials are heavily weighted toward national sponsorships (Adidas, Dove, Riddell) and a vertically integrated apparel business.
* **Media Rights:**
    * **Victory Plus:** A five-year deal provides a consistent streaming home alongside other major sports like the NWSL and MLB.
    * **Linear Reach:** The 2024 championship debuted on ESPN2, reaching 150,000 households and 20,000 simultaneous streamers.
* **Social Engagement:** The league engaged approximately 100 million people across Meta platforms last year, a metric they aim to convert into increased viewership and streaming numbers.

### Athlete Compensation and Player Safety
* **Transitioning Pay Scales:** Players currently earn via performance bonuses and local sponsorships. The Series A capital is intended to shift the league toward a "living wage" or regular game-day salary model.
* **Safety Protocols:**
    * **NFL Standards:** The league "copycatted" NFL head and neck safety protocols, utilizing Sway Medical for baseline concussion testing.
    * **Coaching Certification:** 100% of WNFC coaching staff is CPR and first-aid certified.
* **The Role of Flag Football:** Jenkins views the rise of flag football (and its Olympic inclusion) as a "pipeline driver" rather than a competitor. NFL and NCAA investment in flag has grown the WNFC’s business by 100% by increasing overall female participation in the sport.
