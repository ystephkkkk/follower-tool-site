**TL;DR:** Pirelli CEO Andrea Casaluci explains how the 100-year-old company protects its margins by focusing exclusively on high-performance consumer tires—which now drive 82% of total turnover—while leveraging Formula One virtualization and the data-collecting "Cyber Tire" to prepare for autonomous driving. Amidst fierce Chinese competition and European industrial policy gaps, Pirelli is investing $1 billion to scale its U.S. manufacturing capacity to 6 million tires within seven years.

### High-Value Strategy, Formula One & Virtualization
* **Pure-Play Consumer Focus:** Pirelli has exited truck, mining, and large industrial tires to focus exclusively on consumer vehicles (cars, motorcycles, and bikes) in the high-value segment.
  * **Market Dominance:** 82% of Pirelli’s total turnover—and nearly 90% in Europe—comes from high-performance tires, a segment protected from Asian budget competition by higher pricing power and OEM trust.
  * **Prestige OEMs:** Pirelli supplies top luxury automakers including Ferrari, Lamborghini, Bentley, Maserati, McLaren, Aston Martin, Porsche, and Rolls-Royce.
* **Formula One as an "Open-Air Laboratory":** Pirelli has been the sole tire supplier for Formula One since 2011, participates in 350 motorsport competitions annually, and will become the sole supplier for MotoGP starting next year.
  * **Virtualization & AI:** Because F1 teams develop tires before the actual cars exist and face strict physical testing limits, Pirelli pioneered mathematical modeling and AI simulations. Transferring this to everyday tire development has reduced time-to-market by **30%**.
  * **Brand Positioning:** F1’s 22-car grid provides global visibility every Sunday to a growing, younger, and more gender-balanced audience.
* **EV Tire Demands:** Electric vehicles require specialized tires with a higher load index (due to battery weight), stronger grip (due to instant torque), and lower noise emissions (due to the absence of engine noise).

### The Cyber Tire & Autonomous Driving
* **20 Years in the Making:** Pirelli’s "Cyber Tire" embeds sensors into the tire—the vehicle's only contact point with the road—to collect real-time data on asphalt conditions, driving style, and tire health.
  * **Current Deployment:** Already on the market with high-performance automakers including McLaren, Aston Martin, and Pagani, feeding data to systems like ABS to shorten braking distances.
* **Enabling Autonomous Vehicles:** Casaluci notes that while human drivers rely on instinct to brake or steer around obstacles, autonomous software requires a 100% data-driven "grip estimator" in real time, making the Cyber Tire critical for future self-driving cars.

### Europe’s Auto Industry, China’s Speed & US Expansion
* **Europe’s Industrial Policy Gap:** While 100% of prestige cars are still manufactured in Europe, Casaluci warns that the EU set ambitious Green Deal electrification targets without supporting them through consumer incentives or charging infrastructure.
  * **The Norway Benchmark:** Casaluci points to host Nicolai Tangen’s home country, Norway—where over 90% of new car registrations are EVs—alongside China, the Netherlands, and California as examples of effective industrial policy.
* **Learning from Chinese Speed:** Having operated in China for over 20 years, Pirelli has seen its Chinese operations evolve from a low-cost manufacturing hub into a vital R&D center serving local premium EV makers that demand low rolling resistance, high comfort, and rapid time-to-market.
  * **The Prototype Contrast:** In a recent material tender, European suppliers took two months just to return a signed NDA, whereas a Chinese supplier returned a physical prototype in the same timeframe. Casaluci attributes this to lean organizations, lack of legacy bureaucracy, and fast, single-owner decision-making.
* **The $1 Billion US Supply Chain Bet:** The U.S. represents roughly 40% of the global high-value tire market, yet Pirelli’s market share there lags behind Europe and Asia.
  * **Current Footprint:** Only 5% of Pirelli’s U.S. sales are manufactured domestically; 55% come from Mexico, and 40% are imported from Europe and South America.
  * **Resilience Over Tariffs:** Pirelli is investing $1 billion to expand its plant in Rome, Georgia, scaling production from under 500,000 tires today to 6 million tires in seven years to insulate against tariffs, currency volatility, and logistics costs.

### Leadership, Culture & Personal Philosophy
* **From Apprentice to CEO:** Casaluci joined Pirelli as an apprentice in 2002 and was appointed CEO in 2023 after 21 years, succeeding Marco Tronchetti Provera. He emphasizes knowing the vast majority of Pirelli’s 30,000 employees.
* **Collaborative Leadership:** Casaluci’s management style is built on trust, listening, and allowing teams to make—and learn from—mistakes (though not the same mistake twice).
* **Personal Balance & Routine:** He cites working too much when younger as his biggest personal mistake, advising others to stay present for family. He wakes at 7:00 AM, exercises for 50–60 minutes while listening to classical jazz (Chet Baker, Miles Davis) or classical music, and is an avid sailor who has crossed the Atlantic.
