*   **Reclaiming Core Competencies:** Isaacman identifies a critical loss of internal talent, noting that 75% of the current workforce are contractors provided by staffing agencies, leading to a loss of "muscle memory" in mission and launch control.
*   **NASA Force Initiative:** Launched with the Office of Personnel Management (OPM), this program will:

    *   Implement term-based appointments from industry to provide mentorship and "season" the NASA workforce.
*   **Operational Cadence:** The agency is moving away from the "obsolete" three-and-a-half-year launch cycle. The new goal is to launch moon rockets in "months, not years," mirroring the nine-week turnaround achieved during the Apollo 7 to Apollo 8 transition.

### Restructuring the Artemis Program
*   **Mission Iteration:** NASA is inserting a new mission in 2027 to "buy down risk." This mission will test rendezvous maneuvers in low-Earth orbit (LEO) with landers from providers like SpaceX and Blue Origin, similar to the Apollo 9 mission.
*   **Evolutionary Moon Base:** Instead of jumping to a multi-billion dollar "dream state" habitat, NASA will build the base step-by-step:
    *   **Phase 1:** Utilizing the Commercial Lunar Payload Services (CLPS) and Lunar Terrain Vehicle (LTV) programs.
    *   **Phase 2:** Providing a "demand signal" to industry for power, navigation, and communication services to support long-term habitation.
*   **Standardizing Hardware:** NASA is standardizing the Space Launch System (SLS) rocket to increase cadence, while acknowledging that the current architecture—leveraging 50-year-old Shuttle-era hardware—is the "start, not the finish" of lunar logistics.

### The Leap to Mars and Nuclear Propulsion
*   **The "Near Impossible" Role:** Isaacman defines NASA’s role as pursuing technologies where a commercial business case cannot yet be closed. 
*   **Nuclear Breakthroughs:** The agency is prioritizing Nuclear Electric Propulsion (NEP) and surface nuclear power. 
    *   **Timeline:** Isaacman promises a nuclear reactor will be "underway in space" before the end of the current presidential term.
    *   **Function:** Nuclear power is essential for moving mass to Mars and enabling in-situ resource manufacturing (mining propellant) for return trips.
*   **Proving Ground:** The lunar South Pole will serve as a laboratory for harvesting ice and testing life-support systems before undertaking the nine-month journey to Mars.

## Vast CEO Max Haot on raising $500M in new investment round

- Channel: CNBC Television
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-brivLK0f1U

---

**TL;DR**
Vast has secured $500M in its first major outside funding round to accelerate the development of Haven-1, a private space station set to launch in Q1 2025 as a primary contender to replace the International Space Station (ISS).

### Capital Raise and Strategic Positioning
*   **Funding Milestone:** Vast announced a $500M investment round, marking its first significant outside capital. This follows over $900M in previous funding from founder Jed McCaleb, bringing total investment to more than $1.4 billion.
*   **The "Leapfrog" Strategy:** CEO Max Haot described a strategy of building a "minimum viable product" station (Haven-1) using internal funds rather than waiting for NASA development grants. 
    *   By launching hardware ahead of the competition, Vast aims to become "impossible to ignore" during NASA’s upcoming Commercial LEO Destination (CLD) Phase 2 contract awards.
*   **ISS Replacement Timeline:** The company is laser-focused on the 2030 (or potentially 2032) retirement of the ISS, aiming to provide a seamless commercial transition for the U.S. and allied partners.

### Product Roadmap and Launch Timeline
*   **Haven-1 (Q1 2025):** A single-module space station launching on a SpaceX Falcon 9. 
    *   Initial operations will be remote-controlled to verify systems.
    *   Following verification, a four-person crew will visit the station via a SpaceX Dragon spacecraft.
*   **Haven-2:** The long-term successor, a four-module station designed for mass production, capable of supporting continuous human habitation and significant power/life support requirements.
*   **Haven Demo Success:** The company recently completed its "Haven Demo" mission, a satellite test of non-human technologies (power, solar arrays, propulsion, and flight computers), which Haot characterized as a "100% mission success."
*   **SpaceX Integration:** Vast is the first station developer to integrate Starlink laser terminals, enabling gigabit connectivity for research and crew communications.

### Market Outlook and Business Model
*   **Target Customers:** Vast is prioritizing "sovereign nation" customers—specifically NASA, ESA (Europe), and JAXA (Japan)—who wish to send astronauts for research and science.
*   **Conservative Economic Approach:** Unlike competitors relying on a near-term "LEO Economy" (space manufacturing or tourism), Vast’s business model assumes profitability based on existing government demand. 
    *   **In-space Manufacturing:** While bullish on pharmaceuticals and semiconductors long-term (5–10 years), Haot stated Vast does not require these markets to be viable in the immediate future.
*   **Space Data Centers:** While open to the technology due to high power requirements, Haot views space-based data centers as more suited to high-power satellites than human habitation modules.

### Government and Competitive Landscape
*   **NASA CLD Program:** Congress and NASA are moving toward issuing an RFP for the second phase of commercial space station development. 
*   **Need for Redundancy:** Haot urged NASA to select at least two winners for the replacement program to ensure redundancy and backup for the U.S. and its partners.
*   **Operational Training:** Vast was recently selected for the 6th private astronaut mission to the ISS (launching July 2025). This mission serves as a "stepping stone" to gain operational experience with NASA and SpaceX before launching their own crewed missions to Haven-1.

## FedEx CEO Raj Subramaniam: We are undergoing a tremendous transformation

- Channel: CNBC Television

- Published: 2026-02-12
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260212.html#video-BvpDD6M1q7g
- Speakers: Raj Subramaniam (CEO, FedEx); Morgan Brennan (Anchor, CNBC)

---

**TL;DR:** FedEx is executing a fundamental "One FedEx" transformation to transition from a physical logistics provider to a data-driven supply chain orchestrator, projecting a 13–14% operating income CAGR through 2027 despite modest revenue growth.

### Strategic Financial Outlook
* **Growth Projections:** CEO Raj Subramaniam issued a three-year forecast targeting a 4% revenue CAGR, which is expected to drive a 13–14% CAGR in operating income due to structural cost reductions.
* **Capital Efficiency:** The company aims for a 200-basis-point improvement in Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) and expects to generate $6 billion in free cash flow.
* **Market Focus:** Growth is being driven by high-value industry verticals, specifically healthcare, aerospace, automotive, and the rapidly expanding data center sector.

### The Three-Pronged Transformation
* **Network 2.0:** The integration of the U.S. network is approximately 25% complete. FedEx expects to reach 65% completion by the end of calendar year 2026, with full integration by fall 2027.
* **Organizational Consolidation:** The company has shifted from operating as multiple independent companies to "One FedEx," streamlining internal operations to increase resilience against macroeconomic volatility.
* **Digital Evolution:** FedEx is leveraging its proprietary data—generated by 17 million packages daily—to transition into a "physical AI" company.
    * **FedEx Dataworks:** A dedicated unit focused on externalizing internal capabilities like route optimization and predictive maintenance.
    * **Retail Momentum Index:** A predictive tool created with Dun & Bradstreet that serves as a leading indicator for retail performance.

### Global Logistics and AI Orchestration
* **Supply Chain Waste:** Subramaniam identified $1.7 trillion in global supply chain waste due to lack of coordination, positioning FedEx to capture value by "orchestrating" these complex global movements.
* **European Strategy:** FedEx recently took a minority stake in **InPost**, which operates over 60,000 lockers in Europe. 
    * **B2C vs. B2B:** This allows InPost to handle the B2C "out-of-home" segment while FedEx focuses on its core B2B industrial business in Europe.
* **Emerging Trade Patterns:** Logistics trends are shifting rapidly; Subramaniam noted a rare decoupling where U.S. exports are rising while imports decrease. FedEx is responding by "replatforming" facilities in Japan, Vietnam, India, and Saudi Arabia.

### Structural Milestones and Industrial Trends
* **FedEx Freight Spin-off:** The separation of the LTL (Less-Than-Truckload) business is on track for June. Subramaniam believes this creates two distinct, high-value companies, allowing FedEx Freight to focus exclusively on its status as the largest LTL provider in America.
* **Industrial Recovery:** While the ISM index has been suppressed for nearly three years, recent positive readings in January and massive investments in data center infrastructure suggest a stabilizing industrial backdrop.
