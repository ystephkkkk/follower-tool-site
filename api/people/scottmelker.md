## Hester Peirce Explains What Crypto Still Needs Before She Leaves the SEC

**TL;DR:** SEC Commissioner Hester Peirce outlines how the agency is using existing authority to advance crypto regulation through a new five-year tokenized stock innovation exemption following the Clarity Act's failure. She emphasizes the foundational importance of self-custody, financial privacy, and developer protections for keeping crypto innovation in the United States.

### The Post-Clarity Act Reality and SEC Authority
* **Unexpected Legislative Collapse:** **Hester Peirce** expressed surprise that the Clarity Act failed to pass after more than a year of intensive debate, noting that while a market structure bill would be helpful for regulators, the SEC can still accomplish significant work under its existing statutory authority.
* **Proactive Agency Positioning:** Both the SEC and CFTC had prepared for either legislative outcome, allowing the SEC to move forward immediately with proposals like the new innovation exemption using authorities it has exercised for nearly two years.
* **The Five-Year Innovation Exemption:** Designed as a bridge to a permanent regulatory framework, this time-limited order allows market participants to experiment with bringing tokenized NMS (National Market System) stock trading on-chain using automated market makers (AMMs).
    * **Issuer Controls:** The exemption includes a 30-day issuer consent condition, giving companies the explicit power to veto or approve the tokenization of their shares.
    * **Strict Parity Requirements:** Tokenized shares must carry the exact same rights as traditional shares, including identical dividends and voting rights.
    * **Permissionless Blockchain Focus:** The current order explicitly permits activity on permissionless blockchains, providing transparency while maintaining an open door for permissioned chain discussions.

### Bridging Tokens and Equities
* **Rehabilitating the "Security" Label:** **Hester Peirce** criticized past anti-crypto regulatory approaches that drove projects to fear the security designation, which inadvertently fueled the rise of utility-free meme coins as a gamble-driven workaround.
* **Unified Asset Structures:** The SEC is pushing for a regulatory environment where tokens and equity can be unified when it makes sense for a project, or separated cleanly with clear rights and benefits assigned to each.
* **Investment Contract Lifecycles:** Under guidance like Reg Crypto, teams are encouraged to be explicit about the promises attached to an investment contract token; once those promises are fulfilled or abandoned, the investment contract framework falls away from the token.

### Self-Custody, Privacy, and Developer Protections
* **Self-Custody as a Fundamental Right:** **Hester Peirce** asserted that holding one's own money and assets is a basic American right that does not require regulatory permission, contrasting it with intermediated channels that require standard regulatory oversight.
* **Financial Privacy Reset:** Describing current financial surveillance and the deputization of financial institutions as a system where Americans have surrendered too much privacy, she called for a constitutional balance that protects everyday citizen privacy while giving law enforcement proper channels to investigate illicit activity.
* **Shielding Software Developers:** Comparing blockchain software to general-purpose technology like iPhones, **Hester Peirce** argued that developers must be protected from liability simply because bad actors misuse their tools, warning against turning the SEC into a "developer approval agency."

### AI Integration and Future-Proofing Regulation
* **AI-Driven Fraud and Defense:** **Hester Peirce** noted that while AI magnifies the scale of securities fraud, it is simultaneously being leveraged by regulators and financial institutions for enhanced investigation and customer protection.
* **Future-Proofing Rules:** Acknowledging that future regime changes can reverse policy, she argued that the best way to make crypto regulations permanent is to build products that people genuinely want, need, and use, making them functionally unstoppable.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Hester Peirce** claimed that issuers will show high enthusiasm and uptake for tokenized stock trading under the new five-year innovation exemption framework.
