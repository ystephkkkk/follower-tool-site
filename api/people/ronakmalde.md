### TL;DR
Ronak Malde, co-founder of Trajectory, explains how his platform democratizes continual learning by combining training, evaluation, and deployment into a single, dead-simple interface, enabling companies to train custom models from production data 6x cheaper than frontier models.

### Defining Continual Learning and the "Pseudo" State
* **The Human Analogy:** 
  * The human brain serves as the ultimate model for true continual learning, learning online and updating constantly.
* **The AAA vs. Google Maps Metaphor:** 
  * *Pseudo-Continual Learning (Today):* Akin to highlighting a road trip map at AAA and only being able to consult offline; any course change requires a massive batch update. Trajectory's initial phase is like *MapQuest*—democratizing access to training and customization without needing a central lab.
  * *True Continual Learning (Goal):* Akin to *Google Maps*, where every route and driving experience continuously updates aggregate data in real-time on traffic and speed.
* **Architectural Evolution:** 
  * While traditional transformers relied on stacked attention blocks, recent base models (like DeepSeek V4.1 Flash) incorporate architectural changes like fast/slow memory within weights and skip connections between layers 2 and 50, which are key components for continual learning.

### The Operational Bottleneck & Company Origins
* **Past Laboratory Constraints:** 
  * Training a frontier model previously required hundreds of millions of dollars in R&D and compute, massive research teams, and 6 to 9 months of work with hand-holding consulting models (seen during Ronak's time at Windsurf with Cursor Composer).
* **Co-Founder Backgrounds & The "Aha" Moment:** 
  * Ronak Malde experienced production training and retries at Windsurf.
  * Co-founder Michael worked at DeepMind on Gemini robotics, building continual learning infrastructure for daily robot updates.
  * Co-founder Arjun worked at Apple on the Vision Pro, utilizing artist feedback for multimodal foundation models.
  * *The realization:* While big labs focus on distributing one massive model to everyone, customizing a model for a single customer requires immense infrastructure that individual labs cannot sustainably build customer-by-customer.

### Case Study: Harvey's Legal Customization
* **Overcoming Closed-Source Limits:** 
  * Harvey built interfaces for law firms on top of closed-source frontier models but could not utilize their 30 years of accumulated domain knowledge and nuance.
* **Concrete Performance Metrics:** 
  * Using Trajectory, Harvey trained their own model using a legal agent benchmark constructed from expert data.
  * **Cost Reduction:** The trained model is **6 times cheaper** than frontier models.
  * **Accuracy Gain:** Improved an open-source baseline from approximately **4% accuracy to around 18%**.

### Core Primitives and Research Bets for Continual Learning
* **Trajectory as a Unit of Work:** 
  * Moving away from counting tokens to viewing tasks and rollouts (trajectories) of economic value as the primary unit of work in an RL training stack.
* **Three Core Requirements:** 
  * **Traces:** Logging main agent calls, sub-agents, product events, and user feedback using tools like LangChain, BrainTrust, or Raindrop.
  * **Replayable Environments:** Using environment concepts (such as Docker files or system requirements) to replay production traces.
  * **Training Stack/Algorithms:** Managing loss and reward functions.
* **Research Bet 1: Group Size One (GRPO Modification):** 
  * Standard GRPO requires running the exact same task/level 8 to 16 times in a simulated environment.
  * Trajectory's variant eliminates simulated environments by learning from single real-world rollouts (group size one) across 10,000 distinct tasks.
* **Research Bet 2: Meta-Graders for Rewards:** 
  * Instead of densely labeling all 10,000 traces, users densely label 100 smartly selected representative traces with rubrics/criteria.
  * Trajectory trains a meta-grader that learns from those 100 traces to automatically grade and propagate rewards across the broader 10,000 traces, increasing data efficiency by **100x**.

### The Trajectory Product Platform
* **Platform Architecture:** 
  * Combines training, evaluation, and deployment into a one-click interface inspired by Apple's philosophy of combining internet and phone capabilities into a single handheld device.
* **Target Audience:** 
  * AI-native companies (such as Harvey and Rogo) and larger enterprises (such as Nvidia) that have established agent harnesses and want to optimize the underlying model.
* **Team Structure and Engineering:** 
  * Combines researchers from OpenAI and DeepMind (Muse infrastructure at MSL), infrastructure engineers from Stripe, and Netflix's youngest design engineer.
  * The codebase is fully productionized with microservices and Kubernetes auto-scaling.
  * Design engineers continuously kick off training runs, aided by frontier coding agents acting as software factories.
