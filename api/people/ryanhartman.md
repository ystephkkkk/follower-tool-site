### TL;DR
Regna AI is a new sovereign AI joint venture between Palantir and Ondas designed to provide mission-specific, post-trained open-weights models for defense, intelligence, and edge operations while safeguarding sensitive data.

### Origins and Vision of Regna AI
* **Founding Context:** 
  * Formed after six months of close collaboration between Ondas and Palantir, tackling mission autonomy capabilities like *Sky Weaver*.
  * The name *Regna* translates to "Sovereign Kingdom," reflecting the commitment to delivering sovereign AI solutions for defense and government customers.
* **The Sovereign AI Thesis:** 
  * Inspired by Palantir CEO Dr. Alex Karp’s vision for sovereign AI across industries.
  * Focuses on post-training models for specific missions (such as intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance, or ISR) to compound operational "alpha"—measured in lives saved rather than commercial metrics.
* **Spin-Out Structure:** 
  * Initially bootstrapped by Ondas with Ondas serving as the first customer.
  * Structured to operate as an independent company serving the broader defense ecosystem rather than just a proprietary internal tool.

### Technical Architecture & Edge Constraints
* **Rejection of General-Purpose AI:** 
  * Rather than relying on cloud-hosted frontier models designed for general tasks, Regna AI builds specialized, fine-tuned models tailored to specific operational constraints.
* **Edge Deployment:** 
  * Models are engineered to run on limited form factors and compute footprints found on edge platforms, including stratospheric balloons, long-endurance UAVs, and ground robotics.
* **Disconnected Operations:** 
  * Designed for environments with limited bandwidth and compute, enabling distributed inferencing directly on platforms while maintaining situational awareness.

### Human-in-the-Loop Validation and Trust
* **Forward-Deployed Engineering Model:** 
  * Utilizes Palantir-style Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) working shoulder-to-shoulder with customers to map data inputs directly to operational decisions.
* **"Mini-Labs" for Customers:** 
  * Commanders maintain continuous control over model training, reviewing end-to-end data pipelines and recommended actions.
  * Operators can iteratively accept or reject outputs, ensuring high trust before deploying models to the battlefield or simulated exercises.

### Complex Multi-Domain Use Cases
* **Stratospheric Balloon Routing:** 
  * Managing infinitely complex operations for stratospheric balloons—including dynamic weather, wind patterns, exclusion zones, and inclement weather routing.
* **Multi-Domain Orchestration:** 
  * Connecting assets across the stratosphere, air, space, ground, and surface domains to distill infinite data streams into actionable commander decisions.
* **Building on Existing Frameworks:** 
  * Leverages prior operational learnings from *Sky Weaver*, mission control, and orbit systems to shape the architecture of the new models.

### TL;DR
Through the newly formed Ondas Sentinel, Ondas and Palantir are unifying autonomous defense technologies—ranging from ground robotics and counter-UAS to stratospheric balloons—powered by the Skyweaver Agentic AI orchestration platform to compress military decision timelines from hours to single-digit minutes.

### The Changing Character of Warfare & The Shift to Scalable Mass
- **Evolution of Force Projection:** **Ryan Hartman** noted that unmanned systems combined with autonomy represent a new era of warfare, allowing forces to project power with vastly more assets and fewer personnel, matching deployments like the nearly 100% unmanned force structure currently in Southcom.
- **The Economic Problem of Exquisite Defense:** **Matt McCue** explained that traditional defense contractors rely on exquisite, high-cost technologies that fail to scale, citing the unsustainable cost of using $35,000 Patriot missiles against low-cost targets.
- **High-Rate Automotive and Autonomy Integration:** To achieve mass, **Matt McCue** highlighted how DZYNE utilized manufacturing techniques from the automotive industry and embedded full autonomy into interceptors to remove human operators from direct control loops.
- **IonStrike Rapid Development:** **Matt McCue** and **Ryan Hartman** cited *IonStrike*—a Group 3 air defense protection system built to counter Shahid-class one-way attack drones—as a core technology conceptualized and brought to a demonstrable product in just **six months** in collaboration with the U.S. Army and Air Force.

### Ondas Sentinel: From Aircraft to Architecture
- **Unified Operating Platform:** **Ryan Hartman** described Ondas Sentinel as the go-to-market business unit uniting Ondas's portfolio, built around the mission thesis of seeing further, deciding faster, and acting with purpose.
- **Multi-Domain Spectrum Coverage:** **Ryan Hartman** detailed the integrated footprint spanning from ground-layer counter-UAS and tier-1/tier-2 UAVs, through Group 4 and Group 5 UAVs (via DZYNE), up to stratospheric balloons (via World View Enterprises operating at **60,000+ feet**), noting there is virtually zero technology overlap across the acquired companies.
- **Comprehensive Counter-UAS Layer:** **Ryan Hartman** outlined a full counter-UAS continuum spanning detect, cyber-over-RF, soft kill, and kinetic kill via an expanded product family including *Iron Drone*, *Centrics*, *Drone Buster*, *Sawtooth*, and *IonStrike*.
- **Ultra Long-Endurance Persistence:** **Matt McCue** highlighted *Ultra*, an air-breathing ISR platform with **tens of thousands of flight hours** and multi-day endurance, which reduces expensive SATCOM data usage and bandwidth requirements by executing autonomy and edge effects directly at the platform level.
- **Corporate Integration via AI Agents:** **Ryan Hartman** addressed operational integration challenges, explaining that Ondas is utilizing Palantir's *Foundry* and *Warp Speed* to deploy AI agents over systems of record (ERP, inventory, financial, and manufacturing systems) to streamline post-acquisition integration without halting business operations.

### Skyweaver and Multi-Domain Mission Autonomy
- **Edge GPU Processing at Altitude:** **Matt McCue** introduced *Skyweaver*, an agentic AI orchestration platform running on hardware stacks including GPUs operating at **up to 70,000 feet** on stratospheric balloons and **30,000 feet** on *Ultra* aircraft.
- **Gulf of America Domain Awareness Use Case:** **Ryan Hartman** presented a concrete operational scenario where stratospheric balloons provide wide-area imaging over **750 square miles of open ocean**, using edge AI to classify vessel types, autonomously task *Ultra* UAVs for high-resolution confirmation, and package intelligence products for human analysts in **single-digit minutes** instead of hours.
- **Human-on-the-Loop Governance:** **Ryan Hartman** emphasized that human operators maintain continuous oversight ("human on the loop" rather than in the loop), retaining complete chain of custody and the ability to override autonomous tasking at any point during detection, tracking, and prosecution workflows.

## Palantir, Ondas & World View | Multi-Domain Intelligence in the Stratosphere

- Channel: Palantir
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XgZZRV77D8k
- Published: 2026-05-26
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260526.html#video-XgZZRV77D8k
- Speakers: Eric Brock (CEO, Ondas); Ryan Hartman (CEO, World View); Matt Shepherd (Forward Deployed Engineer, Palantir)

---

**TL;DR**
By integrating World View’s stratospheric balloon technology into Ondas’s multi-domain autonomous portfolio, and leveraging Palantir's Foundry, Warp Speed, and Agentic AI, this partnership is streamlining defense-tech supply chains and enabling autonomous, machine-to-machine mission orchestration to deliver rapid "speed to decision" for warfighters.

### The Multi-Domain Portfolio and the Stratospheric Frontier

*   **Ondas’s System-of-Systems:** Ondas is building a comprehensive portfolio across four primary vectors to establish a "bots before boots" approach, ensuring tactical tasks are handled by autonomous machines rather than putting personnel in harm's way.
    *   *ISR:* Incorporates tactical UAVs, drones, and stratospheric balloons.
    *   *Counter-Drone:* Features fully autonomous soft-kill (autopilot override and safe landing) and hard-kill (net interception) systems.
    *   *Strike Capabilities:* Consists of small and long-endurance strike assets.
    *   *Ground Robotics:* Utilizes quadrupeds (robotic dogs) for hazardous environments like tunnels, alongside larger unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs) equipped with remote weapon stations.
*   **The Stratospheric Advantage:** World View operates custom balloons in the stratosphere, an under-utilized layer of the atmosphere spanning from 50,000 to 300,000 feet.
    *   *Predictable Navigation:* Operating above the troposphere escapes weather interference, allowing vehicles to leverage highly reliable and predictable four-directional wind currents for steering and positioning.
    *   *Persistent Perspective:* Offers 100% space-darkness viewpoints capable of seeing the Earth's curvature, providing wide-area persistence for defense tracking and commercial applications (e.g., methane leak detection for oil and gas infrastructure).

### Transitioning from Tech Development to Scaled Businesses

*   **Overcoming the "Valley of Death":** Many defense technology companies establish product-market fit but fail during the capital-intensive scaling phase. 
    *   *Strategic Consolidation:* By acquiring World View, Ondas mitigated the scaling and capital risks of a "one-trick pony" single-domain company, creating a robust, multi-domain entity backed by public market capital.
*   **Solving the M&A Integration Dilemma:** Historically, consolidating defense contractors has been a slow, expensive process. World View CEO Ryan Hartman noted that late-1990s defense mergers (such as TI Defense and Raytheon) took years to integrate ERP, inventory, and financial systems.
    *   *Palantir Integration Agents:* Ondas is utilizing Palantir's AI capabilities to rapidly integrate disparate financial platforms, ERPs, and supply chains, avoiding the massive capital drain historically associated with defense acquisitions.
    *   *Supply Chain Optimization:* Using Palantir's Warp Speed, the joint team is identifying supply chain vulnerabilities and gaps across all consolidated Ondas companies to inform future investment and manufacturing decisions.

### Moving from Platform Autonomy to Mission Autonomy

*   **Platform Autonomy vs. Mission Autonomy:** 
    *   *Platform Autonomy (Orbit):* Governs how a single asset maneuvers with minimal pilot input. This is critical for scaling stratospheric balloons (formerly known as the AI Flight Director), as manually piloting a balloon is a highly rare and untaught skill. Orbit allows a small crew to operate hundreds of balloons simultaneously.
    *   *Mission Autonomy (Skyweaver):* Links disparate assets across ground, air, stratosphere, and space domains to collaboratively achieve a target objective without constant human intervention.
*   **Rebuilding Maritime Domain Awareness (MDA):**
    *   *The Legacy Process:* Traditional MDA relies on the TC-PED process (Task, Collect, Process, Exploit, Disseminate). Data is collected from high altitudes, sent to a land-based intelligence center, manually assessed, routed to task a secondary asset for closer inspection, and sent back again—a loop that takes hours.
    *   *The Agentic AI Solution:* Using Palantir's ontology, a World View stratospheric balloon can detect a vessel, infer potential threats, autonomously task an Ondas tactical UAV to collect close-up imagery, process that data directly at the edge on the balloon, and deliver an actionable decision package to a human in minutes.

### Future Outlook and Industry Trajectory

*   **The Skyweaver Appliance:** Skyweaver serves as the ultimate connective tissue being co-developed by Palantir, Ondas, and World View to link multi-domain systems, transforming raw sensor feeds into instant tactical effects.
*   **Six-Month Iteration Cycles:** The partner companies are operating on aggressive, six-month development intervals to consistently introduce new nodes and capabilities into the multi-domain network.
*   **Focus on the Defense Industrial Base:** The ultimate goal is to build a highly efficient, re-industrialized defense tech base that can deliver competitive edge and save lives by replacing slow, human-reliant analytical loops with automated, digital-to-digital decision pipelines.
