## Advancing to AI's Next Frontier: Insights From Jeff Dean and Bill Dally

- Channel: NVIDIA Developer

- Speakers: Jeff Dean (Chief Scientist, Google DeepMind); Bill Dally (Chief Scientist, NVIDIA)

---

**TL;DR**
AI is transitioning from supervised task-completion to autonomous "agentic" workflows, necessitating a radical hardware shift toward ultra-low latency inference and AI-assisted chip design to sustain the next frontier of performance.

### The Evolution of AI Models and Autonomous Agents
*   **Performance in Verifiable Domains:** Models have seen massive leaps in math and coding. Jeff Dean highlighted Google’s Gemini model achieving gold-medal status in the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO) and ICPC coding contests.
*   **Transition to Agentic Workflows:** AI is moving from interactive prompting to long-running "agents" that can execute tasks independently for hours or days, correcting their own errors without constant human supervision.
*   **Natural Language Research (NAS):** 
    *   Researchers can now specify search spaces in natural language rather than code (e.g., "Explore new distillation algorithms"). 
    *   Agents act as a productivity multiplier by running experiments, dismissing unproductive leads, and honing in on promising architectures.
*   **Data and Scaling:** Dean disputes the idea that "data is mined out," pointing to untapped video/audio data and the potential for high-quality synthetic data generated by powerful models.

### Hardware Architectures for Ultra-Low Latency
*   **The Latency Goal:** NVIDIA aims to drive inference performance from hundreds to tens of thousands of tokens per second (10k–20k range) to support reasoning-heavy agent workflows.
*   **Communication Bottlenecks:**
    *   **On-Chip:** Using static scheduling to eliminate routing overhead and queuing, aiming for "speed of light" data transfer (e.g., across a chip in 30 nanoseconds).
    *   **Off-Chip:** Reducing Physical Layer (PHY) latency by simplifying signal processing. Dally suggests backing off bandwidth slightly to eliminate the latency-heavy "heroic" error correction and digital signal processing.
*   **Provisioning Bifurcation:** The industry is moving toward specialized hardware SKUs for different tasks:
    *   **Training and Prefill:** Math-heavy and communication-dominated.
    *   **Decode:** Memory-bandwidth limited and highly sequential.

### Efficiency Through "Zero Movement" and Advanced Memory
*   **The Energy Cost of Data:** Reading a number from external memory (HBM) costs approximately 15 picojoules, whereas the actual computation (multiply-add) costs only 10 femtojoules. 
    *   **Core Philosophy:** "The key thing you can do to reduce energy is to just don't move data."
*   **SRAM vs. DRAM:** While SRAM is energy-efficient for computation, its area cost is an order of magnitude higher than DRAM.
*   **Stackable DRAM:** NVIDIA is exploring stacking DRAM mats directly on top of the GPU logic. This could provide an order of magnitude more bandwidth with an order of magnitude less energy by eliminating the distance bits must travel to the pins.
*   **Challenges of Sparsity:** While models are naturally sparse, exploiting that sparsity often destroys the regularity that makes hardware computation efficient, requiring more complex controllers.

### AI-Driven Chip Design (AI for AI)
*   **Automating Library Porting:** NVIDIA’s **NVCell** uses reinforcement learning to port standard cell libraries to new semiconductor processes overnight—a task that previously took a team 80 person-months.
*   **PrefixRL:** Reinforcement learning has been used to design circuit adders that are 20–30% smaller and lower power than those designed by humans.
*   **LLMs for Engineering (ChipNeMo):**
    *   NVIDIA fine-tuned internal LLMs on proprietary RTL code and design documents.
    *   **ChipNeMo** acts as a "patient mentor," explaining complex texture unit architectures to junior designers and summarizing bug reports.
*   **The Future of Design:** The ultimate goal is an end-to-end agentic system where a "Master Agent" coordinates specialized sub-agents to handle floor planning, verification, and timing, collapsing the design-to-tape-out timeline.

### Societal Impact and Personalized AI
*   **Education:** Dean and Dally emphasize the potential for personalized AI tutors that understand an individual's specific knowledge gaps and learning modalities (e.g., turning a biology chapter into an interactive game).
*   **Healthcare:** The "Personal Health Coach" concept—an AI that monitors biometric data (watches, genomics) and provides real-time dietary and medical advice, potentially bridging the gap between clinical data and patient behavior.
*   **Scientific Discovery:** Using AI to accelerate the identification of new materials and susceptibility to diseases based on personalized mutations.

## Insights from NVIDIA Research | NVIDIA GTC

- Channel: NVIDIA Developer

- Speakers: Bill Dally (Chief Scientist and SVP of Research, NVIDIA)

---

**TL;DR:** NVIDIA Research is pivoting toward architectural breakthroughs to achieve 10,000+ tokens-per-second inference, utilizing reinforcement learning during pre-training to overcome the global AI data shortage, and scaling general-purpose robotics through "self-play" and synthetic data generation.

### I. Strategic Impact: From GPU Supply to Market Demand
Bill Dally (Chief Scientist and SVP of Research) oversees a 500-person organization divided into "Supply" (architecture/circuits) and "Demand" (creating markets like autonomous vehicles and quantum computing).

*   **Historical "Greatest Hits":**
    *   **AI Leadership:** Porting Andrew Ng’s Google brain software from 16,000 CPUs to 12 GPUs led to the creation of cuDNN and NVIDIA’s dominance in AI hardware.
    *   **Networking:** The development of NVLink and NVSwitch began as a Research project funded by the Department of Energy, eventually being integrated into the Pascal and Volta architectures.
    *   **Real-Time Ray Tracing:** The "RTX Core" originated in Research as the "Tree Traversal Unit," aiming to make ray tracing 100x faster for real-time graphics.
*   **Integration Moonshots:** The lab periodically unites disparate groups for "moonshots," such as the current focus on ultra-fast inference and the prior development of cuQuantum for simulation.

### II. Engineering Ultra-Fast & Efficient Inference
To meet the demands of agentic AI and real-time reasoning, NVIDIA is redesigning hardware to move from hundreds of tokens per second to over 10,000 tokens per second for a single user.

*   **Eliminating Data Movement:** 
    *   **Near-Memory Math:** In current GPUs, data movement accounts for ~73% of energy consumption. NVIDIA is prototyping "Processing-in-Memory" (PIM) where math units are placed at the edges of SRAM/DRAM to eliminate energy wasted on transport.
    *   **3D Stacking:** Prototyping localized DRAM stacked directly on top of the GPU die to provide high bandwidth and capacity with 10x less energy per token.
*   **Latency-Optimized Communication:**
    *   **On-Chip "Speed of Snail":** To reach 16,000 tokens/sec, on-chip latency must drop to <50ns. This is achieved by using statically scheduled wires that eliminate queuing, routing decisions, and arbitration.
    *   **Off-Chip Tradeoffs:** Current networking optimizes for throughput (bandwidth) at the expense of latency. New prototypes back off throughput slightly to eliminate digital signal processing (DSP) and forward error correction (FEC) delays, aiming for ~100ns switch traversals.

### III. Solving the Data Shortage: RL-Pretraining (RLP)
As AI developers "mine out" the world's available text and image data, NVIDIA is shifting toward "self-play" models—similar to how AlphaGo trained—to gain more value per token.

*   **The Chinchilla Limit:** The industry has reached the point where scaling data by orders of magnitude is no longer feasible.
*   **Moving RL Earlier:** Traditionally, Reinforcement Learning (RL) happens after pre-training. NVIDIA is moving RL *into* the pre-training phase.
    *   **The "Think" Step:** Rather than just predicting the next token, the model is prompted to "reason" internally before answering.
    *   **Verifiable Rewards:** Because the ground-truth next token is known, the model receives a "dense reward" on every token for its internal reasoning process without needing human annotation.
*   **Performance Gains:** RLP shows a ~35% improvement in accuracy on models like Nemotron-12B using only 250 million tokens of RL-pretraining, and these gains are "durable"—they do not wash out during later fine-tuning stages.

### IV. Project GR00T: General-Purpose Robotics
NVIDIA’s robotics foundation model (GR00T) aims to create a single model capable of controlling any form factor, from humanoid robots to simple bimanual arms.

*   **The Data Bottleneck:** Collecting robot data via teleoperation is labor-intensive and slow.
*   **Synthetic Data Pipeline:**
    *   **DreamDojo:** A generative model that works in reverse; it takes robot actions and generates "dreams" (videos) of what the robot would see. This creates massive datasets for training Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models without physical robot time.
    *   **Distillation:** The original DreamDojo was too slow for real-time; Research distilled it into a "student" model that runs 3x faster than real-time.
*   **Sim-to-Real Success:** Using Isaac Lab, NVIDIA can simulate 500,000 robots in parallel to train Whole Body Control. This allows robots to perform complex tasks (e.g., using a screwdriver or folding laundry) one-shot in the real world with zero fine-tuning.
