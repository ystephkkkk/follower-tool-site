## Naveen Rao: 4D Computing, AI's Energy Wall & Beating Biology

### TL;DR
Unconventional AI has developed the world's first physical dynamical computer—a 4D computing chip that merges memory and compute without von Neumann architecture—achieving a thousandfold improvement in energy efficiency to solve AI's impending power wall.

### The Energy Crisis in AI
* **Exponential Token Growth:** **Google** alone crosses 3.2 quadrillion tokens per month, demanding roughly 12 gigawatts of power for AI services alone (with total U.S. data center capacity sitting around 40 gigawatts).
* **The Power Wall Timeline:** **Naveen Rao** estimates the industry will run out of energy within roughly 3 years due to the massive gap between exponential AI market demand and linearized energy supply.
* **Cost Structure Shift:** Energy now accounts for roughly 50% of the cost of serving a token, forcing data center operators to prioritize securing power contracts above all else.

### The Biological Paradigm & 4D Computing Architecture
* **Biological Efficiency:** Human and animal brains operate on remarkably low energy—ranging from a 20-watt human cortex down to an 8-milliwatt squirrel brain capable of highly precise, complex navigation.
* **Eliminating Data Movement:** Standard GPU computing systems move nearly 30 trillion bits in and out of memory per second outside the chip (and 10x-100x more inside), whereas the human cortex moves only about 16 billion bits per second.
* **The 4D Dynamical Computer:** **Unconventional AI** built a chip using "4D computing," leveraging three physical spatial dimensions (including die stacking) and one time dimension in the dynamics. 
* **Integrated Memory and Compute:** Unlike traditional von Neumann architectures that shuttle data back and forth between separate memory and processors, dynamical computers combine compute and memory into a single element where individual computing elements act as memory.

### Technological Breakthroughs & Prototyping
* **Dynamical Systems & Sparsity:** By utilizing dynamical systems theory (emergent behaviors from simple interconnected components, akin to synchronizing metronomes) and introducing *sparsity* (discarding non-essential connections), the system becomes more scalable, trainable, and performant.
* **Rapid Hardware Prototyping:** Starting in January, the team taped out their first physical dynamical computer design to the fab on June 1st (5 months later) and successfully generated the world's first images from such a computer.
* **Power Efficiency Gains:** The physical prototype achieves image generation at roughly 500 nanojoules per image, representing orders of magnitude greater efficiency than standard GPUs.

### Product Roadmap, Software, and Team Dynamics
* **Product Form Factor:** **Naveen Rao** states the company is building a full data center rack system (a new data center product) where users feed tokens in and get tokens out through a network cable, while the inner guts run on dynamical computing.
* **Model Compatibility:** Existing AI models are ported at the model layer rather than the operations layer; the system implements time-varying behaviors that can be characterized mathematically as state transitions without relying on traditional linear algebra/floating-point hardware math.
* **Multidisciplinary Workforce:** **Naveen Rao** notes the company bridges a massive talent gap by combining theorists with math/theoretical neuroscience PhDs (studying dynamical systems) with physical chip architects, coordinated through a Python-based software library designed to express stochastic, time-varying elements.

## Why the Brain Computes 1,000,000x More Efficiently Than A GPU: Unconventional AI's Naveen Rao

- Channel: Sequoia Capital

- Speakers: Naveen Rao (founder and CEO, Unconventional AI)

---

**TL;DR**
Current AI scaling is hitting a physical "energy wall" that conventional GPU architectures cannot overcome; achieving Artificial Super Intelligence (ASI) requires a fundamental shift from digital matrix math to nonlinear dynamical systems modeled after the 1,000,000x more efficient human brain.

### The Impending Energy Wall
* **Scaling Limitations:** Naveen Rao argues that ASI is impossible on our current trajectory because we are hitting the limits of the physical world. 
* **The 2-4 Year Window:** Global energy capacity for AI will be exhausted within the next few years. While the world has ~9,000 gigawatts of total capacity, AI training and inference are already consuming multiple gigawatts at a rate that cannot be sustained by current infrastructure.
* **The Biological Benchmark:** 
    * **Humanity’s Footprint:** The entire human race (8 billion people) runs on only 160 gigawatts of power.
    * **Individual Efficiency:** A human brain operates on ~20 watts. In contrast, a squirrel performs complex navigation on less than 10 milliwatts (1/100th of a smartphone’s power).
    * **The Efficiency Gap:** Current digital chips are roughly three orders of magnitude (1,000x) away from the thermodynamic limit of "intelligence per watt."

### Moving Beyond the 80-Year-Old Digital Abstraction
* **Obsolescence of the Substrate:** Our current computing paradigm (floating point numbers and von Neumann architecture) was designed 80 years ago for entirely different purposes.
* **The Von Neumann Bottleneck:** Traditional systems waste the majority of their energy moving state in and out of memory (writing, retrieving, and operating).
* **Physics-First Computing:** Unconventional AI is moving toward "Non-von Neumann" architectures where there is no separation between state and computation.
    * **Nonlinear Dynamics:** Instead of matrix math, the system uses time-varying interactions between "neurons" to process information.
    * **Computing in the Time Domain:** Rather than explicit digital steps, the system uses the "time axis" of physics to perform computation. You set an initial state, "kick it," and let the physical dynamics converge on an answer.

### Technical Strategy and Prototyping
* **Unconventional AI Prototype:** The company went from a standing start in January to a chip prototype (taping out this summer) in just six months. 
    * **AI-Assisted Design:** This rapid development was made possible by using existing AI to design the next generation of AI hardware.
* **Stochastic Computing:** Unlike digital computers that fail if a single bit is wrong, these new systems are stochastic and robust, similar to biological brains.
* **Generative Proof of Concept:** Rao demonstrated a model where image generation occurs through "converging dynamical systems." The system starts with randomness and naturally "clumps" into meaningful representations (e.g., images of cats or horses) based on trained physical couplings.

### The Future of the Compute Stack
* **Evolution of Hardware:** 
    * **CPUs:** Best for fast, single-threaded operations.
    * **GPUs:** Best for massive parallel matrix operations (moving many operands at once).
    * **Dynamical Systems:** The next frontier where state and function overlap within the physics of the circuit, maximizing "galaxy brain" efficiency.
* **Landauer’s Principle:** The company’s ultimate goal is pushing toward the physical asymptote of the Landauer limit—the theoretical minimum energy required to change one bit of information.
