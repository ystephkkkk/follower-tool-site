* **Lessons from 1982:** Recalling the low morale of the Reagan administration in early 1982, Rubio noted that four law students organized a federalism symposium in New Haven, Connecticut, attended by about 200 people, featuring a 46-year-old Antonin Scalia who diagnosed that conservatives had been outgunned in courts for half a century.

  * Domestic immigration enforcement by ICE, which prompted the ACLU to petition the UN regarding international law violations.

* **Judeo-Christian Foundations:** Responding to questions about Christian attorneys and faith, Rubio stated that the legal system's presumption of innocence and emphasis on individual human dignity are deeply rooted in Judeo-Christian principles rather than government benevolence.

### National Interest in Foreign Policy
* **America First Diplomacy:** Rubio argued that the primary duty of U.S. leaders is to work for Americans, contrasting China's unapologetic pursuit of national interests with America's past drift toward globalism.
* **The Threat from Iran:** Detailing the Iranian regime's history of sponsoring terrorism and pursuing nuclear capabilities, Rubio defined American victory in the conflict as ensuring that the clerical regime—which he described as expansionist revolutionaries waging war on the U.S. since 1979—never obtains a nuclear weapon.
