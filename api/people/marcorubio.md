* **Decline of the *Thumos*:** Rubio argued that modern Western ideologies have prioritized safety and mediocrity over excellence, holding exceptional potential hostage to the average.

* **Greece as a Model Ally:** Greece has proven to be an indispensable friend to Europe and the United States on energy, migration, and defense.
* **Non-Zero-Sum Cooperation:** The transatlantic partnership is not zero-sum, as demonstrated by the unprecedented new agreement between the **United States, Denmark, and Greenland** to reshape the Arctic and bolster NATO security.

### Honor, Duty, and the Moral Imagination
* **The Ideal of the Exceptional Individual:** Ancient Greeks believed in the great man who achieves immortality through great deeds, striving and sacrificing for a cause larger than himself.
* **Historical Paragons of Duty:** 
  * **Socrates:** Refused to abandon the inquiry into truth even at the cost of his life.
