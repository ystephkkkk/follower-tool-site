### TL;DR
Republican gubernatorial candidate Vivek Ramaswamy outlines his 2026 platform for Ohio, emphasizing immediate property tax rollbacks, stringent regulations on data centers, Medicaid fraud reduction, educational choice expansion, and economic growth through business experience and federal alignment.

### Property & Income Taxes
* **Immediate Property Tax Relief:** 
  * **Goal:** Implement the largest property tax rollback in Ohio history by resetting property taxes to pre-pandemic levels and capping future growth.
  * **Implementation Timeline:** Target for homeowners to feel net financial impact by this time in 2027.
  * **Funding Mechanism:** Backstop local shortfalls (police, fire, schools, veteran services) using state economic growth surpluses, similar to the $1.6 billion gas tax suspension playbook.
* **Long-Term Income Tax Phasing:** 
  * **Timeline:** An 8- to 10-year trajectory to reach zero income tax to keep residents and investment capital from moving to zero-tax states like Florida and Tennessee.
  * **Initial Steps (2027):** Eliminate the capital gains tax to attract high-paying investment dollars without risking major immediate state revenue losses.
* **State Budget Context:** Noted Ohio's $30 billion general revenue fund discretionary portion includes $8 billion for Medicaid, $12 billion for K-12 education, $3 billion for universities, and $3 billion for prisons.

### Data Center Regulations & Economic Development
* **Strict Construction Conditions:** 
  * **Mandates:** Data centers must provide free electricity to local neighborhoods, eliminate property tax abatements, ensure discharged water is as clean as intake water, provide transparency (no NDAs), use vegetative insulation (trees), and build on abandoned industrial sites rather than farmland or residential areas.
  * **Enforcement:** Proposed an executive order to halt new data center construction and announcements until enabling legislation passes.
* **Tax Exemption Stance:** Criticized sales and property tax exemptions for data centers as crony capitalism, emphasizing that abatements for data centers are "unforgivable" while homeowners face skyrocketing property taxes.
* **Alternative Growth:** Prioritizing manufacturing booms and defense industrial bases in Springfield, Dayton, and Youngstown over building data centers without local community benefits.

### Government Efficiency & Medicaid Reform
* **Waste, Fraud, and Abuse:** 
  * **Medicaid Spending:** Ohio spends $47 billion annually on Medicaid.
  * **Fraud Estimates:** Independent estimates suggest up to $4.7 billion annually is lost to shell companies, non-existent nursing homes, and fraudulent home healthcare providers.
  * **Action Plan:** Partner with the Attorney General starting in January to prosecute fraud and negotiate with the federal government to keep recovered savings in Ohio rather than sending them back to Washington D.C.

### School Funding & School Choice
* **Educational Choice & Mobility:** 
  * **Vouchers & District Choice:** Supports continuing Ed-choice vouchers and expanding choice within public school districts (magnet programs, special needs) and across public districts.
  * **Personal Background:** Attended public schools for nine years before transferring to St. Xavier High School for high school.
* **Public School Improvements:** 
  * **Fair School Funding Formula:** Committed to keeping the current fair school funding formula as law.
  * **Teacher Compensation:** Wants to raise starting salaries for public school teachers above the current state average of approximately $38,000 per year.
  * **Classroom Focus:** Direct a greater portion of public education dollars away from non-classroom expenditures and toward direct classroom instruction.
  * **Literacy Goal:** Target 100% end-of-third-grade literacy for every medically capable student.

### Campaign, Politics & Federal Relations
* **Relationship with Donald Trump:** Emphasizes that having a governor with a strong relationship with the U.S. President is an advantage for securing federal investment dollars from the $900 billion flowing into the U.S., helping Ohio punch above its weight compared to states like Texas, Florida, California, and New York.
* **Criticism of Opponent:** Criticized the Democratic opponent (former Health Director Amy Acton) for quitting her prior health director post, refusing to participate in debates (such as those offered by CNN and NBC), refusing cameras at events, and blockading interactions at a Fraternal Order of Police barbecue.
* **Personal Motivations:** Highlighted his background as a middle-class kid from Evendale, Ohio, whose father worked at the GE plant and mother worked in nursing homes and the VA, and underscored his success building Roivant, a $25–$30 billion NASDAQ-listed biotech company that developed 6 FDA-approved medicines.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Property Tax Relief Timeline:** Ramaswamy predicts direct property tax relief will be felt by homeowners by this time in 2027.
* **Income Tax Elimination Timeline:** Ramaswamy states that reaching zero state income tax will follow an 8- to 10-year trajectory.
* **Capital Gains Elimination:** Ramaswamy predicts the state will eliminate capital gains taxes in 2027 to attract investment dollars.
* **Literacy Benchmark:** Ramaswamy sets a target to eventually achieve 100% end-of-third-grade literacy for every medically capable student in Ohio.

Vivek Ramaswamy has secured the Ohio Republican gubernatorial nomination, framing the upcoming general election as a choice between "celebrating success" and "socialist" policies while pledging to transform Ohio into the nation’s leading state for economic growth and education.

### The General Election Matchup: Ramaswamy vs. Acton
* **Contrast in Philosophy:** Ramaswamy characterized the race against Democrat Amy Acton as a choice between two diametrically opposed visions for Ohio.
    * **Celebrating Success:** Ramaswamy positioned himself as a "pro-free enterprise, pro-liberty conservative" who views individual and economic success as a state asset.
    * **Critique of Opponent:** He labeled Acton a "socialist" who "villainizes success" and highlighted her background as a billionaire critic despite his own "heir to nothing" upbringing.
* **Unified GOP Front:** Ramaswamy emphasized that the Republican party is entering the general election cycle more united than ever, supported by endorsements from President Donald Trump and current Governor Mike DeWine.
* **Running Mate Selection:** He officially introduced Ohio Senate President **Rob McColley** as his Lieutenant Governor nominee, citing their complementary skills as essential for leading the state.

### Economic Prosperity and Fiscal Reform
* **Tax Reduction:** Ramaswamy proposed a path toward a **zero-income tax state** to compete with high-growth states like Texas, Florida, and Tennessee.
    * He pledged to reduce property taxes, asserting that land belongs to the citizens, not the government.
* **Energy and Housing:** A core pillar of the platform includes lowering electric bills by increasing in-state energy production and reducing "red tape" to lower housing prices and encourage homeownership for the next generation.
* **Government Efficiency:** 
    * **Medicaid Reform:** Plans to "wring out the waste, fraud, and abuse" from the Medicaid system to reduce health insurance premiums.
    * **Regulatory Cuts:** A commitment to making Ohio the most "pro-capitalist" state by fostering an environment where businesses no longer flee to other regions.

### Education and the "American Dream"
* **Educational Standards:** Ramaswamy advocated for a "world-class education" focused on math, reading, and writing rather than "victimhood psychology" or "indoctrination."
* **Personal Narrative:** He cited his own life—as the son of immigrants who attended public schools and built successful businesses—as proof that the American Dream is still achievable in Ohio.
* **National Ambition:** The goal is for Ohio to lead the nation in education and family-centric policies, moving beyond being "the best in the Midwest" to becoming the "national standard."

### Critique of Pandemic Management and Leadership
* **The Lockdown Record:** Ramaswamy leveled a "damning indictment" against Amy Acton’s tenure as Health Director, specifically citing the two-year closure of public schools.
    * He highlighted the disparity between public school closures and private schools (like his alma mater, Saint X), which remained open.
* **Scientific Accountability:** He criticized Acton for failing to distinguish between high-risk seniors and low-risk students, and for ignoring natural immunity.
* **Leadership Fitness:** 
    * **Responsibility:** Ramaswamy argued that Acton’s decision to "quit" when challenged by legislators proved she was unfit for the "hard work" of leadership.
    * **Pledge of Persistence:** He vowed to "never quit on Ohio" and to make decisions based on nuanced data rather than "lazy" or "scientific authority" signaling.

### Historical Context and Path to November
* **Heritage of Innovation:** Ramaswamy invoked Ohio’s history as a leader in the industrial revolution, citing innovators like Thomas Edison and the Wright Brothers to inspire a "new dawn" for the state.
* **Voter Mobilization:** He issued a "1-to-10" challenge to supporters, asking each voter to bring nine others to the polls in November, specifically targeting Independents, Libertarians, and "orphaned Reagan Democrats."
