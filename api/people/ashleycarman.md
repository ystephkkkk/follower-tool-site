## Suno's Mikey Shulman on the AI Music Landscape

- Channel: Bloomberg Live
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZYkFvILSI-0
- Published: 2026-10-01
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20261001.html#video-ZYkFvILSI-0
- Speakers: Mikey Shulman (Co-Founder & CEO, Suno); Ashley Carman (Reporter, Bloomberg); Lucas Shaw (Reporter, Bloomberg)

---

#### TL;DR
Suno CEO Mikey Shulman discusses the company's evolution from a controversial generative music tool into a broader "creative entertainment" platform, detailing industry partnerships, upcoming speech products, and a vision for deeply interactive fan-artist music experiences.

#### Company Vision and Product Ecosystem
* **Creative Entertainment over Passive Consumption:** **Mikey Shulman** frames Suno's primary purpose as providing a wholesome creative experience for everyone rather than competing directly with traditional streaming consumption models.
  * The platform appeals to both Grammy winners refining their work and grandmothers making music for fun.
  * Average user sessions on Suno are about 30 minutes long.
* **Blurring the Line Between Hobbyist and Professional:** **Mikey Shulman** notes that while professional artists, producers, and songwriters widely use Suno bits and pieces in smash hits, the company deliberately avoids rigid distinctions between hobbyists and professionals.
* **Expansion into Speech:** **Mikey Shulman** announced the rollout of Suno's first hyper-realistic, hyper-expressive speech product designed for creative use cases like bringing poems to life or creating expressive audio content.

#### Artist Adoption and Industry Partnerships
* **Overcoming Industry Hesitation:** **Mikey Shulman** explains that public artist adoption is low due to social media noise and fear of being the first to stick their neck out against prevailing industry controversy, though industry usage behind the scenes is widespread.
* **Co-Creative Products with Rightsholders:** **Mikey Shulman** highlights that Suno has secured deals with partners like Warner and BMG to build models and products that let users play with, remix, sample, and mash up music from their favorite artists.
* **The Litigious Landscape:** **Ashley Carman and Lucas Shaw** point out ongoing copyright disputes and lawsuits from holdouts Universal Music and Sony regarding unlicensed training data, to which **Mikey Shulman** responds that the music industry is historically litigious and will eventually come around once the growth potential and new revenue streams are realized.

#### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Mikey Shulman** predicts that within 5 years, fans will be able to buy interactive albums from their favorite artists, featuring fill-in-the-blank or adaptable portions of songs designed to be personalized and co-created.
* **Mikey Shulman** predicts that interactive fan-made song versions will eventually bridge the digital and physical worlds, with artists playing audience-customized tracks during live hometown concerts.
* **Mikey Shulman** predicts that 3 years from now, the number of Suno songs created every day will be "infinite" (many millions, comparable to the daily volume of photos taken).
* **Mikey Shulman** predicts that in the future, whether a song is generated by AI will become entirely irrelevant, comparing the trajectory to the acceptance of Auto-Tune over the last 20 years.

#### Notable Quotes
> *"The magic of AI is that it's a technology that lets us give a creative experience, literally, to everyone. And so, in that light, I think of the purpose of Suno is creative entertainment."* — **Mikey Shulman**

> *"I think that, while AI moves very quickly, there's still so much that is, um unknown, um in a very good way of how the new rules of the road should work."* — **Mikey Shulman**

> *"I think these things take time before people really understand and acclimate to them... I can tell you with every person that I've demoed these things to, it's a pretty magical aha moment."* — **Mikey Shulman**
