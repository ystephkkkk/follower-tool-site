## Chess Club: Bringing Hands and Mixed Reality to Meta Quest

- Channel: Meta Developers
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iOw3zYYlur8
- Published: 2026-09-15
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260915.html#video-iOw3zYYlur8
- Speakers: Pichardo García (Developer, Odders Lab)

---

**TL;DR:** Odders Lab evolved *Chess Club* on Meta Quest by integrating hand tracking and mixed reality, achieving over 100,000 installs and a 4-star rating while proving that natural, controller-free interactions resonate strongly with casual players.

### Project Background & Metrics
- **Company Origins & Team:** 
  - Created by Odders Lab, a spin-off from an e-health company that previously developed *OhShape*.
  - Led by Jaime Pichardo (CSO), with a team of around 10 people working across the project.
- **Adoption & Reception:**
  - Surpassed **100,000 installs** with a **4-star rating** across platforms.
  - Maintained an evergreen status with steady activity and new player influence since launching in 2021.
  - Around the hand tracking update window, Odders Lab saw **2x more positive reviews** mentioning the feature.
  - A large percentage of users tried playing with hands, with a specific subset of casual and older users sticking to hand tracking exclusively.
  - Hardcore competitive players typically tried hands a few times before reverting to controllers.

### Hand Tracking & Interaction Design
- **Natural Mechanics:** 
  - Designed to make reaching for pieces, picking them up, and moving them feel as close to real-life chess as possible.
  - Initial implementation was clunky, but early tests confirmed chess is a natural fit for hand tracking due to familiar, slow-paced actions.
- **Technical Stack:** 
  - Relied primarily on the Meta Interaction SDK.
  - Removed early custom development as the Interaction SDK improved over the years, eventually shifting to **80% to 90%** reliance on the native SDK.
- **Precision & Comfort Adjustments:** 
  - Added an undo button and move cancellation features to mitigate precision errors.
  - Leveraged SDK updates to handle faster movements and reduce initial player frustrations.
- **Emergent Physics & Social Gestures:** 
  - Players naturally engaged in non-gameplay behaviors like balancing, throwing, and playing with pieces, prompting the team to add fun physics.
  - Implemented emoji reactions triggered by social hand gestures (e.g., waving, hand signs).

### Mixed Reality & Real-World Integration
- **Play Space Adaptation:** 
  - Built mixed reality play directly on real tables, allowing users to place virtual boards on physical furniture.
  - Allowed players to remain present in their homes, seeing family and touching physical surroundings without breaking immersion.
- **Cozy Aesthetics:** 
  - Enhanced surroundings to add warmth and coziness, creating a different dimension compared to full VR.

### Player Retention & Long-Term Engagement
- **Competitive & Solo Features:** 
  - Implemented ELO matchmaking to maintain player concurrency.
  - Added daily puzzles last year, significantly increasing player retention and giving users a quick reason to return.
- **Community Focus:** 
  - Joined **Horizon Plus** to boost visibility and player acquisition.
  - Shifted current focus away from heavy feature additions toward community building and reducing matchmaking wait times.

### Development Lessons & Best Practices
- **Effort vs. Impact Evaluation:** 
  - Developers must weigh whether hand tracking genuinely improves the game or if the effort outweighs the value.
  - Do not add hand tracking solely for short-term platform exposure or novelty if it compromises long-term success.
- **Handling Haptics Trade-offs:** 
  - Hand tracking lacks haptic feedback; developers must offset this absence by making visual and physical interactions feel exceptionally cool and smooth.
- **Design Constraints:** 
  - Avoid designing interactions around edge cases where hand tracking tracking fails (unlike controllers, which have flawless tracking).
  - Perfect the physics; clunky hand physics instantly break immersion.
