Google DeepMind introduced the **Gemini Robotics 2** model suite, bringing whole-body intelligence, fine-grained dexterity (supporting hands with up to 20 joints per hand), and multi-robot collaboration to general-purpose robotics. The Embodied Reasoning (ER) model is available via AI Studio and Gemini Enterprise Agents Platform, while action models are accessible through a trusted tester program and deep partnerships.

---

### 1. Core Model Suite: Gemini Robotics 2
* **Whole-Body Intelligence:** **Carolina Parada** noted that the new models understand the spatial position of every part of the robot simultaneously, enabling complex tasks like organizing a garage or cleaning closets.
* **Dexterity Upgrades:** **Jie Tan** explained that the system handles contact-rich, high-degree-of-freedom manipulation (such as packing lunch, screwing in lightbulbs, and tying trash bags).
* **Multi-Robot Collaboration:** **Carolina Parada** highlighted that robots can now recognize their own limits and coordinate with other specialized robots in parallel to accelerate shared tasks.

### 2. Research Arc and Technological Evolution
* **From Reinforcement Learning to VLAs:** **Carolina Parada** traced the team's history from pioneering reinforcement learning and sim-to-real transfer for locomotion, to integrating LLMs/VLMs for semantics, transformers for data-driven robotics, and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) foundation models.
* **Frontier Model Leverage:** **Stuart Bowers** emphasized that instead of solving robotics from scratch, the team latched physical tasks onto the world-understanding capabilities of large frontier models like Gemini, treating robot actions as an added modality.
* **The Data Pyramid Challenge:** **Jie Tan** outlined the data bottleneck: while teleoperation provides high-quality action signals, it does not scale; egocentric human video lacks actuation/force labels; and the lack of an "internet of physical interaction" remains the primary open obstacle to physical AGI.

### 3. Deployment Strategy & Real-World Horizons
* **Broad Tasks Over Brittle Niches:** **Carolina Parada** and **Stuart Bowers** stressed that training on narrow tasks creates brittle policies; the strategy is to build for general-purpose tasks first, even though initial commercial deployments will focus on semi-structured industrial and retail environments rather than messy homes.
* **The Partner Ecosystem:** **Stuart Bowers** noted that all robot hardware used in the lab is built by deep external partners, with whom Google DeepMind works closely to co-accelerate AI capabilities, safety, and hardware readiness.
* **Timeline to Daily Life:** **Jie Tan** revised his estimate for when general-purpose robots will enter daily life down from 10 years (estimated two years ago) to **5 to 10 years**.

### 4. Safety, Benchmarking, and Accessibility
* **Proactive Safety & Clarification:** **Carolina Parada** stated that ER 2.0 is the team's safest model yet, featuring the ability to detect human proximity, evaluate task completion likelihood, and proactively ask clarifying questions when given ambiguous instructions.
* **Asimov Benchmark:** **Carolina Parada** announced the open-sourcing of **Asimov Agentic**, a new safety benchmark featuring real-world examples to test semantic physical understanding and common sense.
* **API & Platform Availability:** **Carolina Parada** confirmed that Embodied Reasoning 2 is accessible via **AI Studio** and coming soon to **Gemini Enterprise Agents Platform**, while action models are available via API, partner integrations, and a trusted tester waitlist for on-device fine-uning.

**TL;DR** 
Google is transitioning Android from a traditional operating system into an "intelligence system" driven by user intent rather than manual inputs. This shift is headlined by the upcoming summer release of the Gemini Intelligence suite and multimodal, vision-enabled AI integration in vehicles like the unreleased Volvo EX60.

---

### Shifting from Operating Systems to Intelligence Systems
*   **The "Micromanager" Constraint:** For over 40 years, computing has forced users to act as micromanagers—breaking down high-level goals into discrete command-line or GUI inputs. 
*   **The Intelligence Paradigm:** Google is steering Android toward a system where users simply articulate a goal, and the platform manages the context, resources, and execution to deliver the outcome.
*   **Focusing on Benefits Over "AI" Hype:** 
    *   During the recent Android Show, Google purposely avoided using the word "AI" to prevent consumer alienation.
    *   Features like *Circle to Search* succeeded rapidly because marketing focused entirely on user utility (e.g., identifying clothing or landmarks on-screen) rather than the complex machine vision powering it.
*   **On-Device Safety and Performance:** 
    *   **Scam Detection:** Android features an on-device Google DeepMind (GDM) model that can listen to calls from unknown numbers, recognize requests a bank would never make, and alert the user to hang up.
    *   **Tiered Optimization:** Google targets premium flagship hardware to push the boundaries of system resources, subsequently optimizing and "flowing down" these capabilities to lower-end devices via server-side support or specialized lightweight models.

### Gemini Intelligence: Summer Feature Suite
Google introduced *Gemini Intelligence*, a suite of premium features coming to high-end Android devices:

*   **Rambler (Voice Dictation):** 
    *   An upgraded keyboard feature that allows users to verbally "ramble." 
    *   Instead of literal transcription, it instantly distills messy, conversational audio (removing "ums" and "ahs") into a polished, structured text message or document.
*   **App Automation:** 
    *   Users can ask Gemini to execute complex workflows inside third-party apps (e.g., compiling an Instacart shopping cart based on a barbecue guest list).
    *   **Virtual Window System:** For privacy and security, Android runs automated tasks in a containerized, virtual environment. The AI cannot access external apps, and the process pauses before committing any financial transaction.
*   **Super Fill:** 
    *   An evolution of Autofill that securely retrieves complex personal data (such as passport numbers, driver's licenses, or license plates) from Google Photos or Gmail to instantly populate mobile forms.
*   **Generative Widgets:** 
    *   Enables users to prompt Gemini to build custom UI elements on the fly (e.g., a real-time F1 lap time tracker pulled from public web sources).
*   **Android Halo:** 
    *   A dedicated location in the status bar where AI agents running long-running background tasks can seamlessly update the user or request necessary feedback.

### The Future of Apps: Experiential vs. Transactional
*   **Debunking the "App-pocalypse":** Sameer Samat argues that the rise of agentic AI will not make apps obsolete, though it will reshape their architecture:
    *   **Experiential Apps:** Users spend 70% to 80% of their mobile time in experiential spaces (e.g., YouTube, TikTok, gaming, messaging). These apps will remain highly manual and immersive, as users do not want agents consuming entertainment on their behalf.
    *   **Transactional Apps:** Apps focused on utility (e.g., booking flights, ordering food) will bifurcate. They will offer a rich "experiential" mode for deep user engagement alongside a background "agentic" mode optimized to answer machine-to-machine queries.

### Multimodal Mobility: Gemini in the Volvo EX60
In a live demonstration inside the unreleased Volvo EX60 featuring Android-powered Google built-in systems, Samat showcased deep AI integration on the road:

*   **Direct Infotainment Integration:** Gemini features high-level control over the car's native software, modifying cabin climate or turning on lane-keeping assist via conversational voice commands.
*   **Multimodal Vision Capabilities:** 
    *   With user permission, Gemini can access the car's front-facing cameras via local buffers.
    *   In the demo, Gemini successfully identified real-world landmarks outside the vehicle—including Google's "Gradient Canopy" solar-roof building, "The Orb" public art installation, and the Shoreline Amphitheatre—explaining their history in real-time.

### The Transition to Agentic AI and the "Anti-Gravity" Harness

* **The Agentic Gemini Era:** Google is transitioning its massive product suite from simple language model integration to agentic-native operations that proactively take actions on behalf of users.
* **The "Anti-Gravity" Connective Tissue:** Born from the Windsurf team acquisition, the Anti-Gravity agent harness has replaced the raw Gemini model as the primary connective tissue across all Google products.
    * **Cross-Product Deployment:** Anti-Gravity powers agentic workflows across Google Search, the Gemini consumer app, Google Cloud, and Google AI Studio.
    * **Specialization and Customization:** While 80% of the underlying codebase is shared, the harness is optimized differently across surfaces—such as "vibe coding" in AI Studio versus always-on consumer behaviors in the Gemini app.
* **Outcome-Driven Metrics over "Eyeballs":** Google is shifting its success metrics away from maximizing user attention and screen time toward maximizing user outcomes. 
    * **Positive-Sum AI Search:** Contrary to early industry fears that generative answers would cannibalize Search traffic, Google has found that AI-integrated Search increases overall user query volume and ecosystem value.
* **The Crawl, Walk, Run Scale:** Most of Google's consumer products are currently in the "crawl" phase. Given Google's responsibility in managing 13 products with over one billion users each, the company introduces agentic features incrementally to prevent sudden, disruptive changes to user habits.

---

### Why the "Model Eats the Harness"

* **The Scaffolding Shelf Life:** Logan Kilpatrick argues that the startup race to build external agent harnesses has a ~12-month window. Models will rapidly absorb these external layers and run them natively.
    * **Upstreaming of Features:** Capabilities that begin as external code orchestration (e.g., tool calling, secure code execution, containerized model runs) are consistently absorbed directly into foundational model weights and native model systems over time.
* **Shifting Value Capture for Startups:** Because the foundational model will inevitably "eat" the infrastructure layer, application developers cannot rely on custom harnesses for long-term defensibility.
    * **Domain Expertise as the New Edge:** Defensibility will reside in deep vertical expertise, proprietary workflows, user trust, and focus.
    * **Focus as a Startup Superpower:** Unlike giant companies that must serve thousands of diverse user needs simultaneously, startups can dominate specific niches by moving faster and embracing a higher risk tolerance.
* **Industry Standard Benchmarking:** To measure how effectively different frontier models adapt to various developer structures, Kilpatrick calls for the creation of an industry-standard "Harness Bench" evaluation.

---

### Coding and the Rise of "Jagged" Superintelligence

* **Narrow Superintelligence in Coding:** Autonomous coding already exhibits properties of narrow superintelligence, drastically accelerating software creation and resetting human developer ambition.
    * **Rapid Internal Prototyping:** Google teams are utilizing Anti-Gravity to build and ship complex software, such as Google's native macOS app, faster than ever before.
    * **Massive User Adoption:** Over 350,000 Android apps have been built inside Google AI Studio in a single week, enabling non-traditional developers to build highly localized, personal software.
* **Post-Training Dominance:** Highlighting recent engineering efficiencies, Gemini 1.5 Flash achieved its massive coding and reasoning gains over previous Pro models entirely through post-training optimization rather than new pre-training runs.
* **"Jagged" Vertical Superintelligence:** Rather than achieving general AGI uniformly, the industry will first see "jagged" superintelligence across specific, highly verifiable verticals, including:
    * Mathematics and formal proofs.
    * Quantitative finance and crypto-related personal productivity tools.
    * Scientific research and therapeutic discovery.
* **Vibe Coding Video Games:** Kilpatrick stands by his prediction that average users will be able to "vibe code" playable video games by the end of 2025.
    * **Current Bottlenecks:** While the core coding capabilities exist, game development remains bottlenecked by the orchestration of non-code elements, such as native sprite generation, Three.js scaffolding, and asset rendering.

### World Models and the Unified "Omni" Architecture

* **The Omni Architecture:** Google’s Omni represents a shift from training eight distinct, routed models (for text, audio, music, images, and video) to a single, natively multimodal model.
* **Redefining World Models:** Omni acts as a true world model by displaying a physical understanding of reality, allowing for highly nuanced, context-aware video editing in real-time.

* **Generative Media and Authenticity:** Kilpatrick views generative media as an accelerant for human creativity, not a replacement for human presence. 
    * **Content Preservation:** Rather than generating synthetic AI avatars, tools like Omni allow creators to preserve their authentic words, voice, and appearance while instantly swapping and customizing background sets, environments, and digital objects.

### Google DeepMind's Scientific Culture and Strategy

## Gemini co-leads on project origins and what's next

- Channel: Google for Developers
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8hfpLa5wPGo
- Published: 2026-05-29

- Speakers: Logan Kilpatrick (Host & AI Advocate, Google); Jeff Dean (Chief Scientist, Google); Noam Shazeer (Co-lead of Gemini, Google)

---

**TL;DR**  
The Gemini project was established by consolidating Google's fragmented AI research teams and compute resources into a unified effort. This strategic alignment has accelerated multimodal "world modeling," yielded highly efficient model distillation (packing "Pro" capabilities into "Flash" versions), and set the stage for agentic self-learning systems.

---

### The Origins and Consolidation of Project Gemini

* **Eliminating Fragmentation:** Prior to Gemini, Google's AI efforts were split across DeepMind and Google Brain (working on Pathways, PaLM, and PaLM 2). Jeff Dean authored a half-page memo arguing that splitting compute and talent was "silly" and that Google must consolidate into a single, unified team to build a frontier model.
* **The "Gemini" Name:** The project's name stems from "twins," representing the map-and-reduce fusion of Google’s two premier AI research groups (and nod to Jeff Dean having twins).
* **Deep Collaborative Roots:** The leadership team shares decades of history:
    * Jeff Dean recruited Noam Shazeer to Google in 2000 and acted as his engineering mentor.
    * Dean recruited Oriol Vinyals in 2012 to the Google Brain team to work on early deep learning models and distillation.
    * Dean and Koray Kavukcuoglu first collaborated by reviewing raw codebase directories together during Google's acquisition of DeepMind.

---

### Model Performance, Distillation, and the "Flash" Phenomenon

* **The Generation Leap:** A consistent, positive surprise for the team is that each subsequent "Flash" generation successfully packs the intelligence of the previous generation’s larger "Pro" model, accelerating efficiency.
* **The Distillation Recipe:** Rather than using complex ensembles of dozens of models, Google uses a highly optimized student-teacher distillation process with modest tweaks to the original distillation paper.
* **Multimodal Joint Training:** By training jointly across text, video, audio, and physical world datasets (such as robotics, lidar, and genomics), the model develops "world modeling" capabilities—understanding and simulating visual consistency, depth, and physical dynamics.
* **The Evaluation Bottleneck:** Robustly evaluating LLM capabilities in a way that aligns with real-world user experiences (rather than overfitting to leaked benchmarks) remains one of the most persistent and critical challenges in frontier AI development.

---

### Predictions and Technical Frontiers for 2027

* **Self-Learning and Agentic Research:** The team predicts that within a few years, models will use agentic workflows to actively research, run experiments, and improve subsequent generations of Gemini under human guidance.
* **Dynamic Continual Learning:** Researchers are aiming for a paradigm shift where models dynamically update their internal knowledge bases through interaction and experience without needing expensive weight updates.
* **Tool Latency as a Bottleneck:** As autonomous agents run for longer periods (e.g., executing tasks over 30 days), the primary bottleneck will be the latency of current digital tools, which were designed for human speed rather than model speed.
* **The Efficiency Gap:** Humans require vastly less data to learn. A human hears roughly one billion words in a lifetime, whereas models require trillions of tokens to achieve similar capabilities. Closing this data-efficiency gap is a key algorithmic priority.

---

### The Evolution of Google's Product Strategy

* **The "One Box" Realization:** Modern general-purpose models fulfill Google’s early search philosophy of a single "one-box" interface, but with a unified backend instead of fragmented custom engines.
* **The Ecosystem Debate:** The leadership team debated whether AI will eventually consolidate into a single central "model product" or diverge into thousands of specialized products:
    * **Single Model View:** Oriol Vinyals noted that there is clearly one unified model powering the backend, though users may still prefer distinct digital interfaces for separation of concerns.
    * **Multitude of Products:** Koray Kavukcuoglu expects a massive expansion of niche products because general intelligence will make it incredibly cheap and easy to build specialized applications.

## Introducing Gemini Omni

- Channel: Google for Developers
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5T0yRNmNRi4
- Published: 2026-05-19
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260519.html#video-5T0yRNmNRi4
- Speakers: Logan Kilpatrick (Host); Nicole Brichtova (Google DeepMind); Dumitru Erhan (Google DeepMind)

---

**TL;DR**  
Google DeepMind has introduced Gemini Omni, a native multimodal model that acts as "Nano Banana for video" by seamlessly combining text, image, audio, and video reference inputs to enable highly controllable 10-second video generation and precise editing.

---

### Architecture & Multimodal Capabilities

* **Fully Multimodal In/Out:** Gemini Omni is built from the ground up to understand and synthesize multiple modalities simultaneously. It processes text instructions, reference images, audio, and video clips to generate highly consistent, synchronized video and audio output.
* **The "Nano Banana for Video" Concept:** While Google's Veo model focused on professional video generation, Gemini Omni specializes in flexible, language-guided video editing and consumer-focused interactions.
* **Multi-Reference Consistency:** 
    * **Subject Modeling:** Users can input up to seven reference photos or videos of a subject. By supplying shots from multiple angles (e.g., side profiles), the model builds a robust 3D-like understanding of the subject's face and structure.
    * **Voice & Speech Matching:** The model retains high vocal and speech consistency during edits, such as seamlessly transferring a human speaker's voice to an animated character or animal.
* **Temporal Awareness and Pacing:** Gemini Omni demonstrates superior chronological control over previous models. Users can prompt it to generate fast-paced sequential frames (e.g., animating the entire alphabet with corresponding objects in under 10 seconds) or request slow-motion outputs.

### Advanced Video Editing & Semantic Reasoning

* **Complex Video Edits:** The model handles challenging editing actions via natural language prompts, such as:
    * **Environmental Transport:** Placing a subject from one source video into an entirely different setting while maintaining action consistency.
    * **Object Removal:** Making a specific object (e.g., a violin being played) completely invisible while keeping the rest of the subject's posture, hands, and audio unchanged.
    * **Perspective Shifts:** Changing camera angles and viewpoint perspectives within an existing scene.
* **Text Rendering Upgrades:** Gemini Omni features a step-change improvement in on-screen text rendering. Because it learns text and visual modalities together natively, it produces highly legible, contextually accurate text overlays.
* **Story Continuity:** The model exhibits emergent storytelling capabilities, allowing users to prompt it to generate "the next scene" in a video. It can intuitively continue a visual sequence—such as panning around a corner or introducing a new character—without explicit training.
* **Style Transfer:** The system generalizes visual styles organically, transforming real-world video footage into creative mediums (e.g., claymation, crochet dolls, or celestial patterns) while keeping the underlying motion and composition stable.

### Ecosystem Integration & Product Availability

* **The Gemini App:** Targeted at mainstream consumers for quick, creative edits and 10-second generations. It is available directly to Gemini Advanced, Ultra, Pro, and Plus users.
* **Google Workspace & Flow:** Designed for professional creators, Flow leverages Gemini Omni for deep, iterative editing, enabling creators to stack scenes, construct persistent characters, and utilize agentic workflows to brainstorm video ideas.
* **YouTube Shorts:** Integrated directly into YouTube Shorts and YouTube Create, allowing users to remix and edit eligible video content using Omni's generative suite.
* **API Access:** Developer APIs are planned for upcoming rollouts to make these generative video tools accessible for third-party application building.

### Safety, Digital Watermarking, and Likeness Control

* **The Avatar Flow:** To protect digital identity and prevent unauthorized deepfakes, users must go through a secure, one-time setup to utilize their own likeness.
    * **Verification Protocol:** Users must record themselves speaking a designated sequence of numbers to verify active consent, alongside submitting multi-angle headshots.
    * **Persistent Storage:** Once generated, the authorized digital avatar is safely stored and can be easily invoked across Google products like YouTube, Flow, and the Gemini App.
* **SynthID and C2PA Integration:**
    * **Resilient Watermarking:** All generated videos are embedded with Google's SynthID watermark, which is imperceptible but designed to persist across subsequent edits, crops, and file compressions.
    * **Cross-Platform Verification:** Users can upload videos to the Gemini App to check if they contain a SynthID watermark. Google also embeds open-standard C2PA metadata in all output files to ensure transparency across external platforms.
