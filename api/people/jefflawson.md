**TL;DR**
The technology sector is undergoing a volatile transition as Wall Street shifts capital from traditional software firms at risk of AI disruption toward hardware infrastructure and next-generation energy solutions like nuclear fusion. 

### The Software Disruption and "Terminal Value" Crisis
* **Market Selloff:** The software index fell over 3% as investors punish companies deemed on the "wrong side" of the AI revolution. 
    * **Impacted Sectors:** Traditional wealth management firms (Charles Schwab, Raymond James) and tax software providers are seeing sharp declines as small AI startups demonstrate the ability to automate complex financial strategies.
* **Valuation Shift:** **Ankur Crawford (Alger)** argues that the "terminal value" of traditional SaaS is being questioned. When AI can write software, the cost to create and maintain it plummets, making it difficult to maintain historical price multiples.
* **Investment Strategy:** 
    * **Infrastructure vs. Software:** Capital is rotating into hardware and "hyperscalers" to support a 14-fold projected increase in AI token growth this year.
    * **Platform Resilience:** Large-cap platform companies are viewed as safer than "point solutions" (small, single-use software tools), which are easily usurped by AI-native competitors.

### Nuclear Fusion: Powering the AI Boom
* **Inertia’s $450M Series A:** Twilio founder **Jeff Lawson** launched a new nuclear fusion startup, Inertia, to address the massive electricity demands of AI data centers.
    * **Technology:** The firm uses laser-based fusion (proven at Lawrence Livermore National Lab). They intend to build a laser 20 times more efficient and 1/10th the size of current lab models.
    * **Timeline:** Inertia aims to bring a 1.5-gigawatt pilot plant online by the 2030s, capable of powering one million homes.
* **The Death of "Per-Seat" Pricing:** Lawson predicts that the AI era will destroy the "per-seat" revenue model for software, as companies will no longer need large teams of humans to perform digital workflows.
