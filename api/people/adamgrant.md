## The one skill you need to succeed in your career in a turbulent world - by Adam Grant

- Published: 2026-04-16

**TL;DR** 
In a turbulent and rapidly changing world, true intelligence is defined by the capacity to rethink and unlearn rather than just the ability to process information. Success now requires leaders and individuals to prioritize character and collective impact over traditional metrics of charisma and individual competence.

### The Evolution of Intelligence and "Rethinking"
*   **The Intelligence Paradox:** Adam Grant argues that in a stable world, intelligence was defined by the ability to think and learn; in a turbulent world, it is defined by the ability to **rethink and unlearn.**
*   **The "I’m-Not-Biased" Bias:** This is the most dangerous cognitive blind spot.
    *   **The Risk for High Performers:** Research shows that the higher an individual scores on an intelligence test, the more likely they are to fall victim to this bias.
    *   **The Rethinking Gap:** Because "smart" people have a long track record of being right, they often struggle more than others to change their minds when the environment shifts.

### Motivation vs. The Attention Span Myth
*   **Stable Concentration Levels:** Grant cites a meta-analysis showing that concentration levels in adults and children have remained stable or even improved over the last three decades.
*   **The Motivation Problem:** The perceived "attention deficit" is actually a lack of motivation.
    *   **Active Engagement:** Teenagers who binge Netflix or play video games for hours are demonstrating sustained, active attention. 
    *   **The Role of Subtitles:** Many young people watch TV with subtitles on, which transitions a passive activity into an active reading and listening exercise.
*   **Gaming and Resilience:** Contrary to stereotypes, video games can build "grit," willpower, and the ability to grapple with failure and collaborative competition.

### The "Confidence-Action" Gap
*   **The Leap of Faith:** Using his background as a junior Olympic springboard diver, Grant explains that most people wait to feel confident before taking action. 
*   **Reverse Causality:** Grant asserts that confidence is the *result* of action, not the prerequisite. 
    *   **Coaching Insight:** When facing a new challenge (like a difficult dive or writing a book), the question shouldn't be "Am I ready?" but "Do I want to do this one day? If yes, why not today?"

### Persuasion and "Motivational Interviewing"
*   **Avoiding the "Preacher/Prosecutor" Trap:** When trying to change minds—even those of extremists—the worst approach is to argue or shame. 
*   **The Daryl Davis Method:** Grant highlights jazz musician Daryl Davis, who talks white supremacists out of their beliefs by using curiosity.
*   **Key Techniques:**
    *   **Motivational Interviewing:** Helping others find their *own* reasons to change rather than providing reasons for them.
    *   **The Fact-Based Pivot:** Asking the question, "What evidence would change your mind?" grounds the conversation in data rather than emotion.
    *   **Complexifying Thinking:** Moving beyond "binary bias" (seeing only two sides) to recognize the "shades of gray" in polarized issues like gun safety or climate change.

### A New Mandate for Leadership
*   **Rejecting the "Dark Triad":** Grant calls for a shift away from leaders who exhibit **narcissism, Machiavellianism, and psychopathy.** 
    *   **Superficial Charm:** These traits are often mistaken for strength or competence in times of flux, leading to a "Faustian bargain" for organizations and voters.
*   **Measuring "Assists":** Organizations should stop promoting people based solely on individual results. 
    *   **The Sales Manager Example:** Research on 30,000 salespeople shows that the best managers aren't the top individual earners, but those who provide the most "assists"—referring clients and helping colleagues solve problems.
*   **Character Over Charisma:** Grant hopes that by 2026, boards and voters will prioritize character, ensuring that those who put ego ahead of mission are deemed "unfit to lead."
