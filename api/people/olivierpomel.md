### TL;DR
Datadog CEO Olivier Pomel reflects on building a multi-product cloud monitoring giant from New York, emphasizing the power of bottom-up adoption, obsessive customer feedback loops, and the urgent necessity of moving faster on hiring and firing.

### Background & Origin Story
- **Roots in New York:** Graduated from Centrale in 1999, did an internship at IBM Research in upstate New York, and stayed through the dot-com boom, bust, and September 11th.
- **The Educational Software Precursor:** Worked with co-founder Alexis at an educational software startup that grew to roughly 800 people, where Olivier led dev and Alexis led tech ops.
- **The DevOps Insight:** Experienced firsthand the friction between developers and operations teams; realized bringing DevOps together into one platform was the missing puzzle piece.
- **The YC Rejection:** Applied to Y Combinator and was rejected by Paul Graham (received a rejection email, and later an accidental invite retraction); framed the rejection email as motivation.
- **Painful Early Fundraising:** Angel round was super painful; New York VCs didn't understand the competitive landscape, and Valley VCs thought it was a "form of mental impairment" to start an infrastructure company outside the Bay Area.

### Product Strategy & Cloud Tailwinds
- **Riding the Cloud Explosion:** Started in 2010 when companies dismissed AWS as a "toy"; targeting small, modern, cloud-first companies proved to be the ultimate tailwind.
- **Category Naming:** Shifted initial descriptions from a vague "data platform for DevOps collaboration" to **Infrastructure Monitoring**, which immediately resonated with buyers and bosses.
- **Portfolio Expansion:** Grew to **20–25 products**, driven by observing customer extensions, internal workflows, and forward-looking "strategic" bets.
- **The AI Shift:** The company serves the top 10 AI companies in the world alongside traditional enterprises (banks, train companies); building observability for agents and baking intelligence directly into the data plane.

### Culture, Leadership, and Staying Grounded
- **No Written Culture Document:** Refuses to put core values on the wall, believing culture flows directly from the top through everyday actions, hiring, and firing.
- **Reading Raw Feedback:** Continually samples actual support requests, sales transcripts, customer feedback, and employee survey comments rather than relying on polished executive summaries.
- **Challenging the Chain of Command:** Randomly drops questions into Slack or email threads regarding low-level complaints, forcing management to stay dialed into ground-level realities.
- **Public Markets Reality:** Went public on March 2019; the lock-up expiration coincided with the COVID lockdown day market crash, causing the stock to drop roughly 65% (from a $10B valuation down to ~$4B) before rebounding.

### AI Impact & Engineering Transformation
- **No-Code Directive:** Co-founder Alexis told the engineering team that within **two quarters**, they won't be writing code anymore.
- **Hack Project Evidence:** Over the holidays in December, excellent developers independently rebuilt whole systems in a couple of days that would have previously required a team of six over six months.
- **Organizational Agility:** Shifted the organization to take more shots, move faster, and accept being wrong more often because smaller teams can now explore ideas previously requiring massive headcount.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
- **AI-Driven Code Generation:** "In two quarters, we're not writing any, any code anymore" — Olivier Pomel (citing his co-founder's directive on the inversion of software development).
