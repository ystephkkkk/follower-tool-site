## OpenAI's CFO Presents the Future of Finance with University of California’s Chief Investment Officer

- Channel: OpenAI

- Speakers: Sarah Friar (CFO, OpenAI); Jagdeep Singh Bachher (Chief Investment Officer, University of California)

---

**TL;DR** 
OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar and UC CIO Jagdeep Singh Bachher discuss how artificial intelligence is transforming finance from a discipline of resource scarcity to one of abundance by automating routine tasks, emphasizing that adaptability, curiosity, and trust remain the ultimate drivers of career and fundraising success.

### The Evolution of a Nonlinear Career Path
* **Embrace the Zigzag:** Friar highlights that successful careers are rarely linear, sharing her transition from growing up in Northern Ireland during the Troubles to pursuing material science engineering, and eventually pivoting to finance and operations.
* **Prioritize Learning Over Titles:** Early in her career, a headhunter challenged Friar’s ambitions by telling her she did not understand what a CFO actually did. This prompted her to work under Salesforce CFO Graham Smith to learn the "guts of the operation" before pursuing executive roles.
    * **The "Protagonist" Lesson:** Friar advises against making career choices based on external perceptions: *"We are all the protagonist in our own movie and everybody else is watching their movie, no one's watching your movie."*
* **Identify Personal Primitives:** Rather than focusing on job titles, students and professionals should focus on their core skills, what brings them energy, and what is economically viable.

### Transforming Finance from Scarcity to Abundance
* **Operating Lean with High Leverage:** OpenAI’s finance department is highly leveraged, supporting a massive global footprint of over 200 million weekly active users with a lean team of approximately 200 people. 
* **Shifting from Sampling to 100% Auditing:** 
    * **Traditional Scarcity:** Historically, limited time and human resources forced auditors to rely on "sampling" (e.g., testing 10 random invoices out of 1,000).
    * **AI-Enabled Abundance:** Using agentic AI, OpenAI’s team can audit 100% of transactions automatically, dramatically increasing financial controls, precision, and risk management.
* **Automating Global Tax Compliance:** The tax team has automated the retrieval, formatting, and pre-filling of complex local tax forms worldwide. 
    * This automation shifts high-value tax professionals from manual data entry to strategic verification and business planning.
* **Custom GPTs for Investor Relations (IR):** During OpenAI's recent multi-billion dollar fundraising round, the finance team built an IR-specific custom GPT trained on internal diligence materials to instantly answer investor queries.
    * **Real-time Translation:** Friar recalled using the custom IR GPT in South Korea to instantly photograph, translate, and formulate accurate answers to a two-page document written in Korean.

### Three Essential Career Skills for the AI Era
* **Curiosity:** Professionals must actively experiment with AI tools to demystify technology and overcome fear. 
    * **Interactive Technology:** Friar noted a paradigm shift in how humans interact with tech—such as using OpenAI's developer platform, Codex, and simply asking the tool to "please fix yourself" when a script fails.
* **Adaptability:** AI will inevitably automate routine, mundane tasks, but it will also create entirely new jobs. Friar compares the shift to the societal integration of electricity: the fundamentals of work will change, requiring continuous learning.
* **Kindness and Empathy:** Despite technological advancements, business remains fundamentally relational. Empathy and helping others without expecting immediate returns is a critical driver of long-term professional success.

### Human Connection and the Future of Education
* **The Irreplaceability of Trust:** Friar and Bachher emphasize that despite sophisticated AI modeling, fundraising and high-stakes investing still rely entirely on human trust, shared values, and relationship-building.
* **Educational Integration Over Fear:** Rather than focusing on students using AI to cheat, educational institutions must shift to "AI-native" learning. 
    * AI acts as an on-demand tutor, helping students maintain their "flow state" (e.g., troubleshooting a chemistry problem at midnight instead of waiting days for a teaching assistant's office hours).
* **Job Market Resilience:** Friar dispels fears of job automation by pointing out that the global software engineering population has actually grown by 6% since the launch of ChatGPT in 2022, as AI lowers the barrier to entry for coding.

## OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar on IPO, AI Rivalries, New Device, and Spending $100B+ on Compute

---

**TL;DR** 
OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar outlines the company's aggressive scaling strategy across multi-cloud infrastructure, custom consumer hardware, and high-intent advertising, fueled by a historic $122 billion fundraise aimed at securing compute capacity through the next decade.

---

### Valuation, IPO Outlook, and Market Positioning

* **Historic Capital Raise:** OpenAI completed a massive private funding round, securing $122 billion in committed capital at an $852 billion post-money valuation, providing the company with maximum financial flexibility.
* **IPO Philosophy:** Sarah Friar views an IPO strictly as a funding milestone rather than a final destination. 
    * *Market Dynamics:* Referring to Benjamin Graham’s famous adage, Friar notes that "the market is a weighing machine, not a popularity machine," dismissing immediate pressure to race competitors like Anthropic to the public markets.
* **Structural Advantage:** OpenAI’s strategy relies on building a single foundational infrastructure layer served through multiple interfaces (e.g., ChatGPT, Codex, Frontier), creating a compounding network effect of more users, better data, and localized personalization.

### Monetization, Growth Metrics, and Enterprise Focus

* **Balanced Revenue Streams:** OpenAI's revenue is currently split roughly 50/50 between consumer and enterprise segments, with Denise Dresser (Head of Revenue since December) leading a highly active push into major corporate verticals (e.g., Thermo Fisher, Travelers, and major financial institutions).
* **The "Commitment Curve" of Engagement:** User behavior changes dramatically as they transition from free to paid tiers:
    * *Free Users:* Average 7 query turns per day.
    * *Basic Paid Tier:* Double the engagement of free users (~15 turns).
    * *Plus Tier ($20/month):* 3x free user engagement (~21 turns).
    * *Pro Tier:* 11x free user engagement.
* **Rapid Global Adoption:**
    * *ChatGPT:* Now commands over 900 million weekly active users. The fastest-growing continent is Africa, and the fastest-growing languages are Kazakh and Azerbaijani.
    * *Codex:* Reached 5 million users, growing from nearly zero in January.

### Solving the Compute Crunch & Infrastructure Expansion

* **Infrastructure Scarcity:** Severe compute shortages are expected to persist through 2026 and 2027. To manage this, OpenAI splits workloads strategically: training runs are centralized in the United States for national security reasons, while inference is scaling globally.
* **The 1-Gigawatt Michigan Project:** OpenAI is breaking ground on a landmark 1-gigawatt data center in Saline, Michigan, as part of its Oracle complex. To maintain community trust, OpenAI has committed to:
    * Preventing local ratepayer utility increases by directly funding its own power infrastructure.
    * Creating 2,500 local union jobs (electricians, HVAC, etc.) and generating $1 billion in local tax revenue.
    * Investing $45 million in education via Codex credits.
* **The "Rubik's Cube" Supply Chain Strategy:** To optimize capital allocation and avoid single-vendor lock-in, OpenAI has diversified its cloud and hardware partnerships:
    * *Multi-Cloud (CSP):* Distributing workloads across Microsoft Azure, Oracle, CoreWeave, GCP, AWS, and neoscalers to effectively shift capital expenditures (CapEx) into operational expenditures (OpEx).
    * *Multi-Chip:* Continuing its primary partnership with NVIDIA (utilizing Blackwell chips in the fall, followed by the Feynman series) while integrating chips from AMD, Cerebras, Broadcom (co-developed custom silicon), and building a custom data center with SoftBank Energy in Texas.

### Next-Gen Consumer Hardware and Advertising Strategy

* **Jony Ive Collaboration:** OpenAI is developing a highly intuitive, lovable consumer hardware device (often described as an earpiece or puck) designed to move users away from "thumb-based" screen interaction to natural voice multi-modality. The device is scheduled to be unveiled in late 2024/early 2025, with sales opening shortly after.
* **High-Intent Advertising:** OpenAI is laying the groundwork for an advertising platform. By combining search intent (similar to Google) with demographic identity (similar to Meta) and localized model memory/context, they aim to build a highly potent, non-disruptive ad network.
* **Search Market Disruption:** OpenAI currently captures at least 11% of the search market. Because entire multi-turn conversational threads are counted as a single query, their true share of user intent is likely much higher.
