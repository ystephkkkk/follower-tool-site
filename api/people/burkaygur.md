### TL;DR
Generative media is one of only three true product-market fits in AI alongside search and coding agents, prompting fal to build the infrastructure powering image, video, audio, and 3D models for developers and studios. Co-founders Burkay Gür and Gorkem Yurtseven explain how the company evolved from Python data infrastructure into a generative media powerhouse driving a 150-person organization with just two reporting levels.

### Company Origin and Pivot to Generative Media
* **Founding Thesis:** Burkay Gür and Gorkem Yurtseven started fal in 2021 with the thesis that Python would be the next big data infrastructure wave—mirroring what Snowflake and Databricks did for SQL by owning the cloud compute layer.
* **The Pivot to AI:** After building cloud infrastructure for machine learning workloads, the breakthrough of chat models and early image models (Stable Diffusion, DALL-E) prompted a strategic shift away from the pre-AI data infrastructure business, which had generated zero revenue in its first year.
* **Naming Generative Media:** Recognizing that image and video models solved fundamentally different problems than LLMs (targeting ads, marketing, and studios), the founders coined the term "generative media" and put it on their website three years ago when no one else understood the category.

### Business Growth and Revenue Dynamics
* **Current Revenue Split:** Video and image models now split fal's revenue 50/50, surprising the founders who initially believed video would completely cannibalize image models.
* **Resurgence of Image Models:** Image editing models brought image models back to life because they allow users to create precise starting frames that video models can then effectively interpolate between.
* **Infinite Demand Despite Rising Costs:** When video models like V2 jumped in price from roughly 30–40 cents per 5-second clip to $5 per 5-second clip, the team initially doubted who would pay; instead, usage surged as professional use cases in ads and movie production expanded.
* **Compute Constraints:** The company acts as an intimate infrastructure and API partner for model labs and application builders during a severe compute shortage, anticipating growth to procure compute and enable ecosystem launches.

### Team Culture and Flat Organizational Design
* **Team Scale and Backgrounds:** fal operates with a ~150-person organization, drawing heavily on talent from Coinbase and Amazon networks built over the founders' careers.
* **Hiring Philosophy:** The company recruits for intense domain interest and "hunger," echoing historical crypto-quiz hiring tactics from Coinbase to find engineers who are genuinely obsessed with the space.
* **Two-Level Hierarchy:** fal maintains an exceptionally flat structure with only two reporting levels, relying on "super ICs" (individual contributors who can both execute hands-on work and lead small teams of 5 to 10 engineers) rather than pure managers.
* **Work-Life Realities:** Despite the high-stakes environment, the company encourages realistic boundaries, noting that engineers go home on weekends while still achieving high velocity.

### Industry Evolution and Future Modalities
* **Modality Expansion:** Generative media encompasses multiple sub-categories and modalities—including text-to-image, image editing, video, 3D/4D/5D for gaming, and text/speech translation—with roughly 5 to 10 companies competing in each sub-category.
* **Abundant Training Data:** Unlike code or specific LLM domains, video training data enjoys an abundance of freely available content; the hard problem is filtering out interesting physics and world data rather than running out of raw material.
* **World Models and Gaming:** Emerging world models allow users to navigate persistent 3D/interactive environments via prompts, which the founders believe could unlock gaming as the next massive AI product-market fit.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Software Engineering Speed** — *Gorkem Yurtseven* claims that with AI assistance, fal’s software engineering team moves **3 to 4x faster**, meaning they would otherwise need **4 times the headcount** to maintain the same velocity.
* **Timeline for Evergreen Content** — *Burkay Gür* predicts that within **the next 12 months**, the industry will produce its first "timeless AI content"—a widely accepted short movie or animation that people will look back on years later and enjoy the same way they do upon release.
