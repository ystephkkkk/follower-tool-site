## AI Terminator Fears Grow & Rates Breach 4.9% | The Weekly Wrap

- Channel: Steve Eisman
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XYD6kWTygho
- Published: 2026-09-11
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260911.html#video-XYD6kWTygho
- Speakers: Steve Eisman (Investor and Host, The Weekly Wrap); Scott Bessent (Founder, Key Square Group); Ed Zitron (CEO, EZPR)

---

The 10-year Treasury yield breached 4.8% and 4.9% (hitting its highest level since November 2023) amid the Iran war, while AI bubble fears grow and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent attempts controversial bond-buying interventions.

### Macro, Interest Rates & Fiscal Policy
- **Iran War & Bond Yields:**
  - **10-Year Yield Surge:** Breached 4.8% and reached 4.9% (highest since November 2023) driven by oil prices rising well above $100 due to the Iran conflict.
  - **Equity Impact:** Steve Eisman warns the equity market cannot tolerate higher long-term rates; while he previously thought 4.5% was the "Rubicon," rates are now higher, making the threshold closer and unknowable. Rising long-term rates negatively impact the real economy.
- **Treasury Intervention (Scott Bessent):**
  - **Initial & Expanded Buying:** Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced a $4 billion plan to buy long-term Treasuries to drive rates down, later upping the repurchase authority to $6 billion. 
  - **Market Reception:** Stanley Druckenmiller criticized Bessent in a WSJ op-ed. Long-term rates climbed higher post-announcement (10-year rose to 4.845% from 4.80%), leading many to call the plan a failure ("Bessent needs a bigger bazooka" against $40 trillion in federal debt).
  - **QE Irony & Fed Balance Sheet:** Incoming Fed Chair Warsh wants to shrink the Fed's $6.7 trillion balance sheet and get out of Quantitative Easing (QE). Ironically, the Treasury is stepping in with $6B, which Eisman calls insufficient for a $40T debt load.
- **Partisan Norm Violation:** Bessent spoke at a Republican midterm political convention in a partisan manner—breaking a 50-year norm for Treasury Secretaries that provides the office with necessary gravitas for bond market manipulation.
- **Fed Rate Cut Threats:** The Trump administration issued threatening statements that the Fed should not raise rates, though the market projects a 60% chance of a rate hike at the September meeting. Eisman believes Warsh will ignore the threats.

### Artificial Intelligence: Ecosystem Debates & Pricing Wars
- **AI Extinction Fears:**
  - **Researcher Warning:** Former Anthropic researcher Jacob Coksun warned AI has a realistic chance of causing human extinction by the end of the decade.
  - **Scepticism:** Eisman is a skeptic, noting no evidence AI will achieve AGI and pointing to Gary Marcus's view that current tech lacks AGI capability and suffers from hallucinations. Eisman attributes this to tech leaders taking science fiction (like SpaceX mining asteroids) too seriously.
- **The Ed Zitron Debate & Circular Financing:**
  - **Hyperscaler CapEx:** Hyperscalers expect returns from AI entirely through Anthropic and OpenAI spending money on compute, which Eisman calls a catastrophe.
  - **Nvidia's Role:** A Substack subscriber argued Nvidia operates at high margins (close to 80%) and acts like a "modern-day Robin Hood," extracting rents from hyperscalers and financing emerging competitors, open-source ecosystems, and neo clouds (such as Nvidia acquiring Hugging Face). 
  - **Systemic Risk:** Eisman cautions that if OpenAI fails within a year before new companies can replace its commitments, it could trigger a dot-com-style market correction.
- **OpenAI Price Cuts:** At a Goldman Sachs tech conference, OpenAI's CFO announced price cuts on the GPT 5.6 Luna model shortly after its July release, claiming a tenfold increase in model usage. Eisman questions if Anthropic will follow and if this signals the start of a price war.
- **Qualcomm & Amazon Partnership:**
  - **Collaboration:** Qualcomm announced a deal with Amazon to build custom AI chips and optical networking equipment to compete with Nvidia.
  - **Warrants Issued:** Qualcomm issued warrants giving Amazon the right to buy 25 million Qualcomm shares at $161.26 (worth over $4 billion, given stock is over $175). Eisman initially questioned why Qualcomm gave away valuable equity when it doesn't need cash.
  - **Explanations:** Subscribers suggested it acts as a strategic customer acquisition cost to guarantee a $60 billion revenue pipeline and secure AWS as an anchor customer, or to leverage Amazon's IP since Qualcomm's XPU expertise is unproven.

### Tech & Corporate Earnings: Tesla, Oracle, Qualcomm, & Others
- **Tesla Robotaxi Event:**
  - **Market Reaction:** Tesla's robotaxi event featured little new information, sending the stock down 6%.
  - **Valuation & Fundamentals:** Tesla's 2026 consensus EPS estimate is $1.66 (59% lower than its 2022 peak of $4.07), resulting in a 2026 P/E of 220x (compared to GM at 6.5x). Eisman notes Elon Musk has a history of over-promising and under-delivering on robotaxis, and the absurd multiple relies entirely on belief in the robotaxi business. Short sellers were right on fundamentals, making Tesla a difficult "cult stock" to short.
- **Oracle Earnings:**
  - **Results:** Revenue and non-GAAP EPS both increased by an impressive 30%. Non-GAAP EPS excludes stock-based compensation.
  - **Backlog & Dependency:** Remaining performance obligations (RPO) reached $664 billion (up slightly from $600 billion prior quarter). Oracle remains highly dependent on OpenAI, with roughly 50% of RPO coming from OpenAI. Stock was up 4% after hours, though weighed down by OpenAI reliance.
- **FICO Short Thesis & Credit Bureaus:**
  - **FHFA Criticisms:** FHFA head Bill Pulte criticized FICO and credit bureaus for price gouging, citing a 1,600% price hike over the past 5 years.
  - **Competition:** Vantage Score is now available to all lenders (previously a pilot). Eisman believes FICO's monopoly will break badly and maintains his short position.
- **Retail & Other Earnings:**
  - **Macy's:** Turnaround showing progress with market cap of $10 billion. Beat and raised; total same-store sales up 2.7%, while high-end division Bloomingdale's surged 11.3% due to the demise of Saks. Stock fell due to worse-than-expected next-quarter EPS guidance.
  - **GameStop:** Q2 adjusted EPS rose, but revenue fell to $790 million (vs. $972 million last year). GameStop now generates more revenue from collectibles than video games; market cap is $9 billion with slow EPS growth and negative revenue growth.

### Mailbag: Gold vs. Treasuries
- **Gold Performance:** A subscriber noted gold rose despite the 10-year yield moving from 4.42% to over 4.81%, defying the logic that a 5% risk-free Treasury yield should pressure gold.
- **Eisman's View:** Investors view gold as an inflation and fiat currency debasement hedge. The Iran war drove oil higher, sparking inflation fears and pushing investors to gold. Eisman does not own gold, preferring a 5% Treasury yield over precious metals.
