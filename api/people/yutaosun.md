### TL;DR
Kimi K3 represents a major milestone as a 2.8T parameter fully open-source MoE model that successfully integrates hybrid attention, advanced routing, and efficient infrastructure. Guest expert Sun Yutao, a Tsinghua University PhD candidate, breaks down the core architectural innovations, training recipes, and engineering CoDesign that enable K3's scale and 1-million-token context length.

### Model Architecture & Innovations
* **Hybrid Attention Design:** 
  * Replaces pure attention or pure linear attention with a hybrid structure (approximately 3:1 ratio of linear to full attention in K3).
  * Proves that mixing attention types does not sacrifice context performance and allows lossless or superior long-context behavior while saving compute.
* **Kimi Linear & Fine-Grained Decay:** 
  * Evolves from RetNet, Mamba-2, and Gated DeltaNet by moving from a scalar decay to a channel-wise ($\text{channel-wise}$) decay.
  * Implements a lower-bound decay ($\text{lower bound decay}$) to keep numerical values within the $\text{BF16}$ dynamic range inside a 16-token tile window, enabling efficient TensorCore utilization.
* **Gated MLA (Multi-head Latent Attention):** 
  * Combines DeepSeek-style MLA with gated attention mechanisms to significantly improve pretraining stability.
  * Solves the training collapse and outlier explosion issues commonly seen in large-scale MoE runs without sacrificing inference efficiency.
* **Attention Residuals & Normalization:** 
  * Leverages deeper residual connections inspired by HyperConnection to increase the model's residual capacity with near-zero inference overhead.
  * Introduces soft-clipping ($\text{Soft Clip}$) in activation functions (like C2GLU) to strictly bound internal hidden state outliers and stabilize training.

### Training, Scaling, & Long-Context Strategies
* **Scaling Efficiency:** 
  * K3 reaches 2.8T total parameters with ~1.04B active parameters—delivering roughly 2.5x higher scaling efficiency compared to K2.
  * Utilizes Cosine Decay over WSD (Warmup-Stable-Decay) due to easier hyperparameter tuning and more predictable decay ratios.
* **Native 1M Long-Context via NoPE:** 
  * Replaces RoPE (Rotary Position Embedding) with **NoPE** (No Position Embedding) in full attention layers within the hybrid setup.
  * Eliminates the need to adjust position embedding parameters when extending context windows, avoiding the long-context degradation caused by raw RoPE.
* **Post-Training & Distillation (OPD):** 
  * Defers FP8/low-precision quantization to the SFT stage rather than full-scratch low-precision training, ensuring project stability.
  * Employs On-Policy Distillation (OPD / reverse KL) to have student models generate paths while teacher models provide step-by-step corrections.

### Infrastructure & Engineering CoDesign
* **MoE Expert Routing & Counter Balancing:** 
  * Implements **Counter Balancing (QB)** to replace heuristic loss-free routing with a principled, analytical bias adjustment computed via histogram binning across distributed DP ranks.
* **Distributed Training & Offloading:** 
  * Uses MoEP (Mixture-of-Experts Parallelism) with a small percentage of redundant experts to dynamically align token counts across GPUs and eliminate execution bubble waste.
  * Leverages aggressive CPU offloading in FP8 for activation states, overlapping communication with Shared Expert compute paths.
* **Multimodal Infrastructure Handling:** 
  * Manages visual token compression and uneven pipeline parallelism (PP) workloads by shifting vision encoders and idle layers to optimize pipeline bubble fill rates.
