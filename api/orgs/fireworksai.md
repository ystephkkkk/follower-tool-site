### TL;DR
Post-training allows companies to embed their unique judgment and taste into AI models after achieving product-market fit, serving as both a strategic moat and a 5–10x cost reduction mechanism for production serving.

### Industry Context & The Shift Toward Owning Intelligence
- **Evolution of Application Development:** Software development has collapsed from taking tens of product engineers and PMs across multiple quarters to a single person building in a few weeks without writing a single line of code, shifting focus from off-the-shelf black-box APIs to building deeper moats.
- **The Value of "Taste":** Every company exists to solve unique problems with its own judgment, taste, and conviction; building on generic APIs risks losing that differentiation.
- **What "Owning Your Intelligence" Means:** 
  - **Data:** Curing production data with high quality and generating synthetic data to enrich it.
  - **Model Weights:** Building on top of existing models and owning the weights using tailored, interoperable techniques.
  - **Serving & Optimization:** Running AB testing to verify product metrics, then feeding insights back into the loop.
- **Audience Breakdown & Engagement Models:** 
  - *Deep Researchers (e.g., Cursor, Cognition):* Want extreme results and control over every knob, using lowest-level APIs with direct RL rollouts and fully controlled trainers.
  - *Product/ML Engineers:* Want high-level control via training SDKs and close iteration with Fireworks' researchers.

### The Post-Training Progression
- **Phase 1: Prompting** – Few-shot prompts used as-is for minutes of interaction to quickly test ideas.
- **Phase 2: RAG & Context Engineering** – Grounding AI usage into your own data for hours of interaction; ideal when factual data is dynamic and constantly changing.
- **Phase 3: Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)** – Taking a few hours of data to reflect personalized taste, product choice, or correct structural/behavioral output issues.
- **Phase 4: Preference Tuning (DPO)** – Collecting user interaction data (thumbs-up, thumbs-down) to help models learn product taste and user preferences.
- **Phase 5: Reinforcement Learning (RL)** – Building domain expertise (legal, finance, healthcare, customer support, recruiting, marketing, sales) when models are weak on specific, complex problems.
- **Phase 6: Distillation** – Training a smaller student model via a teacher model to make serving faster and 5–10x more economical (applicable to LLMs, VLMs, and image generation models).

### Common Pitfalls in Post-Training
- **Data Quantity vs. Quality:** Throwing raw data at training does not guarantee great outcomes; data quality must be controlled, with the product team acting as the ultimate judge of quality.
- **Vibe Evals vs. Systematic Evals:** Relying on "vibe checking" founder sentiment instead of systematic unit and integration tests (similar to traditional software development).
- **Sloppy RL Environments:** Building simulations that fail to reflect reality, leading models to hill-climb on bad simulations.
- **Reward Hacking:** Models finding unintended shortcuts to meet optimization goals (e.g., generating zero lines of code to eliminate compilation errors).
- **Training-to-Serving Stack Mismatch:** Quality drops when moving from a training stack to a serving stack if libraries, numerics, and optimizations differ, causing a loss of precision.

### Real-World Examples & Use Cases
- **Cursor:** Started mid-to-post training early last year to control model supply and customer preference data, successfully competing at frontier quality on par with frontier labs.
- **Doximity:** Uses Clinic AI on Fireworks for deep medical questions, topping the Stanford-Harvard clinic safety benchmark.
- **Factory:** Fine-tuned a model on Fireworks focused on coding security, topping security benchmarks where tolerance for error is extremely low.
- **GenSpark:** Built a general-purpose co-working application for deep professional research and slide generation that competes with frontier models while achieving a 5–10x cost reduction.
- **Target Segments:** 
  - *Frontier Agent Builders:* Build bespoke customized harness systems co-optimized with custom tools via post-training.
  - *Incumbents:* CFOs block AI feature launches due to massive traffic cost burdens; post-training prevents scaling into bankruptcy.
  - *Specialized Model Operators:* Cutting-edge developers performing significant post-training.
