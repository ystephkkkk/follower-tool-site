## Inside the Personal AI Assistant Growing 10% a Day | Instinct Founder

- Channel: Invest Like The Best
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Am7IWP8IpEc
- Published: 2026-09-28
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260928.html#video-Am7IWP8IpEc
- Speakers: Patrick O'Shaughnessy (Host, Invest Like The Best); Noah Shinn (CEO, Instinct)

---

**TL;DR:** Instinct is an app-less, proactive AI personal assistant created by CEO Noah Shinn that is growing 10% day-over-day and already handling over $1 billion a year in transaction volume. Operating via text, voice, and email, the agent functions with high social intelligence to autonomously manage complex real-world workflows like travel, reservations, and personal finance.

### 1. Product Experience & Core Philosophy
- **App-less Architecture:** **Noah Shinn** explains that Instinct has no standalone mobile or desktop application; users interact with it through everyday communication channels like text, email, and voice calls.
- **Focus on Understandability over Raw Capability:** **Noah Shinn** emphasizes prioritizing how well a user understands and predicts the agent's behavior rather than hyping underlying technical features, aiming to reduce cognitive load and user fatigue.
- **Proactive and Autonomous Execution:** Unlike traditional prompt-response AI tools, **Noah Shinn** notes that Instinct operates on higher-level objectives (e.g., booking a last-minute flight to New York via a single voice note, handling entire travel itineraries from Uber to hotel), waking up and sleeping in the background to manage logistics proactively.

### 2. Growth Metrics & Financial Milestones
- **Explosive Viral Growth:** **Noah Shinn** reports that Instinct is currently growing at **10% to 11% day-over-day** entirely through word-of-mouth. 
- **Invite-Only Constraint:** The platform uses an invite-only model where each user receives **5 invites**, creating high social demand and secondary market buzz (with invites reportedly selling for **$300 on eBay**).
- **High Transaction Volume:** Despite a very small user base in an invite-only phase, **Noah Shinn** reveals the platform is approaching **over $1 billion a year in transaction volume**, with **50% coming from travel alone**.
- **Venture Backing:** **Noah Shinn** mentions the company recently raised a **$1 billion round at a roughly $10 billion valuation**, led by tier-one investors including **Zoya, Benchmark, and Kotu**.

### 3. The Instinct-to-Instinct Network & Social Dynamics
- **Trusted Person Network:** **Noah Shinn** details a feature launched about 10 days prior to the interview that allows different users' Instinct agents to communicate directly to solve collaborative tasks like calendar scheduling without manual back-and-forth.
- **Granular Access Controls:** The network relies on strict trust boundaries and customizable sharing levels (e.g., sharing a work calendar or inbox with colleagues versus full access with a spouse).
- **Social Consequences of Trust:** **Noah Shinn** points out fascinating emerging social dynamics where a breach of data privacy by an agent visiting unauthorized areas of a connected user's profile can directly strain the real-world human relationship.

### 4. Trust, Privacy, and Security Architecture
- **Adoption Milestones as Proxies for Trust:** **Noah Shinn** shares that **3 weeks in, 40% of the user base** has shared a personal credit card with Instinct, and users who share at least one sensitive piece of information exhibit an **80% retention rate**.
- **Decoupled Security Guardrails:** **Noah Shinn** explains that safety is managed by infrastructure detached from the main agent architecture:
  - **Firewalls:** Intercept and block malicious text or media inputs attempting prompt injection.
  - **Decoupled Watchdog Monitors:** Actively monitor, pause, and approve/disapprove every agentic action or thought trace before execution to prevent hallucinations or unauthorized data access.

### 5. Business Model & Impact on Existing Industries
- **Alignment Over Exploitation:** **Noah Shinn** explicitly rejects ad-supported models that monetize user attention or manipulate consumer behavior against their will, stating that Instinct acts purely on behalf of the user's true interests.
- **Merchant-Paid Take Rates:** Modeled after payment networks like Apple Pay or American Express, the product remains free for the consumer while generating revenue through transaction take rates paid by underlying merchants and service providers (leveraging high merchant margins in sectors like travel and boutique hotels).
- **Transformation of Digital Services:** **Noah Shinn** predicts that zero-friction agentic interaction will fundamentally rewrite the internet's business model, increasing total transaction volume for merchants by removing the friction of traditional app interfaces and infinite-scroll attention traps.
