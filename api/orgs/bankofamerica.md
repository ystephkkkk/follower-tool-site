### TL;DR
IFS CEO Mark Moffat and Bank of America Managing Director Thomas Köhrer discussed how industrial AI is transforming asset-heavy, mission-critical sectors like manufacturing, aerospace, and utilities, emphasizing that true deployment requires a balance of probabilistic AI reasoning and deterministic safety standards where failure is not an option.

### Speakers & Roles
- **Mark Moffat** — CEO, IFS (Industrial software executive with a 28-year background spanning 24 years in tech services and 4 years at IFS)
- **Thomas Köhrer** — Managing Director, Bank of America (Panel moderator and discussion lead)

### IFS Company Profile & Strategy
- **Asset-Heavy Focus:** IFS has a 40-year history supporting capital-intensive, asset-heavy industries across the asset and service lifecycle, including utilities, manufacturing, aerospace, defense, service-based industries, mining, oil, and gas.
- **Scale & Retention:** The company operates with a 96% Gross Retention Rate (GRR) and supports 100% of commercial aviation flights in the US, carrying 800 million passengers annually.
- **Forward Deployed Engineering:** Pre-sales teams and developers spend extensive time on-site in warehouses and field ride-alongs to identify pinch points, leakage, and cost optimization opportunities in complex industrial value chains.

### Industrial AI vs. Enterprise AI
- **Penetration Gap:** While software engineering, legal, finance, and customer contact centers have heavily benefited from frontier models, industrial value chains remain relatively less penetrated.
- **Deterministic vs. Probabilistic:** Due to strict health, safety, and operational standards (e.g., jet engine maintenance requiring 99.99999% reliability), industrial AI must bridge probabilistic AI thinking with deterministic execution.
- **Sovereign & Agnostic Architecture:** Moffat noted that relying on a single frontier model is negligent; IFS uses an agnostic supply chain approach combining closed frontier models, open-source models, lower-parameter models deployed at the edge, and robotics.

### Case Study: Greenall (Grants Distillery)
- **Deployment & Impact:** Working with Grants (the second-largest global distiller, producing Hendricks gin and other brands) at a new distillery on the west coast of Scotland, IFS ingested design, engineering, PLM, schematics, telematics, and plant telemetry.
- **Results:** Regression-analyzed failure predictions across flow rates, temperature, throughput, volume, and gas reduced unplanned downtime by **38%**.
- **Speed of Execution:** The deal moved from first conversation to contract signature in just **8 days**.

### Boardroom Guidance & Future Outlook
- **Business Fundamentals:** Moffat advised boards to flip the perspective away from "AI for AI's sake" and instead focus on business fundamentals—solving profitability pinch points, performance deviations, and operational challenges.
- **Sovereignty & Geopolitics:** Sovereign AI is treated as an outcome achieved by maintaining supply-chain control over models and partnerships to navigate shifting national security and global political environments.
- **Industry Evolution:** Manufacturing—particularly global supply chain orchestration (production, materials, logistics, and fulfillment planning)—will undergo the most dramatic industry-wide transformation.

### TL;DR
Bank of America CEO Brian Moynihan expects third-quarter trading revenue to be relatively flat compared to an exceptionally strong prior year, while warning that the rapid pace of artificial intelligence development requires deliberate safety guardrails and careful corporate deployment.

### Financial Outlook & Trading Performance
* **Third-Quarter Trading Revenue:** Projected to be "relatively flat" compared to last year's Q3, which Moynihan noted will still rank among the best third quarters in company history.
    * **Equities vs. Fixed Income:** Equities are up more, while fixed income is bouncing around slightly down.
    * **Systemic Leverage:** The AI-driven leverage and risk from earlier in the year have largely come out of the system.
* **Investment Banking Pipeline:** Activity levels and pipelines for IPOs and secondary offerings remain strong, though rate increases of about 100 basis points over a short period have caused a temporary pause.
* **Wealth Management:** Revenue is expected to be up **10% to 15%**.
* **Core Business Metrics:** Core net interest income is **60%** of the business and performing as expected, expenses are well-managed, credit quality is good, and consumer spending remains robust.

### Artificial Intelligence: Risks, Regulation, and Deployment
* **The Need to Slow Down:** Likened AI leaders calling for caution to "NASCAR drivers saying please slow down the cars," raising questions over whether the government, private sector, or society should manage the pacing.
* **Autonomous Agents:** Bank of America refuses to deploy fully autonomous agents because the company cannot see how to adequately control systems designed to independently solve problems.
* **Corporate Investment:** Deployed **$400 million plus** in AI capabilities this year and plans to spend more next year, utilizing internal tools like the 10-year-old assistant **Erica** under strict "human-in-the-loop" controls.
* **Financing & Liability:** As a lender and funder, the financial sector must account for evolving product liability risks associated with AI products.
* **Regulatory Preference:** Advocated for federal regulation over a fragmented state-by-state patchwork of rules across California, Nevada, Texas, and North Carolina.
* **Upcoming Convening:** Participating in King Charles III's Sustainable Markets Initiative meeting in Scotland alongside leaders like Jensen Huang to discuss AI development and safety.

### Macroeconomic Environment & Interest Rates
* **Economic Resilience:** The underlying U.S. economy remains robust, with consumer spending up **4%** in August compared to August of the prior year, strong consumer credit quality, and active borrowing by small- and medium-sized businesses.
* **Interest Rate Normalization:** View the current rate environment as a return to "normal" following the abnormal zero-rate period from **2009 to 2019**.
* **Fed Rate Expectations:** Bank of America's team expects three rate increases, noting that even with these hikes, inflation may not hit target levels until late **2027 or 2028**.

### Global Sports and Community Engagement ("Sports with Us")

*   **Program Philosophy:** The "Sports with Us" program aims to drive broad athletic participation, support local charities, and provide youth opportunities rather than simply sponsoring one-off events.
*   **Golf Initiatives:** BofA's golf program is projected to cross 250,000 youth participants this year. It partners with local clubs to let kids play municipal courses for $5 across 3,000 locations nationwide.
    *   **The Rory McIlroy Impact:** After appearing in a BofA promotional campaign, professional golfer Rory McIlroy returned his appearance fee to purchase an additional 80,000 rounds of golf for participating youth.
*   **FIFA World Cup Partnership:** BofA has signed on as the first-ever bank in FIFA’s global sponsor tier for the upcoming World Cup, leveraging its deep roots in major US host cities (e.g., Boston since 1784, San Francisco for 130 years).
    *   **Support for Veterans:** BofA announced a $2 million donation to Vet Tix to provide free World Cup tickets to veterans and first responders.

### Economic Outlook and Consumer Spending Trends

*   **Resilient Consumer Spending:** BofA internal data shows that consumer spending grew approximately 5% year-over-year (representing about $400 billion in transaction volume). 
    *   **Employment Stability:** Low unemployment claims (sitting around 200,000) and steady continuing claims (1.8 million) remain the primary drivers of sustained consumer spending.
*   **K-Shaped Divergence:** While aggregate spending is healthy, BofA research indicates a clear divide:
    *   **Lower-Income Bracket:** Spending and wage growth are rising but at a slower rate than the middle- and upper-income brackets. Lower-income consumers are actively shifting their spending to big-box and secondhand retailers.
    *   **Upper-Income Bracket:** Discretionary spending remains highly robust, and Boomers/seniors are spending at faster rates as they enter a wealth "de-accumulation" phase.
*   **Growth Forecasts:** BofA research projects US GDP growth at 2% to 2.1% for the current period and global growth at roughly 3%.
*   **Key Economic Headwinds:** Affordability issues—specifically rising housing costs, insurance premiums, and fluctuating energy prices due to geopolitical conflicts—remain critical points of concern.

### Small Business Health and Credit Capacity

*   **Market Position:** As the nation's largest small business lender, BofA services 12 million small businesses, holding $200 billion in deposits and maintaining $50 billion to $70 billion in loans.
*   **Credit Availability:** Small businesses exhibit strong balance sheets. Credit line utilization rates remain low, meaning they have ample borrowing capacity when they choose to expand.
*   **Top Business Concerns:** Small business owners are highly focused on:
    *   **Labor Shortages:** Particularly acute within the construction and trade sectors.
    *   **Tariffs & Policies:** Uncertainty regarding tariffs, margins, and immigration policies are causing business owners to remain cautious with long-term capital deployment.

### Strategic Technology Spending and Artificial Intelligence

*   **Technology Budget Allocation:** BofA spends $13 billion annually on technology. Of this, over $4 billion is allocated to new technology initiatives, including $250 million dedicated strictly to AI deployments.
*   **"Erica" Virtual Assistant:** Developed as a predictive "small language model" to accurately translate conversational language into precise financial data, Erica now has 20 million active users completing 200 million interactions per quarter.
*   **The Human-in-the-Loop Standard:** Moynihan stresses that for any bank function requiring actual financial or ethical judgment, humans must remain central to the process to maintain trust and 100% data accuracy.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Implementing AI across internal IT "break-fix" systems successfully reduced employee support call volumes by 50%.

### Leadership Reflections on Past Crises

*   **The Post-2008 Financial Crisis:** Moynihan identified the years immediately following the 2008 financial crisis as the most challenging period of his career. 
    *   **The Scale of the Crisis:** BofA was servicing one-in-five mortgages in America and subsequently managing one-in-five foreclosures. 
    *   **Organizational Overhaul:** The bank had 50,000 employees dedicated to loan modifications and forebearances, ultimately shifting its mortgage business model away from aggressive market share toward a highly controlled, risk-averse framework.
