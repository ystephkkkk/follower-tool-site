**TL;DR:** Rackspace became "Customer Zero" for sovereign AI by shifting ~70% of its back office to Palantir's Ontology, Foundry, and AIP—starting with a daily sales CRM—while partnering with Dell, NVIDIA, and Palantir to deploy and operate fully controlled sovereign cloud environments for enterprise customers.

### Strategic Pivot & "Customer Zero" Transformation
* **Company Evolution:** 
  * Rackspace is a 27-year-old company that evolved from the old days of cloud computing and managed infrastructure into an enterprise AI company.
  * Over the last 12 months under CEO Gajen Kandiah, the company needed to fundamentally change everything to accelerate growth and move at the speed of digital businesses.
* **Becoming Customer Zero:** 
  * Rackspace decided to become "Customer Zero" for Palantir to learn the technology firsthand, bring those lessons to customers as forward-deployed engineer (FDE) services, and run Palantir on a sovereign cloud.
  * The goal is to move about **70%** of Rackspace's back office onto Palantir, eliminating a ton of legacy tech while retaining necessary systems to handle 27 years of background data and SKUs-to-solutions business motions.
* **Starting with CRM:** 
  * Rackspace targeted its CRM as the initial entry point because it represents the most complex yet high-pace operational bottleneck, touching everything from initial customer contact to the back office and standing up GPUs. Billing was slated to come next.

### The "MCAT" Framework & Data Sovereignty
* **The MCAT Philosophy:** 
  * For sovereignty to be real, an organization must own four core pillars: **Model, Compute, Alpha, and Data** (dubbed "MCAT" by Kandiah).
* **Ontology as a Digital Twin:** 
  * Palantir Ontology acts as a digital twin of the business data, allowing Rackspace to run agents on top of it for real-time visibility rather than running the business using a rearview mirror.
* **Core Sovereignty Questions:** 
  * Rackspace's board subjected the initiative to rigorous tests to answer:
    * *Where does the data live, and can any government compel access to it?*
    * *What do we own across our AI environment versus what does someone else own?*
    * *Can we change models without losing intelligence?*
    * *Can we prove all of the above?*
* **Ring-Fencing Intelligence:** 
  * Although Rackspace uses tools like Anthropic and OpenAI, the company realized the model itself is not the asset—**what you teach the model is the asset** (alpha/intelligence). Ring-fencing data at the core point of access and defining roles directly within the data layer ensures complete sovereignty without data leaving the firewall.

### Sovereign Workloads & Infrastructure Partnerships
* **Categorizing Workloads:** 
  * Rackspace breaks workloads down into regulated, sovereign, and institutional sovereign tiers, recognizing they must run across on-premises, edge, core, and public cloud environments.
* **Sovereign Pods Partnership:** 
  * Through a partnership with **NVIDIA, Dell, and Palantir**, Rackspace is building and deploying single- or multi-tenant "sovereign pods" within the Rackspace environment.
* **Deploy and Operate Motion:** 
  * Kandiah emphasized that deploying alone is insufficient; companies must be able to run and operate sovereign environments continuously due to the iterative nature of the business.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **CEO Vision on Enterprise AI:** Gajen Kandiah noted a prediction made by a frontier model CEO that in the future there will be only two types of entities: that specific company and governments—underscoring the critical importance of organizations retaining control over their own intelligence and alpha.
