## European Defense Sovereignty Starts in the Factory: Stark’s Uwe Horstmann and Johannes Schaback

### TL;DR
Stark is a two-year-old European defense neo-prime with roughly 700 employees that has landed billions in contracts by designing software-driven, mass-manufacturable strike drones close to the battlefield. Co-founders Uwe Horstmann and Johannes Schaback argue that western defense sovereignty relies on an active innovation ecosystem and factory-level deterrence rather than isolated, exquisite weapon systems.

### Company Growth & Scale
- **Rapid Expansion:** Founded two years ago, Stark has scaled to approximately 700 employees and has strike drones actively deployed in Ukraine.
- **Venture-Style Portfolio:** The company runs 10 to 15 concurrent programs like a venture capital portfolio, developing autonomous aerial, naval, and electronic warfare systems.
- **Capital Deployment:** Uwe Horstmann notes that Stark intends to invest roughly €600 million over the next 24 to 36 months into R&D and production capability build-up.

### Battlefield Proximity & User-Centric Design
- **Soldier Feedback Loop:** Stark embeds teams directly in Ukraine and at European training grounds to gather critical feedback from the tactical operators of the equipment.
- **The Staff Sergeant Priority:** While traditional defense focuses heavily on generals and procurement buyers, Stark treats enlisted personnel and staff sergeants as core customers to understand real-world usability and cognitive load.
- **Practical Field Lessons:** An early lesson from the field involved showing up with a lightweight plastic drone box, only for soldiers to request wooden boxes that could double as firewood during freezing winter conditions.

### Product Engineering & Autonomous Capabilities
- **Precision Engineering:** CTO Johannes Schaback highlights the company's ability to achieve sub-meter precision in striking moving targets traveling at 60 km/h by tightly integrating hardware, compute budgets, and memory allocation.
- **Modular Autonomy Stack:** The systems are powered by a platform layer ("capability teams") handling GNSS-denied navigation, object classification, and flight control.
- **Responsible AI Constraints:** Stark hard-codes NATO principles of responsible AI use into its autonomy stack, implementing safeguards such as preventing arming over inhabited areas, borrowing safety frameworks from autonomous driving (IEC 61508).

### Manufacturing, Supply Chain, & Forging Moments
- **Design for Mass Manufacturing:** Stark optimizes for both time-to-market and production readiness from day one, treating products like washing machines that can be scaled infinitely rather than bespoke Formula 1 cars.
- **The Bundeswehr Trial Turning Point:** After an upgrade regression caused a public failure during a high-profile German army experiment, engineers relocated to a freezing spa hotel near the shooting range, worked 20-hour shifts around the clock, and achieved three consecutive spot-on strikes within two weeks.
- **Supply Chain Resilience:** The company balances vertical integration against the need for supply chain resilience, aiming to root production locally within procurement countries while minimizing dependencies on single-source regions like China.
