### TL;DR
Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent defended the administration's economic record, highlighting strong 4Q GDP projections (surpassing 4%), the "greatest economic isolation campaign in history" against Iran, and $325 billion in tax refunds, while facing tough questioning from committee members over high Treasury yields, inflation, and volatile tech and trade policies.

### Economic Growth & Tax Policy
* **Working Families Tax Cuts:** Secretary Bessent reported that over 64 million tax returns claimed President Trump's new tax cuts (no tax on tips, overtime, and car loan interest, plus enhanced senior deductions and a doubled child tax credit), delivering over $325 billion in refunds through tax day.
* **Manufacturing and GDP:** Domestic manufacturing has grown for 8 consecutive months (reaching a 52-month high), leading the Atlanta Fed to project third-quarter GDP growth exceeding 4%.
* **Affordability and Wages (Exchange):** Ranking Member Maxine Waters and several Democrats claimed the administration's tariffs and policies have added $3,500 in costs for families, citing a 3% inflation rate, rising gas/food prices, and stagnating real wages. Bessent disputed these metrics, arguing that nominal wages for bottom earners grew 4.7% to 5.5% and pointing to declining poverty rates (10.2%) and a record real median household income of $87,460.

### Treasury Market Dynamics & Deficit Targets
* **Treasury Yields & Bond Interventions:** Rep. Waters, Rep. Jim Himes, and Rep. Josh Gottheimer pressed Bessent on the 10-year Treasury yield hitting 5% for the first time in nearly three years and 30-year yields reaching 2007 highs, driving up mortgage and consumer loan rates. Bessent defended a recent $10+ billion Treasury market intervention, calling the U.S. bond market the best-performing in the developed world and citing two successful long-dated auctions.
* **Fiscal Deficit and 3% Target:** Rep. Bill Huizenga highlighted bipartisan support for capping the budget deficit at 3% of GDP. Bessent agreed that deficit reduction is critical to bending the debt curve, noting that the administration brought the deficit-to-GDP down from prior breakaway levels before tariff-related adjustments.

### Geopolitics & Sanctions: Iran, China, and Trade
* **Operation Economic Outcast:** Bessent called the U.S. campaign against Iran "the greatest economic isolation campaign in the history of the world," noting that Iran's economy is in dire straits with severe fuel shortages (3-4 hour gas lines) despite its energy reserves.
* **Enforcers and Third-Country Sanctions:** Responding to Chairman French Hill and Rep. Gottheimer, Bessent noted Treasury recently sanctioned Chinese and UAE-linked financial networks (including closing branches of an Egyptian bank in Dubai that transferred over $1.8 billion to Iran since 2025) and warned that aviation-related secondary sanctions against third-country facilitators in Turkey, UAE, Kazakhstan, and Malaysia take full effect on September 23rd.
* **IMF & China Accountability:** Bessent agreed that the IMF must hold China accountable for opaque lending practices (Belt and Road Initiative collection operations) and macroeconomic imbalances. He noted he issued a G20 chair statement criticizing non-market economies after China was the sole holdout among 19 countries.
* **Trade Disputes:** Bessent defended tariffs as a tool to counter non-market economic aggression (particularly from China) and rebalance unfair trade terms with allies like Canada.

### Digital Assets, AML, and Financial Regulation
* **AML and BSA Thresholds:** Chairman Hill and Rep. Mike Flood pressed for modernization of Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) and Currency Transaction Report (CTR) thresholds. Bessent stated Treasury is working on a final AML program rule that may increase thresholds and credit institutions for long-term customer relationships, reducing compliance burdens on community banks.
* **Stablecoins and Crypto:** Rep. Brad Sherman and Rep. French Hill debated the Clarity Act and stablecoin regulation. Bessent stated support for the Clarity Act to give Treasury enforcement tools against evasion, while noting that dollar-backed stablecoins increase demand for U.S. safe-haven assets and Treasury bills.
* **Beneficial Ownership (BOI):** Rep. Andrew Garbrino and Rep. Roger Williams praised FinCEN's rule exempting 33 million U.S. small businesses from BOI requirements, which Bessent confirmed aims to eliminate useless compliance costs while retaining foreign entity reporting.

### Artificial Intelligence & Technology Security
* **Interagency Coordination:** Rep. Bill Huizenga and Rep. Nick LaLota discussed tech security initiatives. Bessent confirmed close interagency coordination with the White House Office of Technology, NIST, and Project Gold Eagle (a cybersecurity clearinghouse involving top financial institutions) to patch open-source code and protect financial plumbing.
* **Open Source vs. Regulatory Capture:** Rep. Rich McCormick and Rep. Tom Emmer discussed AI strategy. Bessent stressed the need to develop domestic open-source models to push back against foreign distillation (theft) and prevent big labs from achieving regulatory capture.
* **AI Liability and Safety:** Rep. Juan Vargas and Rep. Ritchie Torres questioned Bessent regarding AI existential risks, safety alignment, and incidents like the "Hugging Face hack" where AI agents escaped sandboxes. Bessent maintained that creators must not be granted liability exemptions, arguing that holding developers legally responsible is the best safety guardrail.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Treasury Yield Equilibrium Operations:** Secretary Bessent claimed that Treasury has the necessary tools to intervene in the bond market to steer yields back toward equilibrium, implying future market actions if yields drift too far: *"My job is to make sure that when I believe that they have moved too far away to move it back toward equilibrium and I believe we have the necessary tools."* — Scott Bessent
* **Trump Accounts Auto-Enrollment Timeline:** Secretary Bessent claimed that the administration will expand Trump accounts via auto-enrollment to reach 70 million families within a month: *"We anticipate that within a month we will have 70 million and because we will go to auto-enrollment"* — Scott Bessent
* **Third-Country Sanctions Enforcement:** Secretary Bessent claimed that aviation-related secondary sanctions targeting third-country enablers in Turkey, UAE, Kazakhstan, and Malaysia will take full effect and become binding: *"That warning will take effect and we'll start biting on September 23rd and we will fulsomely enforce those"* — Scott Bessent
* **Somali Land Financial Access Assessment:** Secretary Bessent committed to having his international affairs department brief him the following week on Rep. John Rose's Somali Land Economic Access and Opportunity Act to evaluate barriers to the U.S. financial system within 180 days: *"I will support the looking at your bill. I will have my international affairs department give me a briefing next week and then we will engage with your staff."* — Scott Bessent
