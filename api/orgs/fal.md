## How Real-Time AI Video Is Changing How Creators Work

- Channel: a16z
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SDbRJXQrYGY
- Published: 2026-09-17
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260917.html#video-SDbRJXQrYGY
- Speakers: Jennifer Li (General Partner, a16z); Gorkem Yurtseven (Co-founder, fal); Batuhan Taskaya (Head of Engineering, fal)

---

### TL;DR
Fal co-founders Gorkem Yurtseven and Batuhan Taskaya explain how post-training MiniMax's open-weight H3 Max video model combined with heavy systems optimization achieved a 35x speedup and lower costs, unlocking real-time continuous video streaming and advanced professional controls for Hollywood studios.

### Model Performance & Technical Architecture
* **Speed and Efficiency Breakthrough:** 
  * Fal's public **H3 Max Turbo** generates a 5-second video in approximately 1.5 seconds at half the cost of the base model.
  * System-wide optimizations pushed hardware chip utilization from standard inference levels of 30-40% up to 70-80%.
* **Co-Designed Post-Training Infrastructure:** 
  * The team applied RL and post-training pipelines to reduce diffusion model requirements from 50 steps down to 20 steps without sacrificing quality.
  * The inference stack combines optimized large language models (LLMs) for prompt expansion, generation in latent space, VAE decoding, and upscaling running across standard 8-GPU single-node configurations.
* **Hardware Generation Leap:** 
  * Moving from Hopper to Blackwell architectures delivers a 2x to 3x wall-clock time improvement, enabling sub-3-second generations for 5-second clips.

### Real-Time Experiences & H3 Max Director
* **Spontaneous Viral Launches:** 
  * Following internal hackathons, fal engineers and influencers like *levels_io* launched independent real-time video streaming apps on Twitch and the web within days of the model release.
* **Continuous Video Memory:** 
  * **H3 Max Director** maintains an evolving system prompt and raw video memory spanning up to 2 minutes, allowing up to 60 minutes of continuous, action-controlled video streaming.
  * Viewers and creators can interact with live streams using real-time voice prompts or chat inputs to instantly alter scenes, introduce characters, or change directions while maintaining world coherence.

### Professional & Hollywood Workflows
* **Precision Control Features:** 
  * **Camera Controls:** Users can supply precise JSON coordinate data (e.g., specific camera angles at timestamp T0 and T1) to eliminate hallucinations and achieve 3D scene reconstruction.
  * **Additional LoRAs:** Fal is rolling out fine-tuned modules for lip-syncing, character motion transfer, and style adaptations.
* **Blender Integration:** 
  * Professionals combine low-resolution Blender scene renders with H3 Max as an image reference to achieve close to 100% controllability.
* **Enterprise & Legal Readiness:** 
  * Hollywood has become fal's fastest-growing segment, supported by newly introduced US-hosted servers and secure IP integration systems that allow studios to train custom models safely.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Batuhan Taskaya:** Predicts that future development will prioritize improving quality and controllability over raw speed, stating: *"My bet today is we just need to improve quality more than like the speed at these speeds."*
