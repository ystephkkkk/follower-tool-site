### TL;DR
Europe is actively addressing its productivity and competitiveness gaps through a unified "One Europe, One Market" strategy and accelerated financial integration, aiming to establish strategic autonomy, streamline regulatory frameworks, and deepen capital markets in an increasingly volatile geopolitical landscape.

---

### The "One Europe, One Market" Strategy and Competitiveness

*   **Restoring Growth:** The European Council is using strategic insights from the Draghi and Letta reports to turn 2026 into the "year of competitiveness" for Europe.
*   **Completing the Single Market:** Priorities focus on finalizing integration in key sectors left incomplete post-1992, specifically telecommunications, energy, and capital markets.
*   **Savings and Investment Union:** The EU aims to approve fundamental regulations by the end of this year to create a integrated capital market, mobilizing private European savings for corporate investment.
*   **Simplification and De-bureaucratization:**
    *   **The "28th Regime":** A unified legal framework for businesses is scheduled for approval by the end of this year, allowing companies to operate under the same legal rules across all 27 member states.
    *   **Electronic Wallet:** A digital portfolio will enable European companies to do business across the entire single market without needing local registrations.

### Industrial Sovereignty, Energy, and Budgetary Overhauls

*   **Active Industrial Policy:** The EU is shifting toward a coordinated European industrial policy—transitioning what was historically a French concept into a shared European strategy.
*   **Digital and Defense Autonomy:** 
    *   The Commission has launched a digital sovereignty package covering semiconductors, artificial intelligence, quantum computing, and cloud infrastructure.
    *   European preference rules in public procurement are being designed for targeted sectors, particularly defense, by the end of this year.
*   **Energy Price Stability:** The EU is mitigating risks from geopolitical volatility (e.g., Middle East tensions and the war in Ukraine) by prioritizing self-produced electricity through nuclear and renewables, alongside heavy investment in cross-border grid connections.
*   **Budget Restructuring (MFF):** 
    *   The next Multiannual Financial Framework must be approved by the end of this year to be fully operational by January 1, 2028.
    *   The new budget will double investments in research and competitiveness, raising their share from 1/5th of the agricultural (CAP) and cohesion funds to 1/2 of their total.

### Financial Integration and Overcoming Fragmentation

*   **Addressing Capital Barriers:** ECB President Christine Lagarde highlighted that while Europe shares a monetary union, it lacks a true financial union due to systemic national fragmentation. 
    *   The European financial infrastructure remains clogged by 295 market platforms, 14 central counterparties, and 32 central securities depositories.
*   **Unlocking Household Wealth:** EU households currently hold €35 trillion in financial assets, but only 1% of this capital is currently invested in European high-tech.
*   **Global Euro Sovereignty:**
    *   **Repo Lines:** The ECB has opened euro liquidity lines to non-EU national central banks globally (excluding high-risk money-laundering jurisdictions) to boost the international role of the euro, receiving 30 legitimate applications within the first week of announcement.
    *   **TIPS (TARGET Instant Payment Settlement):** The ECB is working to link its instant payment system with major emerging market networks in India and Brazil, bypassing foreign-dominated payment networks.

### Geopolitical Resilience and Economic Defense

*   **Sustaining Ukraine:** Europe demonstrated its strategic capacity by establishing a €90 billion financial loan package for Ukraine for the next two years, proving it could lead support independently of shifting political dynamics in the United States.
*   **Countering Unfair Trade Practices:** To counter Chinese overcapacity, state subsidies, and currency devaluation, the EU implemented targeted defensive trade measures, including increased steel tariffs on July 1 and closing tax loopholes on low-value digital import packages.
*   **The Power of Indispensability:** 
    *   **Critical supply chains:** Rather than trying to immediately replicate giant US tech conglomerates (like NVIDIA or Anthropic), Europe must secure critical, highly specialized niches in global value chains where it is irreplaceable.
    *   **Market leverage:** Europe represents roughly 25% of the total revenue and profits for major US tech hyperscalers, providing significant regulatory and economic leverage.
*   **Payment Infrastructure Independence:** By the end of 2026, the ECB will roll out wholesale interbank payment mechanisms linking Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) platforms with central bank money, laying the groundwork for full interoperability and the retail launch of the digital euro.
