### Market Strategy & Custom Silicon (ASICs)
* **General Purpose vs. Specialized Chips:** Jensen Huang clarified that while custom ASICs are already in the market, Nvidia's GPUs remain general-purpose accelerators that handle the entire AI lifecycle (data processing, pre-training, post-training, and generative AI inferencing) across closed, open, video, language, biological, physics, and robotics models.

  * **Hopper:** ~$18 billion per gigawatt
  * **Grace Blackwell:** ~$25 billion per gigawatt
  * **Vera Rubin:** $40+ billion per gigawatt (incorporating seven different Nvidia chip types)

### Investment Details & Supply Chain
* **$3.5 Billion Investment:** Nvidia is investing $3.5 billion into MediaTek through convertible bonds, reflecting multi-year, decade-long roadmap commitment and deep trust between CEO Jensen Huang and MediaTek CEO Rick Tsai.

* **Current Bottlenecks:** Rick Tsai noted that supply chain challenges—spanning HBM, substrates, and related components—remain a bottleneck, though being based in Taiwan's core AI ecosystem and partnering with Nvidia helps navigate them.
