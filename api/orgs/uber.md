## Socialists Sweep NYC, China Catches Up in Coding, AI Memory Crunch, Micron's Blowout Quarter

- Published: 2026-06-27

- Speakers: Gavin Baker (Managing Partner & CIO, Atreides Management); Travis Kalanick (CEO, City Storage Systems & Co-founder, Uber); Chamath Palihapitiya (Founder, Social Capital & Co-host, All-In Podcast)

---

**TL;DR** 
Democratic Socialists of America (DSA) candidates have swept key New York congressional primaries, signaling a populist shift in the Democratic party, while China’s new open-weight GLM-5.2 model trained on indigenous Huawei silicon demonstrates that US export controls and regulatory hurdles are failing to slow foreign AI development.

### The Socialist Sweep in NYC & the Changing Democratic Party
* **The DSA Victories:** Democratic Socialists of America (DSA) candidates backed by NYC Mayor Zoran Mamdani swept three highly competitive congressional primaries in safe Democratic seats.
    * **Key Upsets:** Brad Lander defeated two-term incumbent Dan Goldman in New York's wealthy 10th district. In the 13th district (Harlem/Bronx), Chevalier—a radical 32-year-old PhD candidate with anti-Western positions—defeated a five-term incumbent backed by House Speaker Hakeem Jeffries.
    * **Demographic Shift:** Panelists noted that the modern socialist base consists primarily of wealthy, highly educated but downwardly mobile progressives, alongside non-assimilated migrant populations.
* **The Israel Wedge Issue:** David Sacks pointed out that the Gaza conflict has become a defining motivator for young Democrats (with 80% of Democrats now disapproving of Israel). Dan Goldman's loss to Lander was largely viewed as a referendum on his pro-Israel stance.
* **The Social Media Debate:** To counter youth radicalization, Chamath Palihapitiya advocated for under-16 social media bans (similar to measures in the UK, Canada, and Australia). Travis Kalanick (founder of Adams) countered that such bans are a Trojan horse designed to force adult de-anonymization and implement state-level censorship.

### China's AI Ascent & The Silicon Bypassing
* **GLM-5.2 Breakthrough:** China's Z.ai released GLM-5.2, a 744-billion-parameter open-weights model with a 1-million-token context window, licensed under a highly permissive MIT license. 
    * **Competitive Performance:** GLM-5.2 scored 51 points on the Artificial Analysis index—the highest ever for an open-weight model—beating Western frontier models like GPT-5.5 on coding benchmarks at an 85% cheaper API cost.
* **API Distillation:** Gavin Baker (Atreides Management) explained that Chinese labs are using vast "iPhone farms" to query Western APIs (such as Anthropic's Claude), harvesting reasoning traces to train their own models at a fraction of the cost.
* **Bypassing US Sanctions:** Z.ai claims the GLM-5 family was trained entirely on domestic Huawei Ascend 910B chip clusters. This signals that China is successfully establishing an independent, end-to-end semiconductor and model ecosystem.
* **Regulatory Self-Sabotage:** Sacks criticized US safety regulations and federal bottlenecks championed by players like Anthropic's Dario Amodei. He argued that halting US frontier releases for "safety audits" only hands an advantage to Chinese competitors who are not bound by US laws.

### Micron Smashes Earnings Amid the AI Memory Crunch
* **The DRAM Bottleneck:** Micron reported a massive earnings blowout, with revenues up 4x year-over-year ($9B to $42B run rate) and its entire High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) supply sold out through 2026. Gavin Baker identified DRAM as the single most critical bottleneck in AI scaling, projected to consume 30% to 40% of all hyperscaler capex next year.
* **Consumer "AI-Flation":** The extreme demand for DRAM in AI data centers has starved consumer supply chains. Apple has begun passing these costs to consumers, raising MacBook Neo prices by 14% ($799) and Mac Studio prices by 25%. Similar price hikes are expected for gaming consoles (Xbox, PlayStation).

### The Economics of Orbital and Distributed Compute
* **Tesla’s "MEGAPOD":** Tesla recently filed a trademark for "MEGAPOD," a modular, self-contained AI data center hardware unit. This could allow Tesla to deploy decentralized GPU clusters at Supercharger stations or bundle them directly into residential Powerwall systems.
* **Terrestrial Energy & Zoning Limits:** Building a 1-gigawatt terrestrial data center currently costs roughly $35 billion in silicon and $25 billion in cooling and electrical infrastructure, with local power grids increasingly unable to support the load.
* **The Math Behind Space-Based Compute:** Gavin Baker outlined the economics of orbital data centers. With SpaceX's Starship expected to drop 1-gigawatt launch costs to $5 billion, modular orbital compute linked by space-lasers becomes highly competitive ($40B total space cost vs. $60B+ terrestrially), completely bypassing land, cooling, and environmental regulations.
* **Distributed Inference:** While distributed training remains unviable over long physical distances due to latency, distributed inference over peer-to-peer consumer networks (utilizing projects like Bittensor or local Groq/Cerebras hardware) is poised to absorb vast amounts of global query volume.

### Tech IPO Market Dynamics
* **The Cerebras Broken Deal:** Following its IPO, Cerebras fell below its initial deal price. Baker explained that many large institutional portfolio managers automatically sell a stock the moment it breaks its deal price, viewing it as a "broken promise" from bankers. He strongly advised future tech IPOs to utilize Dutch auctions to ensure realistic market pricing.
* **Massive Private Backlogs:** The panel discussed the massive backlog of private AI giants preparing to go public, with Baker estimating that Anthropic could trade at a $3 trillion valuation based on future high-margin inference scale.

## Uber CEO on AI, Autonomous Vehicles, and the Future of Transportation

- Channel: Invest Like The Best
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ThMtheE5eO0
- Published: 2026-06-03
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260603.html#video-ThMtheE5eO0
- Speakers: Dara Khosrowshahi (CEO, Uber)

---

### TL;DR
Under the leadership of CEO Dara Khosrowshahi, Uber has transitioned from a chaotic startup into a highly profitable powerhouse generating over $10 billion in free cash flow. By operating as a supply-led marketplace, leveraging its physical-world scale, and establishing over 30 autonomous vehicle (AV) partnerships, Uber is positioning itself to capture a nascent trillion-dollar physical AI and transport ecosystem.

***

### Transitioning Uber from Chaos to Order
* **The "How" of Resolving Corporate Chaos:** Khosrowshahi stabilized Uber post-2017 by breaking down massive, systemic crises into distinct, manageable dimensions.
    * **Board Alignment:** Partnered with new Chairman Ron Sugar to shift the board's focus away from factional control and toward the long-term survival of the firm.
    * **Cultural & Executive Turnaround:** Rebuilt the executive team, retaining critical legacy talent like Andrew Macdonald (President of Mobility/COO) while hiring key new leaders like Chief Legal Officer Tony West.
* **Immigrant Resilience as a Management Tool:** Khosrowshahi’s personal background—fleeing Iran at age nine and watching his family rebuild from scratch in the US—shaped his high tolerance for business pressure.
    * **Engineering Mindset:** Approaches high-stakes crises by systematically listing, testing, and solving individual problems rather than overthinking or panicking. 
    * **Work-Life Segregation:** Maintains strict personal boundaries by remaining "all in" on family time without device distractions, separating personal self-worth from corporate fortune.

### The Core Marketplace Engine: A Supply-Led Strategy
* **The Supply-Led Thesis:** Unlike demand-first platforms (such as Expedia), Uber’s growth is fundamentally supply-led. 
    * **Geographic Expansion:** To launch in secondary and tertiary markets (the "next 50 to 200 cities" in a country), Uber first recruits drivers, couriers, and merchants. Once supply is established, consumer demand follows naturally.
    * **Scale Metrics:** The global platform now supports over 10 million drivers and couriers.
* **Empathy in Supply Aggregation:** To improve retention and platform quality, leaders must experience the supplier's pain points directly.
    * **Firsthand Experience:** Khosrowshahi delivered food via e-bike and drove passengers in a Tesla in San Francisco to understand the UX.
    * **Addressing the "P95" Friction:** While a typical consumer interacts with the app for 30 seconds, a driver is on the platform for 6–10 hours a day. Consequently, minor bugs (P95 errors) that occur weekly for drivers require far higher product engineering prioritization than consumer-facing bugs.

### The Trillion-Dollar AV and Physical AI Frontier
* **Bridging the Digital and Physical Worlds:** Uber operates in a probabilistic real-world environment characterized by unpredictable events (traffic, cancellations, late food). 
    * **Model Scaling:** Uber’s search and feed machine learning models are now 10,000 times larger than legacy models, enabling the app to accurately predict a passenger's destination 75% of the time upon opening.
* **The Turnkey AV Ecosystem:** Uber has established over 30 partnerships across the AV space, including Waymo, Nuro, Lucid, Nvidia, Waabi, and Wayve.
    * **The Value Proposition:** Instead of building proprietary hardware, Uber acts as the commercialization partner for AV developers. Uber provides depot management, charging infrastructure, autonomous insurance, and fleet financing (including a $1 billion vehicle financing deal with Santander).
    * **The 30% Utilization Lift:** AVs operating on the Uber network experience a 30%+ increase in utilization (trips and revenue per vehicle per day) compared to proprietary, single-party AV networks.
* **Market Sizing and Hardware Economics:** Khosrowshahi projects that AVs will unlock a trillion-dollar market as hardware costs fall 30% to 40% per generation.
    * **Target Vehicle Pricing:** Working with Lucid and Nuro on $60,000–$70,000 mid-size purpose-built vehicles to make AV transport cheaper than private car ownership.
    * **Drones:** Delivery drones (e.g., Zipline) face battery density and payload challenges but are projected to scale for ultra-fast, cheap 10-to-15-minute deliveries within 5 to 10 years.
    * **Regional Adoption:** The Middle East (Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Saudi Arabia) is moving fastest due to proactive, entrepreneurial regulators. The US (California, Texas) is scaling rapidly, while European pilots (such as London) are slated to begin by the end of the year.

### Platform Aggregation: Uber One and Pre-Planned Travel
* **Cross-Platform Customer Acquisition:** Uber’s multi-product platform acts as a structural advantage over single-line competitors.
    * **Organic Funneling:** Roughly 13% of all Uber Eats bookings are generated organically from users opening the core mobility app.
* **Uber One Membership Economics:** The loyalty program has grown to over 15 million members, expanding at 50% year-over-year.
    * **The "Prime" Playbook:** Modeled after Amazon Prime, Uber One accepts lower profit margins on a member's first-year transactions in exchange for significantly higher lifetime value, transaction frequency, and retention in subsequent years.
* **Expanding Into Planned Travel and Hotels:** Uber is expanding its temporal reach from "on-demand" to "pre-planned" bookings.
    * **Uber Reserve:** Has quietly scaled to a $5 billion annualized run rate.
    * **Expedia Hotel Integration:** By partnering with Expedia to offer hotel bookings (providing up to 20% back for Uber One members), Uber aims to build an integrated travel assistant that automatically pre-books airport rides, alerts hotel front desks of arrival times, and coordinates room keys.

### Leadership Philosophy and Capital Allocation
* **Getting to the "Ground Truth":** Inspired by his mentor, IAC Chairman Barry Diller, Khosrowshahi actively bypasses processed corporate summaries to find raw data.
    * **Unfiltered Information:** Creating random communication channels across the company to preserve the "edge" of raw operational feedback, which gets lost when reports are filtered by middle management.
* **Fostering Corporate "Mutations":** To survive rapid technological shifts, large organizations must protect and promote internal "troublemakers."
    * **The 5x AI Acceleration:** AI has accelerated the internal rate of organizational change fivefold, making corporate adaptability and structural mutation essential.
* **Capital Allocation Priority:** Uber prioritizes high-return organic investments and strategic AV partnerships over shareholder returns, though its massive cash generation allows for both.
    * **Balanced Capital Return:** The company funds robust share buybacks while simultaneously financing large-scale capital commitments for EV and AV transition fleets.
