### TL;DR
Howie Liu of HyperAgent outlines the evolution of AI agent form factors—from completions to autonomous, always-on agent fleets—culminating in the HyperAgent Startup Battlefield finals where three agent-native startups competed for $100,000 in prizes, with Comment.io taking the grand prize.

### Evolution of Agent Form Factors
* **Progression of Models:**
  * **Completions:** Models accept a string of tokens and emit tokens until a stop token.
  * **Chatbots:** Instruct-tuned models enabling conversational back-and-forth interactions.
  * **Agents:** Models that recurse upon themselves with open-ended tool calls and reasoning steps rather than following prescripted linear workflows.
  * **Always-On Agents ("Claws"):** Agents capable of running for extended periods (minutes to hours) utilizing heartbeat mechanisms to wake up repeatedly, perform multi-turn loops, and creatively unblock themselves.
  * **Orchestrating Agents:** Models capable of complex, parallelized task distribution and agent-to-agent interactions to manage context windows and coordinate advanced workflows.

### Real-World Fleet Deployment: Landscaping Use Case
* **End-to-End Business Automation:** 
  * Highlighted a real customer case where a landscaping business runs end-to-end via a fleet of HyperAgent agents handling prospecting, surveying, pitching, and quote generation.
* **Agent Workflow & Human-in-the-Loop:**
  * **Triager Agent:** Takes inbound client inquiries, assesses project scope, and verifies lead quality.
  * **Surveyor Agent:** Uses a fully capable sandbox VM to manipulate files and analyze imagery/video (via tools like `ffmpeg`) to design bespoke pitch decks and interactive web pages.
  * **Human Oversight:** Human managers intervene only to unblock agents and authorize high-impact actions like final quotes or client pitches, serving as orchestrators of an agent fleet rather than executing micro-tasks.
  * **Real-Time Learning:** Agents accumulate memories, skill updates, and user feedback in real time (via Slack, email, or direct interactions) to adjust performance dynamically without waiting for retraining cycles.
