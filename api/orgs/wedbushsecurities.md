### The Evolution of Software Engineering (FDE Model)
* **Accelerated Deployment:** Ted Mabrey (Palantir, 16-year veteran) reports that the pace of software deployment is "accelerating to zero." Applications that used to take years to develop are now reaching MVP (Minimum Viable Product) status in days or weeks.
* **The "Iron Man Suit" for Operations:** The role of the Forward-Deployed Engineer (FDE) has shifted from standardizing management tools to empowering "the guy at the edge" (e.g., a plant manager).
    * **Operational Experts:** Software is now designed to act as an "Iron Man suit" for subject matter experts, allowing them to dictate how the system runs based on real-world tribal knowledge.
* **Infrastructure Strategy:** Palantir’s core philosophy is building adaptive infrastructure that presumes models will constantly improve, rather than building rigid, legacy-limited systems.

* **Privacy and Rights:** Karp positions himself as a defender of Fourth Amendment (privacy) rights at the executive table, arguing that while AI must be lethal on the battlefield, its domestic use must be strictly litigated to prevent government overreach.
