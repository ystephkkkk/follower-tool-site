### TL;DR
Christine Lagarde announced she will step down early as ECB President in February 2027—eight months ahead of her October 2027 term end—to regain freedom of expression and defend principles like the truth without the neutrality obligations of her office. Meanwhile, the Governing Council unanimously voted to keep the three key ECB interest rates unchanged, citing tightening long-term financing conditions and elevated uncertainty despite upside inflation risks.

### Monetary Policy Decisions & Economic Outlook
* **Interest Rates Held:** **Unchanged:** The Governing Council decided to keep the three key ECB interest rates unchanged following a unanimous vote.
    * **Deliberation:** The Council debated whether a rate hike was appropriate given rising energy prices and long-term financing costs, but ultimately judged that a measured approach was warranted.
    * **Data Dependency:** Future decisions will follow a meeting-by-meeting approach without pre-committing to a specific rate path.
* **Inflation Trends:** **Mixed:** Inflation rose to **3.8% in September** (up from **3.2% in August**).
    * **Energy & Services:** Energy price inflation increased markedly to **18.9%** (from **14.3% in August**), driven by oil, petrol, gas, and refining margins. Services inflation rose to **3.2%** (from **3%**).
    * **Underlying Measures:** Indicators of underlying inflation remained broadly stable; inflation excluding energy rose to **2.3%** (from **2.1%**).
    * **Wage Pressures:** Compensation per employee grew by **3.3% in Q2** (down from **3.6% in Q1**). The forward-looking wage tracker projects negotiated wage growth to fluctuate between **2.5% and 2.8%** for the remainder of **2026** and average **2.6%** in the first three quarters of **2027**. Longer-term inflation expectations remain anchored around **2%**.
* **Growth and Labor Market:** **Moderate Expansion:** The euro area economy continued to expand in Q3, supported by services recovery, AI-related business investment, government infrastructure spending, and defense.
    * **Labor Metrics:** The unemployment rate remained at **6.4% in August**. Employment and labor force growth remain subdued, while productivity has gradually improved.
* **Financing Conditions:** **Tightening:** Long-term sovereign yields have risen significantly across global markets.
    * **Corporate & Mortgage Costs:** The cost of market-based debt for firms rose to **4.1% in August** (from **4% in July**), while bank lending rates for firms held at **3.8%**. Mortgage rates rose to **3.8% in August** (from **3.5% in July**). Growth in bank lending to firms slowed to an annual rate of **4.2%** in August and September.

### Risks to Outlook
* **Upside Inflation Risks:** **Geopolitical & Energy:** Upside risks stem from potential escalation of the Middle East conflict and Russia's war in Ukraine, which could cause further energy supply disruptions, higher gas prices, second-round wage effects, and food price spikes from extreme weather.
* **Downside Growth Risks:** **External Pressures:** Growth risks are tilted downward due to potential energy shocks, worsening global financial market sentiment, tightening financing conditions, fiscal uncertainty, and resurgent trade tensions that could disrupt supply chains.

### Christine Lagarde's Early Departure & Freedom of Expression
* **Resignation Timeline:** **February 7, 2027:** Lagarde announced she will step down at the end of the February monetary policy Governing Council, anticipating the natural conclusion of her term in October 2027.
* **Motivation for Exit:** **Neutrality Constraints:** Lagarde explained that leading the ECB requires strict neutrality on matters outside monetary policy. She stated she no longer feels comfortable maintaining this neutrality in the current global environment.
* **Commitment to Truth:** **Advocacy:** She expressed deep concern over the misrepresentation of truth globally and believes her voice from her decades of international economic experience can help defend principles close to her heart.
* **Institutional Continuity:** **Solid Governance:** Lagarde assured that the ECB's governance and business continuity remain rock-solid, noting that the process to identify her successor has already begun.
