## Modernizing Market Infrastructure | Material Matters with SEC Chairman Paul Atkins

- Channel: U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rES0sl5MrdE
- Published: 2026-11-20
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20261120.html#video-rES0sl5MrdE
- Speakers: Paul Atkins (Chairman, U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission); Jamie Selway (Director of the Division of Trading and Markets, U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission)

---

**TL;DR:** SEC Chairman Paul Atkins and Division of Trading and Markets Director Jamie Selway outline the Commission's push to modernize U.S. market infrastructure through a five-year "Innovation Exemption" sandbox for tokenized securities, an inevitable transition to 24/7 trading, and major cost-cutting reforms to the Consolidated Audit Trail (CAT). They also highlight closer CFTC collaboration, a shift away from regulation-by-enforcement, and upcoming rulemakings on Treasury clearing, off-channel communications, and the dealer definition.

### Division of Trading and Markets: Scope & Engagement
* **Division Structure:** Director Jamie Selway leads ~230 staff across 8 offices—5 policy offices and 3 support offices (legal review, administration, and analytics/research).
  * Key units include the Office of Clearance and Settlement (led by Jeff Mooney, overseeing DTCC) and the Office of Broker Dealer Finance (previously led by Mike Macchiaroli, overseeing the net capital rule, customer protection rule, and SIPC coordination).
* **Market Complexity:** The division oversees 1934 Act registrants, including 18 equity exchanges, 20 options exchanges (the 20th launched recently), thousands of broker-dealers, transfer agents, alternative trading systems (ATSs), security-based swap dealers, security-based swap SEFs, and SROs like FINRA.
* **Industry Outreach:** Under Chairman Atkins' mandate, the division holds 8 to 10 industry meetings per week and has hosted 5 roundtables over the past 1.5 years (including a mid-September roundtable on 24/7 trading).

### Tokenization & The "Innovation Exemption" Sandbox
* **Cross-Act Crypto Strategy:** The 1934 Act strategy complements Chairman Atkins' 1933 Act "Reg Crypto Assets" proposal and Brian Daley's 1940 Act crypto custody strategy. Earlier 34 Act steps include the wallet statement, a DTCC no-action letter for blockchain clearance/settlement, and a tokenization taxonomy with CorpFin distinguishing true tokenized securities from synthetics or swaps.
* **Innovation Exemption Mechanics:** Provides 5-year exemptive relief (cabined by volume thresholds) for U.S. persons to operate Tokenized Securities Venues (TSVs) trading true tokenized NMS securities (not synthetics).
  * Exempts TSVs from traditional exchange obligations, including market data reporting, CAT reporting, and Rule 611 (the trade-through rule).
  * Requires a notice filing, public website disclosures (detailing chains used, operations, and checks and balances), and 30-day issuer notice/consent before launching.
* **"Innovation Without Arbitrage":** Both Atkins and Selway emphasize bringing tokenized activity onshore rather than losing it to offshore synthetics, while ensuring traditional exchanges and ATSs can also modify their rulesets to trade on-chain without being disadvantaged.
* **SIPC & On-Chain Payments:** Questions remain on how SIPC will treat tokenized assets and dollar-based stablecoins. The SEC has already approved money market mutual funds for on-chain payment and is working with bank regulators to enable tokenized bank deposits.

### The Path to 23/5 and 24/7 Trading
* **Current Baseline:** U.S. equity markets have operated 16 hours a day (4:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. ET) for ~20 years. Broker-sponsored ATSs began 24/5 trading a couple of years ago, growing to ~1% of market volume without public market data.
* **Near-Term Milestones:** Exchange 24 X (which applied ~3 years ago) plans to go live with exchange trading and public market data on December 6, operating on a 23/5 schedule (closing 8:00–9:00 p.m. ET for system resets and corporate actions). Over the summer, DTCC began clearing and settling 23/5.
* **24/7 Logistics:** One ATS has already announced plans to move to 24/7 trading. Key operational hurdles include prefunding weekend activity (since banking systems are closed), systematizing corporate actions (splits, dividends, halts), and recalibrating issuer earnings releases away from "after-hours" to maximize liquidity.

### CAT Restructuring & CFTC Collaboration
* **CAT Cost & Governance Overhaul:** The Consolidated Audit Trail (CAT) has ~$2 billion in sunk build-out costs and previously carried a $250 million/year run rate. Under Chairman Atkins, SROs helped reduce the annual run rate to just under $150 million.
  * Following a concept release, the SEC plans to assume governance of CAT and fund it via Section 31 fees, establishing cost-conscious governance (such as advisory committees) and adapting audit trails for new assets like perpetual single-stock futures, event contracts, and tokenized securities.
* **Ending the SEC–CFTC Turf War:** Working with CFTC Chairman Selig, the agencies are collaborating on shared registrants and overlapping exposures (e.g., S&P 500, KPI products).
  * Joint requests for comment cover portfolio margining, definitional boundaries, and swap/security-based swap reporting alignment. Three registrants have applied to trade KPI products as security options.

### Traditional Rulemaking, Exams, & Enforcement Reform
* **Treasury Clearing Mandate:** Led with Commissioner Uyeda (originating from the Clayton era), requiring clearing of cash treasuries year-round in 2026 and repo clearing by mid-year 2027 to lower the cost of capital.
* **Transfer Agent & Dealer Rules:** A transfer agent rule proposal is out for comment (the first update in over 50 years). A proposed dealer rule is nearing release to clarify that a dealer must have a customer/client nexus, addressing prior administration court losses.
* **Exams & Off-Channel Communications:** Chairman Atkins directed Exams to act "more like teachers, less like police," using teach-ins and field feedback to tune rules rather than immediate enforcement referrals. Trading & Markets and Investment Management are nearing a proposal on off-channel communications (text messages) after past enforcement fines lacked any underlying insider trading or tipping.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **24 X Exchange Launch:** Exchange 24 X will go live with both exchange trading and public market data on December 6. — *Jamie Selway*
* **24/7 Trading & Market Data:** "We view 24/7 as being inevitable. So we'll get to formalizing market data and allowing exchanges to move forward in that fashion probably late next year." — *Jamie Selway*
* **Permanent Tokenization Rules:** Rule proposals to make the Innovation Exemption concepts more permanent will be developed "next year." — *Jamie Selway*
* **CAT Restructuring:** "I'm personally hopeful that we can complete sort of this, this one project [CAT restructuring] under your tenure. I think we can." — *Jamie Selway*
* **Treasury Clearing Deadlines:** Clearing of cash treasuries will be required year-round in 2026, and clearing of repo by mid-year 2027. — *Jamie Selway*
