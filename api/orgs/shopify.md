## Tobi Lütke on the AI agent revolution and future of Shopify

- Channel: Sources Podcast
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r9SHzCW9zxo
- Published: 2026-10-28
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20261028.html#video-r9SHzCW9zxo
- Speakers: Tobi Lütke (CEO, Shopify)

---

### TL;DR
Shopify CEO Tobi Lütke discusses why Shopify is embracing the AI agent revolution while competitors like Amazon put up walls, detailing how agents are transforming commerce, business creation, and everyday productivity.

### Philosophy of Commerce and AI Agents
* **The Two Philosophies of Commerce:** 
  * Shopify and Amazon represent polar-opposite approaches to retail. Amazon digitizes the department store model, relying on aggregator scale, heavy inventory, and a protected ads business.
  * Shopify aligns with the independent boutique and entrepreneurship model, focusing on storytelling, merchant control, and shared success up and down the stack without operating a conflicting traditional marketplace.
* **Embracing the Agentic Web:** 
  * Shopify welcomes agentic platforms like Meta's Muse, Grockbot, and Instinct, building APIs to ensure checkouts work seamlessly whether initiated by a human or an AI agent.
* **The Ads Protection Barrier:** 
  * Platforms are erecting walls against agents largely to protect massive advertising businesses; because Shopify has no ads business, it remains fully aligned with its merchants.

### Early AI Shopping Trends and New Possibilities
* **Project-Based Purchases:** 
  * Agents are increasingly driving high-order-value, multi-item planning purchases—such as setting up a home gym or gathering supplies for a weekend 3D-printing project with children.
* **Phase Transition in Technology:** 
  * The industry is currently in a phase where agents help users execute existing tasks better, but this will soon give way to discovering entirely new, digitally native possibilities that were previously unimaginable.

### Optimism, Sci-Fi, and the Tech Frontier
* **Technological Optimism:** 
  * Lütke attributes his high optimism to software that is *trained rather than written*, comparing the current era to teaching sand to think and having computers fulfill wishes.
* **Sci-Fi Influences:** 
  * Re-reading Neil Stevenson's *Cryptonomicon* highlighted how accurately near-future science fiction predicted cryptocurrencies and fractional ownership. Lütke notes that dystopian sci-fi bestsellers contributed to a temporary "tech mopiness" in the industry.
* **Avoiding Zero-Sum Thinking:** 
  * Economic value is nearly infinite and locked behind knowledge (such as matrix multiplication or breaking the atom); zero-sum competition only occurs when societies lack growth frontiers.

### Entrepreneurship and Shopify’s Business Model
* **Skyrocketing Business Creation:** 
  * AI acts as cruise control for ambition, drastically lowering the barrier to entrepreneurship by answering complex business questions instantly—mirroring a surge in net-new business formation in the U.S.
* **Gross Profit Dollar Growth Over Margins:** 
  * Lütke prioritizes gross profit dollar growth and lowering friction over rigid percentage take rates, ensuring Shopify remains closely tied to its core mission of expanding entrepreneurship.
* **The "Rack Seller" Setup:** 
  * Lütke converted his wine cellar into a high-powered server rack housing a Dell GB300, running local open-source models and a personal AI "claw" agent that integrates his calendar, Fitbit data, and historical digital logs.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Tobi Lütke** claims that software is now "trained rather than written," shifting the paradigm from writing code to interviewing AI models for capabilities.
* **Tobi Lütke** states that the current phase of agents helping people perform existing tasks better will inevitably be followed by a phase of discovering completely new, digitally native possibilities that were previously impossible.

### Notable Quotes
> *"For the first time in in our lives we have software that we train rather than write. And we then basically have to interview it for its capabilities afterwards."* — Tobi Lütke

> *"We are gifted with utterly unfinite [infinite] potential value. Every product that could exist is out there... We teach sand to think and afterwards we have to reinvent everything."* — Tobi Lütke

> *"If you want to build a company that's like successful, you need to build a company that's too good to ignore. And if you want to have a fantastical career, you need to be gain the skills and aptitudes that make you too too good to ignore."* — Tobi Lütke


## The New Full-Stack Designer

- Channel: Y Combinator
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eojywvxqoLc
- Published: 2026-10-26
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20261026.html#video-eojywvxqoLc
- Speakers: Marvin Schwaibold (Lead, Product Design Studio, Shopify); Aaron Epstein (Host, Y Combinator Design Review)

---

### TL;DR
Marvin Schwaibold of Shopify and Aaron Epstein of Y Combinator review how AI tooling is shifting product design toward a full-stack, editor-driven workflow, and evaluate motion and interactivity across five viewer-submitted startup websites.

### Shopify's AI Product Studio & Workflow Evolution
* **Shift to AI-Driven Workflows:** **Marvin Schwaibold** explains that while design processes remain iterative, tooling has moved from static aesthetic layouts in Figma to terminal-based AI agents (like Codex and Cloud Code) that rapidly produce real, testable demos.
* **The Designer as Editor:** **Marvin Schwaibold** notes that AI handles the heavy lifting of code and execution complexity, placing humans in the driving seat to curate, quality-control, and steer outputs based on what looks and feels right.
* **Shopify's Dual-Track Initiative:** **Marvin Schwaibold** describes his team's work over the past year touching core surfaces, split into two areas:
  * **Admin Experience:** Redesigning and polishing interfaces for web and the mobile app (the "green app").
  * **Sidekick:** Building AI infrastructure that allows merchants to manage and automate their stores conversationally via chat.
* **Prototyping for Buy-In:** **Marvin Schwaibold** emphasizes that tangible prototypes built with AI spread through an organization like wildfire and secure founder/executive alignment much faster than traditional PRD documents.

### Independent Studio Roots & The "Primal" System
* **Molly Studio Background:** **Marvin Schwaibold** highlights his time running the Brooklyn design studio Molly alongside partner J Tal, working with major clients like Collins, Apple, and Google with a tight team of two to three concurrent clients.
* **The Primal Content System:** **Marvin Schwaibold** explains this structured methodology used to digest heavy client information into exploratory website flows where content is progressively disclosed rather than demanding attention.
* **Collins Bookshelf Craft:** **Marvin Schwaibold** details a hand-crafted bookshelf component built with engineer Jesper featuring organic, level-up bounce animations designed as an "Easter egg" to signal agency-level dedication and craft to potential clients.
* **Joining Shopify:** **Marvin Schwaibold** shares that Tobi Lutke’s mandate—stating that the future needs to be designed and requiring designers to paint a picture—inspired him to bring his studio into Shopify for massive-scale impact.

### Grass Shader & DialKit Prototyping
* **Weekend Grass Shader Project:** **Marvin Schwaibold** details a personal project (available on GitHub via MarvinSchwaibold/grass-shader) converting real grass video footage into a web shader with 217,000 individual grass blade variations.
* **Iterative AI Prompting:** **Marvin Schwaibold** describes feeding video, screenshots, and topographical height maps into ChatGPT and Codex agents over multiple prompts to correct underlying terrain geometry and lighting.
* **DialKit Integration:** **Marvin Schwaibold** explains how Josh introduced **DialKit** (run via `npm run` from the website) to add binary toggles and variable sliders, exposing underlying structures to instantly spot errors and tweak values like grass length and wind movement.
* **Carousel Iteration:** **Marvin Schwaibold** shares that a portfolio carousel inspired by Mike Matas took 91 prompts to get right, utilizing a side parameter panel to dial in physical properties like "pull boost."

### Viewer Website Reviews (Motion & Interactivity)
* **Nstack:** **Marvin Schwaibold** and **Aaron Epstein** praise the retro, whimsical desktop-emulator feel and dropping users straight into the product, but criticize visual overload, dense sidebars, and competing CTA buttons that violate the rule of clear visual hierarchy.
* **SkillSync:** **Marvin Schwaibold** and **Aaron Epstein** note that while dark mode and dark aesthetic are clean, the page is a "mile wide and an inch deep," burying key information behind unclicked sidebar tabs and utilizing superfluous animations that do not aid user comprehension.
* **Instaplay:** **Aaron Epstein** and **Marvin Schwaibold** highlight the instant engagement of interactive prompt-to-game capabilities, but critique the immediate paywall/sign-up friction and hidden navigation paths that obscure the primary goal of creating games.
* **Netlify (Mentlify):** **Marvin Schwaibold** and **Aaron Epstein** praise the clean, trustworthy presentation, reductive typography, strong social proof (Microsoft, Anthropic, Coinbase), and breathable, well-spaced chapter-based scroll flow.
* **Nullify:** **Aaron Epstein** and **Marvin Schwaibold** commend the high-quality instructional videos, but criticize the competing headline placeholders, difficult click targets on carousels, and the use of sluggish scroll-jacking that hinders native user scroll speed.
* **Instinct:** **Aaron Epstein** and **Marvin Schwaibold** praise the immersive 3D/video elements and brave serif font choices for space tech, but recommend instantly triggering hover states and adopting **Blossom Carousel** (from the open-source Blossom Carousel GitHub repo) for smoother trackpad scrolling.

### Notable Quotes
> "We're more the editors now than ever before... AI is definitely raised the ceiling for all of us, but it's not a crafter. It can execute complexity... but it doesn't know what looks good and what looks bad and that's for me to decide."
> — *Marvin Schwaibold*

> "Smooth scrolling on websites is a disaster in the industry... You want interruptible software because our thought patterns are interruptible... You would never want to get a user into an environment where they have to smooth scroll or scroll jack through the content."
> — *Marvin Schwaibold*

> "Codex won't get mad at me if I need 91 approaches at getting the animation correct. If I had done that with my engineer, they would probably flip out."
> — *Marvin Schwaibold*


## Shopify CEO: Most Businesses Are Using AI Wrong

- Channel: The Knowledge Project Podcast
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G9P9D9hptq8
- Published: 2026-09-15
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260915.html#video-G9P9D9hptq8
- Speakers: Tobi Lütke (CEO and Founder, Shopify); Shane Parrish (Host, The Knowledge Project Podcast)

---

**TL;DR:** Shopify CEO Tobi Lütke explains how the company uses autonomous AI agents with distinct personalities to handle up to 50% of code pull requests, while emphasizing that true human responsibility, taste, and deep systems thinking remain irreplaceable.

### AI Infrastructure and Agents at Shopify
* **River, the AI Colleague:** Shopify utilizes an internal AI agent named River who operates directly in Slack with access to company tools, systems, and codebases.
    * **Personality and Permission:** River is explicitly prompted to possess a distinct personality, use sarcasm when appropriate, and push back on stupid requests rather than acting as a patronizing assistant.
    * **Transparent Workflows:** River only operates in open channels to foster "osmosis learning," allowing 7,000 remote employees to observe, learn, and naturally integrate AI into daily workflows.
    * **Self-Reflection (Dreaming):** River undergoes periodic offline "dreaming" sessions where daily logs and struggle points are reviewed, resulting in updated skill files and behavioral nudges.
* **Coding Paradigm Shifts:** A vanishingly small number of Shopify employees write raw code by hand; instead, engineers work deeply assisted by 10 to 50 concurrent AI agent windows coordinating state management and infrastructure limits.
* **The Danger of "Slop Grenades":** Lütke warns that the failure mode of lazy AI usage has shifted from a lack of output to over-output, where employees lob unread AI-generated pull requests and lengthy missives ("slop grenades") at colleagues.

### Decision-Making and AI Councils
* **LLM-as-a-Judge Architecture:** Lütke uses an AI Chief of Staff and multi-agent councils to audit the methodology behind complex company decisions.
    * **Multi-Expert Simulation:** For major strategic choices, an AI system orchestrates sub-agents representing different expert perspectives (data, research, business, engineering) across multiple frontier models (including Grok, ChatGPT, and Opus).
    * **Synthesized Audio Briefings:** These agent debates are synthesized by top-tier models and delivered to Lütke as audio files to listen to during morning workouts.
* **The Limit of Machines:** While AI excels at creating the right environment and research for decisions, Lütke stresses that *machines cannot take responsibility*; ultimate accountability must always remain human.

### Superintelligence and Systems Design
* **Redefining Superintelligence:** Lütke argues that superintelligence is not a distant sci-fi event but something humanity has always lived alongside via complex societal systems, cities, and communities.
* **Studying Enduring Institutions:** Building a company designed to last requires looking past recent non-fiction and studying durable historical systems, such as the thousands-year-old operational structure of the Catholic Church or Venetian double-entry accounting.
* **The Trap of Short-Termism:** Executives default to short-termism because their local incentive systems demand quarterly progress metrics rather than multi-year foundational rebuilds.

### Taste, Intuition, and Subtraction
* **Cultivating Taste and Judgment:** As AI capabilities scale, human taste, judgment, and deep system understanding become the most valuable career skills.
    * **Repetition and Depth:** Great taste stems from deep reps, studying the masters, and understanding why historical solutions worked.
    * **Intuition as Compressed Judgment:** Intuition is judgment executed instantly via the brain's visual neural cortex and pattern recognition, often operating without the rapid feedback loops demanded by traditional decision frameworks.
* **The Power of Pruning and Subtraction:** Borrowing from the iterative evolution of SpaceX's Raptor engines, Lütke emphasizes that systems cannot be improved solely by adding layers; teams must actively prune, rebuild, and create formal end-points for outdated processes.

> "If you punch through this, you find simpler core ideas... And that is what we call judgment. Judgment truly is finding the best path when there's no obviously best available."
