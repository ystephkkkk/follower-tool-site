## Koray Kavukcuoglu on frontier models, coding agents, and building AGI

- Channel: Google for Developers
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rrr2gdbvNFU

- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260901.html#video-Rrr2gdbvNFU
- Speakers: Koray Kavukcuoglu (SVP and Chief AI Architect, Google DeepMind); Logan Kilpatrick (Host, Google for Developers)

---

### TL;DR
Google DeepMind SVP and Chief AI Architect Koray Kavukcuoglu joins host Logan Kilpatrick to discuss the journey from early reinforcement learning milestones to Gemini, the breakthrough of agentic coding, and Google's unwavering commitment to building AGI at the frontier.

### Evolution of AI: From Atari to Agentic Workflows
* **Foundational Research Milestones:** 
  * Early DeepMind research focused on ambitious, highly-focused projects to corral the team around hard problems, leading directly to DQN and the first successful combination of deep learning and reinforcement learning (RL) on Atari games.
  * Over 20 years of deep learning progress have transitioned AI from narrow tasks to general intelligence.
* **The Shift to Agentic Coding:** 
  * Moving from 3.0 to the 3.5, 3.6, and 3.7 series taught the team what it means to build software engineering agents that work with tools and functions every day.
  * Software engineering is considered the most critical domain because it sits at the root of many different capabilities.
* **Navigating Ambiguity:** 
  * While the fundamentals of learning and optimization (loss functions, RL algorithms) remain consistent, modern domains have grown significantly harder due to real-world depth and ambiguity.
  * Language is vastly richer, more complex, and multi-faceted than constrained game action spaces, requiring higher intelligence for models to effectively partner with users.

### Frontier Focus and Gemini Development
* **Uncompromising Frontier Goal:** 
  * **Koray Kavukcuoglu:** Stated bluntly that "there's nothing other than being at the frontier that is important for us," driving all goals, focus, decisions, and prioritizations.
* **Gemini 3.x Rapid Iteration:** 
  * Launched Gemini 3.5 at I/O, followed by 3.6 and then 3.7 just three weeks later.
  * The rapid progress and architectural improvements in Flash models have made them increasingly competitive, serving as a primary progress path while 3.5 Pro is simultaneously developed.
* **Gemini 4 Pre-training:** 
  * **Logan Kilpatrick & Koray Kavukcuoglu:** Gemini 4 is currently in pre-training, described as the most ambitious pre-training run the team has done so far. 
  * **Koray Kavukcuoglu:** Noted the run is going well ("touch wood"), though the team remains cautiously optimistic until the model is placed in front of internal and external users.

### The Google Advantage in Building AGI
* **Long-Term DNA:** 
  * Google has a 15-year history of investing in foundational long-term technologies before they were mainstream—such as building custom AI chips 15 years ago, quantum computing, and Waymo.
* **Multi-Billion User Scale:** 
  * **Koray Kavukcuoglu:** Google provides the full stack to optimize and an organization with a massive number of multi-billion-user products focused on delivering real value and services.
* **Co-Building with Users:** 
  * Success in building AGI relies on a large funnel of ideas, exploration, and direct interaction with users—ranging from individuals managing daily emails to scientists using models to invent things that have never been invented before.
  * User feedback serves as the guide for what problems are worth solving.

### Benchmarks and the Philosophy of AGI
* **The Absence of an AGI Test:** 
  * **Koray Kavukcuoglu:** Argued that no test or benchmark currently exists that definitively defines AGI. 
  * **Logan Kilpatrick:** Invited viewers to send any such benchmark to the team so they can run their models on it.
* **Stacked Sigmoids of Progress:** 
  * Technological advancement often happens through stacked sigmoids—breakthroughs unlock progress, followed by incremental stages that build understanding and lead to the next major discovery.

## Introducing Gemini Robotics 2

- Channel: Google for Developers
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-rYFDefcq3k
- Published: 2026-07-31
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260731.html#video--rYFDefcq3k
- Speakers: Carolina Parada (Researcher, Google DeepMind); Stuart Bowers (Researcher, Google DeepMind); Logan Kilpatrick (Host, Google for Developers)

---

#### TL;DR
Google DeepMind introduced the **Gemini Robotics 2** model suite, bringing whole-body intelligence, fine-grained dexterity (supporting hands with up to 20 joints per hand), and multi-robot collaboration to general-purpose robotics. The Embodied Reasoning (ER) model is available via AI Studio and Gemini Enterprise Agents Platform, while action models are accessible through a trusted tester program and deep partnerships.

---

### 1. Core Model Suite: Gemini Robotics 2
* **Whole-Body Intelligence:** **Carolina Parada** noted that the new models understand the spatial position of every part of the robot simultaneously, enabling complex tasks like organizing a garage or cleaning closets.
* **Dexterity Upgrades:** **Jie Tan** explained that the system handles contact-rich, high-degree-of-freedom manipulation (such as packing lunch, screwing in lightbulbs, and tying trash bags).
* **Multi-Robot Collaboration:** **Carolina Parada** highlighted that robots can now recognize their own limits and coordinate with other specialized robots in parallel to accelerate shared tasks.

### 2. Research Arc and Technological Evolution
* **From Reinforcement Learning to VLAs:** **Carolina Parada** traced the team's history from pioneering reinforcement learning and sim-to-real transfer for locomotion, to integrating LLMs/VLMs for semantics, transformers for data-driven robotics, and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) foundation models.
* **Frontier Model Leverage:** **Stuart Bowers** emphasized that instead of solving robotics from scratch, the team latched physical tasks onto the world-understanding capabilities of large frontier models like Gemini, treating robot actions as an added modality.
* **The Data Pyramid Challenge:** **Jie Tan** outlined the data bottleneck: while teleoperation provides high-quality action signals, it does not scale; egocentric human video lacks actuation/force labels; and the lack of an "internet of physical interaction" remains the primary open obstacle to physical AGI.

### 3. Deployment Strategy & Real-World Horizons
* **Broad Tasks Over Brittle Niches:** **Carolina Parada** and **Stuart Bowers** stressed that training on narrow tasks creates brittle policies; the strategy is to build for general-purpose tasks first, even though initial commercial deployments will focus on semi-structured industrial and retail environments rather than messy homes.
* **The Partner Ecosystem:** **Stuart Bowers** noted that all robot hardware used in the lab is built by deep external partners, with whom Google DeepMind works closely to co-accelerate AI capabilities, safety, and hardware readiness.
* **Timeline to Daily Life:** **Jie Tan** revised his estimate for when general-purpose robots will enter daily life down from 10 years (estimated two years ago) to **5 to 10 years**.

### 4. Safety, Benchmarking, and Accessibility
* **Proactive Safety & Clarification:** **Carolina Parada** stated that ER 2.0 is the team's safest model yet, featuring the ability to detect human proximity, evaluate task completion likelihood, and proactively ask clarifying questions when given ambiguous instructions.
* **Asimov Benchmark:** **Carolina Parada** announced the open-sourcing of **Asimov Agentic**, a new safety benchmark featuring real-world examples to test semantic physical understanding and common sense.
* **API & Platform Availability:** **Carolina Parada** confirmed that Embodied Reasoning 2 is accessible via **AI Studio** and coming soon to **Gemini Enterprise Agents Platform**, while action models are available via API, partner integrations, and a trusted tester waitlist for on-device fine-uning.

---

### Notable Quotes

> *"What we're building here is the intelligence layer to power any robot to do a broad range of useful tasks. And we've been building towards this moment for a very long time... infusing a robot with human-level intelligence, right? So that it can understand the environment as you and I can, so that it can reason about what it means to complete a task."* — **Carolina Parada**

> *"Dexterous manipulation with all these different objects in real world is really an unsolved problem for robotics for now... We're missing this internet of physical interaction data."* — **Jie Tan**

> *"You cannot reach AGI until you solve physical AGI... The AI has passed the bar exam, and code up all these operating systems, but they cannot cook you eggs, or flip a burger."* — **Stuart Bowers**


## Yossi Matias on the golden age of research

- Channel: Google for Developers
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FPBwadTeph0
- Published: 2026-06-11
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260611.html#video-FPBwadTeph0
- Speakers: Yossi Matias (VP & Head of Google Research, Google); Logan Kilpatrick (Host, Google for Developers)

---

**TL;DR** 
Google Research is driving a new "golden age of research" by accelerating the "magic cycle" of innovation—an iterative loop that rapidly translates breakthrough scientific discoveries in climate, medicine, and quantum computing into scalable, real-world tools that amplify human capability.

---

### The "Magic Cycle" of Climate and Planetary Intelligence

*   **The Magic Cycle of Research:** Yossi Matias defines this as the self-reinforcing loop where asking a fundamental question leads to breakthrough research, publication, and real-world application, which in turn prompts the next scientific question. This cycle is now accelerating faster than ever due to AI acting as its own accelerator.
*   **Google Earth AI:** Google Research is consolidating its various geospatial, weather, and remote sensing models into a single agentic platform.
    *   **Global Flood Forecasting:** A system once deemed "impossible" by experts now operates in 150 countries, protecting 2 billion people by predicting river floods up to 7 days in advance. In Nigeria, governments use the API to distribute evacuation funds to villagers before floods hit.
    *   **Urban Flash Floods:** To address the lack of training data for city flash floods, researchers used Gemini to scan public reports, synthesizing a high-quality dataset of 2.6 million flash flood events (a technique called "groundsource") to train operational prediction models.
    *   **NeuralGCM:** Google's collaborative weather model with the University of Chicago has helped provide early weather warnings to 38 million farmers in India.

### Medicine and Healthcare AI Milestones

*   **Med-Gemma Open Platform:** Google’s open-weight medical model has surpassed 5 million downloads. 
    *   **Offline Medical Access:** In Kampala, Uganda, healthcare workers utilize Med-Gemma offline on mobile devices to diagnose life-threatening conditions like eclampsia in villages lacking internet connectivity, successfully reducing maternal mortality.
*   **Clinical Screening Advancements:** 
    *   **Diabetic Retinopathy:** Ten years after Google’s landmark JAMA paper proved AI could screen for diabetic retinopathy at an expert level, the technology is fully operational in Thailand and India, providing retinal scans and results to clinic patients in just 2 minutes.
    *   **Mammography Screening:** A recent Google study published in *Nature* with the UK NHS demonstrated that using AI as a "second reader" in mammograms identified 25% of missed cancer cases while giving 40% of their time back to radiologists.
    *   **Clinical Communication:** Google is piloting AMIE (Articulate Medical Intelligence Explorer) with Included Health to test conversational, diagnostic AI support in active clinics.

### "Gemini for Science" and Automated Discovery

*   **Gemini for Science Launch:** Google has consolidated its frontier scientific AI tools into a unified suite designed to act as a "polymath in your pocket." The suite includes:
    *   **AI Co-Scientist:** A multi-agent virtual lab helper that performs vast cross-disciplinary literature searches and generates novel hypotheses. In a pilot with Imperial College, Co-Scientist generated a bacterium hypothesis in just two days that had previously taken human researchers a decade to formulate.
    *   **Empirical Research Assistant (ERA):** An automated system that searches parameter spaces and tunes computational models for scorable problems, replacing weeks of manual coding. Its release in *Nature* was accompanied by eight distinct peer-reviewed papers in fields ranging from cosmology to economics.
    *   **AlphaFold & NotebookLM:** Integrated into the suite to provide protein structure predictions and tailored literature insights.

### Algorithmic Efficiency and Generative UI

*   **Speculative Decoding:** Google Research developed this algorithmic technique to accelerate LLM inference.
    *   **Performance Gains:** The algorithm doubles or triples inference speed (latency and throughput) on Gemini 1.5 without any loss in output quality. 
    *   **Industry Standard:** The technique has effectively doubled the global capacity of existing chips, allowing the industry to run models as if they had twice the hardware resources.
*   **Generative UI (Gen-UI):** Developed in collaboration with the Google Search team, Gen-UI uses AI to dynamically determine the most effective visual presentation format for search and chat content in real time based on user context.

### AI Education and Quantum Computing Milestones

*   **Reimagining Education:** Google is focusing on adapting AI to enhance and personalize learning through specialized initiatives:
    *   **LearnLM:** A model fine-tuned for educational pedagogy, enabling daily assessments for students in Ghana and saving teachers up to five hours of manual grading per week.
    *   **Learn Your Way:** An experimental program designed to turn static textbooks into personalized, multi-modal experiences (e.g., teaching physics concepts like gravity using soccer examples for a 10-year-old vs. tennis for a 16-year-old).
    *   **Future Skills:** Matias emphasizes that basic computer science remains essential because "it is not learning a language, it's learning how to think," while soft skills like collaboration will become primary differentiators.
*   **Quantum Computing Breakthroughs:** 
    *   **Nobel Recognition:** Google's Quantum AI Lab (founded in 2012) celebrated the physics Nobel Prize awarded to long-time lab scientists and collaborators Michel Devoret, John Martinis, and John Clarke for foundational work dating back to the 1980s.
    *   **The Willow Chip:** Google's latest quantum hardware achieved major milestones in quantum error correction and demonstrated "verifiable quantum advantage," solving a benchmark math problem 13,000 times faster than the world’s most powerful classical supercomputers.
