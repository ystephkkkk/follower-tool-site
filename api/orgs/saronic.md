## Saronic's 4 Co-Founders on Building a $9.25B Shipbuilder | Part III

- Channel: Sourcery with Molly O'Shea

### TL;DR
Saronic, an autonomous maritime company founded in 2022 by four co-founders, has raised approximately $2.6 billion at a $9.25 billion valuation by scaling autonomous surface vessel production to address America's severe shipbuilding gap with China.

### Macro Fleet Statistics & The Shipbuilding Deficit
* **China's Industrial Output:** **230 to 1 shipbuilding ratio** between China and the U.S., with China controlling 57% of total global shipbuilding capacity via heavily subsidized raw materials, labor, and free shipyard financing — building over 1,000 commercial ships in 2024 compared to just 5 large oceangoing commercial vessels in the U.S. (**Dino Mavrookas**)
* **U.S. Navy Fleet Decline:** The U.S. naval fleet currently sits at around **290 ships**, going in the wrong direction despite Congress setting a statutory minimum of **355 ships** in 2018 (which the Navy revised to **381** in 2024), while the 30-year shipbuilding plan carries a projected cost of **$1.2 trillion** if executable. (**Dino Mavrookas**)
* **Budget Allocations & Exquisite Cost Overruns:** Aircraft carriers cost **$13 billion to $19 billion** and take over 10 years to manufacture, while the Department of War's nearly **$1 trillion to $1.5 trillion budget request** allocated only **1% toward autonomy**, highlighting a massive institutional gap. (**Dino Mavrookas**)
* **The Military-Commercial Discrepancy:** The U.S. launches about **5 to 10 military warships** annually compared to China's **30** (a 6:1 or 7:1 ratio), though allies like South Korea and Japan increasingly rely on Chinese commercial modules and labor. (**Dino Mavrookas**)

### Company Scale, Partnerships & Manufacturing Infrastructure
* **Total Capital & Valuation:** Raised **~$2.6 billion** at a **$9.25 billion valuation** just four years after founding in 2022. (**Dino Mavrookas / Doug Lambert**)
* **Port Alpha Investment:** Investing **$3+ billion into Port Alpha** to revolutionize final outfitting, move traditional shipboard work into subassemblies, and scale production capacity. (**Dino Mavrookas / Doug Lambert**)
* **Palantir Integration:** Utilizes Palantir’s **Foundry program** as core backbone software for internal manufacturing execution systems, material resource planning (MRP), and logistics planning across the business. (**Doug Lambert / Vibhav Altekar**)
* **Path Robotics Collaboration:** Partnering with Path Robotics at the Franklin facility and Gulfcraft shipyard in Louisiana to automate **aluminum welding** capable of passing ABS, IMO, and Coast Guard certifications. (**Doug Lambert / Vibhav Altekar**)
* **Workforce & Supply Chain Scale:** Employs **1,600 people** under operations; previously bought up the vast majority of aluminum in the Louisiana area for Marauder production and is proactively establishing domestic steel pipelines for Port Alpha. (**Doug Lambert**)

### Product Evolution & Autonomous Maritime Technology
* **Product Lineage:** Started with early, lesser-known products including *Spy Glass* and *Cutless*, before scaling to *Corsair*, *Marauder* (a **180-foot ship** analogous to stepping stones toward 400-foot, 800-foot, or Panamanian-sized 1,200-person ships), and Port Alpha manufacturing systems. (**Doug Lambert**)
* **Extreme Operating Stress:** Commercial boats are typically designed for human G-force limits of **4 to 6 Gs**, whereas Saronic autonomous boats have recorded accelerations **north of 20 Gs**. (**Dino Mavrookas / Vibhav Altekar**)
* **Software-Hardware Co-Design:** Built around **Modular Open Systems** with easy-to-use software and hardware APIs, utilizing Nvidia GPU compute, redundant power distribution modules for remote power cycling, and deep simulation testing prior to physical sea trials. (**Vibhav Altekar**)
* **Communications & Resilience:** Implements a redundant **pace plan** (including Starlink and Starshield) to handle tumultuous sea states, signal degradation, bio-fouling, and contested RF environments. (**Vibhav Altekar**)

### Defense Contracting & Acquisition Reform
* **Rapid Initial Contracting:** Secured a **Classified/Cooperative Research and Development Agreement (CRADA)** within the first **90 days** of founding through direct fleet-level operator feedback rather than top-down bureaucratic channels. (**Rob Lehman**)
* **Bureaucratic Friction & Accountability:** Highlighting the need to replace rigid risk-averse guidebooks with AI-augmented tools for contracting officers and placing clear operational risk and authority back on program managers. (**Rob Lehman**)

## Dino Mavrookas: America Needs to Build Ships Again

- Channel: Sourcery with Molly O'Shea

- Published: 2026-09-08
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260908.html#video-7bDA_oKBGbk
- Speakers: Dino Mavrookas (Co-Founder & CEO, Saronic); Molly O'Shea (Host, Sourcery)

---

### TL;DR
Saronic CEO Dino Mavrookas explains how his defense tech company is tackling America's critical shipbuilding gap—where China currently outbuilds the U.S. 230 to 1—through private capital, massive commercial-defense integration, and autonomous maritime vessels.

### The Maritime and Shipbuilding Crisis
- **U.S. vs. China Fleet Ratios:**
  - **China's Production Output:** China outbuilds the U.S. 230 to 1 overall; in 2024, China built over 1,000 commercial ships while the U.S. built only 5.
  - **Military Shipbuilding Gap:** China produces roughly 30 military ships annually compared to the U.S. output of 5 to 10 (a 6:1 or 7:1 ratio).
  - **Subsidized Dominance:** China's shipbuilding industry is heavily subsidized across direct construction, raw materials, labor, and free shipyard financing, capturing global capacity and threatening key allies like South Korea and Japan.
- **The Shrinking U.S. Fleet:**
  - **Current Numbers:** The U.S. naval fleet sits at roughly 290 ships.
  - **Statutory Deficit:** In 2018, Congress set a statutory minimum of 355 ships; by 2024, the Navy revised its requirement upward to 381 manned ships (excluding necessary unmanned vessels).
  - **Unaffordable Legacy Costs:** Aircraft carriers take over 10 years to build, with costs escalating from $13 billion to $19 billion per ship. The Navy's 30-year shipbuilding plan is projected to cost $1.2 trillion.

### Saronic’s Scale, Valuation, and Funding
- **Financial Metrics:**
  - **Valuation & Capital Raised:** Saronic is valued at over $9 billion and has raised $2.6 billion in venture capital.
  - **Port Alpha Investment:** Recently announced a $3 billion investment/project for Port Alpha in Brownsville, Texas.
  - **Headcount Growth:** Scaled from inception in 2022 to 1,800 employees over four years, anchored by an all-hands facility hosting events with over 1,000 attendees.
- **Venture Capital Backing:**
  - **Early Partners:** Initial backing came from ABC (Joe Lonsdale) via their build program in 2022.
  - **Later Rounds:** Led by tier-one firms including Caffeinated Capital, Andreessen Horowitz, Al Gore's fund, and Kleiner Perkins.

### Defense Autonomy and Commercial Strategy
- **Investing Ahead of Demand:**
  - **Private Capital Deployment:** Saronic invests private capital ahead of government demand, building products for speed and scale rather than relying solely on cost-plus contracting.
  - **Incremental Scaling:** Proved success through rapid acquisition milestones, including a multi-hundred-million-dollar contract with the Navy for Corsair announced in December 2025.
  - **Expanding Product Lines:** Expanded from Corsair and Mirage to larger autonomous surface vessels like Marauder and Port Alpha.
- **Commercial-Defense Integration:**
  - **Supply Chain Resilience:** Rebuilding commercial shipbuilding is essential to revive the domestic workforce, supply base, and economies of scale.
  - **Dual-Use Applications:** Corsair serves port/harbor security with the Coast Guard; Marauder enables commercial logistics; Port Alpha brings autonomy to container ships, tankers, and roll-on/roll-off (RoRo) vessels critical for wartime force resupply.
- **Cross-Service and International Reach:**
  - **Multi-Service Focus:** While the Navy is the primary customer, Saronic builds for the Army (which operates more boats than the Navy), Marine Corps, Coast Guard, and Air Force (combat search and rescue).
  - **Allied Partnerships:** Extending technology to international allies facing similar ship-manufacturing deficits.

### Bureaucracy and Defense Acquisition Reform
- **Government Momentum:**
  - **Urgency at the Top:** Mavrookas notes that current government leadership is moving faster than at any point during his 11 years in the SEAL Teams (2004–2015, including 5 years on SEAL Team Six).
  - **Policy Drivers:** Highlighted policy shifts include a $1.5 trillion president's budget request, a maritime executive order, a maritime action plan, and the SHIPS Act in Congress.
  - **Budget Imbalance:** Out of the Department of War's trillion-dollar budget for 2026, only 1% is allocated toward autonomy.
- **The Rise of New Defense Primes:**
  - **Industry Consolidation:** The defense base shrunk from 55-60 primes in 1993 down to 5 major legacy primes.
  - **New Prime Projections:** Over the next 5 to 10 years, Mavrookas anticipates 5 to 10 new, highly impactful companies will scale as direct government contractors alongside legacy primes.
  - **Disruptive Partnerships:** Citing collaborations like Castellian (building containerized hypersonics), Saronic partnered directly to test a hypersonic missile launch off an autonomous Marauder ship *before* the Navy completes its first test off a manned vessel.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
- **Ten-Year Port Alpha Target:** Over the next 10 years, Port Alpha will create 10,000 jobs, invest billions of dollars, and generate a $160 billion economic impact in and around Brownsville, Texas — *Dino Mavrookas*
- **New Defense Primes Timeline:** Over the next 5 to 10 years, exactly 5 to 10 new, impactful companies will scale to become direct government contractors (primes) — *Dino Mavrookas*
- **Hypersonic Testing Milestone:** Saronic and Castellian will successfully launch a hypersonic missile off the back of an autonomous Marauder ship before the Navy completes its first test of a hypersonic missile off a manned vessel — *Dino Mavrookas*
- **Industry-Wide Autonomy Shift:** The entire maritime industry—spanning both defense and commercial sectors—will center entirely around autonomy — *Dino Mavrookas*

### Notable Quotes
> *"China outbuilds the US 230 to 1. And the naval fleet that we have today is actually shrinking. There's right around 290 ships in in our naval fleet. In 2018, Congress actually set a statutory minimum of 355 ships. That was 8 years ago. And we're going in the wrong direction."*
> — Dino Mavrookas

> *"We're actually going to be able to launch a hypersonic missile off of the back of Marauder, which is an autonomous ship, before the Navy completes their first test of a hypersonic missile off of a manned vessel."*
> — Dino Mavrookas

### TL;DR
Saronic, founded in 2022 and valued at $9.25B after raising $2.6B, designs and manufactures autonomous maritime vessels and ships at scale—including its 420,000 sq ft Austin factory capable of building 2,000 Corsair boats per year—to counter China’s 230:1 shipbuilding advantage.

### Company Scale, Funding, and Facilities
* **Overall Metrics:**
  * **Valuation & Funding:** $9.25B valuation, $2.6B raised in total funding (Dino Mavrookas)
  * **Headcount:** ~1,800 total employees across all locations; ~1,000 at the Austin HQ; 300 to 400 at the Louisiana shipyard (Dino Mavrookas)
  * **Global Footprint:** 7 locations in the US, 2 internationally (Dino Mavrookas)
* **Austin, Texas HQ Campus:**
  * **Size:** 420,000 square feet across 7 buildings (4 production/manufacturing/industrial spaces, 2 people/engineering/G&A spaces, 1 central collaboration and lunch hall) (Dino Mavrookas)
  * **Evolution:** Started in a ~5,000 sq ft garage less than 4 years ago with 20 people (Dino Mavrookas)
* **Franklin, Louisiana Shipyard:**
  * **Context:** Acquired a closing shipyard about a year ago when headcount was 30; now employs 300 to 400 people (Dino Mavrookas)
  * **Product:** Building Marauder, a 180-foot fully autonomous ship for defense and commercial use (Dino Mavrookas)
* **Port Alpha (Brownsville, Texas):**
  * **Investment & Announcement:** $3.2B investment announced today to build a next-gen shipyard (Dino Mavrookas)
  * **Footprint:** Starts on an 800-acre footprint with expansion capability up to over 4,000 acres (Dino Mavrookas)

### Hardware & Software Integration Strategy
* **Vertical Integration:**
  * **Core Philosophy:** Co-designing hardware, software, and physical manufacturing under one campus environment so engineers can immediately resolve production blockers on the floor (Dino Mavrookas)
  * **Manufacturing Philosophy:** Head of manufacturing came from SpaceX with 10 years experience, focusing on "building the system that builds the system" with a linear workflow that makes bottlenecks instantly visible (Dino Mavrookas)
* **Prototyping Speed:**
  * **Amazon Raft Origin:** The very first subscale test was built on an $800 plastic raft from Amazon combined with $30,000 worth of cameras, batteries, and electronics to test the software stack while hardware was built in parallel (Dino Mavrookas)
* **Autonomy & Control:**
  * **Software Stack:** Built to control fleets at scale (e.g., controlling 2,000 boats simultaneously); software translates across platforms so products aren't rebuilt from scratch (Dino Mavrookas)

### Product Line & Specifications
* **Corsair (Flagship Autonomous Boat):**
  * **Dimensions:** 24 feet long (Dino Mavrookas)
  * **Production Capacity:** 2,000 units per year on the main line; capacity can be doubled utilizing adjacent lines (Dino Mavrookas)
  * **Performance & Payload:** 1,000 nautical mile range with a 1,000 lb payload capacity (Dino Mavrookas)
  * **Testing:** Over 25,000 hours tested internally across 3 US sites, plus international testing in Australia and UK (Dino Mavrookas)
  * **Deployments:** $392 million Navy contract announced in December 2025; operational in the Straits of Hormuz, including an Apache pilot rescue and an offensive attack on an Iranian port taking out an Iranian submarine (Dino Mavrookas)
* **Mirage (Mid-Sized Autonomous Vessel):**
  * **Dimensions:** 52 feet long (2.5x longer than Corsair, but 15x larger volumetrically) (Dino Mavrookas)
  * **Performance & Payload:** 2,500+ nautical mile range with 3,500 lbs of payload capacity (Dino Mavrookas)
  * **Status:** First unit launched about a week and a half ago, actively undergoing sea trials (Dino Mavrookas)
* **Marauder (Large Autonomous Ship):**
  * **Dimensions:** 180 feet long (Dino Mavrookas)
  * **Application:** Fully autonomous ship built in Franklin, Louisiana for defense and commercial use (Dino Mavrookas)

### Defense Macroeconomics and Industry Context
* **U.S. Shipbuilding Crisis:**
  * **Capacity Disparity:** China outbuilds the U.S. 230 to 1; the U.S. naval fleet is shrinking and holds 0.1% of global shipbuilding capacity (Dino Mavrookas)
  * **Historical Shocks:** The U.S. shipbuilding industry was decimated by two watershed moments: the removal of commercial shipbuilding subsidies in 1981, followed by a 41% drop in the Navy budget after 1993 (Dino Mavrookas)
* **Combat Search & Rescue Evolution:**
  * **Historical Context:** Dino Mavrookas served in the Navy SEAL teams from 2004 to 2015; in 2005, a helicopter was shot down attempting to rescue SEALs trapped behind enemy lines (Dino Mavrookas)
  * **Autonomy Impact:** The Apache pilot rescue using Corsair marked the first combat search and rescue executed by an autonomous system, removing human personnel from direct harm's way (Dino Mavrookas)

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Port Alpha Buildout Timeline & Capacity:** Port Alpha will initially support 850-foot ships, expand to support 1,200-foot ships, bring online 150,000 gross tons of capacity within a few years (a 1.5x increase to current U.S. shipbuilding capacity of 100,000 gross tons), scale up to 2 million plus gross tons, and create over 10,000 jobs in the next 10 years — Dino Mavrookas
