## The $100 Heart Scan Brad Gerstner Says Could Save Your Life

### TL;DR
Heart disease kills 800,000 Americans annually, yet the $100 Coronary Artery Calcium (CAC) scan can detect plaque precursors early and prevent up to 90% of heart attacks. Brad Gerstner, Jason Chang, and Rob Goldberg are championing a movement to scale CAC scans to 40 million people a year—transforming it into the "mammogram for the heart."

### The Case for Mass CAC Screening & Prevention
* **The High Cost of Inaction:** Heart disease is the world's number one killer, claiming 800,000 American lives annually.
* **The Mammogram Comparison:** 
  * Breast cancer accounts for roughly 40,000 deaths per year, supported by 40 million annual mammograms (setting the playbook for preventative medicine).
  * Heart disease kills 800,000 Americans annually, yet only 1 million CAC scans are performed each year.
* **Scan Economics & Logistics:** Brad Gerstner and Jason Chang brought a mobile CT scanner to the All-In Summit to demonstrate that CAC scans cost $100, take under 5 minutes on the bed (15 minutes total), are non-invasive, and have high ROI for individual and national healthcare.
* **Daily Trailer Throughput:** In a 9-hour window at the All-In Summit, the mobile trailer completed 33 scans, revealing a wide distribution from zero to severe scores.

### Personal Stakes: The Death of Dave Goldberg
* **A Shocking Loss:** Rob Goldberg’s brother, Dave Goldberg (husband of Sheryl Sandberg and an inspiration for the All-In / Bestie group), died suddenly at age 47 from undiagnosed heart disease while on a friend's 50th birthday trip in Mexico.
* **The EKG False Sense of Security:** Dave had passed a running EKG just five months before dropping dead from ventricular arrhythmia. 
  * EKGs have a ~4% false positive/negative rate.
  * Dave had done so much cardio that he trained his heart to temporarily overcome the underlying heart disease until it ultimately stopped.
* **Autopsy Findings:** An autopsy—made possible by Sheryl Sandberg, Chamath Palihapitiya, and family—confirmed severe, undiagnosed heart disease. His death inspired Sheryl Sandberg’s book *Option B*.

### Flaws in Traditional Healthcare & The Cholesterol Myth
* **The Limits of Standard Labs:** Half of all people who suffer heart attacks have cholesterol levels falling within the normal "green zone" (80 to 120).
* **Why General Practitioners Miss It:** Relying solely on general blood work and EKGs leaves patients falsely confident. Traditional markers fail to show actual arterial plaque buildup.
* **Regulatory Capture & Stent Lobbying:** Brad Gerstner notes that 50 years ago, following Eisenhower’s heart attack, Silicon Valley and Washington favored the stent and cholesterol-tracking lobby over promoting early calcium CT scanning.
* **Aggressive Modern Targets:** Top preventative cardiologists at Stanford, Harvard, and Columbia recommend taking a CAC scan by age 30–35. If a non-zero score appears, patients should aggressively lower LDLs below 40 using statins and ezetimibes.

### Scaling the Mission: Corporate Programs & Policy Goals
* **The Buy-One, Give-One Model:** Brad Gerstner is partnering with major Silicon Valley companies (such as Salesforce/Mark Benioff) and the NFL (during the Super Bowl in San Francisco) on a "buy-one, give-one" program to fund free scans in underserved communities like East Palo Alto.
* **Washington DC Outreach:** 
  * Brad Gerstner has helped 40 to 50 members of the House and Senate get CAC scans, uncovering target-rich populations.
  * The team is actively working with Washington to transition CAC scans into standard care.
* **Federal Coverage Target:** Brad Gerstner anticipates that by the end of the year, diagnostic CAC scans will be approved and covered by Medicaid and Medicare, driving doctors to adopt it as standard clinical practice.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Standard of Care Timeline:** Brad Gerstner predicts that by the end of the year, CAC scans for diagnostic purposes will be approved and covered by Medicaid and Medicare, establishing it as the national standard of care.
* **Heart Attack Reduction:** Leading preventative cardiologists at Stanford told Brad Gerstner that if everyone adopts routine CAC screening, 80% to 90% of all heart attacks can be eliminated.
