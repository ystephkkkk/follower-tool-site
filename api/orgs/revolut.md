## AI in Banking: Risk and Reward | Bloomberg Tech: Europe 10/9/2026

- Channel: Bloomberg Tech
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ9gzzrbq5A

- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20261009.html#video-jZ9gzzrbq5A
- Speakers: Tom Mackenzie (Host, Bloomberg Tech); Jamie Dimon (CEO, JPMorgan); Nik Storonsky (CEO, Revolut)

---

- **TL;DR:** AI deployment in banking has accelerated dramatically—jumping 26% on the Evident index in the past year—while introducing severe systemic cybersecurity risks (such as AI "mythos" threats) and prompting firms like JPMorgan, Revolut, and Bending Spoons to radically reshape their workforces, products, and tech stacks.

### AI Adoption and Rankings in Banking
- **Surging Deployment Pace:** The latest Evident AI Index reveals that the average bank's score jumped 26% over the past year, nearly triple the pace recorded between 2023 and 2025.
- **Top Performers:** 
  - JPMorgan Chase leads the world as the most AI-advanced bank for the fifth consecutive year, ranking first or second across all four Evident pillars (talent, innovation, leadership, transparency).
  - Capital One and RBC maintain their positions in the top three alongside JPMorgan, benefiting from early adoption strategies (such as JPMorgan establishing its research lab in 2017 and setting an "AI-first" mandate in 2018).
- **Workflow Integration:** AI has shifted from general productivity tools into core banking workflows, including credit underwriting, fraud detection, customer verification (KYC), trading, and risk management.

### Systemic Risks and Industry Defense
- **Amplified Cyber Threats:** Jamie Dimon warned that AI creates unprecedented vulnerabilities, stating that systemic cyber risks from AI "mythos" threats are magnified tenfold compared to traditional cyber risks.
- **Cross-Industry Alliance:** To protect critical infrastructure against emerging AI threats, JPMorgan's Jamie Dimon chairs a new coalition—the Alliance for Critical Infrastructure—starting with 50 companies across six U.S. industries (tech, financial services, water, transport, and eventually expanding to healthcare, pharma, and agriculture).

### Workforce Impact: Layoffs vs. Job Creation
- **Contrasting Industry Approaches:** 
  - **Evident Insights Assessment:** Alexandra Mousavizadeh notes that top AI-investing banks are actually growing headcount, generating roughly four new roles for every one job displaced in routine areas like call centers and KYC document ingestion.
  - **HSBC Staff Reductions:** In contrast to broader sector growth, HSBC plans to cut approximately half of its management and specialist positions in its U.K. wealth management business by the end of the month as part of an AI-driven efficiency push for affluent clients.

### Challenger Banks and Tech Scale-Ups
- **Revolut's U.S. Expansion:** CEO Nikolay Storonsky outlined plans to operationalize a credit-first U.S. bank offering credit cards, loans, and reward points at par with Amex or JPMorgan Chase, targeting a primary U.S. listing while dominating global markets.
- **Agentic AI & Commerce:** Revolut is testing its AI assistant ("Air") in December (rolling out first in the U.K. and Europe) to autonomously browse the internet, access Amazon, and conduct secure shopping for customers within the bank's environment.
- **Bending Spoons' Operating Model:** CEO Luca Ferrari shared that the company—which has acquired roughly 50 businesses including AOL, WeTransfer, and Evernote—utilizes AI models to screen applicant performance, optimize monetization, and write between 90% and 100% of its software.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
- **Jamie Dimon:** AI will cure many diseases and improve infrastructure, but will simultaneously multiply systemic cyber risks tenfold through advanced AI agents and "mythos" threats.
- **Alexandra Mousavizadeh:** AI will likely become the biggest job generator in decades for enterprise banking over time, outweighing white-collar job displacement.
- **Nikolay Storonsky:** Revolut's new AI shopping assistant ("Air") will begin customer testing in December, launching first in the U.K. and subsequently across Europe.
- **Nikolay Storonsky:** Over the next 5 years, Agentic AI will drive a massive shift away from UI/UX-driven mobile apps and websites toward back-end logic, API compatibility, and high-level automation.

## Revolut CEO Nik Storonsky on Building a Fintech Giant | The David Rubenstein Show

- Channel: David Rubenstein
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SfIwS5OS9_I
- Published: 2026-04-23
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260423.html#video-SfIwS5OS9_I
- Speakers: Nik Storonsky (CEO, Revolut); David Rubenstein (Host, The David Rubenstein Show)

---

**TL;DR**
Founded by former trader Nik Storonsky, Revolut has grown from a niche currency exchange app into a $75 billion global "super-app" aiming to become the world’s first truly global digital bank with a target of 100 million daily active users.

### The Growth and Scale of the Revolut "Super-App"
* **Product Ecosystem:** Revolut functions as an all-in-one financial hub, offering retail banking, wealth management, stock and crypto trading, insurance, and lifestyle services like eSIMs and hotel bookings.
    * **Market Penetration:** The platform currently serves 75 million customers. In Europe, one in five adults uses Revolut; in Ireland, penetration is as high as 80%.
    * **Revenue Model:** While initially built on offering interbank exchange rates with no fees, the company earns revenue through merchant fees (2-3% per transaction) and a suite of premium banking and business services.
* **Global Expansion:** Currently operating in 40 countries, the company aims to reach 100 markets by 2030.
    * **US and Asia Strategy:** Storonsky is actively pursuing banking licenses in the United States, Mexico, and several Asian markets, including plans for China.
    * **US License Timeline:** While official targets are four months, Storonsky anticipates the process could take up to 12 months, noting that a new administration and existing licenses in the UK and Mexico may streamline the process.

### Origins and Professional Philosophy
* **Founding Context:** Storonsky, a former trader at Lehman Brothers and Credit Suisse, founded the company in 2013 after becoming frustrated by the high fees associated with international spending. 
    * **The Lehman Experience:** Storonsky was on the trading floor when Lehman Brothers collapsed in 2008, an experience that fueled his confidence in his ability to navigate volatile markets.
    * **Bootstrapping:** He launched the product in 2015 using approximately $500,000 of his own savings and did not spend money on marketing for the first five to seven years, relying entirely on word-of-mouth.
* **IPO Outlook:** Storonsky expects the company to be ready for an IPO in roughly two years. He views going public primarily as a way to increase institutional "trust," which is vital for a global bank.

### Quantum Light: A Scientific Approach to Venture Capital
* **VC Disruption:** Storonsky founded Quantum Light, a $250 million venture arm, out of frustration with the "crowd thinking" and lack of logic in traditional venture capital.
* **Algorithmic Investing:** The fund uses machine learning models and millions of data points to identify Series B and C startups, rather than relying on human judgment.
    * **Performance:** The model-driven approach reportedly produces 30-40% annual returns, placing it in the top 5% of global funds.
    * **Value Add:** Beyond capital, Storonsky provides startups with operational knowledge and access to Revolut's massive distribution network.

### Corporate Culture and Technological Future
* **Talent Density:** Storonsky prioritizes a "Dream Team" culture, preferring small groups of high-IQ, ambitious individuals over large teams of average performers.
    * **Hiring Criteria:** The company evaluates candidates based on three pillars: High IQ/logic, character (hunger and ambition), and specific technical skills.
* **The Role of AI:** Rather than fearing AI, Storonsky views Large Language Models (LLMs) as a new "interface" layer that allows customers to interact more seamlessly with the bank's complex backend logic.
* **The Decline of Legacy Banks:** Storonsky predicts significant consolidation in the banking sector over the next 10-20 years, with "digital champions" eventually displacing legacy institutions that rely on physical infrastructure.
