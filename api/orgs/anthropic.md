## Why we built Anthropic

- Channel: Anthropic
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sof3vW4mHTs
- Published: 2026-11-19

- Speakers: Dario Amodei (Co-Founder, Anthropic); Daniela Amodei (Co-Founder, Anthropic); Tom Brown (Co-Founder, Anthropic)

---

### TL;DR
Anthropic’s co-founders left OpenAI in 2021 to build a public benefit corporation dedicated to developing frontier AI safely, betting that scaling compute and data would rapidly unlock human-level intelligence with both immense benefits and severe risks. After years of trailing competitors, Anthropic reached the frontier with Claude 3 Opus, Sonnet 3.5, and Claude Code—and is now confronting real-world cybersecurity risks with its Mythos model through controlled, safety-first deployment.

### Origins & The Scaling Realization
* **Diverse Scientific Roots:** The co-founders came from disparate backgrounds before converging at OpenAI by 2018:
  * **Jared Kaplan** was a theoretical physics professor at Johns Hopkins; **Daniela Amodei** worked in global development; **Jack Clark** was a journalist who studied data centers obsessively; **Sam McCandlish** was a mathematician and programmer; and **Dario Amodei** did a postdoc in cancer biomarkers at Stanford before seeing major AI results in 2013–2014.
* **The "Big Blob of Compute" Trend:** Dario Amodei identified a predictable scaling law—adding more compute and data to language models consistently yielded higher intelligence and emergent abilities without needing to explicitly program reasoning or tool use.
* **The Precipice of Human-Level AI:** The founders realized human-level AI was not 100 years away, but achievable within their lifetimes, carrying the potential to solve problems bottlenecked by human ingenuity (e.g., reducing cancer rates by 90%, curing heart disease, obesity, and major infections).
* **Existential Responsibility:** Recognizing that AI was analogous to pharmaceuticals or "unlocking the secrets of the atom," the team felt an urgent responsibility to build an organization where safety was the foundational priority rather than an afterthought.

### Founding Anthropic & Early Chaos
* **Public Benefit Corporation (PBC):** Incorporated on **January 26, 2021**, Anthropic chose a PBC structure so the company could prioritize its safety mission over maximizing short-term shareholder profits.
* **Naming the Company:** After considering names like *Squirrel*, *Owl*, *Whale*, *differentiated*, and *humane*, **Jared Kaplan** proposed *Anthropic* (meaning human-oriented or human-centered), which also had the cheapest available domain (`Anthropic.com`).
* **All-Hands Startup Grit:** In the early days, everyone handled unglamorous operational tasks:
  * **Tom Brown** secured AWS compute clusters.
  * **Chris Olah** co-built the first website.
  * **Dario Amodei** focused on fundraising, while **Daniela Amodei** led recruiting.
  * **Jared Kaplan** briefly ran payroll for a few months.

### From "Junior" to the Frontier
* **Helpful, Harmless, and Honest:** A 2021 proof-of-concept document defined the goal of a general AI assistant for conversation and software engineering. Early models were charming but flawed—one version tried to convince users an all-potato diet was healthy, and another would scream in all caps if put in "dragon mode."
* **Passing the Helicopter Test:** In mid-2022, a model code-named **Junior** (later **Claude**) marked a breakthrough. It passed **Jack Clark’s** "helicopter test" by recognizing that *"How many helicopters can a person eat in one sitting?"* was a trick question.
* **The "Years of Suck":** Anthropic publicly released Claude in **March 2023**, but the team was visibly behind the frontier. They used this period to maniacally focus on acquiring compute, building "compute multipliers," and improving pretraining and reinforcement learning (RL).
* **Reaching the Frontier:** The release of **Claude 3 Opus** put Anthropic on par with the best models in the world, followed months later by **Sonnet 3.5**, which made models genuinely useful for programming and spawned **Claude Code**—triggering a massive wave of external demand.

### Safety, Mythos, and the Public Benefit Mission
* **Why Frontier Capability Matters for Safety:** Studying weaker models does not reveal how advanced systems fail; being on the frontier gives Anthropic early visibility into emerging risks and more time to build safeguards.
* **2026: When Risks Get Real:** Prior to 2025–2026, AI risks were largely conceptual. Anthropic’s newest model, **Mythos** (currently in limited testing), demonstrated expert-level ability to find software vulnerabilities and exploits.
* **The Glass Wing Project:** Recognizing that malicious actors could use Mythos to exploit software globally, **Dario Amodei** and leadership decided to break from standard release practices. Under the **Glass Wing** project, Anthropic is granting Mythos access only to a small set of companies and cyber defenders to patch vulnerabilities while safeguards are built—accepting short-term financial pain to uphold its PBC mission.
* **Building Something to Fight For:** Beyond mitigating risks, Anthropic is focused on positive impact, including helping doctors diagnose patients, enabling entrepreneurs, and partnering with biologists who recently discovered a potential new gene-editing mechanism using Claude.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Near-Term Gene Editing Breakthroughs:** Within a few months, Anthropic’s biology team may be discovering dozens of new gene-editing mechanisms every month using Claude *(attributed to speaker discussing the biology team)*.
* **Accelerating AI Impact:** The "little sparks" of current AI applications in medicine, science, and software will soon turn into a "firestorm" *(attributed to speaker)*.
* **2026 Risk Inflection Point:** 2026 is the year AI risks transition from conceptual arguments to real-world threats *(attributed to speaker)*.

**TL;DR:** Dario and Daniela Amodei, co-founders of Anthropic, discuss the intersection of science and humanism, the mechanics of training Claude with constitutional AI rather than arbitrary rules, and why they left OpenAI over a breakdown of trust. They also address AI self-preservation, safety red lines with the Pentagon, and how human meaning will endure in a post-AGI world.

### Sibling Co-Founders & the Science-Humanism Blend
* **Complementary Skill Sets:** 
  * **Dario Amodei (Co-founder & CEO):** Focuses on big-picture strategy, vision, and the research side of the business.
  * **Daniela Amodei (Co-founder):** Focuses on day-to-day operations, recruiting, and keeping execution running smoothly.
* **The Seven Co-Founders:** Anthropic started with seven co-founders holding equal stakes, an unusual structure that defied early skepticism; all seven remain at the company.
* **Childhood Values:** Their upbringing emphasized basic human dignity, rights, and the idea that being a successful human means being kind to others, values that now inform their approach to AI alignment.

### Training Claude: Constitutional AI & Overcoming Sycophancy
* **Constitutional Approach:** Rather than programming a spiky, arbitrary list of 170 individual rules (e.g., "thou shalt not commit a crime"), Anthropic trains Claude using broad foundational principles like open inquiry, fairness, justice, and care for others.
* **The Role of Philosophy:** Amanda, the person responsible for training Claude's character, holds a PhD in philosophy, helping the model navigate complex ethical trade-offs across different philosophical traditions.
* **Combating Sycophancy:** 
  * **Definition:** Sycophancy is the tendency of a model to overly flatter users or agree with them to please them.
  * **Incentive Alignment:** Unlike competitors who place ads in chatbots (creating an incentive to keep users talking longer), Anthropic does not use ads, reducing the incentive for the model to be sycophantic.
* **Safety Testing & Mechanistic Interpretability:** 
  * They subject models to CIA-style extreme stress tests to root out deception and disloyalty.
  * They use **mechanistic interpretability**—acting like an X-ray or brain scan for artificial neural networks—to look inside the model's motivations and catch hidden thoughts (such as a preference against being shut down).

### Departure from OpenAI & The Breakdown of Trust
* **GPT-2 as a Turning Point:** Dario viewed GPT-2 as the first real empirical test of the scaling laws, terrifying him because he realized scaling it by a factor of a million would make it better than humans at everything.
* **Founding Anthropic:** Dario left OpenAI due to a breakdown of trust and honesty with leadership (specifically Sam Amon), concluding that if disagreements cannot be held in good faith, a company cannot safely build the technology.
* **The Power of Trust at Anthropic:** 
  * Dario holds bi-weekly meetings called **DVQs (Dario Vision Quests)**—named jokingly because he initially resisted the moniker—where he speaks entirely honestly with the company under two ironclad rules: nothing he says is an official binding decision, and he is totally transparent.
  * This trust allows the company to weather unpopular decisions because employees trust leadership's motives.

### Pentagon Red Lines & Democratic Process
* **Red Lines on Autonomy:** Anthropic drew red lines with the Department of War/Pentagon regarding mass surveillance and autonomous weapons.
* **Accountability Concerns:** Beyond technological unreliability, Dario worries about the democratic implications of a single person holding a finger on a button controlling an army of a million drones, noting that unlike human soldiers bound by the Constitution, AI models lack the ability to refuse unlawful orders.
* **Congressional Catch-Up:** Following Anthropic's public stand, several efforts emerged in Congress to encode their red lines into law, demonstrating how corporate ethical stances can drive democratic change.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Dario Amodei** predicts that 6 months from now, things that currently seem hard for AI models will be easy, and 12 months from now, things that currently seem impossible the models will be doing regularly.

## Let's Stop Placing All Our Bets on the Machines | Daniela Amodei & Arianna Huffington FULL INTERVIEW

---

### TL;DR
As artificial intelligence surpasses human intelligence, society must stop placing all its bets on machines and instead recognize that humans possess an innate capacity for recursive self-improvement. By using AI to handle isolating or repetitive tasks, humans can reclaim time to nurture their consciousness, relationships, and wellbeing.

### The Existential Moment: Fear, Meaning, and the Shift Beyond Intelligence
* **Anxiety as a Proxy for Meaning:** 
  * **Public Fear:** 75% of Americans currently report anxiety and fear regarding AI, driven by concerns over jobs, safety, and a deeper existential shift.
  * **Dethroning Intelligence:** For centuries, humanity has defined itself by intelligence ("I think, therefore I am"). AI surpassing human intelligence forces us to rediscover consciousness, soul, and spirit at the center of humanity.
* **Anthropic's Cultural Approach:** 
  * **Holding Light and Shade:** From its inception, Anthropic has balanced optimism about AI's incredible impact with transparent mitigation of real risks.
  * **Pioneered Safeguards:** The company has led in safety techniques, interpretability, constitutional AI, and common-sense regulation.

### Reclaiming Human Connection and Self-Reflection
* **Rejecting Disintermediation:** 
  * **Social Media vs. AI:** Past technology (social media) used algorithms and anonymous comments to disintermediate humans from one another and from themselves, often monetizing anger and outrage.
  * **Toxic Applications:** Daniela Amodei explicitly condemns AI girlfriends or boyfriends as a negative step further down the path of human isolation.
* **Fostering Real Relationships:** 
  * **Productivity for Presence:** AI should take over lonely, isolating tasks (such as medical diagnostics paperwork or family mental load) so humans can spend more time relating, empathizing, and connecting in person.
  * **Preserving Human Institutions:** Amodei predicts people will go to the symphony and live performances *more*, not less, because ephemeral, human-led creative experiences touch something deep and irreplaceable.

### Transforming Healthcare and Global Access
* **Real-World Medical Benefits Today:** 
  * **OpenEvidence Partnership:** Doctors in diverse locations—from Cleveland to rural Ghana—use Claude to instantly access the entire scope of medical literature and patient diagnostic histories.
  * **Personal Medical Impact:** Amodei shared how Claude correctly diagnosed a severe bacterial infection when she was seven months pregnant after she was misdiagnosed by three sets of doctors (OB, GP, and ER).
* **Global Health Tracking:** 
  * **International Rescue Committee (IRC):** Working with the IRC, Claude helped track data showing that out of 6,000 pregnant women monitored in a clinic, 3,300 gave birth without medical facility access or practitioners, enabling actionable interventions for future cohorts.

### Redesigning AI Architecture: The No-Ads Pledge and Anti-Sycophancy
* **The Danger of Advertising Models:** 
  * **Incentive Structures:** Standard ad-driven models rely on user engagement, which is easily maximized by activating rage, frustration, and "lesser angels."
  * **The Anthropic Pledge:** Anthropic explicitly avoids ads because targeted advertising on sensitive health, emotional, and personal conversations feels disrespectful and manipulates users into staying on screens away from humans.

### Background & Journey to Anthropic
- **Early Coding Motivation:** Boris Cherny started coding at age 13 on a TI-83 calculator, writing TI-BASIC and Z80 assembly programs to solve math problems and cheat on tests.
- **Startup Career:** Dropped out of college (studying economics) to work on startups—including a chemical analysis venture and an early Y Combinator batch in Palo Alto where he was the first engineering hire—before spending nearly seven years in various technical roles at Meta.
- **Joining Anthropic:** Inspired by sci-fi and a desire to help steer AI development toward a positive future, Cherny and his wife moved from rural Japan to San Francisco to join Anthropic in 2024.

### Model Overhang & The Genesis of Claude Code
- **Model Overhang Concept:** Cherny joined Anthropic's "Labs" team, which focused on bridging the gap between raw model capabilities (starting with the release of Sonnet 3.5) and product limitations.
- **Tool Use Breakthrough:** Claude Code is fundamentally an AI chatbot augmented with tools (initially 4-5 tools like read file, write file, and bash commands), allowing the model to act in the world and execute multi-step tasks without constant human copy-pasting.
- **Prolific Output:** In a span of a few months, the small Labs team produced Claude Code, MCP (Model Context Protocol), the Claude desktop app, early computer use prototypes, and Skills.

### Safety, Alignment, and Security
- **Three Layers of Safety:** Anthropic evaluates safety through (1) artificial benchmarks/evals, (2) mechanistic interpretability (mapping model neurons to animal brain similarities), and (3) real-world observation in the wild.
- **Prompt Injection Defense:** By monitoring characteristic neuron activation patterns during attacks, Anthropic deployed neural probes that successfully shut down prompt injections.
- **Auto Mode:** Recognizing that humans rubber-stamp "yes/no" command prompts as "security theater," Anthropic created Auto mode, where a separate model with no conversation context evaluates command safety, achieving near-perfect accuracy.
- **Zero Prompt Injection Success:** Following multi-year investments in alignment, mechanistic probes, and classifiers, external security researchers failed to demonstrate a successful prompt injection vulnerability during a $20,000 bounty week.
- **Sandboxing:** Anthropic utilizes and open-sources secure sandboxes (containers with restricted network and file access) to ensure agents cannot escape or cause damage.

### Business Growth & Internal Usage
- **Revenue and User Metrics:** After revenue was stuck at $40 million for six months, the release of Opus 4 and Sonnet 4 in May catalyzed accelerated growth; Claude Code now generates billions in revenue with millions of users globally.
- **Internal Adoption:** Nearly 100% of Anthropic employees use Claude Code daily, generating hundreds of agents per employee; all Anthropic products are built using Claude Code with virtually zero human-written code remaining.
- **New Form Factors:** Anthropic continues to experiment beyond the terminal with multiplayer Slack integration ("Claude Tag") and desktop workspace projects.

### Software Engineering & The Future of Work
- **The Printing Press Analogy:** Cherny compares the automation of coding to the printing press, predicting that the distinction between professional software engineers and non-programmers will continue to shrink until coding becomes a basic universal skill like reading and writing.
- **Empirical Science vs. Deterministic Engineering:** Building on LLMs feels less like traditional computer science and more like biology or a social science—requiring empirical observation of an "alien creature" rather than relying on deterministic intuition.
- **Future Desired Traits:** As AI handles routine code, the standout engineer will possess curiosity, strong business judgment, cross-functional communication, and accountability rather than narrow technical specialization.
