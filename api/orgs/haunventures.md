### The Three Structural Shifts of the "New Economy"
Katie Haun, Founder & CEO of Haun Ventures, outlines three key areas of investment for her firm’s new $1 billion fund:

*   **New Financial Infrastructure:** Developing 24/7, global "plumbing" to support the next era of innovation.
*   **New Assets and Markets:** Driving the mainstream adoption of stablecoins, the tokenization of traditional securities (stocks, bonds), and the expansion of prediction and perpetual futures markets.
*   **Agentic Finance:** Creating financial services, rails, and products designed specifically for computer programs and autonomous AI agents rather than human users.

---

### Why AI Agents Require Cryptographic Financial Rails
As AI agents increasingly manage tasks like booking travel or managing subscriptions, traditional payment systems are proving insufficient. 

*   **Limitations of Legacy Systems:** Traditional credit cards and legacy banking rails are incompatible with agent-to-agent transactions. Agents do not observe banker's hours, weekends, or wire cut-off times.
*   **The Need for New Rails:** AI agents require instant settlement, transaction finality, global reach, minimal chargebacks, and the ability to execute low-cost micropayments (e.g., when querying LLMs).
*   **The Intersection of Web3 and AI:** 
    *   **Provenance:** In an era of AI-generated content, verifying the "who, what, and when" is critical. Blockchains offer highly secure, distributed, and immutable ledgers to prove data origin.
    *   **Privacy and Cryptography:** AI developers are increasingly turning to Web3 cryptographic innovations, such as Zero-Knowledge (ZK) proofs and fully homomorphic encryption, to solve enterprise data privacy challenges.

### Stablecoins and Market Maturation
The crypto market has transitioned from speculative retail use cases to highly practical B2B and enterprise utility.

*   **Product-Market Fit:** Stablecoins have achieved undeniable mainstream utility.
    *   **In the U.S.:** Primarily utilized by businesses and enterprises to eliminate friction in global business transactions.
    *   **Internationally:** Dominantly used by retail consumers looking to hedge against high-inflation economies.
*   **Major M&A Milestones:** Traditional financial giants are aggressively entering the ecosystem. Haun points to major exits within their portfolio:
    *   **B and K** (acquired by Visa for nearly $2 billion in cash).
    *   **Bridge** (acquired by Stripe for just over $1 billion).

### Regulatory Realities and Advice for Founders
Drawing from her decade-long background as a federal prosecutor handling national security and organized crime at the Department of Justice (DOJ), Haun offers strategic guidance on navigating policy:

*   **The Washington Vibe Shift:** While the political landscape has shifted to become more favorable to digital assets, Haun warns that long-term regulatory success requires persistent bipartisan effort. Major legislative goals, like passing the Clarity Act, still demand support from both sides of the aisle.
*   **Real-Time Global Seizures:** Countering concerns about illicit finance, Haun notes that digital assets allow law enforcement to detect and seize funds globally in real-time—a feat virtually impossible with international bank wires that require slow, multi-jurisdictional treaty processes.
*   **Advice for AI Founders:**
    *   **Avoid Myopic Policy Planning:** Founders often focus exclusively on the executive branch or Congress (Article I and II), entirely overlooking the judicial branch (Article III). Courts rely on scholarly and law review articles to build legal precedents for nascent technologies like AI.
    *   **Think Globally and Locally:** Do not limit regulatory strategies to Washington, D.C. or Brussels; state-level governments and diverse global jurisdictions are taking vastly different regulatory approaches.

### Personal Portfolios vs. Fund Mandates
Haun maintains a strict boundary between the fund’s financial focus and her personal angel investments in frontier tech.

*   **Fund Mandate:** Strictly dedicated to the future of finance, new economic plumbing, and digital assets.
*   **Personal Investments:** Haun writes personal checks for highly competitive, non-mandate frontier startups where she can act as a strategic bridge between tech and government:
    *   **AI/Compute:** Anthropic, OpenAI, Cerebras, Groq, Physical Intelligence, and Cognition.
    *   **Bio/Longevity:** **Neko Health** (founded by Spotify’s Daniel Ek, offering full-body scans utilizing AI for preventative medicine) and **Edison** (using AI agents to accelerate drug discovery from years to hours).
