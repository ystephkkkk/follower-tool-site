### TL;DR
Hyperliquid is a high-performance, decentralized layer-1 blockchain protocol designed to house 24/7 global financial markets—handling perpetual futures, real-world assets, and derivatives through an on-chain order book and clearing mechanism. Jeff Yan, co-founder and CEO of Hyperliquid Labs, explains how the platform allows permissionless deployers to launch custom trading instruments using native HYPE token stakes and decentralized price oracles.

---

### Architecture of the Hyperliquid Protocol
- **Layer-1 Consensus:** Operates as a neutral blockchain governed by a permissionless set of validators (currently 30+ total, with the top 27 by stake active at any moment). The network maintains a single, globally consistent state across independent nodes.
- **HyperCore Submodule:** The underlying execution environment that autonomously carries out core trading functions—such as matching engines, clearing, and rules-based liquidations—directly on chain.
- **Hardware Footprint:** Unlike compute-heavy AI infrastructure, running a network node requires standard consumer-grade hardware (specifically **128 GB of RAM**), avoiding exclusive data center monopolies.

### The Role of Deployers and HIP-3 Mechanics
- **Staking Requirement:** To unlock deployment functionalities on the network, entities (such as **Trade XYZ**) must stake **500,000 HYPE tokens**.
- **Scarcity and Allocation:** Because computational resources for order books and clearing systems are limited, deployment slots are allocated via a **Dutch auction** that resets approximately once a day (**every 31-hour interval**).
- **Custom Specifications:** Deployers define the parameters of perpetual contracts, including leverage limits, clearing rules, and price oracles. Users trust the specific deployer's oracle implementation to accurately track off-chain underlying assets (such as Bitcoin, Nvidia, or crude oil).
- **Protocol Protections:** At the network level, hard-coded safety constraints prevent abrupt price manipulation—restricting relative oracle price updates between consecutive blocks to a maximum of **1%**.

### Ecosystem and Vision
- **Aspirational Goal:** CEO Jeff Yan states that the ultimate objective is housing all of finance—or at least **90%** of global financial markets—on-chain as a collection of modular tools (primitives).
- **Institutional Integration:** Rather than seeking to displace legacy financial institutions, the platform envisions traditional banks and asset managers eventually adopting the technology or acting as deployers to service their clients.
- **Regulatory Landscape:** While the platform currently restricts US users from trading derivatives per its terms of service, recent interest from regulators like the CFTC and statements from President Trump highlight a growing proactive approach toward compliant on-chain financial rails.
