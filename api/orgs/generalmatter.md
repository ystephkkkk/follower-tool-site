## From SpaceX to Founders Fund to Solving America's Nuclear Fuel Problem

- Channel: Invest Like The Best
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m7H42MCzBCQ
- Published: 2026-04-14

- Speakers: Patrick O'Shaughnessy (Host, Invest Like The Best); Scott Nolan (Founder and CEO, General Matter)

---

**TL;DR:** Scott Nolan explores the strategic shift from investing in digital "bits" to building physical "atoms," highlighting how his new venture, General Matter, aims to solve the critical bottleneck in American nuclear energy by restoring domestic uranium enrichment.

### Contrarian Investing: Avoiding Trends and Stagnant Industries
*   **The Thiel Influence:** Scott Nolan describes a decade at Founders Fund learning to avoid "the herd" and "the trend." He argues that when a theme becomes a popular investment trend, competition inevitably drives profits down to an economic equilibrium.
*   **Identifying "Cost-Plus" Stagnation:** Nolan focuses on industries like aerospace, defense, and infrastructure that have stagnated due to "cost-plus" government contracts.
    *   **Incentive Misalignment:** These industries lack incentives to lower costs, leading to dozens of layers of subcontractors and "calcified" architectures.
    *   **Disruption Targets:** Companies like SpaceX and Anduril succeed by breaking these oligopolies through vertical integration and first-principles engineering.
*   **The "Atoms vs. Bits" Gap:** A core thesis at Founders Fund was that progress in the physical world (atoms) has lagged significantly behind progress in the digital world (bits) since the 1970s.

### The Nuclear Fuel "Cliffs" and Domestic Security
*   **The Enrichment Bottleneck:** While the U.S. has capabilities in mining and fuel pellet fabrication, it has outsourced nearly all commercial uranium enrichment. Currently, Russia provides approximately 25% of U.S. enriched uranium.
*   **Three Imminent Supply Cliffs:**
    *   **HALEU (High-Assay Low-Enriched Uranium):** Advanced reactors (SMRs) require fuel enriched to nearly 20%. There is currently no domestic commercial supply, creating a "showstopper" for the next generation of nuclear tech.
    *   **The 2028 Russia Ban:** An act of Congress will ban Russian uranium imports by 2028, forcing utilities to find new sources or deplete existing inventories.
    *   **Naval Stockpiles:** The U.S. stockpile for nuclear-powered naval propulsion is finite and will eventually require replenishment.
*   **General Matter’s Mission:** The company is positioning itself to be a private-sector solution to these cliffs, starting with HALEU for advanced reactors before expanding into the broader $2.5 billion Low-Enriched Uranium (LEU) market.

### Energy Abundance as a Proxy for Prosperity
*   **The Prosperity Correlation:** Nolan cites a near-perfect correlation (R-squared over 0.8) between a country's GDP per capita and its energy consumption per capita.
*   **The Flat Grid Problem:** U.S. energy grid growth has remained essentially flat since the 1990s. In contrast, China’s energy production is projected to be triple that of the U.S. this year.
*   **The AI/Data Center Surge:** AI training and inference demand is so high that data centers could theoretically consume the entire current U.S. grid capacity by 2030 if left unaddressed.
*   **BYOE (Bring Your Own Energy):** To bypass grid constraints and permitting delays, large tech "hyperscalers" are increasingly looking to build "behind-the-meter" power plants, effectively creating energy islands to power compute hubs.

### Engineering-First Principles at General Matter
*   **Vertical Integration:** Taking a page from the SpaceX "Elon Algorithm," General Matter avoids outsourcing.
    *   **Collocation:** Nolan believes first-of-a-kind manufacturing must be in the same building as engineering to allow for rapid iteration and "trading off" interface requirements between teams.
    *   **In-house Construction:** The company aims to act as its own EPC (Engineer, Procure, Construct) firm rather than hiring general contractors, ensuring tighter control over multi-billion-dollar infrastructure schedules.
*   **Talent Density:** Though a nuclear company, only a single-digit percentage of the staff are nuclear engineers. The core team consists of mechanical, electrical, and software engineers sourced from the Southern California aerospace hub.
*   **North Star Metric:** The company tracks **Dollars per Separative Work Unit (SWU)**. This measures the cost of the "entropy reduction" required to refine uranium ore into usable fuel.
