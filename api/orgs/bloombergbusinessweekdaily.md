## Earnings Special: Apple's Forecast Disappoints, Despite Topping Estimates | Bloomberg Tech

### TL;DR
Apple reported a Q4 earnings beat with $114.48 billion in revenue and $2.10 EPS, driven by strong iPhone sales (particularly the iPhone 18 Pro), though Greater China revenue missed expectations at $17.54 billion.

### Q4 Financial Performance & Metrics
- **Top-Line Beat:** Fourth-quarter revenue reached $114.48 billion, topping the Street estimate of $113.47 billion (and noted elsewhere as $114.5 billion).
- **Bottom-Line Beat:** Fourth-quarter earnings per share (EPS) came in at $2.10, beating expectations by $0.10.
- **Segment Breakdown:**
  - **iPhone Revenue:** Beat estimates at $57.58 billion (compared to the $56.49 billion estimate).
  - **Services Revenue:** Met expectations nearly exactly at $31.57 billion (estimate was $31.58 billion).
  - **Products Revenue:** Came in above estimates at $82.9 billion.
  - **Other Segments:** Wearables and iPad revenue both missed expectations; Mac revenue and Americas revenue came in just above estimates.
  - **Operating Expenses:** Total operating expenses landed in line with estimates at $19.19 billion.
- **Greater China Struggles:** Greater China revenue missed estimates again, coming in at $17.54 billion versus the expected $18.75 billion.

### iPhone & Hardware Product Dynamics
- **Driver of the Beat:** The earnings beat was led by sales of the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max, alongside continued demand for the iPhone 17 Pro/Pro Max and early-quarter carrier deals.
- **Product Pipeline Context:** 
  - The report arrived despite a minor year-over-year upgrade cycle for the iPhone 18 Pro, the absence of a regular iPhone 18 or iPhone Air 2 at launch, and major fall products (Touch MacBook Pro, Home View, HomePod Mini, Apple TV, OLED iPad) delayed until the holiday quarter.
- **iPhone Duo Supply & Demand:** 
  - Early reception and demand for the iPhone Duo have been exceptionally strong, bolstered by Apple's lease plan.
  - Unit projections for the year sit at approximately 15 million units.
  - Supply shortages are severe; pre-orders for many buyers do not launch until late January, and physical retail units are nearly impossible for human consumers to acquire due to AI bot buying.
  - Hardware defects: Early-batch screen cracking issues on the iPhone Duo have seen a dramatic slowdown, indicating an isolated early manufacturing defect rather than a systemic color or regional flaw.

### Strategic Growth Drivers & Geographic Markets
- **China vs. India:** 
  - China remains Apple's most important near-term unit growth driver over the next 3 years, despite recent tough comparisons following previous 22%–28% growth quarters.
  - India is an increasingly important aspirational market with expanding middle-class affluence and newly opened retail stores, though its overall revenue contribution remains small and per capita income lags behind China.
- **Services & AI Search Headwinds:** 
  - Services revenue growth has decelerated to around the 10% mark over the past two quarters.
  - A significant portion of services revenue relies on Google search revenue-sharing within Safari; as users migrate toward AI chat apps (ChatGPT, Claude, Muse, Gemini), Google search volume—and subsequently Apple's revenue share—faces downward pressure.

### Leadership & Corporate Strategy Under John Turnus
- **Continuity Over Overhaul:** Under new CEO John Turnus (a product executive), Apple's core strategy remains steady as current initiatives were multi-year developments under Tim Cook.
- **Cost Discipline:** Turnus is pushing the company to run faster and leaner, marked by both under-the-radar and visible layoffs alongside broader cost-cutting measures.
- **Revenue Smoothing:** The launch timing of devices like the iPhone Duo aligns with a broader goal under Turnus to create more consistent revenue distribution across the entire year rather than overloading the back-end holiday quarter.

### AI Strategy & Competitive Position
- **Market Valuation:** Apple entered the earnings print as the best-performing "Magnificent Seven" stock, up roughly 25% year-to-date and holding an enviable free cash flow position compared to heavy-spending AI hyperscalers.
- **The AI Assistant Gap:** Despite solid financial performance, critics point out a lack of evidence that Apple has built a native, competitive conversational AI agent on par with standalone consumer tools like ChatGPT, Claude, or Muse.
- **Siri AI Evaluation:** Siri AI functions effectively as an execution layer for routine phone tasks (utilizing the Gemini model for 90% of basic use cases), fulfilling the original 15-year-old vision of Siri, but it falls short of acting as a true, human-like general-purpose real-world digital assistant.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
- **China Growth:** China will remain Apple's most important near-term growth driver over the next 3 years — Anurag Rana.
- **iPhone Duo Unit Sales:** Apple will sell about 15 million units of the iPhone Duo this year — Anurag Rana.
- **Availability Timeline:** Consumers will be able to easily walk into a store and buy an iPhone Duo without severe inventory constraints by February or March — Mark Gurman.
- **Political Impact:** The midterm election outcome will have zero impact on Apple's business operations or political navigation — Mark Gurman.
