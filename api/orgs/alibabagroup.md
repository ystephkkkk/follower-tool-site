### TL;DR
Professor Zeng Ming argues that the AI era represents a paradigm shift surpassing the Industrial Revolution, predicting that traditional hierarchical companies will die out and be replaced by task-centric, partnership-driven AI-native organizations. Furthermore, he warns that today's leading infrastructure model providers and application companies will likely not survive as the dominant winners of the upcoming Agent and native-application stages.

### The Evolution of Technology Eras: From Infrastructure to Native Applications
- **Three-Stage General-Purpose Technology Framework:** Professor Zeng Ming identifies a consistent historical pattern for disruptive technologies (moving from PC internet to mobile internet, and now AI):
  - **Stage 1 (Infrastructure & Standardization):** Technology becomes widely accepted and establishes standard units of measurement (e.g., the definition of "tokens" as a standard unit of intelligence cost, similar to kilowatt-hours).
  - **Stage 2 (Transitional Application Explosion):** A massive explosion of applications and experiments occurs, giving direction to the technology's next evolution (e.g., the current "Agent" stage, comparable to the early web/browser era of 1992–1993).
  - **Stage 3 (Native Application Era):** True mainstream applications solve core societal needs, marking the arrival of epoch-defining platforms (e.g., Douyin and Pinduoduo in mobile internet).
- **Current AI Timeline (Attributed to Professor Zeng Ming):** 
  - **2026 Completion of Stage 1:** The formal establishment of "tokens" as a standard economic unit marks the maturation of AI as public infrastructure.
  - **The "Browser" Moment is Still Pending:** Just as it took years from the 1992 web browser to Yahoo and Google, the AI era is currently at the pre-Yahoo stage of entropy increase, awaiting a unified standard for Agent discovery and user access.

### The Fate of Industry Giants and Model Providers
- **The "Black Hole Effect" vs. Oligopoly (Attributed to Professor Zeng Ming):** 
  - Foundational model providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Kimi, DeepSeek) are effectively **AI cloud companies** acting as public infrastructure.
  - The model market will consolidate into an oligopoly under heavy government regulation, driven by an "intelligence compounding" feedback loop where models train models.
  - **Elimination Threshold:** In China's current model elimination tournament, any player failing to field a 3T-parameter model by year-end risks being left behind.
- **Why Historical Giants Rarely Survive:** Historical precedent (e.g., Yahoo, AOL, BlackBerry) shows that companies dominating Stage 1 infrastructure almost never survive as the dominant winners of Stage 3 native applications. 
  - **OpenAI and Anthropic's Long-Term Prospect:** While currently valued at trillion-dollar equivalents adjusted for inflation, they are unlikely to remain the ultimate winners of the native application stage.
  - **The Incumbent Trap:** Existing tech giants (Tencent, Alibaba, ByteDance, Google, Microsoft) face extreme difficulty transitioning their massive hierarchical organizations into the next era, much like oil companies failing to evolve into car manufacturers.

### The Rise of AI-Native Organizations and the Death of the "Company"
- **Obsolescence of Traditional Management (Attributed to Professor Zeng Ming):** 
  - The traditional corporate structure born of the Industrial Revolution—complete with job descriptions, reporting lines, hierarchies, and middle management—will decline and die out.
  - **Task-Centric vs. Position-Centric:** Future organizations will be built around fluid workflows and tasks where carbon-based and silicon-based workers collaborate directly.
- **The Neo-Lab Prototype and Partnership Models:** 
  - Current "neo labs" are the early embryonic forms of a new organizational species that resembles professional sports teams or partnerships.
  - **The Death of Job Titles:** With extreme transparency of individual contributions made possible by AI, traditional titles (VP, Director) will vanish, replaced by a pure partnership model where every human team member is indispensable.
- **Shifting Definition of Strategy and Organization:** 
  - Strategy in the AI era cannot be planned via linear extrapolation; it must be *generated* continuously through a new organizational neural network.
  - **The 3-Year Planning Horizon:** CEOs must "look 10 years out, think 3 years out, and act on 1 year out" to maintain dynamic tension and trade-offs.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
- **Task-Centric Organization Realization (Professor Zeng Ming):** The corporate system of hierarchical management will decline and die out, replaced by task-driven organizational forms within approximately **5 years** (as AI-native structures mature past the second stage).
- **Agent Entry Point Emergence (Professor Zeng Ming):** An Agent entry point and unifying standard (comparable to a browser/Yahoo moment) will emerge within the next **2 years**.
- **Native Application Mass Adoption (Professor Zeng Ming):** True native-level AI applications and massive market-shaping successes (comparable to Douyin or Pinduoduo) are still **8 to 10 years away**.
- **Model Parameter Survival Threshold (Professor Zeng Ming):** In the Chinese model landscape, any company failing to field a **3T-parameter model** by the end of the current year will be eliminated from the top tier.
