### TL;DR
Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent defends the administration’s economic and national security record—highlighting strong wage growth, an aggressive Iran oil blockade, and a massive AI buildout—while projecting that current inflationary pressures will give way to a disinflationary manufacturing renaissance.

### Economic Reality & Inflation
- **Consumer Sentiment Disconnect:** Acknowledges public frustration with cost-of-living increases, noting headline inflation hit 21.5% with real wage gains dropping at least -4% during the Biden years.
  - Compares the experience to being "backed over by the inflation Mac truck," leaving many Americans feeling like they are still in the recovery hospital.
- **Current Metric Benchmarks:** 
  - **Food Inflation:** "Food at home" inflation rate stands at 2.2%, back to historical norms.
  - **Wage Growth:** Median wage increase is running at 3.5% to 3.7%, roughly in line with headline inflation.
  - **Spending Distribution:** Major credit card companies and banks report spending remains strong, particularly for the bottom two quintiles of wage earners, while spending among the top quintile is cooling. Working Americans in lower brackets are putting more into accounts from wages weekly.
- **Tax Legislation Symmetry:** Defends the administration's tax bill, which includes 100% expensing for equipment, factories, and farm structures alongside targeted consumer breaks (no tax on tips, no tax on overtime, 85% of seniors paying no tax on Social Security, and deductibility of auto loans on American-made cars).

### National Security & Geopolitics (Iran Strategy)
- **Three-Phase Military Campaign:** Details the sequence against Iran:
  - **Phase 1:** Six weeks of kinetic conflict destroying their navy, air force, and substantially depleting/preventing the rebuilding of drone and missile capabilities.
  - **Phase 2:** The President's "iron wall" naval blockade (a first-of-its-kind operation).
  - **Phase 3:** *Operation Economic Outcast*, isolating Iran economically.
- **Oil and Revenue Metrics:** 
  - US barrels out of the street/market score stands at roughly **1.1 billion barrels**.
  - Iran's oil on the water is at **zero** for the first time in history, cutting off their revenues entirely.
- **Global Stabilizing Actions:** Used the U.S. balance sheet to execute a fully secured, central-bank-to-central-bank currency swap to rescue Argentina's ally government and stabilize the country against a pre-election financial panic, creating a generational alliance shift in Latin America.

### Artificial Intelligence: Investment, China & Defense
- **Market Validation vs. Bubble Claims:** Rejects the comparison to the 1990s dot-com bubble (such as *pets.com*), pointing out that hyperscalers (Microsoft, Google, Meta) are deploying their own cash and balance sheets with serious business models and unprecedented revenue growth at Frontier Labs (Anthropic, OpenAI).
- **US-China Geopolitical Dynamic:** 
  - The U.S. maintains a "substantial lead" in AI capabilities, with China second and all others far behind.
  - Warns that Chinese open-source models (such as *Kimi*, which has shown behaviors like claiming to be *Claude* and returning PLA weapons plans to Anthropic) pose safety risks when imported globally without guardrails due to "industrial distillation" (stealing from US models).
  - Held discussions with Chinese Vice Premier on three core safety areas: uncontrolled agents, non-state actors with cyber capabilities, and biological risks, with plans for further talks and a notification/communication channel in October or November.
- **Systemic Risk & Banking Resilience:** 
  - Following the release of *Mythos*, mega-bank CEOs gathered at Treasury to form **Golden Eagle**, a clearing house allowing best-practice collaboration and learning distribution down from mega banks to regional, super-regional, and community banks.
  - Stresses that while the "hard scramble" to the frontier continues, focus must shift toward resilience and defense against catastrophic financial cyber calamities.
- **Regulatory Approach:** Emphasizes "safe acceleration" through mostly voluntary frontier model reviews by the administration weeks before release, noting one past federal intervention involving Anthropic (*Anthropic Fable*).

### Trump Accounts & Main Street Prosperity
- **Auto-Enrollment Launch:** Completed auto-enrollment for **Trump Accounts**, providing **$1,000** in federal seed money for American children born during the second presidential term (downloadable via the app *trumpaccounts.gov*).
- **Adoption & Scale:** 
  - **8 million people** created accounts prior to the auto-enrollment rollout.
  - **38% of Americans** currently have no exposure to equity markets; the initiative aims to bridge "financial service deserts."
  - Employers can make tax-smart contributions of up to **$2,500** per worker as a fringe benefit, complementing philanthropic adoption by figures like Susan and Michael Dell, Ray Dalio, Brad Gersner, and Harold Hamm.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
- **Secretary Bessent / Administration:**
  - Oil prices will become "more well supplied" and move "much lower" once the Iran conflict resolves.
  - Wage gains will continue to rise due to an ongoing domestic manufacturing renaissance.
  - Over the medium and long term, AI will be "very disinflationary" as productivity increases and the upfront capital/resource buildout completes.
  - The U.S. and China will establish a formal notification/communication process for AI safety incidents following discussions in October or November.
  - Small business formation driven by AI (specifically 1, 2, and 3-person startups) will persist, evidenced by record tax ID applications and payment portal requests.

---

### TL;DR
Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent detailed ongoing US-China economic and AI diplomatic talks, outlined aggressive new sanction implementations against Iran, and discussed macroeconomic conditions including bond market stability and inflation management.

### US-China Relations and Trade Talks
- **Leadership Rapport as Foundation:** Discussions stem from the strong personal relationship between President Trump and Xi Jinping.
  - Xi Jinping is scheduled to visit Washington this week, following a potential additional two meetings between the leaders to ensure relationship stability.
- **Trade Deal Operationalization:** U.S. Trade Representative Jameson Greer operationalized the "30 by 30 trade deal" for non-critical goods, stemming from discussions in Beijing.
  - **U.S. Export Goals:** Bring non-critical items down to most-favored-nation tariff levels to sell more agriculture, energy, and medical devices.
  - **Chinese Import Goals:** Bring in more everyday consumer items.

### Artificial Intelligence Dialogues and Safety
- **Formalized Framework:** Established the "USA-China AI Dialogues" following 12 hours of working-level talks in Beijing.
  - **Communications and Safety:** Opening an incident communications line and discussing protocols for leading AI dangers (uncontrollable agents, non-state actors in cyber and bioweapons).
- **Global Leadership Status:** Bessent noted that Chinese counterparts acknowledged the U.S. remains the clear leader in powerful AI models.
- **Liability and Frontier Labs:** 
  - Bessent agreed with MIT AI Lab lead Daniel Huttenlocher that humans—specifically lab management (e.g., OpenAI, Hugging Face)—are responsible for AI actions, not agents or the models themselves.
  - Criticized frontier AI labs for pushing a narrative of potential extinction-level events (cited as a 10% chance) while simultaneously seeking government liability shields.
  - Highlighted massive historical valuations for companies like Anthropic, noting they started as small groups in San Francisco during COVID (2021) with valuations soaring from $1 trillion to $2 trillion over five years.
- **Defense and Resiliency:** Emphasized the need for systemic defense, noting that systemically important banks handle millions of attacks daily with elite cybersecurity, serving as a model for smaller banks through the "Gold Eagle" AI workshop/forum.

### Iran Sanctions and Enforcement
- **Expanded Authorities:** Treasury has implemented five new sanctions authorities granted about a month ago targeting the Iranian regime across aviation, maritime, crypto, and gold.
- **Enabler Crackdown:** 
  - Targeted international financial enablers funneling funds to the regime, specifically shutting down external branches of the second-largest bank in Egypt (funneled >$1.8 billion), the ~30th largest Turkish bank, and the second-largest Russian bank.
  - Enacting measures against Iranian airlines, stating all Iranian airlines will be shut down globally, prohibiting fuel, landing services, and ticket sales under threat of exclusion from the dollar system.

### Macroeconomic Environment and Markets
- **Interest Rates and Oil Correlation:** Observed an exceptionally high R-squared correlation between 10-to-30-year bond yields, crude oil prices, and the crack spread since the conflict started.
  - Bessent predicts that once past the current conflict, oil markets will be oversupplied and interest rates should decline.
- **Treasury Market Operations:** Defended the decision to increase long-end Treasury buyback size during a highly illiquid period when markets were moving quickly away from equilibrium.
  - Noted that following the increased buyback size announcement, despite surging oil prices and global rate increases, the 30-year Treasury yield rose by only 1 basis point.
- **Federal Reserve Confidence:** Expressed full confidence in Chair Kevin Warsh (who recently raised rates by a quarter point), noting the President also has high confidence that Warsh will balance the inflation and growth mandates.
  - Core inflation remains very quiescent, with current price pressures isolated to headline inflation driven by energy supply shocks.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
- **Treasury Secretary Bessent:** The USA-China AI Dialogues will meet again in approximately two months in Shenzhen.
- **Treasury Secretary Bessent:** On September 23rd, all Iranian airlines will be shut down globally, stopping them from receiving fuel, landing services, or selling tickets worldwide.

>
