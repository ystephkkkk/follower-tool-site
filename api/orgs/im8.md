## Don't launch an ecom brand in 2026 before watching this

#### TL;DR
Danny Yeung built IM8 into a $200M run-rate supplement brand in 18 months by leveraging his background in life sciences and e-commerce, partnering with David Beckham, and focusing heavily on product quality, taste, and aggressive ad scaling on Meta.

### Background & Early Career
* **Early Roots:** Danny Yeung dropped out of high school at 15 in San Francisco after getting kicked out for fighting and started working as a telemarketer selling timeshares.
* **First Exits:** Built a restaurant business at age 24, transitioned into the hotel furniture import-export business (supplying projects like MGM City Center in Vegas), and later founded group-buying e-commerce company *YouBuyIBuy* in Hong Kong in 2010. 
* **Groupon Acquisition:** Groupon acquired 51% of YouBuyIBuy, making it the region's largest e-commerce company; Yeung left in 2014 with $200M in annual revenue after his first major liquidity event.

### Life Sciences & COVID-19 Testing Boom
* **Prenetics Foundation:** Pivoted away from e-commerce in 2014 to found life sciences and DNA testing company *Prenetics* well before the space became mainstream.
* **Pandemic Pivot:** Launched an at-home COVID-19 PCR test within two weeks of the pandemic starting, pricing it at $100 USD compared to the market rate of $400 USD.
* **Massive Scale:** Prenetics scaled to become Hong Kong and the UK's top PCR testing provider, completing 28 million laboratory PCR tests, generating over $700M in revenue, netting over $100M, and listing on the NASDAQ in 2022.
* **The Post-COVID Crash:** Market cap plummeted from over $1B during peak COVID down to $50M as testing demand evaporated, forcing Yeung to rapidly cut 2,000 jobs to ensure corporate survival.

### IM8: Building a $200M Brand in 18 Months
* **Partnership with David Beckham:** Met David Beckham while navigating post-COVID options; persuaded Beckham to join not just as an ambassador, but as a co-founder with the mandate that the brand outlive him.
* **Product Differentiation:** Launched *IM8 Daily Ultimate Essentials* in December 2024 as a red powder (driven by natural ingredients like beets) rather than the standard green supplement category, combining 90 ingredients to replace 16 different daily supplements.
* **Pricing & Value Proposition:** Sells for $90/month, replacing a stack that would otherwise cost $250–$300/month.
* **Hyper-Growth Metrics:** 
  * Hit a **$100M annual revenue run rate** at **11 months**.
  * Hit a **$200M run rate** in **18 months**.
  * Reached **$20M in monthly revenue** shortly after launch.
* **Scientific Rigor:** Recruited a world-class scientific advisory board including Dr. Don Musum (Mayo Clinic), Dr. James Green (former Chief Scientist at NASA), and Suzanne Decoda (Cedar-Sinai); conducted third-party testing via Eurofins and NSF Certification for Sport.

### Marketing Machine & Operational Execution
* **Meta Ads Engine:** Operates between **2,000 to 3,000 ads live simultaneously**, testing 500 to 800 creators weekly across 55–60% static and 40–45% video formats.
* **CAC Efficiency:** Doubled marketing spend in Q2 from **$17M to $33M in a quarter**, yet Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) actually *decreased* from **$305 to $301** (and dropped further to **$239** in July with monthly marketing spend exceeding **$10M**).
* **High-Profile Validation:** Secured organic inbound adoption and partnerships from elite athletes like world number one tennis player Aryna Sabalenka.
* **Offline Events Strategy:** Produced over **200 offline events** (including 7 events in 7 days in New York) to establish tangible, real-world brand trust alongside the scientific advisory board.
