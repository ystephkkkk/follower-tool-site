## Hassett on Powell Staying at Fed, Inflation and Oil Spike

- Channel: Bloomberg Television
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mRLHW9O489w
- Published: 2026-04-30
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260430.html#video-mRLHW9O489w
- Speakers: Kevin Hassett (Director, National Economic Council)

---

**TL;DR**
NEC Director Kevin Hassett anticipates a "sea change" at the Federal Reserve under Kevin Warsh’s expected leadership, advocating for interest rate cuts to fuel a productivity-led growth cycle while dismissing current energy spikes as temporary disruptions that do not warrant rate hikes.

### Federal Reserve Leadership and Independence
* **Leadership Transition:** Hassett expressed disappointment in Chairman Jerome Powell’s recent comments but signaled that the administration is focused on the expected confirmation of Kevin Warsh as the next Chair.
    * **"Sea Change":** Warsh is viewed as "fresh leadership" that will restore institutional independence and shift the Fed's current trajectory.
    * **Confirmation Outlook:** Hassett described Warsh’s confirmation as a "lock."
* **Fed Independence:** The administration maintains it respects the Fed’s autonomy despite public criticisms from the President.
    * **Inspector General (IG) Role:** Hassett defended the Justice Department’s request for the Fed’s IG to review internal matters, arguing it is a "completely respectful" use of the Fed’s own internal oversight.
    * **De-escalation:** The administration hopes for a de-escalation of tensions once the transition in leadership occurs.

### Economic Outlook and Monetary Policy
* **"Blockbuster" Economic Data:** Recent indicators show a robust economy, characterized by high capital spending and historically low unemployment metrics.
    * **Capital Spending Boom:** High import levels are being driven by capital goods used to build domestic factories, rather than perishable consumer goods.
    * **Employment Strengths:** Jobless claims are at their lowest since the 1960s, and government employment is at its lowest level since WWII.
* **The Case for Rate Cuts:** Hassett argues that interest rates should be reduced to "supercharge" the economy, specifically targeting the housing market.
    * **Residential Investment:** Noted as a current weak spot (down 8%) due to interest rate sensitivity; lower rates would stimulate new construction and associated consumer spending.
    * **Productivity Buffer:** Like the 1990s "Greenspan era," Hassett believes a "productivity boom" will keep core prices under control despite high growth.
* **Inflation vs. Energy Shocks:** Hassett warns that hiking rates in response to a "temporary spike" in energy prices would be a "policy error."
    * **Core vs. Headline:** The administration views core inflation as stable and a better predictor of future trends than the volatile topline inflation influenced by oil.

### Energy Markets and the Iran Conflict
* **Strategic Pressure on Iran:** High oil prices and $6/gallon gasoline in California are attributed to the geopolitical conflict with the Iranian regime and its nuclear ambitions.
    * **Sanctions Impact:** Hassett noted that Iranian production has previously been squeezed from 5 million to 1 million barrels per day under strict sanctions, and the administration intends to maintain pressure to stop their nuclear program.
    * **Economic Blockade:** Reports suggest Iran is facing hyperinflation, food shortages, and business closures as a result of the current blockade.
* **Domestic Mitigation Efforts:** The White House has implemented several measures to shield Americans from price spikes:
    * **California Drilling:** Reopening offshore drilling in California that was previously shut down by the state.
    * **Logistics & Standards:** Implementing Jones Act waivers and adjusting ethanol standards to increase summer fuel supply.

### International Relations and European Critique
* **Tensions with Germany:** Hassett addressed recent friction between the President and the German Chancellor regarding Iran policy.
    * **Policy Critique:** Hassett characterized German energy and economic policies as "unwise" and "anti-free market," suggesting these policies have left Europe overly vulnerable to Middle Eastern energy shocks.
    * **Growth Stagnation:** He noted that European economies are currently struggling to achieve even 1% growth and urged partners to help resolve the Iranian nuclear threat to stabilize the global market.

> "I think when Kevin [Warsh] gets in there, it’s going to really be a sea change at the Fed that we’re all looking forward to." — **Kevin Hassett**

> "It would be a policy error for the ECB or the Fed to hike rates into a temporary spike in energy prices... The best forecast of future inflation is not topline inflation, but core inflation." — **Kevin Hassett**

> "Our expectation is that things are going to get better very, very quickly if we can just get the Iranians to finish the negotiations and realize that it’s in the interest of their own people to stop things that could turn into serious hunger events." — **Kevin Hassett**


## JUST IN: Secretary Of Agriculture Brooke Rollins Hosts Other Cabinet Members For Press Conference

- Channel: Forbes Breaking News

- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260415.html#video-Sl1498LQA2U
- Speakers: Brooke Rollins (Secretary of Agriculture); Howard Lutnick (Secretary of Commerce); Kevin (Director, National Economic Council)

---

**TL;DR**
The Trump administration marked Tax Day by highlighting the "Working Families Tax Cut Act," which delivers historic relief to middle-class Americans and rural communities, while simultaneously announcing the launch of the first-ever USDA Office of Seafood to integrate fishermen into federal agricultural support programs.

### Economic Relief and Tax Implementation
* **Broad Tax Savings:** The administration reported that 53 million filers have claimed at least one new tax cut, with the average refund this season exceeding $3,400.
* **Specific Provisions:**
    * **No Tax on Tips/Overtime:** Over 6 million filers claimed "no tax on tips," and 25 million claimed "no tax on overtime," resulting in average savings of $7,000 for service workers.
    * **Senior Relief:** 51 million seniors (88% of those on Social Security) will now pay no tax on their Social Security benefits.
    * **Standard Deduction:** Over 100 million filers utilized the now-permanent double standard deduction.
* **Capital Investment:** The act made permanent 100% immediate expensing for capital-intensive business investments, specifically aiding farmers and small businesses.

### Revitalizing Rural America and Agriculture
* **The "Death Tax":** Secretary Brooke Rollins confirmed the act averted the sunset of the death tax exemption, protecting two million family farms from liquidation.
* **Direct Rural Investment:**
    * **Rural Opportunity Zones:** $100 billion is being driven into rural investment through Opportunity Zones, shifting the program's focus from urban to heartland communities.
    * **Health Transformation:** $50 billion is committed to the Rural Health Transformation program, overseen by Dr. Oz.
* **Commodity Price Protection:** Reference prices for major commodities (corn, soybeans, wheat) will increase by 10% to 21%, providing up to $13 billion in additional revenue protection starting this October.
* **Trade Deficit Reduction:** The administration reported cutting the inherited $50 billion agricultural trade deficit by 40% through 18 new trade deals.

### The Historic Launch of the USDA Office of Seafood
* **"Farmers of the Sea":** Secretary Rollins announced the first-ever **USDA Office of Seafood**, officially recognizing commercial fishermen as food producers rather than just resource harvesters.
* **Integration of Services:** The office will provide fishermen access to USDA tools previously reserved for land farmers, including:
    * Risk management and insurance tools.
    * Marketing support and federal financing.
    * Disaster and weather incident relief.
* **Regulatory and Economic Protection:** 
    * **Inspection Standards:** Officials noted that currently, only 0.1% of imported seafood is inspected; the new office aims to protect domestic markets from "toxic" imported fish.
    * **Defunding Offshore Wind:** Fourth-generation lobsterman Dustin Delano praised the administration for eliminating subsidies for offshore wind projects that threaten traditional fishing grounds.

### Federal Efficiency and Regulatory Reform
* **Permitting Acceleration:** Secretary Doug Burgum highlighted a massive reduction in bureaucracy, reporting that Environmental Impact Statements (EIS) that previously took four years are now being completed in 24 days while still following the law.
* **Eliminating "Bloat":** Secretary Rollins identified several taxpayer-funded studies (e.g., studies on transgender mice and "racism in the pest business") that were defunded to refocus the USDA on core agricultural production.
* **Energy Independence and Inputs:**
    * **Fertilizer Crisis:** Secretary Rollins reported that fertilizer prices have risen 40% in the last month; the administration is engaging DOJ and fertilizer CEOs to prevent price gouging.
    * **Natural Gas Advantage:** Secretary Burgum noted that because the U.S. is a net exporter of natural gas (the primary component of nitrogen fertilizer), the administration intends to build domestic infrastructure to ensure American farmers have the world’s lowest fertilizer costs.

> "For the first time in a long time, commercial fishermen are being recognized for what we truly are: food producers... This is not just about policy; it’s about people. It’s about ensuring that the American fisherman, the farmer of the sea, has a future in this country."
— **Dustin Delano**, *Maine Lobsterman and Fisherman in Residence at NOAA*

> "We’ve taken federal employees and we’ve basically created mind-numbing, soul-sucking jobs where all they do is follow a bunch of regulations that aren’t literally something that Congress passed... When you get rid of the red tape, it not only helps the industry, it helps the federal team members."
— **Doug Burgum**, *Secretary of the Interior*

> "We have a handful of companies in the world supplying fertilizer... I will not leave this building in two years and nine months if we have not made significant progress to build out that infrastructure [in America]."
— **Brooke Rollins**, *Secretary of Agriculture*
