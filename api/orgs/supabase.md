## What Happens When Everyone Can Build Software

- Channel: Y Combinator

### TL;DR
Supabase Co-founder and CEO Paul Copplestone joins the Lightcone hosts to discuss how AI agents and a new class of non-developer "builders" are driving explosive growth, with the platform scaling to 13 million signups, over 50 million databases under management, and a million new databases spun up every week.

### Growth and Scaling Metrics
* **User and Database Scale:**
  * **Signups:** Reached 13 million users (up from approximately 3 million 12 months prior) — *Paul Copplestone*
  * **Databases Under Management:** Reached over 50 million databases (up from 8 million a year ago) — *Paul Copplestone*
  * **Database Velocity:** Spinning up over 1 million databases per week — *Paul Copplestone*
  * **Revenue Growth:** Projected to grow 4x this year — *Paul Copplestone*
* **AI Agent Activity:** Approximately 70% of new activity on Supabase now originates from AI agents — *Harsh*

### Three Eras and Technical Evolution
* **The Dashboard Era (2020 YC Batch):** Built in 100 days to make Postgres accessible via a UI similar to AirTable because even traditional software engineers struggled to use databases — *Paul Copplestone*
* **The API Era (2021):** Shifted to an API-first approach after early partner integrations required programmatic database creation, positioning Supabase perfectly for code-gen tools like Bolt and Lovable — *Paul Copplestone*
* **The Code-First / Agent Era (Current):** Re-architected the CLI, dashboard, and infrastructure-to-code modalities into sandboxes because human dashboard usage declined as agents took over — *Paul Copplestone*
* **Product Expansion:**
  * **Supabase Multi-Graze:** Scales up to petabytes for heavy enterprise workloads — *Paul Copplestone*
  * **Supabase Spark (via Tirso acquisition):** Provides a SQLite- and Postgres-compatible tier designed to scale down for prototyping and lightweight agent/developer workloads — *Paul Copplestone*

### Rise of the "Builder" and Personal Databases
* **Democratization of Coding:** AI models have advanced to the point where non-engineers and domain experts (e.g., domain specialists, a 50-year-old taxi driver in Brazil building a mini-Uber app) can build complex software without convincing a traditional software engineer — *Paul Copplestone*
* **Personal Data and Apps:** 
  * Builders are creating personalized "end-of-one" software for health, personal finance (feature-compatible with Mint or Monarch), and custom CRMs — *Harsh & Paul Copplestone*
  * Users want to own their underlying database layer rather than locking all personal life data inside a single closed-source agent — *Harsh & Paul Copplestone*
* **Top-of-Funnel Dynamics:** Unlike historical product-led growth (PLG) where top-of-funnel acceleration caused activation and conversion rates to drop, signups, activation, and conversion are all simultaneously trending up as models improve — *Paul Copplestone*

### Agent-First Design and Internal Engineering
* **Agent Error Handling:** Error messages have been re-engineered to include explicit instructions and documentation links so agents can automatically self-remedy and complete one-shot loops without hitting dead ends — *Paul Copplestone*
* **Testing and Evals:** Internal engineering effort has heavily shifted from manual building to automated testing and eval suites across multiple AI models to benchmark performance and ensure code quality — *Paul Copplestone & Harsh*
* **Internal AI Adoption:** Internal engineer code generation jumped from 10% writing 80% of code to 60% within six months under a permissive internal AI policy — *Paul Copplestone*

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Global Database Scale:** ChatGPT has 1.2 billion weekly active users, and if all of them launch something monthly, architectures must support 1.2 billion new databases a month — *Paul Copplestone*
* **Every Human as a Builder:** Everyone in the world with access to AI will become a builder of some sort, capable of customizing their own apps and UIs by 2027 — *Paul Copplestone*

## How Supabase Became One Of The Fastest Growing DevTool Companies In The World

- Channel: Y Combinator
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sG5aB79TE44
- Published: 2026-07-23
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260723.html#video-sG5aB79TE44
- Speakers: Paul Copplestone (CEO, Supabase)

---

### TL;DR
Supabase is an open-source, Postgres-backed database and developer tools platform founded in 2020 by Paul Copplestone, which grew from an initial open-source side project into a decacorn valued at $10B after raising a $500M round. The platform has scaled to 10 million developers across 60+ countries with 360 employees, and currently powers 60% to 90% of all AI agent-generated databases.

### Background & Early Ventures
- **Paul Copplestone (Co-founder & CEO):** Originally from New Zealand, grew up with an entrepreneur father, and spent only one brief stint in a corporate consultancy before dedicating his career entirely to startups.
- **Previous Ventures:** 
  - Startup #1: Served as CTO; did not find product-market fit, leading Paul to leave after two years over strategic disagreements with the CEO.
  - Startup #2: Incubated Supabase as a side project inside a different dev tools company for two years before officially launching it out into the market.
- **Why Postgres & Open Source:** 
  - Paul closely monitored Hacker News three times a day and noticed Postgres trending upward in popularity.
  - Chose open source due to personal philosophy and community-building values; monetization took about a year and a half to begin.

### Launch & Developer Experience
- **Early Positioning:** Initially tried a real-time Postgres engine, but growth took off only after rebranding and positioning as the "Open Source Firebase Alternative."
- **Initial Target Market:** High-growth, venture-backed startups (specifically Y Combinator companies). Approximately over 60% of every YC batch now uses Supabase, though almost no one trusted them during their own batch.
- **Time to Value Metrics:** 
  - Measured AWS RDS instance spin-up time at around 8.5 minutes.
  - Set a strict internal goal to drop their own setup time under a minute, which eventually reached roughly 5 seconds.
  - Transitioned the developer experience from a visual dashboard/SQL editor to CLIs, MCPs, and Git-backed declarative schemas designed for AI agents.

### The AI Era & Agent-Driven Scale
- **Explosive Growth (2024–2026):** 
  - Growth accelerated dramatically with the rise of AI builders and "vibe coding" tools like Bolt, Lovable, Claude Code, and Codex.
  - Monthly user metrics jumped from 6.5 million to 10 million developers, with conversion rates and user activation accelerating simultaneously despite zero traditional marketing spend.
- **Supabase for Platforms:** Built to support platforms like Lovable and Bolt; enables the automated spin-up of millions of databases. Also adapted for enterprise innovation labs to manage and monitor large volumes of databases.

### Company Operations & Culture
- **Remote-First Organization:** Employs 360 people across more than 60 countries with zero physical offices.
- **AI-Driven Documentation:** Because every decision over the past six years has been fully documented in writing across Slack and Notion, their internal AI agents possess institutional memory dating back to day one.
- **Funding & Valuation:** Raised a massive series of rounds, culminating in a **$500 million funding round at a valuation exceeding $10 billion**.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
- **Paul Copplestone:** 60% to 90% of all new database launches are now triggered by AI agents.
- **Paul Copplestone:** The next major wave of dev tool opportunity will shift from the "build stage" (prompting and generation) to the "operate stage" (self-driving, automated database management, security patches, and uptime maintenance).
