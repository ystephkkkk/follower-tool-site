### AMI Labs: A $1 Billion Bet on Next-Gen AI
* **Record Seed Funding:** Yann LeCun (AI pioneer and former Meta executive) has raised $1 billion for his startup, AMI Labs, valuing the 12-person company at roughly $3.5 billion despite having no product or revenue.
    * **Investor Profile:** The round includes a diverse international mix: 39% from Europe (Bpifrance, Dassault Systèmes, Xavier Niel), 33% from the US (including Jeff Bezos), and 27% from Asia and Dubai. Notably, no traditional Silicon Valley VCs participated.
* **The "World Models" Vision:** LeCun aims to replace current Large Language Models (LLMs) with "World Models" based on JEPA architecture.
    * **Learning Like Humans:** Unlike ChatGPT, which predicts the next word, these models are designed to learn from video and sensors to understand the physical world, reasoning, and planning, similar to a human child.
    * **Efficiency:** A core goal is drastic energy efficiency, mimicking the low power consumption of the human brain compared to massive GPU clusters.
* **Meta Fallout:** The panel noted that LeCun’s departure from Meta coincided with the rise—and recent sidelining—of Alexander Wang at Meta, signaling internal turmoil at the social media giant over its AI direction.

### The Dismantling of SFR
* **The "Consortium" Strategy:** Orange, Bouygues, and Free have formed an unprecedented alliance to acquire SFR from Altice and divide its assets.
    * **Asset Allocation:** Bouygues is expected to take the largest share due to existing technical partnerships with SFR. Free is targeting the B2B sector (SFR Business), while Orange seeks mobile frequencies and select retail locations.
* **Pressure on Altice:** Patrick Drahi is under intense pressure from creditors to sell within a two-year window (one year remaining). Experts expect a deal could close as early as Spring 2026.
* **Economic and Social Risks:**
    * **Job Losses:** Union representatives warn of thousands of job cuts, particularly among the 3,000+ technicians, as fiber technology requires significantly less maintenance than copper.
    * **Regulatory Hurdles:** Transitioning from four operators to three often leads to price increases; regulators may impose strict conditions to protect consumer costs.

### Geopolitics of AI: Anthropic vs. Washington
* **The Blacklist Conflict:** Anthropic (creators of the Claude AI) has been blacklisted by the Trump administration, leading to a legal battle and a "fronde" (protest) from employees at Google DeepMind and OpenAI who support Anthropic's stance on ethics.
* **State Control:** Michel Levy-Provençal suggests the "Chinese model" of state-controlled AI is penetrating the West, where companies must either cooperate with the government or face extinction.
* **The Cost of Ethics:** Anthropic anticipates losing $5 billion in potential revenue next year due to government sanctions, though its consumer usage is currently outpacing ChatGPT on the iOS App Store.
