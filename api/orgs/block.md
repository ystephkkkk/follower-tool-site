## Jack Dorsey: Every Company Can Now Be a Mini-AGI

- Channel: Sequoia Capital
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YTVSwOY19Qs
- Published: 2026-04-02
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260402.html#video-YTVSwOY19Qs
- Speakers: Jack Dorsey (CEO, Block); Roelof Botha (Partner, Sequoia Capital)

---

**TL;DR**
Jack Dorsey is transitioning Block from a traditional corporate hierarchy to a centralized AI "intelligence layer," reducing the workforce by 40% to eliminate "lossy" human management chains and refocusing the human role on high-level judgment, taste, and creative alignment.

### The Shift: From Hierarchy to "Mini-AGI"
*   **Information Legibility:** Dorsey argues that traditional hierarchies exist primarily to manage information flow, which is inherently slow and "lossy." By capturing all digital artifacts (code, Slack, docs) into an intelligence layer, the company becomes a "world model" or "mini-AGI" that any employee or board member can query in real time.
*   **The "Circle" Model:** Unlike the "Pyramid" (Manager Mode) or "Flat" (Founder Mode), Dorsey proposes a circle with AI at the center and humans at the edge. The system provides the ground truth, while humans provide alignment and course correction.
*   **Eliminating the Roadmap:** Dorsey believes a company’s ultimate limiting factor is its fixed roadmap. In the new model, customer queries and proactive intelligence determine what is built in real time, shifting the company from "navigational interfaces" to "conversational capabilities."

### Reimagining Organizational Roles
Block is normalizing its 6,000-person workforce into just three distinct roles to increase velocity and decrease organizational depth (aiming for 2-3 layers max):
*   **Individual Contributor (IC):** The builders and operators (engineers, designers, sales) who use AI agents to augment their breadth, allowing one person to do the work previously requiring a team of ten.
*   **Directly Responsible Individual (DRI):** The owners of customer outcomes who set strategy, assemble IC teams, and take full accountability for success or failure.
*   **Player Coach:** Replacing traditional managers, these individuals build human capacity and mastery. They do not just "tell" but "show" by doing the work alongside their teams.
*   **CEO Mandate:** The CEO's role shifts from decision-making to "architecting the intelligence" and ensuring the "edge" of humans remains aligned with the company’s values and "taste."

### Executing the 40% Workforce Reduction
*   **The Catalyst:** The decision followed the late 2023/early 2024 release of advanced AI tools like **Goose** (a coding agent harness) and **Cloud Code**, which proved capable of understanding large, legacy codebases.
*   **The "Shit Sandwich" Strategy:** Dorsey and his team decided to execute a massive cut (40%) from a position of strength rather than reacting to market weakness. 
*   **The Math of Excellence:** The team calculated the absolute minimum number of people required for three pillars:
    1.  Maintaining 100% uptime.
    2.  Full regulatory and legal compliance.
    3.  Growth and the transition to an AI-native structure.
*   **Board Transparency:** Roelof Botha noted the decision was supported because it was "principled and logical" rather than reactionary. Dorsey provided a detailed, logic-based manifesto to build trust before execution.

### Leadership and Board Dynamics
*   **The ALE Framework:** Roelof Botha defines timeless CEO qualities through the acronym **ALE**: **A**uthenticity, **L**ogic, and **E**mpathy. 
*   **Board Selection:** Dorsey advises founders to treat investor selection as a "hire you can never fire." He suggests bringing in independent board members early (after product-market fit) to provide mentorship and a buffer between the founder and VCs.
*   **Avoid the "Anti-Pattern":** Dorsey strongly warns against being the CEO of two public companies simultaneously, calling it an "anti-goal" that leads to over-delegation and the accidental creation of disconnected holding companies.
*   **Meditation as Training:** Dorsey views meditation not as a "woo-woo" practice, but as physical training to sharpen focus, observe without reacting, and recognize the impermanence of business crises.

## "We're Not Writing Code by Hand Anymore. That's Over." | Owen Jennings & David Haber - The a16z Show

- Channel: a16z
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=krdrkl38nRw
- Published: 2026-04-01
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260401.html#video-krdrkl38nRw
- Speakers: David Haber (General Partner, a16z); Owen Jennings (Executive Officer and Business Lead, Block)

---

**TL;DR:** Block (formerly Square) recently executed a 40%+ reduction in force (RIF) driven by a paradigm shift where AI agents and "squads of three" now outperform traditional teams of 14, effectively decoupling company output from human headcount.

### The Catalyst: Breaking the Correlation Between Headcount and Output
*   **The "December Paradigm Shift":** Owen Jennings (Business Lead, Block) identifies the first week of December as a turning point where frontier models (Opus 4-6, Codex 5-3) became capable of handling complex, existing codebases rather than just greenfield projects.
*   **Decoupling Growth from Hiring:** Historically, output scaled with headcount. Jennings argues this correlation is now broken; one or two engineers using advanced AI tools can be 10x to 100x more productive than a full department.
*   **Strategic RIF Execution:** The 40% staff reduction was not driven by financial distress or "ZIRP" bloat, but by a functional redesign.
    *   **Development-Heavy Cuts:** Reductions were concentrated in engineering, product, and design (EPD), where AI gains are highest.
    *   **Protected Areas:** Compliance, regulatory, and sales teams saw minimal cuts due to the high risk and nuanced nature of those roles.

### Internal Infrastructure: The Agentic Operating System
*   **Goose (Agent Harness):** A model-agnostic substrate developed by Block that allows the company to swap between 120+ different models (OpenAI, Anthropic, open-source) depending on the task. It serves as the foundation for both internal tools and customer-facing products.
*   **Builder Bot:** An internal autonomous tool that builds features and merges Pull Requests (PRs). 
    *   **Efficiency:** Builder Bot frequently completes 85% to 100% of a feature’s code, with humans acting as "final-mile" editors.
*   **G2 (Agentic OS):** An internal-only system that allows any employee to automate deterministic workflows.
*   **Shift in Workflow:** Engineering has moved from a linear PR-review-merge cycle to a parallelized system where a single developer might oversee 14 different agents building PRs simultaneously.

### Organizational Redesign: From Hierarchies to Squads
*   **The "Small Squad" Model:** Block moved from a functional hierarchy (e.g., 14-person teams with eight server engineers and four client engineers) to "squads" of one to six people.
*   **Flattening the Org:** On the development side, Block cut management layers by 50% to 60%, resulting in only two or three layers between the executive team and the "builders."
*   **The Demise of Meetings:** To facilitate rapid building, Block reduced internal meetings by 70% to 80%, allowing teams to focus on output over coordination.
*   **Role Fluidity:** In the new structure, the lines between roles are blurring. Most designers and Product Managers (PMs) at Block are now expected to ship PRs directly to GitHub.

### Product Innovation: Generative UI and Proactive Intelligence
*   **From Static to Generative UI:** Jennings predicts the end of rigid app interfaces. Instead of every user seeing the same layout, "Generative UI" creates custom interfaces, charts, and visualizations on the fly based on individual user data.
*   **Money Bot (Cash App):** Built on the Goose harness, this serves as a "CFO in your pocket" for users, proactively suggesting financial moves and generating custom data visualizations that do not exist in the hardcoded source.
*   **Manager Bot (Square):** Allows merchants to build custom business applications via natural language. For example, a restaurant owner can ask the bot to create a custom employee scheduling app that integrates with WhatsApp.
*   **QA Challenges:** Moving to non-deterministic, generative outputs for tens of millions of users creates significant Quality Assurance (QA) hurdles that the company is currently solving through "proactive intelligence" filters.

### New Moats in the AI Era
*   **The "Vibecoding" Risk:** While simple apps can now be "vibecoded" (built entirely by AI) in a week, Jennings argues that distribution, network effects, and hardware remain defensive moats.
*   **Deep Context as a Moat:** The ultimate defense is a company’s unique understanding of complex signals—in Block’s case, the interplay between buyers and sellers.
*   **Iteration Speed:** Defensibility now depends on how many times a company can run the "feedback loop" between their proprietary data and their AI builders. Companies that can iterate thousands of times a day via agents will outpace those relying on human-speed development.
