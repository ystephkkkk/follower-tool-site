#### TL;DR
President Trump, Vice President JD Vance, and members of the White House fraud task force announced a new whistleblower incentive program offering a 1% commission for reporting taxpayer fraud, while detailing hundreds of billions in recovered funds and aggressive immigration enforcement.

### Fraud Whistleblower Incentive Program
* **Website Launch:** Citizens and reporters can report fraud at `fraud.gov`, a platform created by Joe Gibia and the National Design Studio.
* **1% Commission:** **Financial Reward:** Anyone who successfully proves and turns in fraudsters will receive a 1% commission on the recovered funds, regardless of total amount.
* **Binding Arbitration:** **Dispute Resolution:** President Trump announced he will personally serve as the binding arbitrator to settle any disputes regarding who gets credit for a tip, ensuring immediate payouts without court delays.

### Task Force Recoveries & Government Waste Metrics
* **Total Recovered Funds:** **$260 Billion:** The Trump administration has uncovered $260 billion in fraud since taking office.
* **Specific Program Cuts:**
  * **Food Stamps:** Removed 60,000 dead people from food stamp lists.
  * **Social Security:** Eliminated 275,000 illegal aliens from Social Security.
  * **Obamacare:** Cut $125 billion in Obamacare subsidies.
  * **Small Business Loans:** Uncovered 562,000 fraudulent small business loans.
  * **Hospice Facilities:** Cut funding to fake hospice facilities that stole an estimated $1.4 billion.

### Specific State-Level Investigations & Actions
* **Minnesota (Child Care & Autism Scams):** 
  * **Nick Shirley's Exposé:** Citizen journalist Nick Shirley exposed billions stolen via fraudulent child care centers (e.g., "Quality Learing Center") and autism service scams where mothers were paid $1,500 to falsely diagnose children.
  * **Arrests & Subpoenas:** 115 Minnesota fraudsters arrested and charged, 75 convicted, and over 2,700 subpoenas issued.
  * **DOJ Seizure:** The Department of Justice filed a complaint to seize half a city block in Minneapolis bought with stolen SNAP funds.
  * **CMS Funding Deferrals:** CMS deferred half a billion dollars from Minnesota across 14 high-risk categories.
* **California (Personal Care & Media Laws):**
  * **Stop Nick Shirley Act:** California passed legislation aiming to restrict citizen journalism regarding immigration fraud.
  * **Medicaid Deferral:** HHS Secretary Kennedy deferred $1.2 billion in Medicaid payments to California due to abnormal 24% growth in personal care services over three years.

### Legislative Proposals & Legal Measures
* **Mandatory Minimums:** President Trump proposed congressional legislation establishing mandatory minimums for all benefits fraud offenses to prevent judges from giving light sentences.
* **State Official Liability:** Proposed civil liability for state officials who knowingly fail to stop massive fraud in their states.
* **Pardon Protection:** President Trump stated he will use his presidential pardon power to protect journalists and whistleblowers like Nick Shirley who are unfairly targeted by state-level weaponization.

### Foreign Policy & Global Observations
* **Ukraine & Russia:** 
  * **Peace Negotiations:** President Trump stated that Ukraine and Russia must make a deal to end a war that cost 32,000 lives last month, noting that the U.S. has provided hundreds of billions of dollars without getting anything in return.
  * **Javelins vs. Sheets:** Contrasted his administration's provision of Javelin anti-tank missiles with the Obama administration's provision of sheets.
* **European Immigration Crisis:** Criticized European immigration policies in France, Spain, and the UK, warning that unvetted mass migration threatens the survival of European nations.
* **Iran:** Stated that oil price increases are a small price to pay to prevent Iran from obtaining a nuclear weapon.

### TL;DR
President Trump and Vice President JD Vance announced a new $25 million Fraud Whistleblower Incentive Program in the Oval Office, offering a 1% commission to citizens and journalists who expose taxpayer fraud. The administration also highlighted massive recoveries across federal programs and targeted state-level officials for failing to stop widespread fraud.

### Whistleblower Incentive Program & Operations
* **New Program Creation:** 
  * **Website & Portal:** A new platform built by Joe Gebbia and the National Design Studio allows Americans to report fraud and corruption at fraud.gov.
  * **Commission Structure:** Whistleblowers and independent journalists (with media outlet approval) who successfully prove and report stolen taxpayer funds will receive a **1% commission** on recovered amounts.
  * **Arbitration:** President Trump will personally serve as the binding arbiter for any disputes regarding who gets credit or how the commission is split.

### Federal Fraud Recoveries & Administration Actions
* **Massive Deficit and Fraud Reductions:** 
  * **Total Uncovered Fraud:** The administration has uncovered **$260 billion** in fraud since taking office.
  * **Social Program Cleanups:** Removed **160,000 dead people** from food stamp rolls, kicked **275,000 illegal aliens** off social security, eliminated **$125 billion** in Obamacare subsidies for foreign migrants, and uncovered **562,000 fraudulent small business loans**.
  * **Hospice and Care Cuts:** Funding was cut to fake hospice facilities that stole an estimated **$1.4 billion**.
* **Minnesota Autism & Childcare Fraud:** 
  * **Arrests & Subpoenas:** Partially resulting from citizen journalist Nick Shirley's investigations, **115 Minnesota fraudsters have been arrested and charged, 75 convicted, and over 2,700 subpoenas issued**.
  * **CMS Actions:** Dr. Mehmet Oz announced CMS deferred **$500 million** in Minnesota autism and childcare fraud categories after discovering roughly one-third of providers in 14 high-risk areas were invalidatable.
* **California Medicaid Actions:** 
  * **Personal Care Services:** HHS Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. deferred **$1.2 billion** in Medicaid payments to California due to personal care services growing at **24% over the last 3 years**—twice the national average.

### Legislative Proposals & Legal Targets
* **Mandatory Minimums for Fraud:** 
  * **Proposal:** President Trump proposed congressional legislation to enact mandatory minimum prison sentences for anyone committing benefits fraud, removing judge discretion for low-level wrist-slaps.
* **State Official Liability:** 
  * **Proposal:** Proposing a new federal law to impose civil liability on state officials (such as those in Minnesota and California) who knowingly fail to stop widespread benefits fraud in their states.
* **First Amendment and the "Stop Nick Shirley Act":** 
  * **California Law:** Criticized by President Trump and Nick Shirley as an outrageous attempt to crush free speech and protect corruption by restricting immigration support service providers from exposing wrongdoing.

### Global Affairs, Foreign Policy, & Immigration
* **Europe & Immigration Crisis:** 
  * **Riots in France:** President Trump attributed the severe economic and societal unrest in France and Europe directly to unchecked, unvetted mass immigration, warning that European countries must remove insurrectionist populations or risk losing their countries.
  * **US Border Status:** Highlighted that the U.S. border has not seen a single illegal entry in **17 months**.
* **Russia-Ukraine War:** 
  * **Peace Negotiations:** President Trump stated that Russia and Ukraine must make a deal to end the war, noting that the war has caused massive casualties (citing **32,000 soldiers lost last month** alone) and that the U.S. has spent hundreds of billions with nothing returned.
