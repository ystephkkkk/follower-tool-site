## AI ‘workslop’ is a leadership problem. Here’s how to fix it

- Channel: World Economic Forum
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTAbIqqAf4Y
- Published: 2026-05-26
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260526.html#video-zTAbIqqAf4Y
- Speakers: Alexi Robichaux (CEO, BetterUp)

---

**TL;DR** 
Artificial intelligence acts as a powerful multiplier of human capability, meaning low-quality outputs ("workslop") are symptoms of weak management rather than technology failures. To capture the real productivity gains of AI, leaders must shift their focus from pure technological acquisition to active human transformation, upskilling teams in essential psychological and leadership capabilities.

---

### Redefining "Workslop" as a Leadership Symptom

*   **Understanding "Workslop":** This term refers to low-quality, repetitive, or overly long-winded work produced through poor human-AI collaboration.
    *   **The Productivity Drag:** BetterUp research shows that 40% of workers have received workslop, and resolving a single incident of it takes an average of two hours of human time to correct or rewrite.
*   **AI as an Amplifier:** AI does not replace human thought; it accentuates it. Excellent, highly clear inputs produce crystalline work, while vague, unthoughtful inputs result in poor work "on steroids."
*   **The Management Diagnostic:** Workslop is rarely a technical limitation. Instead, it is a warning sign of systemic organizational issues, including:
    *   Unclear or non-existent definitions of success.
    *   A lack of defined standards of excellence.
    *   A failure of managers to provide constructive feedback to their teams.

### Tactical Development through AI Coaching

*   **Democratizing Professional Growth:** Historically, executive coaching was reserved for top-tier leaders. BetterUp's Human Transformation Platform utilizes AI to scale personalized, 24/7 support to frontline, hospitality, and military personnel.
*   **The Division of Human and Digital Labor:** Users instinctively treat human and AI coaching differently:
    *   **Human Coaches:** Best suited for high-value issues, identity development, long-term transformational goals, and value alignment.
    *   **AI Coaches:** Highly effective for tactical, immediate, and transactional assistance, such as role-playing difficult conversations or troubleshooting situational management problems.
*   **Safe-Space Skill Building:** AI offers a "practice gym" free from real-world consequences.
    *   **Performance Reviews:** Preparing for performance conversations is the most popular AI simulator use case. It allows managers to practice balancing motivation with critical feedback, avoiding the damage of practicing "with live ammunition" on real employees.
*   **The Vulnerability Paradox:** Counterintuitively, many employees feel more comfortable sharing their professional weaknesses and fears with an impartial AI agent than with a human coach, bypassing psychological barriers to accelerate behavioral change.

### Essential "Soft" Skills in the Era of AI

*   **The Shift in Rate Limiters:** In a world where technical execution is increasingly automated, psychological and soft skills have become the primary drivers of performance.
*   **The Premium on Human Mindsets:** BetterUp's data highlights that workers who thrive alongside AI possess measurably higher levels of key, learnable capabilities:
    *   **Optimism & Resilience:** The capability to view setbacks as temporary and actively bounce back.
    *   **Agency & Curiosity:** Possessing an internal locus of control and a drive to experiment and iterate rapidly.
*   **The Reality of "Vibe Coding":** This shift allows non-programmers to write functional software, build websites, and deploy applications using natural language inputs (such as Claude Code).
    *   **Implication for Knowledge Workers:** Because technical production is no longer the bottleneck, the value of a role shifts entirely to the quality of the initial vision, creative imagination, and conceptual thinking.

### Balancing Technology and Talent Investment

*   **The AI Adoption Bottleneck:** While businesses have poured massive capital into AI infrastructure, the anticipated productivity gains have lagged. Multiple studies indicate the primary bottleneck is human resistance and a lack of baseline user skills.
*   **Rebalancing the Investment Mix:** Leaders must treat human upskilling with the same financial discipline as software deployment.
    *   **The 5-to-1 Rule:** For every $5 spent on technology acquisitions, companies should invest at least $1 in talent development, upskilling, and organizational redesign to support new operational workflows.
*   **Grounded Confidence:** To guide teams through compounding technology cycles, leaders should prioritize self-leadership, stability, and emotional poise in times of rapid technological shift.
