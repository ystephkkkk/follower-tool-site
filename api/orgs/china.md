### TL;DR
Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent detailed ongoing US-China economic and AI diplomatic talks, outlined aggressive new sanction implementations against Iran, and discussed macroeconomic conditions including bond market stability and inflation management.

### US-China Relations and Trade Talks
- **Leadership Rapport as Foundation:** Discussions stem from the strong personal relationship between President Trump and Xi Jinping.
  - Xi Jinping is scheduled to visit Washington this week, following a potential additional two meetings between the leaders to ensure relationship stability.
- **Trade Deal Operationalization:** U.S. Trade Representative Jameson Greer operationalized the "30 by 30 trade deal" for non-critical goods, stemming from discussions in Beijing.
  - **U.S. Export Goals:** Bring non-critical items down to most-favored-nation tariff levels to sell more agriculture, energy, and medical devices.
  - **Chinese Import Goals:** Bring in more everyday consumer items.

### Artificial Intelligence Dialogues and Safety
- **Formalized Framework:** Established the "USA-China AI Dialogues" following 12 hours of working-level talks in Beijing.
  - **Communications and Safety:** Opening an incident communications line and discussing protocols for leading AI dangers (uncontrollable agents, non-state actors in cyber and bioweapons).
- **Global Leadership Status:** Bessent noted that Chinese counterparts acknowledged the U.S. remains the clear leader in powerful AI models.
- **Liability and Frontier Labs:** 
  - Bessent agreed with MIT AI Lab lead Daniel Huttenlocher that humans—specifically lab management (e.g., OpenAI, Hugging Face)—are responsible for AI actions, not agents or the models themselves.
  - Criticized frontier AI labs for pushing a narrative of potential extinction-level events (cited as a 10% chance) while simultaneously seeking government liability shields.
  - Highlighted massive historical valuations for companies like Anthropic, noting they started as small groups in San Francisco during COVID (2021) with valuations soaring from $1 trillion to $2 trillion over five years.
- **Defense and Resiliency:** Emphasized the need for systemic defense, noting that systemically important banks handle millions of attacks daily with elite cybersecurity, serving as a model for smaller banks through the "Gold Eagle" AI workshop/forum.

### Iran Sanctions and Enforcement
- **Expanded Authorities:** Treasury has implemented five new sanctions authorities granted about a month ago targeting the Iranian regime across aviation, maritime, crypto, and gold.
- **Enabler Crackdown:** 
  - Targeted international financial enablers funneling funds to the regime, specifically shutting down external branches of the second-largest bank in Egypt (funneled >$1.8 billion), the ~30th largest Turkish bank, and the second-largest Russian bank.
  - Enacting measures against Iranian airlines, stating all Iranian airlines will be shut down globally, prohibiting fuel, landing services, and ticket sales under threat of exclusion from the dollar system.

### Macroeconomic Environment and Markets
- **Interest Rates and Oil Correlation:** Observed an exceptionally high R-squared correlation between 10-to-30-year bond yields, crude oil prices, and the crack spread since the conflict started.
  - Bessent predicts that once past the current conflict, oil markets will be oversupplied and interest rates should decline.
- **Treasury Market Operations:** Defended the decision to increase long-end Treasury buyback size during a highly illiquid period when markets were moving quickly away from equilibrium.
  - Noted that following the increased buyback size announcement, despite surging oil prices and global rate increases, the 30-year Treasury yield rose by only 1 basis point.
- **Federal Reserve Confidence:** Expressed full confidence in Chair Kevin Warsh (who recently raised rates by a quarter point), noting the President also has high confidence that Warsh will balance the inflation and growth mandates.
  - Core inflation remains very quiescent, with current price pressures isolated to headline inflation driven by energy supply shocks.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
- **Treasury Secretary Bessent:** The USA-China AI Dialogues will meet again in approximately two months in Shenzhen.
- **Treasury Secretary Bessent:** On September 23rd, all Iranian airlines will be shut down globally, stopping them from receiving fuel, landing services, or selling tickets worldwide.
