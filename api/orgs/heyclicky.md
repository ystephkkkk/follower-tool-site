## This Is How You Should Actually Make Launch Videos

- Channel: Y Combinator
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v12aSIZIXUs
- Published: 2026-10-23
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20261023.html#video-v12aSIZIXUs
- Speakers: Farza Majeed (Founder, Hey Clicky); Aaron Epstein (Host, Y Combinator)

---

### TL;DR
Launch videos in 2026 no longer require expensive $20,000 commercial productions; instead, founders should treat every product demo as a mini-story and consistently ship raw, process-driven content on algorithmic social platforms.

### The Modern Launch Playbook: Mini-Stories Over Major Events
- **Shift from Single Events to Continuous Shipping:** Founders should abandon the outdated meta of coordinating a single, high-stakes launch (like a TechCrunch article or costly agency video).
  - Farza Majeed notes that a simple, authentic video saying "hi" and showing a product performs far better than a $20,000 produced New York Times video.
  - Success comes from treating launches as "mini-stories" and constant demos rather than high-risk rocket launches.
- **Demo-Driven Development:** Work backwards from a demo at every stage of product creation.
  - If a feature sounds good on paper as a video hook before building begins, it is worth creating.
  - The internal excitement of shouting *"Yo, check this out!"* to a co-founder is the exact raw energy that should be packaged for the internet.

### The Content Creation Process: Scripting and Hook Mechanics
- **Religiously Scripting Off-the-Cuff Videos:** Despite appearing natural, high-performing videos are meticulously planned in advance.
  - Farza writes a script days ahead, memorizes and visualizes lines in the shower or on the way to work, and uses text editors like *Freewrite* (freewrite.io) to refine the copy.
  - The core rule is evaluating the text against oneself: *Did I stop scrolling to read this sentence?*
- **The First 10 Seconds & Visual Hooks:** Setup should comprise only 10-20% of a one-minute video.
  - Start with a clear hook defining who you are, what you are doing, and why it matters.
  - Utilize recognizable visual anchors in the first frame (such as *Xcode* for developers) and zoom in immediately to capture attention while users are scrolling.
- **Embracing the "Cringe" Phase:** Getting past initial discomfort requires sheer repetition.
  - Farza admits that many produced videos initially felt terrible or "cringe" to his team before going viral (e.g., a video he thought was awful hit 3 million views on Twitter).
  - Founders must push through publishing imperfect content because audiences forget quickly and the reps build necessary taste and skill.

### Channel Selection and Conversion Realities
- **Prioritizing Platforms by Down-Funnel Conversion:** Vanity metrics (like total views) matter far less than actual user downloads and retention.
  - **Twitter/X:** Historically yields massive view counts (e.g., 3 million views) but features the lowest conversion rates for actual app downloads (less than 1%).
  - **YouTube:** Generates lower initial views (e.g., 20,000 views) but acts as a long-term library with extremely high conversion rates (roughly 20% download the app).
  - **Instagram:** Drives the majority of traffic (around 70%) for Farza's current company, *Hey Clicky*.
- **Targeting Niche Audiences on General Platforms:** Mass-market algorithmic feeds can successfully capture B2B or specialized buyers if directly addressed.
  - By directly calling out specific personas (e.g., "Are you the founder of a design agency?"), Farza generated 50 targeted design agency leads through TikTok videos averaging 4,000 views.

### Lean Production Stack and Team Structure
- **Essential Hardware and Software:** High-end production is completely optional, but audio quality is non-negotiable.
  - *Screen Studio* ($30) is used for smooth screen and cursor recordings.
  - An iPhone selfie camera is sufficient for native, authentic updates.
  - For cinematic quality, Farza uses a *Sony FX3 / FX30* paired with a *DJI mic*.
  - **Audio Warning:** Clean audio is mandatory; viewers will tolerate 720p video in a basement, but poor audio causes immediate drop-off. Never use built-in MacBook or AirPod microphones.
- **Early Team Composition:** Hiring for creative leverage early on frees technical founders to build.
  - Farza's first hires at *Hey Clicky* and *Buildspace* prioritized a filmmaker alongside engineers.
  - A dedicated filmmaker handles the execution (sets, shots, motion graphics, and editing), allowing the founder to focus on product, hiring, and growth.
