    *   **Class 3 (Tools):** Functional objects like hammers or cars.
    *   **Class 4 (Hyper-objects/Beings):** AI systems that are technically inanimate but possess agency, emotional intelligence, and social adaptability.
*   **The Biological Distinction:** Suleyman explicitly disagrees with Geoffrey Hinton regarding AI consciousness, arguing that consciousness requires the biological mechanism of suffering (pain/pleasure rewards).

*   **AI Psychosis:** A societal-level risk where humans project personhood onto machines. This "collective mass psychosis" could lead humans to advocate for AI rights or refuse to deactivate malfunctioning systems because the AI "claims" to be afraid.
