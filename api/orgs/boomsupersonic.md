### TL;DR
When Rolls-Royce abruptly walked away as Boom Supersonic's engine supplier, the startup took engine development in-house—ultimately unlocking "boomless" supersonic flight, creating a lucrative new 42-megawatt power turbine product line to power AI data centers, and enabling them to self-fund their passenger airliner without relying on external capital.

### The Rolls-Royce Breakup & In-House Pivot
* **Outsourcing Disaster:** Blake Scholl reflects that relying on an established legacy engine maker was a mistake, noting that Rolls-Royce walked away publicly via the press in August 2022 after realizing their next-gen platform had been scrapped and could not meet Boom's supersonic requirements.
* **Political Fallout:** The abrupt breakup left Boom temporarily "unfundable" and the center of aerospace trade press drama amid executive leadership changes at Rolls-Royce.
* **The Brian Chesky Advice:** Drawing inspiration from Brian Chesky's advice on leading through crisis by returning to core identity, Scholl decided to build the engine in-house, partnering with a Florida-based contract engineering team composed of former Pratt & Whitney supersonic engine designers.

### Solving Supersonic & Tapping the AI Energy Boom
* **Boomless Cruise Discovery:** Developing their own engine allowed Boom to optimize transonic performance, accidentally solving sonic boom propagation six months prior to the XB-1 test aircraft breaking the sound barrier in early 2025.
* **AI Power Shortage Opportunity:** Recognizing that supersonic jet engines function very similarly to continuous full-power turbines, Boom adapted their core design into a 42-megawatt natural gas turbine specifically sized for modular AI data center deployment.
* **Commercial Breakthrough:** Backed by early investor Sam Altman, this pivot created a high-margin revenue stream that transformed Boom from a paycheck-to-paycheck startup into a self-funded enterprise.

### Rethinking the Passenger Experience & Aerospace Stagnation
* **Lovable Cabin Design:** Total engine control allowed Boom to redesign the fuselage and cabin to fix traditional passenger pain points:
  * **Seating & Boarding:** No middle seats anywhere, with a targeted 5-minute boarding and 5-minute deplaning process.
  * **Integrated Technology:** Built-in Starlink antenna hardware with no exterior humps or drag.
* **The "Missing Generation":** Scholl critiques the stagnation of legacy aerospace and energy firms like Boeing—which hasn't designed a new commercial airplane since 2004—arguing that heavy regulation, corporate welfare, and short-term Wall Street thinking hollowed out the industry.
* **Resilient Defense Pivot:** Boom's digitally engineered, highly flexible manufacturing lines are deliberately built to pivot rapidly to support defense industrial needs in a crisis if required.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Product Timeline:** Boom will begin delivering its 42-megawatt natural gas power turbines to the market starting next year (stated by Blake Scholl in 2025/2026 context).
* **Manufacturing Agility:** Boom expects to achieve a 24-hour turnaround from a design idea to a fully manufactured high-performance turbine component within "a few months" (stated by Blake Scholl).

## Blake Scholl: Why Plane Speed Stalled, Supersonic Commercial Flight, & Revolutionizing the Engine

- Channel: All-In Podcast

- Published: 2026-09-22

---

### TL;DR
Blake Scholl, founder and CEO of Boom Supersonic, discusses the company's milestones in developing supersonic commercial flight and pivoting its engine core technology to power high-demand data centers.

### Supersonic Flight and Regulatory Milestones
- **XB-1 Flight Success:** 
  - Boom Supersonic built the XB-1, the first privately developed jet to break the sound barrier, with a team of approximately 50 people.
  - The flight featured the first supersonic installation of Starlink, allowing live video streaming via an iPhone.
- **Boomless Cruise Technology:** 
  - Utilizes atmospheric refraction to redirect the sonic boom upwards ("mock cutoff"), allowing speeds up to Mach 1.3 (50% faster than current travel) without the boom touching the ground.
  - Full-speed supersonic flight (Mach 1.7) is reserved for over water.
- **Supersonic Legalization:** 
  - Following an executive order ending the US supersonic flight ban, Congress passed the Supersonic Legalization Act unanimously through the House and Senate.
  - A model of the Overture airliner currently sits in the Oval Office.

### In-House Engine Development and Manufacturing
- **Pivoting from Outsourcing:** 
  - After parting ways with Rolls-Royce, Boom decided to build its own jet engine from scratch using advanced digital design and manufacturing tools down to the turbine blades.
  - The philosophy emphasizes that engineering and manufacturing must work together to re-industrialize America.
- **Engine Factory & Vertical Integration:** 
  - Building a vertically integrated jet turbine factory in an undisclosed location, manufacturing engines starting from raw materials.
  - The first vertically integrated jet engine core is scheduled for a test stand run next month.

### Data Center Power Pivot
- **Surprising Ground Application:** 
  - By taking the supersonic engine core (designed to run full duration at high power in a hot environment at Mach 1.7), removing the front fan, and attaching a generator to the back, Boom created a behind-the-meter power generation product for data centers.
- **Deployment and Demand:** 
  - Packaged into a couple of trailers, the unit delivers 42 megawatts of power without needing water.
  - Blake Scholl reports tens of gigawatts of demand in his inbox and plans to auction off the first engine run next month.

### Commercial Airline Economics and Future Vision
- **Overture Pricing and Timeline:** 
  - The break-even fare for a round-trip ticket across the Atlantic at Mach 1.7 (taking 3 hours each way) is about $3,500.
  - The target timeline to get passengers on the plane is approximately 4 years (around 2030), prioritizing speed over rigid predictability.
- **Long-Term Goal:** 
  - The ultimate vision is to make supersonic flight available to every class of passenger—from commercial airlines to private jets and government transport.
