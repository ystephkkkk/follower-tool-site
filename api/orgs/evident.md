### TL;DR
Yann LeCun explains that while Large Language Models (LLMs) excel at symbolic tasks, they fundamentally lack the ability to reason, plan, and operate in the physical world; he argues that joint embedding predictive architectures (JEPAs) and world models represent the necessary next paradigm for achieving true physical AI and robotics.

### AMI Labs & Company Overview
- **Corporate Setup:** Yann LeCun explains that AMI Labs (pronounced *Anidance*, meaning "friend" in French) is headquartered in Paris with major offices in New York and smaller offices in Montreal and Singapore.
- **Funding & Scale:** The company started operations in March, raised approximately $1 billion in a seed round (believed to be the largest seed round in history outside the US), and currently employs around 65 people.
- **Core Mission:** Focused entirely on building AI systems for the real physical world ("physical AI") through the creation of world models.

### Limitations of LLMs & The Moravec Paradox
- **The Core Fallacy:** LeCun notes a 70-year-old recurring mistake in AI where researchers assume that if a machine performs a complex human intellectual task (e.g., playing chess, proving theorems, or passing the bar exam), it possesses general or superintelligence.
- **The Moravec Paradox:** Machines easily manipulate discrete symbols, code, and mathematics (via LLMs), but struggle immensely with the real world, which is messy, continuous, high-dimensional, noisy, and unpredictable.
- **Real-World Failures:** Despite LLM advancements, the industry still lacks domestic robots and Level 5 self-driving cars that can learn to drive in a few hours like a teenager.

### World Models & JEPA Architecture
- **Definition of a World Model:** A system that starts with an idea of the state of the world at time $t$, predicts the resulting state at time $t+1$ after taking a specific action, and uses optimization against a cost function to plan a sequence of actions.
- **Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA):** Unlike generative models that try to predict every pixel or token (which fails in high-noise environments like video or financial time series), JEPA learns an abstract representation of the input and predicts only what is predictable while ignoring unpredictable noise.
- **Analogy to Finance and Physics:** Likened to financial data (mostly noise with a little information) and scientific modeling (such as using the ideal gas law or Navier-Stokes equations without needing to simulate every individual air molecule).
- **Hierarchical World Models:** Published a paper last week showing how high-level goals (e.g., traveling from New York to Paris) break down into hierarchical sub-goals (hailing a taxi, walking out a door) because short-term predictions are accurate while long-term physical details are uncertain.

### Global Landscape & Competitiveness
- **Asian Leadership in Physical AI:** Countries like China, Japan, Korea, and Taiwan are heavily pushing robotics and physical AI due to their strong manufacturing foundations.
- **Shift from Silicon Valley:** Because LLMs are inadequate for physical robotics, many researchers in Asia (particularly China) are rapidly publishing and working on world models and joint embedding approaches, outpacing Silicon Valley, which LeCun describes as "hypnotized by LLMs."
