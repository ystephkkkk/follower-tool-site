### TL;DR
Fei-Fei Li—known as the "Godmother of AI"—has launched World Labs, a startup betting on world models and spatial intelligence to push AI beyond chatbots. While large language models focus on text, Li’s team is building technology that enables machines to render, simulate, and plan within fully consistent 3D and 4D environments based on physics.

### World Models and Spatial Intelligence
* **Beyond Chatbots:** **Limitation of LLMs:** Language models cannot put out fires or cook an omelet alone; advancing scientific discovery and robotics requires spatial intelligence.
* **Three Functions of Spatial Intelligence:** **Core architecture:** Encompasses rendering (producing pixels for humans, such as OpenAI's Sora), simulation (capturing physical world geometry for machines), and planning (determining next actions, like a robot picking up a cup).
* **Marbel Platform:** **First product:** A platform allowing users to generate explorable, editable 3D worlds from a single visual or text prompt, currently utilized in virtual production, game development, and robotic training collaborations with NVIDIA.
* **Training Methodology:** **Data and algorithms:** Trained on specially prepared pixel data (which contains rich camera and spatial information) alongside algorithmic and architectural innovations for 3D/4D generation.

### Foundational Legacy and Academic Roots
* **ImageNet Breakthrough:** **2006–2010 initiative:** Built a catalog of 14 million pictures across over 21,000 categories to provide the data algorithms needed to learn.
* **Turning Point of Modern AI:** **2012 competition:** Geoffrey Hinton's University of Toronto team entered AlexNet powered by NVIDIA GPUs, proving that massive data, neural networks, and GPU computing form the golden recipe for modern AI.
* **Early Scientific Curiosity:** **Pure research origins:** Developed during a period of pure curiosity without money, fanfare, or hype, akin to astronomers looking into the sky.

### AI Policy, Safety, and Societal Impact
* **Grounded Policymaking:** **Science over fiction:** Advises world leaders (including Presidents Biden and Trump and the UN) to root policies in scientific facts rather than Silicon Valley rhetoric about extinction or machine overlords.
* **Identified Risks:** **Potential harms:** World models and language models can generate mis- and disinformation, empower bad actors to weaponize robots, and serve as a lazy crutch for unmotivated students.
* **Rejecting the "God Complex":** **Democratic governance:** Rejects the idea of a "God complex" among tech leaders, emphasizing that civil society must not give up on AI governance or throw the baby out with the bathwater, given AI's potential to cure diseases and help the elderly.
