## Hormuz Blockade Impacts and Trump's New Tariff Playbook

- Channel: Center for Strategic & International Studies

* **TL;DR:** US trade experts examine the ongoing Strait of Hormuz naval blockade, its severe economic and energy market impacts, and the Trump administration's legal pivot to Sections 122 and 301 to sustain its tariff regime.

### Customs and Border Protection: IEEPA Tariff Refund Rollout
* **Upcoming Implementation:** 
  * **Refund Program Launch:** Customs and Border Protection is scheduled to roll out its IEEPA tariff refund program on Monday, April 20th.
  * **Application Requirements:** Refunds will not be automatic; importers must submit an application and supply the same data originally provided.
  * **Electronic Mandate:** Claimants must sign up for CBP's electronic refund process, as refunds will be provided exclusively through electronic transfer.

### Middle East Conflict and the Strait of Hormuz Blockade
* **Current Status and Market Impacts:**
  * **Duration and Enforced Blockade:** Over 50 days into the conflict, the US Navy is enforcing a complete blockade of maritime traffic to and from Iranian ports, which US Centcom stated has "completely halted economic trade to and from Iranian ports by sea."
  * **Energy Market Volatility:** Energy prices remain high and volatile, with West Texas Intermediate trading at $92 a barrel.
  * **Geopolitical Realities:** US control of Iranian ports is viewed by some as stabilizing shipping in the Gulf, while others highlight the resulting economic environment as a state of "no war, no peace, and no oil" that threatens global inflation and supply shortages.
* **Internal Iranian Dynamics (Scott Miller's Analysis):**
  * **Three-Panel Split Screen:** The Iranian government operates via three distinct interest groups: the Mullahs (religious leadership, heavily impacted by initial decapitation strikes), the conventional citizen government (normal appearance but limited power), and the Islamic Republican Guard Corps (IRGC, an independent, self-financed organized crime faction).
  * **Endgame Challenge:** Finding a unified authority to negotiate a settlement will be extremely difficult due to the independent command structure of the IRGC.
* **Long-Term Consequences and Alternative Energy (Bill Reinsch's Analysis):**
  * **Unintended Policy Outcomes:** Trump's fossil-fuel-centric administration is inadvertently driving global economies toward alternative energy sources (electricity, batteries, EVs, wind, and solar) as countries weaponize energy dependency.
  * **Vulnerability of Gulf States:** Decades of branding by the UAE, Bahrain, Qatar, and Kuwait as safe, reliable business and transport hubs have been exposed as fragile.
  * **Alternative Perspectives:** Scott Miller counters that the Abraham Accords represent a resilient commercial vision for the greater Middle East that resists Iranian-backed violence and disorder.

### Section 122 Tariff Litigation
* **Court Challenge and Legal Arguments:**
  * **Lawsuit Context:** On April 10th, the US Court of International Trade heard arguments in a lawsuit filed by 24 states challenging the administration's use of Section 122 to impose 10% tariffs on most imports.
  * **Floating Exchange Rates vs. Balance of Payments:** Plaintiffs argue the statute requires a genuine balance of payments crisis, which cannot occur under modern floating exchange rates (unlike the 1971 fixed gold standard crisis that prompted the Trade Act of 1974). Furthermore, critics argue the US is not currently experiencing a balance of payments crisis.
  * **Anticipated Timeline:** The administration is expected to utilize the 10% tariff authority until it expires after its 150-day limit.

### Section 301 Tariff Strategy and Plan B
* **Administration Playbook for July 24th:**
  * **Secretary Bessent's Strategy:** Following the Supreme Court's IEEPA ruling, the administration initiated Section 301 investigations to ensure tariffs remain in place by early July.
  * **Scope of Investigations:** USTR announced 76 investigations—16 regarding production overcapacity and 60 targeting forced labor compliance—with public comments closing on April 15th and public hearings scheduled for the first week of May.
  * **Legal Durability:** While Section 301 explicitly includes the word "tariff" and grants broad presidential discretion, critics and plaintiffs argue the investigations are a pre-cooked scam designed solely to salvage trade agreements previously tied to IEEPA.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Tariff Duration:** The administration will utilize Section 122 tariff authority continuously until it expires at the 150-day mark — Scott Miller.
* **Section 301 Outcomes:** Everyone investigated under the 76 Section 301 cases will be found guilty, and the resulting remedies will reinstate the trade agreements negotiated the previous year — Bill Reinsch.
