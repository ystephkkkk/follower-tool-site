## The World’s Largest Electric Aircraft Just Flew

- Channel: Y Combinator
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nM86DBOqgPM
- Published: 2026-09-01
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260901.html#video-nM86DBOqgPM
- Speakers: Gustaf Alströmer (Partner, Y Combinator); Anders Forslund (Co-founder and CEO, Heart Aerospace)

---

* **TL;DR:** Heart Aerospace has flown the world’s largest electric aircraft (a 100-foot wingspan, 25,000-pound takeoff weight, and $5 in electricity cost) as it builds the ES30 hybrid-electric airliner to revolutionize regional air travel.
* **Makers & Milestones**
  * **Company Evolution:** Founded 7 years ago out of Y Combinator W19 by Anders Forslund and Clara (starting with a 3D-printed model), Heart Aerospace now employs a 40-person team at its pilot plant in Los Angeles.
  * **Record-Breaking Aircraft:** The company built and flew the largest electric aircraft in the world by a factor of two, marking the first clean-sheet airliner flown in the US in any category in the last 18 years.
  * **First Flight:** The historic first flight took place on Wednesday, August 12th in Plattsburgh, New York.
* **The ES30 Aircraft & Economics**
  * **Capacity & Specs:** The upcoming ES30 will seat up to 36 passengers (most commonly configured for 30, giving everyone about 6 inches of extra legroom) and features an all-electric range of up to 125 miles, a hybrid range of up to 500 miles, and a 30-minute recharge time.
  * **Operating Economics:** Driven by rising oil prices, Heart's overall operating economics have improved from 33% better to 48% better compared to legacy aircraft.
  * **Conventional Design Strategy:** Unlike competitors building futuristic-looking craft, Heart deliberately designed the ES30 to look like a conventional turbo-prop to blend seamlessly into existing infrastructure across the US's 5,000 airports.
* **Electric Motor & Component Advantage**
  * **Simplified Engineering:** Electric motors have only one moving part (compared to thousands in jet engines), feature zero combustion, experience virtually zero wear, and create constant torque that allows them to run virtually silently when standing 100 feet away.
  * **In-House Manufacturing:** Heart designs and builds actuators, 6061 aerospace aluminum cylinder bodies, and 15 machine components in-house rather than relying on traditional aerospace suppliers.
* **Battery Technology & The Hybrid Solution**
  * **Cell Selection:** The team evaluates cells from Chinese, American, and Korean manufacturers in their battery lab to optimize flight cycles, safety, cost, and energy density.
  * **Energy Density Target:** While current cells on their lab table reach up to 370 Wh/kg, one manufacturer is producing a 400 Wh/kg cell expected within a couple of months.
  * **The Reserve Challenge & Hybrid Fix:** Because 1-in-1,000 US flights divert and require 45 minutes of loiter time plus a 100-mile alternate, battery-only aircraft would need 2/3 of their battery dedicated to reserves; Heart solved this by adding a simple, inexpensive turbo-prop hybrid engine for ranges up to 500 miles.
* **Go-To-Market & Capital Strategy**
  * **Early Validation:** Anders Forslund secured Letters of Intent (LOIs) from SAS and three Nordic airlines before having underlying technology.
  * **Inbound Customers:** United Airlines discovered Heart via an inbound message in their spam/info email folder after seeing their 400 kW motor.
  * **Capital-Raising Rule:** Anders Forslund advises founders that every time capital is needed, it must be tied to something material and physical that can be touched and shown.
