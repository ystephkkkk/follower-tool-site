## Robinhood CFO on Retail IPO Demand, Agentic Trading

### TL;DR
Robinhood CFO Shiv Verma discusses retail demand for major upcoming IPOs and the growth of agentic trading and prediction markets, while White House and congressional figures react to the midterm elections and policy impacts.

### Robinhood & Retail IPO Demand
* **Mega-IPO Environment:** Robinhood CFO Shiv Verma noted that Anthropic is viewed as a market bellwether and confirmed Robinhood will be on the deal.
    * **Retail Allocation Goals:** Verma emphasized that retail customers should be treated as first-class citizens, noting that while historical allocations ranged from 1% to 5%, companies are increasingly discussing allocations of 10% to 20%, reflecting retail's 25% share of the overall equities market.
    * **Customer Engagement:** Verma reported strong retail engagement with deposits up 50% year-over-year.

### AI Agents and Trading Tools
* **Robinhood Agents:** The company launched Robinhood Agents a few months prior, allowing customers to use AI for research and automated trading.
    * **Account Safeguards:** Verma noted that agentic trading accounts are kept completely separate from core investment and retirement accounts, with guardrails ensuring the customer must approve the final intent.
    * **Adoption Metrics:** Approximately 200,000 people currently hold agentic accounts with about $250 million in assets.

### Prediction Markets
* **Revenue and Growth:** Verma reported that Robinhood's prediction market business—just two years old—generates a run rate a little over $500 million in revenue, representing less than 10% of total revenue.
* **Asset Class Breakdown:** Non-sports prediction trades account for about 30% of the platform's volume (up from 15% a quarter prior), driven by midterm election contracts and crypto views.
* **Regulatory Stance:** Verma stated that Robinhood offers these products because they are regulated by the CFTC and aligned with the company's mission of democratized access.

### Midterm Elections & Political Strategy
* **White House & Polling Context:** Bloomberg News White House Correspondent Kate Sullivan discussed President Trump's remarks from the Lincoln Memorial reflecting pool on the eve of the midterm elections, noting that Democrats lead in polls across key battleground states and that Republican candidates are navigating the president's low approval ratings.
* **Data Center Opposition:** Former White House Senior Counselor Satya Thallam discussed how local opposition to data centers has rapidly surged as a top voter concern regarding higher electrical costs and environmental disruption.
* **Congressional Campaign Strategy:** Rep. Michael Lawler (R-NY) discussed his tightly contested race in New York's 17th district, emphasizing local roots, constituent service, and bipartisan housing legislation.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Robinhood Agentic Trading:** Shiv Verma claimed that agentic trading "could be really large" as customer demand for advanced tooling increases.
* **Congressional Tech Regulation:** Satya Thallam predicted that serious tech regulation (such as repealing Section 230) will get "crowded out for at least a year" due to the House focusing heavily on oversight and potential impeachment.
* **Tech Policy Determinants:** Satya Thallam predicted that tech policy will ultimately be determined not by political parties, but by exogenous shocks such as an increase in disruptions and hacking.

### Notable Quotes
* *"We're making sure the companies know retail is here, they are forced to stay, and given they are 25% of the equities market, they should consider a large allocation."* — Shiv Verma
