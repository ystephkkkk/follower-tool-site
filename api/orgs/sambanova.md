## SambaNova CEO Rodrigo Liang & ElevenLabs CEO Mati Staniszewski at Silicon Valley & The World

- Channel: Semafor
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PTYCiUAvxwg
- Published: 2026-11-13
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20261113.html#video-PTYCiUAvxwg
- Speakers: Rodrigo Liang (CEO, SambaNova); Mati Staniszewski (CEO, ElevenLabs); Liz Rappaport (Senior Business Editor, Semafor)

---

### TL;DR
Despite short-term financing and infrastructure headwinds, SambaNova CEO Rodrigo Liang and ElevenLabs CEO Mati Staniszewski agree that AI adoption is accelerating—shifting from experimental hype to measurable ROI, energy-efficient hardware, and sovereign, on-premise deployments. As compute remains constrained, winners will be defined by heterogeneous architectures, smart model orchestration, and differentiated enterprise applications.

### Market Health & The Shift to ROI
* **Healthy Market Strengthening:** Liang characterized any recent AI slowdown (over the last few weeks or months) as a necessary "market strengthening" in financing and data center strategies that will build a stable, scalable AI economy without altering the long-term trajectory.
* **Three Customer Tiers (Liang):**
  * **Neo-clouds & Hyperscalers:** Experiencing massive traffic acceleration; primary focus is securing capacity, power, financing, and energy efficiency (especially following recent US elections).
  * **Governments (Sovereign AI):** Ramping up infrastructure buildouts to ensure national independence.
  * **Enterprises (Banks, Pharma):** Just getting started after two years of experimentation, transitioning from using AI to *save* money to using AI to *gain* market share and protect margins.
* **Model Orchestration for ROI (Staniszewski):** Moving past "adopting AI for adoption's sake," enterprises must now achieve "maximum outcome for minimum token spent" by matching tasks to the right model:
  * Simple product inquiries can rely on open-source or fine-tuned models.
  * High-stakes tasks (e.g., authenticating users and reviewing medical records) require reliable frontier models.
* **Enterprise Proof Points:** Staniszewski highlighted clear ROI at major enterprises including **Deutsche Telekom** and **T-Mobile** (deploying agents across customer experience and internal networks), as well as digital natives like **Stripe**.

### Compute Constraints, Energy, & Infrastructure
* **Short-Term Compute Crunch:** Staniszewski confirmed that ElevenLabs remains compute constrained in the short term, particularly as companies look to fine-tune and own their own models to extend their competitive moats.
* **The Three Pillars of Compute:** Liang noted that winning the fight for compute requires chips, data centers, and financing, alongside creative architectural shifts:
  * **Disaggregated Inference:** Splitting inferencing workloads across mixed architectures (e.g., pairing **SambaNova** with **Nvidia**) to drive significantly higher throughput from existing infrastructure.
  * **Hardware Efficiency:** Liang noted that pairing SambaNova's **M50 chip** with an Nvidia system generates more output using only **10% more power**.
* **Grid Limits & Data Center Reclamation:**
  * Governments are slowing approvals for new gigawatt, liquid-cooled data centers due to community pushback, while global grids are already strained (e.g., **Japan** and **Singapore** are at **98%** grid capacity).
  * Consequently, the industry is shifting toward reclaiming smaller, air-cooled existing data centers where power and cooling already exist, minimizing community disruption.

### Security, Repatriation, & Global Adoption
* **Repatriation of Computing:** Liang identified a major wave of enterprises and governments with sensitive IP bringing workloads back on-premise behind firewalls tested for **30 years**, using air-cooled infrastructure to maintain full control over data and privately trained models amid regulatory uncertainty.
* **Public Sector Trust & Transparency:** Staniszewski emphasized that government voice AI requires strict transparency, including smart watermarking so citizens know they are speaking with technology:
  * **Government of Ukraine:** Built a central crisis-information app that is now evolving into a citizen-support agent for travel, healthcare, and education.
  * **City of Midlands, Texas:** Deployed multilingual voice AI (including Spanish) so residents and the elderly can ask questions directly without navigating complex government websites.
* **Europe's Applied AI Opportunity:** Staniszewski—whose company grew from a **5-person team** (backed early by risk-taking Silicon Valley customers) to over **1,000 people**—hopes the EU and UK will leverage public healthcare, social benefits, and education systems to lead in applied AI, provided European companies embrace greater risk-taking.

### IPOs & Long-Term Strategy
* **Fundamentals Over Hype:** Addressing **Anthropic's** upcoming IPO, Liang stated that while he is cheering for Anthropic's success, SambaNova is focused on building a solid business with "real value, real dollars, and real growth" before considering an exit.
* **The Next Frontier:** Staniszewski echoed a long-term focus, noting that as intelligence becomes widely accessible, the next major challenge is designing how humans interact with AI to bring it to everyone.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Enterprise Acceleration:** Enterprises will see an "incredible acceleration" in the **next year** as companies deploy differentiated AI services to protect their margins — *Rodrigo Liang*
* **Data Center Shift:** New gigawatt liquid-cooled data centers will face delays, driving a wave of reclaiming smaller, existing air-cooled data centers — *Rodrigo Liang*
* **Heterogeneous & On-Premise Growth:** The industry will continue moving toward heterogeneous chips, models, and deployments, alongside the repatriation of computing back on-premise — *Rodrigo Liang*
* **Compute Constraints:** The AI industry will remain compute constrained in the short term — *Mati Staniszewski*
* **Government AI Agents:** Citizen-support AI agents (modeled on Ukraine's deployment) will become a much bigger pattern globally, provided safety and trust are maintained — *Mati Staniszewski*

## How SambaNova Is Challenging Nvidia With High Performance Inferencing Technology

- Channel: Forbes
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6xEnDREk3b8
- Published: 2026-04-16
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260416.html#video-6xEnDREk3b8
- Speakers: Rodrigo Liang (CEO and Cofounder, SambaNova)

---

**TL;DR**
SambaNova is challenging NVIDIA’s GPU dominance by utilizing a proprietary "data flow" architecture optimized for high-speed, low-power AI inference, specifically targeting the shift toward autonomous agentic workflows and sovereign AI infrastructure.

### Data Flow Architecture vs. Legacy GPUs
SambaNova CEO Rodrigo Liang argues that while GPUs were essential for the "training" phase of AI, they are fundamentally inefficient for the "inference" phase (production use).

*   **Architectural Limitations:** GPUs (Graphics Processing Units) are legacy chips designed for gaming and video; data flow chips are designed natively for how neural networks move data.
*   **Performance Metrics:** SambaNova’s technology achieves 5x to 10x faster inference performance than traditional GPUs.
*   **Power Efficiency:** A standard NVIDIA rack consumes roughly 140 kilowatts, necessitating nuclear-scale power support; SambaNova’s solution provides higher performance at 10 kilowatts per rack.
*   **The "Slow Internet" Analogy:** Liang posits that once users experience "fast inference," there will be zero market demand for "slow inference" provided by traditional hardware.

### The Shift to "Agentic" Workflows
The hardware requirements for AI are evolving from supporting human-to-AI interactions to supporting complex, serial chains of AI agents.

*   **Agent Latency Requirements:** Humans typically have a two-second patience threshold for AI responses. In a "serial agent chain" (e.g., a voice agent passing data to a categorization agent, then to a search agent), each individual step must complete in ~0.2 seconds.
*   **Beyond Human Reading Speeds:** While critics argue SambaNova produces text faster than humans can read, Liang explains the speed is intended for "reading bots" that process vast amounts of data instantaneously to execute complex tasks.
*   **Integrated Solutions:** To support these agents, SambaNova is partnering with Intel to combine high-speed inference with necessary storage, networking, and systems of record.

### Sovereign AI and Global Market Traction
SambaNova is seeing significant adoption among nations seeking "Sovereign AI"—the ability to run national-level AI models within their own borders without relying on American hyperscalers.

*   **Infrastructure Compatibility:** Many countries lack gigawatt-scale data centers. SambaNova’s chips can run in standard air-cooled data centers (which typically cap at 30 kilowatts per rack).
*   **National Security and Culture:** Sovereign AI allows countries to:
    *   Keep sensitive government and healthcare data "air-gapped" from the public internet.
    *   Train models on regional laws, history, and traditions rather than relying on US-centric datasets.
*   **Key Partnerships:** The company has already announced sovereign cloud deployments in the UK, Germany, Japan (via SoftBank), Australia, and France (via OVH Cloud).

### Strategic Growth and Future Outlook
Despite raising $1.4 billion in capital, SambaNova is not aiming to become a cloud provider, but rather a technology enabler for service providers.

*   **Commodity vs. Premium Tokens:** Liang views NVIDIA chips as providers of "commodity tokens" where margins are thin. SambaNova aims to provide "premium tokens" for real-time, private, and secure AI services.
*   **Capital Deployment:** The recent $350 million funding round is dedicated to accelerating the chip roadmap; the company recently announced its fifth-generation chip.
*   **The 6-Month Prediction:** Liang predicts that within six months, the transition to personal agentic fleets—AI that books reservations, writes emails in a user's specific voice, and manages productivity—will be in the hands of everyday users.
