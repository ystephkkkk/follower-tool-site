## The Trillion Dollar Question: Financing AI's Infrastructure | Modular, IREN & More | RAISE 2026

- Speakers: Tim Davis (Representative, Modular); Kent Draper (Representative, IREN); Jeremie Eliahou Ontiveros (Moderator, SemiAnalysis)

### TL;DR
AI infrastructure is experiencing an unprecedented capital-intensive buildout driven by massive demand-supply imbalances, requiring creative financing structures like investment-grade debt, asset-backed leases, prepayments, and behind-the-meter nuclear power uprates. To de-risk this trillions-dollar investment, the industry is increasingly leveraging vertical integration, software-enabled compute fungibility, and multi-tenant platforms to balance long-term infrastructure supply with short-term token demand.

### Panelists & Participants
* **Moderator:** Jeremie Eliahou Ontiveros (SemiAnalysis)
* **Tim Davis:** Co-founder, Modular (recently acquired by Qualcomm; building a unified hardware abstraction layer across silicon)
* **Kent Draper:** Chief Commercial Officer, IREN (vertically integrated AI cloud service owning GPUs and data centers)
* **Erwan Menard:** Crusoe (vertically integrated AI factory building gigawatt-scale data center campuses and providing megawatts, GPU hours, and tokens)
* **Wayne Nelms:** CTO & Co-founder, Orn (compute exchange aggregating excess compute capacity and serving smaller frontier labs/inference providers)
* **James B. Krellenstein:** CEO & Co-founder, Alva Energy (nuclear energy technology provider enabling gigawatt-scale project-financed power uprates)

### Financing Structures & Capital Intensity
* **Capital Intensity and Debt Structuring:** IREN notes that top-tier GPUs in liquid-cooled data centers require capital commitments of **$40 to $50 million per megawatt**, and IREN raised **$14 billion in the 12 months leading up to May 2026**. IREN secured an investment-grade rated debt instrument (blended cost of **3%**) for a five-year Microsoft contract for GB200s, funding **96%** of the capital commitment via prepayments and debt.
* **Residual Value and Post-Contract Valuation:** Lenders are increasingly willing to attribute value to the post-contracted period of data center assets (which have **20+ year useful lives**, compared to shorter-lived GPU cycles), allowing providers to borrow significantly more than what is covered by initial contracts.
* **Sub-Investment Grade Counterparties:** Capital providers are showing a significantly increased willingness to fund against sub-investment grade counterparties (smaller AI labs and enterprises), opening up broader end-customer bases despite shorter-term token-based models.

### Energy Sourcing & Nuclear Solutions
* **Grid Load Growth:** Alva Energy highlights that **50% of electric power load growth in the United States** is currently coming from data centers, representing a structural shift not seen since Thomas Edison founded the industry **100 years ago**.
* **Hyperscaler Nuclear PPAs:** Major tech companies have secured large nuclear power purchase agreements:
  * **Amazon:** Partnered with Talen for the Susquehanna nuclear plant.
  * **Microsoft:** Committed to buying **800+ megawatts electrical** from Three Mile Island Unit 1 for **15+ years at ~$116/MWh** tied to CPI.
  * **Meta:** Procured **440 megawatts electrical** of uprates from Vistra through **2035**.
  * **Google:** Bringing back Duane Arnold Unit 1 in Iowa.
* **Project-Financed Nuclear Uprates:** Alva Energy’s technology can operate existing pressurized water reactors to add **200 to 300 megawatts electrical per reactor** across **41 U.S. sites**. Unlike corporate-debt-reliant utility models, Alva's approach achieves **100% firm fixed-price EPC contracts** for project financing without relying on utility rate bases, bypassing new licensing regulatory bottlenecks on a **3 to 5 year timeline**.

### Vertical Integration & Compute Monetization
* **Vertical Integration Strategy:** Crusoe builds gigawatt-scale data center campuses (for themselves and hyperscalers like OpenAI and Microsoft) to maintain control over delivery schedules, margins, and reliability. 
* **Monetization Tiers:** Crusoe monetizes capacity via three distinct models with varying margin profiles:
  1. Selling megawatts (data center real estate/power infrastructure).
  2. Selling GPU hours (clusters).
  3. Selling tokens (managed inference via API endpoints with SLAs).
* **Open-Demand and Pricing Volatility:** Orn tracks on-demand compute pricing, noting that H100 pricing dropped to roughly **$70 in fall 2025** but rebounded to **$2.50**. 

### Software, Fungibility, and Heterogeneous Compute
* **The Software Moat:** Modular notes that the primary moat for incumbent silicon (Nvidia/AMD) is software (e.g., CUDA), and debt underwriting today heavily favors Nvidia/AMD due to liquidity and software lock-in.
* **Compute Heterogeneity:** Workloads are shifting from raw GPU inference to agentic long-running workloads requiring CPU orchestration, pre-fill/decode disaggregation, and memory scaling (**memory prices have 12x'd in the last year**). 
* **Hardware Abstraction:** Modular's software aims to make compute fungible across multiple silicon types (including Qualcomm's platform post-acquisition), enabling data center providers to decouple from single-vendor hardware locks.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Tim Davis (Modular):** Software will become the primary mechanism to radically change the underlying financing of the entire industry by driving compute fungibility across diverse silicon.
* **James B. Krellenstein (Alva Energy):** Nuclear power uprates will deploy on a concrete timeline of **3 to 5 years**, while brand-new nuclear builds will take **10-year (decade) timelines**.


## State of AI Infrastructure: What's Powering AI Today | IREN, Mirantis & NVIDIA | RAISE Summit 2026

- Channel: RAISE Summit
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XzXSzeeWLSY
- Published: 2026-07-21
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260721.html#video-XzXSzeeWLSY
- Speakers: Kent Draper (Chief Commercial Officer, IREN); Alex Freedland (Co-Founder & CEO, Mirantis); Dion Harris (Senior Director, HPC & AI Hyperscale Infrastructure, NVIDIA)

---

### TL;DR
Energy constraints and physical data center buildouts are the fundamental bottlenecks of the AI era, requiring deep vertical integration from energy to orchestration to turn power into intelligence. The industry is rapidly moving beyond bare-metal hyperscaling toward fully orchestrated, open-standard AI clouds that can democratize access for smaller AI labs and enterprises.

### State of the AI Infrastructure Race
* **The Energy Bottleneck:** Energy generation, transmission, and watt-level efficiency are the primary physical constraints on AI growth, creating a massive gap between real-world infrastructure timelines and digital demand cycles.
* **The Three Layers of AI Compute:** IREN frames the stack across three tiers: (1) physical infrastructure (data centers, power, substations), (2) the compute layer (GPUs, backend networking, storage), and (3) software/services that unlock the first two layers without capping overall capability.
* **Vertical Integration Advantage:** IREN operates as a vertically integrated AI cloud service provider, owning both the underlying data centers and compute layer to control scaling and improve time-to-power without third-party general contractor dependencies.
* **European Expansion & Sovereignty:** IREN entered the European market via a recent acquisition of a Spanish data center platform, capitalizing on heavy North American hyperscaler demand and strong European requirements for data sovereignty and security.

### Software, Orchestration & the AI Cloud
* **The New Operating System for Intelligence:** Just as traditional computers required an OS, modern AI clouds act as an "intelligence efficiency game," requiring sophisticated orchestration to match infrastructure capacity precisely to workload demands.
* **NVIDIA's Platform Strategy:** NVIDIA operates as an AI infrastructure platform rather than just a chip maker, exposing APIs (such as the DSX platform and DSX OS) to independent software vendors and partners like Mirantis to maximize performance-per-watt.
* **Shifting Consumption Models:** While historical hyperscalers (Microsoft, NVIDIA) consumed compute on a bare-metal basis, the expanding enterprise and smaller AI lab markets require fully orchestrated platforms rather than bare metal.
* **The "Offtake API" Standard:** Mirantis and NVIDIA are helping define an open standard for how AI is consumed—comparable to what AWS APIs achieved 20ovenants ago—through programs like the AI-ready cloud ISV program.

### The Role of Open Source in AI Infrastructure
* **Foundation of Infrastructure:** Open-source architectures (Linux, Kubernetes, PyTorch, Slurm) have dominated infrastructure for the past 20 years because they allow the ecosystem to keep pace with rapid innovation cycles.
* **Ecosystem Ingestion at Scale:** Mirantis (utilizing its open-source-first product, Cordant) integrates advancements rapidly by combining its orchestration platform with IREN's tens of thousands (moving toward hundreds of thousands) of NVIDIA GPUs.
* **Reference Implementations:** The partnership aims to build reference implementations that democratize customer access and use cases, ensuring that deployments can be replicated across the broader industry.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Future Scaling Capacity:** "6 gigawatt pipeline is only the beginning... and all of that is going to be available to everybody." — Alex Freedland
