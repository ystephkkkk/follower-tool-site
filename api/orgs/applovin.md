## Adam Foroughi, Applovin CEO: Surviving a 92% Drawdown, Ads as ML 1.0 & the $50B Game Ad Market

### TL;DR
AppLovin CEO Adam Foroughi details how the mobile gaming ad network transformed from a regression model to a deep learning engine, generating roughly $6 billion in cash this year with 84% EBITDA margins by successfully scaling performance-based advertising and aggressive share buybacks.

### Company Overview & Market Dynamics
* **Core Business Model:** AppLovin operates as an ad platform monetizing mobile game developers, helping them match users with relevant content and driving app-to-app intent.
* **Massive Market Scale:** 
  * Over 1 billion adults play mobile casual games daily.
  * The mobile gaming ad spend market is estimated at roughly **$50 billion** annually.
  * AppLovin's platform platform ad spend grew 60% year-over-year from a baseline disclosed nearly two years ago of $11 billion.
* **Expansion into E-Commerce:** Deep learning models have advanced to the point where AppLovin can take adult gamers and deliver shopper behavior experiences, tapping into much larger economies.

### Advertising & Machine Learning Evolution
* **ML 1.0:** Advertising served as the original implementation of deep learning models and recommendation systems, laying the groundwork for modern AI trajectories.
* **Discovery vs. Search:** 
  * Bottom-funnel search advertising (like Google) competes where consumer intent is already established.
  * Discovery advertising (used by Meta and AppLovin) creates intent for products consumers didn't know existed, generating genuine economic expansion and GDP growth.
* **Privacy & Ad Relevance:** Despite privacy rule changes (such as Apple's tracking restrictions) and initial user resistance, technology adapted via deep learning to serve relevant ads that consumers engage with via rewards and previews.

### Financial Turnaround & Capital Allocation
* **The 2021–2023 Tumble:** 
  * AppLovin went public in April 2021 at a **$28 billion** market cap ($600M EBITDA).
  * Due to supply overhang from private investors and lack of blue-chip research during the SPAC/IPO boom, the stock plummeted to a **$3.8 billion** market cap in 2022 despite generating **$1 billion** in EBITDA that year.
* **Becoming Their Own Best Investor:** 
  * Realizing the market was mispricing the business, Foroughi halted investor relations and launched an aggressive buyback program.
  * The company bought roughly **$6 billion** of its own stock, retiring 20% to 25% of shares outstanding.
* **The Re-engagement Surge:** When Foroughi returned to New York to meet investors in September 2023 after launching an ML 2.0 deep learning model in April 2023, the stock jumped from $80 to $150 in a single week.

### Operating Strategy, Margins & Global Engineering
* **Lean Execution:** Maintaining a lean, hyper-focused team of subject matter experts allows AppLovin to move faster than tech giants.
* **Margin Profile:** AppLovin boasts an industry-leading **84% EBITDA margin**, driven by automated, algorithmically focused performance arbitrage where advertisers cover consumer acquisition costs immediately upon transaction.
* **Global Talent Footprint:** Engineering offices are distributed across Palo Alto, Singapore, and Beijing, tapping into elite, hardworking international engineering talent.

## Inside AppLovin’s $100B Ad Engine

- Channel: Sourcery with Molly O'Shea
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZHwulZQixQ
- Published: 2026-08-14
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260814.html#video-IZHwulZQixQ
- Speakers: Molly O'Shea (Host, Sourcery); Adam Foroughi (CEO, AppLovin); Giovanni Ge (CTO, AppLovin)

---

#### TL;DR
Driven by its Axon 2 recommendation engine, AppLovin transformed from a struggling post-IPO company into a $100B+ ad tech powerhouse. Co-Founder & CEO Adam Foroughi and CTO Giovanni Ge explain how they achieved this with a remarkably lean engineering team and an AI-first approach.

### Company Origin and Lean Philosophy
* **Early Profitability Roots:** **Adam Foroughi** notes that AppLovin started with around 10 people and stayed very lean because early capital was difficult to raise, forcing the company to be profitable from the beginning.
* **Individual Contributor Culture:** **Adam Foroughi** shares that up until CTO Giovanni Ge joined, early lead engineer Basil wrote roughly 60% of the company's code himself, establishing a culture driven by individual contributors rather than heavy meetings, management frameworks, or standard HR processes.
* **Lean Headcount Stability:** **Adam Foroughi** states that the core engineering organization has remained around 100 people and total company headcount around 400 for years, even through periods of massive business expansion.

### Re-Architecting the Ad Engine: Axon 2
* **Overcoming Outdated Infrastructure:** **Adam Foroughi** explains that AppLovin was a $5.5B company in late 2022 relying on the outdated Axon 1 model, prompting them to recruit researcher **Giovanni Ge** to architect Axon 2 just as the stock dropped 30% on earnings day.
* **Semantic Embeddings vs. Trees:** **Giovanni Ge** details how Axon 2 replaced legacy tree-based models (hundreds of thousands of rigid if-else branches that failed to capture dynamic real-world relationships) with learnable semantic embedding tables and deep neural networks.
* **GPU Hardware Optimization:** **Giovanni Ge** notes that neural networks utilize standard Generalized Matrix Multiplications (GEMM), which are highly optimized for modern GPU architecture compared to selection trees, making Axon 2 both more accurate and cheaper to run.
* **The Immersive Ad Funnel:** **Adam Foroughi** highlights that mobile gaming ads function like 60-second interactive mini-games, generating massive engagement data from a user base of roughly 1 billion people before closing the loop at the conversion funnel.

### Scaling Beyond Gaming into E-commerce
* **Vertical Expansion:** **Adam Foroughi** and **Giovanni Ge** explain that a year after scaling Axon 2 in gaming, they realized the core neural architecture was general enough to handle other verticals, successfully launching into e-commerce ads.
* **The Napkin Design Session:** **Giovanni Ge** recounts how CTO Basil and senior leaders sketched the e-commerce data flow engine on a restaurant napkin in Vegas, building the first prototype before returning to San Francisco, resulting in positive ROAS within months.
* **Product-Less Engineering:** **Adam Foroughi** emphasizes that AppLovin operates without a traditional product management organization; instead, competent engineers understand business problems, write their own architecture, and rely on business teams solely for advertiser relationships.

### AI Integration and Engineering Management
* **Engineers on Top of AI:** **Giovanni Ge** states his philosophy that engineers should not sit next to AI, but rather *on top of* AI, taking human accountability for code while letting AI handle syntax, completion, and generation.
* **Junior Talent Acceleration:** **Giovanni Ge** and **Adam Foroughi** criticize the tech industry practice of handholding junior engineers with low-level tasks, noting that AppLovin allows new hires to push code and run live tests within their first week.
* **The Value of Taste:** **Giovanni Ge** argues that as AI commoditizes building, **taste**—knowing what to build and what *not* to build—becomes the ultimate differentiator, attributing Axon's success largely to what they deliberately decided not to do.

### Market Recovery and Future Vision
* **Surviving a 92% Drop:** **Adam Foroughi** details how AppLovin survived a 92% stock decline post-IPO by keeping headcount low to avoid excessive stock-based dilution, halting investor relations, generating strong cash flow, and aggressively buying back their own shares.
* **Evaluating Future Expansion:** **Adam Foroughi** outlines potential 3-to-10-year R&D horizons, such as connected TV and open web video placements, where discovery-based advertising can replicate their success outside of mobile gaming.
* **Path to a Trillion-Dollar Valuation:** **Adam Foroughi** calculates that reaching a trillion-dollar valuation requires scaling free cash flow past $30 billion annually, necessitating continuous expansion beyond gaming into broader consumer and e-commerce markets.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Giovanni Ge** predicts that while AI currently assists with coding and questioning, human engineers will remain essential for long-horizon planning and making critical architectural decisions for the foreseeable future.
* **Adam Foroughi** projects a 5-plus year time horizon for AppLovin to expand its discovery-based advertising model into new spaces like connected TV and open web video placements.
* **Adam Foroughi** estimates that to achieve a trillion-dollar valuation, AppLovin must scale its free cash flow to over $30 billion annually by expanding its addressable market beyond mobile gaming into broader consumer verticals.
