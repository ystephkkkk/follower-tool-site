## NYSE Texas President & AT&T on Dual Listings

- Channel: Bloomberg Live
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D42y7FWFjkA
- Published: 2026-10-27
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20261027.html#video-D42y7FWFjkA
- Speakers: Bryan Daniel (President, NYSE Texas); Brett Feldman (Senior Vice President, Treasurer & Head of Investor Relations, AT&T); Dani Burger (Host, Bloomberg)

---

**TL;DR:** AT&T and NYSE Texas highlight the strategic advantages of dual listings and Texas's booming capital markets, positioning the region as a growing hub for corporate headquarters, startups, and established enterprises alike.

### The Competitive Exchange Landscape in Texas
* **Market Dynamics:** 
  * **Exchange Competition:** Bryan Daniel notes that while competition among exchanges in Dallas and Texas is fierce, it mirrors historical regional competition and drives innovation rather than constituting an all-out "exchange war."
  * **Rival Exchange Activity:** A handful of companies (including Energy Transfer, Sunoco, and Dillard's) have transferred primary listings to rival exchanges, while Schwab maintains a secondary listing. Daniel emphasizes that issuers make independent decisions based on individual relationships and needs.
* **NYSE Texas Scale:** 
  * **Current Footprint:** NYSE Texas currently boasts **133** dual listings, comprising a mix of corporate issuers and ETFs.
  * **Proven Infrastructure:** Bryan Daniel stresses that NYSE Texas leverages a 200+ year-old battle-tested computing and trading model, distinguishing it from early-stage startup exchanges relying on regulatory cash incentives and fee credits.

### AT&T's Dual Listing and Milestone Year
* **Founding Class Participation:** 
  * **No-Brainer Decision:** Brett Feldman shares that AT&T joined NYSE Texas as part of its founding class because the dual-listing process required zero cost, no incremental obligations, and minimal paperwork with full support from the NYSE group.
  * **Landmark History:** The move coincides with AT&T's milestone year: the **150th** anniversary of the first phone call (celebrated at the NYSE) and the **125th** year of AT&T's listing on the NYSE.
* **Strategic Rationale for Dual Listings:**
  * **Amplified Footprint:** Bryan Daniel explains that dual listings allow established companies to maintain their primary New York Stock Exchange experience while enhancing their visibility, workforce, customer base, or investor presence specifically in Texas.
  * **Anticipating the Hub:** Brett Feldman notes that while the exact long-term financial benefits are still unfolding, AT&T wanted to get in on the front end of Texas becoming a major listing hub.

### Corporate Governance and State Regulatory Environment
* **NYSE Philosophy:** 
  * **Board Autonomy:** Bryan Daniel states that the NYSE group has never attempted to dictate corporate governance outcomes, leaving those decisions entirely up company boards and C-suites.
* **Texas Regulatory Appeal:**
  * **Lighter Touch:** Discussions surrounding state-level regulatory environments focus on offering companies maximum freedom to select structures that work best for their customers and communities.
  * **AT&T's Stance:** Brett Feldman notes that AT&T remains happy with its current governance structure and has no immediate plans for changes.

### Texas Economic Investment and Real Estate
* **AT&T's Local Footprint:**
  * **Financial Commitment:** AT&T invests approximately **$23 billion a year** globally into its network. Over the last **5 years**, the company has invested **$13 billion** specifically in Texas.
  * **Network Reach:** AT&T connects **6 million locations** in Texas to its networks, with fiber network access reaching nearly half of the state. 
  * **Infrastructure Scale:** The company has installed enough fiber optic cabling in Texas to circle the Earth **800 times**.
  * **Headquarters Relocation:** AT&T is moving **6,000 employees** north to a new horizontal facility in the Dallas suburbs to build an optimal workspace while staying close to its roots.
* **Venture Capital and Startup Ecosystem:**
  * **Life Cycle Support:** Brett Feldman highlights that Dallas is no longer just a destination for corporate HQs escaping regulatory structures; events like Venture Dallas show that founders and venture capital are establishing a full lifecycle from startup to public offering.

### Notable Quotes
> *"The value proposition, as I see it, is... you don't have to choose between two locations. You don't have to leave one to come to the other. I do think it's very conceivable that you can do both."* — **Bryan Daniel**

> *"Why wouldn't we want to get it on the front end of this? I don't know what the benefit is going to be today, but as this becomes a listing hub... being here longer and having access to that market longer... is going to prove to be a good thing for you."* — **Brett Feldman**


## AT&T CEO on Earnings, Mobile Subscriber Gains, AI

- Channel: Bloomberg Television
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xkfinAbtuU
- Published: 2026-07-22
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260722.html#video-8xkfinAbtuU
- Speakers: John Stankey (CEO, AT&T)

---

#### TL;DR
AT&T reported a strong second quarter, adding 432,000 monthly wireless phone subscribers and a record 370,000 fiber net adds, driven by multi-year capital investments and a successful convergence strategy. CEO John Stankey expressed confidence in sustaining this momentum through 2028 while highlighting the company's critical infrastructure role in the upcoming AI era.

### Financial Results & Q2 Performance
* **Subscriber Growth:** 
  * **Wireless Additions:** Added **432,000** monthly wireless phone subscribers in Q2, beating analyst expectations of **325,000**.
  * **Fiber Net Adds:** Reached a record **370,000** fiber net adds during the quarter.
* **Broadband & Mobile Convergence:** 
  * **Convergence Rate:** **42.5%** of households purchasing broadband also buy mobile phone service from AT&T (rising to **45%** when normalized for the recently acquired Lumen footprint).
  * **Customer Benefits:** Multi-product customers are more lucrative, exhibit lower churn, and show higher brand affinity.
* **Financial Metrics:** 
  * Growth manifested in accelerated service revenue growth and accelerated EBITDA growth.
  * Achieved growth in business strategic services for the first time in a long period.
  * Margin performance reached record levels for the company.

### Market Positioning & Competitive Landscape
* **The Convergence Strategy:** Providing world-class networks in both wireless and fixed broadband covers **98%** of a customer's internet needs on a single bill with unified support infrastructure.
* **Pricing and Market Stratification:** 
  * Rejecting the "price war" characterization, Stankey described the market as stratified.
  * AT&T traditionally played well upmarket with high-performing symmetrical gigabit services and robust wireless plans.
  * Recent focus on value-oriented offerings yielded new account additions at a **three-year high**, improving down-market capture without sacrificing margins.
* **Competitive Moats (Urban vs. Rural):** 
  * Satellite services (like Starlink) and cable companies dominate less densely populated and rural areas.
  * AT&T's historical investments focus on dense urban and suburban metro areas, making it difficult for competitors to replicate hard-to-reach infrastructure (such as stadiums).

### Artificial Intelligence & Network Infrastructure
* **AI ROI and Internal Efficiency:** 
  * Disciplined AI investments in software development, customer service channels, engineering applications, and pricing support algorithms are delivering strong returns.
  * Differentiated between table-stakes efficiencies (which get competed away into lower prices or better service) and strategic advantages (like pricing and network yield software).
* **AI Era Readiness:** 
  * Symmetrical networks (offering as much upstream bandwidth as downstream bandwidth) are viewed as indispensable for future AI workloads, alongside chips, memory, and data centers.

### Capital Allocation & Share Repurchases
* **CapEx Cycle Contrast:** Unlike tech companies currently spending heavily to raise capital and build out AI infrastructure, AT&T has largely completed its multi-year asset investment cycle.
* **Share Buybacks:** The company is accelerating the pace of planned share repurchases.
* **Market Valuation:** Stankey characterized the current market as experiencing a temporary "dislocation" and rotation out of AT&T stock due to capital chasing AI tech opportunities, but believes the market will eventually recognize the value of AT&T's indispensable infrastructure.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Sustained Growth:** AT&T expects its current execution and growth momentum to sustain itself through **2028** — John Stankey.
* **Satellite Partnership Timeline:** AT&T will be able to solve out-of-range connectivity gaps via satellite industry partnerships by **this time next year** — John Stankey.
