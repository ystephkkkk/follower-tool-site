## AeroVironment CEO on the future of drones and defense

- Channel: CNBC Television

**TL;DR** 
AeroVironment is leveraging its massive scale and strategic acquisitions like BlueHalo to lead a paradigm shift in global drone warfare, space-based communications, and stratospheric solar flight while addressing critical military and civilian defense gaps.

---

### Leadership in the Global Drone Market and Scale of Operations
* **Unprecedented Global Footprint:** AeroVironment is the largest producer of military drones in the U.S., with over 60,000 to 70,000 active systems deployed globally across more than 55 countries.
* **Robust Financials and Production Scale:** The company generates approximately $2 billion in annual revenue, with 90% of its product portfolio currently in full-rate production. 
    * **International Revenue:** Historically, international markets account for 40% to 60% of total revenue, though a recent domestic surge from the BlueHalo acquisition has slightly altered that ratio.
* **The "Scale" vs. "Prototype" Distinction:** CEO Wahid Nawabi emphasizes that prototyping represents only 10% of the challenge in defense tech; the remaining 90% lies in manufacturing systems at scale that can survive extreme storage (up to 10 years) and harsh environmental conditions (deserts, water, dust).
* **Office of Strategic Capital (OSC) Alignment:** While the Pentagon’s OSC initiative targets startups lacking capital, AeroVironment remains a highly profitable, self-sustaining prime contractor that does not rely on this funding to scale.

---

### Layered Counter-Drone Defense and the Freedom Eagle One
* **Asymmetric Economic Solutions:** Current warfare economics are unsustainable, requiring $2 million to $10 million military missiles to shoot down cheap $50,000 to $150,000 drones.
* **Freedom Eagle One Kinetic Defeat:** AeroVironment is addressing this economic gap with the *Freedom Eagle One* kinetic defeat missile.
    * **Cost-Efficiency:** Engineered to sit at a $100,000 to $150,000 price point to target Group 1, 2, and 3 drones, with future capabilities extending to missiles and hypersonics.
    * **Army Program of Record:** Developed as part of the U.S. Army's Next-Generation Counter-UAS Missile program, with full-rate production targeted to begin in 1 to 1.5 years at an expanded facility in Huntsville, Alabama.
* **Multi-Layered C-UAS Tech:** The company's defensive suite includes the *Titan* series (jammers acquired via BlueHalo), directed energy laser weapons, and kinetic missiles.
* **The "Golden Dome" Initiative:** A major commercial and civil defense opportunity exists to protect critical domestic infrastructure (nuclear plants, dams, hospitals, airports, and public stadiums) from rogue drone attacks, pending FAA and FCC regulatory shifts.

---

### Deep Space Communications and NASA Mars Exploration
* **Laser Communications Program of Record:** AeroVironment has secured a major program of record for long-haul satellite-to-satellite and satellite-to-ground laser communications, bypassing vulnerable and congested radio frequency (RF) networks.
* **Project Skyfall:** NASA recently announced this program to return to Mars, naming AeroVironment as a key partner. Following the success of the *Ingenuity* Mars helicopter, NASA will deploy multiple AeroVironment-developed drones to scout habitation sites and conduct surveillance.
* **Mars Atmospheric Focus:** The company focuses heavily on Mars over the Moon due to Mars' atmosphere (which, despite being less than 1% of Earth's density, allows for aerodynamic flight).

---

### The Sunglider: Disrupting Stratospheric Flight and 5G Connectivity
* **Unparalleled Aircraft Specs:** The *Sunglider* is a 100% solar-powered stratospheric airplane with a wingspan of over 260 feet (wider than an Airbus A380 double-decker).
* **Alternative to LEO Satellites:** Developed in partnership with Japan's SoftBank Telecom, the aircraft is designed to compete directly with satellite systems like Starlink to deliver 5G/4G connectivity to remote populations.
* **Performance Capabilities:**
    * **High-Altitude Loitering:** It operates at 65,000 feet—well above standard commercial and military air traffic—flying in tight circles to act as a pseudo-stationary satellite.
    * **Military Utility:** Capable of conducting massive land and ocean surveillance at a fraction of the cost of traditional platforms like the Global Hawk, Reaper, or P-8.
    * **Proven Concept:** The technology was successfully demonstrated during COVID-19 via a high-definition, four-way Zoom call routed through the aircraft from Spaceport New Mexico.
