**TL;DR**
Trust must be treated as critical physical infrastructure—like power grids or highways—to safeguard the global economy against "Q-Day," the point between 2028 and 2030 when quantum computers will likely break current RSA encryption and jeopardize all digital sovereignty.

### The Quantum Threat and "Q-Day"
*   **The Q-Day Timeline:** Carlos Moreira projects that by 2028–2030, quantum computers will reach the ~10 million qubit threshold required to break RSA encryption.
    *   **Systemic Vulnerability:** Current financial systems, hospitals, defense departments, and cryptocurrencies rely on 50-year-old RSA/Triple DES encryption that will become "legacy" and "dangerous" post-quantum.
*   **AI vs. Quantum Hype:** Moreira argues that "AI-washing" is currently hiding the real problem; the world is currently in a "quantum state" similar to where AI was five years ago.
    *   **Quantum Security:** 2024 has been dubbed the "year of quantum security" as governments (specifically the US via CNSA 2.0) mandate quantum-resilience by 2027–2030.
*   **The "Dark Side" of Quantum:** While quantum will solve cancer and discover new materials, its ability to train "hostile AI" on Dark Web data could destroy the line between truth and fake news.

### Trust as Infrastructure and Sovereignty
*   **Technological Sovereignty:** The internet is decoupling into vertical silos. Countries are now "bunkerizing" their technology to avoid dependence on foreign nations that could block their economies during disputes.
*   **Europe’s Trillion-Dollar Challenge:** Moreira emphasizes that Europe must build trillion-dollar companies to remain relevant, but the continent remains too fragmented by language and culture to scale as effectively as the US or China.
*   **Local Trust, Global Security:** While security standards can be global, trust is inherently local. Moreira views WISeKey and SEALSQ as "guardians" of this infrastructure, using Swiss neutrality to build a global trust model.

### Redefining Digital Identity (Web 3.0)
*   **Identity as a Birth Certificate:** In the digital world, identity should not be a corporate-owned asset but a decentralized "birth certificate" owned by the individual.
    *   **Consent Mechanism:** True digital identity allows users to provide and retrieve consent at will, rather than being "data-mined" by social media platforms.
*   **The Web 2.0 Failure:** Moreira critiques the current model where users "single sign-on" through platforms like Facebook, effectively losing ownership of their personal identifiable information (PII).
*   **Financial Inclusion:** Using digital identities on $20 mobile phones (as seen in India) allows the unbanked to enter the digital economy via decentralized ledgers that even quantum computers cannot easily hack.

### The Future of Human-Centric Technology
*   **The Last Generation:** Moreira warns that the current generation is the last one capable of ensuring technology remains safe for humans before it reaches autonomous, potentially hostile levels.
*   **"Goodness" as a Metric:** In the next decade, big tech will be judged not by profit but by the "goodness" they create and their ability to unleash human potential rather than control it.
*   **Personal AI Assistants:** To combat "infected data" and brainwashing, individuals will need personal AI agents that understand their specific trust models and protect them from manipulation.
*   **The Machine Economy:** Moreira details the launch of **SealCoin**, a $500 million token project designed to allow "Object Identities" (like cars and charging stations) to transact directly without intermediaries like Visa or Mastercard.
