## Episode 49: Yoni Assia - Co-Founder and CEO of eToro

---

**TL;DR:** Yoni Assia, CEO and co-founder of eToro, details the platform’s evolution from a 2007 startup into a $2.5 billion "investment super app" that leverages social networking and blockchain to democratize capital markets and challenge the opaque user experience of legacy financial institutions.

### The Genesis of eToro: Bridging Finance and Design
* **Founding Philosophy:** Launched in 2007 by Yoni and his brother Ronen, eToro was designed to solve the "horrible user experience" of professional trading terminals. While Yoni brought a background in computer science and market patterns, Ronen brought industrial design expertise to simplify global market access.
* **Legacy Bank Paradigm:** Assia argues that traditional banks (Goldman Sachs, Citi, UBS) deliberately maintain poor app experiences to discourage daily engagement, viewing frequent customer interaction as a cost or a sign of an impending problem. eToro inverted this by aiming for high daily engagement.
* **Early Entrepreneurship:** Before eToro, Assia co-founded **CDRide**, which installed hardened computers on roller coasters to sell on-ride videos.
    * **Key Lesson:** The experience convinced Assia to focus exclusively on software over hardware due to the scalability and reliability issues inherent in hardware development.

### The Social Trading Revolution and CopyTrader
* **Platform Scale:** eToro currently serves over 35 million users across 75+ countries, facilitating over $1 trillion in volume and providing access to 25 different capital markets.
* **CopyTrader Innovation:** Launched in 2010, this feature allows users to automatically replicate the trades of successful investors in real-time.
    * **Pro Investor Program:** Top retail traders on the platform now manage significant assets. For example, eToro’s leading investor has a 12-year track record with a 29% average return and $300 million in assets "copying" him.
* **Investment Super App:** The platform has expanded beyond trading to include multi-currency bank accounts, Visa debit cards, options, and 200+ crypto assets, aiming to be a one-stop-shop for wealth management.

### Market Cycles and the Crypto Pivot
* **2008 Financial Crisis Impact:** Watching the global financial system "pull the plug" in 2008 sharpened Assia’s interest in decentralized finance. He noted that while the internet is 24/7, the financial system still relies on T+2 settlement and opaque Excel-based reporting.
* **Early Crypto Adoption:** Assia was an early pioneer in Bitcoin, authoring papers with Vitalik Buterin in 2012. 
    * **Growth Spurt:** In 2017, eToro saw funded accounts jump from 200 per day to 20,000 per day during the crypto rally, forcing the company to automate its entire backend.
* **GoodDollar Project:** A non-profit initiative born from Assia’s conviction that the financial system's tech stack and wealth distribution are broken. It uses blockchain to explore a more transparent ecosystem for interest and identity.

### Strategic Forecast: Three "Inevitable Truths"
* **Tokenization of Everything:** Assia predicts an "inevitable" shift of $100 trillion to $400 trillion in assets (real estate, bonds, stocks) moving from legacy stacks to 24/7 blockchain-based ledgers over the next 20 years.
* **Generational Wealth Transfer:** As wealth moves to younger generations, product-led companies with superior mobile experiences will replace insurance companies and "old-stack" financial institutions.
* **The Rise of AI Agents:** Assia believes AI will become the primary financial advisor for the next generation.
    * **Autonomous Trading:** He shared a personal experiment where an AI agent (using Claude) successfully opened Ethereum and Solana wallets and executed trades on prediction markets autonomously via text instructions.

### Notable Quotes
> "Financial institutions like Bloomberg or big banks don't really want to improve the user experience because something about the complexity and ugliness of that software is what makes it attractive to professional traders and pushes retail investors out." — **Yoni Assia**

> "To be a great investor means you need to devote the 10,000 hours... if you don't have a coach telling you you're actually good at this, you're going to drop out from doing that sport to actually just watching that sport." — **Yoni Assia**

> "Everything is going to get tokenized. This is $100 trillion moving from one tech stack on the back end to a completely new 24/7 tech stack. This is inevitable." — **Yoni Assia**
