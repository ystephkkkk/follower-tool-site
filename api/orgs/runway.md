## You Can't Tell What's Real Anymore. Here's What Comes Next - Cristóbal Valenzuela, Runway

- Channel: AI Engineer
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q4ITwvlFplQ
- Published: 2026-11-03
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20261103.html#video-q4ITwvlFplQ
- Speakers: Cristóbal Valenzuela (Co-founder and CEO, Runway)

---

### TL;DR
Runway co-founder and CEO Cristóbal Valenzuela explains how scaling next-frame video prediction teaches models real-world physics, enabling interactive world interface models, robotics, and early operating system prototypes like Continuum where any screen content becomes a live simulation.

### Evolution from Pixel Generation to State-of-the-Art Video
* **Prehistoric AI Roots:** Runway's first AI film in 2019, "The Orange," generated pixels at a 128x128 resolution before being forcibly upsampled.
* **Current Generation Quality:** Modern video models generate characters and locations that successfully pass the Turing test, with human evaluators unable to distinguish generated footage from real video.
* **Art and Science Intersection:** The company maintains deep roots in artistic and creative communities alongside a research lab of top scientists specializing in diffusion models and large video models.

### Physics and Simulation via Next-Frame Prediction
* **Cinema as Optical Illusion:** Video functions as an optical illusion created by stitching frames fast enough to produce motion pictures.
* **Emergent World Physics:** Scaling next-frame prediction causes models to inherently learn complex real-world mechanics without explicit programming:
  * **Core Physical Laws:** Models learn gravity, occlusion, and movement.
  * **Object Interactions:** Models simulate how objects interact, such as a camel standing on a ball or water moving inside a container.
* **Simulation Systems without Rules:** By scaling next-frame prediction in 3D environments, models function like physics engines and game systems—such as generating non-GTA interactive virtual worlds—without explicit descriptions of physical laws.

### Real-Time Interaction and World Interface Models
* **From Async to Real-Time:** While traditional generation takes seconds or minutes, the next 3 to 4 years of video models focus on real-time interactivity where users can act at any point during frame prediction.
* **Robotics Integration:** Runway has spun up an entire robotics lab in its New York office to use these video models as the brains for autonomous, embodied systems.
* **World Interface Models:** Images can be transformed into interactive applications (e.g., turning on car lights, opening a hood, or moving objects in a room) without any underlying 3D representation like Unity, Unreal, or Blender.

### Continuum: Video Models as Operating Systems
* **Early Research Prototype:** Continuum integrates video models directly into daily computing, streaming operating system content straight from a video model.
* **Portals and Memory:** Users can select any text on a website and instantly spawn an interactive real-time video "portal" based on the content, while a desktop memory tracks past visualizations.
* **Reactive Spatial Scaling:** Resizing an application window dynamically changes the behavior and visual experience—such as navigating cellular structures during a lesson on photosynthesis.
* **Visual Thinking for Coding Agents:** Connecting Continuum to a coding agent allows the agent to visually think through problems (like generating a 3D model of a bicycle) rather than relying solely on code.
* **Live Reactive Experiences:** Searching for topics like Mars prompts the system to generate a real-time, interactive Mars Outpost interface based on actual and projected data.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Product Release Timeline:** Runway will release most of the Continuum research and simulation capabilities for public use within the **next 8 to 12 months** — Cristóbal Valenzuela.

### Notable Quotes

## Opening Keynote | Runway AI Summit SF 2026

- Channel: Runway
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mV5rte32LBc
- Published: 2026-10-16
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20261016.html#video-mV5rte32LBc
- Speakers: Anastasis Germanidis (Co-Founder, Runway); Kamil Sindi (Co-Founder, Runway); Anthony Maggio (Head of Product, Runway)

---

### TL;DR
Runway announced major expansions across generative video, enterprise marketing, robotics, and interface design at its SF summit, highlighting exponential adoption and introducing products like Runway Ads, Praxis-1, and Solaris.

### Company Growth and Vision for World Models
- **Explosive Business Growth:** Anastasis Germanidis noted that since March, Runway's revenue has tripled, September marked the highest growth month in the company's eight-year history, and model usage has grown nearly 10 times since the beginning of the year.
- **The Shift to World Models:** Runway positions itself as a world model company, arguing that language models are limited by text distilled from human knowledge, whereas video models trained at scale learn directly from raw data and observation to become learned approximators of physics.

### Runway Ads: Autonomous Performance Marketing
- **Product Launch:** **Anthony Maggio** announced **Runway Ads**, an autonomous engine designed to streamline performance marketing by automating the creative production loop.
- **Internal Impact:** Performance marketing campaigns at Runway scaled from launching ~70 new ads per week in July to ~900 ads per week by early September after building the tool.
- **Performance Metrics:** Return on ad spend doubled, cost per subscriber decreased by 41%, and conversion rates increased by 34%.
- **Capabilities & Automation:** Connects to existing ad accounts and brand assets to produce channel-ready creative across multiple languages and audiences; an integrated agent monitors performance, creates variants of winning ads, and publishes directly to platforms like Meta and Google while keeping human teams in control.
- **Early Partners:** Tested with early design partners including Lovable.

### Robotics and Praxis-1
- **The Robotics Bottleneck:** **Andy Chen** highlighted that real-world robot data is scarce, slow, and expensive to collect, while traditional hand-built simulators struggle with contact-rich manipulation and long-tail edge cases.
- **GWM Worlds Validation:** Runway's GWM Worlds model predicts how a robot policy will perform in the real world with a **0.95 correlation**, outperforming expensive 3D reconstruction approaches.
- **Product Launch:** **Praxis-1** is an open-weights world action model that turns video pre-training into control for real robots by learning from third-party video of humans performing tasks rather than relying solely on scarce robot demonstrations.
- **Adaptability:** Works across any embodiment (arms, grippers, robots) with light fine-tuning and a few minutes of demonstrations.
- **Early Partners & Release:** Tested with early partners **Noble Machines**, **Ultra**, and **Standard Bots**; open weights will be released publicly in the coming months.

### Digital World Models, Characters, and Solaris
- **Runway Characters:** **Kamil Sindi** demonstrated Runway Characters, a live, interactive single-player world model featuring a planner and a world model renderer that allows users to create, steer, and give knowledge bases or tool-calling capabilities to avatars via Runway Dev.
- **GWM Worlds 2:** A research preview showcasing multi-player environments where users can spin up shared worlds for humans and agents using natural language "world prompts" without code or game engines.
- **Solaris Interface World Model:** **Solaris** is a new operating system interface world model that generates interactive software interfaces frame by frame in real time, converting static websites into dynamic, intent-driven experiences (e.g., storefronts becoming dressing rooms) without code; available soon via Runway Dev.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
- **Anastasis Germanidis:** By the end of this decade, interactive world models will achieve human-scale world simulation, creating interactive worlds indistinguishable from the real world.
- **Anastasis Germanidis:** Within about 10 years, models will be able to simulate physics and biology well enough to solve major scientific challenges.
- **Andy Chen:** Within five years, most new robot skills will be learned within a world model before a robot ever tries them in the physical world.
