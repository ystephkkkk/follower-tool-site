### TL;DR
OpenAI President and co-founder Greg Brockman discusses the rollout of GPT-6 to over a billion people, the resolution of nearly 400 century-old math problems by AI agents, and how proactive AI tools like "Dots" are transforming productivity and the future of work.

### Scientific Discovery & Mathematical Breakthroughs
- **AI-Generated New Knowledge:** Recent milestones in mathematics demonstrate that AI can definitively generate new knowledge rather than just retrieve it.
  - OpenAI recently published resolved results for nearly 400 significant, open mathematical problems.
  - Domain experts reviewing the proofs note surprising and sophisticated applications of ideas and inter-domain connections rather than brute-force mechanical case-checking (unlike historical proofs like the Four Color Theorem).
- **Application to Biology and Drug Discovery:** Scientific breakthroughs in mathematics and theoretical physics serve as a leading indicator for what is possible in biology, where deep learning physics (compute + data + algorithms) can accelerate discovery.
  - Drug discovery is highlighted as the most critical and impactful application of AI to improve human quality of life.
  - Neural networks are exceptionally well-suited to crack the "language of biology," enabling massive parallelism and dramatic shortening of a traditionally 15-year, $1.5 billion pipeline.
  - Non-biological bottlenecks in the drug pipeline—such as writing regulatory filings and clinical trial documentation—can also be streamlined by AI.

### AI Agents, Form Factors, and the "No UI" Future
- **Proactive Productivity with Dots:** OpenAI's "Dots" is an always-on, always-working proactive agent designed to track uncompleted tasks, monitor Slack and inbox feeds, manage scheduling conflicts, and anticipate user needs.
  - The form factor changes how users work by running continuously in the background even when the user stops working.
- **Consumer vs. Enterprise Market Pressures:** While consumer assistants focus on mass-market scale, free-tier availability (such as ChatGPT's memory features for over 1.2 billion consumers), and features like payments and travel, workplace AI prioritizes peak intelligence, admin controls, security toggles, and paying higher compute costs for higher productivity returns.
- **Dynamic and Invisible User Interfaces:** The ultimate UI for AI is no UI at all. 
  - GPT-6 introduces intelligent, generative UI where the AI crafts custom, bespoke visual interfaces on the fly (such as flight booking options or assembly guides) tailored precisely to the moment and user request.

### Cybersecurity and the Defender's Window
- **The Physics of Cybersecurity:** Cybersecurity dynamics are shifting into a critical "defender's window" where peak frontier AI capabilities allow defenders to proactively find and fix deep-seated historical flaws in human-written code.
- **OpenAI Internal Security Response:** During model releases, OpenAI pauses approximately 25% of its production engineers from feature work, assigning them exclusively to use peak frontier models to red-team and secure infrastructure until vulnerabilities are saturated.
- **Tech Debt and Code Resilience:** Decades of internet infrastructure built for resilience rather than security have left immense technical debt (highlighted by single-maintainer open source vulnerabilities). AI enables formal verification of software and the generation of secure-by-default code from inception.

### Human Agency and the Future of AGI
- **Keeping Humans in the Driver's Seat:** OpenAI's core mission remains ensuring AGI benefits all of humanity by keeping humans in control and amplifying human agency.
  - Models are jagged and surpass humans in specific domains, but human judgment, values, and oversight function similarly to a CEO or board member directing specialized teams of domain experts.
- **Alignment via Debate:** Techniques like debate and iterative amplification (conceptually discussed since 2017) train AIs to provide verifiable arguments and counter-arguments, ensuring humans can easily oversee complex outputs without needing to manually verify every detail.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
- **Cyber vs. Software Spend:** Security spend should vastly outstrip software spend as software becomes cheap and commoditized, making security, reliability, and resilience the true drivers of value — Greg Brockman

### TL;DR
Jason from OpenAI’s developer experience team explains how moving from single-player AI agents to collaborative, multiplayer systems—powered by persistent cloud agents called "dots," shared spaces, app shots, and voice integration—prevents individual burnout and distributes work across teams.

### 1. Context Gathering: Meeting You Where You Are
* **App Shots for Instant Context:** 
  * Pressing the spacebar with two command buttons captures a screenshot alongside metadata, URLs, and below-the-fold content from any application.
  * Named as a primary entry point, accounting for roughly 30% of all sessions started in Codex.
* **Persistent Cloud Agents ("Dots"):** 
  * Unlike local automations that stop when a computer shuts down, dots run continuously in the cloud with their own computer, browser, and file environments.
  * They soak up background context from Slack, email, and browsers without requiring manual heartbeats or markdown vaults.
* **Embodied and Multi-Surface Access:** 
  * Dots can operate across phones, browsers, and local machines.
  * Users can enable settings allowing cloud agents to use the local machine as a "last mile" of work (e.g., logging into a browser, testing a local app).

### 2. Voice Integration and Team Collaboration
* **Agentic Voice:** 
  * Allows two-directional voice interaction on mobile and desktop apps, operating roughly 3 to 4 times faster than typing.
  * Enables messy, unscripted brainstorming sessions that the AI cleans up automatically.
* **Meeting Notes and Team Prompts:** 
  * ChatGPT meetings transcribe conversations and allow dots to pick up action items and context automatically.
  * Users can prompt agents mid-call to draft blog posts, create timelines, or send Slack messages.
* **Team Tagging:** 
  * Users can tag their dot in Slack or Teams (e.g., `@Jason dot, please investigate`) to delegate tasks following team design discussions.

### 3. Organizing Work in Shared Spaces
* **Spaces as Cloud Memory Vaults:** 
  * Acts as a single source of truth where agents and team members contribute without duplicating work across departments.
* **Agent MD Files and Page Instructions:** 
  * Directory-based agent MD files guarantee that automated writes remain human-readable with correct citations and formatting.
  * Custom artifacts, embedded HTML, and visualizations can be defined via page instructions to keep team agents aligned.
* **Cross-Departmental Use Cases:** 
  * *Finance:* Weekly analytics reports updated by cloud agents connected to cloud services.
  * *Engineering:* Organizing pull requests, project scope, and identifying designated DRI (Directly Responsible Individual) pages.
  * *Research:* Tracking long-running experiments.

### 4. Sharing Results via Plugins and Extensions
* **Plugins and Extensions:** 
  * Serve as shareable, updatable, and versioned instruction-based plugins distributed widely across teams.
  * Codex app acts as a cockpit; products like the meetings and code review tools are built on these exact plugin components.
* **MCP and WebMCP Integration:** 
  * Sites can serve Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers with authentication.
  * WebMCP allows the creation of tools that both humans and AI can use simultaneously.
* **Broader Creative and Production Workflows:** 
  * Used beyond engineering for production shoots (keeping sets updated during breaks) and content planning with creative and marketing teams.

### Notable Quotes
> "As I got more productive, I felt like at some point I was the glue for all these systems and I felt like I was a bottleneck for a lot of the things I was doing."
> — Jason

> "At some point, your whole team should get involved... The conversations we have with my teammates ultimately become the prompts that we use for our agents that are running in the cloud."

#### TL;DR
Tibo Sottiaux, Head of ChatGPT and Codex at OpenAI, discusses the launch of Dots (OpenAI's new personal agent platform), predicting that the majority of internet actions will soon be executed by autonomous agents that operate 24/7.

---

### 1. Dots & The Personal Agent Vision
- **Primary vs. Multi-Dot Setup:** OpenAI launched the primary dot for pro users to configure, connect to apps, and learn deep preferences; the ability to create multiple dots to form a virtual team for specific tasks (like monitoring Twitter) will follow soon.
- **The Octopus Architecture:** Unlike traditional setups where the harness runs on the local device, dots run on their own hardware (or VMs like Mac Minis) and can connect to and control multiple devices simultaneously.
- **Real-World Incident Response:** Sottiaux shared that during a ChatGPT production outage 5 minutes before his live DevDay demo, his dot pinged him to warn him and offered to try and fix it itself.
- **Frictionless Design:** Dots feature no model picker and no manual configuration; users simply talk to them, and all complex capabilities will eventually be integrated straight into ChatGPT.

### 2. The Shift Beyond Loops, Graphs, and UI
- **Death of Fiddly Workflows:** Setting up and fiddling with loops and graphs is a passing phase; systems should instead learn continuously from user feedback and long-term goals without manual routing.
- **Agent-First Internet:** As the majority of actions on the internet shift to agents, products will need to scale to handle automated traffic, making interface design secondary to API and agent accessibility.
- **Merging Chat and Work Interfaces:** OpenAI is merging the ChatGPT chat and work toggles to reduce complexity and lifting the floor for its 1.2 billion users.

### 3. The Open Ecosystem & Economic Sharing
- **Sign-in with ChatGPT:** OpenAI has secured 16 major partners (such as Notion and Figma) for its "Sign-in with ChatGPT" auth ecosystem.
- **Revenue Sharing:** Popular plugins and integrations that drive genuine user retention and utility will receive a share of subscription revenue.
- **Plugin Recommendation Engine:** OpenAI ranks plugins based on retention and utility rather than traditional SEO or keywords, actively recommending high-performing tools during conversations.

## OpenAI’s Dots lead on the future of ChatGPT

- Channel: Sources Podcast
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VnHOFQE7nCw
- Published: 2026-10-01
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20261001.html#video-VnHOFQE7nCw
- Speakers: Alexander Embiricos (Dots Product Lead, OpenAI)

---

### TL;DR
OpenAI has launched "Dots"—proactive, persistent personal AI agents equipped with their own cloud computers and unified context across apps like Slack and voice—marking the transition into "Active Intelligence" where agents propose actions rather than waiting for prompts.

### The Evolution to Active Intelligence & The "Dots" Form Factor
* **Beyond Passive Chat:** **Alexander Embiricos** noted that while Chat and coding agents are powerful, they require users to drive every action, functioning like a principal engineer who refuses to check Slack or Jira. 
* **The "Dots" Breakthrough:** Named "Active Intelligence" (OpenAI's third major era), Dots are designed to be persistent, operate over long horizons, and handle open-ended tasks (e.g., "Stay on top of this feedback channel").
* **Cloud Computers over Local Control:** OpenAI debated building local agents (like the *slash-goal* feature in Codex) versus cloud-embodied agents; they chose cloud-hosted computers with unique identities so agents can work autonomously without relying on the user's local hardware.
* **Embodiment and Unified Context:** Giving agents identities (e.g., prefixed with user handles like `AE-dot`) makes them intuitive for humans to interact with across multiple surfaces (commute voice calls, Slack threads, and desktop apps) while maintaining a single, unified context.
* **Single Agent vs. Multiple Agents:** OpenAI discovered that letting users create multiple agents with whimsical names caused cognitive overload in shared environments like Slack; most consumers will likely want just one primary agent that feels like an extension of themselves.

### Spaces: Collaborative Workspaces for Humans and Agents
* **Bridging Messaging and Docs:** While messaging works well for quick chats, long-form outputs require a dedicated interface. OpenAI built **Space**, a collaborative workspace designed for equal-weight human and agent collaboration.
* **Agentic Workflows:** Users can continuously tag their Dot in a document to pull data, flesh out sections, check off completed tasks, or ping teammates.
* **HTML & Agent-First Design:** Unlike traditional documents optimized strictly for human typing, Spaces leverage modern HTML and CSS to host live interactive charts, data visualizations, and clickable UI prototypes that agents can keep up to date.

### Safety, Trust, and Permission Architecture
* **Conservative Rollout:** To ensure Dots are the most capable and trustworthy agents, OpenAI initially rolled them out strictly to Pro, Business, and Enterprise tiers (their most AI-literate audiences) with highly conservative default behaviors.
* **Default Confirmation Policy:** By default, Dots perform proactive research using connected tools but **never take action without user confirmation** (e.g., asking before moving a conflicting calendar meeting or purchasing an item).
* **Defense-in-Depth Architecture:** Running on the **Codex harness**, the system uses GPT-6 Astra combined with **Auto-Review**—a secondary model acting as a police officer to monitor base-model behavior against user rules—alongside full sub-agent auditability logs.
* **Ecosystem and Web-Bot Standards:** Rather than bypassing web platforms aggressively, OpenAI is taking an ecosystem-first approach, collaborating with partners like **Cloudflare** on `web-bot auth` so agents explicitly declare themselves as bots.

### Pricing and the Future Roadmap
* **Flat Monthly Contractor Pricing:** OpenAI is moving away from traditional strict rate limits for Dots, designing pricing more like a responsive human contractor. Users pay a flat monthly fee for 24/7 availability, with the agent intelligently managing its own compute costs (e.g., running heavy tasks overnight or switching to more cost-efficient models as limits approach). Dot usage is separate from main Codex limits.
* **Coexistence with ChatGPT:** ChatGPT (with 1.2 billion weekly users) remains the consumer assistant for everyone; OpenAI plans to eventually merge the Work tab into Chat and bring Dot capabilities all the way down to free users over time.
* **Specialist Dots:** While personal assistant Dots act as extensions of individuals, OpenAI is developing **Specialist Dots** centrally managed by IT and designed to accelerate entire enterprise teams or workflows.

- **Latent Demand for Software:** Engineering coding models have not replaced developers; instead, they have unlocked massive latent demand.

- **Regaining Deep Work:** Altman’s new always-on agent, **"Dot"** (or "Dottie"), acts as a persistent triage system that protects his early morning window of maximum creativity by handling overnight fires and surfacing only truly urgent matters.
