### TL;DR
Horizon Robotics founder and CEO Dr. Kai Yu explains how his 11-year-old "Wintel"-style computing platform powers one in three intelligent vehicles in China, detailing the intense competitive market, autonomous driving timelines, and the inevitable convergence of cars and humanoid robotics.

### Company Overview & Positioning
* **Core Business:** Dr. Kai Yu describes Horizon Robotics as the computing platform provider for generic robots, currently focused 95-100% on passenger vehicles by supplying chips and software spanning camera perception, trajectory planning, and control.
* **Market Share & Customer Base:** 
  * Supplies chips and software to **one in three** intelligent cars on Chinese roads (up from one in four previously).
  * Major Chinese original equipment manufacturer (OEM) customers include BYD, Geely, Chery, Changan, and Great Wall.
  * Global OEM customers include Volkswagen, Toyota, and Suzuki.
* **Competitive Landscape:** Competes with Mobileye, Huawei, Black Sesame, Qualcomm, MediaTek, Texas Instruments, Renesas, and NXP. Yu notes that the automotive computing space in China has consolidated around three primary players: Nvidia, Huawei, and Horizon Robotics.
* **The "Wintel" Strategy:** Deliberately chose never to build a car, instead modeling Horizon Robotics after Microsoft and Intel ("a Vintel") to enable all car brands and the broader robotics ecosystem rather than competing with them.
* **Company Scale:** Employs around **2,000 people**, the vast majority being software and hardware engineers operating under a high-ownership, flexible-hour culture.

### Market Dynamics & Supply Chain Intensity
* **China's Hyper-Competitive Car Market:** Describes the domestic market as "crazy," noting there are **60 to 70** different car brands and that more than **580 new car models** were launched in the first half of the year alone.
* **Development Cycle Discrepancy:** 
  * Initiating a new chip and solution generation takes **3 to 4 years** to ready for car makers.
  * Securing a nomination requires a production cycle of **2 years** for Chinese OEMs, compared to **5 years** for global OEMs.
  * Total cycle time spans **5 to 8 years** from start to finished platform.
* **China's Supply Chain Advantage:** Yu highlights China as a "wonderland for supply chain" due to its rich ecosystem, geographic proximity ("taking the entire China as one village"), and speed, allowing innovations to be realized rapidly through local partners.
* **The High-Barrier Paradox:** Acknowledges that automotive is "terrible business" requiring heavy investment with very slow returns, but argues that these mission-critical safety and quality standards create an extremely high entry barrier that filters out casual VC-funded newcomers.

### Autonomous Driving Timelines & Robotics Convergence
* **Three-Stage Autonomous Roadmap:** Reaffirms milestones established in January of the previous year to measure progress for consumers:
  * **Hands-off:** Target by the end of **2028** (currently enabled by the Horizon Superdrive software stack running on the Journey 6T SOC).
  * **Eyes-off:** Target by the end of **2030** (requiring larger knowledge bases, test mileage, and statistical safety proof).
  * **Minds-off:** Target by the end of **2035** (allowing passengers to sleep while the vehicle operates).
* **Robotics Convergence:** 
  * Horizon Robotics is already a top-two player in generic robotics computing in China, supplying chip solutions to humanoid robot companies.
  * Yu predicts that car technology and humanoid robotics will eventually converge into a unifying computing platform because cars fundamentally function as moving robots.
  * Foresees meaningful volumes of humanoid robots in home service and elderly care within **5 years** (optimistic projection).
  * Argues that winning the automotive supply chain directly translates to winning the robotics supply chain due to shared actuators and sensors (like lasers and radars) optimized by high-volume car manufacturing.

### Philosophy & Personal Leadership
* **Zen & The Power of Now:** Emphasizes a Zen Buddhist philosophy of focus—working entirely when working, sleeping entirely when sleeping, and letting go of failures immediately through extreme optimism.
* **Vision of Future Cities:** Envisions cities where autonomous fleets operate as coordinated systems rather than competing traffic, giving human freedom back by eliminating tedious two-hour commutes and transforming cars into moving living spaces.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Dr. Kai Yu predicts** all passenger vehicles in China will achieve 100% hands-free operation by the end of **2028**.
* **Dr. Kai Yu predicts** vehicles will achieve 100% eyes-off capability by the end of **2030**.
* **Dr. Kai Yu predicts** vehicles will achieve 100% minds-off capability (allowing sleeping while driving) by the end of **2035**.
* **Dr. Kai Yu predicts** meaningful volumes of humanoid robots will emerge for home service and elderly care within **5 years** (optimistic timeline).
