## Energy and AI: Sustainable, Sovereign Industrial Campuses | Alain Wilmouth, 2CRSi | RAISE 2026

* **TL;DR:** 2CRSi CEO Alain Wilmouth and host Ethan Pierse discuss building sustainable, sovereign industrial AI infrastructure across Europe, detailing a new French consortium ("Aether") that combines immersion cooling, biomass energy, and unused agricultural land to power edge AI data centers while generating negative carbon emissions.

### The Energy & Cooling Challenge in AI Infrastructure
* **Early Pioneer in Liquid Cooling:** Alain Wilmouth noted that 2CRSi began building servers specifically for immersion cooling and Direct Liquid Cooling (DLC) in 2013 and 2015, initially supplying US universities and national labs.
* **The Cooling Energy Burden:** Historically, removing 1,000 watts of heat required spending an additional 1,000 watts on cooling. 
* **Regulatory Pressures:** Strict European regulations now enforce low Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) targets, such as ~1.3 in Germany and ~1.2 in the Netherlands.

### The Aether Consortium and Sustainable Power
* **Consortium Launch:** Announced in July 2026, "Aether" is a consortium of established industrial companies (many over 100 years old), including Dassault System, Varian, and Equans, with partners like Hafner Energy.
* **Initial Capacity:** The consortium currently has 42 megawatts available across two sites near Strasbourg, France, with a roadmap scaling up to 400 megawatts.
* **Biomass and Gasification:** To move beyond mere carbon neutrality to negative carbon footprints, the project integrates biomass energy using gasification technology via partner Hafner Energy, allowing carbon to be stored for centuries while burning cleaned wood gas.
* **Agricultural Land Integration:** Alain Wilmouth pointed out that France has 15 million hectares of land, of which 2 million hectares have been unused by farmers for at least five years. Utilizing these lands for biomass production could create approximately 250,000 jobs for struggling farmers without requiring state grants.
* **Grid Strain Concerns:** Pointing to grid constraints (such as an evening in late December when only 0.5 gigawatts of power remained available in France), Wilmouth argued against massive centralized gigawatt-scale data centers that require tens of billions in cabling, favoring decentralized edge infrastructure.

### European Sovereignty, Security, and Corporate R&D
* **Infrastructure vs. Data Sovereignty:** Beyond cloud and data sovereignty, the Aether model provides infrastructure sovereignty, allowing companies to keep their data on-premise.
* **Protection Against Geopolitical Restrictions:** Alain Wilmouth highlighted risks for European companies with partial foreign ownership (e.g., a major European car manufacturer with 32% Chinese ownership and Chinese board members blocked from buying US AI chips). 
* **Secure Enterprise AI:** The Aether model allows enterprises to run any Large Language Model (LLM) locally via black fiber connections without exposing proprietary R&D or data to cloud or LLM providers.
* **SMB Solutions:** Partnerships with companies like Goodwick and Outscale (Dassault System's Numsec Cloud) enable small and mid-sized businesses (SMBs) to maintain data security and avoid tech giants.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Roadmap to 17 AI Factories:** Alain Wilmouth stated a roadmap exists to build 17 different AI factories across Europe through partner networks owning land and power, expanding from France through Germany, Czechia, Hungary, Poland, and Romania.
* **Space-bound Servers:** Alain Wilmouth revealed that 2CRSi has already validated three servers specifically designed for space operations over the past three years.
