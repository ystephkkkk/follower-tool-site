### TL;DR
Bo Hines (CEO of Tether USA) and Charley Cooper (President of Ava Labs) discuss the launch of Aave’s V4 RWA hub on Avalanche using Tether as its inaugural stablecoin, while outlining how the Genius Act and regulatory clarity are driving traditional financial institutions toward 24/7 onchain settlement.

### Regulatory Landscape & Institutional Policy

*   **The Genius Act Foundation:** **Bo Hines** explains that the Genius Act was prioritized over combining stablecoin bills with market structure to efficiently establish clear on- and off-ramp rails for tokenized securities and a 24/7 financial system.
*   **Treasury Rulemaking Timeline:** **Bo Hines** notes that the U.S. Treasury is currently working through rulemaking on the Genius Act, which is expected to be fully cemented within the next **6 months**.
*   **Regulatory Leadership:** **Bo Hines** expresses high confidence in administration officials like Paul Atkins at the SEC and Mike Sel at the CFTC to deliver comprehensive regulations that move the industry forward.
*   **Political Evolution:** **Bo Hines** highlights the dramatic shift in government attitude, noting a joke from the President at the first White House crypto council that industry CEOs had a better chance of being in jail last year than sitting in the White House.

### The Avalanche & Aave Partnership

*   **V4 RWA Hub Launch:** **Charley Cooper** announces that Aave is launching V4 of its Real World Asset (RWA) hub on Avalanche with Tether serving as the inaugural stablecoin.
*   **Strategic Rationale:** **Bo Hines** explains that Tether chose to back this partnership to demonstrate real-world asset utility—such as borrowing against assets and traditional finance mechanics onchain—which he views as the future of finance.
*   **Collaborative Ecosystem:** **Bo Hines** positions Tether, Avalanche, and Aave as trusted, aligned partners capable of turning tokenization theory into practical execution for institutional adopters.

### Overcoming Institutional Friction & Education

*   **Internal Inefficiencies:** **Bo Hines** states that large financial institutions are motivated to adopt blockchain primarily by internal operational inefficiencies and high costs associated with moving capital.
*   **The Education Barrier:** **Bo Hines** emphasizes that bridging the gap with Goliath institutions requires translating complex crypto terminology into plain language by working backwards from their specific business goals.
*   **Intermediary Role:** **Bo Hines** describes Tether’s product as an agnostic intermediary settlement tool accepted worldwide, bridging traditional finance institutions and digital asset exchanges.

### Why Tether Outpaces Consortium Efforts

*   **Rejection of Bank Consortiums:** **Bo Hines** dismisses consortium-backed stablecoins due to back-end governance friction, control questions, and yield-distribution complications.
*   **The Competitor Problem:** **Bo Hines** points out that major banks (e.g., Wells Fargo) will not settle on competitor-backed infrastructure (e.g., JPMD) because it fails to optimize their balance sheets.
*   **Unmatched Advantages:** **Bo Hines** attributes Tether's market dominance to having the deepest liquidity, broadest distribution network, interoperability between USDT and USAT, and pre-built infrastructure that eliminates the need for banks to bootstrap their own liquidity.

### Predictions & Forward Calls

*   **Treasury Rulemaking:** Treasury rulemaking on the Genius Act will be completed within the next **6 months** — *Bo Hines*.
*   **Institutional Adoption Timeline:** Real-world asset adoption and mainstream stablecoin integration will scale broadly over the course of the next **3 to 4 years** — *Bo Hines*.
