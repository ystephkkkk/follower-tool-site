### TL;DR
Alex Davis, founder of Dallas-based Disruptive, has launched a new $10 billion fund to concentrate massive growth-stage capital on roughly 10 elite "N of one" companies—such as Palantir, Groq, Databricks, and Shield AI—leveraging an active, relationship-heavy approach that includes executive-level storytelling, global business development, and hands-on operational leadership.

### Investment Strategy & the Macro Environment
* **The "Haves and Have-Nots" Reality:** 
  * **Parabolic Unicorn Growth:** The market has expanded from roughly 10 venture-backed unicorns in 2011 to 5,000–6,000 today.
  * **The End of Garage Computing:** Modern frontier AI models cannot be built on a family budget or bootstrapping savings; compute realities separate the dominant players from companies that will struggle or get acquired.
* **Concentrated Offense Over Spray-and-Pray:** 
  * **The 10-Company Thesis:** Disruptive targets a hyper-focused list of the top 15-20 companies globally, deploying capital into roughly 10 distinct entities via large, concentrated checks.
  * **Proprietary Sourcing:** The firm actively identifies and pursues target companies rather than relying on inbound pitch decks, viewing inbound deal flow as adverse selection.
* **Value Investing is Dead:** 
  * **Exponential Outcomes:** Davis argues that obsessing over a 5% to 15% valuation discount is a mistake; generational returns are driven entirely by backing great, exponential companies regardless of premium pricing.

### Compute, Neoclouds, & AI Infrastructure
* **The Compute Shortage & Neoclouds:** 
  * **Infrastructure Necessity:** A massive, prevalent compute shortage requires neoclouds and sophisticated financing to stand up and operate infrastructure.
  * **Speculative Landlords Warned:** Davis cautioned against speculative landlords without proper funding, experience, or chip access—predicting failure for players sitting on raw land in Texas relying on debt without deep infrastructure expertise.
* **Compute Efficiency & Frontier Costs:** 
  * **No Shortcuts to Frontier Quality:** Despite social media claims of models achieving frontier performance for $10,000 in compute via distillation, Davis asserts these do not meet the rigorous demands of sovereign customers or major financial institutions.

### Palantir & Groq Case Studies
* **Palantir (Pre-IPO Conviction):** 
  * **Decade-Long Backing:** Disruptive invested consistently in Palantir for roughly 10 years, recognizing their out-competition for engineering talent and ability to create entirely new enterprise budgets.
  * **Doubling Down Pre-IPO:** Right before Palantir's direct listing during COVID, Disruptive doubled down with a large insurance partner, betting heavily on Alex Karp's unique leadership and ability to win over public markets.
* **Groq (Inference & Operational Takeover):** 
  * **The Nvidia Licensing Deal:** Following Nvidia's non-exclusive $20 billion licensing deal for Groq (which saw Jonathan and core team members move to Nvidia), Disruptive stepped in to ensure tax-efficient shareholder distributions.
  * **Running the Company:** Alex Davis took over the board chair and operational reins of Groq's remaining assets, upgrading 53 existing megawatts of compute, shifting into liquid-cooled sites, and positioning the infrastructure as an inference neocloud to serve portfolio companies and hyperscalers.

### Fund Structure & Private Market Risks
* **Shift to Discretionary Capital:** 
  * **SPV Vulnerabilities:** While Disruptive historically relied on large SPVs, changing market dynamics and a proliferation of grey-market brokers/SPVs with unverified capital calls have created regulatory risks and potential "black eyes" for premier private companies (e.g., Anthropic, Databricks, SpaceX).
  * **Committed Institutional Fund:** The new $10 billion fund provides secure, transparent, and committed capital directly matching what top-tier private companies demand.
* **Disruptive Firm Architecture:** 
  * **Lean and Aligned:** Operating with a team of roughly 30 employees, Disruptive rejects traditional AUM-chasing management fee bloat, earning its returns strictly through performance carry.

### Defense Tech & Sovereign AI Opportunities
* **New Prime Contractors:** 
  * **Breaking the Century-Old Mold:** Private companies like Shield AI (which recently signed massive contracts with the Navy) are successfully building relationships with the US and foreign governments, paving the way for the first new defense prime contractors in 100 years.
  * **AI and the Pentagon:** Because frontier models require heavy regulatory navigation, safety frameworks, and national security integrations, AI companies are effectively evolving into defense companies.
