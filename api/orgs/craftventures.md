## David Sacks Criticizes Rush To Judgment On Jensen Huang | Silicon Valley & The World

- Channel: Semafor

- Speakers: David Sacks (Co-founder, Craft Ventures); Jensen Huang (Former CEO, Nvidia)

---

**TL;DR:** David Sacks argued that the public and Nvidia’s board rushed to judgment in ousting CEO Jensen Huang, noting that the "Chinese spy" narrative is falling apart and urging that any smuggling or governance allegations be adjudicated through proper legal due process rather than off-the-record leaks.

### The Collapse of the "Chinese Spy" Narrative
* **Unproven Allegations:** Sacks emphasized that the singular event triggering Huang’s ouster—an alleged relationship with a suspected Chinese spy—lacks on-the-record backing from any Republican or Democratic official.
  * Following a closed-door briefing on Thursday, the ranking Democrat on the House Intelligence Committee told reporters "I don't know" when asked if the woman was a spy.
  * CNN’s Natasha Bertrand reported that no official on the record has conclusively proved the claim, quoting CIA and ODNI sources who described the intelligence as "not entirely accurate" and "over torqued."
* **Politically Motivated Leaks:** Citing *The Washington Post*, Sacks noted that an original CIA assessment did not conclude the woman was a spy.
  * A second, "secret memo" was reportedly created that was far more aggressive than the underlying CIA intelligence and was leaked specifically to reduce Huang’s influence with President Trump.
  * Sacks argued that targeting a citizen for holding policy views disliked by "China Hawks" is not a crime, and warned against "shadowy actors in the national security state" using government surveillance powers to retaliate via leaks.

### Corporate Governance, Mega Speed, and Due Process
* **The Smuggling Investigation:** While the interviewer noted Huang was removed over corporate governance issues (including dishonesty with the board) and pointed to the Mega Speed investigation, Sacks drew a clear line on potential wrongdoing.
  * Sacks stated he "unequivocally" denounces smuggling, adding that if Huang knew about or participated in it, he should not return as Nvidia's CEO.
* **Demand for Legal Adjudication:** With the DOJ investigating, Sacks argued that Huang and Nvidia deserve legal representation and due process—including the right to an attorney and to confront accusers—before being judged guilty.
* **Board Independence:** Responding to Scott Bessent’s warning that Nvidia’s board should look outside the company for a new CEO, Sacks said the board can figure out its leadership on its own, but reiterated that the government should not drive executives out through press vortexes.
  * Sacks cited Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney’s warning that this episode sets a "dark path" for how the government relates to companies and individuals.

### California vs. Texas: Politics and Governance
* **The Texas Shift:** Sacks, who moved his family to Austin ahead of a proposed California billionaire tax that ultimately failed, acknowledged the irony of Texas turning blue despite Elon Musk spending $200 million in the state's political races.
* **Value of Political Competition:** Sacks argued that Texas Democrats tend to be more pragmatic because Texas is a competitive political environment where poor performance leads to being voted out.
  * By contrast, he attributed California’s governance issues to a lack of political competition, claiming the "Democrat machine" picks election winners.
* **Silicon Valley’s "Resource Curse":** Sacks attributed Silicon Valley’s continued dominance to a powerful "geographic network effect" of founders, engineers, and VCs rooted in Stanford University, Hewlett Packard, Intel, and Fairchild Semiconductor.
  * Referencing Peter Thiel’s theory, Sacks noted this creates a "resource curse"—an inexhaustible "gusher" of wealth that allows California to be poorly governed without immediate economic collapse.
  * He blamed California’s nation-leading inequality and poverty on government employee unions thwarting reform, noting that Texas and Florida spend roughly half as much per citizen while delivering better services and less inequality.

### Wealth Creation and AI’s Impact on Employment
* **Broadening Ownership:** Sacks applauded Brad Gerstner’s support for "Trump accounts," calling it a strong program to give the next generation of Americans actual ownership in companies and a vested interest in the system.
* **AI as an Entrepreneurial Enabler:** Sacks rejected the narrative that AI and superintelligence will drive inequality or mass unemployment, calling it a tool that turbocharges individual capabilities and allows non-coders to build software and businesses.
  * Citing recent reporting from *The Economist*, Sacks noted that over 1 million jobs have already been created by AI, arguing that "doomsday prophets" have overstated the risks.

### Predictions & Forward Calls

* **AI’s Societal Impact:** AI "is going to be a positive force for society" and will make entrepreneurship significantly easier rather than putting everyone out of work. — David Sacks

## Anthropic’s $30B Ramp, Mythos Doomsday, OpenClaw Ankled, Iran War Ceasefire, Israel's Influence

- Published: 2026-04-10

- Speakers: Brad Gerstner (Founder & CEO, Altimeter Capital); Chamath Palihapitiya (Founder & CEO, Social Capital); David Sacks (Co-founder, Craft Ventures)

---

**TL;DR**
Anthropic has achieved an unprecedented $30B revenue run rate while sparking intense debate over its decision to withhold its "Mythos" model due to cyber-security risks, amid accusations of anti-competitive behavior toward open-source projects like OpenClaw.

### AI Security and the "Mythos" Disclosure
* **Mythos Model Security:** Anthropic is withholding its latest model, Mythos, claiming it is too dangerous for public release.
    * **Vulnerability Discovery:** The model autonomously identified thousands of bugs, including a 27-year-old vulnerability in OpenBSD and a 16-year-old bug in FFmpeg.
    * **Chaining Exploits:** Dario Amodei (Anthropic CEO) reported that the model can string together four or five minor vulnerabilities to create sophisticated offensive exploits.
* **Project Glass Wing:** Anthropic established a 100-day "cyber coalition" with 40 major companies (including Apple, Microsoft, and JP Morgan) to patch these vulnerabilities before hackers can exploit them.
* **Strategic Skepticism:** The Besties debated whether this is genuine safety or a "marketing stunt."
    * **Marketing Tactics:** David Sacks noted Anthropic has a proven pattern of using "fear-mongering" to market new products, citing a previous "blackmail study" that failed to manifest in reality.
    * **"Theatrical" Safety:** Chamath Palihapitiya characterized the move as "theater," comparing it to the GPT-2 release in 2019, which was hyped as dangerous but turned out to be a "nothing burger."

### Market Dominance and the "OpenClaw" Conflict
* **"Ankling" Open Source:** Anthropic is accused of trying to stifle OpenClaw, the most popular open-source project on GitHub.
    * **Pricing Shift:** Anthropic moved OpenClaw users from $200/month flat-rate subscriptions to metered API usage, effectively increasing costs by 10x for power users.
    * **Feature Copying:** Shortly after raising costs for OpenClaw users, Anthropic released its own "safer" first-party version of the agent technology.
* **Coding Market Share:** David Sacks argued that Anthropic now holds "dominant market share" in AI coding, providing over 50-60% of coding tokens.
* **Antitrust Concerns:** Sacks warned that the government might eventually view these "price discrimination" tactics against open-source competitors as anti-competitive behavior.
* **The "Barbell" Approach:** Brad Gerstner noted that while frontier models are winning, 65-70% of many companies' token consumption remains on open-source models for cost optimization.

### Anthropic’s $30B Revenue Ramp and AGI Threshold
* **Unprecedented Growth:** Anthropic’s revenue run rate hit $30B in March 2025, a ramp described as the fastest in technology history.
    * **Monthly Gains:** The company added $11B in annualized run rate in March alone—surpassing the combined total revenue of DataBricks and Palantir in 30 days.
    * **Projection:** Brad Gerstner expects Anthropic to exit the year at an $80B–$100B revenue run rate.
* **Efficiency Metrics:** Anthropic reached these milestones with only 2,500 employees, compared to incumbents like Google which required 120,000+ employees to reach similar scale.
* **The TAM for Intelligence:** Gerstner argued that the "TAM for intelligence" is effectively infinite and fundamentally different from previous software markets because it focuses on labor augmentation and replacement.
* **OpenAI’s Response:** Despite the "vibe shift" toward Anthropic, OpenAI is preparing to release "Spud" (a Blackwell-trained model), which insiders claim is on par with Anthropic’s Mythos.

### Geopolitics: Iran War and Middle East Influence
* **Ceasefire and Negotiation:** A two-week ceasefire is in effect as a U.S. delegation, including JD Vance and Jared Kushner, travels to Islamabad to negotiate peace terms.
* **The "Trump Doctrine":** Brad Gerstner summarized Trump’s strategy as destroying military capabilities and exiting quickly, rather than attempting "Wilsonian democracy" or nation-building.
* **Israeli Influence:** The panel discussed the perception that Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu is driving U.S. foreign policy. 
    * **Public Sentiment:** David Sacks highlighted a "serious situation" for Israel, noting polling data from former PM Naftali Bennett showing Israel’s popularity is declining significantly in the U.S.
    * **Escalation Trap:** Sacks praised the ceasefire as a necessary move to prevent the war from moving further up the "escalation ladder."
