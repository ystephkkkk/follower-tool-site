## How This Founder Turned “Uber For Kids” Into A $1B+ Tech Unicorn

### TL;DR
Ritu Narayan, co-founder and CEO of Zum, discusses how she scaled her student transportation unicorn from a B2C "Uber for kids" startup into an enterprise-grade "AWS for student transportation" managing over $2.6 billion in total contract value.

### Evolution of Zum & Business Model
* **Company Origins (2015):** 
  * Launched as a direct-to-parent ride-sharing service ("Uber for kids") out of Narayan's personal need as a working mother at eBay.
  * Narayan personally drove early pilot rides to understand user and driver experiences.
* **The 2020 Pandemic Pivot:** 
  * Transitioned 100% to serving large school districts rather than individual parents.
  * Leveraged a six-month pandemic pause to upgrade the technology stack and sign an early major contract with **Oakland Unified School District**.
* **Current Enterprise Model ("AWS for Student Transportation"):** 
  * Manages entire transportation contracts for school districts, including leasing yards, financing fleets, hiring drivers, and deploying proprietary technology.
  * **Scale Metrics:** Operating across **18 states**, serving over **5,000 schools**, with **$2.6 billion+ in total contract value** and **155 million student rides** delivered.
  * **Contract Terms:** Operates on long-term, essential-infrastructure contracts lasting **5 to 10 years**.

### Technology & Operational Impact
* **Connected Mobility Experience (Zoom CMX):** 
  * Replaces legacy infrastructure (such as 2017 fax machines and 16-page route sheets on clipboards) with real-time digital routing and tracking.
  * **Student Tracking:** Students tap RFID cards upon boarding, instantly notifying parents and schools.
  * **Dynamic Rerouting:** Automatically adjusts routes for traffic, road conditions, or canceled rides in real time, eliminating driver wait times and parent anxiety.
* **Addressing the National Driver Shortage:** 
  * **Driver Shortage Context:** National data shows school bus drivers remain down nearly **10%** compared to pre-pandemic levels.
  * **Zum's Solution:** Achieves **zero driver shortages** across districts by providing digital-native tools, modern vehicle fleets, electrification, and a supportive workplace culture ("Zoom vibe").
* **Measurable District Outcomes (Kansas City Public School District Case Study):** 
  * **Reliability:** Ratings increased from **4% to 97%**.
  * **Absenteeism:** Transportation-related absenteeism dropped from **25% to 5.6%**.
  * **Graduation Rates:** Reached **88%**, marking a decade high.

### Funding Journey & Market Opportunity
* **Early Funding & Crucible Moment:** 
  * Secured early backing from raving-fan customers turned angel investors.
  * Faced a critical juncture when down to **three weeks of runway** before closing a Series A with **Sequoia Capital**, turning down an alternate term sheet to pursue the harder path.
  * Subsequent investors include **Spark Capital**, **SoftBank**, **GIC**, and **TPG** (who led the latest round).
* **Total Addressable Market (TAM):** 
  * The U.S. student transportation and electrification market is sized at **$50 billion**.
  * Long-term vision includes international expansion and applying the framework to other mass-mobility challenges.

### Leadership & The "50 Over 50" Perspective
* **The Power of Being an Underdog:** 
  * Narayan views being an underdog over 50 as a major professional advantage, noting that being underestimated allows leaders to quietly deliver sustained results.
* **Early Inspirations & Mentorship:** 
  * Inspired by watching India's first space mission on TV and aspiring to be an astronaut.
  * Credits her mother—dubbed a "dream catcher"—for unconditionially supporting her pursuit of engineering at a time when few women entered the field.
* **Advice for Younger Professionals:** 
  * Reminds women in their 20s and 30s that "it all will be fine" if they balance long-term decade thinking with meticulous daily execution.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Ritu Narayan** predicts that Zum will continue expanding its footprint across the United States, getting closer to its ultimate goal of serving all **26 million school children** nationwide.

**TL;DR**
Nectir AI has secured a $12.5 million Series A round led by ReThink Impact to scale its "purpose-built" AI infrastructure across all 50 U.S. state school systems, offering FERPA-compliant tools that have already demonstrated significant improvements in student GPA, retention, and intrinsic motivation.

### Strategic Expansion and Efficacy Data
* **Series A Funding:** Kavitta Ghai, co-founder and CEO of Nectir AI, announced a $12.5 million funding round aimed at scaling the platform from the California community college system to educational institutions nationwide.
* **Student Outcomes:** Data from the California community college system (serving 2.1 million students) indicates that using Nectir AI leads to:
    * **GPA and Scores:** A 20% increase in GPA and a 13% rise in final exam scores.
    * **Motivation and Retention:** A 36% boost in intrinsic motivation to learn and higher student retention rates, helping students avoid unnecessary tuition costs.
* **Workforce Readiness:** The tool provides AI literacy; Ghai noted an instance where a student secured a job as an AI prompt engineer specifically because of their experience using Nectir in the classroom.

### Responsible AI Infrastructure and Safeguards
* **FERPA Compliance:** Unlike generic LLMs (ChatGPT, Claude), Nectir is purpose-built to meet FERPA standards (the educational equivalent of HIPAA), ensuring student data remains private.
* **Data Privacy:**
    * **Model Training:** Proprietary data uploaded by teachers for their courses is not shared back with model providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini) for training purposes.
    * **Student Privacy:** Student conversations with AI assistants remain private to the student, encouraging them to ask "stupid questions" without fear of judgment.
* **Teacher-Led Guardrails:** Educators can customize AI behavior via a "prompt library."
    * **Instructional Methods:** Teachers can mandate the Socratic method or forbid the AI from providing direct answers to homework or writing essays.
    * **Role of TAs:** Ghai emphasized that the tool is meant to scale human support, not replace teachers or TAs, who remain essential to the classroom.

### Personalized Learning and Campus Integration
* **Comprehensive Support:** Beyond classroom tutoring, Nectir acts as a 24/7 campus assistant, offering:
    * **Administrative Help:** FAFSA advisors to navigate complex financial aid paperwork.
    * **Career Coaching:** Assistants that match a student's grades and course history to specific internship and job opportunities.
* **LMS Integration:** To ensure high adoption, Nectir integrates directly into existing Learning Management Systems (LMS) like Canvas or Blackboard. 
    * **Usage Statistics:** In some high school pilots, 98% of students are daily active users, averaging 500 messages per day to their AI assistant.
* **Neurodiversity and Accessibility:** Ghai, who is autistic and has ADHD, built the tool to solve the "one size fits all" classroom model. The AI adapts to how individual brains learn best without requiring students to disclose disabilities.

### Fundraising Realities and Market Challenges
* **VC Disparities:** Ghai highlighted the extreme difficulty of fundraising as a woman of color and a first-time founder from a non-Ivy League background, noting that only 0.4% of VC funding goes to women of color.
* **The "Female Founder Tax":** Ghai argued that female founders often face higher scrutiny, requiring "10 times the traction" and "perfect" metrics to receive a fraction of the valuation given to male-led counterparts.
* **Adoption Barriers:** The primary obstacle to growth is not the technology, but society’s readiness. Ghai warned that state leaders do not have years to pilot AI; they must adopt safe infrastructure immediately to keep pace with the technology's integration into the workforce.

## The U.S. Is Falling Behind Its Global Peers When It Comes To Women In Leadership

- Channel: Forbes
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L06SMilSfOc
- Published: 2026-03-05
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260305.html#video-L06SMilSfOc
- Speakers: Diana van Maasdijk (CEO, Equileap); Maggie McGrath (Editor, ForbesWomen)

---

**TL;DR**
Equileap’s eighth annual study of 3,500 global companies reveals that female leadership has reached a three-year plateau, with U.S. corporations significantly regressing in transparency and representation compared to peers in Europe and Asia where government mandates are driving progress.

### Global Stagnation in Leadership and Pay
*   **Leadership Plateau:** For the third consecutive year, the percentage of female CEOs globally remains stalled at just 7%. 
    *   Representation at the board and executive levels fluctuates narrowly between 20% and 30% with no significant upward movement.
*   **The "Broken Pipeline":** While women represent roughly 38–40% of the total workforce, representation drops significantly as seniority increases, confirming a persistent "leaking pipeline" in corporate hierarchies.
*   **Board vs. Executive Disparity:** Globally, women hold approximately 33% of board seats but only 7% of CEO positions.
    *   **Diana van Maasdijk** notes that the 33% figure reflects years of targeted "30% club" initiatives, suggesting that progress only occurs when specific, publicized targets are set.

### The U.S. Decline and Transparency Crisis
*   **Falling Rankings:** Only seven U.S. companies made the Global Top 100 list in 2024, a sharp decline from 17 companies just four years ago.
    *   **Top Performers:** Despite the national decline, companies like International Flavors & Fragrances, Procter & Gamble, Boston Scientific, and Bank of America remain leaders in equity.
*   **The Transparency Gap:** The U.S. currently ranks last among developed markets for transparency regarding gender pay and representation data.
    *   At the current rate of progress, it would take the U.S. nearly 40 years to reach the transparency standards that Europe achieved in just four years.
*   **Institutional Regression:** Equileap is observing a decline in U.S. companies publishing gender pay gaps and a reduction in supplier diversity programs, partly attributed to a shifting political climate surrounding Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI).

### Legislation as the Primary Catalyst
*   **Mandated Progress:** Research indicates that corporate gender balance rarely improves through "grassroots" or voluntary company efforts alone; it is almost exclusively driven by legislation.
*   **Japan’s Rapid Shift:** In 2021, 91% of Asia-Pacific companies did not disclose pay data. After Japan introduced mandatory disclosure laws, over 90% of Japanese companies now publish their gender pay gaps.
*   **The French Model:** After reaching 44% female representation on boards through quotas, France has now implemented new laws to force 30-40% representation at the executive committee level by 2030 because voluntary growth stalled.
*   **Upcoming EU Directives:** The EU Tax Transparency Directive is expected to further accelerate disclosure across Europe by mandating the publication of gender pay gaps for all large companies.

### The Business Case and Performance Criteria
*   **Financial Outperformance:** Equileap’s data correlates higher gender equality scores with better financial performance. Indices tracking gender-equal companies consistently show higher revenue and profit compared to market averages.
*   **Equileap’s Evaluation Metrics:** Companies are scored across 19 criteria, including:
    *   **Four-Level Balance:** Gender ratios at the Board, Executive, Senior Management, and Workforce levels.
    *   **Pay Equity:** Publicly disclosing and actively closing the gender pay gap.
    *   **Parental Leave:** Providing leave for both primary and secondary caregivers.
    *   **Policy Integrity:** Anti-harassment and diversity policies within both the internal workforce and the supply chain.

### Notable Quotes
> "The truth is that I believe they [women] wouldn't be promoted at the same levels in Europe if there wasn't legislation that was forcing it... if you really just leave it to the companies and hope that there will be gender balance, you see that that movement does not happen." — **Diana van Maasdijk**

> "According to your analysis, the U.S. ranks last among developed markets in transparency... it would take nearly four decades for the U.S. to reach the transparency standards that Europe achieved in four years." — **Maggie McGrath**

> "Gender equality is not only a nice to have but it's a great to have when it comes to the financial benefit of investing in equality." — **Diana van Maasdijk**

### Strategic Market Disruption: Housing and Healthcare
* **Solving the "Ghosting" Problem:** In the rental market, 40% to 50% of inquiries typically go unanswered. EliseAI’s conversational AI automates responses to 100% of calls and emails, enabling 24/7 scheduling and lease management.
* **Vertical Expansion:** The company expanded into healthcare because the administrative "pain points"—scheduling appointments, insurance verification, and patient instructions—mirror the friction found in real estate leasing.
* **Economic Impact:** Housing and healthcare represent over 40% of average American spending. By automating these sectors, EliseAI aims to lower administrative costs and improve service quality in "life's most critical areas."
* **Adoption Metrics:**
    * **Market Share:** Now serves 1 in 6 US apartments (up from 1 in 10 earlier in 2024).
    * **Efficiency:** In healthcare, the platform automates 90% of conversations and has reduced call abandonment rates by 90%.
    * **Velocity:** Real estate clients see a 65% increase in lead-to-lease velocity.

### Technology Infrastructure and Scalability
* **Foundation on Transformers:** Founded in 2017—the same year the Transformer architecture (the "T" in GPT) was invented—the company has evolved from basic conversational bots to sophisticated Generative AI.
* **Architecture Rebuild:** In 2021-2022, Song made the "painful" but necessary decision to rebuild the company's entire tech stack. 
    * This allowed for a "plug-and-play" system where internal and external models can be swapped instantly.
    * **Speed Mandate:** The team has an internal mandate to test any new AI model the same day it is released.
* **Security-First Product Management:** Because housing and healthcare are highly regulated, security and encryption are treated as core product features rather than afterthoughts.

### Leadership and Organizational Culture
* **In-Office Synergy:** Unlike many tech peers, EliseAI maintains a heavy in-office culture across hubs in New York, San Francisco, Boston, and Chicago to facilitate rapid communication.
* **Communication Efficiency:** To maintain speed while scaling, the company prioritizes **Writing over Meetings**. Documentation allows new hires to gain months of context quickly without constant all-hands meetings.
* **Hiring "Founders":** Song specifically recruits former founders or individuals with an entrepreneurial spirit. 
    * **Decentralized Decision-Making:** Hiring "business leaders" instead of "swimlane workers" prevents the CEO from becoming a bottleneck.
    * **CEO Involvement:** Song personally interviewed the first 400 employees to ensure cultural alignment.

### Fundraising and Growth Strategy
* **Opportunistic Fundraising:** Following a $250 million Series E, Song notes that recent rounds were preempted by investors. This allowed the company to accept favorable terms without undergoing exhausting six-month fundraising processes.
* **Sunk Cost Fallacy:** Song emphasizes the importance of being willing to replace existing internal technology if a better external innovation emerges, prioritizing product excellence over pride of authorship.
* **Future Outlook:** The company is focused on "generational" impact. While an IPO remains a possibility, the current priority is maximizing operational efficiency for customers in private markets.
