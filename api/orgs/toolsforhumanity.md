## How to prove who's human online (ft. Ben Horowitz and Alex Blania)

- Channel: a16z crypto
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BLhVdjnmmXs
- Published: 2026-04-17
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260417.html#video-BLhVdjnmmXs
- Speakers: Ben Horowitz (Co-founder, a16z); Alex Blania (CEO, Tools for Humanity); Erik Torenberg (Host, a16z)

---

**TL;DR**
As AI capabilities outpace traditional bot detection, World (formerly Worldcoin) proposes iris-based biometric verification as the only scalable, privacy-preserving method to distinguish unique humans from autonomous agents in an increasingly deepfaked digital economy.

### The Escalating Crisis of Digital Personhood
*   **The "1% Glimpse":** Alex Blania (CEO of World) warns that current bot activity is less than 1% of what is coming; as the cost of intelligence drops, AI agents will become superhuman in their ability to manipulate and "program" humans.
*   **Identity Categories:** The internet is shifting from a binary (human/bot) to a three-tier interaction model:
    *   **Humans:** Unique biological individuals.
    *   **Human-Directed Agents:** AI acting with specific permissions on behalf of a human.
    *   **Autonomous Agents:** Purely AI-driven entities with their own agendas.
*   **The Failure of Digital Signals:** Previous "Web of Trust" models (relying on GitHub history or social media longevity) are now obsolete. AI can easily forge years of digital history and "attest" to the humanity of other AI accounts, creating self-validating bot networks.

### Why Conventional Solutions Fall Short
*   **The Entropy Problem:** Standard biometrics like FaceID or fingerprints work for 1-to-1 authentication (checking if you are the same person who owns the phone). However, they lack the mathematical entropy required for 1-to-N uniqueness (checking if you are a unique human not already in the system). FaceID "hits a wall" after tens of millions of users, whereas the iris provides enough information to scale to billions.
*   **Government ID Limitations:** Relying on state-issued IDs is dismissed due to:
    *   **Privacy Risks:** Instant loss of anonymity and the danger of centralized government surveillance.
    *   **Fragmentation:** Government systems are not globally interoperable; a "perfect" system in Singapore does not help a platform like Meta manage 3 billion global users.
*   **Replay Attacks:** Digital-only verification is vulnerable to deepfakes injected directly into camera streams. World argues that custom hardware (the Orb) is necessary to verify physical presence through multi-spectral sensors that can distinguish a real eye from a high-resolution screen.

### World’s Technical and Privacy Architecture
*   **Privacy-Preserving Verification:** To avoid a "centralized database of eyeballs," World utilizes two primary cryptographic techniques:
    *   **Multi-Party Computation (MPC):** The iris code is broken into fragments and distributed across multiple independent computers. No single entity ever possesses the full biometric template.
    *   **Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKP):** Users hold a "secret" on their phones. They can prove to a third-party platform (like Reddit or X) that they are a unique human verified by the MPC system without ever revealing their biometric data or real-world identity.
*   **Hardware Evolution:** The Orb has transitioned from a "wild" futuristic concept to a necessary utility. Blania notes that iris scanning is becoming normalized through devices like the Apple Vision Pro.

### Macro Applications: From Dating to Democracy
*   **Economic Integrity:** Ben Horowitz notes that $400 billion was stolen during COVID stimulus programs due to identity fraud. Cryptographic "proof of human" is essential for the efficient distribution of Universal Basic Income (UBI) or social services.
*   **Protecting Democracy:** Current systems (like mail-in ballots and Social Security numbers) are vulnerable to AI-scale fraud. Without proof of human, high-scale impersonation could render the "will of the people" impossible to determine.
*   **Consumer Use Cases:** 
    *   **Dating:** Tinder is testing World ID in Japan to ensure users are interacting with authentic humans.
    *   **Video Conferencing:** As deepfakes become real-time and photorealistic, high-value users (e.g., fund managers) will need to cryptographically verify their identity on calls.
    *   **Gaming:** Preventing AI from "destroying" human players in competitive environments where money or status is at stake.

### Scaling and the US Market
*   **Current Scale:** The project currently has **18 million verified users** and 40 million total app users.
*   **US Focus:** After navigating regulatory uncertainty, the company is shifting 90% of its effort to the US. The goal is to deploy **50,000 Orbs** to ensure every American is within 15 minutes of a device.
*   **Distribution Innovations:** 
    *   **Partnerships:** Potential deployments in high-traffic locations like Starbucks or coffee shops.
    *   **Orb on Demand:** A pilot program for the Bay Area and NYC where an Orb can be delivered via motorbike to a user’s location within 15 minutes for verification.

## 99.9% of the internet is about to be AI?

- Channel: Sourcery with Molly O'Shea
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7PnaQzw_K8E
- Published: 2026-02-16
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260216.html#video-7PnaQzw_K8E
- Speakers: Alex Blania (CEO, Tools for Humanity); Molly O'Shea (Host, Sourcery)

---

**TL;DR**
Alex Blania, CEO of Tools for Humanity and Co-Founder of Merge Labs, discusses the creation of a global "Proof of Human" network to combat an internet where 99.9% of traffic is AI-driven, while announcing a $250 million investment from OpenAI into Merge Labs to bridge biological and artificial intelligence.

### The World Ecosystem: Establishing a "Real Human Network"
* **The Core Problem:** Alex Blania asserts that because AI has passed the Turing test and can mimic human behavior perfectly, the internet requires a fundamental "Proof of Human" layer to maintain authenticity.
    * **Projected AI Dominance:** Blania predicts that nearly 99.9% of all internet traffic will eventually be AI-driven, making human-to-human interaction a premium feature.
    * **The Full Stack:** The ecosystem consists of **World ID** (the identity protocol), **World Chain** (the blockchain), and **World App** (the consumer-facing wallet and interface).
* **Privacy and Biometrics:** Blania addresses the controversy surrounding biometric data collection, calling privacy concerns the project's biggest misconception.
    * **Fundamental Anonymity:** The system is designed to be mathematically anonymous and privacy-preserving through advanced cryptography.
    * **Necessity of Biometrics:** Blania argues that biometrics are the only technical solution capable of solving the "Proof of Human" problem at a global scale.
* **Platform Integrations:** World ID is currently being integrated into major platforms.
    * **Tinder Japan:** A live integration allows users to prove they are real humans, verify profile pictures, and confirm their age.
    * **Future Verticals:** Key areas for integration include social networks, dating apps, gaming, and ticketing (e.g., Ticketmaster) to eliminate bot interference.

### Merge Labs: $250M Seed for Neural Research
* **Strategic Investment:** Merge Labs recently raised a $250 million seed round with direct investment from OpenAI (not its venture arm), signifying a deep research collaboration.
* **Mission and Technology:** The lab aims to bridge artificial and biological intelligence through high-bandwidth Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs).
    * **Technical Differentiation:** Unlike Neuralink’s mechanical threads, Merge Labs focuses on the combination of **ultrasound and molecular sensors** to interface with the brain.
    * **High-Bandwidth Goals:** The long-term objective is to move beyond the current "few thousand neurons" limit to interface with the entire brain, enabling significantly higher data transfer than a keyboard or screen.
* **Roadmap:** The company is currently structured as a research lab with a projected 15-year horizon.
    * **Phase 1:** Focused research on neural read/write capabilities.
    * **Phase 2:** Medical applications to assist those with disabilities or sensory loss.
    * **Phase 3:** Broad consumer applications as the successor to AR/VR technology.

### Operational Philosophy and Growth Metrics
* **Network Scaling:** World currently has 40 million users, with 17 million verified via "The Orb." Blania notes that the project's mission requires reaching hundreds of millions to billions to be truly successful.
* **Privacy as a "Fail-Safe":** One of the company's core operating principles is "fail-safe privacy." Blania believes a single data leak could "kill the company in a second," leading to a culture of extreme obsession with cryptographic security.
* **Leadership and Sam Altman:** Blania and Sam Altman have worked together for six years.
    * **Cadence:** They hold weekly in-depth strategy sessions covering every major decision, including hiring, fundraising, and partnerships.
    * **The "Hardcore" Approach:** Blania describes himself as "completely hardcore," forgoing social and personal life for the next five years to focus on the window of time leading up to the Singularity.

### The Future of Social Platforms
* **The Decay of X (Twitter):** Blania predicts that platforms like X will become "unusable" within months to two years unless they implement a technical proof-of-human solution.
* **Distinguishing vs. Combating:** The goal is not to eliminate bots—as Blania believes that is a "lost cause"—but to enable users to distinguish between authentic human content and AI agents.
* **Agentic Systems:** As AI systems become more agentic and open-source, the speed of platform degradation is expected to go "exponential."
