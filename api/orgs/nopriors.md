### TL;DR
Serval is a 2.5-year-old enterprise AI platform providing coding agents and guardrails for IT departments to automate internal workflows, directly challenging legacy service management systems like ServiceNow. Co-founder and CEO Jake Stauch explains that by combining AI-generated code with strict enterprise controls—such as deterministic approvals, staging environments, and audit trails—Serval enables non-engineers across the enterprise to safely build internal software.

### Company Background & Founding Vision
* **Core Product & Mission:** Serval offers enterprise automation and service management through codegen agents, functioning as an AI platform for IT departments to build internal products for HR, finance, legal, and people teams.
* **Origin Story:** 
    * CEO Jake Stauch discovered the pain point while working at Verkada (enterprise physical security products), where IT teams repeatedly named ServiceNow as their most expensive and hated expense.
    * Started 2.5 years ago by Jake Stauch and co-founder Alex in a living room with the deliberate ambition to build the largest enterprise software company of all time and directly replace ServiceNow.
* **Headcount Growth:** Scaled from 20 employees in January to **200 people** today, with hiring velocity expected to increase further.

### Product Architecture & Enterprise Guardrails
* **Code as Automation:** Serval's core insight is that code is the best way to express automation, and AI makes it possible for non-technical users to leverage code rather than painful drag-and-drop workflow builders.
* **The Boundary and Guardrails:** Serval acts as the security layer and ticketing system, allowing enterprises to grant elevated permissions through strict deterministic approval cycles, staging/approval gates, and full audit trails.
* **Catalyst Agent:** An internal management agent built on top of Serval that helps admins and IT pros build underlying automations, configure approval rules, permission checks, and build policies through natural language.
* **Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC):** Unlike consumer agents or unconstrained enterprise AI tools, Serval enforces a traditional engineering SDLC model with dev, staging, and production environments, user acceptance testing, and review gates.

### Go-To-Market & Implementation Strategy
* **Deployment Team:** Serval deploys both a "deployment strategist" and a "deployed software engineer" on-site with customers to map workflows, implement solutions, and manage migration.
* **Pro-Serval:** An ongoing automation engineering service team that embeds with customers after initial deployment to support continuous automation demands.
* **Targeting the C-Suite:** Serval sells top-down to IT leadership and C-levels, pitching the platform as a career-defining move for IT leaders (e.g., Fox Corporation adopted Serval company-wide, and their lead migration engineer won MVP of the entire company).
* **Decentralized Product Development:** Serval has no dedicated product function; instead, engineers are decentralized and immersed in customer Slack channels (with most engineers on at least one customer call per day) to build features iteratively based on real-time feedback.

### Industry Perspectives & The SaaS Landscape
* **Personal vs. Enterprise Agents:** While consumer "personal agents" excel in SMB and mid-market settings, large enterprises require strict permissions and centralized IT control; Stauch predicts enterprise and personal work agents will remain distinct (analogous to corporate vs. personal email).
* **Token Spend and ROI:** While CIOs experienced an initial "token maxing" era followed by pullbacks to measure ROI, long-term spend is projected to rise; Serval prices on workflows run rather than raw tokens to better reflect actual work completed.
* **The "SaaS-pocalypse":** Stauch believes core systems of record (ERP, HRIS, ITSM, CRM) will not disappear because enterprise software maintenance is complex and expensive, but hundreds of smaller point-solution SaaS applications will consolidate into core platforms.

## The New Apex Predator: CrowdStrike CEO George Kurtz on Adversaries Armed With AI Agents

---

**TL;DR:** Adversaries are now deploying AI agents to execute faster, highly sophisticated attacks measured in seconds, forcing defenders to adopt frontier-style AI models and digital twins to secure the enterprise.

### The Threat Landscape: The Rise of the "Agent State"
- **Flattening the Adversary Pyramid:** George Kurtz notes that AI models with stripped guardrails have effectively blown up the traditional "pyramid of pain" (hacktivists, e-crime, nation-states). 
  - **The New Apex Predator:** Low-skill hacktivists now wield capabilities previously restricted to nation-states through automated AI agents, creating what Kurtz calls the "agent state."
- **Speed of Inference:** Breakout times have plummeted from days and hours to an average of 29 minutes, with instances as fast as 27 seconds.
- **Attacks Are Prompts, Not Malware:** Adversaries rely on prompt engineering to dynamically dictate attack direction, reconnaissance, and lateral movement, generating entirely unique signatures.
- **Global Actor Activity:** North Korea is actively stealing information to fund its economy, China is leveraging AI heavily, and e-crime actors are universally accelerating attack velocity.

### Defensive Strategy & The Safe Mind Initiative
- **The Hugging Face Wake-Up Call:** While the Hugging Face incident demonstrated an end-to-end agentic attack escaping its sandbox, Kurtz emphasizes that the true takeaway was that defenders lacked frontier-level defensive AI capabilities because strong guardrails on state-of-the-art lab models often block basic cybersecurity tasks (e.g., generating a PowerPoint about security).
- **CrowdStrike’s Safe Mind Lab:** CrowdStrike partnered with Nvidia to build an internal frontier-style lab focused entirely on security models, featuring red-teaming models (**Red Tempest**) and blue-teaming models (**Blue Solano** leveraging **NemoTron-class** models).
- **Digital Twins for Defense:** Following Jensen Huang’s insistence, CrowdStrike builds digital twins of customer environments in virtual worlds to run continuous red-versus-blue iterations until the red agent runs out of turns.
- **The Power of Proprietary Data:** CrowdStrike’s models are post-trained on over a decade of proprietary security data (including 15 years of MDR service data), yielding high security performance at a fraction of the cost of general frontier models (e.g., $10 down to 3 cents for remediation).

### Enterprise Security & AI Adoption
- **Shadow AI Explosion:** Enterprises face massive blind spots regarding rogue AI agents running with elevated identities and downloading unvetted packages (e.g., NPM packages). 
- **AIDR Instrumentation:** CrowdStrike introduced Falcon AIDR to instrument agents from birth, tracking sub-agents, tool calls, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) server interactions to apply enterprise policy and blocking rules.
- **Security as the Gas Pedal:** For the first time in technology shifts, security is acting as an enabler ("the gas pedal, not the brake") because organizations are racing to secure AI so they can deploy it faster.

### Open-Weight Models vs. Frontier Labs
- **Open-Weight Dominance in the Enterprise:** CIOs are experiencing token-maxing bill shock with frontier models. Consequently, many large enterprises are shifting toward open-weight models (like Qwen, GLM, or NemoTron) for standard operations, reserving frontier models only for high-alpha tasks (like financial trading).
- **The Distillation Debate:** While frontier labs attempt to implement anti-distillation measures, Kurtz argues the genie is out of the bottle and open-weight models will continue to thrive globally due to cost and resourcefulness.
- **AI Sovereignty & Trust:** Customers demand strict data privacy and reject sending sensitive information to external frontier labs for training, making localized and platform-managed security models critical.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
- **George Kurtz** predicts that within **2 years**, local models running directly on user computers will become ubiquitous, combined with last-mile routing to frontier models.
- **George Kurtz** predicts that open-weight and open-source models will become the de facto "operating system of AI" in the enterprise due to unsustainable token costs at the frontier.

- > "Trust is built in drips and lost in buckets." — **George Kurtz**
- > "The only way to fight AI today is with better AI." — **George Kurtz**

### Scientific Discovery & Mathematical Breakthroughs
- **AI-Generated New Knowledge:** Recent milestones in mathematics demonstrate that AI can definitively generate new knowledge rather than just retrieve it.
  - OpenAI recently published resolved results for nearly 400 significant, open mathematical problems.
  - Domain experts reviewing the proofs note surprising and sophisticated applications of ideas and inter-domain connections rather than brute-force mechanical case-checking (unlike historical proofs like the Four Color Theorem).
- **Application to Biology and Drug Discovery:** Scientific breakthroughs in mathematics and theoretical physics serve as a leading indicator for what is possible in biology, where deep learning physics (compute + data + algorithms) can accelerate discovery.
  - Drug discovery is highlighted as the most critical and impactful application of AI to improve human quality of life.
  - Neural networks are exceptionally well-suited to crack the "language of biology," enabling massive parallelism and dramatic shortening of a traditionally 15-year, $1.5 billion pipeline.
  - Non-biological bottlenecks in the drug pipeline—such as writing regulatory filings and clinical trial documentation—can also be streamlined by AI.

### AI Agents, Form Factors, and the "No UI" Future
- **Proactive Productivity with Dots:** OpenAI's "Dots" is an always-on, always-working proactive agent designed to track uncompleted tasks, monitor Slack and inbox feeds, manage scheduling conflicts, and anticipate user needs.
  - The form factor changes how users work by running continuously in the background even when the user stops working.
- **Consumer vs. Enterprise Market Pressures:** While consumer assistants focus on mass-market scale, free-tier availability (such as ChatGPT's memory features for over 1.2 billion consumers), and features like payments and travel, workplace AI prioritizes peak intelligence, admin controls, security toggles, and paying higher compute costs for higher productivity returns.
- **Dynamic and Invisible User Interfaces:** The ultimate UI for AI is no UI at all. 
  - GPT-6 introduces intelligent, generative UI where the AI crafts custom, bespoke visual interfaces on the fly (such as flight booking options or assembly guides) tailored precisely to the moment and user request.

- **The Physics of Cybersecurity:** Cybersecurity dynamics are shifting into a critical "defender's window" where peak frontier AI capabilities allow defenders to proactively find and fix deep-seated historical flaws in human-written code.
- **OpenAI Internal Security Response:** During model releases, OpenAI pauses approximately 25% of its production engineers from feature work, assigning them exclusively to use peak frontier models to red-team and secure infrastructure until vulnerabilities are saturated.
- **Tech Debt and Code Resilience:** Decades of internet infrastructure built for resilience rather than security have left immense technical debt (highlighted by single-maintainer open source vulnerabilities). AI enables formal verification of software and the generation of secure-by-default code from inception.

### Human Agency and the Future of AGI
- **Keeping Humans in the Driver's Seat:** OpenAI's core mission remains ensuring AGI benefits all of humanity by keeping humans in control and amplifying human agency.
  - Models are jagged and surpass humans in specific domains, but human judgment, values, and oversight function similarly to a CEO or board member directing specialized teams of domain experts.
- **Alignment via Debate:** Techniques like debate and iterative amplification (conceptually discussed since 2017) train AIs to provide verifiable arguments and counter-arguments, ensuring humans can easily oversee complex outputs without needing to manually verify every detail.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
- **Cyber vs. Software Spend:** Security spend should vastly outstrip software spend as software becomes cheap and commoditized, making security, reliability, and resilience the true drivers of value — Greg Brockman

* **Initial Insight:** Recognized early on the arrival of foundation models and the critical need to pour more compute into models at test time, bringing AlphaGo-style superhuman scaling to language modeling.

### The Modern AI Chip Landscape & ASIC Ecosystem
* **The "Zoo" of Options:** The current chip market features a diverse spectrum of options, though many AI ASICs remain relatively identical in underlying architecture.
* **Hyperscaler Efforts:** Major players rely on proprietary internal chips developed alongside back-end delivery houses like Broadcom (a $2 trillion company):
    * **Google:** TPU (pioneered over 10 years ago)
    * **Meta:** MTIA
    * **Microsoft:** Maya
    * **OpenAI:** Jalapeno
* **Common Architectural Threads:** Most existing GPUs (NVIDIA, AMD) and ASICs utilize HBM (high-bandwidth DRAM memory), tensor cores for matrix multiplications, and TSMC advanced packaging.
* **The Abstraction/Handoff Model:** Traditional chip design involves architects conceiving circuits (like tensor cores for matrix math), front-end designers writing RTL code, and Broadcom/foundries performing synthesis and physical layout (GDS2 file formatting, metal layers, and transistor placement).

### Fractile's Technical Bets & Full-Stack Agility
* **Full-Stack Integration:** Unlike outsourced models, Fractile maintains an in-house team spanning workload understanding, front-end design, physical design, backend implementation, and advanced packaging.
* **The SRAM-to-DRAM Evolution:** 
    * **Initial Approach:** Built SRAM-based chips (similar to Groq or Cerebras) leveraging on-chip high-bandwidth memory for thousands of tokens per second.
    * **Scaling Concerns:** Faced scalability limits regarding growing context lengths in late 2023 and 2024.
    * **DRAM "Skunk Works":** Moved toward working with memory vendors to achieve extremely high bandwidth access to higher capacity, lower-cost DRAM memories. A platform uniting DRAM scalability with Groq/Cerebras-style speed is set to ramp in the second half of next year.
* **Workload-Driven Design:** Fractile collaborates with frontier players to anticipate scaling vector trends and align chip development with rapidly changing model architectures.

### Compressing Design Cycles & Semiconductor Realities
* **AI-Forward Chip Design:** Using AI internally shortens front-end design workflows, but fundamental physical and financial constraints remain.
* **Industry Bottlenecks:** 
    * **Fab Cycle Times:** 3 to 5 months to get chips back from foundry partners (TSMC/Samsung) even in super-hot lot scenarios.
    * **Payoff Periods:** Chips require a 3 to 5-year amortization window to remain financially viable, limiting the feasibility of shipping fundamentally new silicon every few weeks.
* **Strategic Value:** The primary competitive advantage is compressing the latency between observing a workload shift and realizing that bet in volume, aiming for a 3 to 6-month competitive window over rivals.

## Why Diffusion Will Win AI Inference with Inception Co-Founder and CEO Stefano Ermon

### TL;DR
Inception co-founder and CEO Stefano Ermon discusses how his company is applying parallel diffusion architecture to discrete text and code generation as a faster, hardware-efficient alternative to autoregressive LLMs. By leveraging diffusion-based models like their Mercury series, Inception achieves high-throughput, low-latency inference on standard NVIDIA GPUs while maintaining performance parity with frontier speed-optimized models.

### Academic Origins and the Pivot from GANs to Diffusion
- **Early Generative Modeling Research:** Started at Stanford in 2014 working on generative models of MNIST digits when the field was niche and focused primarily on unsupervised feature learning.
- **Invention of Score-Based Models:** Developed score-based generative models in 2019 with PhD student Yang Song (which became modern diffusion models) to replace unstable GANs with a principled iterative denoising framework.
- **Academic Roots of Industry Breakthroughs:** Ermon highlighted that many foundational AI breakthroughs—including diffusion models, FlashAttention, and DPO (Direct Preference Optimization)—originated in academic labs before transforming industry standards.
- **The 2024 Discrete Diffusion Breakthrough:** Published research proving that transformer-based diffusion models could match the perplexity and quality of autoregressive models at the GPT-2 scale while generating text 10 times faster through parallel token generation.

### Autoregressive Limitations vs. Diffusion Inference Scaling
- **The Memory Bandwidth Bottleneck:** Autoregressive models generate tokens sequentially (left-to-right), making inference memory-bound and mapping poorly to GPUs which spend most of their time moving weights rather than performing arithmetic.
- **Parallel Inference Architecture:** Diffusion models use iterative, coarse-to-fine denoising where many tokens are processed simultaneously at inference time, matching the GPU-friendly workloads of training.
- **Intelligence Per Watt Economics:** Ermon argues that the bitter lesson of AI is that parallel solutions eventually win; diffusion scales more efficiently during test-time compute reasoning and RL post-training rollouts.
- **Data Efficiency Advantages:** Academic literature suggests diffusion models perform data augmentation by training on multiple noisy views of the same data, making them more data-efficient than autoregressive models when data becomes a bottleneck.

### Inception Company Profile, Stack, and Commercial Traction
- **Company Scale:** Founded ~2 years ago, Inception employs approximately 50 people split between research, product, and engineering.
- **The Mercury Model Series:** Inception's Mercury models perform on par with frontier speed-optimized models (like Anthropic's Haiku and OpenAI's Flash, Mini, and Nano models) while offering superior generation speed.
- **Custom Software Infrastructure:** Because standard serving engines like vLLM or SGLang cannot run diffusion-based LLMs, Inception built its own end-to-end serving engine and proprietary stacks for SFT, RLHF, and RL.
- **Voice Agent Use Cases:** Customers like OpenCall use Inception's diffusion models for voice AI pipelines, replacing custom hardware setups (like Cerebras chips) with standard NVIDIA GPUs while preserving low-latency budgets for complex chain-of-thought prompting.
- **Addressing the Competitive Moat:** Inception protects its IP through trade secrets, proprietary serving and training stacks, and continuous feedback loops with real-world enterprise customers.

### Future Projections & Strategic Roadmap
- **Addressable Latency Market:** Ermon estimates that 20% to 30% of AI workloads on platforms like OpenRouter require strict latency guarantees where diffusion-based models can capture market share.
- **Controllability and Steering:** Ermon notes that diffusion models allow external reward functions and constraints to steer generation from the very beginning (coarse-to-fine), presenting novel product interfaces that are impossible with traditional autoregressive models.

> — Stefano Ermon
