* **Coalition Purpose:** Formed around 2021 by the World Economic Forum, the First Movers Coalition aggregates collective purchase demand for clean tech by 2030 to give investors and project developers commercial confidence.
* **Hard-to-Abate Sectors:** The initiative covers seven high-emissions sectors—aluminum, steel, cement/concrete (materials); shipping, trucking, aviation (mobility); and carbon dioxide removal (cross-cutting).

### Commercializing Carbon Recycling and Sustainable Fuels
* **LanzaTech Process:** **Jennifer Holmgren** explains that LanzaTech captures carbon monoxide and CO2 from steel mills and uses bacteria fermentation taking seconds to convert waste gas into 90% protein animal feed and ethanol.
* **Commercial Scale & Products:**

* **Investment Examples:** 
  * **Hera (Steel):** Backed by Khosla for its single-step, low-equipment capex architecture producing high-purity iron or commodity steel.
  * **Verdigy (Hydrogen):** Repurposed a chlor-alkali system designed for PVC pipe manufacturing to produce green hydrogen at $3 per kilogram without subsidies.

### The AI Landscape and Khosla’s Contrarian Investment Philosophy

*   **The OpenAI Gamble:** In early 2019, Khosla Ventures committed a $50 million check to OpenAI—double the size of any initial check Vinod Khosla had written in his 40-year career. 
    *   **The LP Apology:** Alongside the check, Khosla sent an apology letter to his Limited Partners (LPs), admitting the investment made no logical sense as OpenAI was then a non-profit with no business plan or product lines.

*   **Geographic Concentration:** Silicon Valley remains the absolute epicenter of AI innovation. 
    *   **The Unicorn Divide:** SF Bay Area accounts for 91% of all AI unicorns globally. New York is a distant second at just 2%.
*   **Scaling and Token Dynamics:** Khosla predicts exponential growth in token volume (quantity of inference) alongside rapidly declining costs per token. While "intelligence per token" (quality) will rise more modestly, progress in AI memory and continuous learning will become routine engineering milestones.

---

### Transforming Healthcare, Education, and the Power of "Agency"

*   **The End of Human Doctors:** Khosla predicts that within five years, human doctors will only be required for interventional medicine (such as surgery or setting broken bones).
    *   **Triage Accuracy:** Standard ChatGPT has a 20% to 30% triage error rate (similar to human GP error rates). However, by layering domain-specific guardrails over models like GPT-5, the triage error rate drops to nearly zero.
    *   **India Non-Profit Initiative:** Khosla is actively working on a non-profit project to deliver AI-driven healthcare across India within the next two years.
*   **"Founder Mode" on Personal Challenges:** Khosla highlighted GitLab's founder, who upon receiving a terminal cancer diagnosis, went "Founder Mode" on his illness. *   **Fostering "Agency" Over Rote Education:** 
    *   **The Real Value of College:** Universities must pivot away from lecturing from obsolete textbooks. Khosla advises institutions like IIT Delhi to scrap traditional lecture halls and open more dorms and meeting rooms to foster debate, curiosity, and "agency"—the proactive mindset to solve problems rather than complain about them.

---

### The Robotics Revolution and Severe Economic Deflation

*   **The "ChatGPT Moment" for Robotics:** Khosla expects a major physical AI breakthrough in the next 2 to 5 years, leading to highly functional humanoid robots capable of doing household tasks like cooking.
    *   **Automotive-Scale Manufacturing:** Humanoid robots will become the dominant physical form factor. They will be built in automotive-style automated factories, driving acquisition costs down dramatically to the raw cost of materials.
*   **A Highly Deflationary 2040:** By 2040, the baseline cost of living will plummet. Core services like healthcare, education, legal representation, and entertainment will become virtually free.
*   **A Warning to India’s IT and BPO Sector:** India’s massive IT services and offshore business process outsourcing (BPO) industries "will be gone" due to AI automation. To survive, India must pivot toward becoming the global hub for deploying and integrating AI systems.

---

### Overcoming the AI Energy Bottleneck: Fusion and Geothermal

*   **A Five-Year Power Crunch:** AI's massive data center power consumption was not forecasted by utility planners. Consequently, energy will bottleneck AI scaling for the next five years. However, this is a short-term issue that will be fully resolved by the early 2030s as new capacity comes online.
*   **The 2018 Fusion Bet:** Khosla Ventures invested in Bob Momgaard’s MIT spin-out (Commonwealth Fusion Systems) in 2018. 
    *   **Superconductors as the Catalyst:** The bet was unlocked by high-temperature superconductors, which allowed for 20 Tesla magnets. This technology effectively shrank the required experimental reactor size to 1/50th of previous designs, accelerating research cycles.
    *   **The Fusion Timeline:** Khosla is highly confident that within two years, the scientific community will agree that fusion works, and within five years, they will agree it is economically viable.
*   **Fission vs. Fusion:** Khosla views nuclear fission as politically unfeasible in the West due to endless local lawsuits. Once fusion or super-hot geothermal energy (tapping heat above 400°C) is commercialized, the need for fission disappears.

## Hard truths about building in the AI era | Keith Rabois (Khosla Ventures)

- Channel: Lenny's Podcast
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xCd9ykretlg
- Published: 2026-04-12
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260412.html#video-xCd9ykretlg
- Speakers: Keith Rabois (Managing Director, Khosla Ventures); Lenny Rachitsky (Host, Lenny's Podcast)

---

**TL;DR**
Building a world-class company requires identifying "barrels"—individuals who independently drive initiatives from inception to success—and maintaining a relentless operating tempo that offsets the complacency naturally generated by success.

### High-Performance Talent Strategies
* **The Barrel Framework:** Rabois distinguishes between "barrels" and "ammunition." 
    * **Barrels:** Rare individuals who can take an idea from inception to completion "come hell or high water." A company’s capacity to innovate is limited by its number of barrels, not its total headcount.
    * **Ammunition:** Supporting talent (PMs, engineers, designers). Stacking ammunition behind a limited number of barrels creates a "collaboration tax" that slows the organization down.
* **Hiring "Undiscovered Talent":** Startups cannot win by outbidding incumbents for consensus talent. 
    * **Finding Alpha:** Look for high-potential individuals with "no data points" (often younger talent) whom large companies' "black box" recruiting systems will miss.
    * **Promoting from Within:** Successful companies like Ramp and Fair often skip hiring senior external VPs in favor of grooming internal talent (e.g., former Chiefs of Staff) into executive roles.
* **Ruthless Referencing:** Rabois advocates for extreme due diligence in hiring.
    * **The 20-Reference Rule:** High-performing CEOs (like Tony Xu at DoorDash) may perform 20 references for a single senior hire.
    * **Framing the Question:** Don't ask if a candidate was a "good employee"; ask if they are "capable of being a world-class entrepreneur." 
    * **The Persuasion Test:** Ask candidates why they were unable to persuade their previous CEO to adopt their strategic ideas to test their influence and clarity.

### The Evolution of Roles in the AI Era
* **The De-valuation of the PM:** Conventional Product Management (managing year-long roadmaps) is becoming obsolete due to the rapid pace of AI capability shifts.
    * **The New Standard:** The future PM role is more like a CEO, requiring intense business acumen and the ability to decide *what* to build and *why*, rather than just managing inputs.
* **The Rise of the Commercial Engineer:** AI is "eating the middle" of software development. 
    * **The "Chef" Model:** Like a head chef, technical leaders will spend less time "cooking" (coding) and more time "sampling" and editing AI-generated work.
    * **Executive Token Usage:** In top organizations, the CMO—not just engineers—is often the top consumer of AI tokens, using the technology to ship work product directly without deputies.
* **Design as Alpha:** As building becomes easier, storytelling and snappier design become the primary differentiators to "cut through the clutter."

### Contrarian Operating Principles
* **Relentless Application of Force:** Rabois argues that the CEO’s primary job is to offset complacency.
    * **The Success Trap:** The more a company wins, the more comfortable employees become. A CEO should actually push *harder* when things are going well. 
    * **Support vs. Criticism:** When a company is struggling, a leader should be a supportive coach. When a company is thriving, a leader must be highly critical to isolate future problems.
* **Criticizing in Public:** Unlike traditional management advice, Rabois advocates for public critique.
    * **System Optimization:** Public feedback ensures the entire team knows a standard is being upheld and allows others to offer collaborative solutions.
* **Rejecting Psychological Safety:** Rabois dismisses "psychological safety" as a core tenet for startups. 
    * **Winning Mindset:** High-performance machines are modeled after championship sports teams (e.g., Michael Jordan’s Bulls) where the focus is exclusively on winning rather than comfort.
* **The "Anti-Customer" Stance:** For consumer and SMB products, Rabois advises against talking to customers. 
    * **Subconscious Logic:** Customers often don't know why they make decisions. Relying on their feedback can lead to derivative, uninspired products. Focus instead on foundational insights and "selling tickets" (traction).

### Personal Productivity and Mindset
* **Extreme Consistency:** Rabois follows a "#NoDaysOff" mantra for both work and fitness, having missed only seven days of exercise in seven years.
* **The Power of Stress:** He recommends *The Upside of Stress* by Kelly McGonigal, arguing that stress is a biological necessity for those pursuing high-level success and happiness.
* **The iPad Workflow:** Since 2010, Rabois has operated entirely from an iPad and iPhone, citing the benefits of flexibility and the removal of "heavyweight" computing distractions.

> "The team you build is the company you build. If you have the right people, everything else will be easy. If you have the wrong people, everything else is going to be difficult." — **Keith Rabois**

> "High performance machines don’t have psychological safety. They’re about winning." — **Keith Rabois**

> "The single role for the CEO is offsetting that complacency. The better you’re doing, the more the CEO should push." — **Keith Rabois**

*   **Innovation Hub:** Khosla highlighted the speed of Indian startups, citing "Immergent," which reached a **$100 million ARR** in just eight months.
*   **2047 Vision:** By India’s 100th anniversary of independence, Altman predicts a world where the delta in technology will be greater than the gap between 1970 and today.

* **Software vs. Hardware Gap:** While "intelligence AI" (software) is ready today, "robotics AI" is approximately 5–7 years behind.
    * **Physical Interventions:** Complex surgeries and high-end physical diagnostics will still require human intervention (triage) or physical hospitals for the next decade.
    * **Infrastructure:** AI can eventually reduce costs in "hard" sectors like housing and transit by optimizing materials and enabling self-driving public transport (similar to a low-cost, AI-driven Uber/Ola service).

### Policy as the Primary Bottleneck
* **The "UPI" for Health and Education:** Khosla urges the Indian government to use the **Aadhaar/UPI** framework to deliver universal health and education services. 
* **Global Competition:** China has a centralized national goal for AI leadership; Khosla warns that if Indian voters or politicians reject AI due to fear of job losses, they will miss the massive welfare benefits.
* **Economic Mobility:** With the right policy, AI can drive unprecedented economic mobility by removing the cost barriers to elite education and professional advice.
