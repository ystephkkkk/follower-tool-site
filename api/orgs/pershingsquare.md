### TL;DR
Bill Ackman, founder of Pershing Square, discusses his concentrated, long-term investment strategy, the structural advantages of permanent capital, and how integrating a specialty insurance operation transforms his business model. He also shares insights on AI, his engagement on X (formerly Twitter), and how a personal family tragedy has shaped his recent philanthropic and technological focus.

### Investment Philosophy & Strategy
* **Definition of a Great Business:** **Royalty-like Annuities:** Ackman defines great businesses as those where markets could shut down for 20 years, yet one would know with enormous certainty that the company remains immensely valuable.
  * *Characteristics:* They look like royalty-collecting companies or annuities that grow at high rates without needing constant cash reinvestment.
  * *Exclusions:* Pershing Square avoids commodity-sensitive, interest-rate-sensitive, and regulatory-sensitive businesses.
* **Portfolio Concentration:** **Limited Opportunities:** Out of roughly 200 to 300 truly great businesses globally, very few become available at prices offering required returns.
  * *Scale:* Pershing Square manages a concentrated portfolio of 12 to 14 investments with a team of 48 people, including 9 investment professionals led by CIO Ryan Israel.
  * *Consensus Model:* Investment ideas are vetted by a two-person deep-dive team, two reviewers (including Ackman or Israel), and an objective remainder of the team; the firm requires collective consensus rather than individual P&L incentives.
* **Competitive Advantage of Permanent Capital:** **Escaping Short-Termism:** Mirroring Warren Buffett's playbook, Ackman emphasizes that most asset management capital is short-term and redemption-prone, forcing managers into volatility-intolerant behavior.
  * *COVID Deployment:* Permanent capital allowed Pershing Square to aggressively deploy capital during the COVID-19 market meltdown without redemption fears.

### Evolution of Activism & Market Dynamics
* **From Door-Busting to Collaboration:** **Reputational Equity:** While historically known as an activist who forced change, Ackman has not launched a traditional hostile activist campaign since 2016.
  * *Current Engagement:* Today, Pershing Square buys stakes and typically receives a thank-you note from CEOs, leveraging its reputation to act as a supportive, long-term owner rather than executing proxy contests.
* **Market Volatility & Indexation:** **Deep Discounts on Misses:** Ackman notes that increasing indexation and leveraged short-term capital cause high-quality stocks to drop 30% to 40% on a short-term earnings miss, creating rare opportunities to acquire durable growth companies at discounted prices.

### Howard Hughes Corp & Permanent Capital Transformation
* **Pivoting to an Insurance Model:** **Replicating Berkshire Hathaway:** Pershing Square is transforming Howard Hughes Corp from a real estate developer into an insurance-driven compounder.
  * *Timeline & Targets:* Within a decade, Ackman aims for Howard Hughes to be 80%+ an insurance company and 20% real estate.
  * *Leadership Team:* Recruited top-tier insurance executives including Mark Brenesen (formerly Arch Capital Group), David Gansburg, and Lucy Fetto (former Vice Chairman/General Counsel of AIG) to run a sub-$2 billion capital specialty insurance company.
  * *Asset Management:* Upon closing on the insurer in June, Pershing Square immediately sold intermediate/long-term fixed-income assets, reinvested into short-term treasuries, and allocated a portion of equity to a common stock portfolio of top global businesses.

### Information Diet, AI, and Social Media
* **X (Twitter) as Real-Time Intelligence:** **Real-Time Arbitrage:** Ackman follows roughly 1,000 people on X, utilizing it as his primary real-time news source that feels "a week to two weeks ahead" of conventional media.
  * *Seeking Dissenting Views:* He deliberately seeks out the most articulate, thoughtful voices on opposing sides of an issue, treating intellectual debate as the best path to truth—similar to stress-testing an investment by consulting the best short sellers.
* **Artificial Intelligence Outlook:** **Abundance and Creativity:** Ackman views AI as a democratizer of low-cost super intelligence, predicting an explosion in entrepreneurship and global GDP growth.
  * *Human Edge:* He argues that while LLMs can process existing knowledge, human creativity (such as Einstein's theory of relativity) remains uniquely irreplaceable.
  * *Portfolio Exposures:* Pershing Square holds stakes in AI-related infrastructure and beneficiaries, including Amazon (AWS), Meta, and Microsoft.

### Personal Tragedy & Neuro-Technology Focus
* **Daughter's Brain Hemorrhage:** **A New Mission:** Earlier in the year, Ackman's 26-year-old daughter suffered a severe brain hemorrhage from an undiagnosed arteriovenous malformation (AVM) while alone, leaving her under pressure for nearly 19 hours.
  * *Recovery Progress:* Though she lost ~98% of her vision due to optic nerve damage and initially lost basic motor/speech functions, she has retained cognitive faculties and is actively relearning to speak and walk.
* **Brain-Computer Interfaces & Biotech Institute:** **Investing in Neuro-Rehab:** Recognizing a lack of aggressive rehabilitation solutions in neurology, Ackman has assembled a 3.4-acre site in New York housing a 400,000-square-foot biotech building.
  * *Future Outlook:* He is actively recruiting top-tier researchers and backing brain-computer interface technologies (alongside leaders like Neuralink) to help restore sight to the blind and mobility to paraplegics.

## Pershing Square founder and CEO Bill Ackman Talks Howard Hughes Plans, IPOs and Anthropic |...

---

### TL;DR
Pershing Square founder and CEO Bill Ackman discussed his strategy for Howard Hughes Corp., the capitalization of insurance venture Vantage, Pershing Square USA's market discount, AI frontier models, and a new brain-care institute inspired by his daughter's recovery.

### Howard Hughes & Vantage Insurance Strategy
* **Taking a Page from Buffett:** Pershing Square is replicating Berkshire Hathaway's playbook by utilizing Vantage—a specialty insurer and reinsurer closed in June—as a cash-flow-generating vehicle with low leverage.
* **Leadership & Asset Allocation:** Mark Risan (former CEO of Arch Capital Group) serves as executive chair of Vantage, alongside David Gansburg and Lucy Vetto. 
* **Treasury & Equity Split:** Fixed income assets were immediately sold upon closing; approximately 65% of Vantage assets are in short-term treasuries and 35% in a managed common stock portfolio, with common stock exposure ultimately targeted at 40% to 45%.
* **Howard Hughes Real Estate Restructuring:** 
  * Howard Hughes is shifting from 100% equity financing to bringing in third-party partners (such as Brookfield or Blackstone) to reduce equity commitment in real estate by up to 80%.
  * The restructuring aims to free up **$2 billion to $3 billion** in equity by the end of next year to redeploy into Vantage.

### Pershing Square USA (PSUS) & Market Dynamics
* **Fund Structure & Cost:** PSUS is a liquid public closed-end fund charging no incentive fee, offering retail investors access to Pershing Square's strategy at the lowest cost globally.
* **Performance & Deployment:** Net Asset Value (NAV) is down about 3% from the IPO (with ~240 basis points driven by IPO costs); capital has been fully deployed into "super durable growth companies."
* **Discount and Valuation Realities:** PSUS shares trade at a 25% discount to NAV; Ackman attributes this to a marketing and awareness challenge rather than underlying portfolio weakness, pointing to discounted entry prices on holdings like Meta, S&P Global (bought at 21x earnings vs. typical 30x), and Intercontinental Exchange (bought at 17x vs. 24x).

### AI, Frontier Models, and IPOs
* **Anthropic and OpenAI:** Ackman praised Anthropic's rapid revenue ramp and product quality (noting internal use of Claude), questioning whether frontier model companies can maintain market presence against open-source and open-weight models.
* **Capital Intensity vs. Durability:** Pershing Square avoids fast-growing, capital-consuming AI companies where investors bet on future line crossings, preferring predictable businesses like Microsoft, Visa, and Mastercard.
* **Catastrophic Risk & Regulation:** Ackman is less concerned about regulated labs like Anthropic or OpenAI causing systemic harm—arguing that massive financial and legal disincentives provide self-regulation—and more concerned about rogue actors exploiting open-source models without guardrails.

### Medical Innovation & New Brain Institute
* **Daughter Lucy’s Recovery:** Ackman detailed the severe brain hemorrhage suffered by his daughter Lucy, who lost the ability to speak, walk, and see light, but is making progress through aggressive care, nutrition, and experimental mitochondrial therapy (which restored light perception to 22,000 in one eye).
* **New Brain Institute:** Inspired by Lucy's journey, Ackman and his wife are building a new institute focused on delivering direct clinical care, leveraging data generated by emerging brain-interface technologies (such as Neuralink, Precision Neuroscience, and Synchron) combined with AI data-processing capabilities.
