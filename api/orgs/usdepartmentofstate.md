## Secretary Marco Rubio Joins Breitbart News

**TL;DR:** Secretary of State Marco Rubio details the Trump administration’s America First foreign policy, emphasizing the defense of Western civilization, a renewed focus on the Western Hemisphere, energy independence, and the neutralization of Iran's conventional and nuclear threats.

### Defense of Western Civilization & Global Receptivity
* **Heritage and Shared Values:** 
  * **Civilizational Roots:** Secretary Rubio emphasized during his speech at the Acropolis in Athens that U.S. government, culture, and values are backed by thousands of years of Western civilization inherited from Greece, Rome, and Europe.
  * **Cultural Confidence:** Rubio noted that the West is the only civilization currently apologetic about its history and achievements, which negatively impacts foreign policy. He stressed the need to rediscover and embrace this shared history under President Trump.
* **International Receptivity:**
  * **Shifting Tides:** Matthew Boyle highlighted that European leaders, particularly in Greece (where Boyle interviewed the prime minister twice and multiple ministers), are slowly aligning with U.S. policies on security and migration.
  * **Political Obstacles:** Rubio confirmed increased private receptivity among foreign leaders, though many remain hesitant to openly support these views due to criticism from their domestic left, media, academia, and entrenched bureaucracies. He predicts the true shift will play out through elections.

### Energy Independence & Global Infrastructure
* **Greece's Strategic Position:** 
  * **Exploration and Transit:** Greece is uniquely positioned politically as open to energy exploration and geographically as a key transit corridor connecting Europe, Africa, and the Middle East to transport oil, gas, and electricity.
* **Lessons from European Vulnerability:**
  * **Russian Blackmail:** Rubio recalled how President Trump warned Germany during his first term about becoming vulnerable to Russian energy blackmail—a warning that was mocked at the time by European leaders and the media, but later validated when Vladimir Putin invaded Ukraine.
  * **Economic Stagnation:** Rubio criticized 20 years of "green hyperbole" and over-reliance on single energy sources, noting that high fuel and transport costs are currently driving economic stagnation across Europe.

### The Western Hemisphere & Foreign Assistance Reform
* **Focus on the "Backyard":**
  * **Geographic Reality:** Rubio stressed that despite instantaneous global communication, geography remains real, meaning instability in the Western Hemisphere impacts the U.S. faster than crises in Africa, the Middle East, or Asia.
  * **Pro-American Shifts:** Rubio pointed to a historic shift in Latin America, with countries like Colombia, Peru, and others electing pro-American, anti-drug-cartel leaders. He credited President Trump with growing the "red" pro-American map in the region.
* **Overhaul of Foreign Aid and USAID:**
  * **Strategic Alignment:** Rubio stated that pulling foreign aid away from independent organizations like USAID and bringing it into the State Department has ended the funding of anti-American global NGOs disguised as environmental or indigenous groups.
  * **Humanitarian Response:** Rubio highlighted that the administration's humanitarian response is significantly faster and more effective under the new model, citing successful rapid deployments for disasters in Venezuela, Jamaica, and Panama (noted in coordination with Ambassador to Panama Kevin Cabrera).

### Russia-Ukraine Conflict & Strategic Priorities
* **Core Territorial Dispute:**
  * **Donetsk Region:** Rubio explained that the war centers on the Ukrainian province of Donetsk, where Russia occupies about 89% of the area. Ukraine is defending the final 11% containing a critical defensive line protecting Kyiv.
  * **Battlefield Dynamics:** With Russian ground advances stalled, Moscow is using drone and ballistic missile strikes to destroy Ukrainian energy infrastructure ahead of winter, while Ukraine has retaliated by striking Russian oil and gas refineries deep into Moscow and St. Petersburg.
* **U.S. National Interests and Energy Interventions:**
  * **Diesel Moratorium:** Rubio explained that President Trump enacted a 30-day moratorium on sanctions to allow Russian diesel deliveries into the global marketplace to support the U.S. and global economies.
  * **Friction Points:** Rubio noted frustrations with Ukraine over strikes hitting energy vessels in the Black Sea carrying American oil, as well as frustration with Russian ballistic missile attacks on civilian apartment buildings in Kyiv.

### Iran, Middle East Strategy, and the "America First" Doctrine
* **Dismantling Iran's Conventional Shield:**
  * **Military Objectives:** Rubio stated that President Trump achieved his military objective to prevent Iran from building a nuclear weapon behind a conventional shield of tens of thousands of missiles and drones. The U.S. destroyed Iran's navy, air force, missile/drone factories, and air defenses.
  * **Strait of Hormuz Control:** Rubio noted that Iran failed to take the Straits of Hormuz hostage; currently, 13 to 14 million barrels of oil flow daily through the strait, and the U.S. military is escorting ships while Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent enforces crippling sanctions and a blockade preventing Iran from exporting a single barrel of oil.
* **Middle East Paradigm Shift:**
  * **Identifying the Root Cause:** Rubio argued that the Islamic regime in Tehran—through sponsors like Hizballah in Lebanon, Shia militias in Iraq, and the Houthis—is the central driver of instability, war, and conflict in the Middle East.
* **America First Foreign Policy:**
  * **Common Sense Governance:** Rubio defended the America First doctrine, stating that any nation's primary duty is to put its own national interest first. He affirmed that the U.S. will use its position as the largest economy, most powerful military, and most influential culture to advance American interests ahead of the United Nations or the global order.
