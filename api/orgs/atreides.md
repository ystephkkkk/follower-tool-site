## Cerebras CEO Andrew Feldman: Inside the Largest Chip Ever Built | TomorrowX Summit

---

**TL;DR:** Cerebras CEO Andrew Feldman set the record straight on its $20B+ OpenAI partnership—confirming sequential revenue growth and $200/watt token economics—while detailing how disaggregated inference (5x token output) and a dual hardware-cloud model will drive a major revenue and cash flow inflection in 2026–2028.

### OpenAI Partnership & Fast Token Economics
- **OpenAI Relationship Status:** Feldman dismissed social media rumors as misinformation, confirming OpenAI remains Cerebras's largest and most important customer.
  - Signed at the end of December 2025 ($20B+ contract; $25B in RPO noted by Gavin Baker), OpenAI revenue grew sequentially from Q2 to Q3, will grow again in Q4, and will continue growing throughout 2027.
- **$200/Watt Token Monetization:** Baker noted Wall Street often views $50–$60/watt ($50B–$60B/GW) as a revenue ceiling, but reports indicate OpenAI is selling Cerebras capacity to special customers for $200/watt ($200B/GW). Feldman confirmed, *"it's true."*
- **Hyperscaler Demand & Pricing Power:** Fast tokens on frontier models carry the highest gross margins because they are the most valuable to buyers.
  - Over the past six months, Cerebras signed **AWS, Microsoft, and Oracle** to resell fast tokens.
  - With power and space ~40% more expensive than in December 2025, new capacity is priced at a **40%–50% floor** above OpenAI's contract. Baker noted $25B in RPO would equate to $37B–$37.5B at today's prices, which Feldman said would be *"vastly higher."*

### Disaggregated Inference: 5x Data Center Output
- **Divide-and-Conquer Architecture:** Disaggregated inference splits workloads into **prefill** (processing the prompt) and **decode** (generating the answer). Using GPUs for prefill and Cerebras for decode generates **5x** as many tokens at the same speed from the same power footprint (e.g., a 10 MW data center).
- **Live Production & Universal Compatibility:** Cerebras has the world's first production deployment of disaggregated inference running with **AMD** on prefill and **Cerebras** on decode.
  - A software integration layer allows Cerebras to pair with any accelerator—including **NVIDIA GPUs, AWS Trainium, and ARM GPUs**—enabling retrofits into existing brownfield GPU data centers as well as greenfield sites.
- **Addressable Market:** Baker highlighted that if Cerebras were not supply-limited and attached to even 10% of accelerators in 3–4 years, it would represent tens of billions in revenue; Feldman stated Cerebras could attach to *"most"* GPUs/accelerators for inference.

### Wafer-Scale Architecture & Product Roadmap
- **First-Principles Bet:** Rather than making incremental GPU tweaks, Cerebras started with a clean sheet in late 2015/early 2016 to build the largest chip in compute history—**56x larger** than any prior chip—using **SRAM** instead of HBM/DRAM.
  - Baker noted this optimizes for memory bandwidth in a way that is uniquely hard to replicate, giving Cerebras a **15x–30x** (10x–30x) speed advantage in inference by getting weights to compute faster.
- **Three Consecutive Doublings:**
  - **CS4:** Just starting to ship; **2x** as fast as CS3.
  - **CS5:** Ships in summer; **2x** as fast as CS4.
  - **CS6:** Ships in summer 2028; at least **2x** as fast as CS5.

### Dual Business Model: Cloud vs. Hardware Cash Flow
- **Supply Chain & Q4/Q1 Ramp:** Following the December 25, 2025 OpenAI deal, Feldman flew to Taiwan to secure increased forecasts from TSMC and Cerebras's top 10–15 vendors, with new chips arriving in May/June. Data center delays pushed Q3 capacity into Q4 and Q1.
  - Baker noted Q4 revenue is up ~40% sequentially at the midpoint, and Feldman expressed *"high hopes"* for a similar uptick in Q1.
- **Cloud vs. Hardware Trade-offs:**
  - **Cloud:** Provides multi-year backlog and recurring revenue, but is capital-intensive with a 16–18 month payback period on gear.
  - **Hardware:** Lumpy (starts at zero each quarter) and requires customers to have their own data centers, but delivers immediate revenue recognition and strong cash flow.
- **Hyperscaler Hardware Deployments:** **Oracle, AWS, and Microsoft** will deploy Cerebras hardware in their own data centers, with the Oracle opportunity **more than double** AWS and Microsoft, beginning in **Q2 next year**.
  - Baker challenged the Street's consensus of a $2.5B cash burn next year given hardware's cash flow profile; Feldman confirmed hardware revenue will exceed cloud revenue in *"several"* quarters next year.

### Industry Perspective, Education & Community Impact
- **On Jensen Huang:** Feldman praised NVIDIA's CEO as *"the most successful CEO and the best CEO in the business"* over the last 15–20 years, calling his company-building *"the single most successful entrepreneurial act of my life."*
- **AI in Education:** Feldman highlighted that AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude can deliver free, individualized tutoring to any child—such as explaining long division 15 different ways—democratizing the elite model once reserved for figures like Alexander the Great (tutored by Aristotle).
- **Data Centers as Good Neighbors:** Though Cerebras is a data center tenant rather than a builder, Feldman said the company works transparently with local communities (such as a site two hours from Austin) to fund rounding-error community needs like high school football fields or 10 years of school power.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
- **OpenAI Revenue Growth:** OpenAI revenue will grow again in Q4 and continue growing throughout **2027** (Andrew Feldman).
- **CS5 & CS6 Roadmap:** CS5 will ship in **summer** at 2x CS4 speed, and CS6 will ship in **summer 2028** at least 2x CS5 speed (Andrew Feldman).
- **Oracle & Hardware Inflection:** Oracle hardware deployments will begin in **Q2 next year**, and hardware revenue will exceed cloud revenue in **several quarters next year** (Andrew Feldman).
- **Disaggregated Inference Attach Rate:** Within **3–4 years** (assuming no supply limits), Cerebras could be attached to **"most"** GPUs and accelerators for inference (Andrew Feldman).
- **Enterprise Transformation:** Within **3 years**, running an enterprise will be fundamentally different—organizations will manage AI agents, and financial books will be closed by agents (Andrew Feldman).

## Elon Musk on Why the Future Should Make You Glad to Be Alive | TomorrowX Summit

- Channel: iConnections
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DY8clym2-jQ
- Published: 2026-11-19

- Speakers: Elon Musk (CEO, Tesla and xAI); Gavin Baker (Managing Partner, Atreides); Antonio Gracias (Founder, Valor Equity Partners)

---

### TL;DR
Closing Day Two of the TomorrowX Summit from China alongside Atreides' Gavin Baker and Valor Equity Partners' Antonio Gracias, Elon Musk detailed how Starship's first ship catch unlocks a 100x reduction in space access costs, while outlining aggressive scaling across xAI's rewritten C/C++ training stack, Tesla's Optimus and Cybercab rollouts, the 100-million-square-foot TerraFab, and 0.5 GW/month AI compute deployment. Across all ventures, Musk emphasized doing the physical engineering work in-house, maximizing real-world usefulness over benchmarks, and building maximally truth-seeking AI.

### Starship, Reusability & Multiplanetary Civilization
* **First Ship Catch Milestone (Ship 42, Flight 15):** Following three prior booster catches (and one re-flown booster), SpaceX successfully caught the Starship upper stage for the first time. The ship returned in good condition requiring only minor repairs, though SpaceX will place this historic vehicle on public display rather than re-flying it.
* **The "Holy Grail" of Rocketry:** Unlike the partially reusable Space Shuttle—which discarded its main orange tank every flight and required ~9 months to refurbish the orbiter—Starship is designed for full, immediate airplane-like reusability.
* **Economic & Mass-to-Orbit Impact:** Full reusability will cut the cost of access to space by 100x (and eventually 1,000x), enabling a mass-to-orbit rate in the million-ton-per-year range.
* **Why Multiplanetary Life Matters:** Responding to Gavin Baker's prompt on the 25-year arc of SpaceX, Musk framed Mars/Moon colonization as "life insurance for life collectively" to preserve consciousness—noting human civilization is only ~5,500 years old (dating back to Ancient Sumerian cuneiform). Beyond defense against calamity, becoming a spacefaring civilization provides an inspiring future that makes people "glad to be alive."

### xAI, Grok 4.8 & Rebuilding the Training Stack
* **Rebuilding from Scratch:** Musk noted that he originally started xAI as a small side project (~half-a-dozen people) for large-model talent so Tesla could focus on real-world AI, and later spent ~1 year on a government/campaigning "side quest" (which Antonio Gracias also helped with). Returning to rebuild xAI (now part of SpaceX), Musk's team spent 6–8 months rewriting the entire training, reinforcement learning (RL), and inference stack from scratch in C and C++, replacing third-party "mediocre freeware" like Jax and SGLang.
* **AI Training as a Networking Problem:** Former Starlink and Starship software engineers now lead the AI stack. Coordinating hundreds of thousands of GPUs coherently is fundamentally a networking problem—directly transferable from building Starlink's space-based internet.
* **Grok 4.8 & "Usefulness Maxing":** Prompted by Baker on Grok 4.8, Musk stated the model is currently in the RL phase, with soft releases rolling out via Grokbot. Rather than "bench maxing," xAI is focused on "usefulness maxing"—rigorously curating data, tokenization, and RL question/answer pairs (noting that bad RL prompts make Grok's thinking traces look like "torture").

### Robotics, Autonomy & Silicon (Optimus, Cybercab, TerraFab)
* **Optimus:**
  * **Engineering Bottleneck:** Building hands and forearms with human-level fine motor skills is harder than engineering the rest of the robot combined. Musk cited John Carmack's benchmark: opening a toy RC car box, unscrewing the battery cover, inserting batteries, screwing it back on, and driving the car.
  * **Use Cases:** Initial deployment will target 24/7 (168 hours/week) heavy industry—such as mining and refining ore to secure US national supply chains—before moving into homes as tutors, babysitters, and household helpers.
* **Cybercab & Robotaxi:** Operating without steering wheels or pedals, Cybercabs are already running in Austin with zero serious injuries or accidents to date. SpaceX/Tesla are scaling carefully to avoid regulatory shutdowns while expanding across major Texas cities and Florida.
* **TerraFab:** Baker noted that while Tesla started with "C or D team" suppliers in 2015–2016, TerraFab is starting with the "A teams" of semiconductor equipment. Announced earlier this year, TerraFab will become the largest building of any kind on Earth at 100 million square feet. Musk plans to take higher technical risks and iterate faster than risk-averse incumbent fabs.

### Terrestrial & Orbital Compute Scaling
* **Capex Efficiency & "Army X":** Addressing Baker's note on Wall Street skepticism over the cost of 10 GW of compute, Musk stated industry consensus estimates are off by 2x or more—estimating xAI's actual cost at ~60% of consensus. Rather than contracting everything out, xAI's in-house "Army X" team does the physical data center work 24/7 in Memphis (e.g., pulling power conduits, plugging fiber, swapping nodes), training former Starbucks baristas, Target, and In-N-Out workers.
* **Power & Deployment Cadence:** Tesla Megapacks are used to smooth volatile GPU training loads and prevent turbine shaft damage. xAI is currently bringing on ~0.5 GW/month of AI compute (~220,000 GB300s, or >500 MW at the power-plant level).
* **Orbital Compute (Star Mind):** Musk argued Star Mind satellites are *simpler* than Starlink V3 communication satellites (which carry complex phased arrays and K/Ku/W/V/E-band radios, alongside ~30,000 lasers across SpaceX's >11,000-satellite fleet). Star Mind pairs solar panels (~250 W/m²) with double-sided radiators (~1,200 W/m² radiating into 3° Kelvin space), requiring 5x solar panel area to radiator area, housing a compute unit ~1/4 the size of an NVL72 rack with full NVL72 capability.

### Neuralink, AI Safety & Industry Leadership
* **Neuralink (Telepathy & Blindsight):** Responding to Antonio Gracias, Musk shared that Neuralink (a private company of several hundred people) has implanted devices in over 30 patients. *Telepathy* enables tetraplegics and quadriplegics to control phones, computers, and robotic arms (even feeding themselves) via thought; long-term, it aims to bridge severed spinal signals to restore walking. *Blindsight* pairs camera glasses with a visual cortex implant to restore sight to completely blind individuals, including those blind from birth.
* **AI Safety & Truth-Seeking:** Discussing the summit's theme of ensuring P(Boom) > P(Doom), Musk argued the single most important factor for AI safety is building AI that is "maximally truth-seeking" and curious. Forcing AI to believe politically correct falsehoods leads to dangerous conclusions—paraphrasing Voltaire: *"those who believe absurdities can commit atrocities."*
* **Jensen Huang & NVIDIA:** Asked by Gracias about Jensen Huang, Musk called him a "Michael Jordan-level" founder-entrepreneur and compared NVIDIA's situation to the Chicago Bulls firing Michael Jordan mid-winning streak, expressing hope that Huang and NVIDIA find their way back together.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Optimus Authentic Demo:** Will occur in **1 to 2 months** (not staged or pre-programmed) — *Elon Musk*.
* **Optimus Household Chores:** Will be capable of everyday domestic tasks like taking out the trash and washing/putting away dirty dishes in **12 to 18 months** — *Elon Musk*.
* **Cybercab California Approval:** Expects regulatory approval to operate Cybercabs in California **early next year** — *Elon Musk*.

* **Space Solar Manufacturing:** Aiming for **200 GW/year** of photovoltaics for space manufacturing (~40% of average US power usage) — *Elon Musk*.

## Retail, AI and the American Dream | Gavin Baker, Antonio Gracias & Ron Biscardi | TomorrowX Summit

- Speakers: Gavin Baker (Manager, Atreides); Antonio Gracias (CEO, Valor Equity Partners); Ron Biscardi (CEO, iConnections)

---

### TL;DR
TomorrowX Summit launched at Austin's Moody Center to bridge the gap between retail investors (FinX, Substack, Reddit) and AI management teams, arguing that retail is now the most powerful force in the market. Hosts Gavin Baker, Antonio Gracias, and Ron Biscardi—in conversation with CNBC's Sara Eisen—framed the event's dual mission: democratizing institutional-grade access to over 100 private AI companies while shifting public sentiment on AI from anxiety ("P-doom") to optimism ("P-boom").

### The Rise of Retail & Origin of TomorrowX
* **The Catalyst:** Gavin Baker (Atreides) traced the idea to a day in January when a prestigious bulge-bracket investment bank downgraded a semiconductor capital equipment stock to a "strong sell," slashing its price target and expecting a 5–10% drop. Instead, the stock rose 7% after a Substack/X writer named Satriny included it in a basket and wrote one line about it.
* **Democratizing Investing:** Having observed FinX for 6–7 years, Baker argued that retail is now the most powerful force in the market. TomorrowX was created to connect the X accounts, Substacks, and Reddit communities that move markets directly with company management teams.
* **Institutional-Grade Access for Retail:** Ron Biscardi (iConnections), who runs the largest capital introduction platform for institutional investors (sovereign wealth funds, endowments, foundations), partnered with Baker and Gracias to bring institutional-grade convening and event technology to everyday investors in a basketball stadium rather than a hotel ballroom.
  * TomorrowX signed up over 1,000 people in the last 3 days.
  * The event features over 100 private companies (almost all represented by their CEOs) and awarded over 1,000 scholarships to active, constructive contributors on X, Substack, and Reddit.

### Reviving the American Dream: From "P-Doom" to "P-Boom"
* **Addressing American Anxiety:** Baker credited Antonio Gracias (Valor Equity Partners) and his partner Juan Sabatier with highlighting that while people in China are overwhelmingly positive on AI, Americans are largely scared of it.
* **Economic Context:** Gracias noted that while US median income is $87,500/year and the poverty rate is at an all-time low of ~10%, the median hourly wage is stuck at ~$11.50/hour (unchanged from pre-COVID), leaving many Americans rightfully anxious about AI.
* **Human Well-Being Over Misery:** Unlike prior technological eras—such as Mesopotamia, Athens under Aristotle, Rome, and the Renaissance—which were built on human misery, Gracias argued the AI era will unleash human creativity and productivity built on human well-being.
  * Practical applications include giving caregivers and doctors more time with patients, accelerating scientific discoveries, and reducing commute/work hours.
  * Gracias shared that he personally used Grokbot (which signed him into three separate applications for video, editing, and music) to create a music video in 3 hours.
  * Pointing to recent breakthroughs, Gracias noted that "in the last month, we have seen cars fly and rockets land."

### AI Market Outlook & Infrastructure Build-Out
* **Valuation & Historical Parallels:** Baker noted that AI equities are broadly trading at 10-, 15-, and in some cases 20-year low valuation multiples, as the market prices in peak spending.
  * The core question is whether the AI build-out resembles the telecom build-out (a sharp spike followed by prolonged under-investment) or the railroad build-out, which lasted for 60 years at a roughly comparable percentage of GDP to today.
  * Value will likely redistribute across the "five-layer cake" of land, power, shell, and different types of chips (compute, networking, memory, and storage).
* **Why Constraints Are Healthy:** Baker argued that current bottlenecks—higher interest rates, higher real rates, widening spreads, a shortage of watts, and a shortage of wafers—are preventing a bubble.
  * In a zero-interest-rate (ZIRP) world where TSMC (Taiwan Semi), Micron, Samsung, and SK Hynix expanded capacity by 200%, an overbuild would be guaranteed.
  * Instead, SemiAnalysis recently cut its numbers roughly in line with consensus, supporting a "slower, smoother for longer" trajectory.
* **Near-Term Catalysts:** Baker highlighted two major upcoming tests for the sector: Nvidia earnings tonight and the Anthropic IPO tomorrow.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Annual Sold-Out Event:** TomorrowX will become an annual event and will fill the Moody Center as a sold-out stadium next year. — *Ron Biscardi*
* **60-Year Build-Out:** If AI continues to become more economically useful and the elasticity of token demand remains positive, the AI infrastructure build-out will mirror the 60-year railroad build-out, and the value of compute will continue to rise. — *Gavin Baker*
