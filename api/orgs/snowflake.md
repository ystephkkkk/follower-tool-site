## Snowflake CEO: How AI Agents Will Transform the Workplace | Podcast | In Good Company

- Speakers: Sridhar Ramaswamy (CEO, Snowflake); Nicolai Tangen (Host, In Good Company)

---

**TL;DR** 
Snowflake CEO Sridhar Ramaswamy outlines how AI agents and consumption-based data processing are transforming the software landscape, positioning generative AI model companies as Snowflake's primary competitive threat while driving rapid internal shifts toward spec-driven software development.

### Snowflake’s Cloud Data Architecture and Consumption Model

* **Decoupled Storage and Compute:** Founded in 2012, Snowflake revolutionized database architecture by separating storage from compute on cloud infrastructure.
    * Users can instantly spin up thousands of computers for heavy analytic tasks and shut them down immediately after, avoiding the physical hardware constraints of traditional databases.
    * **Client Footprint:** Snowflake serves half of the addressable Global 2000 companies across financial services, healthcare, and advertising in over 25 countries. 
    * **NBIM Case Study:** Nicolai Tangen notes that Norges Bank Investment Management (NBIM) runs roughly 3 million queries daily on 2 petabytes (2 million gigabytes) of data stored in Snowflake.
* **Consumption-Based Pricing:** Rather than traditional per-seat licensing, Snowflake charges customers only for the data storage and compute power they actually use.
    * **Value Alignment:** This model aligns cost with actual business value and helps Snowflake amortize spiky, bursty demand across its 13,000+ customers.

### The Disruptive Threat of AI Agents and "Industrialized" Software

* **The New Competitors:** Ramaswamy considers frontier AI model companies (e.g., Anthropic) and coding agents to be Snowflake's most significant competitive threat, outpacing traditional hyperscale cloud providers like Amazon and Microsoft.
    * **Industrialization of Software:** AI is turning software engineering from an artisan "craft" into an industrialized pipeline, with AI agents serving as the new "front door" to all computing and information.
* **Spec-Driven Development:** Software development is shifting rapidly to English-language specification design. 
    * Engineers write functional English specs, while AI agents automate the code generation, testing, and deployment. 
    * Superstars leveraging these tools are proving to be 50 to 100 times more productive than average engineers.
* **The Rise of Agents:** AI agents combine language models with programmatic tools to execute multi-step tasks natively.
    * **Model Control Protocol (MCP):** An open interoperability layer that connects external language models and AI agents directly to secure data sources.
    * **Snowflake Intelligence:** Enables non-technical users to access, analyze, and execute actions on structured data and documents using natural language.

### Overcoming Legacy Data Messiness and Navigating Regulation

* **AI-Driven Data Cleansing:** Cleaning legacy data sets—historically a massive barrier to AI adoption—is being simplified by AI agents.
    * **Pipeline Automation:** Tasks like altering data pipelines, which previously took database engineers a week, are now automated in under an hour using English-language "skills."
    * **Rapid Migrations:** Snowflake is deploying agent-driven migration tools to move legacy data estates to the cloud in a matter of weeks rather than years.
* **The Regulatory Landscape:** Ramaswamy describes GDPR as a "mixed bag" with major unintended consequences for Europe.
    * **The Positives:** Forcing companies to guarantee consumer data deletion rights was a forward-looking success.
    * **The Negatives:** GDPR has raised the cost of business for European startups, creating protective regulatory moats for tech giants who can easily afford compliance, while leaving consumers fatigued by constant cookie prompts.

### Turning Failure into Leadership: From Neeva to the Snowflake CEO Role

* **Lessons from Neeva's Failure:** Ramaswamy co-founded the privacy-focused search engine Neeva after 15 years at Google. He notes two key takeaways from its failure:
    1. Consumer products must be 10x to 100x better than incumbents to drive adoption; marginal improvements are not enough.
    2. Consumers admire privacy in theory but rarely prioritize it over convenience in practice.
* **Stepping into the CEO Role:** Ramaswamy took over as CEO from Frank Slootman during a growth slowdown, prompting a sharp drop in Snowflake's stock price. He stabilized the company by intensely focusing on product-led growth.
* **The Weekly War Room:** To accelerate development in the AI era, Snowflake utilizes cross-functional "War Rooms" that strip away middle management layers. Teams plan goals on Monday and must show working software by Friday.
* **Personal Philosophy:** Raised in a lower-middle-class family in Tamil Nadu, India, Ramaswamy attributes his career to three core pillars: 
    * **Hard Work:** Commitment to mastering deep details while keeping sight of high-level strategy.
    * **Malleability:** Staying adaptable and willing to change one's mind in a rapidly evolving world.
    * **Resilience:** Developing a grounded sense of self that remains unaffected by individual victories or failures.

1. **TL;DR**
The Snowflake Summit 2026 Platform Keynote unveiled the transition of Snowflake from a unified data repository to a fully integrated agentic enterprise engine, spearheaded by the rebranding of its AI capabilities into Snowflake CoCo (developer/coding) and Snowflake CoWork (business collaboration). Key technical breakthroughs include the introduction of Snowflake Datastream, a 40x faster Interactive Compiler, and deep-security guardrails designed to secure and optimize AI agents on a single trusted data foundation.

2. ## The AI Control Plane: Introducing CoCo and CoWork
Snowflake has consolidated and simplified its AI surface area into two distinct, highly integrated agentic workspaces to streamline developer and business user workflows.

*   **Snowflake CoCo (formerly Cortex Code):** Rebranded to represent Snowflake’s AI-powered coding, development, and system operations assistant.
    *   **New Capabilities:** Features Cloud Agents (near General Availability), local development sandboxes for the CLI, and a "skill catalog" to share and discover plugins.
    *   **CoCo for Desktop:** A new, locally running desktop application designed to build applications, create skills, and diagnose complex data pipelines in real time.
    *   **AI-Powered Migrations (AIM):** CoCo now drives automated translation of legacy Spark (RDD) and Teradata SQL/BTEQ code directly into optimized Snowflake environments.
*   **Snowflake CoWork (formerly Snowflake Intelligence):** Rebranded as the universal, user-centric interface for business leaders and front-line workers.
    *   **Personal Work Engine:** Eliminates manual agent selection by utilizing multi-agent orchestration, which automatically routes requests to the correct specialized agent.
    *   **User Memory:** Enables CoWork to learn individual user preferences and patterns over time to contextualize future analytical responses.
    *   **Cortex Sense:** A new runtime capability that boosts out-of-the-box coding agent accuracy from 24% to 83% by analyzing user activity and metadata already stored inside Snowflake.
    *   **Natoma Acquisition:** Integrates native connectivity between CoWork/CoCo and over 100 enterprise business systems.

3. ## Performance Breakthroughs & Streaming Infrastructure
Snowflake introduced massive architectural upgrades to its core data engine, focusing on sub-second data ingestion, drastically reduced compile times, and near-instant query responsiveness.

*   **Snowflake Datastream:** A newly announced, fully managed streaming service built directly into Snowflake (entering private preview).
    *   **Architecture:** Features separated storage and compute, delivering zero-copy, sub-second latency streaming that is fully Kafka wire-compatible.
*   **Interactive Compiler:** A brand-new query compiler designed for high-concurrency, low-latency workloads. Early benchmarks show a **40x faster compile time**, leading to a 3x to 4x overall acceleration of workloads.
*   **Adaptive Compute:** Officially moving to General Availability (GA). The next-generation compute paradigm delivers up to **2x faster performance** compared to original Generation 1 warehouses.
*   **Unistore & Snowflake Postgres:** 
    *   **8x Latency Improvement:** An engine optimization for Unistore and hybrid tables has improved latency and throughput by 8x (public preview).
    *   **Postgres Data Mirroring:** A simple, single-switch replication capability (public preview) that handles change data capture (CDC) and low-latency synchronization into Snowflake.

4. ## Trust, Intent-Driven Governance, and Agent Security
With the rise of autonomous AI agents, Snowflake has reinforced its Horizon governance suite with strict compliance boundaries and advanced security tooling.

*   **Intent-Driven Governance:** Utilizing CoCo, administrators can declare natural-language goals (e.g., *"protect all PII data in my database"*), and Snowflake automatically handles classification and policy enforcement.
*   **Agent Identity Control:** Snowflake now registers a distinct "agentic context" function, allowing administrators to restrict or mask sensitive data views specifically when accessed by AI agents.
*   **Data Movement Policies:** Enables teams to block specific tagged datasets from being downloaded via the UI or copied to external stages.
*   **Multi-Party Approvals:** Built for cyber resiliency and anti-ransomware protection, this private preview feature requires mandatory second-person verification for critical admin operations (such as disabling MFA).
*   **Horizon Context:** Automatically compiles semantic metadata, BI signals, and transformation logic to supply AI agents with complete enterprise context.

5. ## Interoperability, Open Sharing, and Zero-Copy Partnerships
Snowflake continues to champion open standards and zero-copy integrations, eliminating the need to move or duplicate data across external platforms.

*   **Apache Iceberg V3 & V4:** Snowflake reaffirmed its commitment to the open format by supporting the Apache Polaris REST catalog within Horizon, allowing bidirectional reads/writes across external catalogs and engines.
*   **Open Sharing:** A public preview feature leveraging Iceberg REST catalog APIs to allow Snowflake users to seamlessly share secure data assets with non-Snowflake consumers.
*   **Multi-Party Collaboration:** Clean room technology is now Generally Available, enabling multiple organizations to securely analyze co-mingled datasets under pre-defined operational roles.
*   **Zero-Copy Ecosystem Expansion:** SAP zero-copy integration is now Generally Available, alongside newly announced partnerships with Workday, IBM Watsonx (for DB2/mainframe data), and AVEVA Connect.

6. ## Enterprise Case Studies: AI in Production
Executive guests highlighted how they have successfully shifted AI from trial phases to live, high-impact production.

*   **Thomson Reuters (Caitlin Halferty, VP):** Deployed *Co-Counsel*, its flagship AI capability built on Snowflake's trusted data foundation, supporting over 1 million legal, tax, and audit professionals globally. 
*   **Under Armour (Patrick Duroseau, Chief Data & AI Officer):** Developed the conversational AI assistant *Ada* to empower internal teams to query cross-functional data, moving beyond traditional static reporting.
*   **Samsung (Jung Suh):** Integrated the *Shopper's Insight Action Agent (SIA)* on Snowflake CoWork to drive "AX" (AI-Based Process Transformation). Over 1,000 global executives and marketers use the tool to instantly synthesize Amazon and global sales performance, bypassing weeks of manual reporting.

### The Four Pillars of the Agentic Enterprise
To transition from isolated AI pilots to a fully autonomous "Agentic Enterprise," organizations must coordinate four foundational components through a unified system of decision.

* **Enterprise Context:** Proprietary data (structured and unstructured) acts as a company's ultimate competitive moat, serving as the foundational context that makes AI outputs relevant and strategically valuable.
* **AI Models:** Organizations require the flexibility to deploy a mix of frontier and open-source models (including Anthropic’s Claude, Mistral, Gemini, and OpenAI’s GPT) optimized for specific speed, reasoning, or cost demands.
* **SaaS & Application Integration:** AI must interact directly with the daily systems of record that run the business, such as SAP, Salesforce, Zoom, and Microsoft 365.
* **The Agentic Control Plane:** Snowflake introduced two major solutions to act as a secure, governed mission control:
    * **Snowflake Intelligence:** A personal assistant for business users that queries enterprise data and triggers actions across SaaS applications using natural language.
    * **Cortex Code ("CoCo"):** An AI-powered developer agent that automates database migrations, builds data pipelines, monitors query costs, and writes code using natural language.
    * **Notoma Acquisition:** Snowflake announced its intent to acquire Notoma to bring Anthropic's Model Context Protocol (MCP) natively into the AI Data Cloud. This secures and governs AI interactions across external platforms (e.g., Slack, GitHub, Google Drive) with human-in-the-loop approvals.

### Realizing P&L Outcomes: Accenture's Strategy
Accenture leadership outlined how the global services giant is executing its own internal agentic transformation while steering thousands of clients toward tangible business outcomes.

* **Workforce Restructuring:** Accenture reorganized roles for all 750,000 of its global employees around seven C-suite personas, establishing a standardized Snowflake-backed data foundation for each.
* **Overcoming Data Debt:** Accenture estimates that 85% of enterprise clients are currently held back by severely fragmented data and legacy process debt.
* **Focus on the P&L:** Leadership emphasizes that AI projects must directly tie to a line item on the P&L—either driving revenue or reducing Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) and SG&A.
* **Quantifiable Success Metrics:** In a joint engagement for a major European utility company, Accenture and Snowflake delivered:
    * Project execution cycles reduced from months to 12 weeks.
    * Analytical query times dropped from weeks to two seconds.
    * Compute resource times reduced by 85%.

### Custom Workflows on the Data Lake: Sanofi Case Study
Pharmaceutical giant Sanofi explained how it shifted from passive business intelligence (dashboards) to active agentic workflows to support operations across R&D, manufacturing, and sales.

* **Bypassing SaaS Silos:** Rather than building brittle pipelines to pull data out of expensive SaaS applications, Sanofi designs deterministic agentic workflows directly on top of its Snowflake data lake. 
* **Targeted Value Areas:** Sanofi is deploying these custom agentic workflows to optimize its €18 billion annual procurement spend, automate IT and HR support, and empower its field sales teams.
* **Live "Concierge" Demo:** Sanofi demonstrated its real-time, voice-activated "Concierge" agent built for field sales representatives:
    * The agent pulled doctor metrics directly from live production systems, detailed historical patient treatment trends, suggested conversational icebreakers, and drafted and sent a post-meeting summary email instantly.

### The Future of Models, Scaling Laws, and Trust
Daniela Amodei, President and Co-founder of Anthropic, joined Sridhar Ramaswamy to discuss the exponential trajectory of AI capabilities and how enterprises should prepare.

* **Designing for Future Scaling Laws:** Because AI models improve predictably and rapidly with more compute and data, Amodei advises businesses not to build solely for today’s capabilities, but to "dream big" and design architectures capable of absorbing rapid model advancements.
* **Trust as an Accelerant:** In the enterprise space, safety, predictability, and low hallucination rates are not obstacles to speed—they are prerequisites. Organizations can only scale AI once they have built absolute trust in the safety of the output.
* **The Power of Precision:** Snowflake's AI strategy prioritizes precision over recall (e.g., in text-to-SQL conversions with Cortex Analyst), ensuring the model safely declines to answer a query rather than returning a hallucinated or incorrect data point.
* **Real-World Deployment:** Payments firm Block (formerly Square) is combining Snowflake's data platform with Claude Code to run real-time fraud analysis and payment processing insights at massive scale.

## Who’s Winning The AI Race? + Software’s Future — With Sridhar Ramaswamy

- Channel: Alex Kantrowitz
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P_KI6HC8fpQ
- Published: 2026-02-12
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260212.html#video-P_KI6HC8fpQ
- Speakers: Sridhar Ramaswamy (CEO, Snowflake); Alex Kantrowitz (Host, Big Technology)

---

**TL;DR**
The AI race has shifted from an OpenAI monopoly to a fierce, multi-polar competition where technical leads shrink in months, forcing software companies to transition from static SaaS tools into "agentic platforms" that prioritize internal data integration over simple chat interfaces.

### The State of the AI Race: Shrinking Leads and High Stakes
* **Vanishing Leads:** Sridhar notes that a "lead" in the current market lasts about a year—an eternity in tech—and even that is shrinking. While OpenAI and Anthropic held early advantages, Google has leveraged its "bottomless well of money," distribution, and proprietary TPUs to close the gap with Gemini.
* **The Google "Code Red" Culture:** Drawing on his 15 years at Google, Sridhar explains that while the company may look placid from the outside, it is "well-calibrated for crises." He attributes their AI resurgence to the integration of DeepMind and the hands-on involvement of founders like Sergey Brin.
* **The Rise of Alternatives:** The aura of OpenAI’s invincibility is being challenged by open-source and international models like DeepSeek and Qwen2. Sridhar highlights that specialized models (like Anthropic’s coding model) can temporarily claim the "state of the art" title, forcing competitors to play a constant game of catch-up.

### The Shift to Agentic Enterprise AI
* **Snowflake Intelligence:** Snowflake’s agentic platform is its fastest-growing product, with over 2,000 customers using it within three months of General Availability (GA). 
* **Beyond the Chatbot:** Sridhar argues that the future of work isn't just a chatbot, but a system that connects "Past, Present, and Future":
    * **The Past:** Historical data stored in Snowflake.
    * **The Present:** Ambient information like emails, documents, and market trends.
    * **The Future:** Prediction models that recommend actions based on data.
* **Operational Gains:** 
    * **Support Efficiency:** Snowflake’s own support team saw a **10x reduction** in time spent debugging complex cases by using "Cortex Code," a data coding agent.
    * **Complex Scaling:** Sridhar cites a manufacturer with **5 million SKUs** using agents to analyze dynamic market pricing, potentially unlocking hundreds of millions in revenue that was previously impossible to manage manually.

### The Future of Software: Platforms vs. "Dumb Backends"
* **SaaS Multiple Contraction:** The market is currently devaluing traditional SaaS (multiples dropping from ~33x to ~23x) because of uncertainty over who wins the AI era. Sridhar suggests that "AI as a bolt-on" is not a winning strategy.
* **The "Dumb Backend" Risk:** Niche SaaS providers that rely on data lock-in are at risk. If they don't provide agentic value, they become mere data pipes for "master agents" like ChatGPT or Gemini. 
* **Platform Concentration:** Sridhar believes value will concentrate in platforms that own the "entirety of the experience"—the model, the critical enterprise data, and the integrations into operational systems.

### Geopolitics and the Open Source Ecosystem
* **The Academic Gap:** Sridhar expresses concern that leading US AI labs (OpenAI, Anthropic) have become "walled gardens" that publish very little. This has caused academia to diverge from cutting-edge research.
* **The Chinese Advantage:** Paradoxically, because Chinese companies (like Alibaba with Qwen or DeepSeek) are publishing their work to gain influence, they are becoming the primary resource for global students and professors to build upon.
* **Net Positive Competition:** Despite the geopolitical friction, Sridhar views the rise of high-quality open-source models as a "net positive" because it prevents a monopoly and forces US companies to innovate faster.

> "The AI race changes every month. We should all feel great about making predictions because one of them will come true and the world will change enough that we have to make new predictions." — **Sridhar Ramaswamy**

> "A lot of these [SaaS] players risk becoming dumb backends to the models, which is why Snowflake is leaning so far forward on agentic AI... that’s the place where value is going to get created." — **Sridhar Ramaswamy**

> "It’s a bit of a red pill moment where you’re like, 'Wait, you mean I can release new software products pretty much every day because releasing a new piece of functionality is as simple as writing a recipe in English?'" — **Sridhar Ramaswamy**
