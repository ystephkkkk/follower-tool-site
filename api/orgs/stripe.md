### TL;DR
John Collison, Co-Founder of Stripe, discusses how AI agents and computer use will fundamentally rewire the internet, commerce, and software infrastructure. Stripe processed **$1.9 trillion** in 2025 (up 34%), and Collison defines January 1, 2026, as Day One of the "singularity epoch."

### Stripe's Metrics, AI Transition, and Internal Transformation
* **Payment Volume:** Stripe processed **$1.9 trillion** in 2025, representing a **34% growth rate year-over-year**.
* **AI-Driven Firm Creation:** Stripe is seeing the highest rate of new firm creation in recent memory because intelligent tools make starting and succeeding at companies significantly easier.
* **Internal Workflows:** 
  * **Sales:** Sales teams have completely abandoned old workflows, self-serving custom internal AI apps built on complex privacy and access control layers to generate personalized customer pitches.
  * **Legal:** The legal team built an automated agent to ingest massive global regulations (such as new 600-page laws dropped in India or Finland) to track compliance requirements relevant to Stripe.
* **The "Jagged Frontier":** Collison notes that while consumer AI apps face uneven performance, business adoption requires pairing AI directly with complex, permission-guarded internal corporate data.

### Agentic Commerce and Computer Use
* **Obsolescence of Web Forms:** Collison states that filling out web forms is a "non-value-add activity" that no one chooses to do, making personal agents handling tasks like renewing a driver's license a major shift.
* **Computer Use as Backward Compatibility:** Computer use is the ultimate backward compatibility layer for the real world because waiting years for legacy institutions like the DMV to build MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers is unnecessary when an agent can directly navigate existing web interfaces.
* **E-Commerce and Merchant Shifts:**
  * **First-Order Change:** Personal agents will execute a massive share of everyday consumer commerce.
  * **Second-Order Change:** AI discovery will serve as the new point of aggregation, empowering long-tail niche brands with strong internet reviews over traditional big-box retailers.
  * **Historical Analogy:** Collison compares AI e-commerce discovery to the early days of Facebook and Instagram ad targeting, which natively birthed companies like Wish, Teespring, Temu, and Shein.

### Cybersecurity Realities in the AI Era
* **Accelerating Breach Rates:** Collison predicts that over the next **5 years**, the world will see more security breaches than over the past **5 years** due to advanced cyber capabilities embedded in new AI models.
* **Startup Advantage:** Startups are disproportionately well-positioned because they operate smaller, homogeneous tech stacks, whereas legacy companies running 50-year-old unpatched infrastructure face extreme vulnerabilities.

### Strategic Acquisitions and Pricing Shifts
* **Recent Acquisitions:** Stripe has strategically acquired **Bridge, Privy, Metronome, and OpenRouter** to inject cryptonativity, usage-based billing, and multi-model routing into its ecosystem.
* **Usage-Based Billing via Metronome:** Because modern products involve heavy AI inference costs, almost every company now requires usage-based billing or overage structures rather than flat subscriptions.
* **Multi-Model Routing via OpenRouter:** Stripe bet on OpenRouter because businesses require multiple models simultaneously—routing high-value users to frontier models and lower-value users to cheaper models, or adapting as models rapidly change.
* **Build vs. Buy Philosophy:** Stripe prefers buying when entering a brand-new, fast-evolving market where internal development cycles would leave the final product outdated within 12 months.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Cybersecurity Breaches:** Collison predicts that over the next **5 years**, the world will experience more security breaches than it did over the past **5 years** due to the rapid advancement of adversarial AI cyber capabilities. — John Collison

Sea founder and CEO Forrest Li shared the origin story of building Garena, Shopee, and SeaMoney into a multi-billion-dollar enterprise by embracing bottoms-up localization, an unrelenting startup culture, and extreme customer obsession.

### Company Origins & The Gaming-to-Commerce Evolution
* **Garena's Founding Insight (2009):** Forrest Li started Garena based on his own passion for gaming (playing *World of Warcraft* and *League of Legends*), recognizing a massive mismatch between intense gaming culture in Southeast Asia and zero revenue generation for developers.
* **The Payments Breakthrough:** 
    * Due to negligible credit card penetration among teenage gamers 15–20 years ago, Sea designed its own prepaid cards.
    * The first office in a Chinatown shophouse doubled as a warehouse filled with prepaid card bags, delivered directly to internet cafes by computer science graduates driving small vans.

    * Launched 5 years after the gaming business despite skepticism that a gaming company could build e-commerce.
    * Motivated by the fundamental belief that if successful online marketplaces existed in the US and China, they *must* exist in Southeast Asia.

### Regional Nuances & Bottoms-Up Localization
* **The Fragmented Southeast Asian Market:**
    * Outsiders from the US or China often fail by blindly applying optimized home-market playbooks to Southeast Asia.
    * Singapore's proximity to neighbors (e.g., Vietnam being a two-hour flight away) masks stark differences in economic development and consumer lifestyle, requiring bespoke bottom-up solutions per market.
* **Solving the Indonesian ATM Payment Challenge:**
    * Shopee faced an early hurdle where customers could order online via mobile app but had no online payment options, requiring physical trips to ATMs with codes.
    * Compounding this, Indonesian bank security prevented buyers from disclosing identity or bank account numbers to merchants.
    * **Creative Workaround:** Sea priced identical water bottles at fractional differences (varying by fractions of a rupiah), using the exact price point to identify which anonymous buyer made the purchase, requiring hundreds of staff to manually match payments daily.
* **Unlocking Brazilian Logistics (2019):**
    * When bringing the *Free Fire* gaming community to Brazil, leadership discovered e-commerce penetration was stagnant because high logistics costs ($5+ per parcel) restricted online shopping exclusively to wealthy consumers buying expensive goods.
    * Sea applied its Southeast Asian supply chain experience to drive down logistics costs, proving e-commerce could serve everyday consumers.

### Fintech & Credit Services Scale
* **SeaMoney's Natural Extension:** 
    * Developed out of the ShopeePay mobile wallet, which solved friction by allowing users to store funds rather than making individual ATM trips.
    * Noticed transaction surges mid-month and end-month corresponding with regional bi-weekly/monthly payroll cycles.
* **Bridging the Credit Gap:** 
    * With credit card penetration still below 20% in markets like Indonesia, consumers previously relied on predatory loan sharks or delayed medical/school payments.
    * Shopee PayLater and financial services grew to a **$10 billion loan book** supporting **50 million people** with outstanding credit (averaging roughly **$200 per person**).
    * **Partnership Announcement:** Tyler Bryson announced that by the end of the year, ShopeePay and SPayLater will integrate into the Stripe business network across Southeast Asia as Stripe’s first buy-now-pay-later option in the region.

### AI Adoption & Tangible Results
* **Customer Service Automation:** 
    * Over **80% of customer inquiries** are 100% handled by AI chatbots via Shopee, achieving satisfaction rates *higher* than human customer service agents.
* **Video Game Development (*Free Fire*):**
    * Operates the world's largest mobile game (*Free Fire*) with over **100 million daily players**.
    * **Art Generation:** Routine concept art and asset generation are heavily outsourced to AI, with senior artists handling only final touches.
    * **Smart Bots for Social Play:** Advanced AI-powered bots provide realistic multiplayer experiences for solo or introverted players, encouraging team-based participation and social interaction.

### Leadership, Culture & Personal Passions
* **The "Startup" Mindset at Scale:** Even at roughly 60,000 employees, Forrest Li emphasizes to every new management associate cohort (GMAP, selecting 10-20 top grads annually) that they are joining a startup, not a big corporation.
* **Five Core Cultural Values:** Defined during an offsite in the company's fifth year as: **We serve, We run, We adapt, We commit, We stay humble.**
* **Direct Customer Accountability:** Li personally reads customer service emails sent directly to him daily, using them as a diagnostic tool where over half uncover underlying process flaws.
* **Football Leadership:** Li serves as President of the Football Association of Singapore and owns the Lion City Sailors, treating football matches as a 90-minute mental reset and viewing sport as a powerful unifying force that transcends societal divides.

*   **Demographic Alignment in Germany:** While critics worry about job displacement, Germany's severe demographic crisis—a structural deficit of 500,000 workers annually—means that automating 1% of jobs per year via AI and robotics is a necessary economic stabilizer rather than a crisis.
*   **Supply-Side Career Shifts:** Traditional entry-level roles are facing disruption, but the shift is heavily driven by candidate supply rather than just employer demand.
    *   **Declining STEM Pipeline:** US computer science university enrollments fell from 660,000 to 600,000 over the last two years as students proactively avoid career paths they assume will be automated.
*   **The "AI Native" Cohort:** A sharp divide has emerged between legacy workers seeking excuses to avoid AI and "AI natives" (current university graduates) who use AI as a standard, baseline utility. Conor McNamara noted that Stripe data confirms once users adopt AI tools, they "tool hop" but never return to non-AI workflows.

### Agentic Commerce: Hype vs. Practical B2C and B2B Realities

*   **B2C Shopping Limitations:** Autonomous shopping agents will scale slowly because they conflict with core human shopping behaviors. Humans enjoy "window shopping," comparison, and selecting from a visual matrix of options. 
*   **High-Value Early Consumer Use Cases:** Agentic commerce will succeed first by taking over high-friction administrative tasks rather than product discovery.
    *   **Consumer Claims:** Automatically filing delayed travel claims (e.g., against Deutsche Bahn) to recover refunds.
    *   **Local Services:** Booking appointments with dentists, doctors, hair salons, and restaurants.
*   **Rapid B2B Procurement Adoption:** Unlike consumers, businesses prioritize efficiency over experience. B2B agentic commerce will scale rapidly to handle tactical corporate spending, high-frequency procurement, and API-to-API transactions.

### Revitalizing German and European Innovation

*   **Transitioning from Administration to Engineering:** Klöckner noted that German business education trains graduates to optimize existing processes by 5% to 10%. To compete globally, Germany must empower engineers who build entirely new business models that render old processes redundant.
*   **Eliminating Growth Inhibitors:** Europe must resolve several structural barriers to foster a competitive tech ecosystem:
    *   **Digital Single Market:** Startups are severely hindered by navigating 20+ distinct tax regimes, languages, and company formation rules across Europe.
    *   **Talent Acquisition Friction:** European founders struggle with long visa wait times for foreign engineers. Countries must implement fast-track, 4-week visas for company-vouched talent.
*   **Capitalizing on Biotech and Life Sciences:** Germany cannot win in low-cost robotics or heavy manufacturing due to high energy costs and a lack of natural resources (areas dominated by China). Instead, Germany should leverage its world-class academic institutions to build "the next ten BioNtechs" using AI-driven pharmaceutical research.
*   **The Imperative for Open Source AI:** To avoid a wealth transfer to US tech monopolies, Europe must heavily support a robust open-source LLM framework. This ensures European enterprises can build sovereign AI tools without being locked into expensive foreign proprietary models.
