## When AI Stops Being a Project: Turning Technology into Real Value for Patients and Providers

- Channel: Stanford Online
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZlLV9ZHauAM
- Published: 2026-08-28
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260828.html#video-ZlLV9ZHauAM
- Speakers: Matt Lungren (Faculty, Stanford University); Justin Norden (Faculty, Stanford University); Sandeep Dadlani (CEO, Optum Insight)

---

### TL;DR
AI is rapidly shifting from isolated pilot projects to the core of enterprise business strategy in healthcare. Leaders are grappling with exploding token consumption, multi-model architectures, and the complex challenge of transitioning workers toward long-running agentic workflows.

### Enterprise Scale & Internal AI Infrastructure
* **Engineering Firepower:** 
  * **Sandeep Dadlani** notes that UHG employs approximately 22,000 engineers experimenting with large language models and agents.
* **The "United AI Studio" Harness:** 
  * **Model Portfolio:** Houses 117 LLM models, including 20 internal open-weight Small Language Models (SLMs) built for specific purposes, alongside commercial options.
  * **Token Management:** Features a token gateway to prevent runaway costs; UHG consumes between 10 billion and 25 billion tokens per day.
  * **Personal Limits:** Dadlani’s personal token limit is set at $1,000, triggering a call from the CTO if exceeded without production progress.
  * **Cross-Domain Agents:** 92 registered agents operate across domains (payer, provider, pharmacy) with monitored, responsible data sources.
* **The OCR Paradox:** 
  * **Sandeep Dadlani** highlights a stark industry baseline: parts of the enterprise still process 9 billion faxes annually using OCR technology because it was embedded into past workflows and is difficult to extricate.

### Model Choice, Costs, and the Open Source Shift
* **Frontier Model Lock-in Concerns:** 
  * **Matt Lungren** and **Sandeep Dadlani** express growing concern over enterprise dependency on single model providers and APIs, especially given rapid security updates and the emergence of models like GLM-5.2.
* **Open Source & Chinese Models:** 
  * **Justin Norden** points out that open-source models (including Chinese-based models) are increasingly dominating US-based company access via platforms like OpenRouter due to cost and performance.
* **Token Efficiency vs. Cost:** 
  * **Matt Lungren** notes that while open models like GLM-5.2 achieve frontier-level coding performance (equivalent to versions like Claude 4.8), they can consume 5 to 10 times more tokens to achieve the same outcome and require immense infrastructure (e.g., 750+ billion parameters).
* **Commoditization of the Model Layer:** 
  * **Matt Lungren** observes that the industry is commoditizing the model layer, pushing true enterprise value up into custom business contexts, harnesses, and specific workflows.

### The Rise of Agentic Workflows
* **Consumer-Driven Examples:** 
  * **Sandeep Dadlani** shares a personal anecdote of using Gemini at a retail mall to instantly match size, color, fit, and in-stock online availability for his daughters.
* **OpenAI Internal Data on Agents:** 
  * **Justin Norden** cites a recent OpenAI internal paper showing agents taking over tasks in finance, recruiting, and legal, with roughly 25% of non-engineering tasks spanning over 8 hours of estimated human work time.
* **Enterprise Mislabeling:** 
  * **Sandeep Dadlani** cautions that organizations frequently mislabel simple APIs or "super APIs" as "agents"; true agents require *agency* to reason, decide, and act across end-to-end workflows rather than sitting in isolated silos.

### Leadership Cadence and Operational Speed
* **Cross-Disciplinary Executive Meetings:** 
  * **Sandeep Dadlani** describes mandatory 6-hour monthly meetings at UHG where the 50-person leadership team—comprising all business CEOs, the CIO, CTO, CFO, CMO, and Dadlani himself—obsess exclusively over AI use cases, token spending, financial value, clinical outcomes, and NPS metrics.
* **Zero Distance to the Problem:** 
  * **Sandeep Dadlani** advocates getting problem solvers (product managers and engineers) at "zero distance" to end-users, patients, and receptionists to drive 100x speed improvements, citing examples where teams build superior HTML UI mockups using Claude Code within 60 seconds during strategy meetings.
* **Delegated vs. Centralized AI:** 
  * **Justin Norden** notes that healthcare organizations historically treat AI as an delegated IT innovation problem rather than a foundational CEO-level mandate.

### Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Sandeep Dadlani:** Predicts that within two years, for the health systems advised by Justin Norden, "either AI will be a project, or AI will be the business."

## Using AI to Increase Your Intelligence & Enrich Humanity | Dr. Fei-Fei Li

- Channel: Andrew Huberman
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N5AQFYtqx8Q
- Published: 2026-08-10
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260810.html#video-N5AQFYtqx8Q
- Speakers: Andrew Huberman (Host, Huberman Lab); Fei-Fei Li (Professor of Computer Science, Stanford University)

---

#### TL;DR
AI systems are powered by the convergence of massive internet-scale data, advanced neural network algorithms, and high-performance GPU computing. While AI excels at pattern recognition, statistical generation, and processing explicit data, it fundamentally differs from human cognition, which is driven by biological agency, motivation, and inaccessible personal experiences.

---

#### ## Foundations and Evolution of Artificial Intelligence
* **Biological Origin of Vision:** **Dr. Fei-Fei Li** notes that 540 million years ago, the evolution of photoreceptive cells in simple ocean trilobites sparked the Cambrian explosion of animal speciation. In human brains, approximately **50% of cortical activity** is dedicated to visual function, making vision a cornerstone of both biological and artificial intelligence.
* **Neural Network Parallels:** **Dr. Fei-Fei Li** explains that modern neural network algorithms trace their inspiration back to the **early 1950s**, aligning with neuroscientists Hubel and Wiesel recording hierarchical visual cells in mammalian brains. Today's neural networks run on **hundreds of billions to trillions of parameters**.
* **The Big Data Turning Point:** **Dr. Fei-Fei Li** launched the **ImageNet project** in **2006** at Princeton, collecting **15 million internet-scale images** across **1,000 object categories** because existing algorithms lacked sufficient training data. This data scarcity was solved by converging ImageNet, mature neural networks, and **GPU computing** around **2012**, dropping classification error rates from random chance (1 in 1,000) down into the teens.

#### ## Transition from Vision to Language and Video
* **Natural Language Processing:** **Dr. Fei-Fei Li** highlights that the **Transformer paper** published in **2016–2017** shifted the AI frontier from vision to natural language. Utilizing vast text data on the internet, companies like OpenAI and Google built models that led to the **ChatGPT moment in 2022** (taking roughly 5 years).
* **Video Generation and Dynamics:** **Dr. Fei-Fei Li** states that in **January 2024**, Sora was released, opening the floodgate for video generation. By training on massive internet video datasets, models learned plausible physical movements (such as a cat running toward a mouse) through statistical pattern matching rather than explicit muscle-structure programming.
* **Limitations of AI vs. Human Learning:** **Dr. Fei-Fei Li** contrasts AI with children: a young child learns what a "kitty cat" is after seeing only **3 to 10 cats**, whereas AI requires downloading billions of internet images. Furthermore, deeply personalized thoughts, abstract art, and individual emotional histories are never uploaded to the internet and remain completely inaccessible to AI.

#### ## AI in Healthcare and Scientific Discovery
* **Biomedical Research:** **Dr. Fei-Fei Li** and **Andrew Huberman** discuss how AI can revolutionize scientific discovery by synthesizing vast amounts of cross-disciplinary data (e.g., combining neuroscience and olfactory research) at speeds unattainable by the human brain.
* **Robotic Surgery:** **Dr. Fei-Fei Li** shares a personal example of her father's complex liver surgery performed using the **da Vinci robot system** driven by a human surgeon, resulting in **10 times less blood loss** than typical traditional surgeries. 
* **Data Scarcity in Medicine:** **Dr. Fei-Fei Li** cautions that highly complex, vascular organs like the liver lack abundant surgical training data across global patient pools, meaning fully autonomous surgical robots require careful human-machine collaboration until simulation environments improve.

#### ## Human Agency, Emotion, and Societal Impact
* **Preserving Human Agency:** **Dr. Fei-Fei Li** emphasizes that agency, motivation, and dignity are essential to humanity. AI should be deployed as a tool to augment and empower human communication and learning rather than strip away human autonomy.
* **Lack of True Emotion:** **Dr. Fei-Fei Li** clarifies that chatbots do not feel empathy or pain; when a model says "I'm sorry you're sick," it is executing pattern recognition based on training text, lacking the genuine biological memory of suffering that a human friend possesses.
* **The Founding of World Labs:** **Dr. Fei-Fei Li** explains that she co-founded **World Labs in early 2024** to build foundation models focused on **spatial and physical intelligence**. The company aims to generate 3D and 4D environments to empower creators, aid architectural design, and provide training grounds for robotics.

#### ## Public Discourse, Education, and the Next Generation
* **Empowering Educators and Parents:** **Dr. Fei-Fei Li** criticizes Silicon Valley and tech investors for ignoring teachers and parents. Following the launch of ChatGPT in **November 2022**, she immediately emailed her child's elementary school principal to guest lecture and help educators navigate the technology.
* **The Prompting Skill:** **Dr. Fei-Fei Li** compares effective prompt engineering to the **Socratic method**, suggesting that schools should actively teach students how to ask precise questions to extract maximum value from AI tools.
