## BONUS: Nebius’s Arkady Volozh | On building in the agentic era | Spotlight On | AccelVC

- Speakers: Arkady Volozh (Founder, Nebius); Matt Weigand (Partner, Accel)

---

**TL;DR**
Nebius is positioning itself as a vertically integrated "AI-native hyperscaler," controlling the entire stack from land and power to agentic software to provide cost-effective, high-performance infrastructure for the next generation of AI developers.

### Full-Stack Vertical Integration
*   **The Five-Layer Strategy:** Arkady Volozh defines Nebius’s competitive edge through its ownership of every layer of the AI value chain:
    *   **Layer 1:** Land, Power, and Shell (LPS), including industrial permits and electrical equipment.
    *   **Layer 2:** Proprietary hardware, specifically building their own GPU racks.
    *   **Layer 3:** Bare metal services and Kubernetes layers for wholesale clients.
    *   **Layer 4:** Infrastructure-as-a-Service (IaaS) and platform services for retail cloud users.
    *   **Layer 5:** High-level AI services, including inference (Token Factory) and agentic tools.
*   **Efficiency Gains:** By avoiding third-party margins for data center construction and rack building, Nebius claims superior economic and engineering efficiency, optimizing the system "down to the last bolt."

### Strategic Financing via Hyperscale Partnerships
*   **The Meta Commitment:** Nebius recently secured a $27 billion commitment from Meta, which Volozh clarifies is structured as two distinct parts:
    *   **$12 Billion:** Direct bare metal capacity provided to Meta.
    *   **$15 Billion:** A "backstop" agreement that Nebius uses as collateral to secure bank financing for its own independent cloud capacity.
*   **The Microsoft Precedent:** A similar $17 billion+ deal with Microsoft serves as a financial engine, allowing Nebius to use long-term, credit-worthy contracts to build out "spot market" capacity for smaller AI startups.
*   **NVIDIA Partnership:** Beyond capital, the deepening partnership with NVIDIA involves deep engineering collaboration to optimize systems, positioning Nebius as a preferred partner for large-scale GPU deployments.

### Shifting Focus: From Bare Metal to the Agentic Era
*   **AI-Native Demand:** Nebius is moving beyond selling "GPU hours" to selling "tokens" and agentic outcomes. 
    *   **Inference Growth:** Through its "Token Factory," Nebius serves frontier labs and open-weight model developers who are currently capacity-constrained.
    *   **Agentic Services:** The recent acquisition of Taviely (agentic search) highlights a shift toward serving "agents" rather than human software engineers.
*   **New User Profiles:** Volozh notes that modern AI developers are often multi-cloud and "agentic," choosing platforms based on measurable efficiency and reliability rather than legacy ecosystem loyalty.

### Scaling to Meet "Objective" Demand
*   **Capacity Targets:** Nebius is planning for a footprint measured in hundreds of thousands—and eventually millions—of GPUs, with a power pipeline reaching into the gigawatt range.
*   **Capital Requirements:** Fulfilling current programs for 3+ gigawatts of capacity will require over $100 billion in total investment. 
*   **Market Outlook:** Volozh views the current market as a "blue ocean" rather than a "red ocean." He argues that demand is "totally objective" because AI allows industries to do things 10 times cheaper or 10 times faster, creating a structural shift in the global economy.

> "Demand is so high, coming from so many directions. Whatever is built is consumed. And demand is totally objective. Everybody sees how much new value this thing generates. You can do either 10 times more things or 10 times cheaper, and it's a huge, huge industrial next step." — **Arkady Volozh**

> "We use these big bare metal contracts not as our main line of business, but as one of the ways to finance building our own cloud for the rest of the market." — **Arkady Volozh**

> "We are building the cloud for this new, today I would say agentic world... these new users are choosing the platform which is measurably better, more reliable, more efficient, cheaper." — **Arkady Volozh**


## BONUS: Armadin's Kevin Mandia | Redefining Security in a New Age of Threats | Spotlight On | AccelVC

- Channel: Accel
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dEQ92H39BrY
- Published: 2026-03-10
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260310.html#video-dEQ92H39BrY
- Speakers: Kevin Mandia (Co-founder and CEO, Armadin); Ping Li (Partner, Accel)

---

**TL;DR:** Cybersecurity is entering a paradigm shift where all attacks will soon be executed by autonomous AI agents at "compute speed." Kevin Mandia’s new venture, Armadin, aims to counter this by building the "ultimate cyber cannon"—using high-end AI offense to train and automate autonomous defense systems.

### The Evolution of the Cyber Threat Landscape
* **From Scripted to Agentic:** Early hacking (late 90s/early 2000s) was scripted and human-led, often focusing on low-level extortion or espionage. 
    * The future consists of **AI Agents** that can think, learn, instantiate "normal" behavior, and possess total recall, making them significantly more dangerous than previous scripted tools.
* **The Speed of Compute:** Mandia argues that defense must move from "human speed" to "compute speed." 
    * If an attacker achieves remote code execution, an AI agent can compromise a network in seconds—a timeframe where "human-in-the-loop" defense is guaranteed to fail.
* **100% Probability of Attack:** In the past, boards questioned the probability of specific red-team attacks. 
    * With AI, Mandia asserts that every attack path is now "probable" because AI can automate and scale bespoke attacks at a drastically lower cost.

### The Armadin Strategy: Offense as Defense
* **The "Ultimate Attacker":** Armadin is building nation-state grade offensive AI agents. 
    * The goal is not to facilitate hacking, but to provide an "offensive platform" that enterprises can use to pressure-test their own networks.
* **Autonomous Defense Swarms:** Mandia envisions a future—roughly five years out—where "agents train agents." 
    * By constantly attacking a company’s own network with AI, Armadin aims to train a "swarm" of defensive agents that can provide high-fidelity, autonomous prevention without human intervention.
* **Third-Party Validation:** Much like Mandiant became the "seal of approval" for incident response, Mandia wants Armadin to be the standard for "exploitable risk" validation. 
    * An external "ultimate attacker" provides better security assurance than internal assessments.

### Founding Mandiant and the "Cleaners"
* **A Gap in the Market:** Mandiant was founded in 2004 on the then-unpopular premise that "breaches are inevitable." 
    * While giants like Symantec and McAfee focused on the first line of defense, Mandiant became the "oh crap button" for CEOs when those lines were inevitably breached.
* **State-Sponsored Shift:** The company’s growth was fueled by the transition of Chinese and Russian operations from purely military targets (.mil) to the private sector (defense industrial base).
* **Mission Over Transaction:** Mandia highlights that Mandiant had 14 letters of intent to buy the company before finally selling to FireEye. 
    * **Founder Advice:** Do not build a company to transact; build it to be the best in the world at a specific "true north."

### Leadership and Crisis Management Playbook
* **Managing Through People:** Mandia describes his evolution from a hands-on technical expert to a leader who manages "through" people. 
    * At scale, the CEO’s job is to recruit specialists who are better at their specific functions (Sales, HR, Engineering) than the founder is.
* **The Social Contract:** A core tenet of Mandia’s leadership is that employees should leave the company more capable than when they arrived. 
    * At a recent RSA conference, 43% of the main-stage presenters were Mandiant alumni.
* **The CEO’s Role in a Breach:** 
    * **Emotional Temperament:** Leaders must stay in a "narrow lane," avoiding over-pivoting to either glee or despair.
    * **Ownership:** When a major breach occurs, the CEO cannot delegate the response. As Mandia told one executive: "This is now your day job."

### Notable Quotes
> "The cheapest way to build autonomous defense: build the ultimate attacker and hack yourself." — **Kevin Mandia**

> "When the animal [AI] comes out of the cage, it will be targeted. You're not going to spray and pray great offense with agents... they're going to find a zero-day and then use it in a targeted way." — **Kevin Mandia**

> "I am the most boring leader on the planet emotionally. You better stay this narrow a lane of temperament... there's a whiplash to either side when you're the founder or the CEO." — **Kevin Mandia**
