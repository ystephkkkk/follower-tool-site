## He Helped Create Google Drive. This Is What He’s Building Next.

**TL;DR** 
Shan Sinha, Co-Founder and CEO of Canopy and former co-founder of DocVerse (which became Google Drive), discusses his journey of persistence through 60 investor rejections, the critical need to protect healthcare workers from rising workplace violence using wearable technology, and why AI will act as a job creator rather than a disruptor.

---

### The Journey from DocVerse to Google Drive

*   **Riding the Early Cloud Wave:** DocVerse was founded in the early 2000s, capitalizing on the launch of Amazon S3 (the foundation of AWS), which sparked a massive industry shift toward cloud storage.
*   **The Strategic Pivot:** The company originally set out to digitize physical paper files. 
    *   Realizing that paper scanning was too operationally complex, the team pivoted to solving a daily pain point: enabling real-time, cloud-based collaboration on Microsoft Word and Excel files. 
    *   This was a novel concept developed prior to the widespread adoption of Google Docs.
*   **Overcoming 60 Rejections:** Sinha and his co-founders pitched their cloud collaboration idea to roughly 60 investors before securing their first funding check.
*   **The Google Acquisition:** Google acquired DocVerse to integrate its technology into Google Apps (now Google Workspace). This software eventually became the foundation for the Google Drive Sync Client used by millions of users today.

### Canopy: Protecting Healthcare Workers with Wearable Tech

*   **The Workplace Violence Crisis:** Hospitals employ 10 million people in the U.S., but workers face an underreported crisis of high violence rates from escalating patients. This has led to severe staffing shortages and a mass exodus from the profession.
    *   **Demographic Vulnerability:** Women make up 80% of the healthcare workforce and are frequently isolated alone in rooms or dark hallways with patients, especially during overnight shifts.
*   **The Wearable Solution:** Canopy developed a battery-powered, locatable, wearable duress button that clips onto a healthcare worker’s badge.
    *   With a discreet press, workers can instantly signal for help and connect with hospital security or support teams.
*   **Scale and Collaboration:** Canopy's solution was co-developed in partnership with healthcare leaders and Thomas Jefferson University Hospital in Philadelphia. 
    *   It is currently used by more than 350,000 healthcare workers across 70 health systems nationwide.

### Redefining the American Dream and Global Talent

*   **A Product of the American Dream:** Sinha grew up in Dallas, Texas, went to public schools, lived in a two-bedroom apartment with his single mother and sister, and attended MIT via extensive financial aid. 
*   **Systemic Affordability Challenges:** Sinha warns that the American Dream is becoming increasingly unaffordable due to skyrocketing costs for housing, childcare, and college education. 
*   **Policy Recommendations:** Rather than focusing on the number of billionaires, Sinha advocates for federal policies that drive social mobility and secure global competitiveness.
    *   **Attracting Global Talent:** The U.S. should actively recruit and monopolize the best technical and entrepreneurial talent from around the world to build future industries domestically.
    *   **Prioritizing Science and Education:** Strengthening the domestic public education system with a rigorous focus on science, technology, and excellence is vital to growing the future middle class.

### Entrepreneurship, AI, and the Future of Work

*   **AI as an Economic Enabler:** Sinha firmly believes that AI will be a net positive for society, functioning as a human enabler that will ultimately create more jobs than it eliminates.
*   **Internal Canopy Operations:** Canopy has integrated AI across all internal company functions to boost productivity:
    *   Engineers use AI tools to generate product documentation and code new features.
    *   Marketing, customer success, and sales divisions are actively deploying AI agents to optimize their workflows.
*   **AI-Driven Hospital Optimization:** Canopy is applying AI to its proprietary location-tracking technology. By analyzing location data, the system helps hospitals track mobile equipment (such as wheelchairs and IV pumps) and optimize patient flows to reduce wait times and eliminate operational bottlenecks.
*   **Advice for Founders:** Aspiring entrepreneurs should immediately learn to use AI tools and seek out early-stage environments where new, untested ideas are being brought to life.
