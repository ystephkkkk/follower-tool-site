---

### TL;DR
Vice President JD Vance emphasized that AI developers must take product liability for safety rather than relying on heavy-handed government regulation, while detailing the administration's interagency task force efforts to combat systemic welfare and healthcare fraud.

### AI Governance & Product Liability

* **Opposition to New Regulatory Monopolies:**
  * **Avoiding "Panic" Regulation:** Vance cautioned against creating new regulatory bodies (like an FDA or FAA for AI) in the middle of a panic, noting that bureaucrats know far less about the technology than builders.
  * **Section 230 and Cartels:** Vance argued it would be a "huge mistake" to give Section 230 protections to new technology companies or allow the 4 or 5 biggest model companies to form a "unified cartel against the American consumer."
* **Primary Reliance on Product Liability:**
  * **Pre-existing Regulators:** Vance pointed to the FTC and the Department of Justice as the appropriate enforcement channels for consumer rights violations and unsafe products.
  * **Holding Labs Responsible:** Developers must realize they cannot build dangerous or flawed products and then ask the government to regulate them; they must exercise responsibility over their own tools.
* **Cybersecurity and Competition:**
  * **Cyber Defense over Censorship:** Vance noted that frontier models have a remarkable ability to enable hackers, and the solution is to give technology companies the tools to patch their code and protect themselves.
  * **Open Source and Startups:** Vance expressed strong support for robust antitrust laws to protect open source debates and ensure young upstarts can enter and compete in the industry.

### Government Fraud & Welfare Administration

* **Interagency Anti-Fraud Task Force:**
  * **Sustaining DOGE Efforts:** Building on the short-term work done by Elon Musk and DOGE, the administration established a permanent anti-fraud focus with teeth.
  * **Whole-of-Government Approach:** Combating fraud requires cross-agency coordination; for example, SNAP food stamp fraud involving illegal immigrants requires the DOJ to coordinate with the Department of Homeland Security and the Department of Agriculture.
* **Healthcare and Benefit Verification:**
  * **Dr. Oz's Task Force:** Vance praised Dr. Oz and his team for identifying parts of Medicare and Medicaid that route money to fraudsters rather than providing actual care.
  * **The Eligibility Crisis:** Vance highlighted a massive systemic failure where hundreds of billions or trillions of dollars are distributed without basic identity verification or eligibility checks (citing California's inability to state who is on its welfare rolls).
* **Protecting the Social Contract:**
  * **Preserving Generosity:** Vance affirmed his support for social welfare programs that help Americans in need, but stressed that public generosity relies on trust that the IRS and government are careful with taxpayer dollars.
