## CEO Michael Arougheti on Ares Record Q4 '25 Earnings, Scale in Private Markets, Retaining Top Talent

- Channel: Ares Management
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=64lcqWQTIKo
- Published: 2026-02-09
- Briefing: https://ystephkkkk.github.io/follower-tool-site/20260209.html#video-64lcqWQTIKo
- Speakers: Michael Arougheti (CEO, Ares Management); Dani Burger (Host, Bloomberg Open); Matt Miller (Host, Bloomberg Open)

**TL;DR**
Ares Management reported record AUM of $622 billion for Q4 2025 (29% annual growth), signaling a massive disconnect between public market volatility and the robust institutional demand for private credit despite concerns over AI disruption and tightening spreads.

### Record Financial Growth and Market Disconnect
*   **Record AUM:** Total assets under management reached **$622 billion** in Q4 2025.
    *   The firm’s assets grew by **29% in 2025**, driven by record inflows for its US direct lending business.
*   **Fundamental Divergence:** CEO Michael Arougheti highlights a "huge disconnect" between public equity markets (software indices down 20-22%) and the fundamentals of private credit.
*   **Guidance and Dividends:** 
    *   Declared a **20% forward increase** in the dividend.
    *   Five-year guidance projects earnings growth of **16-20%** and dividend growth of **20%+**.
*   **Yield Performance:** Private credit continues to generate **150 to 200 basis points of excess return** over broadly syndicated loans, even as spreads tighten across the broader market (high yield, high grade, and loans).

### Defensive Credit Profile and AI Exposure
*   **Software Exposure:** Just **9% of the private credit book** (6% of total AUM) is tied to software, countering market fears of AI-driven sector collapse.
*   **Seniority and Safety:** These loans are largely senior secured with a **30% loan-to-value (LTV)** ratio.
    *   Portfolios are comprised of mature enterprise software businesses with **40%+ EBITDA margins**.
    *   Weighted average maturity is short, at **3 to 4 years**, with stream yields between **10-12%**.
*   **AI as a Catalyst:** Rather than just a risk, Arougheti views AI as a growth driver for Ares’ digital infrastructure and renewable energy practices.
    *   **Portfolio De-leveraging:** AI implementation is expected to improve margins and free cash flow across general portfolios, naturally de-leveraging the book.

### The Strategy of Scale and Industry Consolidation
*   **Scale Advantage:** Unlike public markets where size can diminish performance, Arougheti argues that **size drives performance in private markets** due to the resource-intensive nature of sourcing and managing "esoteric" investments.
*   **M&A Activity:** Recent consolidation (e.g., KKR/Arctos, CVC/Marathon, EQT/Coller) is driven by institutional investors wanting to consolidate capital with fewer global managers.
    *   **Succession Planning:** Much of the current M&A wave is driven by founders in their 60s and 70s seeking to solidify their legacies.
    *   **Ares M&A:** Approximately **25% of Ares’ historical growth** has come from acquisitions, which remains a key part of their future strategy.
*   **Fee Consistency:** Despite "retail" expansion, Ares is not experiencing fee compression in high-alpha products; fee rates have remained steady at approximately **1%**.

### Talent Retention and the AI-Driven Shift in Labor
*   **Leadership Stability:** Ares maintains low turnover among senior staff; all co-founders remain in management roles.
*   **Succession Execution:** Arougheti recently promoted two long-standing partners to **Co-Presidents**, following his own 10-year tenure as CEO and a smooth transition from predecessor Tony Ressler.
*   **Evolution of Junior Roles:** AI is expected to replace the "mundane" aspects of junior analyst work (e.g., 24-hour model grinding).
    *   **Future Talent Profile:** The firm will pivot toward hiring talent capable of **systemic and critical thinking** rather than pure manual data synthesis.

> "There's a huge, huge disconnect... I think what the market is missing is how attractive these loans have been and how difficult it will be to actually displace them." — **Michael Arougheti**

> "Value is driven through your ability to source, develop, structure and manage esoteric investments... the larger you get the higher the likelihood that you can actually capture whatever excess return and alpha's in the market." — **Michael Arougheti**

> "Everything that we're deploying [in AI] now is being deployed to enhance the work that's already getting done... we hope that we're hiring young people that know how to think systemically." — **Michael Arougheti**
