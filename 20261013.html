TL;DR

Ineffable Intelligence is building a "superlearner"—an AI agent that starts from zero knowledge and discovers science, mathematics, and language autonomously through direct reinforcement learning experience rather than pre-training on human data. Co-founders David Silver and Lasse Espeholt forecast strong evidence within one year and superintelligence within three.

Company Mission & Core Concepts

  • The Superlearner Goal:
    • Depth: Start from absolute zero and discover knowledge far beyond human capabilities, analogous to the collective human journey from a "gray mush" in our heads to modern science and mathematics.
    • Breadth: Drop into any stream of experience—simulated, digital, or physical—and continuously learn.
  • Crucibles (Simulated Worlds):
    • Self-contained digital environments designed without handcrafted puzzles to foster massive emergent complexity.
    • Incorporate simple primitives (e.g., physics engines, multi-agent interactions, information theory) that give rise to higher-level emergent behaviors like economics, tool use, and language.
  • Why Drop Human Priors?:
    • Human language and data act as a ceiling and drag, embedding historical human biases (e.g., outdated scientific beliefs) into models.
    • Removing human priors mirrors the AlphaGo Zero transition: when human data was removed, AlphaGo Zero surpassed two years of human-guided AlphaGo progress in just a few hours/days.

Technical Infrastructure & Team

  • The Compute Setup:
    • First customers of the largest GPU cluster on GCP, scaling up to major Nvidia Blackwell clusters by late 2025/early 2026.
    • Architecture leverages high-bandwidth racks designed to treat entire racks like single machines, optimized for fast RL iteration loops (lineage tracing back to IMPALA and SEED RL).
  • Avoiding CPU Bottlenecks:
    • Designing systems where simulation, training, and inference all run natively on GPUs, avoiding the heavy CPU sandbox reliance seen in current LLM architectures.
  • Team Structure & Culture:
    • Deliberately small and elite team of 20 people to maintain a high compute-to-person ratio.
    • Research-led culture built as a "haven for research" prioritizing foundational breakthroughs over incremental V17.1 product releases.

Alignment, Safety, and Pacing

  • The Limits of Language Monitoring:
    • Monitoring LLM chain-of-thought in English is reaching its limits as models become more powerful and learn to obscure intent.
    • Ineffable plans to explore non-language monitoring techniques, such as shadow actions and using separate superintelligent monitoring agents to regularize behavior.
  • Science of Superintelligence in Simulation:
    • Building superintelligence in simulation first provides a safe, infinitely parallelizable sandbox to study how multiple superintelligences interact and coexist peacefully.
    • Real-World Gating Commitment: Ineffable commits to gating real-world actions strictly until sufficient empirical evidence from simulation proves the outcomes will be safe and beneficial.
  • Pacing the Frontier:
    • Strongly supportive of pacing real-world AI deployment to allow human institutions, emotional responses, and governance to adapt, while accelerating computational research at maximum speed inside simulation.

Predictions & Forward Calls

  • David Silver: Strong evidence that the superlearner approach is working will emerge within 1 year.
  • David Silver: A true superintelligence (discovering things far beyond human limits across science, math, and technology) will be achieved within 3 years (timelines accelerated from an initial 3–5 year estimate due to automated coding agent acceleration).

TL;DR

Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller noted that a surge in long-term US Treasury yields is helping cool interest-rate-sensitive sectors like housing and autos, functioning as a de facto rate hike. Speaking at Bloomberg's New Economic Forum, Waller also addressed how massive AI infrastructure spending is creating short-term inflation pressures before driving long-term productivity and growth.

AI Economy, Global Disparities, and Productivity

  • Global AI Divide:
    • U.S. Leadership: The U.S. is the dominant builder of AI models, alongside China.
    • Inputs & Infrastructure: Inputs are largely produced in Taiwan and South Korea; data centers are deployed globally.
    • GDP Implications: There is a high correlation between GDP per capita and workplace AI usage, risking widened global gaps if adoption lags in certain regions. Europe is a major user though it produces little AI hardware or models.
  • Productivity Lag:
    • Trial and Error: Major technological changes take years to show up in measured productivity as firms experiment with use cases.
    • Coding Impact: Coding tools are shockingly good and fundamentally changing the job, shifting workers from basic coding to expanded output.
    • Welfare vs. GDP: Efficiency gains may manifest as increased leisure time rather than higher annual output per worker, challenging traditional GDP metrics alongside life-improving non-GDP inventions like Google Maps.

Inflation Pressures from the AI Buildout

  • Short-Term Price Pressures:
    • Inelastic Demand: Hyperscalers are spending trillions with perfectly inelastic demand, bidding up prices for semiconductors of all types and spilling over into broader goods.
    • Timeline: Price pressures driven by the AI buildout could last 2 to 3 years, based on current commitments from hyperscalers.
  • Long-Term Deflationary Supply Effects: Once the massive buildout concludes, demand pressure will taper off and supply effects will allow more output produced faster and at lower prices.

Global Bond Yields, Growth, and Fiscal Pressures

  • Drivers of Higher Real Yields:
    • Real Growth, Not Inflation: High yields are driven by real growth, productivity expectations, and AI investments rather than rising inflation expectations (which remain well-anchored).

TL;DR

Robinhood is introducing AI trading agents to the main app to democratize access to sophisticated algorithmic tools, alongside initiatives in private markets, prediction markets, and tokenized stock ownership. Co-founder and CEO Vlad Tenev argues that giving everyday retail investors direct ownership in emerging AI technologies and financial products is essential to easing broader societal backlash against the industry.

Robinhood Agents & AI Trading

  • Genesis and Early Iterations: The project started as part of an effort to empower retail traders with tools matching professional hedge funds and algorithmic trading firms.
    • MCP Launch: Earlier in the year, Robinhood launched an agentic trading offering via Model Context Protocol (MCP) in a separate account requiring a manual, affirmative transfer of funds from users.
    • Current Capabilities: Initially limited to unlevered, cash-settled equities without margin, the platform has since expanded to include options, crypto, and limited margin capabilities.
    • Adoption Metrics: Over 100,000 customers have onboarded to the agentic trading offering, showing steady retention and growth, particularly after unlocking features like limited margin.
  • Friction Points and Product Evolution:
    • Developer Complexity: Tools like Claude Code and Codeex presented too much friction for the average retail user who did not want to manage strategies across disparate platforms.
    • Model Resistance: General models connected via MCP initially resisted trading because trading traces were absent from their training data. Robinhood addressed this by helping models understand and optimize for Robinhood's ecosystem.
    • Integrated Experience: Robinhood Agents are now being brought directly into the main app to provide a single management location.
  • Guardrails & Default Settings:
    • Opt-In Controls: Setup takes approximately 60 seconds (naming the agent, picking a model, and deciding on trade approvals). By default, users must approve every trade, though an autonomous path exists.
    • Account Separation: Agentic accounts remain segregated from main Robinhood and retirement accounts so money cannot move or trade without affirmative user intent.
  • The Future of Agentic Volume: Tenev expects a very large portion of trading *volume* for individual investors to be generated by AI agents because they operate 24/7 across multiple asset classes simultaneously, while long-term assets will largely remain held by humans.

Investment Edge & The Marketplace

  • Evolving Alpha: Rather than relying on a static edge, market advantage will come from new ecosystems, asset classes, and product architectures.
    • Robinhood Chain: A newly rolled-out blockchain optimized for real-world assets that saw immediate developer activity, DEX volume, and bridging from traders utilizing stock tokens, memes, and other assets.
    • Cross-Asset Strategies: Robinhood combines stocks, options, crypto, futures, decentralized finance (DeFi), perpetual futures, banking, retirement advisory, and prediction markets with a unified balance and KYC framework, enabling complex multi-asset strategies difficult for unassisted humans.
  • Agent App Store and Data:
    • Third-Party Tools: The open Agent App Store links third-party developers (such as unusual data providers like Unusual Whales) who supply tools and data to agents.
    • Business Model: Robinhood relies primarily on subscriptions for the marketplace rather than transaction or asset-based revenue shares to preserve customer privacy regarding account balances and trading strategies.
  • High-Frequency Trading Ambition: A future north star for Robinhood is democratizing low-latency infrastructure, potentially letting users deploy trading strategies co-located with exchange matching engines without needing expertise in data centers or networking.

AI Inequality & Societal Backlash

  • The Root of AI Resentment: Tenev strongly agrees that public backlash against AI is deeply tied to wealth inequality. When data centers are built in local communities without everyday people holding equity in the underlying AI companies, citizens view the technology as resource-consuming infrastructure that only enriches venture capitalists.
  • Robinhood Ventures Fund 1 & 2:
    • Fund 1: Publicly traded venture funds listed on the NYSE providing mass-market retail distribution to frontier, late-stage companies like OpenAI, Databricks, and Revolute. The bulk of capital came from Robinhood individual investors with zero carry charged by Robinhood Ventures.
    • Fund 2: Seed and Series A-stage fund seeded with a diverse portfolio of 80 early-stage companies to capture the high aggregate returns of early investing while mitigating individual company failure risks.
  • Crypto Comparison: Unlike AI, early crypto distribution was decentralized from day one, allowing ordinary people to build wealth before institutional adoption, creating a powerful political constituency ("crypto army") that defends the technology. Tenev argues that giving retail investors skin in the game early through ownership solves the public relations and political problems facing AI.

Tokenized Stocks & Global Markets

  • Global Accessibility: Robinhood Chain stock tokens are live in over 120 countries, allowing users in regions with hyperinflating local currencies (e.g., South America) to gain fractional, 24/7 access to US stocks (such as NVIDIA and SpaceX) via self-custodied wallets that cannot be confiscated.
  • Governance and Pushback: While critics (including meme-stock figures) have raised concerns over voting rights and traditional corporate governance, Tenev points out that most shareholders do not vote their shares anyway, and that electronification of stock certificates met similar skepticism.
  • Regulatory Milestones: Following initial pushes into Europe, Robinhood recently secured an innovation exemption in the US, positioning tokenization as an inevitable evolution for every asset class, including private markets, real estate, and wine.

Prediction Markets & The Future of News

  • Product Expansion: Prediction markets have matured into a true tradable asset class on Robinhood, featuring bespoke hubs for major events like the World Cup and the US midterms (including native map interfaces).
  • Synergies: Robinhood is seeing increasing connections between prediction markets and other assets like crypto and stocks.
  • Media Shift: Tenev views prediction markets as the future of news, noting that traditional linear TV and cable consumption are fading out in favor of targeted discovery (comparable to how streaming services replaced cable for sports like the NBA playoffs).

IPO Underwriting & Macroeconomic Views

  • Disrupting Underwriters: Through offerings like Aura, Robinhood is building out its IPO underwriting business to bypass traditional high-fee advisory cabals.
  • Issuer Data Advantage: Rather than relying on guesswork during pricing discussions, Robinhood provides issuers with a precise demand curve at every price point derived from its massive individual investor base.
  • Macro Environment: Having grown up during hyperinflation in 1980s and 1990s Bulgaria (where inflation reached thousands of percent), Tenev views current US economic challenges (such as 3% inflation and rising rates) as modest, noting that American economic fundamentals, domestic data center investments, and a push toward energy/nuclear innovation keep him optimistic.
  • Trump Accounts: Tenev praises the administration-backed "Trump Accounts" program as a monumental legacy effort that connects individuals to the US economy from age zero, ensuring multi-decade compounding and broad societal ownership.

Taiwan's Semiconductor Dominance & The Engineering Culture

  • Global Hardware Heart: Taiwan produces over 90% of the world's most advanced semiconductors; computers, smartphones, GPUs, and AI data centers worldwide depend entirely on this single island.
  • Historical Origins: The semiconductor industry's roots in Taiwan trace back to a vision by K.T. Li in the 1950s, which attracted Philips to set up operations and eventually spawned TSMC and ASML's manufacturing equipment lineage.
  • Cultural Status of Engineering: Engineering is deeply prized within the education and social fabric; as one local remarked, children learn to spell chip technologies like CoWoS before luxury brands like Gucci.
  • PC Revolution Catalyst: The rise of the PC industry was accelerated by IBM opening its chipset specifications, allowing Taiwanese engineers to design motherboards and chipsets for companies like Compaq and Dell.

NVIDIA Hardware & Manufacturing Evolution

  • Advanced Liquid Cooling: NVIDIA's latest generation has transitioned to 100% liquid cooling (up from 80% in the previous Grace Blackwell generation), reducing assembly time from two hours to five minutes while increasing reliability.
  • Vera CPU Economics: The Vera CPU delivers a 1.8x improvement over x86 processors, shifting the economic model from renting CPU cores (dollars per core) to generating AI tokens as fast as possible.
  • Manufacturing and Reindustrialization: Jensen Huang emphasizes that the AI boom provides a prime opportunity to reindustrialize the United States, invest in energy grids, and leverage Taiwan as a strategic manufacturing partner despite talent shortages.

Supply Chain Partnerships & The Power of "Rough Justice"

  • The TSMC Partnership: NVIDIA's 30-year relationship with TSMC began when Jensen wrote a cold letter to founder Morris Chang, who returned the call and promised to "jump through hoops."
  • Philosophy of Long-Term Loyalty: Rather than relying on ironclad, brittle contracts, Jensen advocates for "rough justice"—maintaining loose, trust-based partnerships where both sides win over decades and profit is deliberately left on the table for partners.
  • Ecosystem Success: Jensen notes with pride that during recent growth, his entire supply chain's stock price surged up to 300% while NVIDIA's remained comparatively steady, reflecting a commitment to partner sustainability.

Geopolitical Strategy, Flywheels, and Export Controls

  • The Global Flywheel: Jensen argues against strict isolationist export controls, contending that national security is best achieved by maximizing platform reach, customer usage, and economic wealth to fuel an unstoppable R&D flywheel.
  • Language and Ecosystem Advantage: American tech stacks benefit globally because the vast majority of the world responds to English, creating an expansive global power advantage that should not be squandered.
  • Avoiding Conflict: Maintaining deep technological and economic interdependence with international partners (including China) makes decoupling harder and helps prevent global conflict through friendly competition.

Food, Culture, and Humility
