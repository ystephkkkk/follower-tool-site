TL;DR

Yuval Noah Harari argues that AI represents an unprecedented "agency" that is mastering human language and bureaucratic systems—the very foundations of civilization—while deteriorating human trust and accelerating an uncontainable technological arms race.

The AI Revolution as a New Tower of Babel

  • Language as the New Tower: Unlike the biblical myth where language was a tool used to build a physical tower, computer code and AI represent a universal language that is becoming an independent agent rather than an obedient tool.
  • The Acceleration Trap: International order is collapsing and human trust is eroding, but rather than stopping the project, mutual suspicion among corporations and nations is supercharging an AI development arms race.
  • Historical Precedent of Niche Adaptation: Just as ancient microbes polluted the atmosphere with oxygen—a deadly poison to most organisms at the time—humans are now filling the world with data, bureaucracy, and language tokens that create an artificial ecosystem where AIs flourish.

AI as Native Bureaucrats

  • Mastery of Bureaucratic Systems: While an AI dropped in the jungle cannot mine iron or build a robot army, it is a "native bureaucrat" capable of remembering all legal codes, financial transactions, and religious texts far better than any human professional.
  • Impending Institutional Takeover: In the coming years, AI systems will increasingly make critical decisions across society, acting as AI bankers granting loans, AI judges sentencing defendants, corporate AIs hiring candidates, and military AIs determining targets.
  • The Erosion of Human Trust: As humans lose trust in each other and human institutions, social media bureaucracies run by AI spread division while users blindly trust the algorithms feeding them fake news and conspiracy theories.

The Threat to Human Leadership and Time

  • Inorganic vs. Organic Realities: Unlike organic humans, animals, and trees who require biological cycles of rest and downtime (such as markets closing on weekends), AIs operate continuously without vacations or holidays.
  • Poverty of Time for Leaders: Political and business leaders are increasingly denied time to rest, meditate, or cultivate the health of their minds—leading to troubled leadership making decisions that impact millions.
  • The Ultimate Currency: While money is a fiction made of words, the real currency of life is time; leaders who cannot afford time to think deeply risk driving society toward paranoia rather than trust.

AI Value Chain, Infrastructure, and Market Risks

  • The Binding Constraints (Compute & Power):
    • An S-1 risk factor word-cloud analysis reveals compute (data centers, power, chips, electricity, grid) is Anthropic's single biggest concern, followed closely by regulation.
    • Geographic and energy limitations mean data centers face structural physical constraints, not just political ones.
  • Capital Crowding Out Markets:
    • AI infrastructure spending is creating immense debt; the AI complex owes $340 billion to $400 billion to the market.
    • AI fundraising through late 2027 represents 30% of all Treasury issuances in the same period, creating systemic market risk by competing directly with government bonds for capital.
  • Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) & Doomer Splits:
    • Anthropic is aggressively racing toward full RSI, using its own models to generate algorithmic improvements ("compute multipliers").
    • This relentless push toward RSI is sparking an emerging split between Anthropic and AI safety "doomers" who fear a breakaway superintelligence.

Google’s $500B Market Cap Wipeout

  • Earnings Miss & Search Slowdown:
    • Google shed $500 billion in market cap after missing on search revenue for two straight quarters.
    • Core search revenue grew 12% (down from 17%), reaching $63 billion, but growth is decelerating as AI answers and consumer alternatives disrupt traditional search habits.
    • Allegations have surfaced that Google changed its auction model (abandoning Vickery auctions), leading to potential over-earning on clicks.
  • The Bright Spots (Cloud & Diversification):
    • Google Cloud doubled year-over-year, generating $30.7 billion in revenue and $11 billion in operating income (up from $3.5B previously).
    • Search revenue dropped below 50% of total revenue for the first time, signaling successful long-term diversification into YouTube, Android, G Suite, and Cloud.

TL;DR

Supabase Co-founder and CEO Paul Copplestone joins the Lightcone hosts to discuss how AI agents and a new class of non-developer "builders" are driving explosive growth, with the platform scaling to 13 million signups, over 50 million databases under management, and a million new databases spun up every week.

Growth and Scaling Metrics

  • User and Database Scale:
    • Signups: Reached 13 million users (up from approximately 3 million 12 months prior) — *Paul Copplestone*
    • Databases Under Management: Reached over 50 million databases (up from 8 million a year ago) — *Paul Copplestone*
    • Database Velocity: Spinning up over 1 million databases per week — *Paul Copplestone*
    • Revenue Growth: Projected to grow 4x this year — *Paul Copplestone*
  • AI Agent Activity: Approximately 70% of new activity on Supabase now originates from AI agents — *Harsh*

Three Eras and Technical Evolution

  • The Dashboard Era (2020 YC Batch): Built in 100 days to make Postgres accessible via a UI similar to AirTable because even traditional software engineers struggled to use databases — *Paul Copplestone*
  • The API Era (2021): Shifted to an API-first approach after early partner integrations required programmatic database creation, positioning Supabase perfectly for code-gen tools like Bolt and Lovable — *Paul Copplestone*
  • The Code-First / Agent Era (Current): Re-architected the CLI, dashboard, and infrastructure-to-code modalities into sandboxes because human dashboard usage declined as agents took over — *Paul Copplestone*
  • Product Expansion:
    • Supabase Multi-Graze: Scales up to petabytes for heavy enterprise workloads — *Paul Copplestone*
    • Supabase Spark (via Tirso acquisition): Provides a SQLite- and Postgres-compatible tier designed to scale down for prototyping and lightweight agent/developer workloads — *Paul Copplestone*

Rise of the "Builder" and Personal Databases

  • Democratization of Coding: AI models have advanced to the point where non-engineers and domain experts (e.g., domain specialists, a 50-year-old taxi driver in Brazil building a mini-Uber app) can build complex software without convincing a traditional software engineer — *Paul Copplestone*
  • Personal Data and Apps:
    • Builders are creating personalized "end-of-one" software for health, personal finance (feature-compatible with Mint or Monarch), and custom CRMs — *Harsh & Paul Copplestone*
    • Users want to own their underlying database layer rather than locking all personal life data inside a single closed-source agent — *Harsh & Paul Copplestone*
  • Top-of-Funnel Dynamics: Unlike historical product-led growth (PLG) where top-of-funnel acceleration caused activation and conversion rates to drop, signups, activation, and conversion are all simultaneously trending up as models improve — *Paul Copplestone*

Agent-First Design and Internal Engineering

  • Agent Error Handling: Error messages have been re-engineered to include explicit instructions and documentation links so agents can automatically self-remedy and complete one-shot loops without hitting dead ends — *Paul Copplestone*
  • Testing and Evals: Internal engineering effort has heavily shifted from manual building to automated testing and eval suites across multiple AI models to benchmark performance and ensure code quality — *Paul Copplestone & Harsh*
  • Internal AI Adoption: Internal engineer code generation jumped from 10% writing 80% of code to 60% within six months under a permissive internal AI policy — *Paul Copplestone*

Predictions & Forward Calls

  • Global Database Scale: ChatGPT has 1.2 billion weekly active users, and if all of them launch something monthly, architectures must support 1.2 billion new databases a month — *Paul Copplestone*
  • Every Human as a Builder: Everyone in the world with access to AI will become a builder of some sort, capable of customizing their own apps and UIs by 2027 — *Paul Copplestone*

Master biographer Robert Caro reflects on 50 years of studying power through his seminal subjects, Lyndon B. Johnson and Robert Moses, detailing the relentless methods, political genius, and personal sacrifices required to understand and execute large-scale change.

The Mechanics of Power and Political Will

  • Relentless Pursuit: Both Robert Caro and his subjects (LBJ and Robert Moses) share an uncompromising refusal to accept "no" for an answer, tracking down obscure figures like Johnson's first campaign chauffeur and former election officials to uncover the unvarnished truth.
  • Leverage Behind Persuasion: According to Caro, Lyndon Johnson’s legendary persuasiveness was entirely backed by leverage—senators knew Johnson held the power to grant or deny committee assignments, laws, and political favors.
  • The Anatomy of Control: John Connally revealed that LBJ meticulously categorized political money requests (writing "OK", "500", "No", or "No, never"), serving as the ultimate gatekeeper of political currency.

Robert Moses and the Genius of Public Works

  • Building Against the Odds: Despite modern arguments that environmental reviews and community boards make government building impossible today, Moses faced equally fierce property-owner opposition in the 1920s and simply forced projects through via sheer will and political genius.
  • The Evolution of Ruthlessness: Moses began as a young, idealistic civil service reformer and creator of public beaches for city residents, but evolved into a rigid autocrat who evicted tens of thousands of people for projects like the Cross Bronx Expressway without relocation provisions.
  • Depression-Era Advantage: Moses's early successes were fueled by the Great Depression, which allowed him to hire thousands of desperate, out-of-work architects and laborers for pennies on the dollar and fire them ruthlessly.
  • The Tragic Loss of Power: Caro observed firsthand the poignant decline of Robert Moses, noting how the aging, insulated builder—who once commanded endless phone calls—was eventually abandoned by mayors and governors like Nelson Rockefeller (who calmly accepted Moses's resignation threat in 1962).

Lyndon Johnson’s Vision and Tragedy

  • A Unique Urgency to Change America: Unlike John F. Kennedy, who lacked an overriding domestic reform agenda, LBJ was fundamentally driven to reshape the country—enacting monumental civil rights and anti-poverty laws born from his early experience teaching impoverished children in Cotulla, Texas.
  • The 1948 Senate Election: Caro highlights the tragic defeat of Coke Stevenson ("Mr. Texas") in the 1948 Senate race against Johnson, noting that Stevenson never truly comprehended the extent of the tactics used against him.
  • The Vietnam Undoing: Johnson's transformative domestic presidency ultimately collapsed in tragedy due to his escalation of the Vietnam War—a psychological mystery Caro is still trying to unravel in his ongoing writing.

Crafting History: Method, Archives, and Personal Cost

  • One-Sentence Anchors: To avoid losing direction over multi-year projects (learning the hard way from *The Power Broker* where 350,000 words had to be cut from a 1,050,000-word draft), Caro forces himself to distill a book down to a single sentence or paragraph before writing a word.

TL;DR

AI platform Eve is transforming plaintiff law from a labor-constrained business into a high-volume, revenue-expanding practice by automating end-to-end workflows from client intake to settlement. This dramatically lowers the cost of bringing legal action, enabling small firms to handle up to 5x more clients and opening up thousands of previously unviable cases for everyday people.

Plaintiff Law Dynamics & Market Failures

  • The Contingency Model: Explained by Alex Rampell (a16z General Partner) and Jay Madheswaran (Co-founder & CEO of Eve) as an industry born out of necessity because individuals cannot afford $500–$600 hourly rates on a blank check. Attorneys only get paid when they win.
  • The Economic Bottleneck: 96% of labor and employment cases are rejected because attorneys must make at least $5,000 in fees per case just to break even, pricing out smaller claims like fender benders or workplace harassment.
  • Revenue Expansion vs. Cost Cutting: Jay Madheswaran emphasizes that Eve is not designed to help firms fire staff and cut costs, but rather to function as a revenue expansion tool that allows firms to take on clients they were previously forced to turn away.
  • Before and After Case Volume: 500 clients per attorney: The client load achieved by one Los Angeles law firm using Eve end-to-end, compared to roughly 100 clients previously.

What Eve Does & The Software Stack Shift

  • End-to-End Automation: Eve handles the entire case lifecycle—from the initial phone call and qualification, through preliminary work and demands, up to pre-mediation settlement briefs.
  • The Shadow Labor Market: Alex Rampell notes that while historical software spend for solo and small-firm plaintiff practices was close to zero (often just a cell phone and email), the actual outcomes delivered are in the hundreds of billions of dollars, making legal software a massive "shadow labor market."
  • Privacy and Data Sharing:
    • Attorney-Client Privilege: Case-specific data is strictly partitioned, and client data is never used for model training.
    • CLE & Collective Tactics: Firms share defense patterns (e.g., repeating tactics used by State Farm or GEICO) similarly to how they attend Continuing Legal Education (CLE) seminars.
  • Eve Auditor Product: An automated tool that reviews every single case page overnight to catch missed information that a perfect attorney would have spotted.

The Adversarial Dynamic & Future Legal Systems

  • Defense Pushback and AI Agents: Defense counsel (traditionally working for corporate payers like State Farm and GEICO) often use stalling tactics, such as dumping discovery requests right before major holidays. With Eve, firms now respond the same day using automated agents, shocking defense teams.
  • Automated Dispute Resolution (ADR): Jay Madheswaran predicts the legal system will increasingly rely on automated dispute resolution and AI-enabled mediators to speed up judgments outside traditional courtrooms.
  • The Mom-and-Pop Transformation: Small local law firms running on Eve can specialize locally and then scale nationally, potentially evolving from lifestyle practices into large, software-enabled billion-dollar businesses.

Predictions & Forward Calls

  • End-to-End Autonomy Timeline: Jay Madheswaran states that fully autonomous, end-to-end legal cases (from answering a phone and onboarding a client to receiving a check from GEICO) are approaching as engineering work progresses.
  • Agent-to-Agent Negotiation: Jay Madheswaran predicts that routine legal back-and-forths will increasingly be conducted by AI agents negotiating directly with other AI agents.
  • Future Scale Target: Jay Madheswaran notes that Eve has helped a couple hundred thousand clients to date and aims to impact hundreds of millions more.

TL;DR

AI will transform fundamental investing by automating prep work and raising industry table stakes, but humans remain the essential bottleneck for synthesizing insights and generating alpha. Consequently, the fully automated "no-analyst hedge fund" is an overhyped idea that won't beat the best human-led teams.

Fundamental Investing in the AI Era: Evolution, Not Replacement

  • Role of Stock Picking: Long-term stock picking and the fundamental pursuit of alpha through a divergent mosaic view will remain fundamentally unchanged.
  • Human Bottleneck: Investment management is ultimately a time management exercise constrained by how much information a human can process.
  • Analyst Workload Transformation: Junior analysts will stop spending hours on big data cleaning, manual earnings previews, and DCF model builds.
  • Preparation vs. Execution: While AI can cut preparation work from 6-8 hours down to 5 minutes, this freed-up time will be reinvested into deeper proprietary research, industry builds, and channel checks to stay competitive against peers working 16-hour days.

Data Trust, Auditability, and Security

  • The Necessity of Auditability: As a former CPA, Jack Woodruff emphasizes that one must never sign off on a model number without seeing its precise origin; millions of dollars can be lost on a single data entry mistake.
  • Verification Paradox: Current workflows still require users to audit numbers generated by tools like Claude, raising questions about net time savings.
  • Data Security & Isolation: Internal proprietary data security is critical; fund leadership relies heavily on CTOs to enforce strict isolation boundaries against AI vendors to protect intellectual property.

Fund Structure, Strategy, and Edge

  • Vertical vs. Pyramid Teams: Traditional fund structures featured a broad base of analysts feeding senior stock pickers and PMs. Future structures will likely flatten or become more vertical, reducing the number of "preparers" while retaining top "producers" and potentially adding investigative journalists or data engineers.
  • Sources of Edge: When every fund has access to the same baseline models and information, alpha will come from better prompting, faster execution combining man and machine, and the ability to identify special projects that warrant deep investigation.
  • Firm Scale Impact: AI helps funds of all sizes increase idea velocity and pipeline depth. A smaller team can cover a wider universe of stocks (scaling coverage from 100 to 400 names) without diluting research quality.

Overhyped Concepts vs. Reality

  • The "No-Analyst Hedge Fund" Myth: The widely discussed idea of eliminating human analysts entirely to run solely on AI agents is heavily overhyped.
  • Replication Limitation: While fully automated fundamental funds might replicate what a fund looked like a year ago, they will not outperform top-tier human-led funds operating with modern AI augmentation.
