TL;DR

Yuval Noah Harari argues that AI represents an unprecedented "agency" that is mastering human language and bureaucratic systems—the very foundations of civilization—while deteriorating human trust and accelerating an uncontainable technological arms race.

The AI Revolution as a New Tower of Babel

  • Language as the New Tower: Unlike the biblical myth where language was a tool used to build a physical tower, computer code and AI represent a universal language that is becoming an independent agent rather than an obedient tool.
  • The Acceleration Trap: International order is collapsing and human trust is eroding, but rather than stopping the project, mutual suspicion among corporations and nations is supercharging an AI development arms race.
  • Historical Precedent of Niche Adaptation: Just as ancient microbes polluted the atmosphere with oxygen—a deadly poison to most organisms at the time—humans are now filling the world with data, bureaucracy, and language tokens that create an artificial ecosystem where AIs flourish.

AI as Native Bureaucrats

  • Mastery of Bureaucratic Systems: While an AI dropped in the jungle cannot mine iron or build a robot army, it is a "native bureaucrat" capable of remembering all legal codes, financial transactions, and religious texts far better than any human professional.
  • Impending Institutional Takeover: In the coming years, AI systems will increasingly make critical decisions across society, acting as AI bankers granting loans, AI judges sentencing defendants, corporate AIs hiring candidates, and military AIs determining targets.
  • The Erosion of Human Trust: As humans lose trust in each other and human institutions, social media bureaucracies run by AI spread division while users blindly trust the algorithms feeding them fake news and conspiracy theories.

The Threat to Human Leadership and Time

  • Inorganic vs. Organic Realities: Unlike organic humans, animals, and trees who require biological cycles of rest and downtime (such as markets closing on weekends), AIs operate continuously without vacations or holidays.
  • Poverty of Time for Leaders: Political and business leaders are increasingly denied time to rest, meditate, or cultivate the health of their minds—leading to troubled leadership making decisions that impact millions.
  • The Ultimate Currency: While money is a fiction made of words, the real currency of life is time; leaders who cannot afford time to think deeply risk driving society toward paranoia rather than trust.

And this is the challenge posed by the AI revolution. The AI revolution creates unprecedented opportunities, but also unprecedented threats and dangers. To survive and flourish in the age of AI, humanity requires leaders who can create radical new visions, but also leaders that can maintain and strengthen the human bonds of trust. If humans cannot trust one another, we will all be easy prey for out-of-control superintelligent AIs. Let me begin by trying to help us understand the broad historical meaning of the AI revolution. Often when we try to understand something new, we compare it to something old and familiar. That makes it particularly difficult to understand the AI revolution because humanity has never experienced anything like it. Even our mythologies lack the necessary imagination. For example, in his thoughtful and inspiring encyclical about AI, published this May, Pope Leo tried to make sense of the AI revolution by comparing it to the biblical myth of the Tower of Babel. Babylon. That myth narrates how humankind sought to build a huge tower that would reach the sky, presumably challenging God. Let me quote the key passages in the biblical story. The Bible says that all the earth was one language, one set of words. And they said to each other, "Come, let us build a city and a tower with its top in the heavens." In this myth, language is the most important tool that humans use in order to cooperate and try to acquire divine powers, divine abilities. God thwarts the human plans not by striking them with lightning or earthquake, but simply by depriving humans of their tool, the common language. There are many interesting similarities between the Tower of Babel story and the AI revolution, but there are two crucial differences. First, in the Tower of Babel, humans used language as a tool to cooperate and build the tower. What is happening today, in contrast, is that language is itself the tower we are building. The AI revolution is the development of a new language, universal language, the language of computer code. This language will allegedly be so powerful that nothing we propose to do with it will be impossible. This language, for example, would enable us to reach the sky, colonize Mars, and who knows what else. Instead of humans using computer language to do whatever we want, computer language is learning how to do things by itself and might conquer the world and the solar system for itself instead of for the humans. The second crucial difference is that in the biblical myth, building the tower required universal human cooperation. And when human cooperation broke down, the building project stopped. With the AI tower, human cooperation is breaking down, but the but the building project only accelerates faster and faster. The international order is collapsing. Societies all over the world are in chaos. We humans are losing our shared reality and our ability to understand and trust each other. But this does not impede the building of the tower. The tower is being built faster and faster. Precisely because corporations and countries cannot trust each other, they are caught in an arms race and invest ever more resources to develop AI. Everybody is afraid of being left behind. Consequently, we are in a double bind. Lack of trust speeds up the development of AI and the same lack of trust simultaneously makes it harder for us to keep the AIs under our control. So, in the Tower of Babel myth, God confused human language in order to hamper human cooperation and thereby stopped the building of the tower. In the AI revolution, humans are building a super language that is becoming independent of us and we cannot slow down the building project or keep the super language under control because we cannot cooperate. These ideas are not easy to grasp, so let me explain all of that in simpler terms. The first thing to understand is that ever since the Stone Age, language has been the basis for the human domination of planet Earth. Humans conquer the world by learning to cooperate in very large numbers and what makes large-scale human cooperation possible is language. Individually, this is crucial. Individually, humans are not stronger or even smarter than other animals. In a one-on-one fight, a human will lose to a chimpanzee or a tiger or an elephant. That's why humans control the world because we know how to cooperate better than anyone else. And how do we cooperate? By using language. Language is crucial not for building personal relationships. Chimps are fully capable of building personal relationships even without language. Language is essential for building bureaucracies. And bureaucracies, like the legal system, the financial system, states, corporations, churches, are essential for any large-scale cooperation. Bureaucracies are the under-appreciated heroes of human history. Most humans dislike bureaucracies, but we cannot live without them. The job of bureaucracies is to build trust between large numbers of strangers and thereby enable large-scale cooperation in hospitals, armies, schools, and banks. For example, what banks do is to build trust between strangers so that millions of people can pool their resources and talents together to work on common projects such as constructing towers and spaceships. In books like the Bible, in laws, regulations, protocols, forms, archives, and so forth. Now, AI is mastering language. TL;DR

AWS CEO Matt Garman projects the cloud unit will sustain 40%+ year-over-year growth for the next two years, driven by widespread enterprise AI adoption and a $580 billion backlog, positioning AWS on a multi-year path toward a $1 trillion revenue business.

Financial Performance & Growth Projections

  • Current Scale: AWS is currently operating at a $184 billion run rate, growing at 39%, and has accelerated every single quarter for the last six consecutive quarters.
  • Forward Guidance: Matt Garman projects that AWS will achieve 40%+ year-over-year growth in both 2027 and 2028.
  • Contracted Backlog: The division's backlog has grown to $580 billion, representing a large base of multi-year, contracted enterprise commitments that provide multi-year visibility.
  • Profitability and Margins: The AWS business maintains operating margins in the high 30% range, generating strong cash flow alongside upfront capital expenditure requirements.

Customer Base & AI Demand Drivers

  • Widespread Enterprise Adoption: Growth is broadly distributed across tens to hundreds of thousands of customers spanning diverse industries (including banks, healthcare companies, and startups) rather than being concentrated solely in a few top AI labs.
  • Billion-Dollar Cohort: AWS has more customers committing to billion-dollar annual spends than ever before in the company's history.
  • Bedrock and Inference Volume: High-profile partnerships—such as with Anthropic and OpenAI—feed through AWS Bedrock, where a vast number of end-customers run their production inference workloads.
  • Early Stages of Transformation: Customers are currently only transforming 1% to 5% of their businesses using AI, signaling a massive runway for future expansion as utilization scales toward 10% and 50%.

Infrastructure, Supply Chain & Hardware Scaling

  • Rapid Hardware Deployment: AWS is installing one accelerator chip (GPUs or Trainium) every 14 seconds to meet surging demand.
  • Current Bottlenecks: The company remains supply constrained, with daily bottlenecks shifting dynamically across power availability, data center real estate, DRAM, advanced chips, and network components.
  • Deep Supply Chain Integration: AWS manages its supply chain three tiers deep, securing multi-year supplier commitments to balance infrastructure build-out with customer demand.
  • Trainium Expansion: AWS plans to begin selling Trainium chips outside of AWS next year, enabling enterprise customers to deploy Trainium on-premises or across other hybrid environments alongside cloud deployments.
  • Supplier Financing Mechanism: Amazon has arranged approximately $80 billion of supplier financing for technology infrastructure starting next year to responsibly fund aggressive CapEx build-outs (such as 5-to-6-year server lifecycles and 20-to-30-year data center/power assets) while balancing revenue cash flows.

Predictions & Forward Calls

  • 40%+ Growth Target: Matt Garman projects AWS will grow at 40%+ year-over-year in each of the next two years (2027 and 2028).
  • Trillion-Dollar Milestone: Matt Garman (referencing CEO Andy Jassy's outlook) projects AWS is on a path to become a $1,000,000,000,000 revenue business in five to ten years.
  • Trainium Commercialization: Matt Garman states that AWS will begin selling Trainium chips outside of AWS starting next year.
  • Infrastructure Financing: Matt Garman confirms that approximately $80 billion in supplier financing for technology infrastructure is arranged to begin deploying next year.
