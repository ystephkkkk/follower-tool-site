## FOMC Rate Decision and Economic Assessment
- **Interest Rate Hold:** The FOMC maintained the target rate for the federal funds rate at **3.75%–4.00%** following its October meeting.

  - First-half GDP growth was revised upward from an earlier estimate of **1.8%** to **2.4%**.

- **Labor Market Conditions:** Job gains have kept pace with the workforce, payroll growth is noticeably above last year's pace, and the four-week moving average of unemployment insurance claims continues to run at the lowest level in generations.

## Financial Conditions and Monetary Policy Strategy
- **Financial Tightening:** Since the September policy action, financial and credit conditions have tightened for some businesses and households as market participants become more discerning in capital allocations.
- **New Monetary Medicine:** Chair Warsh outlined the Fed's 5-month-old framework, which includes doubling down on the **2% inflation target** (measured by PCE), taking direct institutional responsibility, laying out a transparent monetary transmission framework, and periodically interrogating reality.
- **Rejection of Forward Guidance:** Warsh explicitly rejected forward guidance and prejudging upcoming meetings, stating that such communication is reserved for exigent circumstances or crises rather than normal, strong economic times.

## FOMC Meeting Schedule Changes (2027–2028)
- **Schedule Reduction:** Starting next fall, the FOMC will transition to **eight meetings per year**, which Warsh believes is the right number for regular policy-setting.
- **Strategy Meetings:** 
  - In **2027**, **one meeting** will focus exclusively on broad monetary policy strategy with no vote on the federal funds rate and no policy statement anticipated.
  - In **2028**, the Fed plans to hold **two meetings** dedicated to strategic discussions and **six** for setting policy.

## Task Forces, Productivity, and Public Hearings
- **Internal Task Forces:** Five internal task forces are examining productivity and jobs, an inflation framework, data sources, and balance sheet tasks under a year-end deadline.
- **November 16th Public Field Hearing:** The productivity and jobs task force will host a **3 to 4-hour** public field hearing on November 16th to evaluate near-, medium-, and long-term implications for jobs and the broader economy, with Chair Warsh planning to attend.
- **Balance Sheet Timeline:** Recommendations from the balance sheet task force are expected to be fed into the FOMC around the **early first quarter**.
