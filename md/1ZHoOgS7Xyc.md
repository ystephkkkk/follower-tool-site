JPMorgan CEO Jamie Dimon warns that India will find it increasingly difficult to remain non-aligned amid rising geopolitical tensions, while urging Washington and New Delhi to conclude a stalled trade agreement. Dimon also highlights structural capital demands from AI, infrastructure, and remilitarization that could keep interest rates higher for longer.

## India-US Relations & Trade
* **Strategic Alignment:** Dimon notes that while India can attempt to stay non-aligned, it will become "harder and harder" in the current global environment. He urges both governments to reach out as natural partners.
* **Stalled Trade Negotiations:** The trade agreement discussed a year ago has stalled. Dimon urges both administrations to finish the pact to ensure consistency of rule, law, and capital treatment.
* **JPMorgan's Footprint in India:** The firm's presence has expanded from 6,000 employees in 2005 to 60,000 across technical areas, investment banking, and research covering 200 Indian companies and 600 multinationals.
