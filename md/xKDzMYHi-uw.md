## Historic Asia Trip, Trilateral Summit & Global Diplomacy
* **First-Ever US-Russia-China Trilateral Summit:** In Shenzhen, China, Trump met jointly with President Xi Jinping and President Vladimir Putin, calling it the most momentous summit in years.
  * **Nuclear Risk Reduction:** The three nations—which hold 92% of the world's nuclear weapons (U.S. ~4,000, Russia ~3,000+, China ~600)—signed a formal trilateral peace declaration agreeing nuclear conflict must never happen and launching first-ever trilateral talks on nuclear risk reduction.
  * **Energy & International Courts:** The three leaders agreed to increase oil, gasoline, and diesel production to lower global prices and jointly condemned the International Criminal Court (ICC) for overreach.
  * **Russia-Ukraine War:** Trump held a bilateral meeting with Putin, stating Putin wants a diplomatic end to the war, which is killing an average of 25,933 soldiers per month. Trump noted deep personal hatred between Putin and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy remains the primary obstacle.
* **Quad & ASEAN Summits in Manila:** Trump attended the Quad Leaders Summit (U.S., India, Australia, Japan) and addressed 14 nations at ASEAN.
  * **Quad Initiatives:** Announced the first-ever Quad Bureau Leaders Forum (meeting early next year), a new Quad Superintelligence (SI) accelerator, and expanded maritime security and surveillance cooperation.
  * **Greenland & Undersea Defense:** Trump announced that U.S. control/access to Greenland was approved the previous day, thanking Denmark and NATO's Mark Rutte, and highlighted new efforts on cybersecurity and defending undersea cables.
* **Record-Setting Flight:** Trump noted the new Air Force One completed the return flight in 14.5 hours without refueling—beating the previous record by 4 hours and avoiding the typical 20-hour flight with fuel stops.
* **Rubio's Diplomatic Framing:** Secretary of State Marco Rubio emphasized that regular engagement among the U.S., Russia, and China is essential, calling the idea of not engaging "absurd" and "dangerous" given their nuclear arsenals, and noting that head-of-state relationships set the tone for trade negotiators and diplomats.

## Trade Deals, Tariffs & Domestic Manufacturing
* **Philippines & Thailand Trade Agreements:** Trump secured two major trade deals during the trip, alongside 10 more expected soon.

  * **Philippines:** Reached a framework deal to eliminate the U.S. trade deficit by slashing Philippine tariffs and committing the Philippines to buy 100% of its agricultural products (starting January 1) and 100% of its energy from the U.S.
  * **Commercial & Defense Contracts:** Secured over $17 billion in commercial deals at ASEAN, including Philippine Airlines purchasing 20 Boeing 787 Dreamliners with General Electric engines (supporting 47,000 U.S. jobs), cloud infrastructure agreements with the Philippines and Malaysia, and over $2 billion in U.S. military equipment purchases by the Philippines.

  * **Canada:** Met with Prime Minister Carney; Trump cited an $80 billion U.S. trade deficit with Canada and said talks will advance once it reaches zero, though he expressed support for Carney's desire to build the Keystone pipeline.
* **Domestic Economic & Manufacturing Metrics:**

He also praised Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang as "brilliant" and suggested he be given a second chance and allowed to return to work after an apology.
