## TL;DR
Nvidia is officially acquiring AI platform Hugging Face for $12.93 billion to secure and accelerate the open-source AI ecosystem, uniting Nvidia's computing platform with Hugging Face's 18 million developers and 3 million models.

## Acquisition Deal & Valuation
* **Financial Terms:** Nvidia is acquiring Hugging Face for **$12.93 billion** after competitive bidding (**$4.5 billion** was Hugging Face's valuation in 2023 following a round backed by Google, Nvidia, and Salesforce).
* **Strategic Rationale:** — **Jensen Huang** noted the price reflects the company's true worth and strategic importance to Nvidia, preventing it from falling into alternative hands while driving the open-source growth vector that powers half of Nvidia's business.
* **Catalyst for the Deal:** — **Clément Delangue** explained that during the summer, it became clear open-source AI had reached a turning point requiring greater scale, resources, and visibility, prompting him to approach Jensen Huang.

## Open Models vs. Proprietary AI
* **The Two Paths in AI:** — **Clément Delangue** highlighted a branching future: one dominated by proprietary APIs where everyone outsources AI, versus an open-source path where everyone can own and build AI.
* **Defending with Open Models:** The rise of advanced agents over the summer proved that proprietary closed-source APIs are insufficient for self-defense, necessitating open models.
* **Nvidia's Balanced Ecosystem:** — **Jensen Huang** emphasized that Nvidia supports both sides—cloud providers and closed labs (which are major customers), as well as enterprise on-premise and sovereign AI users leveraging open models for domain-specific, proprietary intellectual property.
* **Scale of Hugging Face:** — **Jensen Huang** noted Hugging Face's massive reach across 200,000 enterprise customers, 18 million developers, and 3 million models spanning languages, physics, chemistry, biology, and robotics.

## Leadership & Integration
* **Team Retention:** — **Clément Delangue** confirmed that all three founders and the entire Hugging Face team are joining Nvidia, having already held an all-company team meeting with Jensen Huang.
* **Operational Independence:** Hugging Face will continue to run as an independently neutral platform within the Nvidia organization.
