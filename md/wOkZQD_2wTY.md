## San Francisco’s Resurgence and Corporate Philanthropy
* **The City's Historical Cycles:** 
  * Benioff emphasized that the current AI wave is just the latest and potentially biggest in a long history of San Francisco gold rushes, including the Summer of Love, Haight-Ashbury, Levi's, and The Gap.
* **The 1% Philanthropy Model:** 
  * Salesforce pioneered the 1-1-1 model (giving 1% of equity, profit, and time), resulting in 11 million volunteer hours, $1 billion in grants, and supporting 65,000 nonprofits/NGOs for free.
  * Salesforce contributes $100,000 annually to every middle school principal in San Francisco and Oakland.
  * Over 20,000 companies have joined the model via pledge1%.org.

## SaaS Apocalypse & Multi-Dimensional Pricing
* **Refuting the SaaS Apocalypse:** 
  * Benioff dismissed the "SaaS apocalypse" narrative, pointing to Salesforce's strong Q2 results, high contract lengths, low attrition, and guidance ahead of analyst expectations through 2030.
  * Benioff personally purchased $25 billion of Salesforce stock in the public market during the market dip.
* **Six-Dimensional AI Pricing:** 
  * Salesforce utilizes six pricing mechanisms to handle $50+ billion in projected revenue:
    1. **Named Users:** Traditional per-user/month fees.
    2. **Named Agents:** Paying per autonomous agent.
    3. **Usage:** Volume of product used.
    4. **Consumption:** Token or storage-based consumption.
    5. **Transaction Outcomes:** Per-completed transaction.
    6. **Business Outcomes:** Percentage of money saved or generated.

## Slack as the AI Operating System
* **Long-Term Vision for Slack:** 
  * Salesforce acquired Slack for roughly $25 billion ($900M in revenue at ~25x multiple)—a deal initially criticized—because leadership envisioned it as the business agent interface 6 years prior, guided by Chief Futurist Peter Schwarz.
* **Enterprise Momentum & Integrations:** 
  * Slack is now running core operations for top AI companies (OpenAI, Anthropic, Nvidia) as well as major enterprises like Meta, Nike, and General Motors (transitioning from Teams).
  * New features include Slack Code (coding in channels), Slack CRM (operating Salesforce inside Slack), Slackforce, and zero data retention models powered by Claude.
  * Salesforce also recently acquired customer agent platform **Finn** by Owen to accelerate customer agent success.

## The Future of TIME Magazine and AI Interfaces
* **AI Readers for TIME:** 
  * TIME magazine is experiencing an ascendant phase; notably, more AI agents now read TIME daily than humans, pulling real-time information to answer queries. TIME has restructured its content and advertising to be agent-friendly.
* **The Next Big Shift—Dynamic Interfaces:** 
  * Benioff predicts enterprise and consumer interfaces will undergo a radical transformation. Moving beyond static icons and email lists, future interfaces will be intelligent, conversational, adaptive, and dynamic.
