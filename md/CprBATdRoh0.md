## 1. AI-Driven Software Development and Autonomous Agents

AWS is shifting the paradigm of software creation and operations from basic code completion to multi-step, autonomous agent execution.

* **KIRO and Spec-Driven Development (SDD):** KIRO introduces a plan-ahead approach combining design, requirements, and execution before writing code.
    * **Dhan (Stockbroking):** Developed a trading interface using KIRO with just 1 developer in 2 weeks—a task that typically requires 3 to 4 full-time employees over 6 to 9 months—resulting in a 20x faster time-to-market and 95% lower costs.
    * **Wipro’s Implementation:** Wipro utilized KIRO to align employee cost structures with new Indian labor codes in less than a week, and completely reimagined and launched its global talent management platform for 230,000 employees in under two months.
* **Frontier Agents for Software Lifecycle Management:** A new class of autonomous, long-running, and massively scalable agents designed to manage end-to-end operational tasks:
    * **KIRO Autonomous Agent:** Continuously runs alongside workflows to tackle independent, complex tasks (e.g., upgrading libraries across dozens of microservices and creating merge-ready pull requests).
    * **AWS Security Agent:** Automatically reviews design documents, scans code for vulnerabilities, and conducts automated penetration testing before deployment.
    * **AWS DevOps Agent:** Investigates system alarms independently, diagnoses root causes (such as CDK errors), integrates with observability platforms like Dynatrace, and prepares proposed fixes before the on-call engineer even logs on.

## 2. Modernizing Legacy Systems and Enterprise Workflows

AWS is addressing the high cost of legacy code maintenance, which typically consumes over 70% of IT budgets, by introducing automated migration tools.

* **AWS Transform & Transform Custom:** Built to modernize legacy platforms like VMware, mainframe, and Windows. It has already saved 1.35 million hours of manual effort and modernized over 1 billion lines of mainframe code.
    * **Custom Code Migration:** "Transform Custom" allows organizations to build agents that convert unique APIs, frameworks, and programming languages (e.g., Python, Node.js, C) into modern languages like Rust while continuously learning from developer feedback.
    * **Air Canada:** Modernized thousands of Node.js 16 Lambda functions to newer versions in just a few days, saving 80% in time and cost.
* **Amazon Quick:** A global workforce assistant that unifies structured and unstructured enterprise data to generate business intelligence and create personal productivity agents.
    * **3M Case Study:** Sales managers used Amazon Quick to analyze over 100,000 sales representatives' accounts and generate priority deal summaries, reducing preparation time from 5 hours to minutes.

## 3. Powering Digital India and Citizen-Scale Public Infrastructure

AWS infrastructure serves as the backbone for India's Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI), executing massive public welfare, healthcare, and educational workloads.

* **Scale of India's DPI:** 
    * **UPI:** Supports nearly 21 billion monthly transactions, with AWS customer Juspay managing 9 billion of them.
    * **DigiLocker:** Services over 625 million beneficiaries and has generated 9.2 billion digital documents on AWS.
* **Public Welfare Impact:**
    * **Poshan Tracker:** Leveraged by 1.4 million Anganwadi centers to monitor undernutrition and distribute food to 90 million beneficiaries (including 76 million children under six) using Amazon Rekognition.
    * **Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY:** Automates 15,000 healthcare claims daily for 550 million beneficiaries, reducing claim processing times from 20 days to hours.
* **Educational Democratization:**
    * **Physics Wallah:** Built "Aryabhatta," India’s first education LLM (20 billion parameters), helping 4.6 million students solve 100 million questions in regional languages at 10x faster speeds and 5x lower costs.
* **AWS Financial & Infrastructure Commitment:** AWS will invest $35 billion in India between now and 2030 (adding to $40 billion already invested), supporting fully featured three-zone regions in Mumbai and Hyderabad.

## 4. Next-Generation Infrastructure and Agentic Commerce

To run advanced AI models at scale, AWS has optimized its compute, storage, and data architectures to work in tandem.

* **Custom Silicon & Serverless Innovations:**
    * **Graviton5:** AWS’s next-generation general-purpose processor offers 25% better performance than previous versions at the same energy efficiency.
    * **Lambda Durable Functions:** Eliminates "undifferentiated heavy lifting" by providing built-in state management and error handling, allowing serverless functions to pause and resume over hours or days while waiting for background agents.
* **Unified Data on Amazon S3:** S3 supports open data formats like Apache Iceberg to prevent data silos.
    * **Cox Automotive:** Unified 8 petabytes of data across 5.1 trillion vehicle insights, deploying 18 AI solutions that cut fleet repair estimation times from 48 hours to 30 minutes.
* **Swiggy's Transition to "Agentic Commerce":** Food and convenience platform Swiggy (serving 25 million monthly users) is re-architecting its service from "AI-assisted" to "AI-native."
    * **Sia & Crew Agents:** Swiggy showcased a prototype assistant ("Sia") and concierge service ("Crew") that execute purchases and plan travel using natural language intent (declarative) instead of multi-step application clicking (imperative).
    * **Open Source Initiatives:** Swiggy has open-sourced "Real-World Skills" to bridge the gap between user context and agent execution, and launched the "Builders Club" to co-develop AI-commerce apps with external developers.
* **Amazon Bedrock & AgentCore:** Bedrock has doubled its model selection (including Gemma, GPT-OSS, and Nemotron). The newly launched **Bedrock AgentCore** provides developers with a modular, managed runtime with session memory and unified security layers to transition agents from prototype to production.
