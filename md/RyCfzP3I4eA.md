**TL;DR:** Senior Trump administration officials—led by Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, FBI Director Kash Patel, CMS Administrator Mehmet Oz, FTC Chairman Andrew Ferguson, and Assistant Attorney General Colin McDonald—held a press conference in St. Cloud, Minnesota, to announce a major whole-of-government crackdown on fraud, highlighting hundreds of billions recovered nationally and a wave of new indictments, convictions, and multi-agency task force initiatives targeting public benefit programs in Minnesota.

## National Fraud Metrics and Whole-of-Government Enforcement
* **Administration-Wide Tally (Scott Bessent):** 
  * Uncovered more than **$260 billion** in fraud since January 2025.
  * Preventing **$72 billion** annually in taxpayer funds from going to illicit actors.
  * Brought enforcement actions against nearly **$60 billion**.
* **U.S. Treasury & IRS Actions (Scott Bessent):**
  * Returned **$175 million** in payments intended for deceased individuals over the past year.
  * Expanded "Do Not Pay" access from **4% to 99%** of federal programs.
  * Stopped almost **$190 million** and returned over **$100 million** to U.S. victims of overseas fraud.
  * Ordered an audit of approximately **$9 billion** in Treasury contracts.
  * Partnered with IRS-CI on proposed stronger eligibility checks for health insurance tax credits.
* **FinCEN Targeting and Whistleblower Portal:**
  * Issued geographic targeting orders and alerts regarding cross-border transactions by money service businesses (MSBs), identifying over **$8 billion** in potentially suspicious foreign agent-directed payments between 2021 and 2025.
  * Launched a centralized whistleblower portal at **america.gov/fraud** backed by a **$25 million** fraud whistleblower incentive program offering **$30,000** rewards for information leading to convictions or enforcement actions.
* **FBI "Most Wanted Fraudsters" Initiative (Kash Patel):**
  * Captured four of the most wanted fraudsters on four separate continents within four months of launch; suspects had been on the run for nearly **5,000 days** and had defrauded Americans of **$2 billion**.

## Minnesota-Specific Takedowns and Criminal Prosecutions
* **Recent Sweeps and Indictments (Kash Patel & Colin McDonald):**
  * FBI Minneapolis and partners executed **17 search warrants in a single day**, resulting in a **26-count indictment** covering three child care centers, two owner residences, and an early intensive development/behavioral intervention unit.
  * Four alleged fraudsters arrested and charged with health care fraud and conspiracy:
    * **Faysal Ali** (Aspen Associates): 11 counts of health care fraud, **$8 million** in intended loss.
    * **Jamma Mohamed** (American Home Health Care): 6 counts of health care fraud, **$3.27 million** in intended loss.
    * **Faisa Ahmed** (Home Sweet Home): 8 counts of health care fraud, **$10 million** in intended fraud.
    * **Habiba Mohamed** (Smart Therapy): 1 count of conspiracy to defraud the U.S., nearly **$20 million** in intended fraud and loss.
* **Medicaid Housing Stabilization Program Case (Mehmet Oz & Colin McDonald):**
  * Highlighted convicted fraudster **Charmaine Meadows**, who stole over **$3.5 million** from the now-defunct program.
  * The program ballooned from **$2.5 million** in 2020 to **$107 million** in 2024 before Minnesota was forced to shut it down entirely due to rampant fraud.
* **Sentencing and Asset Recovery (Colin McDonald):**
  * **Salim Said** sentenced to **30 years** in prison for the "Feeding Our Future" scheme, responsible for more than **$44 million** in fraud proceeds.
  * **Said Farah** sentenced to **78 months** in prison for bribing a juror in a prior trial with **$2,000** in cash delivered to their home.
  * Over the past **115 days**, **14 fraud defendants** in Minnesota have been sentenced to a combined **988 months (over 82 years)** in prison.
  * Filed a civil forfeiture complaint for three Minneapolis properties valued at approximately **$519,000** purchased with fraud proceeds intended to feed children.
  * Under the "Defeating Our Futures" program, **81 subjects** have been charged and **70 convicted** with a fraud value exceeding **$1/2 billion**.
  * FinCEN data supported law enforcement efforts leading to the arrest and charging of **115 fraudsters**, **75 convictions**, and nearly **3,000 subpoenas** across Minnesota.

## CMS Provider Revalidations and Interagency Integration
* **Provider Revalidation Findings (Mehmet Oz):**
  * CMS required Minnesota to revalidate high-risk providers across **14 categories**.
  * Nearly **one in three (approx. 30%)** of all providers in Minnesota could not be revalidated due to being unlicensed, unmonitored, or ineligible.
  * In New York's social adult daycare program, **50%** of providers failed revalidation.
* **Interagency Structural Collaboration (Mehmet Oz):**
  * CMS signed a memorandum of understanding (MOU) with the Department of Treasury earlier this month to track fund flows, cross-reference enrollment records, banking details, OIG tips, and fraud prevention systems.
  * The IRS Criminal Investigation (IRS-CI) arm—housing **2,900 gun-carrying accountants**—is actively collaborating to target tax evasion tied to stolen public funds.
  * Connected cross-border and multi-state networks, including shutting down a web of California hospices funneling stolen funds into a single bank account network.

## Broader Policy and Systemic Arguments
* **Systemic Social Trust (Andrew Ferguson):** 
  * Argued that unchecked fraud severely damages public trust in benefit systems, government institutions, and the social contract.
  * Pointed to secondary harms of fraud, including terminated housing programs for homeless veterans, unnecessary medical surgeries on elderly Americans, and public safety threats from fraudulent commercial truck driver licenses issued to unauthorized individuals.
* **Targeting Kingpins over Street Operators (Mehmet Oz & Scott Bessent):**
  * The administration's current phase focuses on moving upstream from low-level "street operators" and "capos" to dismantle entire multinational criminal organizations and international financial loops (including terror finance connections to al-Shabab).
