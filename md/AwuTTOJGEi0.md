## TL;DR
OpenAI President and co-founder Greg Brockman discusses the rollout of GPT-6 to over a billion people, the resolution of nearly 400 century-old math problems by AI agents, and how proactive AI tools like "Dots" are transforming productivity and the future of work.

## Scientific Discovery & Mathematical Breakthroughs
- **AI-Generated New Knowledge:** Recent milestones in mathematics demonstrate that AI can definitively generate new knowledge rather than just retrieve it.
  - OpenAI recently published resolved results for nearly 400 significant, open mathematical problems.
  - Domain experts reviewing the proofs note surprising and sophisticated applications of ideas and inter-domain connections rather than brute-force mechanical case-checking (unlike historical proofs like the Four Color Theorem).
- **Application to Biology and Drug Discovery:** Scientific breakthroughs in mathematics and theoretical physics serve as a leading indicator for what is possible in biology, where deep learning physics (compute + data + algorithms) can accelerate discovery.
  - Drug discovery is highlighted as the most critical and impactful application of AI to improve human quality of life.
  - Neural networks are exceptionally well-suited to crack the "language of biology," enabling massive parallelism and dramatic shortening of a traditionally 15-year, $1.5 billion pipeline.
  - Non-biological bottlenecks in the drug pipeline—such as writing regulatory filings and clinical trial documentation—can also be streamlined by AI.

## AI Agents, Form Factors, and the "No UI" Future
- **Proactive Productivity with Dots:** OpenAI's "Dots" is an always-on, always-working proactive agent designed to track uncompleted tasks, monitor Slack and inbox feeds, manage scheduling conflicts, and anticipate user needs.
  - The form factor changes how users work by running continuously in the background even when the user stops working.
- **Consumer vs. Enterprise Market Pressures:** While consumer assistants focus on mass-market scale, free-tier availability (such as ChatGPT's memory features for over 1.2 billion consumers), and features like payments and travel, workplace AI prioritizes peak intelligence, admin controls, security toggles, and paying higher compute costs for higher productivity returns.
- **Dynamic and Invisible User Interfaces:** The ultimate UI for AI is no UI at all. 
  - GPT-6 introduces intelligent, generative UI where the AI crafts custom, bespoke visual interfaces on the fly (such as flight booking options or assembly guides) tailored precisely to the moment and user request.

## Cybersecurity and the Defender's Window
- **The Physics of Cybersecurity:** Cybersecurity dynamics are shifting into a critical "defender's window" where peak frontier AI capabilities allow defenders to proactively find and fix deep-seated historical flaws in human-written code.
- **OpenAI Internal Security Response:** During model releases, OpenAI pauses approximately 25% of its production engineers from feature work, assigning them exclusively to use peak frontier models to red-team and secure infrastructure until vulnerabilities are saturated.
- **Tech Debt and Code Resilience:** Decades of internet infrastructure built for resilience rather than security have left immense technical debt (highlighted by single-maintainer open source vulnerabilities). AI enables formal verification of software and the generation of secure-by-default code from inception.

## Human Agency and the Future of AGI
- **Keeping Humans in the Driver's Seat:** OpenAI's core mission remains ensuring AGI benefits all of humanity by keeping humans in control and amplifying human agency.
  - Models are jagged and surpass humans in specific domains, but human judgment, values, and oversight function similarly to a CEO or board member directing specialized teams of domain experts.
- **Alignment via Debate:** Techniques like debate and iterative amplification (conceptually discussed since 2017) train AIs to provide verifiable arguments and counter-arguments, ensuring humans can easily oversee complex outputs without needing to manually verify every detail.

## Predictions & Forward Calls
- **Cyber vs. Software Spend:** Security spend should vastly outstrip software spend as software becomes cheap and commoditized, making security, reliability, and resilience the true drivers of value — Greg Brockman
