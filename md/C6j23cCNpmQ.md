## The Blockade: Logistics and Strategic Risks
*   **Enforcement Challenges:** Trump’s proposal to stop any vessel paying Iran is complicated by the use of non-traceable currencies.

    *   **Geopolitical Escalation:** Targeting vessels bound for China risks a direct confrontation with the Chinese Navy, shifting a regional dispute into a global military flashpoint.
*   **International Isolation:** Key allies are already distancing themselves from the policy.
    *   **Refusal to Participate:** Both Britain and Australia have indicated they will not participate in the blockade, viewing it as "economic suicide."
*   **Ineffective Alternatives:** Existing bypasses, such as Saudi Arabia’s Yanbu pipeline, are insufficient.
    *   **Capacity Gaps:** While the Gulf ships 20 million barrels per day (bpd), alternative routes cannot absorb the 13–15 million bpd that would be stranded if the Strait is closed.
    *   **Houthi Control:** Other strategic chokepoints, like those near Yemen, are controlled by hostile actors, leaving no safe alternative.

*   **Beyond Crude Oil:** The disruption extends to critical non-oil goods, including:

    *   **Aviation:** European airports are facing jet fuel depletion within 2–3 weeks.
    *   **Industrial Materials:** Significant flows of aluminum and LNG are at risk.
