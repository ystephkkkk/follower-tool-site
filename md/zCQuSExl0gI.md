## Business Model & Commercial Applications
* **Civilization Tech & Secondary Markets:** Beyond de-extinction, the company develops fundamental biotechnology platforms with massive commercial applications:
  * **Invasive Species & Biocontrol:** Utilizing gene drives to combat multi-billion-dollar problems like the screwworm in Texas (which threatens beef prices and cattle) and tick-borne Lyme disease, potentially eradicating specific populations in **6 to 7 months** compared to traditional multi-year tax-funded programs (Ben Lamm).
  * **Human Healthcare & Drug Discovery:** Astromech applies AI to evolutionary trends (such as cancer-suppressing p-53 gene expressions in elephants) to build predictive drug and therapeutic models for human health, bird flu, and disease resistance (Ben Lamm).

* **Conservation Strategy & Open Source:** Open-sourcing all proprietary technologies for free to the global conservation community, resulting in a measurable increase in funding and awareness for partner organizations (Ben Lamm).
