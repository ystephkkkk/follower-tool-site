## The AI Infrastructure Boom and Capital Allocation
* **Investment vs. Mortgage Rates:** Higher interest rates serve as the market mechanism to reallocate capital from housing and apartment construction toward building data centers, power, and water infrastructure for the AI buildout.
* **Efficiency and Resource Intensity:** Kashkari highlighted the massive resource intensity of AI (noting that creating a 6-second video using AI takes as much electricity as running a clothes dryer for an hour) and suggested that higher capital costs will force the industry to become much more efficient.
* **Productivity Paradox and Risks:** Kashkari is skeptical of Silicon Valley's hyper-optimistic growth claims (such as 10% real GDP growth), estimating that a realistic good scenario is raising productivity from 1.5% to 2%; a primary risk is that if the massive AI investment does not deliver expected productivity gains, it will result in "malinvestment" and an economic hangover.
