## TL;DR
Sequence Holdings Co-Founder and CEO Michael Lee joins Sarah Guo to discuss Sequence's $7.7 billion take-private transaction of Baldwin with the Dell Family Office, and explains why permanent holding company structures and frontier engineering can transform market incumbents from the inside out.

## Company Overview & Structure
- **Sequence Holdings:** Founded 20 months ago by Michael Lee and co-founder Alex, operating as a permanent holding company partnering with management teams to buy and refound businesses into market leaders using AI.
- **The Permanent Holding Company Model:** 
  - Aligns the duration of capital with sustained operational commitments and end-to-end transformation.
  - Culturally oriented around forging market leaders rather than deploying capital on short fund timelines.
  - Leverages retained earnings to support existing portfolio companies and net-new investments.
- **Why Ownership Beats Services and Software:**
  - **In-house hiring limit:** Traditional Fortune 500 companies cannot easily recruit and retain top-tier engineering talent because their celebrated persona is typically the investor, not the engineer.
  - **Services misalignment:** Consulting and service providers (e.g., Accenture, McKinsey) optimize for getting into and growing a client's wallet, leading to incrementalism rather than structural change.
  - **Software limitations:** Enterprise software is built on historical homogeneous workflows designed around existing human assembly lines, missing new paradigms.
- **Scarcity & Focus:** Sequence aims to do only **one deal per year**, avoiding traditional fund deployment pressures and focusing intensely on business quality and team.

## Baldwin Transaction ($7.7B Take-Private)
- **The Deal:** Announced the largest AI take-private transaction to date: Baldwin for **$7.7 billion**, executed in partnership with the Dell Family Office (DFO).
- **Partnership & Control:** Sequence and DFO are co-controlling Baldwin together, with DFO providing the crucial certainty of capital required for the multi-billion-dollar commitment letter.
- **Why Baldwin (Insurance Brokerage):**
  - Represents a **$2 trillion+** annual gross written premium market with extreme defensiveness and 90% gross retention rates.
  - Features carriers that generate most of their profit from investing rather than underwriting, heavily incentivizing asset gathering and broker distribution.
  - Characterized by centralized operations and a management team (led by CEO Trevor Baldwin) that was already early in adopting technologies like Anthropic end-to-end and running on a single instance of Applied Epic (core AMS).

## BankSouth Pilot & Real-World Results
- **The Genesis:** Started as a customer engagement in August 2024 through Jamie Reynolds (co-founder of Aanir) whose family owns BankSouth in Georgia. Sequence operated in a services motion from August to November before investing for a minority stake, closing the investment in March after Fed and OCC approval.
- **Regulated Banking as a Feature:** Regulatory oversight provided well-defined operations, excellent data hygiene, and centralized underwriting physics that scale easily across branches.
- **Concrete Results (Post-March Investment):**
  - **Consumer Underwriting Speed:** Average consumer loan underwriting time decreased by **94%** since March.
  - **Commercial Underwriting Speed:** Average commercial loan lifecycle dropped from **30 days to 11 days**.
  - **Volume Capacity:** In Q2 relative to Q1, loan volumes **doubled** at the bank, handled with a significantly smaller underwriting team while maintaining exact underwriting standards (headcount shifted to higher-value roles: one retirement, one moved to front office).

## Platform Technology & Engineering Playbook
- **Atlas Platform:** Sequence's shared platform built at BankSouth and designed to generalize across industries, consisting of four layers:
  - **Data Ontology:** Defines the business and its motion in code to make data legible to models.
  - **Agent Builder:** Builds high-performance agents grounded in ground truth.
  - **Lattice:** The orchestration engine that instruments workflows using built agents.
  - **Artifacts:** The application builder sitting on top of the stack.
- **Engineering Philosophy:** About 80% of a business's atomic units are homogeneous and 20% vertically specific. Unlike application software, Sequence celebrates model performance improvements as an expansion of their toolkit.
