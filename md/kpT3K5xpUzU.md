## TL;DR
Driven by Q3 2026 total revenue and reserve income of $865 million—up 17% year-over-year—Circle highlighted the upcoming January implementation of the Genius Act, the mainstream scaling of the Arc blockchain network, and over 150% sequential growth in annualized payment volume on the Circle Payments Network (CPN).

## Q3 Financial Results & Metrics
* **Top-Line Performance:** Total revenue and reserve income reached **$865 million**, up 17% year-over-year and 23% sequentially. This included **$182 million** from the Arc token pre-sale.
* **Profitability & Margins:** Adjusted EBITDA came in at **$317 million**, up 85% year-over-year. Revenue less distribution cost (RLDC) margin was **52.4%** (39.7% excluding the Arc token pre-sale). Net reserve margin stood at **37.8%**, down ~80 bps from Q2 following the hyperliquid arrangement.
* **Operating Expenses:** Adjusted operating expenses were **$139 million**, up 13% year-over-year (driven by Arc, AI investments, and Chelsea FC marketing) but down 5% sequentially.
* **USDC Circulation & Usage:** Average USDC circulation was **$73.3 billion** (up 8% year-over-year; exiting the quarter at **$73.9 billion**). Onchain transaction volume grew **49%** year-over-year to **$13.9 trillion**. USDC carried **79%** of all stablecoin transaction volume globally on roughly 27% of supply.
* **Liquidity & Primary Market:** A record **$28 billion** of USDC was minted and redeemed in Q3 (up 42% year-over-year), averaging ~$2.3 billion per day.
* **Other Digital Assets:** EURC grew **92%** year-over-year to **$413 million**, and USYC grew **230%** to **$2.4 billion** in assets.

## Platform Ecosystem: CPN & Acquisitions
* **CPN Scaling:** Annualized payment volume on CPN reached **$41 billion** at the end of Q3 (up 15x year-over-year). By October 30, it surpassed **$58 billion** (>150% sequential growth). Enrolled financial institutions grew 4x year-over-year to **205**.
* **Enterprise & Tech Integrations:** CPN treasury tools integrated with Kyrie and Tina for SAP ERP customers, launched on Arc mainet as the preferred settlement layer, and expanded managed payments to support AI agents.
* **Tazipe Acquisition:** Circle announced an agreement to acquire Tazipay for **$400 million** in Class A common stock (expected to close in 2027). Tazipay processes **>$25 billion** in annualized payment volume (~60% stablecoin-involved) across 100+ payout markets and 60+ bank/fintech partners.

## Arc Network Launch & Ecosystem
* **Mainnet Go-Live:** Arc mainet launched on September 16 with **11 founding validators** (including BNY, HSBC, Société Générale, State Street, JCB, Mastercard, Moneygram, and Visa) and **>100 applications**.
* **Network Activity:** As of late October, Arc held **~$1.5 billion** in USDC, surBTC, and EURC. Over **$30 billion** of USDC has moved on Arc since launch (~$1 billion/day recently), processing **~500,000 transactions per day** with sub-second finality and fees under one cent paid in USDC.
* **Token Value Accrual (Jeremy Fox-Geen):** Circle captures Arc value through staking rewards, selective token sales, and ecosystem incentives. Additionally, Circle's 25% allocation of Arc token holdings is unreflected on GAAP statements until sold or distributed.

## Strategic Partnerships & Global Expansion
* **Binance Agreement:** Signed a new 5-year partnership alongside a **$100 million equity investment** from Binance in Circle, placing USDC at the center of a platform with 300+ million registered users.
* **Chelsea FC Partnership:** Circle became a principal partner of Chelsea FC, featuring USDC on the front of shirts for the 2026-27 season to reach 650 million fans and 1.2 billion homes globally.
* **Agentic Economy:** Over 99% of agent payments on the X42 protocol settle in USDC, and nearly 2,800 endpoints are listed in Circle's agent marketplace.
