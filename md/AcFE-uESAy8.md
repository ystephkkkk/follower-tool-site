**TL;DR**
Amazon CEO Andy Jassy defended the company's $220 billion CapEx plan by highlighting that AWS's generative AI revenue has surpassed $25 billion in its first three years—growing 440 times faster than early AWS—and argued that the U.S. cannot afford to slow down data center construction and cede AI leadership to China.

## AWS AI Growth, CapEx, and Demand Signals
* **Unprecedented AI Revenue Velocity:** In response to CNBC's Kate Rooney on Wall Street's reaction to Amazon's $220 billion CapEx plan, Jassy noted that AWS's generative AI business has reached over $25 billion in annual revenue within its first three years, compared to a $57 million annual revenue run rate in AWS's first three years (~440x faster).
* **Long-Term Capacity Reservations & Backlog:**
  * Big chunks of AWS capacity are already largely reserved for 2027, much of it for 2028, and a significant portion for 2029.
  * Amazon's backlog stands at ~$580 billion, with no single company representing even 25% of it.
* **Enterprise Adoption & Barbell Demand:**
  * Over 80% of the Fortune 100 are using Bedrock, Amazon's inference service.
  * Jassy described current demand as "barbelled": AI labs consuming massive compute (e.g., ChatGPT, coding) on one end, and enterprises using AI for cost avoidance and productivity (customer service, business process automation, fraud) on the other, with core enterprise production applications in the middle still ramping up.
* **Capital Cycle & Free Cash Flow:**
  * Data centers require capital outlays ~2 years ahead of monetization, whereas servers and networking gear (the largest capital expense) are purchased just a few months ahead, giving Amazon real visibility into demand before pulling the trigger.
  * While heavy CapEx creates short-term free cash flow headwinds, Jassy expects strong long-term return on invested capital.

## Data Center Buildout, Community Impact, and US-China Competition
* **Historic Infrastructure Scale:** Jassy compared the current AI infrastructure buildout to the 1950s U.S. highway and bridge construction, noting that the country and the world had underinvested in upgrading the power grid.
* **Geopolitical Imperative:** Addressing state-level moratoriums and public backlash, Jassy warned that stopping U.S. data center construction and allowing China to establish global AI norms would be a "historic mistake" that the U.S. would regret for a long time.
* **Community Partnerships & Tax Contributions:**
  * In St. Joseph County, Indiana, Amazon will contribute over $3 billion in taxes over the next few years, compared to ~$1 million from previous landowners.
  * Through its Build Together program, Amazon has contributed another $1 billion to local neighborhood priorities.
* **Hyperscaler Capacity:** Amazon added significant capacity in 2026 and has substantially larger capacity in progress for 2027 and 2028.

## Custom Silicon, Nvidia, and AI Model Partnerships
* **Custom Silicon Scale:** Amazon's chips business—comprising Trainium (AI accelerator) and Graviton (CPU)—has surpassed a $25 billion annual revenue run rate (as of Q2). The majority of Bedrock inference runs on Trainium chips.
* **Nvidia & "Coopetition":** Jassy downplayed any impact on Amazon from recent Nvidia news, calling Nvidia and Jensen Huang a long-term, important partner alongside Amazon's own custom silicon. On export controls, Jassy stated Amazon will abide by all U.S. laws.
* **Multi-Model Strategy (Anthropic & OpenAI):**
  * Anthropic, which is set to price and list on the Nasdaq, trains models and runs inference on Trainium. Jassy dismissed "circular financing" criticisms, noting that interconnected customer-vendor relationships (like Amazon using Adobe, which runs on AWS) are standard.
  * Driven by customer demand for choice, Amazon also partnered with OpenAI to offer its models on AWS.

## Agentic Shopping and Consumer/Enterprise Health
* **Third-Party Shopping Agents:** Jassy explained that Amazon has not permanently closed amazon.com to third-party agents (like Instinct or Dots), but requires a good customer experience and a fair exchange of value.
* **Alexa for Shopping:** Over 400 million people have used Alexa for shopping in the last 12 months, driving significant downstream revenue. Jassy believes most agentic shopping will happen natively within the retailer's own app, though Amazon is in talks with horizontal agent companies.
* **Resilient Consumer & Enterprise Spending:**
  * Consumer spending has been consistent over the last 18 months, with shoppers remaining price-conscious and deal-seeking ahead of events like Amazon's "Turkey 12."
  * On the enterprise side, Jassy has seen "no attenuation" in demand, with companies resuming cloud migrations over the past 18 months.

## Predictions & Forward Calls
* **AWS $1 Trillion Run Rate:** AWS can become an over $1 trillion annual revenue run rate business within the next 5 to 10 years (Andy Jassy).
* **Frontier Model Landscape:** In 3 to 5 years, there will be 6 to 8 frontier models that are roughly comparable in quality—each excelling at different tasks like coding, math, reasoning, visual arts, or lower cost/latency—and one of them will be Amazon's own model (Andy Jassy).
* **Cloud Migration Flip:** Today, 80% to 85% of global IT spend is still on-premises; that equation will flip to majority cloud over the next 10 to 20 years (Andy Jassy).
