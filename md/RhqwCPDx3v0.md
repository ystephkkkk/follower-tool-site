## TL;DR
JPMorgan Chase, Ford, and the State of Michigan have launched **Michigan LIFT** (Launchpad for Industrial Innovation and Transformation) to onshore advanced manufacturing supply chains, secure critical components, and boost domestic industrial independence.

## Michigan LIFT Initiative & Supply Chain Onshoring
* **Program Purpose:** **Jim Farley** explained that Michigan LIFT is designed to fix supply chain vulnerabilities by onshoring capabilities—such as advanced manufacturing, new technology, magnets, and rare earth elements—that were previously offshored for lower costs.
* **Ford's Commitment:** **Jim Farley** stated that Ford is committing $1 billion in purchasing power for new Michigan-based companies building advanced manufacturing capabilities.
* **Role of Startups & Small Businesses:** **Jamie Dimon** emphasized that innovative startups need actual orders and production-scale plants (not just experimental ones) to succeed, making initiatives like LIFT essential for bridging that gap.
* **Cross-Border Partnerships:** **Governor Gretchen Whitmer** acknowledged Canada as a vital ally and noted that Canadian companies in the industrial and aluminum supply chains can apply to the program, highlighting that all Ford truck aluminum ingots cross the Detroit border.

## Skilled Trades & Workforce Development
* **The Trades Deficit:** **Jim Farley** characterized the U.S. skilled trades deficit as a "crisis" with 1.7 million open skilled trade jobs, noting we are currently in the "second inning" of addressing it. 
* **Educational Bias:** **Jim Farley** pointed out that high school advisors recommend 4-year degrees while only 2% recommend trade schools, despite high-quality trade schools boasting nearly 100% graduation rates.
* **State Workforce Rankings:** **Governor Gretchen Whitmer** highlighted that Michigan is ranked #4 in the country for workforce efforts by *Site Selector Magazine* and noted the state has the highest concentration of engineers per capita in the country.
* **Debt-Free Training:** **Governor Gretchen Whitmer** detailed Michigan's investments in free community college and accessible apprenticeships that allow workers to earn a good living without carrying student debt—jobs she noted will not be replaced by AI.

## National Economic Resilience & Capital Markets
* **Connection to Military Power:** **Jamie Dimon** argued that a powerful military is predicated on having a pre-eminent U.S. economy, which anchors the dollar as the global reserve currency.
* **JP Morgan's Broader Initiative:** **Jamie Dimon** reported that the one-year anniversary of JPMorgan's security and resiliency initiative is approaching, noting the firm has invested $5 billion directly into non-venture, pre-public companies focused on security and resiliency.
* **Advantage of U.S. Capital Markets:** **Jamie Dimon** contrasted vibrant U.S. venture capital, private equity, debt markets, and risk-taking capital with a stagnated Europe, while identifying government borrowing—not AI—as the primary source of capital crowding out.
* **Global Competition:** **Jim Farley** discussed the intense speed of global competition, specifically noting that Ford developed a small group in the U.S. and California to out-innovate competitors and is rolling out a $30,000 vehicle engineered in California and Michigan and built in Louisville, Kentucky.

## Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Jamie Dimon** predicted that JPMorgan may exceed its initial $1.5 trillion goal for its security and resiliency initiative by its one-year anniversary.
* **Jim Farley** claimed that Ford's upcoming $30,000 vehicle—engineered in California and Michigan and built in Louisville, Kentucky—will be fully competitive with Chinese EV giant BYD.
