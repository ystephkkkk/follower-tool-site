## TL;DR
President Trump announced a historic $18 billion investment by Mesabi Metallics (SR Group) to build the largest steel plant in U.S. history in southeastern Iowa, backed by a related $2.5 billion iron ore mine in Minnesota. The project aims to create thousands of jobs and establish a fully integrated domestic supply chain.

## Steel Plant & Mining Investment
* **Iowa Steel Complex:** Mesabi Metallics will build an integrated steel plant in southeastern Iowa, representing an estimated $18 billion investment.
* **Production Scale & Timing:** The facility will produce 10 million tons of steel per year using advanced technology, competing globally on size, quality, and cost. Construction is slated to start immediately.
* **Minnesota Iron Ore Mine:** Backed by the Export-Import Bank, a $2.5 billion project opened the first new iron ore mine in the U.S. in over 50 years on the Minnesota iron range, producing high-grade "Patriot pellets" to supply the Iowa mill.
* **Supply Chain Integration:** The project creates a fully domestic supply chain: ore mined in Minnesota, transported on American railroads, and refined into steel in Iowa using American energy.
