## TL;DR
Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg and executives unveiled Meta's expanding personal AI and wearables ecosystem at Meta Connect 2026, headlined by the personal AI agent Muse, new Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3 styles, audio glasses, FDA-cleared hearing aids, and a breakthrough form factor of VR glasses.

## Introduction and Vision for Personal Superintelligence
* **Optimistic AI Philosophy:** Mark Zuckerberg emphasized that Meta's core mission is to empower people, believing that the highest purpose of AI is creation and invention rather than automation, and that technology should be distributed widely.
* **Muse Personal Agent:** Launched a few weeks prior, Muse is designed as a personal agent providing its own secure virtual machine (**Muse Secure VM**) and specialized AI model (**Muse Spark**). 
* **Business Model:** Muse is offered free for a huge number of tokens, with Meta planning to profit over time by taking a small fee from transactions.

## Muse Ecosystem, Connectors, and Developer Platform
* **Alexander Wang on Growth:** Alexander Wang highlighted the explosive early growth of Muse, describing it as a "banger" with real-world savings and utility.
* **New Mac App & Computer Use:** The Muse Mac app launched with native dictation, and new computer use capabilities allow Muse to drive any app on a Mac.
* **Deep Retail & Service Integrations:** Meta announced major retail partnerships including Walmart, Best Buy, Dick's, Gap, Fanatics, Michael Kors, Sephora, Ulta, and Wayfair, alongside travel (Expedia), grocery (Instacart), and productivity tools (Box, GitHub, Granolah, Notion).
* **Payment Integrations:** Added support for Stripe, Shop Pay, and PayPal.
* **Open Connector Platform:** Opened up to developers, receiving over 1,500 applications in less than a week.

## Wearables, AI Glasses, and Hearing Enhancement
* **Shift to AI Glasses:** Zuckerberg explained that Meta has shifted its primary focus from the metaverse to building the best devices for personal superintelligence, positioning glasses as the ultimate AI form factor.
* **Real-Time Voice and Customization:** Meta introduced a state-of-the-art real-time voice model enabling custom voices, joyful animations, and background action execution.
* **Audio Glasses:** A new category of glasses without cameras, featuring open-ear audio, the longest battery life of any Meta glasses (full day), and a new Ray-Ban Clubmaster style.
* **Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3:** Slimmer frames, adjustable temple tips, Dolby Atmos spatial audio capture, six microphones, and an expanded lineup to 51 different style combinations starting at **$249**, adding Capri, Nova, and a Kylie ivory colorway, plus a Lisa collaboration collection.
* **Neural Band and Display Glasses:** Meta Ray-Ban display glasses with full-color high-resolution displays and the Meta neural band are catching up with demand and rolling out internationally.
* **FDA-Cleared Over-the-Counter Hearing Aid:** Hearing enhancement turns Meta glasses into an FDA-cleared hearing aid compensating for perceived mild to moderate hearing loss, featuring a guided in-app assessment.
* **Privacy and Security:** Introduced the **Muse Confidential VM** (where even Meta cannot see information) and private processing for glasses (starting with live translations) using the same technology as WhatsApp.

## Spatial Computing and Breakthrough VR Glasses
* **Andrew "Boz" Bosworth on VR Breakthrough:** Boz demonstrated Meta's new glasses-form-factor VR device featuring high-resolution displays, surface typing, hand/voice tracking, and assistant **Cooper**.
* **Entertainment and Cinema:** Certified as the first IMAX-enhanced VR device with Dolby Atmos and Dolby Vision. Disney Plus app coming later this year; Disney Immersive Cinema by ILM featuring Marvel Studios and Star Wars; plus apps from Peacock, Paramount Plus, HBO Max, YouTube, Prime Video, and Crunchyroll.
* **Live Sports & Gaming:** More than 100 immersive live events per year (including ESPN college football, MLB, UFC, TNT Sports, and NBC Sports). Features 75 hands-only gaming titles at launch, full Quest library support via optional attached controllers, and Xbox cloud gaming.
* **Hologram Calling:** Introduced full-body photorealistic holograms in VR (solving the legs problem) and bringing them to Meta Ray-Ban display glasses.
* **Pricing & Availability:** Priced at **$12.99** [sic: likely $1,299 based on context] and shipping in the spring.

## Muse Charm (Keychain AI)
* **Hardware Form Factor:** Zuckerberg revealed **Muse Charm**, a keychain-mounted hardware device packing the full Muse experience, real-time voice, and avatar stack with a fingerprint sensor.
* **Timeline:** Slated to ship in time for the holidays in **December**.
