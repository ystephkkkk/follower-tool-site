* **Target Audience for DOTS:** Starting as a premium, prosumer, and enterprise-focused product using the Astro model, Altman noted that OpenAI will introduce a mass market consumer version over time.

* **Model Release Decisions:** OpenAI recently held back the release of Astra 6.1 and paused training on a separate model to dedicate more attention to safety, alignment, monitoring, and security.
