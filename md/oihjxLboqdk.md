**TL;DR**
Cisco delivered record Q3 results with 12% revenue growth and a 35% surge in product orders, driven by an massive acceleration in AI infrastructure demand. The company significantly raised its FY26 AI order forecast to $9 billion as its Silicon One and Acacia optics portfolios secure major design wins with global hyperscalers.

## AI Infrastructure and Hyperscaler Acceleration
* **Surging AI Demand:** Cisco now expects to take approximately $9 billion in AI infrastructure orders from hyperscalers in FY26, up from a previous estimate of $5 billion and 4.5x the FY25 total.
    * **Revenue Impact:** The company expects to recognize $4 billion in AI infrastructure revenue from hyperscalers within the current fiscal year.
* **Silicon One Momentum:** Cisco secured five new design wins with hyperscalers in Q3. This includes the first two wins for the **Silicon One P200** system for "scale-across" use cases and a **G200** win for "scale-out" architecture.
* **Acacia Optics Leadership:** The Acacia business saw its strongest quarter ever with over $1 billion in orders.
    * **Market Volume:** Cisco has shipped over 750,000 400-gig and 40,000 800-gig coherent pluggable optics, which management believes far exceeds the shipments of its nearest competitors.
* **Expanding Customer Base:** Beyond hyperscalers, Cisco took $300 million in AI orders from "neocloud," sovereign, and enterprise customers, with a total pipeline of $3 billion for this segment.

## Enterprise and Campus Network Modernization
* **Campus Refresh Cycle:** Campus networking orders reached record levels, growing 25% YoY. This is driven by an urgent need to modernize infrastructure to handle AI-driven traffic, which is expected to triple over the next three years.
* **Wireless and Wi-Fi 7:** Wireless orders grew more than 40% YoY. Wi-Fi 7 adoption is rapid, accounting for 50% of the wireless mix in Q3.
* **Enterprise Data Center:** Switching orders for enterprise data centers grew over 40% YoY, marking double-digit growth in seven of the last nine quarters.
* **Industrial IoT:** This segment reported its strongest quarter ever, benefiting from the onshoring of manufacturing and the rise of physical AI.

## Financial Performance and Strategic Realignment
* **Record Financials:** Revenue hit a record $15.8 billion (up 12% YoY), while Non-GAAP EPS rose 10% to $1.06, both exceeding guidance.
* **Operational Efficiency:** Despite headwinds from rising memory prices, Cisco maintained a 66% gross margin and 34.2% operating margin.
    * **Memory Mitigation:** Engineers implemented over 20 programs to reduce memory utilization, including new wireless products that require 50% less memory.
* **Restructuring for Growth:** Cisco announced a restructuring plan involving a $1 billion pre-tax charge to reallocate resources away from legacy areas and into Silicon, Optics, Security, and AI.
* **Capital Returns:** The company returned $2.9 billion to shareholders in Q3 ($1.7B in dividends and $1.3B in buybacks), with $9.6 billion remaining in the repurchase program.

## Security Integration and Splunk Transition
* **Core Security Recovery:** Excluding Splunk, Cisco’s core security portfolio saw double-digit order growth, specifically led by strong performance in firewalls.
* **New Product Traction:** Over 1,000 new customers purchased recently launched products like **Hypershield**, **XDR**, and **Secure Access** in Q3.
* **Splunk Cloud Shift:** As expected, Splunk is seeing a rapid shift from on-premise deals to cloud subscriptions. While this creates a near-term revenue drag due to accounting timing, Cisco remains on track to add 1,000 new Splunk customer logos in FY26.
* **AI Security Innovation:** Cisco announced "DefenseClaw," an open-source solution to help customers safely deploy AI agents, and a "Secure AI Factory" partnership with NVIDIA to embed security into AI infrastructure from the start.
