* **Addressing 40 Years of Tech Debt:** The internet was not originally designed for security, resulting in decades of accumulated tech debt that AI is now exposing.
* **Proactive Defense:** OpenAI advocates for a "defender's window" advantage where AI tools are placed in the hands of defenders to proactively close security gaps.
* **Accelerated Patch Cadence:** Organizations must transition from quarterly patching to patching within months, weeks, days, hours, or minutes.
* **Government Engagement:** OpenAI has floated controversial ideas—similar to FBI actions clearing malicious botnets over the past five years—such as government-backed automated patching entities, and has given advanced notice to the U.S. and international governments regarding major vulnerabilities like PHP.
