AMD Chair and CEO Dr. Lisa Su discussed her career trajectory from an engineer to leading a major semiconductor company, emphasizing the value of running toward difficult problems, maintaining a long-term vision, and embracing ambition.

## Background and Early Influences
* **Family Background:** Lisa Su moved to the United States from Taiwan at age two or three after her father attended graduate school at Columbia University, growing up in Queens, New York, in an environment that prioritized education and ambition.

## Leadership Philosophy and Career Advice
* **Run Toward Hard Problems:** The best career advice Su received was to volunteer for the hardest, most important problems where nobody knows the answer.
* **Embrace Ambition:** Advises women in business to be ambitious, believe in themselves, and raise their hands for challenging assignments because they will learn immensely and gain recognition for their willingness to tackle tough obstacles.
* **Impact-Driven Focus:** Motivated daily by the ability to build tangible products that make a real difference in people's lives and contribute to the broader economy and national security.

* **Challenging Environment:** Joined Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) when it was a much smaller player compared to industry behemoth Intel (which held roughly 80% market share at the time), and later assumed the CEO role when the company faced severe financial distress near bankruptcy.
* **Semiconductor Industry Shift:** Notes that 30 years ago few people talked about chips, but today semiconductors sit at the center of the global economy and national security.
* **Dual Capability:** Highlights AMD's unique market position in delivering both CPUs and GPUs, enabling the company to scale its value in an AI-driven era.
