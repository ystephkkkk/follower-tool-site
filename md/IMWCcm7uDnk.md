## TL;DR
Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang announced a major $500 billion business partnership with South Korea's SK Group at an AI summit in San Francisco, while highlighting critical supply chain constraints, the necessity of open AI models for cybersecurity, and the massive future expansion of the semiconductor industry.

## Partnerships and Investments in South Korea
* **SK Group Partnership:** Nvidia and SK Group entered into a business partnership encompassing over **$500 billion** in mutual business, covering memory consumption, purchases, and supercomputer sales as SK scales toward gigawatt-scale factories.
* **SK Hynix Collaboration:** Nvidia is working closely with SK Hynix on a multi-generational roadmap for High Bandwidth Memory (HBM), including HBM2, HBM3, HBM3E, HBM4, HBM4E, and beyond.
* **SK Telecom AI Cloud:** Nvidia is investing **$1 billion** in SK Telecom to help build South Korea's leading AI cloud, which intends to scale up to **200 megawatts** in the near term and ultimately reach up to **two gigawatts**.

## Semiconductor Industry Transformation and Supply Chain
* **Shift to Non-Human Users:** Huang noted that the semiconductor industry has fundamentally changed because computers are no longer built exclusively for humans, but for future AI agents and robots.
* **Supply Chain Constraints:** Nvidia is currently constrained in HBM memories, LPDDR memories, land, power, and construction workers needed to set up data centers.
* **Industry Growth Rate:** Huang stated the industry has the ability to double infrastructure each year, noting it is difficult to scale faster than that due to the physical limitations of land, power, and building shells.

## US vs. China Approaches to AI
* **Differing Philosophies:** Discussing a conversation with the SK Group chairman, Huang noted that China is heavily focused on lowering the **dollar per token**, while America remains focused on token quality.
* **Research Talent:** Huang emphasized that China produces more AI researchers than any other region annually, creating what he called "manufacturing intelligence," and noted that many prominent AI researchers in Silicon Valley are of Chinese descent.

## Open Models vs. Closed Models
* **Role of Open Models:** Huang defended the importance of open-weight models, signing an open letter with industry peers like Satya Nadella to highlight that open models are essential for cybersecurity, innovation, startups, and company sovereignty.
* **Cost and Control Trade-offs:** While closed models are often cheaper and easier to use off-the-shelf, open models are necessary when organizations require proprietary control, specialized adaptation for regulated industries, or strict sovereignty.
* **Cybersecurity Defense Example:** Huang cited a recent incident where Hugging Face utilized an open model (**GLM 5.2**) to identify vulnerabilities and patch a penetration after proprietary models were inaccessible, demonstrating the value of massively distributed self-defense over single points of failure.

## Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Semiconductor Industry Size:** Huang predicts the semiconductor industry will likely have to grow **ten times larger** than it is today over the next decade or so to support future AI agents and robots. — Jensen Huang
