## Science Corner: Mapping the Brain in 64 Dimensions

*   **Drosophila Connectome & Hyperbolic Space:**
    *   **Speaker:** David Friedberg
    *   **Details:** A Cambridge and Princeton team used electron microscopes to map the complete connectome of a *Drosophila* fruit fly brain—139,000 neurons and 50 million synaptic connections. Budapest researchers found that predicting neural connections failed in standard 3D Euclidean space, but succeeded using hyperbolic space or a 64-dimensional Euclidean framework.
*   **Biological Complexity:**
    *   **Speaker:** David Friedberg
    *   **Details:** Friedberg emphasizes that biological systems achieve consciousness, vision, and body control by organizing complexity across 64 dimensions, far outstripping current silicon models and highlighting the vast limits of human scientific understanding.
