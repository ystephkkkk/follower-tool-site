## California's High-Speed Rail Project

## The "Stop Nick Shirley Act" (AB 2624)
* **Legislative Retaliation:** **Nick Shirley** discussed California's passage of AB 2624, dubbed the "Stop Nick Shirley Act," which allows immigration support service providers and state-funded NGOs to issue formal demand letters prohibiting the upload of recorded footage under threat of a $4,000+ fine plus legal fees.
* **Sponsorship and Conflict of Interest:** **Nick Shirley** and the panel noted the bill was co-sponsored by CHIRLA (an advocacy non-profit receiving $80 million) and authored by Mia Bonta, the wife of California Attorney General Rob Bonta.
* **Legal Challenge:** **Nick Shirley** confirmed he is suing the state of California over the bill, characterizing it as a direct violation of his First Amendment rights.
