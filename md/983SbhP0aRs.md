- **Funding Shorts Explained (Colin):**
    - **Standard Short:** Borrow a stock, sell it at $100 for cash, buy it back later at $50, return it to the owner, and pocket the $50 spread (minus borrow fees).
    - **Funding Short:** Borrow a stock and sell it at $100 to generate $100 in cash, then immediately use that cash to buy stock in a high-conviction long position. The goal is to make substantial gains on the long while breaking even or making a small return on the short position.
