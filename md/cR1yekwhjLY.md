* **Models Improving Models:** Microsoft uses models across its fleet to check for bugs, produce training data, and create evaluations—and models are increasingly involved in improving themselves.

## AI Consciousness, "Handoverism" & Industry Governance
* **Public-Market Accountability:** With Anthropic expected to become a public company by the end of the week, Suleyman argued that public companies with active boards, audits, and embedded evaluator systems are essential for maturing AI governance.
* **Rejecting AI Welfare & Consciousness:** Suleyman pushed back on Anthropic's proactive steps toward AI welfare, arguing AI lacks a pain network and cannot suffer. Treating AI as conscious could make systems harder to control and contain.
* **Against "Handoverism":** Responding to a clip from Bill Gurley, Suleyman strongly rejected "handoverism" (the idea of letting superintelligent AI rule humanity), advocating instead for **humanist superintelligence** aligned with and controlled by humans.
