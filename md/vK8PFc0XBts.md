**TL;DR**
Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Bjorn Lomborg called for the IMF and World Bank to abandon "climate fixation" and "luxury beliefs" to refocus on their core missions: driving global economic growth, ensuring financial stability, and alleviating poverty through reliable energy and infrastructure.

## Refocusing Multilateral Institutions on Core Missions
*   **Prioritizing Growth:** Secretary Bessent emphasized that the primary risk to global financial stability is a lack of economic growth. He advocated for a "back-to-factory-settings" mindset for the Bretton Woods institutions.
    *   **IMF Role:** Should focus strictly on global financial stability, monitoring global imbalances (such as China’s trade surplus), and stabilizing countries in "bad equilibriums."
    *   **World Bank Role:** Must return to its primary mandate of pulling people out of poverty rather than prioritizing "Western elite issues."
*   **Institutional Critique:** Both speakers criticized the "climate fixation" of international financial institutions (IFIs).
    *   **Resource Misallocation:** Bjorn Lomborg noted that 48% of World Bank financing now goes to climate finance, despite poor populations ranking climate change low on their list of priorities (31st out of 34 concerns in Africa).
    *   **Discarding Short-termism:** Bessent criticized policy decisions based on refuted academic studies, such as the 2024 *Nature* article suggesting a 60% GDP loss from climate change, calling for a return to stable, long-term frameworks.

## Energy Realism and Economic Development
*   **Energy Abundance:** Bessent argued that affordable, reliable energy is fundamental to poverty reduction. He noted the U.S. withdrawal from the Green Climate Fund was due to its goals running contrary to this necessity.
*   **The "All-of-the-Above" Strategy:** The World Bank is reportedly pivoting back to supporting nuclear energy and diverse energy sources.
    *   **Nuclear Energy:** Cited as a critical, clean energy source that was mistakenly sidelined; Bessent pointed to France as a successful model for powering the European grid.
    *   **Transition Skepticism:** Lomborg highlighted the "hypocrisy" of forcing renewables on poor nations, noting that Germany spent 700 billion euros only to remain 79% reliant on fossil fuels, while China’s "green" exports are largely coal-powered.
*   **Innovation vs. Restriction:** The speakers advocated for "innovating our way out" of environmental challenges rather than enforced privation, citing Bill Gates’ $13 billion investment in energy innovation as the proper model.

## Evidence-Based Policy and Human Flourishing
*   **Cost-Benefit Analysis:** Lomborg argued for "rational priorities" where development policy (e.g., preventing infant mortality or improving education) provides a significantly higher return on investment than climate policy.
    *   **Resilience through Prosperity:** A key theme was that "prosperity protects." For example, a hurricane in poor Haiti kills hundreds, while the same storm in wealthy Florida kills almost no one due to better infrastructure.
*   **Foundational Needs:** Tim Adams and the Secretary identified specific levers for human flourishing that IFIs should support:
    *   **Property Rights:** Essential for capital access; cited the example of farmers unable to get irrigation loans due to lack of land titles.
    *   **Capital Formation:** "Capitalism without capital is just another ism."
    *   **Success Stories:** Argentina under Javier Milei was cited as a "fantastic success" in accumulating reserves and reducing poverty by following a sustainable economic path.

## U.S. Leadership and Global Cooperation
*   **America First, Not Alone:** Bessent clarified that the U.S. "wants to be in it to win it" as a partner in multilateral banks, but with a focus on basic infrastructure, health, and hygiene.
*   **Upcoming Leadership:** With the U.S. leading the G20 this year and the G7 next year, the administration intends to coordinate with the IMF and World Bank to remove hindrances to global growth.
*   **Global Imbalances:** Specific concern was raised regarding undisclosed debt and "loan to own" programs by certain nations (implied China) that create downward debt spirals for developing countries.
