## US-China Relations & Indo-Pacific Trip
- **Diplomatic Milestones:** Bessent highlighted trade deals with the Philippines and Thailand, a Quad meeting in Manila, and a stop in Shenzhen.
  - President Trump and President Xi held their third meeting of the year, which Bessent called "unprecedented."
- **AI & Nuclear Agreements:** Trump and Xi agreed that humans must always be involved in military decisions made by AI, prohibiting autonomous AI for major military actions.
  - Leaders also held a "robust discussion" on containing the growth of nuclear weapons.
- **Strategic Posture:** Bessent rejected the "China hawk" label, stating he is "neither a hawk nor a dove" and is tasked with managing the world's most important economic relationship.
  - Reiterating remarks from the 60th anniversary of the Committee on US-China Relations in New York, Bessent stated: "We don't want to decouple, we just need to derisk."

## AI, Chip Controls, & Tech Policy
- **H200 Chips & NVIDIA:** Bessent stated that H200 chips remain the threshold level approved for sale to China and that no policy change is anticipated.
  - Addressing NVIDIA's recent earnings report that it stopped shipping H200s to China despite having US licenses, Bessent attributed this to a "paucity of orders from the Chinese" as they attempt to build their own chip stack, which he believes is "many years behind."
  - Jensen Huang did not come up in discussions with Chinese officials.
- **Remote Access Crackdown:** Treasury and the National Security Council (NSC) implemented rules to curtail overseas data centers from renting compute power to China (e.g., MegaSpeed, which Commerce referred to the DOJ for investigation).
- **Anthropic IPO & Regulation:** Anthropic's recent IPO—making it an almost $2 trillion company—was hailed by Bessent as the "most valuable IPO in history," noting it started in 2021 with seven people in a San Francisco park.
  - Bessent pushed back on concerns over potential new regulations from incoming Senate Banking Committee Chair Elizabeth Warren, noting the administration has had "safe and sound policies" in place since the March release of Mythos, including pre-testing and exploit notifications.
  - He argued that US frontier labs are "our shield against AI" exploits by foreign actors, which predominantly stem from Chinese models.

## Macroeconomics, Markets, & The Fed
- **Market Uncertainty & Politics:** Bessent attributed recent market declines since the election to investors seeking policy certainty from Democrats following the "blue wave," noting a leadership challenge against Senator Schumer.

- **Federal Reserve Criticism:** Addressing talk of a 50 basis point rate hike in December, Bessent criticized the Fed governor pushing for it, pointing out that the same governor dissented at the January meeting in favor of a 50 basis point cut when the Fed cut by 25 basis points—calling the shift 11 to 12 months later "peripatetic."
- **Energy & Inflation:** Bessent urged the Fed to "look forward not backwards," noting that headline inflation has been noisy due to energy supply shocks.
  - He highlighted "some of the biggest drops in history" in crude oil and gasoline since the election, and the "biggest dollar decrease in history" over the past two weeks for "crude for delivery" (crude price, insurance, and shipping).
