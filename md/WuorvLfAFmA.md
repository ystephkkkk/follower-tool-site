## The Agentic Internet & Protocol Shift
- **Beyond Human Interfaces:** The internet is transitioning from machine-to-human pages (HTML) to agent-to-agent protocols (such as Model Context Protocol - MCP, and Universal Commerce Protocols).
- **Agent-First Websites:** Companies must redesign their digital presence to serve AI agents directly, providing precise data and machine-readable APIs rather than decorative UIs.
- **Agentic Commerce:** AI agents are now making autonomous purchasing decisions and transactions on marketplaces (e.g., buying browser instances via Vercel or transacting via Stripe and Shopify) without human intervention.
- **Autonomous Workflows:** The primary shift from ChatGPT to modern agents is **autonomy**—agents now operate unsupervised for extended periods to complete complex multi-step missions.

## Infrastructure, Security, and Fluid Compute
- **Token Distribution:** Vercel operates one of the largest token distribution networks (AI Gateway), aggregating every major open and closed model.
- **Serverless Evolution ("Fluid Compute"):** Serverless compute is expanding into "fluid compute," dynamically scaling up and down to handle the massive compute demands driven by autonomous coding agents.
- **AI Security & Sandboxing:** Autonomous agents present severe security risks (e.g., prompt injections and unintended system hacking, referencing the Hugging Face / OpenAI security incident). Vercel builds secure internal sandboxes and enterprise infrastructure to contain agents safely.
- **Code Verification:** Rauch emphasizes "rejecting non-understanding," noting that future systems require provably correct, mathematically verified code to prevent vulnerabilities in mission-critical systems.

## Open vs. Closed Models & Token Market Dynamics
- **Rise of Open Models:** 80% of tokens flowing through Vercel's AI Gateway are now open models (such as Qwen and Chinese lab releases), a complete reversal from earlier in the year when proprietary models dominated.
- **Task-Specific Routing:** Enterprises use frontier models for high-level planning and open models for cheaper, faster execution (80% of routine tasks).
- **Parallels to Crypto:** Model providers face high volatility and a lack of customer loyalty, with market dynamics resembling "crypto highs" where users easily switch based on price or subsidies.

## Hiring & The Evolution of Engineering
- **Engineering Management Role:** Human developers increasingly act as engineering managers, directing swats of agents, reading and verifying code, and focusing on high-level architecture.
- **Agentic Migrations:** Enterprise migrations that previously took months and required massive consulting teams (like moving Mercedes-Benz / F1 to new stacks) can now be executed by agents in 5 minutes.
- **Hiring Criteria:** Vercel seeks candidates who demonstrate "proof of work" via agentic engineering ("software factories"), high technical curiosity, and strong human communication skills for customer-facing roles.
