## TL;DR
President Donald Trump hosted a State Department roundtable to announce historic investments in American mining, including $100 million for mining schools and multi-billion-dollar project funding to restore U.S. mineral independence and supply chain dominance.

## Administration Policy, Permitting, and Tariffs
* **Permitting Reforms:** Federal mining permit approval times reduced from 3–5 years down to 25 days under the current administration.
* **Trade Tariffs:** Implemented 232 tariffs on foreign copper, aluminum, and steel to protect domestic producers and incentivize domestic smelting.
* **Global Mineral Agreements:** The U.S. has signed 27 bilateral critical mineral agreements with other countries, alongside the creation of the international partnership "Forge" chaired by the U.S. to coordinate public finance and supply chains (Marco Rubio).
* **Energy Emergency Declaration:** Early in the administration, critical minerals were integrated into an energy emergency declaration, establishing the National Energy Dominance Council for a whole-of-government approach (Doug Burgum).
* **Strategic Mineral Reserve:** Rebuilding the national defense stockpile with a $12 billion investment in a brand new strategic mineral reserve.
