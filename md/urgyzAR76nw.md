## National Security, Tech Outages, and DHS Oversight
* **Tech Outages & AI:** DNI Jay Clayton testified before the House Homeland Security Committee that recent service disruptions—including at the FAA and NSA—were separate, routine maintenance and patching issues rather than a coordinated or state-sponsored cyberattack.
  * Clayton warned that "super intelligence" is rapidly increasing the speed, scale, and sophistication of offensive and defensive cyber operations, making legacy systems obsolete faster than before.
  * Rep. Dan Goldman (D-NY) pressed Clayton on the FAA ground stop that canceled 18,000 flights and criticized the administration for cutting cybersecurity personnel at CISA and the FBI.
* **Iranian Threats:** Rep. Michael McCaul (R-TX) questioned Clayton on a CNN report alleging Iran is planning a "dirty bomb" attack in Washington, D.C. Clayton called the motive "aspirational" and noted an ongoing New York indictment involving an Iranian-sponsored plot, while Secretary of State Marco Rubio stated there is no imminent threat.
* **DHS Secretary Travel & Accountability:** Ranking Member Bennie Thompson (D-MS) criticized Secretary Markwayne Mullin for using a government plane to attend an LSU-Texas college football game in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, and visiting the private suite of a company holding a $2.2 billion DHS contract while Americans were stranded by the FAA ground stop.
  * Mullin defended the trip, stating the airspace had been open for nearly 24 hours, 36,000 planes flew safely that Saturday, and he is required to use the government plane for 24/7 continuity—having worked 239 consecutive days—and reimbursed the government personally.
* **ICE Custody Deaths:** Rep. LaMonica McIver (D-NJ) accused Mullin of "killing people," citing 65 deaths in ICE custody under the administration, including two recent deaths at Delaney Hall in her district, and an internal ICE investigation showing nearly 80% of first-year custody deaths followed poor medical care.
  * Mullin denied the allegation, stating he visited Delaney Hall and that it is in better condition than New Jersey's state penitentiary.
* **Committee Absence:** Committee Chair Andrew Garbarino (R-NY) criticized FBI Director Kash Patel for skipping the annual worldwide threats hearing, calling it a significant departure from precedent.
