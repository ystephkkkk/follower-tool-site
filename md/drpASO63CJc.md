* **Wall Street's Flawed Math:** The market is discounting AI stocks based on an overly simplistic calculation—comparing $1 trillion to $2 trillion in CapEx against ~$80 billion in Anthropic revenue and ~$40 billion in OpenAI revenue (~$120 billion total, or under $200 billion even when generously adding Groq, Muse, and Gemini).

  * **Dell AI Factories:** Michael Dell reported selling 3,200 enterprise AI factories across 2024 and 2025 combined, and 3,300 in the last nine months alone—representing over 6,000 discrete enterprises buying GPU servers to run open-source AI with real ROI that does not appear in Wall Street's frontier-model math.
* **Voting Machine vs. Weighing Machine:** Quoting Warren Buffett, Baker notes the market is in a "voting machine" phase focused on second derivatives, but will shift to a "weighing machine" phase as 2027 guidance rolls out. Even if Nvidia and AI stocks only grow 5%, 8%, or 10% annually relative to next year's guide, they are cheap.

## Nvidia's Leadership Crisis & The Case for Jensen Huang
* **Letter to the Board:** Gavin Baker and Brad Gerstner sent a letter to Nvidia's board urging a slow, measured, deliberate approach—noting there is no need to rush a CEO hire given Collet Kress is a superb CFO and the business is strong. They suggested considering bringing Jensen Huang back as CEO once all facts emerge from the independent investigation and Congressional testimony.
* **Singular Leadership:** Having invested in semiconductors for 27 years (and knowing Jensen since 2000), Baker calls Jensen singular ("I've met one Jensen and I've met one Elon"), warning that nothing stops him from becoming CEO of a competitor if pushed out.
* **Broad Support:** A vast number of Nvidia employees, shareholders, and partners have reached out to Baker wanting Jensen back as CEO if cleared of national security innuendo (he has not been charged with anything). In Taiwan, buildings and highways have been lit up with signs reading "Bring Jensen back."
* **Irreplaceable Supply Chain Ties:** Jensen has never had a contract with TSMC (Morris Chang once asked him to be TSMC's CEO). He speaks the native dialect, makes handshake deals, and secures more volume at lower prices than anyone else—relationships Baker says cannot be replicated.

## National Security, China & Open-Source AI
* **Patriotism & China Strategy:** Baker describes Jensen—who immigrated from Taiwan at age 9, attended a reform school, and worked as a janitor—as deeply patriotic. Jensen's consistent stance has been that America must beat China and prevent an alternative Chinese AI hardware ecosystem from emerging and spreading to adversaries like Iran, North Korea, and Russia.
* **Unintended Policy Consequences:** Previously, Chinese AI labs preferred Nvidia GPUs (less powerful than US versions, per Jensen's "America should have the first, the best, and the most" standard), which slowed Huawei because hardware requires usage to improve. New policies forcing Chinese labs onto Huawei hardware risk accelerating Huawei's capabilities rapidly.
* **Protecting American Open-Source AI:** Jensen was the biggest proponent of American open-source AI. Baker warns against a world dominated by one, two, or three frontier AIs with narrow value systems, noting he wants to use Fireworks to fine-tune an American open-source AI for his 8-month-old daughter on *Star Wars*, *Star Trek*, *Dune*, *Lord of the Rings*, *The Hobbit*, Shakespeare, Homer, and Greek philosophers.

## SpaceX, Starship & GroqBot
* **Starship Breakthrough:** SpaceX successfully caught the upper stage of Starship (Starship 15) using its "Mechazilla" control tower arms—a massive milestone for rapid reusability.
* **National Defense & Global Impact:** SpaceX is America's most important defense contractor, accounting for 85% of mass to orbit. Beyond defense, Starlink is connecting every school in Honduras and the Amazon region of Brazil, saving lives at sea, and transforming the Global South.
* **Orbital Compute & Direct-to-Cell:** SpaceX operates 11,000 satellites today, and Starlink V3 consumes ~10–15% the power of an orbital compute satellite. SpaceX engineers have already solved the cooling and design challenges; Starship reusability was the true bottleneck.
* **GroqBot & First-Party AI:** Baker spends almost as much time in GroqBot as on X, calling it the most useful AI tool available. Based on third-party data, he estimates SpaceX's high-margin, fast-growing first-party AI business is approaching $10 billion in revenue.
