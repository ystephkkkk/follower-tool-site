**TL;DR**
Secretary of War Pete Hegseth administered the oath of enlistment to a new cohort of Missouri recruits, emphasizing a strategic shift toward higher recruitment standards, the elimination of bureaucratic "distractions," and the rapid domestic expansion of the U.S. "Arsenal of Freedom."

## The "Arsenal of Freedom" and Industrial Speed
Secretary Hegseth linked the enlistment ceremony to a broader national tour focused on revitalizing the American defense industrial base.
*   **Production Priorities:** The Department of War is demanding that domestic manufacturers—both large corporations like Boeing and small-scale firms—build capabilities at a significantly higher scale and speed.
*   **Reducing Bureaucracy:** Hegseth noted a policy shift under the Trump administration to move away from offshoring and to bypass "Pentagon bureaucracy" that previously slowed procurement.
*   **Human-Centric Strategy:** While highlighting advanced technology like the F-47, Hegseth asserted that the "decisive factor" in warfare remains the personnel—engineers, medics, and infantry—rather than just the platforms.

## Recruitment Renaissance and Standards
The Secretary reported a significant turnaround in military recruitment numbers, attributing the surge to a shift in national spirit and departmental policy.
*   **Historic Enrollment:** Hegseth stated that 2025 saw historic recruiting records, and early indicators suggest 2026 is on track to break those previous records.
*   **Higher Standards:** The administration is intentionally moving away from lowering entrance requirements. Instead, the focus is on raising standards to attract high-caliber candidates, arguing that a more elite force is the best deterrent against future conflict.
*   **The "1%":** Hegseth categorized the recruits as the "real 1%" of the country due to their willingness to serve the Constitution over self-interest.

## Leadership and Combat Lethality
A primary theme of the address was the "unleashing" of military commanders to focus strictly on mission success and lethality.
*   **Removing Distractions:** Hegseth promised recruits and their families that leadership would prioritize training and accountability while removing "politically correct" distractions from the chain of command.
*   **Commander Empowerment:** The Secretary emphasized a return to "traditional" military discipline, ensuring that commanders at every echelon have the authority and support to focus on winning fights.
*   **The "1775 Business":** Hegseth distinguished between the "idea" of America (1776) and those in the "1775 business"—the service members who must defend that idea with force before it can exist as a political reality.

## Formal Enlistment and Recognition
The event concluded with the formal administration of the oath of enlistment for both National Guard and active-duty components.
*   **The Oath:** Recruits swore to support and defend the Constitution of the United States against all enemies, foreign and domestic, and to obey the orders of the President and appointed officers.
*   **Recruiter Recognition:** Hegseth specifically recognized Sergeant First Class Burtness of the Missouri National Guard, who was nominated as a top recruiter for FY 2025 for enlisting over 30 recruits.
