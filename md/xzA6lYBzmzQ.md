## Operational Structure: The Three Core Products
- **Talent Platform:** 
  - Connects enterprises and AI labs with vetted domain experts.
  - Utilizes Stripe Connect on the backend to handle global payouts reliably.
- **Studio (Data Platform):** 
  - An onboarding platform where experts use AI co-pilots and agentic QC/automated tools to create Reinforcement Learning (RL) environments.
  - RL environments consist of three parts: *Apps* (clones of tools like Google Workspace, Salesforce, Adobe), *Worlds* (the content/context inside apps like messages, docs, sheets), and *Tasks* (prompts and rubrics used to train and grade AI models).
- **AI Project Manager:** 
  - Autonomous agents that run projects, hire people, determine data quality, set KPIs, and remain accountable for customer outcomes.

## Market Thesis & AI Industry Economics
- **The Data & Jevons Paradox:** 
  - As AI agents make human experts 3x to 5x more productive, demand for human teaching expands exponentially rather than shrinking.
  - 99% of knowledge remains in human heads; humans must measure the frontier and provide feedback/evals so models can learn.
- **Enterprise & Sovereign AI:** 
  - Every Fortune 500 company will eventually build its own AI intelligence to codify internal competitive advantage rather than sharing proprietary data with frontier labs.
  - **Inference Spend Thresholds:** Companies spending under $10M on inference are figuring things out; $10M–$100M spend triggers an investment in *evals* (accounting for roughly 10% of budget); $100M+ spend (like Harvey or Casetext) triggers *post-training* their own models.

## Predictions & Forward Calls
- **Brendan Foody** predicts that every Fortune 500 company will eventually operate like a "neolab," building its own core data sets, evaluations, and sovereign AI intelligence.
- **Brendan Foody** predicts that enterprise inference spend will ramp materially, driving companies to deploy evals at the $10M–$100M spend range and post-training at the $100M+ spend range.
