**TL;DR:** Contour Asset Management, a $6B fundamental long/short tech fund, measures AI ROI by running live A/B tests on analyst research and tracking a proprietary "cost per useful insight" metric. The fund deploys custom agents for idea generation, market timing, and consolidating alternative data to maximize research and operational efficiency.

## Firm Profile & The AI ROI Challenge
- **Company Context:** Contour Asset Management is a $6B fundamental long/short hedge fund with 15–20 employees focused primarily on the TMT (Tech, Media, Telecom) sector.
- **The Core Problem:** As detailed by Hari Kumar, Head of AI, proving the return on investment (ROI) for AI spend is extremely difficult because financial impact (P&L) takes months or years to materialize rather than showing immediate results.
- **Mandate:** Kumar's role focuses on introducing AI workflows not just to research, but across trading, operations, compliance, and finance.

## Live A/B Testing & Research Workflows
- **Analyst Coverage:** The fund employs about 10 analysts, with each person covering 30 to 40 names.
- **A/B Testing Methodology:** Contour records and documents complete analyst workflows without AI tools versus workflows utilizing new AI tools over a standard 3-month earnings cycle per name.
- **Granular Process Evaluation:** 
  - **Expert Calls:** Comparing manual note synthesis versus agentic note synthesis.
  - **Consumer Surveys:** Evaluating human-led survey fielding against agent-driven consumer surveys.
- **Focus on Process Improvement:** Because ROI cannot be measured instantly via P&L, success is measured by whether AI fundamentally improves the quality and speed of thesis validation and model updates.

## Idea Generation, Timing Agents, and Alt Data Integration
- **Manual Idea Generation Agents:** Take alternative data points and industry expert call transcripts to layer on conclusions for short- and long-term stock price impact.
- **Market Timing Agents:** 
  - **The Retention Problem:** Analysts often spend days on an idea that gets deprioritized by the Portfolio Manager (PM), resulting in missed price windows months later when the idea becomes relevant again.
  - **The Solution:** Agents continuously scan past notes and track them against current market conditions to alert the analyst, PM, and trader when an old idea becomes actionable.
- **Alternative Data Unification:** 
  - **Data Silos:** Funds spend heavily on niche data sets like satellite mapping, granular credit card panel data (e.g., Yipit), and call transcripts (e.g., AlphaSense).
  - **Agentic Synthesis:** Using Model Context Protocol (MCP) and APIs to feed disparate alt data sources into internal agents, allowing expert call notes and credit card spend data to interact seamlessly.

## Cost Per Useful Insight & Time Tracking
- **Analyst Time Allocation:** The firm actively tracks time spent per name to understand where analyst hours go.
- **Cost Per Useful Insight:** Contour developed this metric to track the daily insights generated by analysts, the time invested, the tools used, and the eventual P&L outcomes of those decisions.

## Extending AI Across Trading, Operations, and Compliance
- **Trading Operations:** AI agents track industry-wide position spreads and entry/exit times, freeing traders from pure execution to feed ideas to analysts and associates.
- **Ops and Compliance Consolidation:** 
  - **Fragmented Tools:** Existing platforms like Bloomberg or order-keeping agents offer piecemeal solutions for order reconciliation and vendor tracking.
  - **Unified Platform Goal:** Bringing order flow, financial operations, and compliance into a single internal tool to eliminate repetitive manual work.
- **Up-Leveling Existing Spend:** Moving beyond static subscriptions (like AlphaSense or Yipit) by integrating data feeds into proprietary internal platforms that learn from individual analyst model patterns and habits.

## The Hidden Costs of AI Adoption
- **Bug Fixes and Downtime:** Part of Contour's internal ROI calculation accounts for the time lost when model calls or system connectors break and require technical troubleshooting.
- **Efficiency Trend:** While tool maintenance detracts from time-spent-per-insight metrics, the administrative drag is steadily decreasing as workflows mature.
