## Jevons' Paradox and Technological Disruption
* **The Economic Principle:** Named after James Jevons, the paradox states that as technology makes resource use more efficient, total consumption and demand increase rather than decrease (e.g., steam engines increasing total coal usage, or the cotton gin dramatically increasing slavery and cotton output).
* **AI and Code:** Sam Parr argues that AI will make code creation significantly cheaper, driving a massive, mind-boggling explosion in total software demand rather than a net-negative reduction in jobs.
* **Historical Parallels:** The hosts compare current anti-AI sentiment and worker anxiety to historical Luddites smashing mechanical looms during the Industrial Revolution, arguing that human desire to expand and fill space always generates new, unforeseen industries.
