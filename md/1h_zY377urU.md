Intel is pivoting its entire portfolio to lead the "Agentic AI" era, powered by the full-scale ramp of its **18A process technology** and a strategic shift toward heterogeneous, rack-scale computing and hybrid local/cloud AI orchestration.

## Next-Generation Client Computing and the 18A Ramp
* **18A Process Maturity:** CEO Lip-Bu Tan confirmed that Intel’s 18A process is now at full scale, serving as the foundation for a new lineup of products with hundreds of design wins.
* **Core Ultra Series 3:** Launched as Intel’s first 18A mobile product, focusing on high-performance NPUs for agentic AI and industry-leading battery life.
    * **Market Reach:** Over 300 designs are currently shipping across consumer and commercial segments.
* **Mainstream & Gaming Expansion:** 
    * **Core Series 3:** A tailored version for the mainstream market, already securing 70+ designs since its April introduction.
    * **Arc G3 GPU:** A dedicated chip for handheld gaming derived from the 18A architecture; it delivers 40% faster performance than competitors while consuming half the power.
* **Physical AI:** Intel is pivoting its PC IP into a projected $25 trillion market by 2050, targeting robotics, manufacturing, and retail through over 4,000 edge ecosystem partners.

## Data Center Evolution: Intelligence at Scale
* **Xeon 6+ Launch:** Built on the 18A process, the new Xeon 6+ features **288 E-cores** and 576MB of L3 cache, designed for maximum density in foundational data centers.
* **The Shift to Agentic AI Infrastructure:** As AI moves from simple prompts to complex "agents" that plan, act, and use tools, the workload is shifting from GPU-heavy to CPU-heavy orchestration.
    * **Agent Density:** A single Intel-powered rack can now support up to 150,000 AI agents.
    * **Efficiency:** In agentic workflows (web fetching, linting, unit testing), the CPU performs the bulk of the "thinking" and orchestration, bringing CPU-to-GPU ratios closer to parity.
* **Rack Scale Blueprints:** Intel is moving beyond the socket to provide open-standard "Blueprints" for entire racks, partnering with Foxconn to commercialize differentiated AI infrastructure.

## Heterogeneous and Disaggregated Inference
* **SambaNova Partnership:** Intel and SambaNova introduced the **SN50 SambaRack**, utilizing Xeon 6 processors and SambaNova RTUs for agentic workloads.
* **Disaggregated Inference Stack:** A live demonstration showed a heterogeneous stack (Xeon CPUs + SambaNova RTUs + NVIDIA GPUs) outperforming standalone GPU stacks by **2x to 3x** in latency.
    * **Roles:** Xeon CPUs handle tool execution; RTUs handle token generation/decoding; GPUs handle prompt caching and prefill.
* **VC2 Architecture:** In partnership with Vista Equity Partners and Cambium Capital, Intel is deploying "Agentic Neo Clouds" across the U.S. to convert existing data centers into high-speed inference hubs.

## Custom Silicon and Biological Computing

* **Biomedical Frontiers:**
