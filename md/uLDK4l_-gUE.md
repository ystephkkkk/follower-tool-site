## TL;DR
Coinbase co-founder and CEO Brian Armstrong details Coinbase's evolution into the "Everything Exchange" via tokenized real-world assets, explosive stablecoin payments, and agentic finance (AIFI) tools for AI agents. Additionally, Armstrong discusses his longevity startup, New Limit, and its AI-driven epigenetic reprogramming platform working toward upcoming human clinical trials.

## Coinbase & The Everything Exchange
* **Core Business Shift:** 88% of Coinbase's revenue now comes from non-Bitcoin trading, pointing to broader asset class expansion on-chain.
* **Tokenized Stocks:** Coinbase launched a true tokenized stock product (held 1-to-1 against actual securities in custody), targeting the roughly 4 billion people globally lacking access to US brokerage accounts (currently available outside the US while pursuing SEC pathways).
* **Prediction Markets:** Grew rapidly within the Coinbase app, hitting a $100M revenue run rate within months of launch and compounding at over 100% quarter-over-quarter. Armstrong sees long-term societal utility for prediction markets beyond sports and politics, envisioning use cases for government policy forecasting and complex "big life questions."
* **Global Economic Footprint:** Crypto currently powers about 0.5% of global GDP, with an estimated 700 million people having used or held crypto (50M–100M monthly active users), a footprint Coinbase aims to scale past 1 billion users.

## Agentic Finance (AIFI) & AI Integration
* **Banking the AIs:** Coinbase created tools allowing any AI agent to get its own self-custodial financial account via a single prompt, avoiding traditional KYC bottlenecks.
* **Micro-Transactions & Rails:** ~76% of agentic commerce transactions are under $0.30 (used for tasks like scraping data, paywalled information, and specialist agent tool calls), rendering traditional credit card flat fees ($0.30 + percentage) economically unviable and driving adoption of low-cost stablecoin rails and the Linux Foundation-housed **X42** protocols incubated by Coinbase.
* **Internal Software Factory (Toshi):** Coinbase uses internal AI agent harnesses ("Toshi") linked to team and repository "brains" (recording past incidents, financial controls, and pull-request histories) to build toward recursive self-improvement and higher acceptance rates on PRs.

## New Limit & Biotech Ventures
* **Founding Thesis:** Inspired by cellular reprogramming discoveries from Shinya Yamanaka's lab, Armstrong co-founded **New Limit** with Jacob Kimmel, Blake Buyers, and Greg Johnson to focus on epigenetic reprogramming to restore cellular function to a younger state.
* **Platform & Scale:** Headquartered in South San Francisco with 50-60 employees, the company uses a proprietary AI frontier model for epigenetic reprogramming to run large-scale pooled screens testing millions of transcription factor hypotheses.
* **Therapeutic Roadmap:**
    * **Initial Indications:** The website roadmap targets three initial cell types: hepatocytes (liver), vascular cells, and T-cells (immune system).
    * **Alcoholic Liver Disease (ALD):** The first phase 1 clinical trial is slated to launch next year targeting ALD, addressing a condition with an initial market estimate near $20B due to high unmet medical needs.
    * **Future Pipeline:** 3 to 10 additional drug candidates are slated to come off the platform in coming years, with ultimate long-term ambitions targeting chronic age-related cell degradation across skin, muscles, and the brain.
