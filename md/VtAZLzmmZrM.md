## TL;DR
Alphabet President Ruth Porat and FedEx CEO Raj Subramaniam argued that massive AI infrastructure investments are justified by measurable economic returns—from boosting national GDP and small-business revenues to orchestrating $1.9 trillion in global supply chain inefficiencies—provided tech and logistics leaders secure local community buy-in and build on proprietary data foundations.

## Community Buy-In, Financing, and the Economics of AI Infrastructure
* **Alphabet’s "Done Right" Framework:** Ruth Porat acknowledged that voter skepticism toward AI stems from both storytelling and execution failures around affordability and opportunity, prompting Alphabet to publish its "done right" community commitment.
  * **Energy & Resource Protection:** Alphabet commits to upfront transparency (no NDAs), investing in energy capacity and infrastructure even if unused so utilities can return value to ratepayers, and protecting local water and air.
  * **Local Job Creation:** Porat cited a visit to West Memphis, Arkansas (population 23,000) with Governor Sarah Huckabee Sanders and the mayor, noting that responsible data center commitments flip local polling from majority opposition to majority support.
* **Fortress Balance Sheet & Equity Issuance:** Addressing why Alphabet, Meta, and Oracle have recently issued equity to fund AI buildouts, Porat stated that demand is eclipsing capacity because partners see real operating leverage and revenue growth.
  * **Nubank Case Study:** Porat highlighted Brazilian startup Nubank, which targeted the 40% unbanked population, drove down lending costs across incumbents, and is credited with boosting Brazil’s GDP by ~50 basis points (on ~2–2.5% overall growth).
  * **Small Business Uplift:** More than 80% of small businesses using AI report an uplift in revenue, earnings, and hiring.
  * **Unit Economics:** Porat noted that agentic AI radically reduces unit economics, making previously unaffordable services—like bespoke financial advice—accessible to everyone.

## FedEx’s Three-Layer AI Strategy and Supply Chain Orchestration
* **Layer 1 — Network Efficiency:** Raj Subramaniam explained that FedEx uses deep learning models to optimize a physical network spanning 700 planes, 200,000 trucks, and 5,000 facilities across more than 220 countries and territories.
* **Layer 2 — Customer Differentiation & Predictability:** FedEx uses AI to shift from reporting what happened yesterday to predicting tomorrow.
  * **Predictive Maintenance:** FedEx’s **Mobius** system identifies equipment issues before they fail across its 5,000 facilities.
  * **Healthcare Leadership:** Predictive scheduling has helped FedEx become the largest express transportation provider for healthcare over the last three to four years.
* **Layer 3 — Global Supply Chain Orchestration:** With $1.9 trillion in global supply chain inefficiencies, FedEx is leveraging the 2 petabytes of data it generates daily (from 18 million packages connecting 3 million shippers and 300 million consumers) to orchestrate end-to-end supply chains.
  * **US Army Partnership:** FedEx and Alphabet/Google recently announced a combined deal to streamline the US Army’s supply chain.
* **Model-Agnostic & Unreplicable Moat:** Subramaniam stated FedEx is agnostic on open-weight versus closed or Chinese models, focusing instead on its data foundation; he dismissed fears of frontier labs reverse-engineering FedEx’s business, noting its global physical network for high-value commerce (healthcare, aerospace, defense, data centers) is impossible to replicate.

## Global Expansion, Earth AI, and Full-Stack Differentiation
* **Emerging Markets & Global South:** Following visits to Malaysia and Thailand (Bangkok), Porat emphasized that heads of state view AI as essential for economic transformation.
  * **Earth AI & Farmers:** Partnering with World Bank President Ajay Banga, Google layered Gemini onto Google Earth to create **Earth AI**, delivering pre- and post-harvest insights to farmers on Android phones that drive a low double-digit uplift in earnings, alongside crisis alerts for flash flooding and fires.
  * **Healthcare Screening:** Google’s AI-powered early screening for diabetic retinopathy has completed more than 1 million scans to date.
* **TPUs & Full-Stack Approach:** Asked whether Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s departure creates an opening for Google’s TPUs, Porat noted that global demand for TPUs is strong regardless, driven by Alphabet’s decade-long full-stack strategy (research, models, TPUs, and platforms serving over 1 billion people), though Nvidia GPUs remain a strong part of Google’s fleet.

## Geopolitics, Tariffs, and Cross-Border Compliance
* **From Basic Math to Advanced Calculus:** Subramaniam noted that moving goods across borders has grown far more complex over the past three to four years due to shifting tariff environments, requiring "advanced calculus" that FedEx embeds into customer workflows via AI.
* **Export Controls & Chip Diversion:** Addressing concerns about AI chips reaching restricted destinations, Subramaniam stated that FedEx’s digital manifest tracking ensures 100% visibility into what customers declare and that the company strictly screens anything on export control lists.
