**TL;DR**
At Microsoft Ignite 2026, Satya Nadella unveiled Microsoft’s end-to-end "frontier ecosystem" built around three imperatives: keeping humans at the center, enabling organizations to own their intelligence, and establishing verifiable trust. Key announcements include the broad availability of Work IQ, Copilot Workspaces, Microsoft Decision 1.1, Perception, Horizon DB (integrated into Fabric), Cobalt 200 VMs, and the Microsoft AI Transparency Initiative.

## Copilot & The Multiplayer Workplace
* **Copilot as the Work OS:** Brings together chat, co-work, code, autopilot (24/7 autonomous agents), and all of Office into one experience.
  * **Work IQ:** The underlying organizational database (people, relationships, emails, meetings, chats, projects) is now broadly available.
  * **Role-Based Extensions & Plugins:** New skills, connectors, and business plugins for sales, legal, finance, and service, including **Dynamics 365** and a new partnership with **Salesforce**.
  * **Copilot Workspaces:** A new multiplayer form factor enabling people, their agents, and other teammates to collaborate toward a shared goal with shared context.
* **GitHub Advancements:**
  * **Projects:** Allows developers to define a goal, connect repositories, and have Copilot coordinate task sessions and recurring work.
  * **GitHub Copilot Fusion:** A new composite model family that orchestrates multiple models; ranked #1 coding agent on **Terminal Bench**, scoring **5 points** over **Claude Core** at **less than half the cost**.

## Microsoft IQ, Foundry & The Model Ecosystem
* **Microsoft IQ:** Externalizes the system underneath Copilot as a first-class platform combining Work IQ, Fabric IQ, Foundry IQ, Web IQ, and third-party contexts, supported by **hundreds of new connectors**.
* **Microsoft Decision 1.1:** Now generally available with multimodal support, **10x faster** speed, and **200x lower cost** than using an LLM.
  * Already used by **over 4,000 customers**, including **Epic** (patient experience, clinical care, back-office) and **Xbox** (sorting tens of thousands of open-ended game launch feedback items).
* **Foundry Model Additions:** Added **GPT-62** (including **GPT-62 Astra**, which outperformed its predecessor at more than half the estimated cost), **Claude Fable 52**, **Mythos 52**, and **Meta’s** latest models.
* **MAI Model Family & Licensing:**
  * **Momentum:** **MAI Transcribe 2** requests are up **12x** since launching on OpenRouter; **MAI Code Flash** reached **3 million users** on GitHub Copilot last month; **MAI Cyber** powers **over 40%** of scans across Perception code scanning.
  * **New Models:** **Code 2** (flagship agentic coding), **Code 2 Flash** (high-volume tasks), and **Cyber 2 Flash** (integrated with Perception).
  * **MAI Licensed Weights:** Trusted partners can now access Microsoft AI model weights.
  * **Managed Agent Harness:** Foundry now offers the same harness powering Copilot, complete with continuous improvement loops and private benchmarks/evals.

## Trust, Governance & Agentic Security
* **Agent 365:** The enterprise control plane for agent governance, identity, and security already manages **50 million agents**.
  * **Agent 365 Business Analytics:** Newly announced "ERP system of AI usage" to track spend, connect it to business outcomes, and optimize ROI.
* **Perception (Generally Available):** An end-to-end agentic security system combining red team (attack simulation), blue team (detection), and green team (remediation) agents across Entra, MXC, Cloud Containers, Purview, and Defender.
  * In a recent test using the **ArtX** autonomous AI attack framework, Defender’s automated attack disruptions contained compromised accounts and isolated devices at machine speed.
* **Microsoft AI Transparency Initiative:** A three-part effort to establish open, independently verifiable industry standards for AI transparency, security, and governance—already backed by **40 organizations** and being built into MAI models, Foundry, and Copilot.

## Agent-Scale Infrastructure, Data & Silicon
* **GitHub Core Rebuild:** Decoupled storage and compute to handle agentic scale; in testing, **5,000 agents** pointed at one repo sustained **7,200 writes per second** (**180x** current throughput).
* **Azure Scale & Sustainability:** Operates **500+ data centers** across **80 Azure regions**, matching **100%** of annual global electricity consumption with renewable energy and reusing/recycling **92%** of servers and components.
* **Azure Agentic Primitives:** New micro-VMs (sub-1-second startup, full sandboxing, confidential computing enclaves), an agent grid for identity/policy-aware networking, context caching, and AKS / AKS Flex integration.
* **Horizon DB & Fabric Unification:**
  * **Horizon DB:** Distributed managed PostgreSQL database delivering **8x faster** performance than self-managed PostgreSQL, now **generally available** in Azure.
  * **Fabric Integration:** Bringing Horizon DB into Fabric unifies transactional and analytical data, allowing agents and Copilot to reason over live transactions and terabytes of historical records in real time.
* **Custom Silicon:**
  * **Cobalt 200 VMs:** Next-generation general-purpose VMs, now **generally available**.
  * **Maya 300:** Newly announced AI accelerator alongside Azure Boost and Azure Integrated HSM.
