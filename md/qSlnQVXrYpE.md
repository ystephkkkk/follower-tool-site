T-Mobile delivered a strong Q1 2026 performance, raising full-year guidance for EBITDA and free cash flow while emphasizing a "widening differentiation" strategy that leverages network superiority to drive industry-leading growth in mobile, broadband, and emerging AI sectors.

## Financial Performance and Guidance Raise
* **Updated 2026 Guidance:** On the strength of Q1 momentum, leadership raised several full-year targets:
    * **Core Adjusted EBITDA:** Now expected to be between $37.1 billion and $37.5 billion.
    * **Adjusted Free Cash Flow:** Raised to between $18.1 billion and $18.7 billion.
    * **Postpaid Net Account Additions:** Increased to a range of 950,000 to 1,050,000.
* **Service Revenue Growth:** Postpaid service revenue grew 15% year-over-year, with total service revenue up 11%—a rate CFO Peter Osvaldik noted is more than four times that of the nearest competitor.
* **ARPA Expansion:** Postpaid Average Revenue Per Account (ARPA) grew 3.9% in Q1.
    * **Premium Mix:** Over 60% of new accounts are selecting premium-tier rate plans.
* **Capital Allocation:** The company increased its 2026 stockholder return authorization by $3.6 billion, bringing the total authorization to $18.2 billion.

## Broadband and Fiber Expansion
* **Market Leadership:** T-Mobile remains the fastest-growing ISP in America, adding over 500,000 total broadband customers in Q1.
* **New Fiber Joint Ventures:** CEO Srini Gopalan announced two new JVs with infrastructure partners to acquire **GoNetSpeed, Greenlight Networks, and i3 Broadband**.
    * **Strategic Focus:** The company is prioritizing "first to fiber" assets and local scale over a specific "homes passed" target to ensure double-digit IRRs.
    * **Integration:** T-Mobile will manage all retail consumer operations for these assets, using a common IT platform to ensure a unified customer experience.
* **Business Super Broadband:** In partnership with SpaceX/Starlink, T-Mobile is launching a "Super Broadband" product for B2B customers, providing satellite-based redundancy and nationwide coverage for businesses.

## Network Innovation and Physical AI
* **5G Advanced and Robotics:** T-Mobile is connecting its 5G Advanced network to **Figure AI’s F3 humanoid robots**.
    * **Edge Inferencing:** CTO Dr. John Saw highlighted that the network's low latency and "fallow compute" capabilities at the edge are being designed specifically to support the evolution of physical AI.
* **Network-Native AI:** The company is beta-testing a "live translation" application that translates voices into 80 languages directly through the network core, requiring no third-party apps.
* **Business Applications:** The "automated ball-strike system" recently rolled out by Major League Baseball (MLB) was cited as a primary example of 5G Advanced being used for real-time challenge systems.

## Market Differentiation and Governance
* **Customer Loyalty:** T-Mobile reported an industry-leading Net Promoter Score (NPS) of 45, which is 20% higher than its closest competitor.
* **Postpaid Account Churn:** Leadership addressed a slight rise in account churn, attributing it to the mathematical weighting of the high-growth broadband business, which structurally has higher churn than wireless.
* **Merger Speculation:** Addressing rumors regarding a merger with Deutsche Telekom, Srini Gopalan clarified that such a transaction would require a "majority of the minority" approval process by disinterested shareholders.
* **Rural and Business Growth:** T-Mobile currently holds only a 24% share in smaller markets and rural areas, representing what Gopalan called a "substantial growth runway" as network perception improves.
