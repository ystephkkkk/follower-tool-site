**TL;DR**
Anthropic (ticker: ANT) made history on the Nasdaq as the first pure-play frontier AI lab to go public and the largest IPO of all time, pricing at $83 per share, opening at $111.50 (a ~33–34% premium and ~$2 trillion valuation), and raising $107.3 billion. Backed by a ~$232 billion post-IPO cash war chest and $81 billion in run-rate revenue, executives and major investors framed the listing as "day one" for enterprise AI diffusion, even as broader equity markets fluctuated on AI capex ROI concerns.

## The Historic Nasdaq Debut & Deal Mechanics
* **Record-Breaking Deal Size:** Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 per share and raised $107.3 billion (which CFO Krishna Rao noted could be nearer to $120 billion in total), including a $26 billion private placement largely backed by hyperscalers and strategic investors.
  * The offering was nearly 7x oversubscribed, with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley serving as lead underwriters (Morgan Stanley orchestrated the opening cross).
  * Shares opened at $111.50—a 33–34% premium—putting Anthropic at a fully diluted valuation of roughly $2 trillion before settling around $106–$108.
* **Retail & Market Structure:** Nasdaq President Nelson Griggs noted that ~13% of Anthropic's IPO went to retail investors, compared to ~25% for SpaceX.
* **Nasdaq's Banner Year:** Griggs highlighted that Nasdaq has captured 91% of 2026 operating company IPO proceeds, buoyed by transfers like GM this week (bringing over $4 trillion in market cap to the exchange).
  * The last five months have seen the three largest IPOs in history: SpaceX (June), SK Hynix, and Anthropic.
  * Average 2026 IPOs are up ~16% versus 15% for the broader market, though Griggs noted a "pretty slow fall" due to market volatility and the midterm election cycle, with only two clear IPO weeks remaining in December.

## Financial Performance, Compute Strategy & Balance Sheet
* **Hyper-Growth Trajectory:** CFO Krishna Rao emphasized that Anthropic is under six years old and generated its first dollar of revenue in Q1 2023, scaling to over $81 billion in run-rate revenue last month.
  * Since the start of 2025, revenue has grown 45x while compute costs grew 10x.
  * Compute and infrastructure costs fell from 10x revenue in 2023 to 55% of revenue in the first half of this year, with a long-term steady-state target of 45–50%.
* **Fortress Balance Sheet:** Entering the IPO with over $130 billion in cash and raising ~$120 billion, Anthropic now holds roughly $232 billion in cash and short-term investments.
* **"Anthropic Direct" & Diversified Compute:** Rao explained that Anthropic manages a "cone of uncertainty" around future model capabilities and demand by maintaining a diversified, fungible compute fleet across four different accelerator platforms.
  * Alongside cloud partners, the company is increasingly using **Anthropic Direct**—leasing data centers and chips separately—and is positioned to opportunistically acquire distressed cloud or data center assets if needed.

## Investor Perspectives: Growth, Diffusion & Enterprise Focus
* **Altimeter's Conviction (Pauline Yang):** Altimeter, which helped lead Anthropic's $65 billion raise in May and first invested at the $180 billion round (after passing on the early 2023 $4 billion round due to gross margin concerns), requested a $5 billion IPO allocation for its public fund and plans to hold long-term.
  * Yang noted that model releases drive step-function revenue spikes—such as Opus 3.5 adding $10 billion in October ARR, and Opus 4.5 (late 2025) turning "vibe coding" into production code.
  * She highlighted that all seven co-founders remain at Anthropic, giving it the best talent retention in the industry.
* **Baillie Gifford's "Bigger, Faster" Thesis (Peter Singlehurst):** Baillie Gifford first invested in August 2025 (Series F, G, and H) and views Anthropic as an outlier that broke the 15-year "private for longer" trend (SpaceX took 24 years to go public vs. 6 years for Anthropic).
  * Singlehurst emphasized **diffusion**—distributing existing frontier intelligence across industries—as a massive growth driver alongside new model releases, noting that Anthropic currently captures just 0.1% of aggregate Fortune 500 spend.
  * Both Yang and Singlehurst stressed that Anthropic's enterprise focus (coding, cybersecurity, law, healthcare, biotech, robotics) offers far greater pricing power and ROI than consumer AI, and both see room for multiple $5–$10 trillion winners, including OpenAI.

## Governance, Safety & Market Backdrop
* **Public Benefit Corp & Safety:** Anthropic's S-1 acknowledges existential AI risks and notes that safety safeguards may impact commercial growth. Both Yang and Singlehurst argued there is no long-term tension between safety and shareholder value, praising CEO Dario Amodei's conviction—including holding firm during an earlier entanglement with the Department of War—and noting that investors must accept that Anthropic may withhold models deemed too powerful.
* **Broader Market Volatility (Isabelle Lee):** The IPO occurred against a choppy market backdrop as investors questioned the ROI of massive AI infrastructure spending and the implications of a Democratic "blue sweep" on data center build-outs.
  * **Indices:** The Nasdaq swung from +2.5% pre-market into negative territory before hovering between +0.1% and +0.7%; the S&P 500 fell 0.3% (threatening its 6th straight loss, the longest streak since April 2024) before flipping to +0.1%.
  * **Semiconductors:** The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index rose 1.5–2%. Nvidia gained 1.5% (snapping a 5-day losing streak post-earnings), Broadcom rose 1.5%, and AMD dropped 6% (after falling as much as 11% on a short-seller report).

## Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Growth Moderation:** Altimeter expects Anthropic's revenue growth to moderate from 10x (seen in Q3) to closer to 2.5x next year, supported by underlying customer data — *Pauline Yang, Partner at Altimeter*.
* **Cybersecurity as 2027 Catalyst:** Cybersecurity—specifically AI identifying and patching code vulnerabilities—will be a major revenue driver for Anthropic in 2027, followed by biotech and robotics — *Pauline Yang, Partner at Altimeter*.
* **Multi-Trillion-Dollar Winners:** The AI market will produce more than one $5 trillion to $10 trillion company over time, with both Anthropic and OpenAI in that category — *Pauline Yang, Partner at Altimeter*.
* **Stock Volatility:** Anthropic will be a "very volatile stock" quarter-to-quarter due to lumpy model releases, non-linear compute deliveries, and policy sensitivity, though trending up and to the right over the long term — *Pauline Yang, Partner at Altimeter*.
* **OpenAI & 2027 IPO Pipeline:** OpenAI will likely follow Anthropic to the public markets soon, while Nasdaq expects a rich, diverse 2027 IPO pipeline (including power-generating companies) once regulatory clarity on data center build-outs improves — *Pauline Yang (Altimeter) & Nelson Griggs (Nasdaq President)*.
