## Campus Antisemitism and Institutional Failures

## Policy Recommendations and Federal Accountability
* **Financial Leverage:** Commission Chairman Dan Patrick and Dr. Phil McGraw argued that the most effective tool for reform is the threat of cutting federal and state funding. Patrick noted that university leaders "respond to that more than anything else."
* **Title VI Enforcement:** Witnesses called for the Department of Justice and Department of Education to mandate strict investigations into civil rights violations with set timelines, tying compliance directly to federal grants.
* **Foreign Funding Transparency:** Shabbos Kestenbaum recommended a total ban on funding from foreign adversaries (e.g., Qatar and China) to American universities, citing that Qatar is the largest state sponsor of U.S. higher education.
* **Mask Bans:** Yitzchok Frankel suggested a federal mandate or recommendation for university mask bans to prevent agitators from hiding their identities while engaging in harassment or violence.
* **Adoption of IHRA Definition:** Coach Bruce Pearl recommended that all federal and academic institutions adopt the International Holocaust Remembrance Alliance (IHRA) working definition of antisemitism to provide legal clarity.

* **Interfaith Coalitions:** Rabbi Berman highlighted a "Coalition of Angels," including leaders from HBCUs and Christian colleges, who have united to issue moral condemnations of terrorism when elite secular institutions refused to do so.

## The Role of the Executive Branch
