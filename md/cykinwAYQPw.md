* **The Role of an Investor:** **Howard Marks** states there is no reward for hyperactivity in investing; the core goal is simply buying pieces of growing companies and lending to reliable borrowers.

## AI and Inscrutability
* **Unprecedented Innovation:** **Howard Marks** notes that AI is unlike anything seen in his 60-year career, defined by its inscrutability, novelty, rapid progress, autonomy, and unpredictability.
* **The Value Investing Dilemma:** **Howard Marks** points out that traditional value investing relies on forecasting 5-year earnings, which is nearly impossible for pure-play AI companies that appeared out of nowhere.
* **Rapid Displacement Risk:** **Howard Marks** questions whether a company like Anthropic, which emerged from nowhere in 3 years, could similarly be supplanted by a new competitor in another 3 years.
* **Bursting the Bubble:** **Howard Marks** observes that recent stumbles in the AI sector have helpfully burst the illusion of absolute infallibility, making investors more thoughtful and skeptical.
