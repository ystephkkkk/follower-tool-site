## TL;DR
Decagon has scaled rapidly by building enterprise AI agents that follow complex business processes, shifting 90% of its inference to fine-tuned open-source models to optimize latency and task-specific accuracy. Co-founders Jesse Zhang and Ashwin Sreenivas explain how the company operates as a model factory, leveraging a product-driven "glass box" approach and rapid go-to-market execution to serve some of the world's largest enterprises.

## Decagon's Open-Source & Model Strategy
* **Shift to Open Source:** **Jesse Zhang** notes that Decagon moved ~90% of its workflow to open-source models primarily to optimize latency for voice agents and gain control over smaller models through fine-tuning.
* **The "Dumber" Model Advantage:** **Ashwin Sreenivas** challenges the false trade-off between expensive smart models and cheap dumber models, stating that fine-tuned smaller models outperform large state-of-the-art models on specific tasks while being faster and cheaper.
* **The Model Factory Approach:** Decagon Labs acts as a continuous model factory, training net-new models and deprecating old ones as the AI frontier advances, compressing the time from new model release to task-specific deployment.
* **Dual-Model Architecture:** While open-source fine-tuned models handle 90% of primary conversational flows, closed-source frontier models are still utilized for 10% of auxiliary, broad, exploratory tasks (such as "Duet Autopilot" which reviews millions of conversations).

## Product Evolution, "Glass Box" Approach, and Forward-Deployed Engineering
* **From Customer Support to AI Concierge:** **Jesse Zhang** shares that Decagon expanded its vision beyond customer support (inbound sales, proactive operational workflows) because the core competency is building agents that follow complex business processes rather than just answering tickets.
* **Duet and Duet Autopilot:** Decagon developed "Duet"—a secondary agent that automatically writes Agent Operating Procedures (AOPs), handles system integrations, builds tests, and monitors conversations. "Duet Autopilot" reviews live interactions and drafts continuous improvements.
* **The FDE Trap:** **Ashwin Sreenivas** warns that forward-deployed engineers (FDEs) are only necessary early on to discover new AI workflows, but companies must quickly productize those workflows rather than running a glorified consulting shop.
* **Glass Box vs. Black Box:** Decagon differentiates itself from competitors like Sierra by providing a "glass box" approach where enterprises retain control and visibility, allowing customers to spin up new workflows rapidly (e.g., a customer deploying 7 new journeys in a month compared to 3 in a year with previous vendors).

## Go-To-Market and Enterprise Sales Execution
* **Founder-Led Intensity:** **Jesse Zhang** estimates spending ~80% of his time on sales and go-to-market execution, focusing heavily on deal speed, breaking down massive enterprise deployments into manageable piecemeal wins, and staying close to the feedback loop.
* **Navigating Enterprise Bureaucracy:** Decagon maps out model risk governance, testing, and compliance procedures in granular detail during early meetings, helping regulated financial services and airline customers move from initial meeting to 100% deployment.
* **Early GTM Team:** The company built a strong early sales team consisting of nontraditional backgrounds and Ivy League athletes, utilizing top-down pressure from boards and CEOs wanting to adopt AI quickly.

## Economic Impacts and the Future of Work
* **Jevons Paradox in Customer Support:** **Ashwin Sreenivas** highlights that when customer support costs drop by 30%, companies do not automatically lay off 60% of staff; instead, latent demand drives them to make support more accessible across every page, resulting in more total support activity.
* **AI Kills Jobs, Not Careers:** **Jesse Zhang** asserts that AI will eliminate mundane, repeatable tasks that "should not be done by humans," but will not eliminate careers because people will pivot to higher-value and revenue-generating human-centric activities.
