## The 1870 Analogy and Big Tech's Debt Boom
- **Historical Parallel:** Liaquat Ahamed’s book *1873* draws a direct link between the collapse of the Northern Pacific Railway build-out and today's AI moment. 
  - Using a 1,200x adjustment factor, the $500 annual investment in US railway bonds during the 1870s boom equates to roughly **$600 billion** in 2026—matching projected spending by major tech companies.
  - Jay Cooke's aggressive retail bond sales and reliance on endless capital needs ultimately triggered the Panic of 1873 and a multi-year depression.
- **The Hyperscaler Balance Sheet Shift:** 
  - **Microsoft:** Alone among the hyperscalers, it still boasts substantial free cash flow (**$19.6 billion** last quarter) and abides by the rule that CapEx isn't funded by debt (cited *1873* on its recent earnings call).
  - **Oracle, Meta, Alphabet, and Amazon:** Issued a combined **$80 billion** in debt between September and November to build out infrastructure.
  - **Escalating Issuances:** After raising a combined **$108 billion in 2025**, these four companies raised **$194 billion** by July 7th.
  - **Deteriorating Terms:** Spreads are rising, 86% of bonds issued in 2026 are trading at higher yields than at issuance, and cover for recent issuances has fallen to **less than 2x** from **5x in February**.
- **Google’s Equity Move:** At the beginning of June, Google announced it would raise **$85 billion in equity**, including a **$10 billion** special issuance to Berkshire Hathaway, serving as a powerful signal that expected demand outstrips current expectations.

## Google’s DeepMind Turmoil and Infrastructure Pivot
- **Leadership Shakeup:** 
  - Demis Hassabis was kicked upstairs to chairman (no longer running day-to-day operations), and Gemini co-lead / chief scientist Jeff Dean departed alongside a host of reinforcement learning researchers.
  - **SemiAnalysis Assessment:** Declared that Gemini is cooked, stating that Google's odds of reaching state-of-the-art again have dropped to zero due to a bureaucratic, painfully slow, and strategically timid culture.
- **Google Cloud Triumph:** 
  - Thomas Kurian won internal turf wars over compute allocation.
  - GCP revenue growth accelerated to **82% year-over-year** (compared to 62% the previous quarter and 32% a year ago), with margins reaching **36%** (up from 33% last quarter and 21% a year ago).
  - Sundar Pichai confirmed on the earnings call that short-term costly bridge deals (renting third-party capacity) are designed to support major cloud customers like Anthropic in multi-year opportunities.
- **TPU Strategy and Anthropic Partnerships:** 
  - More than **20% of total TPU shipments from Q3 2026 to Q4 2027** are being sold directly to Anthropic.
  - TPUs are positioned as general-purpose infrastructure (serving customers like Citadel and the Department of Energy) and offer a significant cost advantage over Nvidia GPUs in a world where compute is commoditized.

## Nvidia’s "Investable Asset Class" Financing Platform
- **New Financing Platforms:** Jensen Huang announced partnerships with **Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, and KKR** to establish independent financing platforms mobilizing over **$500 billion** of third-party capital over time.
  - **Shift in Model:** Moves the industry from project-by-project chip buying to financing AI factories as productive infrastructure backed by repeatable platforms and long-term institutional capital.
- **Nvidia's Backstop Risk:** 
  - Nvidia is backstopping opportunities with up to **25% residual value-based financing**, effectively offering a hidden price cut and putting its own profits on the line to reduce capital costs for data center builders.
  - This structure relies on safety-seeking assets like insurance floats and pension funds, carrying considerably higher risk than equity if AI revenue fails to materialize.
- **Software Moat Pressures:** 
  - Anthropic has not been dependent on CUDA for years, and OpenAI is moving in that direction for inference. 
  - If frontier labs successfully escape CUDA, Nvidia's profits will face severe squeezing, amplifying the urgency behind Huang's open-source advocacy and aggressive financing plays.
