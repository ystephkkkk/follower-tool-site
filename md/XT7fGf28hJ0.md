## TL;DR
AMD opening keynote at IFA 2026, presented by Jack Huynh, outlining the transition into the "Era of Personal AI" driven by local device compute, unified memory architectures, agentic workflows, and an open software ecosystem.

## AMD's Computing & AI Vision
* **Evolution of Computing:** 
  * Computing has progressed from desks to pockets, and is now putting intelligence everywhere.
  * AI is described as "the new electricity," shifting computing from executing commands to actively understanding user intent and collaborating as a partner.
* **Global and Everyday Impact:** 
  * **Space Exploration:** AMD technology powers systems inside the Orion spacecraft for the Artemis II mission.
  * **Healthcare:** Powers intuitive robotic-assisted surgery systems (translating a 3mm hand movement into 1mm while filtering tremors) and cancer detection algorithms in Finland's LUMI supercomputer.
  * **Entertainment:** Powers EPYC processors and Threadripper for cloud platforms, PlayStation 5 Pro (Spider-Man 2), and game development pipelines.
* **The Agentic AI Shift:** 
  * AI has evolved from conversational models to reasoning systems, and finally to **agentic AI** (capable of planning, using tools, checking work, and operating continuously in 2025).
  * **Productivity Multiplier:** A single user orchestrating an agentic team can dramatically scale output, but this creates a massive compute and token consumption demand.
  * **Token Growth Projections:** Monthly global AI token processing jumped from 0.7 quadrillion to 1.7 quadrillion in one year, with estimates reaching **120 quadrillion AI tokens per month by 2030**.
  * **Economic Pressure:** **93% of companies are exceeding their AI budgets**, highlighting the need for efficient local compute to avoid unsustainable cloud token costs (e.g., high-volume cloud models can cost nearly €100,000 per user annually).

## Next-Generation AI PCs & Hardware
* **The Pillars of Personal AI:** Local compute, privacy and control, and personal context.
* **Gorgon Processors & Strix Halo:**
  * **Efficiency Gains:** Newer, smaller models match or beat larger cloud counterparts (e.g., Qwen 3.5 with 9 billion parameters beating older models; open-source Laguna S2.1 beating cloud SANA 5 on software engineering benchmarks).
  * **Strix Halo Specs:** Features **120 gigabytes of unified memory**, enabling local execution of models up to **200 billion parameters** directly on a desktop or laptop.
  * **Local vs. Cloud Economics:** Eliminates per-token cloud charges, allowing users to run parallel agents locally (up to 10 simultaneous local agents on Strix Halo).
* **Gorgon Halo & Sunday (HP ZBook):**
  * **Gorgon Halo Specs:** Increased unified memory from 128 to **192 gigabytes**, running 200 to 300 billion parameter models locally (such as GLM 5.3 Flash outperforming cloud Fable 5 benchmarks).
  * **Hardware Launches:** Lenovo introduced the **ThinkCentre X** in Berlin powered by Gorgon Halo. HP announced **Sunday**, a next-gen ZBook built for personal AI featuring **190 gigabytes of unified memory**, capable of rendering real-time 3D worlds from a single prompt locally.
* **Personal Context Layer:** 
  * Beyond applications, models, and compute, personal AI introduces a **Context Layer** that understands user intent, history, and goals to deliver tailored outcomes.

## Microsoft Partnership & Developer Tools
