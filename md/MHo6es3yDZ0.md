## **The $3 Billion Carnegie Mellon Donation & Strategy**
* **Record-Breaking Financial Commitment:** 
  * Citadel founder Ken Griffin is making a $3 billion gift to Carnegie Mellon University (CMU), marking the largest single gift ever committed to a U.S. university and doubling his total philanthropic investments in CMU.
* **Allocation of Funds:**
  * **$2 billion** is designated to build and establish a new campus in Miami.
  * **$1 billion** goes to the main campus in Pittsburgh, which includes naming the institution's top-ranked computer science program the **Kenneth C. Griffin School of Computer Science**.
* **Institutional Rankings & ROI:** 
  * According to Ken Griffin, U.S. News rankings place CMU **number three** in the country for return on investment on a college education, propelling the school to **number 14 overall** in the U.S.

## **A New Model for Higher Education**
* **Breaking Down Silos:** 
  * According to President Farnam Jahanian, the new Miami campus and educational model will organize around grand societal challenges (such as human health, national security, cyber security, and energy/climate resiliency) rather than traditional departments, colleges, and schools.
* **Balancing Technical Rigor and the Humanities:** 
  * Ken Griffin emphasizes the need for well-rounded students who combine foundational technical and scientific knowledge with human skills like problem-solving, communication, and ethical application.
  * President Jahanian and Griffin noted CMU's deep roots in the humanities, highlighting alumni achievements including **nearly 70 Tony Award winners**, the first school of drama in the U.S. (founded in **1914**), and artist Andy Warhol.
* **Urban Re-industrialization and Economic Impact:** 
  * Pittsburgh’s economic renaissance is anchored by CMU, which currently features **100 different robotics companies** driving the re-industrialization of the U.S.
  * Griffin intends for the new CMU Miami campus to similarly transform Miami by attracting hundreds of technology companies, startups, and career opportunities.

## **Higher Education Reform and Core Mission**
* **Shifting the Campus Narrative:** 
  * Ken Griffin stated that American campuses have improved over the past **two years** (since **2024**), noting that the pendulum is shifting back to the center after universities briefly "lost the narrative" of preparing students for the workforce.
* **The Ethos of College:** 
  * Griffin criticized viewing college as "four years of fun" or "four years of an entitlement," arguing that the true ethos should be preparing young people to make a tangible difference in America.
  * President Jahanian affirmed that universities must stay true to their core mission of educating the next generation, creating knowledge to benefit society, and driving economic and cultural impact.
