## TL;DR
Tesla delivered a strong Q3 2026, driven by soaring FSD demand, exponential growth in Austin Cybercab miles, and an almost sold-out 2027 Megapack pipeline. Management raised FY2026 capex guidance to approximately $20B while projecting massive multi-year investments in autonomy, robotics, and the Tarafab semiconductor facility.

## Automotive & FSD Growth
- **Record Deliveries & Backlog:** Achieved two consecutive quarters of vehicle deliveries exceeding 480k units, exiting Q3 with the highest backlog in four years.
  - Model Y was the #1 selling BEV in Europe and passenger car in South Korea, alongside delivery records in Australia, Japan, and Taiwan.
- **FSD Attach Rates:** Over 75% of North American deliveries included a paid FSD subscription sign-up at delivery (20% higher than Q2).
  - Global paid FSD customers surpassed **1.7 million**, with over 50% on subscriptions for the first time.
- **Financial Performance:** Automotive gross margins excluding credits rose sequentially from 16.3% to 18.1%, aided by a higher APAC delivery mix.

## Robotaxi & Cybercab Expansion
- **Austin Ramp:** Austin Cybercab fleet grew by a factor of 10 since launch one month prior, scaling cumulative weekly mileage exponentially.
- **Geographic Rollout:** Dallas launch scheduled for next month, followed by software scaling to other cities and Model Y robotaxis via the V15 release.
- **Target Economics:** Long-term target remains **30 cents per paid robotaxi mile**, with fleet size and density scaling rapidly over the coming months.

## Optimus Humanoid Robot Progress
- **Production Infrastructure:** Early production has started on a high-volume line in Fremont, housing the vast majority of a **1-million-unit-per-year** production line.
  - A second factory is under construction in Austin.
- **Technical Hurdles:** The hand and bespoke gears remain the most challenging electromechanical areas, requiring design finetuning for wear-and-tear and delicate handling.
- **AI Integration:** AI5 chip integration planned for approximately April of next year, delivering an order of magnitude more compute than AI4.

## Energy & Semiconductor Infrastructure
- **Energy Storage Momentum:** Delivered 13.7 GWh of energy storage in Q3 (second best quarter), with energy gross margins improving from 20.4% to 33.1%.
  - Mega Packs for **2027 are almost entirely sold out**, supported by in-house LFP battery deliveries.
- **Tarifab Semiconductor Project:** The Austin-based research lab is progressing quickly, with machinery commissioning in the first half of next year.
  - Aiming for a first test chip around May or June, volume useful chip production around **Q3 2028**, and multi-decade capex scaling into the tens and eventually north of $100 billion long-term.

## Financial Position & Guidance
- **Cash & Liquidity:** Ended Q3 with positive free cash flow of **over $1.5 billion** and a cash/investment balance of **over $45 billion**.
- **Capex Revision:** Full-year 2026 capex guidance revised to approximately **$20 billion**, funded primarily from operating cash flows.
- **Credit Facilities:** Secured capacity to borrow **up to $30 billion** to support expected capex growth, with no draws planned for the current calendar year.
- **P&L Impacts:** Net income was impacted by a mark-to-market SpaceX holding reduction of **$142 million**, offset by a Bitcoin mark-to-market gain of approximately **$290 million**. Services and other margins reached an all-time high of 15.9%.
