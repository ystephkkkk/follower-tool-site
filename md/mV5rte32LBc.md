## TL;DR
Runway announced major expansions across generative video, enterprise marketing, robotics, and interface design at its SF summit, highlighting exponential adoption and introducing products like Runway Ads, Praxis-1, and Solaris.

## Company Growth and Vision for World Models
- **Explosive Business Growth:** Anastasis Germanidis noted that since March, Runway's revenue has tripled, September marked the highest growth month in the company's eight-year history, and model usage has grown nearly 10 times since the beginning of the year.
- **The Shift to World Models:** Runway positions itself as a world model company, arguing that language models are limited by text distilled from human knowledge, whereas video models trained at scale learn directly from raw data and observation to become learned approximators of physics.

## Runway Ads: Autonomous Performance Marketing
- **Product Launch:** **Anthony Maggio** announced **Runway Ads**, an autonomous engine designed to streamline performance marketing by automating the creative production loop.
- **Internal Impact:** Performance marketing campaigns at Runway scaled from launching ~70 new ads per week in July to ~900 ads per week by early September after building the tool.
- **Performance Metrics:** Return on ad spend doubled, cost per subscriber decreased by 41%, and conversion rates increased by 34%.
- **Capabilities & Automation:** Connects to existing ad accounts and brand assets to produce channel-ready creative across multiple languages and audiences; an integrated agent monitors performance, creates variants of winning ads, and publishes directly to platforms like Meta and Google while keeping human teams in control.
- **Early Partners:** Tested with early design partners including Lovable.

## Robotics and Praxis-1
- **The Robotics Bottleneck:** **Andy Chen** highlighted that real-world robot data is scarce, slow, and expensive to collect, while traditional hand-built simulators struggle with contact-rich manipulation and long-tail edge cases.
- **GWM Worlds Validation:** Runway's GWM Worlds model predicts how a robot policy will perform in the real world with a **0.95 correlation**, outperforming expensive 3D reconstruction approaches.
- **Product Launch:** **Praxis-1** is an open-weights world action model that turns video pre-training into control for real robots by learning from third-party video of humans performing tasks rather than relying solely on scarce robot demonstrations.
- **Adaptability:** Works across any embodiment (arms, grippers, robots) with light fine-tuning and a few minutes of demonstrations.
- **Early Partners & Release:** Tested with early partners **Noble Machines**, **Ultra**, and **Standard Bots**; open weights will be released publicly in the coming months.

## Digital World Models, Characters, and Solaris
- **Runway Characters:** **Kamil Sindi** demonstrated Runway Characters, a live, interactive single-player world model featuring a planner and a world model renderer that allows users to create, steer, and give knowledge bases or tool-calling capabilities to avatars via Runway Dev.
- **GWM Worlds 2:** A research preview showcasing multi-player environments where users can spin up shared worlds for humans and agents using natural language "world prompts" without code or game engines.
- **Solaris Interface World Model:** **Solaris** is a new operating system interface world model that generates interactive software interfaces frame by frame in real time, converting static websites into dynamic, intent-driven experiences (e.g., storefronts becoming dressing rooms) without code; available soon via Runway Dev.

## Predictions & Forward Calls
- **Anastasis Germanidis:** By the end of this decade, interactive world models will achieve human-scale world simulation, creating interactive worlds indistinguishable from the real world.
- **Anastasis Germanidis:** Within about 10 years, models will be able to simulate physics and biology well enough to solve major scientific challenges.
- **Andy Chen:** Within five years, most new robot skills will be learned within a world model before a robot ever tries them in the physical world.
