## Data Center Infrastructure: Helios & Instinct GPUs
- **Helios AI Rack Launch:** In full production with shipments starting late Q3 and ramping in Q4 2026. Tom Brown confirmed Anthropic is deploying up to **2 gigawatts of Helios**, while Satchin Katti confirmed OpenAI will deploy Helios at massive scale starting late 2026 through 2027.
- **Hardware Specs & Metrics:** 
  - **MI455 Accelerator:** Features 320 billion transistors built on TSMC 2nm and 3nm, 12 compute/IO chiplets with 432 GB of HBM4 memory connected via 3D chip-stack packaging.
  - **Performance vs. Competitors:** Helios delivers 15% more compute, 50% more HBM4 capacity/bandwidth, 50% more scale-out bandwidth, and up to **30% more tokens per dollar** at a fixed rack power.
- **Deep Partnerships & Custom Silicon:**

  - **Cerebras:** CEO Andrew Feldman announced a disaggregated low-latency inference solution combining Helios with the Cerebras wafer-scale engine to deliver 5x higher throughput.

## Server CPUs: The Venice (Zen 6) Family
- **Divided Server Workloads:** Compute is splitting into GPU servers (speed/IO), agent sandboxes (density/cores-per-watt), and general-purpose enterprise servers (efficiency/TCO).
- **Venice Family Details:** Built on TSMC's 2nm process with 203 billion transistors and up to 512 threads per socket (Zen 6 core):
  - **Venice HF:** 96-core processor optimized to feed GPU host nodes (up to 5 GHz).
  - **Venice 256-core:** Highest compute density in the industry, built with 8 compute chiplets (32 cores each) for agentic sandboxes.
  - **Venice 128-core:** Tuned for lower-cost, lower-power enterprise and general-purpose workloads.
  - **Future Roadmap:** Verando (next year for AI) and Venice X (utilizing 3D V-Cache stacking for HPC).
- **Performance Benchmarks:** Venice delivers up to **1.8 times more tokens per second** than x86 competitors in GPU servers, **2x more agents per watt** in sandboxes, and **2.8x more agents per watt** than leading ARM processors at the chip level. Venice is in full production with rollouts in Q4.
