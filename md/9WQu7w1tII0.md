**TL;DR**
Republican gubernatorial candidate Steve Hilton proposes a radical "pro-worker" overhaul of California, centered on eliminating state income tax for households earning under $100,000 and slashing $80 billion in annual government waste through a "California Department of Government Efficiency" (CalDOGE).

## Fiscal Reform & The "CalDOGE" Initiative
*   **Tax Structural Overhaul:** Hilton’s "pro-growth" plan eliminates state income tax for the first $100,000 of household income and implements a 7.5% flat tax for all income above that threshold.
    *   **Affordability Impact:** Approximately seven million households (over one-third of the state) would pay $0 in state income tax. 
*   **The CalDOGE Audit:** To fund tax cuts, Hilton plans to reduce state spending by 18.5%, returning the budget to 2019-2020 levels.
    *   **Fraud Identification:** Hilton claims to have identified $425 billion in "fraud, waste, and abuse" over the last five years.
    *   **Example of Mismanagement:** Out of $1 billion allocated for solar panels for low-income housing, only $72 million reached the actual projects; the remaining $928 million went to political nonprofits and "environmental justice" campaigns.
*   **Billionaire Tax Opposition:** Hilton warns that proposed wealth taxes are driving the tech ecosystem and "wealth creation" out of the state, putting California near an economic "cliff edge."

## Housing, Energy, and "Climate Dogma"
*   **The Cost of Building:** California homes cost 2–3x more to build than those in neighboring states due to three factors: union power, litigation (CEQA), and "climate dogma."
    *   **CEQA Litigation:** 70% of California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) lawsuits are used to block housing. Hilton notes unions use these lawsuits as leverage to force "Project Labor Agreements" that mandate union-only shops at 2–3x market wages.
*   **Energy Incoherence:** California imports nearly 80% of its oil—primarily from Iraq and the Amazon rainforest—despite having abundant local reserves.
    *   **Fuel Prices:** High gas prices are driven by regulatory refusals to issue drilling permits. Hilton aims for "$3 gas" by doubling local production every two years.
    *   **Refinery Decay:** The state has dropped from 40 refineries to just seven, largely because current regulations make refining local "heavy crude" nearly impossible.

## Education and Public Safety
*   **Education Outcomes:** California spends roughly $27,000 per student annually, yet 65% of students fail to meet basic math standards and over 50% fail English standards.
    *   **Proposed Reforms:** Hilton advocates for universal "phonics-based" reading instruction and "school choice" to break the union monopoly.
    *   **Accountability:** Implementing a "repeat third grade" policy for students who cannot pass basic reading benchmarks, a model that successfully revitalized Mississippi’s education system.
*   **Crime and "Catch and Release":** Hilton argues that closing state prisons has forced violent criminals into overcrowded county jails, leading to a "catch and release" culture where local law enforcement feels paralyzed.
    *   **Policy Shift:** Stop the prison closure program and increase capacity to ensure criminals are punished and rehabilitated rather than cycled back onto the streets.
*   **Homelessness Mandates:** Hilton supports a "rehab or jail" approach for the 80% of the homeless population struggling with addiction or mental health issues.
    *   **Infrastructure:** Plans to build large-scale mental health facilities by waiving the federal "16-bed rule" (IMD rule) that currently prevents Medicaid reimbursement for large institutions.

## The Path to a Republican Victory
*   **Working-Class Coalition:** Hilton believes he can win by building a multi-racial, working-class coalition focused on "Cal-fordable" policies (lower gas, lower electric bills, and no tax on tips).
*   **The "Top Two" Primary:** Currently leading the field on the Republican side; he expects a general election matchup against a Democrat that will serve as a referendum on "Change vs. More of the Same."
*   **Voter Turnout Strategy:** Hilton notes that Donald Trump received 6.1 million votes in California in 2024 without campaigning; Hilton estimates he needs 5.9 million votes to win the 2026 midterm.
