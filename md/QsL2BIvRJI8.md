## TL;DR
General Matter, led by CEO Scott Nolan, is building a domestic uranium enrichment facility in Paducah, Kentucky, to supply High-Assay Low-Enriched Uranium (HALEU) and replace Russian imports ahead of a 2028 Congressional ban.

## Background & Geopolitics of Uranium Supply
* **Historical Post-Cold War Shift:** Following World War II and the Cold War (when the US produced over 90% of global supply), the US shut down its domestic enrichment plants and relied on the "Megatons to Megawatts" program, blending down imported Russian warheads.
* **Current Import Dependency:** The US currently sources about a quarter of its enriched uranium from Russia (operating under waivers until the ban takes effect), with the remainder supplied by European providers like France and the Urenco consortium.
* **The 2028 Ban:** Congress passed a ban on imports of Russian uranium that goes into effect on January 1, 2028, creating an urgent domestic gap that General Matter aims to fill.

## Nuclear Fuel, Reactors, and the HALEU Shift
* **The 5-Step Fuel Stack:** Uranium is (1) mined/milled from the ground into U3O8, (2) converted into a gas, (3) separated/enriched to increase U-235 density, (4) turned back into a solid, and (5) manufactured into fuel pellets.
* **Traditional vs. Advanced Reactors:** 
  * Traditional light water reactors (such as the 94 grid reactors providing 20% of US power) use low-enriched uranium (LEU) at 3-5% enrichment and require massive containment domes.
  * Modern Small Modular Reactors (SMRs) and advanced reactors require HALEU (High-Assay Low-Enriched Uranium) enriched to just under 20% (specifically 19.75%).
* **Why HALEU Matters:** Higher enrichment allows for a much smaller core size, enabling factory-built modular reactors and significantly longer refueling cycles (e.g., 5-to-10-year intervals) for remote or off-grid deployments.
* **Regulatory Oversight:** Commercial nuclear operations are regulated by the NRC (Nuclear Regulatory Commission), which Nolan notes focuses efficiently on safety, security, and low environmental impact.

## General Matter’s Execution Strategy & Paducah Facility
* **Founding & Team:** Founded in late 2023 by Scott Nolan (after 10 years at Founders Fund), General Matter has scaled to just over 200 people with headquarters in Los Angeles.
* **Facility Location:** Building a first commercial facility in Paducah, Kentucky—on the site of the last US enrichment facility (shut down in 2013)—chosen for community familiarity, existing talent, and pre-characterized DOE site environmental benefits.
* **In-House Construction:** Copied the Tesla playbook by establishing an in-house engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) general contracting team rather than outsourcing facility construction.

## Nuclear Cost Structures and the 1970s Stasis
* **Root Cause of Nuclear Stasis:** Nolan argues that high costs and stalled nuclear deployment were primarily driven by 1970s macroeconomic shocks (oil price spikes, mortgage interest rates hitting 18% in 1981) rather than safety panics like Three Mile Island or Chernobyl.
* **The Cost-Plus Trap:** Traditional aerospace and nuclear primes operated under cost-plus models that lacked downward economic pressure to innovate, reduce prices, or execute rapidly.
* **Competitive Advantages:** General Matter avoids legacy stagnation by applying decades of untapped post-1950s/60s technology improvements, clean-sheet design, and lean execution modeled after SpaceX.
