## Partner Ecosystem Roles: Sophos vs. OpenAI
- **Sophos Domain Expertise:** Contributes deep cybersecurity know-how, threat intelligence, incident response playbooks, and decades of operational experience to combat attacks at scale.
- **OpenAI Frontier Intelligence:** Programs like Daybreak provide Sophos with cutting-edge frontier intelligence, allowing the company to scale its domain expertise and stay a step ahead of the attacker community to protect its 650,000 customers.

## AI-Powered Threat Investigation with Sophos Fusion
- **Data Ingestion & Scale:** The Sophos Fusion system—which powers Managed Detection and Response (MDR), EDR, and XDR products—gathers sensor data from over 500 third-party and proprietary integrations generating trillions of events daily.
- **Case Filtering:** Trillions of daily events are distilled down into 1,000 to 2,000 cases per day, investigated across nine different security operations centers on behalf of customers.
- **Investigation Agents:** Cases are fed into planning models that create a plan-execute-review loop, gathering customer context, detections, indicators of compromise, and threat landscape knowledge to conduct investigations and draft analyst summaries.
- **Efficiency & Talent Scaling:** By leveraging Daybreak-powered agents, Sophos scales compute rather than personnel, helping alleviate the severe industry-wide shortage of cybersecurity talent and 24/7 security operations staff.

## Customer Control and Human Judgment
- **Flexible Operating Modes:** To match varying customer comfort levels and expertise, Sophos structures its service around three modes:
  - **Notify:** The SOC investigates fully but never takes independent response actions, only guiding and recommending to the customer.
  - **Collaborate:** The SOC conducts the full investigation and works directly with the customer, never acting without their direct involvement.
  - **Authorize:** Sophos is fully authorized to take response actions on behalf of the customer to keep them protected.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards:** Agents handle a large volume of investigations, but human analysts remain essential for supervising actions, making trust-based decisions, and handling anything potentially destructive in a customer environment.
