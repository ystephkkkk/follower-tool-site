## Ground-Level Power Constraints & Telepocalypse Perspectives
* **Data Center Power Bottlenecks:** **Steve Eisman** shared insights from subscriber **Joseph** (an electrical contractor building data centers), who stated that power is the true binding constraint rather than GPUs, HBM, or capital:
    * **Labor Premiums:** Builders are paying workers an extra $150 per day just to show up, on top of $65/hour wages.
    * **Lead Times:** Newer transmission lines are 24+ months out, and substations require 1–2 years, resulting in widespread "stranded CapEx" where data centers lack power poles and electrical connections.
    * **Corporate Positioning:** **Joseph** believes Amazon and Meta will feel the squeeze more than Microsoft and Google (who are better planned and managed), while CoreWeave and Oracle are "way back on the gonna get power next line."
* **Starlink Mobile Viability:** **Steve Eisman** evaluated viewer **Wayne**'s feedback regarding the "Telepocalypse" (Starlink entering terrestrial mobile):
    * **Satellite Hurdles:** Device-to-satellite communication requires massive constellations of very low-orbit satellites with short lifespans, making upkeep prohibitively expensive compared to terrestrial cell towers.
    * **Market Niche:** Satellite connectivity is primarily beneficial for remote areas with a small premium-paying consumer base, leading Wayne to conclude terrestrial cellular companies remain safe for now.
