**TL;DR**
W5 Solutions delivered record-breaking Q4 2025 results, achieving 27% annual organic growth and a significant pivot to profitability for its Power segment. The company is on track to meet its 2027 targets of 1 billion SEK in revenue and 10% EBIT margins, supported by a strong 1.5 book-to-bill ratio.

## Financial Performance and 2027 Targets
*   **Record Growth:** Net sales for the full year 2025 reached 492 million SEK, representing a 27% increase over 2024.
*   **Profitability Milestone:** The group achieved 38 million SEK in EBIT for the full year, resulting in an ~8% margin, nearing the long-term goal of 10%.
*   **Quarterly Outperformance:** Q4 2025 saw exceptional margins as turnover more than doubled compared to the year's average quarter.
    *   **Revenue Recognition:** High Q4 margins were driven largely by the **Training** business area, which uses "point-in-time" revenue recognition upon delivery.
*   **2027 Roadmap:** The company remains committed to reaching 1 billion SEK in turnover by 2027 through a strategy of at least 20% organic growth supplemented by targeted M&A.

## Business Area Analysis
*   **Integration:** Remains a high-performing segment with strong profitability.
    *   **New Scope:** Secured a major contract for system integration services involving the design and physical integration of complex third-party systems onto military platforms.
*   **Power:** Marked a significant turnaround in Q4.
    *   **Profitability Pivot:** After struggling with a "top-line problem" and negative profitability in the first half of 2025, the segment gained momentum in August, turning its backlog into profitable deliveries by year-end.
*   **Training:** Highly active in Q4 deliveries. The segment offers a full range of hardware and software for live fire training, spanning both defense/security and sports/hunting markets.

## Order Backlog and Market Demand
*   **Strong Pipeline:** Total order intake for 2025 was 730 million SEK, with a closing backlog of 566 million SEK.
*   **Book-to-Bill Ratio:** The company maintained a 1.5 book-to-bill ratio, providing high visibility for 2026.
    *   **Delivery Timeline:** The absolute majority of the current backlog is scheduled for delivery within the 2026 calendar year.
*   **Segment Variation:** Backlog structures differ by area; Training has a short order-to-delivery cycle, while Power manages large contracts spanning multiple years.

## Operational Outlook and M&A Strategy
*   **Capacity Management:** Management confirmed that current facilities are sufficient to support 20% growth in 2026 without relocation, though headcount must increase to meet demand.
*   **M&A Environment:** The company is actively pursuing targets in the Nordics with a defense-heavy customer base. Competition for acquisitions is high, but W5 views its deep market network as a competitive advantage for sellers.
*   **Cash Flow Challenges:** Operating cash flow for the year was negative due to working capital requirements. Management noted that capital tie-up is a standard challenge in defense, where prepayments are rare, but improved profitability will help mitigate this.
