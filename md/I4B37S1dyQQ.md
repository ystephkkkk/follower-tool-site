**TL;DR:** Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang explains that Nvidia's founding technology in 1993 was completely wrong, but the company survived by confronting reality, learning from textbooks, and focusing on accelerated computing rather than chips. He advises founders to embrace systems thinking, adopt an attitude of "how hard can it be?", and rely on resilience to build breakthrough companies.

## The Origin Story: Wrong Technology and Survival
* **Flawed First Principle:** Founded in 1993 with the vision of turning every personal computer into a game console using a novel 3D graphics algorithm, which Huang realized in 1995 was "exactly wrong."
* **The Fry's Textbooks:** Facing impending business failure and lacking know-how, Huang went to Fry's and bought three textbooks on OpenGL and pipeline design, giving them to engineers to pivot the company.
* **The Sega Contract:** Nvidia had a $12 million contract with Sega to build the console after the Saturn (which became Dreamcast), but Huang had to inform Sega CEO Irimajiri-san that the technology did not work. 
* **The Lifesaving $5M:** Huang asked to keep the remaining contract money despite failing to deliver; Irimajiri agreed, keeping Nvidia alive until its $300 million IPO in 1999.

## The Big Bet: Accelerating Algorithm Domains
* **Universal Function Approximator:** Upon seeing AlexNet 15 years ago, Huang recognized deep learning not as a single model, but as a universal function approximator capable of learning any imprecise function.
* **The Five-Layer Cake:** Nvidia realized early that success required reinventing the entire industrial stack simultaneously—processors, middleware, algorithms, and applications—to accelerate domain-specific algorithms like molecular dynamics and inverse physics.
* **Open Source Advocacy:** Drawing parallels to Linux, Kubernetes, and PyTorch, Huang emphasized the vital importance of open source and open weights (such as OpenClaw, Hermes, and PyTorch) to let every company build its own domain-specific AI.

## Founder Mode and Organizational Philosophy
* **First-Principles Curiosity:** Huang operates from continuous curiosity, diving deep into technical weeds, reading papers, and speaking directly with scientists to maintain a tactile sensation of fast-moving technology.
* **F1 Car Analogy:** Founders build a company/car to fit their own driving style rather than adopting conventional management techniques; when a founder leaves, the next leader can reshape the organization.
* **Enduring Mindset:** Huang rejects anxiety and fear, advising founders to approach every unknown challenge with the question: *"How hard can it be?"*

## The Impact of AI on Jobs and the Economy
* **Tasks vs. Jobs:** AI automates cognitive tasks, not entire jobs. Increased productivity drives ambition and backlog reduction, leading to employment growth.
    * **Software Engineering:** Code generation tasks are automated, but software engineering jobs grew 10% year over year.
    * **Radiology:** Scan reading tasks are automated, yet radiology jobs grew 20% over several years due to higher patient capacity.
    * **Legal:** Paralegal jobs are expanding as law firms process larger volumes of lawsuits.

## Physical AI and Robotics
* **The Chat GPT Moment for Robots:** The breakthrough of generative video and physics-grounded reinforcement learning for robot articulation occurred inside labs a couple of years ago.
* **Physical AI Stack:** Nvidia developed foundational world models (like Cosmos) and simulation environments (Isaac Sim) to execute real-to-sim and sim-to-real training.
* **Current Scale and Projections:** Nvidia's autonomous vehicle and physical AI business has reached approximately **$10 billion**, and Huang projects it will become a **$100 billion business** in less than 10 years.
