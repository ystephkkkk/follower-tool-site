Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang and Salesforce CEO Marc Benioff discussed the transition of enterprises into "agentic AI companies" at Dreamforce 2026, emphasizing that AI serves as a new technological layer that drives productivity and transforms every industry rather than destroying jobs.

## Nvidia's Evolution and the New Industrial Revolution
* **The AI Infrastructure Layer:** Jensen Huang compared the current AI shift to historical industrial revolutions powered by electricity and the internet, positioning artificial intelligence as a planetary infrastructure layer where humanity can "know everything and do anything."
* **Transformation into AI Factories:** Nvidia evolved from a pure GPU company into a full-stack AI infrastructure provider that builds giant systems capable of turning enterprise data and electricity into intelligence.
* **The Rise of Agentic Enterprises:** Every enterprise, company, and country is shifting toward becoming an AI company driven by agentic technology.

* **Nvidia's Frontier Model Suite:** Nvidia leads five distinct frontier model categories, all completely open:
  * **NeMoTRON:** Language models
  * **Cosmos:** World foundation models

  * **BioNeMo:** Complex protein models
  * **Groot:** Humanoid and robotics models
* **Agentic Harness Systems and OpenShell:** Nvidia created completely open agentic harness systems and runtimes, alongside **OpenShell**—the world's first secure sandbox and runtime—to enable safe enterprise deployment at scale.
* **Custom Enterprise Models:** While closed models remain vital, every company will have the capability to build private, customized models, exemplified by Nvidia's work with Salesforce.

## AI Pacing, Safety, and Market Realities
* **Engineering Safety:** Huang stated that safety is "job one" but must be treated as an engineering and computing problem rather than requiring entirely new laws or regulations.
* **Confidence-Driven Pacing:** Companies should innovate as fast as possible, but pause and pace themselves if they lack confidence in a product's functionality, capability, or safety. Innovation speed and safety are not mutually exclusive.
* **Rapid Technology Iteration:** Huang advised users not to give up on AI if it initially meets only 80% of expectations, noting that the supporting industry improves the technology continuously over the span of a few weeks.
