### TL;DR
Nvidia introduced an end-to-end physical AI and healthcare robotics platform at GTC 2026—anchored by the open-source Open H data set, Groot H action policies, Cosmos world foundation models, Isaac for healthcare, Holoscan 4.0, and IGX Thor—designed to bridge the gap between digital intelligence and physical patient care.

## Physical AI Foundation & The Three Tectonic Shifts
- **Core Need in Healthcare:** While 160,000 hospitals, 72,000 procedures, and 8 million medical devices are increasingly digitized, care is ultimately delivered physically across unpredictable operating room environments and surgical workflows.
- **Pillar 1: World Foundation Models:** Models like Nvidia Cosmos understand and generate complex medical environments, enabling post-trained variants such as Cosmos H for surgical scenes and clinical workflows.
- **Pillar 2: Robotic Policy Models:** Grounded in Groot and Groot H (announced at GTC), these models translate visual perception and reasoning into generalizable action policies for medical robots.
- **Pillar 3: Physics Simulation:** High-fidelity simulation bridges classical computing, neural simulation, and generative physics to allow robots to learn and practice safely before real-world deployment.

## Data Flywheel & Open-Source Ecosystem
- **Open H Data Initiative:** Launched with founding members Axel Krieger, Dr. Nasir Nawab, Sean Hoover, and Madi Aizian, this collaboration spans 35+ partners (including CMR Surgical and Moon Surgical) across 11 embodiments, releasing over 750+ hours of healthcare robotics data.
- **Model Availability:** Groot H is fully open-source with weights available on Hugging Face and source code on GitHub.
- **Synthetic Data Generation:** Nvidia Cosmos H Transfer and Cosmos H Predict enable controllable synthetic data generation and action-state prediction using digital twins grounded in patient anatomy from CT and MR scans.

## Isaac for Healthcare & Platform Extensions
- **Platform Scope:** Nvidia Isaac for healthcare provides domain-specific extensions, sensor simulation libraries, synthetic data generation pipelines, end-to-end workflows, and developer blueprints.
- **Sensor Simulation Libraries:** 
  * *Ultrasound Simulation:* End-to-end pipelines mapping tissue acoustic properties to probe configurations using Nvidia's optics (Isaac for healthcare v0.5).
  * *X-ray Simulation:* Physics-based pipelines covering CT-derived volumes, ray propagation, and detector response.
- **Hospital Automation Expansion:** Extended beyond surgical robotics into hospital automation via Project Rio (digital twin blueprints), with partners like Peritas AI deploying humanoid concepts (the Perry robot) in evaluations with Advent Health.

## Deployment Stack: Holoscan 4.0 & IGX Thor
- **Holoscan 4.0 Features:** The physical AI-native deployment stack features direct EtherCAT motor control support, interoperability with Ross 2 and NG streamer, and fully GPU-accelerated resident graphs for speed-of-light latency.
- **IGX Thor Edge Compute:** An industrial-grade edge AI platform providing safety-certified compute with 8x more performance to process complex Vision Language Action (VLA) models directly on edge devices.

## Medical AI Models & "Raw to Insights"
- **Raw to Insights for Ultrasound:** Developed with Siemens research, Altera, and Nvidia IGX/H100, this AI performs physics-based sensor processing directly from raw RF signals for real-time patient-adaptive imaging.
- **Raw to Insights for MRI:** Evolving from the CMRX recon challenge (where it ranked #1 on the leaderboard), this model delivers consistent high-quality reconstruction across scanners and sites.
- **NV Generate for CT and MR:** A state-of-the-art 3D diffusion model that takes CT images and generates higher-resolution MR images to diversify training datasets (not intended for direct clinical decision-making).
- **Ecosystem Integration:** Open models built on MONAI are integrated into Hopper's AI foundry and adopted by partners like Philips for scanner-specific post-training.
