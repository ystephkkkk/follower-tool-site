## The 2027 Refinancing Wall and Equity Wipeout Mechanics
- **Corporate Debt Structure vs. Mortgages:** Unlike mortgages that amortize principal, corporate loans require full refinancing at maturity, leaving companies exposed to variable-rate debt expenses.
- **The Mathematical Problem:** 
  - A hypothetical 2022 buyout of a $1 billion software company funded with 50% variable private credit debt ($500M debt, $500M equity) facing a 50% valuation drop due to the SaaS apocalypse is now worth $500M total.
  - Because debt claims must be satisfied before equity, the new valuation ($500M) matches the debt ($500M), reducing the equity value to **$0**.
  - To refinance in 2027, private credit will demand PE firms inject another $500M in fresh equity, leading PE firms to likely walk away and surrender ownership to lenders.
- **Early Precedents:** 
  - In 2024, Blue Owl and other lenders took control of **Pluralsight** (workforce development platform), wiping out Vista’s entire equity stake.
  - In August, **Medallia** (Thomas Bravo deal) was taken over by lenders, wiping out equity.
