## Downshifting and Neural Wind-Down Techniques
* **Three Sleep Prerequisites:** Falling asleep requires turning off thoughts, slowing heart rate, and forgetting the position of limbs in space (*proprioception*).

* **Confusing the Proprioceptive Circuit:** Slow, deliberate eye movements under closed eyelids (or emerging MIT-designed REM-measuring smart eye masks slated for release in 7 to 12 months) help trick the brain into shedding body awareness and falling asleep faster.

## The AI-to-Biotech Convergence and Neural Technology
* **AI Labs as Biotech Companies:** Huberman predicts that major AI labs (Meta, OpenAI, Anthropic, and Neuralink) will ultimately become biotech companies whose core arms race is non-invasive reading and writing to the brain.
* **Precision Reading vs. Writing:** While neuroscience understands peripheral sensory organs and deep hypothalamic switches well, memory and thought remain murky, complicated by findings that artificial sequence activation of neurons can still trigger the same behaviors (e.g., in mouse hippocampal studies).
* **Non-Invasive Future:** The industry will inevitably shift away from blunt pharmacological tools (like SSRIs, stimulants, and psychedelics) toward precise, AI-driven, non-invasive neuromodulation of waking brain states using ultrasound or light-activated genetic vectors.
