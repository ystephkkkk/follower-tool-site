## Fed Chair Kevin Warsh’s Dovish Reality

* **The Illusion of Hawkishness:** While markets expected Fed Chair Kevin Warsh to restore central bank independence and implement hawkish monetary policies, Hayes argues Warsh is actually a dove in disguise.
* **The Task Force Stall:** Instead of using his executive power to aggressively raise rates, reduce the balance sheet, or dismantle forward guidance, Warsh announced five task forces to study Fed reform.
    * **Delayed Policy Action:** Hayes points out that task forces typically take six to twelve months to produce a report, allowing the Fed to delay difficult decisions and quietly keep rates on hold while money printing continues.
    * **Easy Money Evidence:** Hayes points to absurd market behavior—such as space-based data center startups launching at 100 times sales—as proof that monetary conditions remain far too loose.
