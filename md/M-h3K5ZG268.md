- **Helios Architecture:** **- Derek Dicker:** Described Helios as an AMD rack-scale reference design featuring 72 GPUs, embedded high-performance networking, and an open silicon design.
- **Hyperscaler Deployments:** **- Derek Dicker:** Highlighted that Helios and other AMD technologies are being deployed by major players, including Microsoft, Meta (which showcased its own version), and Frontier Labs.
- **Hardware Diversity:** **- Derek Dicker:** Emphasized that AMD's broad architecture portfolio covers physical AI (via FPGAs), AI PCs (Ryzen for local laptops), local developer boxes (Halo), and massive data center server racks.

## Software, Open Standards, and Cost Optimization
- **Open Standards & Software:** **- Derek Dicker:** Stated that AMD's strategy centers on open ecosystems, including open standards like Ultra Ethernet and UALink, alongside the open-source **ROCm** GPU software stack, which is seeing growing developer momentum and regular release cycles.
- **Rising Token Expenses:** **- Derek Dicker:** Noted that rising token costs on frontier infrastructure have led some companies to exhaust a full year's budget in a very short period.
- **Right-Sizing & Hybrid Strategies:** **- Derek Dicker:** Explained that customers are increasingly turning to hybrid approaches—using open-source models on-premise or utilizing intelligent routers—to lower token costs and right-size workloads.
- **SMB & Channel Enablement:** **- Dr. Ali Baghdadi & Derek Dicker:** Discussed how channel distributors can act as trusted advisors to small and medium businesses, using downloadable open-source models and "t-shirt sizing" hardware options to help SMBs securely enter AI without overspending on cloud tokens.
