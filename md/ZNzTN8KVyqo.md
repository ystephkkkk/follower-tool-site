* **TL;DR:** JPMorgan Chase CEO Jamie Dimon warned that relentless global government borrowing and AI capital expenditure will create long-term inflation and credit risks, while urging policymakers to prioritize growth-oriented structural reforms like deregulation over ad hoc taxes.

## AI Boom Financing & Infrastructure Pressures
* **Capital Demand vs. Rates:** Jamie Dimon noted that while the Tech Stars conference has grown from 20 attendees to 2,000, rising capital costs and heavy debt financing create near-term risks.
* **Capex and Borrowing Realities:** Addressing forecasts of **$700 billion** in AI capex this year and potentially **$1 trillion** next year, Dimon noted that the totals reflect massive corporate commitments alongside governments globally borrowing **$2 trillion**, creating intense supply-demand pressures on capital.
* **Community Integration:** On data center bottlenecks, Dimon stated projects succeed by working locally—noting that half of new US data centers are in Texas and Virginia—and committing to terms that protect consumer electricity costs.

## Cybersecurity & Systemic AI Vulnerabilities
* **Amplified Risks:** Dimon stated that AI has increased cyber risks tenfold, noting that cyber security was already JPMorgan's biggest risk before new threats like *Mythos* emerged.
* **Cross-Industry Defense:** To combat these emerging vulnerabilities, JPMorgan helped start the **Alliance for Critical Infrastructure**, which currently includes **50 companies** across **6 industries** (tech, financial services, water, transport) in the US, with plans to expand to healthcare, pharma, and agriculture.

## Global Debt, Bond Selloffs & Fiscal Warning
* **Fiscal Warning:** Commenting on the French bond selloff, Dimon called it a broader warning to governments that they cannot borrow and spend endlessly without market pushback.
* **US Government Debt:** Dimon contrasted healthy corporate and consumer debt with soaring US government debt, which has climbed from **50% to 100% of GDP** with a **6% deficit**.
* **Global Treasury Holdings:** Addressing concerns over China and Japan reducing Treasury holdings, Dimon noted that China has already drawn down its holdings from **$2 trillion to $700 billion** over the past two years without causing immediate panic, though supply-demand dynamics warrant attention.

## Regulatory Policy & Growth Strategy
* **Cost-Free Growth:** Dimon argued that "good policy is free," suggesting that deregulation, permitting reform, rational climate policy, and adjusted immigration could drive growth **1% higher** and reduce debt-to-GDP ratios without costing money.
* **UK Bank Taxation:** Addressing potential windfall taxes on UK banks, Dimon noted that banks have already been paying through a windfall tax for **12 years**, warning that a lack of tax principle raises the cost of capital and hurts capital formation.
