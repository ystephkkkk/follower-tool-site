## The AI Extinction Warning "Shell Game"
* **The Doomsday Narrative:** 
  * 27-year-old AI researcher Jacob Coxon resigned from Anthropic and claimed labs are racing toward self-improving superintelligence that could kill everyone by decade's end.
  * Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei followed up on CBS News stating the industry lied/downplayed risks and called to slow down model capabilities.
  * OpenAI CEO Sam Altman agreed that the industry needs to slow down.
* **Historical Precedent of Hysteria:** Eisman compares this to hacker Kevin Mitnick, who in 1995 was sentenced to 5 years (with 8 months in solitary) after law enforcement convinced a judge Mitnick could start a nuclear war by whistling into a payphone.
* **The Real Motivation (According to Eisman):**
  * **False Premises:** Slowing down is impossible for Anthropic and OpenAI due to massive commitments to hyperscalers (e.g., OpenAI represents **$300 billion** of Oracle's **$600 billion+** backlog) and Anthropic's imminent IPO.
  * **The Strategy:** Facing a slowing business cycle, the end of "token maxing," rising data center/capital costs, and increasing open-weight market share, Amodei and Altman are creating hysteria.
  * **Pricing Moats:** By inviting federal regulation, they hope to foster an AI duopoly that protects their pricing power.
  * **The Backfire:** President Trump stated he has no interest in regulating AI, making the doomsday narrative likely to vanish quickly, though local data center regulations and public fear persist.
