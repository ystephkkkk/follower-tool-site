**TL;DR**
In the 21st century, national sovereignty is defined by the "new geometry of power"—the twin foundations of energy security and digital intelligence. To ensure true independence, India must transition from being a service provider for foreign platforms to owning the energy, compute, and data infrastructure that drives its future.

## The Fractured Global Reality
*   **The End of the "Flat World":** Gautam Adani argues that the era of open, borderless supply chains and technology is over. The world is now fractured, with energy, data, and semiconductors treated as instruments of statecraft.
*   **The New Geometry of Power:** National power is no longer just political; it is built on two inseparable dimensions:
    *   **Energy Security:** The country that controls its energy drives its industrial future.
    *   **Digital Intelligence:** The country that controls its compute capacity drives its intelligence future.
*   **Global Precedents:** 
    *   **United States:** Transformed from import-dependent to an energy exporter via the shale revolution and is now negotiating for global compute dominance.
    *   **China:** Built massive industrial scale through a mix of coal and record-breaking renewable installations (440 GW in 2025) while pursuing "AI sovereignty" to avoid reliance on foreign models.

## India’s "Compounding Acceleration"
*   **Economic Velocity:** India took 67 years to reach a $2 trillion GDP, but only 12 years to add the next $2 trillion. Adani predicts India will add the equivalent of a "new European economy" to its GDP every decade.
*   **Energy Milestones:** 
    *   As of March 2026, India surpassed **500 GW** of installed power capacity.
    *   The goal is to reach **2,000 GW by 2047**, representing a massive nation-scale execution of industrialization and decarbonization.
*   **Demand-Led Growth:** Unlike other nations building for abstract futures, India’s advantage is that every unit of capacity built already has a waiting, rising market of consumers and businesses.

## The Three-Layer AI Stack
Adani redefines AI not as mere software, but as physical infrastructure. He outlines a three-tier "AI Stack" that India must own:
*   **The Power Layer:** Reliable, scalable energy is the prerequisite for all digital processing.
*   **The Compute Layer:** The "factories" of the intelligence age, including data centers, GPUs, and cooling systems.
*   **The Application Layer:** Where AI reaches the end-user (farmers, doctors, small businesses) to expand productivity rather than eliminate jobs.
*   **Evolution of the IT Model:** Adani calls for a shift from the "Old IT Model" (writing code for foreign platforms) to a "New Model" where India owns the data, the models, and the platforms.

## Adani Group Strategic Commitments
The Adani Group is positioning itself as the builder of India’s "AI spine" through three foundational investments:
*   **Energy Transition:** A **$100 billion** commitment to clean energy, including the world’s largest single-site renewable plant (30 GW) at Khavda, Gujarat.
*   **Sovereign Compute:** A separate **$100 billion** commitment to the data center business, including a gigawatt-scale campus in Visakhapatnam in partnership with Google.
*   **Human Capital:** A **₹60,000 crore** commitment via the Adani Foundation for education and AI-integrated skilling to ensure the workforce can manage the physical infrastructure of the digital world.
