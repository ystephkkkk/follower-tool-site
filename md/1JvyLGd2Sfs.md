* **Rigorous Execution:** AI excels at meticulous execution once given a general direction, avoiding human errors in fine-grained details such as verifying epsilon-delta arguments or handling delicate linear programming constraints.
* **Contextual Cleanliness & Backtracking:** Unlike human mathematicians whose context windows become polluted by failed paths—making it difficult to completely reset intuition—AI models can systematically make mistakes, backtrack, update probabilities of success, and pivot without psychological friction.
* **Comparison to Human Collaboration:** The reasoning traces often resemble reading disorganized notes from a colleague, capturing the natural struggle of choosing promising approaches, pruning search trees, and combining ideas from across the literature.

## Impact on the Mathematical Community and Future of the Field
