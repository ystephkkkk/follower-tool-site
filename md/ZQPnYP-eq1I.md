## Cultural Transformation & Leadership
* **Revival of the Warrior Ethos:** **Cultural Reset:** The Department of War has replaced social engineering, diversity quotas, and risk aversion with a focus on training, readiness, accountability, discipline, and lethality.
* **Personnel and Standards:** **Merit-Based Standards:** The department has eliminated DEI evaluations (such as B-Cap and C-Cap) and established gender-neutral standards across all combat MOS roles set exclusively to the highest male standard.
* **General Officer Accountability:** **10% Reduction:** The department has fired 10% of generals and admirals and reduced overall general officer slots by an additional 10% (a total 20% reduction across the board).
* **Retention and Recruitment:** **Record Metrics:** Retention rates are at historic highs (including over 80% on the USS *Abraham Lincoln*), and recruiting across all branches has reached decades-long highs following the November 2024 election.

## Six Strategic Announcements
* **Autonomous Warfare Command (Auto-Warcom):** **Four-Star Command:** Creation of a new four-star combatant command with service-like authorities to scale autonomous and robotic capabilities rapidly across the joint force.
  * **Project Agincourt:** An interim step replacing the Unmanned Systems Derpom within the DIU to prototype "warfighting acquisition," fusing operators with entrepreneurs in rapid adaptation cycles.
  * **Leadership Appointments:** Owen West and Navy SEAL Senior Chief Max Strazer appointed to a CEO-COO partnership to guide Project Agincourt over the next year.
* **Project Meridian:** **Future of Warfare Study:** An initiative led by CTO Emil Michael and co-led by Elon Musk, Palmer Luckey, and Newt Gingrich, alongside private-sector and academic experts, tasked with mapping war's domains and technologies over the next 120 days.
* **Fortress America (Federal Operations for Resilience, Troop Resources, Energy, Supply, and Survivability):** **National Insurance Policy:** A directive requiring every major U.S. military installation to generate its own power independent of the civilian grid, bringing critical supply chains (rare earths, semiconductors, propellants) home, and building deep strategic reserves for munitions, fuel, medicine, and blood.
* **The Next Great American Base:** **First New Base in 40 Years:** A call to governors and Adjutant Generals (TAGs) to pitch state sites to host 15,000 to 25,000 troops on hundreds of thousands of acres featuring classical architecture, modern air defenses, and organic power generation.
* **America's Corps of Cadets:** **New Academic Pipelines:** Partnering with premier higher education institutions—specifically Louisiana State University (LSU), Liberty University, Tuskegee University, Mississippi State University, and Hillsdale College—to establish new corps of cadets, designated as distinguished military colleges with priority in officer commissioning.
* **Office of Religious Affairs:** **Direct Reporting:** Establishment of a direct-line Office of Religious Affairs reporting straight to the Secretary of War to protect chaplain budgets, infrastructure, and spiritual support for a force where less than 5% identify as non-religious.
