**TL;DR:** OpenAI’s AI models helped uncover a critical, long-standing security vulnerability in the PHP programming language—which runs roughly 70% of the world's websites—allowing Cloudflare and other partners to implement protections before public disclosure. 

## The PHP Vulnerability Discovery
* **Role of AI in Finding the Flaw:** 
  * **Initial Discovery:** In August, a security researcher using the `GPT-5.6 Sol` model (guided by expert intuition) identified an early version of the bug.
  * **Autonomous Discovery:** In September, OpenAI used its latest internal model with extensive compute in a set-up environment to autonomously discover the full, more complete version of the exploit.
* **Scale of the Underlying Software:** 
  * **Global Reach:** PHP runs on approximately 70% of the world's websites and web applications.
  * **Latent Risk:** The flaw had been hidden in the codebase for years, potentially exposed to and exploited by nation states or hackers prior to discovery.

## Mitigation and Industry Coordination
* **Responsible Disclosure:** 
  * OpenAI worked confidentially ahead of public release with Cloudflare, WordPress, the PHP community, and government partners to ensure protections were ready.
* **Cloudflare's Defense Efforts:** 
  * Cloudflare worked behind the scenes to secure customer networks before the flaw went public.
  * Once the exploit details became known, hackers began attempting attacks at rates of millions per minute across Cloudflare's network.
* **Immediate Remediation Required:** 
  * Matthew Prince stressed that servers running PHP must immediately update to the latest free version of PHP to stay safe, as the exploit no longer requires AI to execute and can be triggered with a couple of requests to a web server.
