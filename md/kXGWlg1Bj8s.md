## TL;DR
John Collison, Co-Founder of Stripe, discusses how AI agents and computer use will fundamentally rewire the internet, commerce, and software infrastructure. Stripe processed **$1.9 trillion** in 2025 (up 34%), and Collison defines January 1, 2026, as Day One of the "singularity epoch."

## Stripe's Metrics, AI Transition, and Internal Transformation
* **Payment Volume:** Stripe processed **$1.9 trillion** in 2025, representing a **34% growth rate year-over-year**.
* **AI-Driven Firm Creation:** Stripe is seeing the highest rate of new firm creation in recent memory because intelligent tools make starting and succeeding at companies significantly easier.
* **Internal Workflows:** 
  * **Sales:** Sales teams have completely abandoned old workflows, self-serving custom internal AI apps built on complex privacy and access control layers to generate personalized customer pitches.
  * **Legal:** The legal team built an automated agent to ingest massive global regulations (such as new 600-page laws dropped in India or Finland) to track compliance requirements relevant to Stripe.
* **The "Jagged Frontier":** Collison notes that while consumer AI apps face uneven performance, business adoption requires pairing AI directly with complex, permission-guarded internal corporate data.

## Agentic Commerce and Computer Use
* **Obsolescence of Web Forms:** Collison states that filling out web forms is a "non-value-add activity" that no one chooses to do, making personal agents handling tasks like renewing a driver's license a major shift.
* **Computer Use as Backward Compatibility:** Computer use is the ultimate backward compatibility layer for the real world because waiting years for legacy institutions like the DMV to build MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers is unnecessary when an agent can directly navigate existing web interfaces.
* **E-Commerce and Merchant Shifts:**
  * **First-Order Change:** Personal agents will execute a massive share of everyday consumer commerce.
  * **Second-Order Change:** AI discovery will serve as the new point of aggregation, empowering long-tail niche brands with strong internet reviews over traditional big-box retailers.
  * **Historical Analogy:** Collison compares AI e-commerce discovery to the early days of Facebook and Instagram ad targeting, which natively birthed companies like Wish, Teespring, Temu, and Shein.

## Cybersecurity Realities in the AI Era
* **Accelerating Breach Rates:** Collison predicts that over the next **5 years**, the world will see more security breaches than over the past **5 years** due to advanced cyber capabilities embedded in new AI models.
* **Startup Advantage:** Startups are disproportionately well-positioned because they operate smaller, homogeneous tech stacks, whereas legacy companies running 50-year-old unpatched infrastructure face extreme vulnerabilities.

## Strategic Acquisitions and Pricing Shifts
* **Recent Acquisitions:** Stripe has strategically acquired **Bridge, Privy, Metronome, and OpenRouter** to inject cryptonativity, usage-based billing, and multi-model routing into its ecosystem.
* **Usage-Based Billing via Metronome:** Because modern products involve heavy AI inference costs, almost every company now requires usage-based billing or overage structures rather than flat subscriptions.
* **Multi-Model Routing via OpenRouter:** Stripe bet on OpenRouter because businesses require multiple models simultaneously—routing high-value users to frontier models and lower-value users to cheaper models, or adapting as models rapidly change.
* **Build vs. Buy Philosophy:** Stripe prefers buying when entering a brand-new, fast-evolving market where internal development cycles would leave the final product outdated within 12 months.
