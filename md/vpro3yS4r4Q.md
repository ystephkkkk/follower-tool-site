AMD Chair and CEO Lisa Su and Hugging Face CEO Clem Delangue discussed how open models, accessible local compute, and developer collaboration are accelerating the agentic AI era. They highlighted new hardware-software integrations like the Ryzen AI Max platform and emphasized transforming people from passive AI users into active AI builders.

* **Domain Expansion Beyond Text:**

## The Importance of Openness in Agentic AI
* **Transparency and Control:** 
  * **Autonomy Requires Oversight:** Autonomy without transparency, control, and understanding is risky, dangerous, and problematic (Clem Delangue).
  * **Avoiding Black Boxes:** Companies cannot outsource their agents to a black box; they need open models to own, inspect, and understand how their agents work and perform (Clem Delangue).
* **Ecosystem Diversity:** 
  * **No Single Monopoly on Ideas:** No single company, group, chip, or model holds all the good ideas, requiring an open ecosystem to mix and match computing for diverse workloads and industries (Lisa Su).
  * **Flexibility:** Open ecosystems ensure developers are not tied down to a single underlying architecture when better technology emerges (Lisa Su).

## Local Compute, Accessibility, and Hardware Partnerships
* **Shift Toward Local AI Workloads:** 
  * **Resurgence of Local Dev Stations:** Driven by a desire to build on local hardware, developers want to understand which models fit specific hardware, quantify them, and optimize them for better tokens-per-dollar (Clem Delangue).
  * **AMD-Hugging Face Hardware Integration:** Jack announced a new platform combining Ryzen AI Max and Hugging Face to make leading-edge AI hardware barrier-free (Lisa Su, referencing Jack).
* **Practical Edge Computing:** 
  * **Offline Accessibility:** Local hardware like AI PCs and the Ryzen AI Max "Halo box" solve real-world productivity issues, such as maintaining AI access while traveling on planes with failing Wi-Fi (Lisa Su).

## Robotics, Edge Experiments, and Democratization
* **Physical World Interaction:** 
  * **Richie Mini Robot:** Hugging Face shipped over 10,000 units of the "Richie Mini" robot worldwide as an experiment, allowing visual interaction and real-world dataset collection to improve system reasoning (Clem Delangue).
* **Empowering Software Engineers:** 
  * **Lowering Barriers:** Software engineers can now act as AI builders rather than just AI users—optimizing, running, experimenting, and training models (Clem Delangue).
  * **Small Teams, Big Ideas:** Five people with a good idea can leverage open models and accessible hardware to build amazing solutions without needing thousands of employees or tens of thousands of GPUs (Lisa Su).

## Predictions & Forward Calls
* **Model Count Explosion:** Clem Delangue predicts that in the next few years, the ecosystem will move from 3 million models toward **hundreds of millions of different models**—approaching a scale where there are as many models as specific use cases, companies, or even individual people.
