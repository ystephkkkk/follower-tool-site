President Trump and EPA Administrator Lee Zeldin announced the repeal of the 2009 EPA "endangerment finding," characterizing it as the largest deregulatory act in U.S. history intended to end electric vehicle mandates and reduce the average cost of new vehicles by approximately $3,000.

## Major Rollback of EPA Regulations
* **Repeal of the Endangerment Finding:** President Trump announced the immediate elimination of the 2009 Obama-era EPA endangerment finding, which served as the legal foundation for greenhouse gas regulations.
    * **Scope of Action:** The administration is terminating all additional green emission standards imposed on vehicle models and engines between 2012 and 2027.
    * **Legal Justification:** Administrator Lee Zeldin cited Supreme Court precedents (*Loper Bright* and *West Virginia v. EPA*), arguing the EPA previously exceeded its statutory authority under the Clean Air Act.
* **Termination of Mandates:** The action effectively ends federal electric vehicle (EV) mandates and the "start-stop" engine feature.
    * **Consumer Choice:** Zeldin emphasized a return to "freedom and choice," allowing consumers to choose between gas, diesel, hybrid, or electric vehicles without government pressure.
* **Industrial Equipment:** Trump noted the administration is working to remove environmental restrictions on farm equipment and heavy machinery (e.g., John Deere tractors, Caterpillar bulldozers) to reduce costs and improve performance.

## Economic Impacts and Projections
* **Cost Savings:** The administration estimates the repeal will save American taxpayers over **$1.3 trillion** in total regulatory costs.
    * **Per-Vehicle Impact:** Trump projected a savings of nearly **$3,000** for the average new vehicle, while Zeldin cited a figure of over **$2,400**.
* **Deregulatory Milestones:** Trump claimed his administration has reached a ratio of **129 regulations cut for every one new regulation** implemented, far exceeding his initial campaign promise of a 10-to-1 ratio.
* **Manufacturing Investment:** 
    * **Auto Industry:** Trump reported that global automakers have committed over **$100 billion** to build new plants in the U.S. due to the combination of deregulation, tax cuts, and tariffs.
    * **Stock Market:** Trump highlighted the Dow hitting 50,000 and the S&P hitting 7,000 within his first year as evidence of economic strength.

## Energy and Environmental Strategy
* **Energy Sources:** Trump criticized wind energy as "weak, ineffective, and the most costly," claiming turbines are "ruining fields" and posing a threat to military and civilian air traffic.
* **Resource Abundance:** The President asserted that the U.S. has "500 to 1,000 years" of fossil fuel reserves "under our feet," arguing that current fuel efficiency efforts are unnecessary.
* **Public Health Response:** In response to concerns about the health impacts of rolling back emissions standards, Trump labeled the "endangerment finding" a "scam" and stated that savings would be better utilized by families for healthcare and lower prescription drug costs.
