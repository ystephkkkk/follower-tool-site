## TL;DR
President Trump announced that over 220 entities have signed the Ratepayer Protection Pledge, requiring major tech companies and AI data centers to fund or build their own energy infrastructure to prevent consumer rate hikes while keeping the U.S. ahead in AI and manufacturing.

## Ratepayer Protection Pledge and Energy Infrastructure
* **Self-Funded Tech Infrastructure:** 
  * Large tech companies and AI data centers are formally committing to fund or build all required energy infrastructure rather than passing costs to consumers.
  * AI data centers and big tech are essentially becoming their own utilities and building dedicated power plants because current electricity demand is more than double current production levels.
* **Pledge Adoption and Rate Protections:** 
  * Over 220 utilities, tech companies, state governments, and partners have signed the Ratepayer Protection Pledge since its introduction a few months ago.
  * Electricity rates for 80% of power distributed in America are currently kept in check by the plan.
* **Specific Utility and State Impacts:**
  * **Georgia Power:** Froze base rates for households until 2029; revenue from Google's data center construction is allowing the elimination of property taxes entirely (noted by Gov. Brian Kemp).
  * **American Electric Power (AEP):** Projects $16 billion in cost offsets for consumers through data center contracts.
  * **Alliant Energy (Iowa):** Data center deals allow them to freeze electric rates for consumers for at least 5 years.
  * **Meta Project (Richland Parish, Louisiana):** A $27 billion project provides public school teachers with up to $50,000 in bonuses this year and saves local residents $2.6 billion on electricity bills (noted by Sheldon Jones).
