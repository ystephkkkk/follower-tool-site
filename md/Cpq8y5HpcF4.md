### TL;DR
Bill Ackman breaks down his investment philosophy, the future of AI and market bubbles, and how a family medical emergency inspired him to build a world-class brain institute. He also discusses scaling Pershing Square, transforming Howard Hughes into a modern Berkshire Hathaway, and shifting away from traditional activism toward friendly dialogue and large-scale permanent capital.

## Personal Crisis & The Brain Institute
- **Daughter's Medical Emergency:** Bill Ackman’s daughter, Lucy, suffered a sudden brain hemorrhage due to an undiagnosed arteriovenous malformation (AVM) that caused 19 hours of brain pressure.
- **Resilience and Recovery:** Despite grim initial prognoses, Lucy has made remarkable progress in regaining cognition, speech, and mobility through round-the-clock family support, friends visiting daily, and aggressive rehabilitation.
- **Ackman-Oaksman Institute (AIOI):** 
  - Ackman and his wife Neri closed on a vacant 3.4-acre biotechnology building at 65th and 11th Avenue 60 days after discovering it, intending to build a world-class brain institute focused on rehab, recovery, and longevity.
  - The institute aims to solve systemic inadequacies in hospital nutrition, short insurance-funded rehab windows, and medical nihilism surrounding brain injury recovery.
  - Leveraging technology and AI (including future bionic vision glasses and AI speech therapy) to make advanced neurological recovery accessible to all patients.

## Investment Process & Public Markets
- **Research Methodology:** A two-person team conducts deep dives starting with SEC filings (10-Ks, 10-Qs), earnings transcripts, expert networks, and competitor analysis, followed by an independent peer review before committing capital.
- **Criteria for "Super Durable" Companies:** Focuses on simple, predictable, high free-cash-flow businesses with wide moats, strong balance sheets, and low risk of disruption.
- **The Checklist & Risk Management:** Following a major loss in 2015–2016, Ackman engraved investment principles on stone tablets and instituted strict checklists, an aversion to short selling, and a focus on asymmetric macro bets (e.g., predicting COVID-19 shutdowns and inflation).
- **Netflix Case Study:** Pershing Square initially bought Netflix on a subscriber dip, sold three weeks later when management abruptly shifted strategy to an ad-supported model (breaking the certainty thesis), and rebought the stock years later when it was cut in half after Netflix successfully won the streaming wars and executed on ads.

## AI, Bubbles, and the Venture Landscape
- **Assessing AI Disruption:** AI represents the fastest industrial revolution and pace of improvement ever seen, dramatically increasing the risk of disruption for dominant businesses.
- **AI Use Cases:** Ackman views AI as a powerful research tool (not used for financial modeling to avoid groupthink) and used it to help vet medical decisions during his daughter's trauma.
- **Venture Capital Bubbles:** Points to extreme private market froth—such as Series A rounds commanding $5B pre-money valuations and companies raising $50M at $400M valuations only to jump to $1B weeks later—driven by FOMO.
- **Advice to Founders:** Raise capital now while available, but treat every dollar as your own and maintain years of runway rather than burning cash expecting perpetual liquidity; a high-profile blow-up will eventually reset the market.

## Building a Modern-Day Berkshire Hathaway
- **Howard Hughes Transformation:** Transforming Howard Hughes from a real estate developer of master-planned communities (MPCs like The Woodlands in Houston and communities in Texas, Las Vegas, and Hawaii) into a conglomerate.
- **The Berkshire Playbook:** 
  - Modeled after Warren Buffett, Ackman and CIO Ryan are shifting cash generated from land sales ($300M+ NOI and $4B in Hawaii condos under contract) into a growing P&C specialty insurance and reinsurance business (Bantage Holdings).
  - Pershing Square manages the assets of the insurance float while the company maintains a permanent capital base with zero stock dilution.
- **Ownership Alignment:** Ackman owns ~47% of Howard Hughes, ensuring a long-term, unpressured strategic vision.

## Activism, Leadership, and Public Life
- **Shift Away from Activism:** Pershing Square has not made an activist investment since 2016; Ackman believes he no longer needs to run proxy contests because 3 million Twitter followers and a strong long-term track record grant sufficient influence to dialogue directly with CEOs.
- **Criteria for CEO Success:** Prefers leaders who have "done it before" (e.g., recruiting Brian Nicols to Chipotle) and assesses candidates based on passion, high energy, capability, and unyielding character.
- **Personal Reflections:** Reaches age 60 with a perspective that every bad experience yields a silver lining, viewing his career skills as providentially aligned to save his daughter and build a transformative brain institute.
