## ChatGPT Work & Automated Dots
*   **Autonomous Agent Capabilities:** **Dots** live in the cloud with their own computer and browser environments, giving them ongoing responsibilities like monitoring feeds, updating content sheets, and pinging users on Slack when urgent attention is needed.
*   **Omnichannel Reach:** **Cross-Platform Access:** Users can text, call, or message their Dot while commuting or away from their desk, and Dots can access local files when the user is on their laptop.
*   **Customization:** **Dot Setup:** Users name their Dot, pick accessories/avatars, define ongoing responsibilities, and integrate communication channels (like Slack or text messaging), with Dots learning user preferences over time.

## Collaborative Spaces & Knowledge Sharing
*   **ChatGPT Spaces:** **Shared Workspaces:** Allows teams and agents to work together on the same page simultaneously, supporting real-time comments, inline questioning, and shared page edits while individual chat threads remain private.
*   **Page Instructions:** **Agent Guidelines:** Similar to an `.md` file for codebases, users can write instructions into pages to ensure agents maintain a consistent style when organizing new research or meeting notes.
*   **Scaling Expertise:** **Institutional Knowledge:** Leaders can aggregate disparate meeting notes, papers, and articles into shared knowledge bases that feed interactive coaching sites (powered by GPT Live) so teams can access expertise without direct 1:1 meetings.
