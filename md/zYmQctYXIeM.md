## The Iran Conflict and Peace Negotiations
* **Military Status:** President Trump characterized the 100-day-old conflict (Operation Epic Fury) as a "military exercise" and "blockade" rather than a war. 
    * **U.S. Casualties:** Trump noted that the U.S. has lost 13 service members, comparing this favorably to high-casualty historical conflicts like the Vietnam War.
    * **Troop Presence:** Approximately 50,000 U.S. troops deployed in the region will remain there until "completion" because keeping them stationed there costs very little and they may still be needed.
* **Degraded Iranian Capabilities:** Trump claimed that the U.S. military has "decapitated" Iran's military leadership and destroyed its naval, air, and anti-aircraft capacities.
    * He estimated that Iran has only 21% to 22% of its missiles left and that most of its drone manufacturing and launch pads have been destroyed.
    * He asserted that Iran's underground nuclear enrichment facilities were totally obliterated by U.S. B-2 bombers and are currently buried under collapsed mountains.
* **Negotiation Status & Leadership:** Trump stated that the U.S. is "very close" to a deal, mediated in part by officials from Pakistan.
    * **New Interlocutors:** The U.S. is negotiating with a "third group" of Iranian leaders, including the severely injured son of the former Supreme Leader, whom Trump described as "more rational" and "brave."
    * **Key Deal Terms:** Trump is insisting on strict language that prevents Iran from not only "developing" but also "purchasing, acquiring, or buying" nuclear weapons. 
    * **Sanctions Leverage:** Trump stated he will not unfreeze any Iranian assets or lift sanctions upfront; economic relief will only occur after Iran complies with the deal.

## Economic Outlook, Agriculture, and Federal Reserve Pressure
* **Monetary Policy:** Trump expressed strong support for newly appointed Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh but actively campaigned against potential interest rate hikes.
    * **Economic Philosophy:** Trump argued that economic growth does not cause inflation and that raising rates "penalizes" success. He advocated for lowering rates to incentivize further industrial expansion.
* **Support for Farmers:** Addressing reports that 70% of American farmers are struggling to afford fertilizer, Trump asserted that "farmers love me and the farmers trust me."
    * He cited the $28 billion aid package he provided to farmers during his first term (funded by tariffs on China) as proof of his commitment.
    * **Energy Prices:** Trump promised that once the conflict with Iran is finalized—either via a signed agreement or final military action—domestic gasoline and fertilizer prices will "drop like a rock," aiming for gas prices below $1.90 per gallon.

* **Interview Walkout:** When interviewer Kristen Welker pointed out a lack of evidence for his election fraud and J6 conspiracy claims, Trump attacked Welker and major media networks (NBC, ABC, CBS, and CNN) as "dishonest" and "crooked" before ending the interview early.
