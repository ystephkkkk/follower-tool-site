## TL;DR
Rocket Lab Founder & CEO Sir Peter Beck walked through the company's Long Beach headquarters, highlighting its evolution from a bootstrapped New Zealand startup into a vertically integrated, publicly traded space enterprise spanning launch, satellite manufacturing, and mission-critical components.

## Company Origins & Capital-Efficient Culture
* **Bootstrapped Beginnings:** Founded in 2006 in New Zealand with a total capitalization under $100,000 NZD (~$60,000 USD).
* **Rutherford Engine Philosophy:** Named after Ernest Rutherford's quote ("We have no money so we have to think"), utilizing resourcefulness and salvaged parts (like junkyard gas fittings) to overcome tight financial constraints.
* **Early Milestones:** 
  * Launched the first private suborbital rocket in the southern hemisphere (RT1) in 2009, just three years after inception.
  * Secured DARPA propellant and propulsion work in 2010.
  * Raised a $5 million Series A from Khosla Ventures in 2013, flipping the company into a US entity and enabling the first 3D-printed Rutherford engine hot-fire.
  * Closed a Bessemer-led B round in 2014, broke ground on Launch Complex 1 in New Zealand in 2015, finished it in 2016, launched their first vehicle in 2017, and achieved unicorn status with their D round.

## Going Public & M&A Strategy
* **Rigorous Public Discipline:** Went public in 2021 to enforce extreme financial discipline and build a multi-generational space company that persists beyond its founder, ensuring long-term impact.
* **Complex Acquisitions:** Acquired three companies simultaneously in 2021—a painful and resource-intensive integration process that underscored the operational friction of M&A.
* **The Space Component Moat:** While rockets capture public attention, owning the entire stack of spacecraft components (reaction wheels, buses, propulsion) serves as a massive competitive moat due to a fractured, constrained supply chain.

## Facilities & Mission Capabilities
* **Global Footprint:** Spans 13 acres of factories globally, including locations across the United States (Long Beach, Virginia, Albuquerque, Tucson, Middle River for Neutron rockets, Stennis), Canada (Toronto), Germany (Manarak), and New Zealand.
* **Long Beach Facility:** Encompasses ~800 employees across two campuses, housing executive offices, satellite manufacturing clean rooms, component testing, and a mission control center.
* **High-Profile Missions:** 
  * **CAPSTONE Moon Mission:** Controlled out of Long Beach and New Zealand, testing a stable gateway lunar orbit for NASA.
  * **ESCAPADE:** Mars-bound spacecraft currently managed out of the Long Beach mission control.
  * **LOxSAT:** A NASA program building a liquid oxygen storage sphere on orbit for long-duration interplanetary missions.
  * **Venus Life Detection Mission:** A conceptual and ongoing scientific pursuit to probe Venus's cloud layer for phosphine gas and potential organic life.
