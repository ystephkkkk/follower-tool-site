* **Historical First Rescue:** Saronic’s 24-foot fully autonomous speedboat, **Corsair**, recently executed the US Navy's first-ever autonomous rescue mission in the Strait of Hormuz, successfully bringing two downed American pilots home safely in a contested combat environment against Iran.
* **Cost and Capability Discrepancies:**

* **Design Optimization:** Removing human support systems (commissaries, latrines, beds, stairs, separate human electrical systems) dramatically reduces the complexity, weight, and cost of autonomous craft.

Saronic's autonomous fleets provide the scale, persistence, and risk reduction needed to lock down or patrol such perilous straits.

## Defense Acquisition, Policy, and AI Integration
* **Moving Away from Cost-Plus:** Saronic operates on firm fixed-price contracts funded by private capital rather than traditional government "cost-plus" contracts (where contractors make a 10-15% margin on top of however long and expensive a project gets).

* **Autonomous Weapons Policy:** US autonomous systems operate under military standards such as **3009**, using artificial intelligence to distinguish between enemy and friendly vessels and non-combatants, while humans retain command intent and engagement thresholds.
