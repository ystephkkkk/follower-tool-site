## TL;DR
President Trump, House Speaker Mike Johnson, and tech executives (including Elon Musk, Jensen Huang, Sam Altman, Dario Amodei, Mark Zuckerberg, and Sundar Pichai) announced a White House accord committing the industry to self-regulation, safety controls, and community partnerships as they build out superintelligence infrastructure.

## White House Accord & Industry Self-Regulation
* **Official Renaming:** President Trump announced that artificial intelligence (AI) has officially been renamed **"SI" (Super Intelligence)**, calling "AI" a fake or inappropriate word.
* **The Accord:** Tech leaders signed an accord establishing industry commitments to safety, robust internal controls, and detection mechanisms for issues.
* **Tiered Auditing & Oversight:** Mark Zuckerberg detailed the multi-layered oversight framework agreed upon:
    * Internal risk reviews conducted by each company.
    * External auditors and evaluators assessing the technology.
    * Independent reviews of audit reports by each company's Board of Directors.
* **Self-Policing vs. Guardrails:** President Trump rejected government slowdowns or heavy federal guardrails, asserting that the industry will successfully self-police and watch over one another.

## Economic Growth, Jobs, & Global Competition
* **Scale of Buildout:** Jensen Huang described the movement as the largest industrial revolution and single largest infrastructure buildout in human history, creating chip plants, computer factories, and a million U.S. jobs alongside hundreds of billions in venture capital.
* **Global Race:** President Trump emphasized that the U.S. is leading China and Europe significantly in SI infrastructure, warning that whoever wins SI wins the world, and that China or European nations would eagerly take any data centers the U.S. rejects.
* **The Future of Labor:** Elon Musk predicted an "age of abundance" characterized by "universal high income" rather than just basic income, comparing job evolution to the historical shift away from human "computers" who did manual calculations.

## Data Centers & Community Partnerships
* **Community Benefits:** President Trump and Speaker Mike Johnson emphasized that data center developers will form direct partnerships with local communities, providing financial support such as funding for school teachers (e.g., Meta paying Richland Parish teachers over $50,000 each).
* **Energy and Grid Protections:** Speaker Johnson highlighted the House's recent codification of the Ratepayer Protection Act, ensuring tech companies produce their own energy resources, put power back on the grid, and protect local ratepayers.
* **Location Strategy:** Trump stated he wants data centers built inside America rather than driven abroad to places like Finland, provided they are located properly within communities to lower taxes and raise incomes.

## Political Context & Border Enforcement
* **Midterms and Party Unity:** Speaker Johnson stated that House Republican candidates are actively campaigning on maintaining America's technological edge over China and balancing community concerns. President Trump asserted that the Republican party is unified and that he won 21 out of 22 swing areas.
* **Immigration & Enforcement:** Trump defended ICE enforcement actions, attributing low crime and poverty numbers to removing violent criminals who entered through the prior administration's open border policies.
