## TL;DR
Qualcomm is spearheading the transition of physical and industrial AI from the cloud to the operational edge, supported by strategic developer integrations and the launch of the IQ10 robotic reference design for humanoid robotics. 

## The Shift to Edge and Physical AI
- **The Operational Plane:** Industrial AI is shifting from cloud-based processing to local, edge-based execution to support real-time decisions.
  - Historically, the edge captured data and shipped it to the cloud; the new architecture processes and acts on data locally.
  - Qualcomm's hardware supports a wide power continuum ranging from 2 milliwatts up to 200 kilowatts.
- **Three Core Tiers of Edge AI:**
  - **Human-Facing AI:** Interactive, consumer-facing experiences, such as retail cameras recognizing products and prices instantly.
  - **Instrumented AI:** Continuous sensory measurements and predictive algorithms to anticipate equipment failures or safety hazards.
  - **Physical AI:** Automated machinery, wearables, and robotic systems operating autonomously in real-world environments.

## Developer and Industrial Ecosystem Expansion
- **Democratizing Developer Access:** Qualcomm acquired Arduino in October 2025 to put edge AI hardware directly into the hands of developers and prosumers.
  - Launched the *Arduino Uno Q* and the upcoming *Ventuno Q* platforms, which feature integrated vision AI models for quick prototyping.
  - Developer boards enabling out-of-the-box AMR (Autonomous Mobile Robot) development will be commercially available on Amazon in August 2026.
- **Key Vertical Implementations:**
  - **Energy & Utilities:** Collaborating on edge AI sensors in dry, high-wind regions like San Diego to detect and mitigate catastrophic fire risks.
  - **Oil & Gas:** Deploying edge AI infrastructures in partnership with Aramco (Saudi Arabia), ADNOC (UAE), Schlumberger, Siemens, and Honeywell.
  - **Retail:** Engineering hybrid environments that combine human presence with full automation, allowing stores to run unmanned during off-hours.

## Robotic Cognitive Architecture (Systems 2, 1, and 0)
- **The Tri-System Framework:** To mimic human-like operations, Qualcomm breaks robotic systems into three tiers of processing:
  - **System Two (The Brain):** High-level reasoning, strategic planning, and cognitive evaluation.
  - **System One (The Body):** Action execution, mobility, and spatial navigation.
  - **System Zero (The Reflexes):** The low-latency nervous system designed for instantaneous reaction and motor safety.
- **Decentralized Motor Control:** Qualcomm is designing chipsets that move motor control from the central brain down to individual limbs, joints, and digits to enable human-like dexterity and high-frequency actuation.

## Hardware Innovation & Humanoid Robotics
- **The IQ Product Line:** The company offers a tiered industrial product line including the IQ8, IQ9, and the newly announced IQ10.
- **IQ10 Robotic Reference Design (RRD):** Announced as a turn-key platform built specifically for humanoid robotics development.
  - **AI Performance:** Delivers 700 sparse TOPS of AI processing.
  - **Compute Power:** Equipped with 18 safety-grade CPU cores.
  - **Sensor Density:** Supports up to 40 cameras simultaneously.
  - **Power Efficiency:** Operates at a Thermal Design Power (TDP) of 50W to 120W.
- **Neura Robotics Partnership:** Established a rapid partnership in early 2026 (initiated in January, finalized in March) to showcase Neura's "4NE1" humanoid robot running on the Qualcomm IQ9 platform.
