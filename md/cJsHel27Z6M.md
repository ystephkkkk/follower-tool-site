## AI and the Changing Economics of Cyberattacks
* **Scale and Speed Advantage:** AI allows attackers to operate at a scale and speed that dwarfs human capability. What takes AI a microsecond would take 70 humans—making human response in the detect-and-respond loop too slow (Kevin Mandia).
* **The Equalizer for Less Capable Attackers:** Models democratize expert-level attack capabilities, meaning less technical attackers will appear far more successful while attribution becomes increasingly difficult (Kevin Mandia).
* **Tactics Shift (Sniper vs. Swarm):** While traditional nation-state espionage relied on targeted "sniper rounds," AI enables a "drone swarm" approach that is louder and sloppier, but vastly more comprehensive and effective (Kevin Mandia).

## Armadin's Offensive & Defensive Approach
* **Armadin Red (Continuous Offense):** Throws a "hyperattack"—a drone swarm of agents mapping every service, route, and system—to create a metadata twin of the target network and test for exploitable risk (Kevin Mandia).
* **Unprecedented Zero-Day Discovery:** Since January 2026, Armadin has found over 90 zero-days at Fortune 500 customer production sites using black-box testing from the internet without source code access (Kevin Mandia).
* **Armadin Blue (Autonomous Defense):** True North for cisos must be effective autonomous response; Armadin works with platforms like CrowdStrike and Palo Alto Networks to create compensating controls and tourniquets at speed before bad actors strike (Kevin Mandia).
* **Caging the Beast:** Armadin combines 15-year veteran red teamers with AI developers, implementing hypervisors, secure proxies, and passive classifiers to ensure safety and prevent agents from going rogue (Kevin Mandia).

## The Future of the Security Stack and the SOC
* **Obsolescence of Traditional Pen Testing:** Traditional pen testing is a hygiene step that scans for known CVEs and creates noise; AI-based attacks find logic flaws, verify exploitability, and carry out actual exploits (Kevin Mandia).
* **Transformation of Prevent, Detect, Respond:** Prevention will be governed by AI, and detection/response will be executed autonomously, drastically narrowing windows and eliminating the traditional human-driven SOC loop (Kevin Mandia).
* **Complete Stack Revamp:** Every tech stack in cybersecurity will be ripped out and revamped over the next few years as companies race to build autonomous defense apparatuses (Kevin Mandia).
