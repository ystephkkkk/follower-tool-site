* **Creating 1,000+ Millionaires:** Following Cerebras's landmark IPO, Feldman emphasized that making employees wealthy is a leader's job, while cautioning them to ignore daily or intraday stock fluctuations.

* **Advice for Newly Wealthy Employees:**
  * **Diversify:** Professional investors do not keep 95%–99% of their wealth in a single stock, and employees shouldn't either; it is okay to sell a little.
