## Tokenization & Financial Infrastructure
* **Blockchain Rails:** Blockchain offers a single source of truth, immediate updates, elimination of costly reconciliation, and smart contract automation that will eventually replace legacy mainframe rails.
* **Tokenized Money Market Fund:** Developed the first SEC-approved natively on-chain tokenized money market fund; parallel processing revealed massive cost efficiencies:
  * Lowers account opening minimums from **$500** (traditional) to **$20** (on-chain), with potential to drop to **$5**.
  * Calculates interest every second, enabling atomic settlement, 24/7 trading, and real-time interest payouts for corporate treasurers and hedge funds.
