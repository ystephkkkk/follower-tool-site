**TL;DR**
The Bank of Japan (BoJ) has signaled its intention to continue raising interest rates as inflation is projected to remain aligned with its 2% target through FY2028, despite significant economic risks posed by geopolitical tensions in the Middle East.

## Economic and Inflation Outlook (FY2026–2028)
* **Price Projections:** Governor Kazuo Ueda noted that inflation (excluding fresh food) is expected to stay in the upper 2% range for FY2026, settling toward 2% in FY2027 and FY2028.
    * **Upward Revision:** The price outlook for FY2026 was revised significantly upward compared to the previous report.
    * **Wage-Price Cycle:** The mechanism where wage increases lead to price hikes remains intact, and medium- to long-term inflation expectations are rising.
* **GDP Growth:** Japan’s economy is expected to continue a moderate recovery.
    * **Short-term Headwinds:** Growth may slow in FY2026 as high oil prices weigh on corporate profits and household real income.
    * **Long-term Recovery:** Recovery is expected to accelerate again from FY2027 as the negative impact of high energy prices fades and the cycle of income-to-spending strengthens.

## Geopolitical Risks and Oil Price Assumptions
* **Middle East Uncertainty:** The BoJ’s core outlook assumes that Middle East tensions will ease and large-scale supply chain disruptions will be avoided.
    * **Oil Pricing:** The forecast is based on Dubai crude starting at approximately $105 per barrel and gradually declining to the $70 range by the end of the forecast period.
* **Dual Risk Profile:** Ueda highlighted a divergent risk balance for FY2026:
    * **Economic Downside:** Prolonged tensions and high oil prices pose a significant downside risk to economic growth.
    * **Inflation Upside:** Conversely, these same factors pose an upside risk to prices, as oil serves as a primary raw material across various industries.

## Monetary Policy Stance
* **Interest Rate Path:** Ueda stated that because real interest rates are currently "extremely low," the BoJ will continue to raise the policy rate if economic and price developments align with their projections.
* **Policy Normalization:** The BoJ aims to adjust the degree of monetary easing to ensure the sustainable and stable achievement of the 2% price target.
    * **Timing and Pace:** Future adjustments will be determined by monitoring the Middle East situation and the degree of certainty regarding the central price outlook.
* **Internal Governance:** The board rejected proposals from members Takata and Tamura to describe price stability as "already achieved" or "closely aligned," opting for a more cautious official description of the progress.

## Shifting Corporate Behavior
* **Price-Setting Activity:** Ueda observed that Japanese companies are becoming more proactive in adjusting wages and prices compared to historical trends.
* **Impact of Cost Shocks:** Due to this shift, increases in raw material costs (like oil) are now passing through to a wider range of goods and services more easily than in the past, potentially accelerating underlying inflation.
