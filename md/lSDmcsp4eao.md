## TL;DR
Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan told Yahoo Finance’s Brian Sozzi that the GPU-to-CPU ratio has shifted from 8:1 to parity (or even higher for certain inference and agent workloads), while emphasizing that Intel will not commit capital without verified customer backing.

## AI Infrastructure and Compute Demand
* **GPU-to-CPU Ratio Shift:** 
  * The historical 8:1 GPU-to-CPU ratio has shifted toward parity as the industry transitions from training to inference, generative AI reinforcement, and orchestration.
  * In certain inference and agent workloads, CPU demand has actually surpassed GPU demand.
* **AI Market Dynamics and Corrections:** 
  * AI is a permanent structural shift comparable to the internet, but rapid over-expansion has led to early corrections and potential market casualties.
  * Security has become critical as millions of autonomous agents operate without proper guidance.
* **Capital and Resource Constraints:** 
  * Memory constraints have driven prices up 6x to 7x, with extreme scarcity meaning buyers may not secure supply even at inflated prices.
  * Raw materials, packaging, and power availability present significant operational hurdles.
  * Total AI capital requirements reach into the trillions of dollars, creating a widening gap between heavy capex spending and realized revenue generation that requires investor patience.

## Intel Corporate Culture and Turnaround Strategy
* **Cultural Re-Engineering:** 
  * Lip-Bu Tan stepped in to lead a multi-year turnaround for an iconic company facing bureaucratic bloat after 50 years of success.

* **Engineering Rigor:** 
  * Emphasizing that engineering must focus past the initial 90% of "cool stuff" to nail the final 10%—specifically defect density, variation control, and yield.
