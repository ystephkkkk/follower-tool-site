## Disaggregated Inference: 5x Data Center Output
- **Divide-and-Conquer Architecture:** Disaggregated inference splits workloads into **prefill** (processing the prompt) and **decode** (generating the answer). Using GPUs for prefill and Cerebras for decode generates **5x** as many tokens at the same speed from the same power footprint (e.g., a 10 MW data center).
- **Live Production & Universal Compatibility:** Cerebras has the world's first production deployment of disaggregated inference running with **AMD** on prefill and **Cerebras** on decode.
  - A software integration layer allows Cerebras to pair with any accelerator—including **NVIDIA GPUs, AWS Trainium, and ARM GPUs**—enabling retrofits into existing brownfield GPU data centers as well as greenfield sites.
- **Addressable Market:** Baker highlighted that if Cerebras were not supply-limited and attached to even 10% of accelerators in 3–4 years, it would represent tens of billions in revenue; Feldman stated Cerebras could attach to *"most"* GPUs/accelerators for inference.

## Wafer-Scale Architecture & Product Roadmap
- **First-Principles Bet:** Rather than making incremental GPU tweaks, Cerebras started with a clean sheet in late 2015/early 2016 to build the largest chip in compute history—**56x larger** than any prior chip—using **SRAM** instead of HBM/DRAM.
  - Baker noted this optimizes for memory bandwidth in a way that is uniquely hard to replicate, giving Cerebras a **15x–30x** (10x–30x) speed advantage in inference by getting weights to compute faster.
- **Three Consecutive Doublings:**
  - **CS4:** Just starting to ship; **2x** as fast as CS3.
  - **CS5:** Ships in summer; **2x** as fast as CS4.
  - **CS6:** Ships in summer 2028; at least **2x** as fast as CS5.

## Dual Business Model: Cloud vs. Hardware Cash Flow
- **Supply Chain & Q4/Q1 Ramp:** Following the December 25, 2025 OpenAI deal, Feldman flew to Taiwan to secure increased forecasts from TSMC and Cerebras's top 10–15 vendors, with new chips arriving in May/June. Data center delays pushed Q3 capacity into Q4 and Q1.
  - Baker noted Q4 revenue is up ~40% sequentially at the midpoint, and Feldman expressed *"high hopes"* for a similar uptick in Q1.
- **Cloud vs. Hardware Trade-offs:**
  - **Cloud:** Provides multi-year backlog and recurring revenue, but is capital-intensive with a 16–18 month payback period on gear.
  - **Hardware:** Lumpy (starts at zero each quarter) and requires customers to have their own data centers, but delivers immediate revenue recognition and strong cash flow.
- **Hyperscaler Hardware Deployments:** **Oracle, AWS, and Microsoft** will deploy Cerebras hardware in their own data centers, with the Oracle opportunity **more than double** AWS and Microsoft, beginning in **Q2 next year**.
  - Baker challenged the Street's consensus of a $2.5B cash burn next year given hardware's cash flow profile; Feldman confirmed hardware revenue will exceed cloud revenue in *"several"* quarters next year.
