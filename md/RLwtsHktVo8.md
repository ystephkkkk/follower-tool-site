## 2. AI-Powered Payments Optimization & Pricing Flexibility
* **Platform Growth Studio & Stripe Console:**
  * **AI-Generated Insights:** Console acts as an agentic payments expert built directly into the Stripe dashboard, proactively raising issues (e.g., volume rising 7.9% while take rate fell 6.5 basis points).
  * **Automated Root-Cause Analysis:** Console investigates payment mix, methods, geographies, and network costs; in a demo case, it traced a margin decline to a single connected account (*Northeast Yoga*) due to an increase in average transaction size.
  * **Network Benchmarking:** Compares platform performance against Stripe network medians (e.g., comparing Northeast Yoga's 271 bps take rate to a platform median of 400 bps and a Stripe vertical median of 290 bps).
  * **Ecosystem Data Scale:** Platforms add 35,000 new merchants and process 67 million transactions daily.
  * **Early Adoption & Expansion:** Private preview users include *Set* (sized opportunity for in-person payments), *Daysmart* (spotted underpriced card volume), and *Circle* (resolved support tickets without engineering hours). Console is rolling out globally in the next few weeks.
  * **Developer Access:** Data teams can access this intelligence via Stripe Sigma, Stripe data pipeline, and Stripe's MCP for external agents like GPT.
* **Granular Pricing Controls:**
  * **Funded Discounts:** Platforms can choose individual merchants, give custom discounts, and fund the difference from their revenue share.
  * **Pass-Through vs. Absorbed Fees:** Platforms owning their own pricing can configure fee-by-fee costs (e.g., *TSD Mobility* charges core processing fees like per authorization and network fees; *CAB9* charges for value-added services like 3D secure lookup) using the platform pricing tool.

## 3. Going Beyond Payments: Embedded Finance Upgrades
* **Financial Home Impact:** 
  * Platforms embedding multiple financial services see a **40% increase in customer lifetime value**.
  * Platforms embedding multiple financial products see **49% faster revenue growth** than those offering payments alone.
* **Stripe Treasury Updates:**
  * **Demand Deposit Accounts (DDAs):** Launching real, FDIC-insured bank accounts in the US via partner bank Fifth Third.
  * **New Banking Features:** Customers can accept/send checks (physical checkbooks launching early next year), load cash at over 60,000 retail locations (Walgreens, CVS), receive monthly statements, and earn cash rewards on balances.
  * **Federal Reserve Connection:** Direct connection enables same-day ACH payments hours closer to end-of-day cutoff, launching in two weeks.
  * **Direct Treasury Deposits:** Customers can receive payments directly into their Treasury balance without transferring from a separate payments balance.
* **Stripe Issuing:**
  * Platforms like *Shopify*, *Lope*, and *Give Butter* have distributed over **400 million cards** via Stripe.
  * New features include offering rewards on card spend without running a separate card business.
* **Stripe Capital Expansion:**
  * **Growth Impact:** Businesses accepting capital offers on Stripe grew **27 percentage points faster** over two years than those that didn't.
  * **Momentum:** The number of platforms distributing capital with Stripe grew **89% year-over-year**.
  * **Notable Users:** *Brightwheel* (preschools), *Jane* (health practitioners), and *FedEx* (shipping tens of thousands of SMBs).
  * **Acquisition:** Stripe agreed to acquire *Paraffin* (which powers platforms like *Gusto*, *Jobber*, and *MindBody*) to expand credit products.
  * **Future Capital Roadmap:** Building capital lines of credit, shorter-duration financing (draw-down as needed), context-specific capital (pre-funding marketing, advancing invoices, covering payroll), and expanding eligibility to merchants with zero historical Stripe volume.

## 4. Monetizing AI Products & Agentic Workflows
* **Agentic Commerce Explosion:**
  * Agent volume processed on Stripe grew **21x in September** alone following launches of *Muse* and *Instinct*.
  * Use cases span hotels, doctor appointments, concert tickets, and philanthropy (e.g., *Donor Box* accepted the world's first agentic donation in September).
* **Stripe Checkout (Agent-Ready):**
  * Uses Model Context Protocol (MCP) to let verified agents land on pages and check out.
  * Trusted agents complete transactions **98% of the time** and **39% faster** than prior Checkout versions.
  * Early adopters include *Woo*, *Thinkific*, and *Simple Practice*.
* **Hybrid & Usage-Based Pricing via Metronome:**
  * **The Challenge:** Platforms like *HubSpot* (launching AI agent hubs for CRM and marketing) need to monetize AI without destroying seat-based pricing models.
  * **Metronome Acquisition:** Acquired by Stripe earlier this year to simplify hybrid usage-based and outcome-based pricing.
  * **Platform Credits:** Allows mixing seat-based pricing with credit purchases (e.g., AI receptionist consumes 5 credits; automatic marketing campaign consumes 40 credits).
  * **User Control & Visibility:** Includes product dashboard widgets showing remaining credits, usage tracking, and automatic limits.
  * **Frictionless Top-Ups:** Offers scaling credit discounts and pre-configured automatic recharges for heavy users.
  * **Enterprise Adoption:** Used in production by *HubSpot*, *Notion*, and *C1*.
