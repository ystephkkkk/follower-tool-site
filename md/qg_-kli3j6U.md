The European Union and the United States have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) and a new action plan to secure critical mineral supply chains, combat global steel overcapacity, and deepen transatlantic economic security to reduce dependencies on non-allied nations.

## Critical Minerals and Supply Chain Resilience
*   **MoU and Action Plan:** EU Trade Commissioner Maroš Šefčovič and U.S. counterparts established a framework for cooperation spanning the entire mineral lifecycle—from extraction and processing to recycling and recovery.
*   **Market Stabilization Tools:** To support long-term projects, the partners are exploring "plurilateral trade initiatives" that may include:
    *   **Price Floors and Corridors:** Guaranteed price levels to protect investors from market volatility.
    *   **Off-take Agreements:** Long-term contracts to ensure demand for newly extracted minerals.
    *   **Joint Investments:** Co-investing via the European Investment Bank and U.S. financial instruments to provide the "patient capital" required for 7-to-10-year development cycles.
*   **Strategic Diversification:** Commissioner Šefčovič emphasized that the move is a response to the "expensive" lesson of fossil fuel dependency, aiming to diversify suppliers for critical technologies and minerals.

## Steel Sector Protection and "Ring-Fencing"
*   **Combating Overcapacity:** Global steel overcapacity is projected to reach 721 million tons next year—five times the EU’s annual consumption.
*   **New EU Trade Barriers:** Starting in July, the EU will implement aggressive measures:
    *   A **47% cut** in tariff-free imports.
    *   A **50% tariff** on volumes exceeding set quotas.
    *   **"Melt and Pour" Principle:** Strict rules to define the origin of steel and prevent third countries from circumventing tariffs.
*   **Transatlantic "Ring-Fencing":** The EU and U.S. are discussing a "defensive mechanism" to create a shared trade zone that allows favorable terms for allies while blocking subsidized or dumped steel from external markets.

## Economic Security and Diplomatic Alignment
*   **The "Transatlantic Muscle":** Combined, the U.S. ($30 trillion) and EU ($20 trillion) economies represent a massive "pull factor" capable of setting global standards for mining, processing, and recycling.
*   **Shifting Relationships:** Šefčovič noted that the EU-U.S. relationship has shifted from confrontational to deeply cooperative over the last year, moving away from "hyper-globalization" toward a strategy of economic security.
