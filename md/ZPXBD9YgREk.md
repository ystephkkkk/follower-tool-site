**TL;DR:** Meta Connect 2026 announced major developer updates across four pillars—expanding the Muse AI model family and general availability of the Meta Model API, developer tools for Ray-Ban AI glasses with SDK and web app support, the V207 SDK for upcoming Meta VR Glasses, and the launch of Horizon Create and Studio for prompt-driven game development.

## AI Models, APIs, and Development Tools

* **Muse Model Family Expansion:** Meta Superintelligence Labs released 10 models and products over the past few months, tailored for reasoning, media, and voice.
  * **Muse Spark 1.3:** Competes at the frontier with max reasoning capabilities, allowing developers to delegate toughest problems to teams of sub-agents. Consistently a top-two model on OpenCode Go, powering over 120 million sessions and 79 trillion tokens.
  * **Muse Spark on OpenRouter:** Reached over 2 trillion tokens per week within eight weeks of launch, with over 90% of tokens coming from AI-native companies and professional developers.
  * **Muse Image:** Launched in August for programmatic generation of production-grade images at scale.
  * **Muse Voice Transcribe:** Features state-of-the-art speech-to-text performance at launch, with text-to-speech and voice-to-voice capabilities planned.
* **Muse Code CLI Agent:** A terminal-installed coding agent powered by Muse Spark, featuring multi-agent parallel execution in isolated workspaces, structured planning for long-horizon work, and predictable flat-rate subscription pricing.
* **Open-Weight Form Factors:** Meta expanded developer access with two distinct open-weight models:
  * **Muse Glimmer:** A 30-billion dense model for always-on local agents that runs on a single GPU or Mac Mini, available under an Apache 2.0 license.
  * **Muse Spark 1.2:** Open weights will soon be released directly to the developer community.
* **Meta Model API General Availability:** Now generally available globally for enterprise-scale workloads, featuring standard production tiers with zero data training and a contributor tier offering frontier intelligence at up to 80% below standard rates. Also available via Oracle Cloud AI platform and in private preview with Google Cloud.
* **Meta Global AI Hackathon:** Kicks off virtually this October, backed by free API credits and a $1 million cash prize pool.

## Wearables and AI Glasses SDKs

* **AI Glasses Growth:** Usage of Meta AI glasses has tripled in the last year, with millions of users worldwide relying on context, voice UI, and agentic capabilities for "mental offload."
* **Device Access Toolkit Extensions:** Allows mobile apps to tap into AI glasses context (camera, microphone, sensors). New updates add advanced camera capture controls, motion and speech capabilities (using IMU data for gaze-aware experiences like bird identification), and voice invocation via the Meta AI wake word.
* **Web Apps Toolkit & Web MCP Support:** Enables web standards to build new experiences for Meta Ray-Ban displays, with web MCP support allowing agentic control of web apps through single-line-of-code voice commands.
* **Discovery Surfaces:** Two new surfaces allow users to discover developer apps: direct on-device discovery on Meta Ray-Ban Display, and a curated section in the Meta AI app.
* **New York Times Cooking Partnership:** Integrated their mobile app with AI glasses using the Device Access Toolkit and Neural Band gesture navigation to launch a hands-free cooking mode this fall.
* **Meta AI Connectors:** Developer preview launched for connecting services directly into Meta AI and Muse without users leaving the conversation.

## VR Glasses and Quest Ecosystem

* **Meta VR Glasses and SDK V207:** Meta VR Glasses ship in 2027, but SDK V207 is live immediately. The glasses are light (~100 grams), controller-free (using eye, hand, and voice tracking), and backward compatible with controllers.
* **Developer Readiness and Agentic Workflows:** Features full agentic workflows for VR across game engines, Android, and web tools (Meta XR Operator and Immersive Web Emulation Runtime), enabling tasks like frame rate optimization in 15 minutes.
* **Testing and Simulation Tools:** 
  * **XR Simulator:** Test full VR scenes on desktop with glasses inputs via webcam.
  * **Spatial Simulator:** Desktop emulator running real OS pieces for 2D Android apps with pin-mode checking.
  * **Field of View Emulator:** Preview Quest apps on VR glasses fields of view.
* **Store Performance and Benchmarks:** Store search rebuilt for intent, categories, and misspellings, increasing daily searches by 15%+ and user conversions by 20%+. Major updates launched for the Benchmarks tool, including peer sets.
* **Meta VR Start Developer Competition:** Second annual competition launched with an $1 million prize pool and an 8-week build window.

## Horizon Game Creation Engine

* **Horizon Create (Mobile):** A mobile app where users can play community games and build playable 2D/3D games using natural language prompts while on the go.
* **Horizon Studio (Web/Browser):** A browser-based one-stop-shop tool running with desktop-grade depth. Features visual editing, scene views, script parameters, and **Canvas Mode** (a structured map of game logic, enemies, and leveling that populates automatically). Also acts as a live ops partner post-publishing.
* **Feed Distribution:** Published games are instantly available on Horizon and surfaced directly in Facebook and Instagram feeds without app downloads or store page navigation.
* **Future Collaborative Vision:** Real-time, collaborative multi-creator development in a shared world supported by a swarm of AI agents is currently in closed Alpha and opening broadly in coming weeks.
