**TL;DR**
President Donald Trump, joined by Vice President JD Vance, Secretary of State Marco Rubio, and Chief of Staff Stephen Miller, recapped a four-day Asia trip highlighted by a historic US–Russia–China trilateral nuclear summit, over $17 billion in ASEAN commercial deals, and new trade agreements with Thailand and the Philippines. Domestically, the administration touted Senate-passed permitting reform, 10 straight months of manufacturing growth, and a 97% drop in maritime fentanyl, while announcing a ban on US companies building data centers abroad and a $50 billion request to overhaul air traffic control.

## Asia Trip, Trade Deals & Global Diplomacy
*   **Trip Overview & Air Force One:** President Trump reported meeting more than 20 world leaders, attending four major summits, securing two new trade deals (with ~10 more pending), and completing a record 14.5-hour nonstop Air Force One flight (4 hours shorter than the previous record, compared to a typical 20-hour flight with fuel stops).
*   **First-Ever US–Russia–China Trilateral Summit:** Held in Shenzhen, China, bringing together the nations holding 92% of the world's nuclear weapons.
    *   **Nuclear & Security Agreements:** The three nations signed a trilateral peace declaration stating nuclear conflict must never happen. China agreed for the first time to trilateral nuclear reduction talks (Trump noted the US has ~4,000 nuclear weapons, Russia ~3,000+, and China ~600).
    *   **Energy & ICC:** The three leaders agreed to increase oil, gasoline, and diesel production, and jointly condemned the International Criminal Court (ICC).
*   **ASEAN & Bilateral Deals (Manila):** President Trump secured more than $17 billion in commercial deals across 14 ASEAN nations.
    *   **Philippines:** Philippine Airlines will purchase 20 Boeing 787 Dreamliners with General Electric engines (supporting 47,000 US jobs). Following a state visit with President Ferdinand Marcos, the Philippines committed to buying >$2 billion in US military equipment, 100% of its agricultural imports (starting January 1), and 100% of its energy (up from 20%) from the US, alongside a framework deal slashing Philippine tariffs.
    *   **Thailand:** Signed an agreement eliminating all tariffs and non-tariff barriers on US products and committing to buy $2.6 billion/year in US agricultural goods.
    *   **Cloud Infrastructure:** Both the Philippines and Malaysia agreed to use US technology for their cloud infrastructure.
*   **Quad Leaders Summit (Manila):** Leaders of the US, India, Australia, and Japan launched the first-ever Quad Bureau Leaders Forum (for economic security and supply chain resiliency), a Quad Super Intelligence (SI) accelerator, and new maritime security and surveillance collaboration.
*   **APEC & Western Hemisphere (Shenzhen):**
    *   **China:** Trump noted fentanyl entering the US by sea is down 97%, citing his prior 145% tariffs and 20% fentanyl penalty.
    *   **Mexico & Canada:** Trump discussed cartels and trade surpluses with Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum, and pushed Canadian PM Carney on the Keystone Pipeline and the $80 billion US trade deficit with Canada.
*   **Ukraine, Iran & Greenland:**
    *   **Ukraine–Russia:** Trump met with President Putin, noting 25,000–30,000 soldiers (averaging 25,933/month) are dying. He criticized the Biden administration for giving Ukraine $350 billion in cash and equipment, noting the US now sells weapons to the EU.
    *   **Iran:** Trump stated Iran "cannot be allowed to have a nuclear weapon," noting the US Navy has total control over the Strait of Hormuz and that Iran has killed 75,000 protesters over the last eight months.
    *   **Greenland:** Trump announced Greenland was "just approved yesterday," thanking Denmark and NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte.

## Economic Performance, Manufacturing & Tech Policy
*   **Macroeconomic Metrics:** Trump reported 10 straight months of manufacturing growth, a 21% increase in new equipment investment this year, $25 trillion in new wealth created since taking office (with $4 trillion lost since the midterms), and an all-time record low poverty rate.
*   **Energy Prices:** Crude oil is down 20% since early November to ~$80/barrel, and gasoline is down 60 cents/gallon in the last month (hitting $1.85/gallon in Iowa prior to the Iran operation).
*   **Federal Reserve & Interest Rates:** Trump criticized the "political Fed" for raising rates, stating each percentage point costs $650 billion and arguing lower rates could push US GDP to 14–20% (compared to India's 7%).
*   **Manufacturing & Defense Reshoring:** Audi announced a $4 billion plant, Toyota is building a $5 billion plant, and GM and Ford are expanding—including a 1906 Detroit Ford plant now operating 24/7. Defense contractors are also building 21 new plants for Patriots, Tomahawks, and THAAD systems.
*   **Super Intelligence (SI) & Data Centers:**
    *   Trump and President Xi agreed to establish joint guardrails on SI.
    *   Trump announced the US will not allow American companies to build data centers abroad (e.g., in Finland), threatening "tremendous" taxes and penalties. He cited local deals where data centers pay teachers $50,000 in Louisiana and residents $10,000 apiece elsewhere.
    *   Trump praised Anthropic's recent launch and noted the US is 10 years ahead of China in chipmaking, stating only older chips—not Nvidia's latest Blackwell chip—should go to China.

## Domestic Policy, Infrastructure & Bipartisan Prospects
*   **Permitting Reform:** Landmark permitting reform legislation passed the Senate and heads to the House, projected to put at least $1,300/year back into Americans' pockets.
*   **Air Traffic Control (ATC) & Tech Outages:** Trump attributed recent bank and corporate outages to separate, routine system upgrades. However, he called the 55-year-old US ATC system "obsolete" and requested $50 billion from Congress (on top of $12 billion already allocated) to build a unified, modern system.
*   **Crime & Border Security:** Trump reported D.C. crime is down 87%, Memphis crime is down 81%, and the US murder rate is at a 125-year low (since 1900). He stated zero people have entered the US illegally in the last 18 months (compared to 25–30 million under Biden).
*   **Bipartisan Legislation:** Trump expressed openness to working with Democratic leaders Hakeem Jeffries and Chuck Schumer on healthcare, a federal entertainment/film production incentive bill (backed by Sen. Adam Schiff), and a college sports/NIL bill (backed by Randy Levine and Coach Nick Saban) to prevent colleges—some losing $500 million/year—from going bankrupt.

## Administration Perspectives (Vance, Rubio, Miller)
*   **Vice President JD Vance:** Connected the administration's global trade deals with the Senate's bipartisan permitting reform, arguing both are required to incentivize domestic investment in US workers and factories. On the Iran operation, Vance defended the goal of preventing a nuclear Iran, stating his job is to support the President's decisions.
*   **Secretary of State Marco Rubio:** Explained that ASEAN, the Quad, and APEC directly create markets and jobs for American workers. He emphasized that regular engagement among the US, Russia, and China—the world's three largest nuclear powers—is essential for global safety and sets the tone for diplomacy.
*   **Chief of Staff Stephen Miller:** Highlighted the administration's record, including 10-to-1 net deregulation, ending DEI, record fossil fuel and coal production, the first new nuclear reactors since the 1970s, taking over Venezuela in under an hour (doubling US oil reserves), and defeating 20%+ inflation.
