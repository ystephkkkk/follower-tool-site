## 3. Impact on Jobs, Labor Shortages, and Tradecraft
* **Labor Market Paradox:** Rather than causing widespread unemployment, the massive infrastructure build-out is creating a historic boom in tradecraft jobs.
* **Skilled Trade Shortages & Salaries:**
  * High demand for plumbers, electricians, construction workers, steelworkers, and network technicians.
  * U.S. trade salaries have nearly doubled, commanding **six-figure salaries** for workers building chip, computer, and AI factories.
* **Healthcare Professions (Jensen Huang):**
  * **Radiology:** Despite 100% AI diffusion over the past 10 years, the number of radiologists has *increased*. AI accelerates scan analysis, allowing doctors to spend more time diagnosing patients and boosting hospital patient capacity and revenue.
  * **Nursing:** U.S. hospitals face a **5 million nurse shortage**. Tools like Abridge automate charting and clinical transcription (which consumes half a nurse's time), allowing nurses to see more patients and driving hospital hiring.
* **Purpose vs. Task Framework:** AI automates *tasks* (such as typing or reviewing scans), which enhances the human *purpose* of a job (such as caring for people).
