Kalshi co-founder and CEO Tarek Mansour discussed the 98% accuracy rate of Kalshi's prediction markets during the midterms—where trading volume exceeded $2.5 billion—and addressed future regulation, bipartisan insider trading bans, and market manipulation concerns.

## Midterm Accuracy & Market Dynamics
* **Election Precision:** 
  * Kalshi markets achieved a **98% accuracy rate** during the midterms, with total trading volume exceeding **$2.5 billion**.
  * The prediction markets signaled over a **50% chance of a "blue tsunami" back in April**, despite political operatives and pundits dismissing the data for months.

* **Information Filtering & Rigor:**
  * Mansour compared the integration of financial market rigor into politics to the stock market analyzing Apple or Tesla.

  * Markets successfully filter out false rumors; for example, a viral social media rumor about a candidate being in an Ohio hospital moved prediction markets **0%**.

## Market Manipulation & Integrity
* **Resistance to Manipulation:**
  * While bad actors attempt to manipulate markets—such as an instance in the LA mayoral race where **$1.5 million to $2 million** was dumped to move a price from **25% to 40%**—the distortion lasted only **9 seconds** before correcting back to **25%**.
  * Mansour compared artificial price-pushing to the Treasury trying to control the 10-year yield without enough capital to sustain it.
* **Transparency and Policing:**
  * Kalshi flags suspicious activity transparently on charts, and intentional market manipulation constitutes a financial crime.
  * Mansour strongly advocated against insider trading, arguing that free markets cannot exist if participants have exclusive privileges. He supports banning members of Congress, the administration, and others from trading on things under their control.

## Regulation & Future Outlook
* **Regulatory Stance:**
  * Founded in **2018**, Kalshi spent its first **3 to 4 years** getting regulated under the Biden administration and maintains a "regulatory first," pro-innovation, and pro-regulation approach.
  * Mansour noted that Kalshi engages with both sides of the aisle and supports sensible guardrails, including adding more CFTC commissioners to create productive push-and-pull oversight.
  * Regarding potential ethics bills (such as those suggested by Rahm Emanuel targeting stock and prediction market trading for politicians and White House staff), Mansour stated he would fully favor strict rules and bans on insider trading for officials.
