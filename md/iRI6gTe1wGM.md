AI agents could drive 5,000x to 10,000x more web activity than humans, fundamentally transforming the internet from a reactive pull model to a proactive push model. Parag Agrawal, former Twitter CEO & CTO and current Founder & CEO of Parallel Web Systems, is building the search infrastructure for this agent-first internet, which recently closed a $100 million Series B at a $2 billion valuation.

  - Latency requirements for voice agents demand web search results in **150 to 200 milliseconds** (compared to human sub-second products at 600-700 ms).

## The Macro Shift: Agent Web Activity & GPUs
- **Agent Traffic vs. Human Traffic:**
  - Automated agent traffic on the web is "ripping," but actual agent utilization is estimated at **10% to 20% of humans**.
  - Assuming Parag's personal agent usage (several thousand searches per day) becomes mainstream, there is an estimated **5,000x to 10,000x growth** remaining.
- **GPU Sourcing & Infrastructure:**
  - Parallel rents GPUs at scale to meet search traffic demands.
  - The GPU market is characterized by complex physical requirements (placement within 5 milliseconds of a web index) and informal sourcing channels (coordinating availability via iMessage).

## The Economic & Business Model Crisis of the Web
- **Breakdown of Traditional Models:**
  - Traditional monetization (subscription paywalls, human-viewed display ads, affiliate actions) relies on humans "random walking" the web.
  - Because buying actions remain but attention monetization breaks, ad networks and perceived subscription models are not durable.
- **Parallel's Compensation Solution:**
  - Built a system running a model to compute how much to pay each content owner every time an agent reads their content.
  - Acts as a modern "AdSense" equivalent to provide content owners financial incentive and keep the web open.

## Cybersecurity, Safety, and Team Building
