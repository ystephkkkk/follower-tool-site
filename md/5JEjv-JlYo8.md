## TL;DR
Gagan Biyani, Co-Founder of Udemy and Maven, has launched the Horowitz Andreessen Academy (backed by a $42M funding round) as an AI-native, unaccredited residential program in San Francisco designed to optimize high agency young builders for proof of work rather than traditional grades.

## Academy Structure & Founding Class Model
- **Founding Class Fellowship:** **Gagan Biyani** announced a one-year, tuition-free fellowship starting in September 2027 that will bring roughly 50 students to San Francisco for deep immersion in Silicon Valley culture, builder-first mentalities, and interpersonal skills.
- **Tuition & Scale Target:** Pending regulatory approval, the academy plans to launch its two-year paid program in 2028 with tuition comparable to elite private universities, aiming for full financial self-sufficiency and profitability.
- **Capital Runway:** **Gagan Biyani** noted that the $40M+ raised from a16z and angel investors (including Adam D’Angelo, Tobi Lütke, Tony Xu, Fidji Simo, and Shyam Sankar) was deliberately sized to fund the school for several years, ensuring long-term operational stability.
- **The Student Persona:** The academy targets high-agency "young builders" who are intrinsically motivated, with **Gagan Biyani** noting applicants are evaluated primarily on slope, portfolio proof of work, and personal projects rather than 4.0 GPAs or traditional college credentials.

## Curriculum & Pedagogical Design
- **75-80% Project Focus:** Students spend the vast majority of their time on research, learning, and entrepreneurial building projects rather than sitting through top-down lecture classes.
- **Didactic Seminars:** Classes are strictly used to teach concepts like product sense, AI architecture, negotiations, and interpersonal skills, which students then immediately apply to their independent projects.
- **Peer Accountability Pods:** Inspired by Oxford tutorials, Montessori, and Alpha School, students meet regularly in small pods to present what they built the previous week and commit to the next week's milestones.

## Industry Partnerships & Ecosystem Access
- **Founding Partners:** The academy is built alongside foundational partners including Anthropic, Anduril, Coinbase, Meta, NVIDIA, OpenAI, Palantir, Replit, and Stripe.
- **Compute and Curricular Support:** Founding partners provide curricular feedback, advisory support, compute credits, and direct access to frontier models and top engineering talent.
- **Hiring & Collaboration Partners:** The ecosystem includes over 30 hiring partners, such as Shopify (noted by **Gagan Biyani** as an investor alongside leaders Archie and Tobi Lütke), to bridge the gap between student output and industry employment.
