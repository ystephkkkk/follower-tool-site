US Ambassador Mike Waltz detailed a paradigm shift in American foreign policy characterized by aggressive UN budgetary reforms and the "Donroe Doctrine," prioritizing Western Hemisphere security and results-oriented "focused multilateralism" over traditional, symbolism-heavy diplomacy.

## UN Reform and "Make the UN Great Again" (MUNGA)
*   **Budgetary Reductions:** Ambassador Mike Waltz announced the first-ever budget cut in UN history, emphasizing a "back to basics" approach to peacekeeping and peacemaking.
    *   **15% overall budget reduction** approved in December.
    *   **3,000 headquarters bureaucrats** and 18% of total personnel to be eliminated.
    *   **25% cut to peacekeeping** funds to ensure the organization is "fit for purpose."
*   **Fiscal Responsibility:** Waltz argued that US leadership in reform is mandatory because the US pays more in dues than 180 other countries combined, making the "status quo" unsustainable for American taxpayers.
*   **Operational Focus:** The US is pushing the UN to abandon its attempt to "be everything to every nation" and return to its core founding mandates.

## The "Donroe Doctrine" and Hemispheric Security
*   **Strategic Shift:** The US has articulated a "Trump corollary" to the Monroe Doctrine (termed the "Donroe Doctrine"), asserting explicit US leadership and power projection within the Western Hemisphere.
*   **Colombia’s Role:** Defense Minister Sanchez emphasized that the drug trade is a global economic scourge rather than just a local military issue.
    *   **Human Cost:** Over 2,000 Colombian military and police personnel were killed or injured in the last 3.5 years fighting the drug trade.
    *   **"Americas First":** President Petro adjusted the "America First" slogan to "Americas" (plural), signaling a desire for a continental partnership rather than US isolationism.
*   **Combatting Disinformation:** Both Waltz and Sanchez highlighted disinformation as a primary threat to regional security, noting it nearly derailed US-Colombia relations by misrepresenting Colombia’s efforts against drug cartels.

## The Gaza Plan and the "Board of Peace"
*   **Focused Multilateralism:** Waltz touted the creation of a "Board of Peace" to bypass the perceived stagnation of the UN Security Council regarding the conflict in Gaza.
    *   **Regional Inclusion:** The board involves Gulf Arab nations and Muslim-majority states, including Turkey, Pakistan, and Indonesia.
    *   **Troop Contributions:** Indonesia has publicly committed 8,000 troops to the peace efforts.
*   **Alternative to Hamas:** The strategy seeks a "third way" between Hamas rule and endless war, utilizing a Palestinian Technocratic Committee and a specialized funding mechanism.
*   **Diplomatic Wins:** Waltz cited the first direct flight between Azerbaijan and Armenia and new wheat shipments through the Caucasus as evidence that this focused approach is yielding results.

    *   **The "Allies" Advantage:** Kallas noted that the primary difference between the US and other superpowers (like Russia or China) is the US's network of allies who choose to cooperate.
