## TL;DR
The European Central Bank (ECB) raised its three key interest rates by 25 basis points in a unanimous decision, aiming to stabilize inflation at its 2% target amid ongoing energy shocks and geopolitical conflicts. While headline inflation remains well above target, the Eurozone economy has demonstrated unexpected resilience.

## Economic Outlook & Projections
- **Headline Inflation Projections:** Averaging 3% in 2026, 2.5% in 2027, and 2.1% in 2028 (unchanged for 2026 compared to June, but revised up for 2027 and 2028).
- **Core Inflation Projections (excluding energy and food):** 2.5% in 2026, 2.6% in 2027, and 2.3% in 2028.
- **Economic Growth Projections:** Revised upward to 0.9% for 2026, 1.4% for 2027, and 1.5% for 2028, reflecting greater-than-expected economic resilience.
- **Labor Market Performance:** Unemployment rate remained unchanged at 6.4% in July; compensation per employee grew at an annual rate of 3.3% in Q2 (down from 3.5% in Q1); unit labor costs slowed to 2.6%.

## Inflation Drivers & Market Conditions
- **Energy Pressures:** August headline inflation increased to 3.3% (up from 2.9% in July), driven by energy price inflation rising to 14.3% due to higher refining margins on liquid fuels and energy commodity prices.
- **Food Price Stability:** Food price inflation remained unchanged at 1.2%, aided by milder early-year weather and contained overall cost pass-throughs.
- **Financing Costs & Lending:** Bank lending rates for firms rose to 3.8% in June and July (up from 3.6% in May); market-based corporate debt stood at 4% in July; mortgage rates remained unchanged at 3.5%.
- **Balance Sheet Reduction:** The ECB’s liquidity pool has contracted from 4.7 trillion down to 2.1 trillion euros as the central bank lets bonds expire to maturity without new purchases.

## Geopolitical & Structural Challenges
- **Geopolitical Risks:** The Middle East conflict and Russia's war against Ukraine continue to push energy prices upward, with potential risks of gas supply disruptions during winter.
- **Structural Reforms:** President Lagarde emphasized the need to simplify single market rules, accelerate the energy transition, complete the savings and investments union, and reach an agreement on the digital euro framework.
- **Sovereign Debt Stability:** Lagarde dismissed debt cancellation proposals as illegal under Article 123 of the European treaty, technically unviable, and financially dangerous.

## Predictions & Forward Calls

- **Digital Euro Legislation:** Agreement and voting on the single currency package (digital euro legislation) in the European pre-log are anticipated before the end of the year — Christine Lagarde.
- **Future Rate Path:** The ECB is following a data-dependent, meeting-by-meeting approach and is explicitly *not* pre-committing to a particular future rate path — Christine Lagarde.
