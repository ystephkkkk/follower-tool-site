* **Token Efficiency:** **Remarkable Price-Performance:** Astra features highly impressive token efficiency, delivering significant work output for a relatively small amount of tokens and driving down the price per task.

* **Cyber-Critical Threshold:** **Zero-Day Exploits:** Astra hit cyber-critical triggers during testing by finding and developing zero-day exploits without human intervention, requiring strict adherence to OpenAI's preparedness framework and safety alignments.

## Public Perception and Industry Messaging
* **Communication Failure:** **Terrible Job on Benefits:** Responding to Treasury Secretary Scott Besson's G20 critique, Altman agreed that the AI industry and OpenAI have done a poor job communicating how AI benefits ordinary Americans and empowering them with autonomy.
* **Balancing Risk and Empowerment:** Altman noted that while the world should want developers to worry about risks and debate them openly, messaging has mistakenly focused on high-level promises (like curing cancer) at the expense of fostering a boom in human creativity and entrepreneurship.
