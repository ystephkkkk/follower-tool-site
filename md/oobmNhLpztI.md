## TL;DR
Infineon is addressing the massive energy demands of the AI revolution by delivering power and sensing solutions across the entire value chain—spanning grid infrastructure, ultra-high-density data center cores, and robotic physical AI. Through key innovations in silicon, silicon carbide (SiC), and gallium nitride (GaN), Infineon aims to solve the industry's critical bottleneck: scaling AI sustainably.

## Grid-to-Data-Center Power Infrastructure
- **Solid-State Grid Modernization:** Infineon is working with partners to replace conventional 20-ton transformers with semiconductor-based solid-state transformers.
  - The new solid-state transformers are projected to be 30% lighter with significantly increased energy efficiency.
- **Ultra-Fast Fault Protection:** Solid-state circuit breakers are now actively deploying to protect high-voltage data centers and microgrids.
  - **CoolSiC JFET Technology:** These breakers achieve microsecond-level fault isolation, representing a massive leap from legacy millisecond-level mechanical isolation.
  - Infineon announced an expanded portfolio of JFETs supporting up to 1200V with advanced package technology to scale this deployment.
- **Data Center Rack Power Evolution:** Racks are rapidly transitioning from legacy single-phase designs (below 200–300 kW) to Gen 2 three-phase architectures (500–660 kW), heading toward a "North Star" goal of 1-megawatt racks.
- **Agentic AI CPU Support:** Infineon is partnering with major vendors to support the "miniature revolution" of CPU-centric Agentic AI, which demands highly reliable, scalable power architectures as CPU-to-GPU ratios shift.

## Materials Strategy: Silicon, SiC, and GaN
- **A Three-Technology Approach:** Infineon believes no single material can address the AI bottleneck; optimal efficiency requires a hybrid application of Silicon, Silicon Carbide (SiC), and Gallium Nitride (GaN).
- **Silicon Leadership & OptiMOS 8:** Silicon remains the fundamental backbone for power management.
  - Infineon announced its **OptiMOS 8** portfolio, delivering a 44% improvement in RDS(on) compared to OptiMOS 5.
  - New 18-kilowatt and 30-kilowatt Power Supply Unit (PSU) Power Factor Correction (PFC) systems have been launched utilizing this advanced silicon.
- **Optimized Silicon Carbide (SiC):** Designed specifically for high-frequency, high-power functional blocks. Production is scaling out of Kulim, Malaysia, to ensure cost-effective supply security.
- **Gallium Nitride (GaN) Scaling:** GaN is reaching a tipping point, excelling in high-switching-frequency environments like intermediate bus conversion (from 48V down to 6V).
  - To meet soaring demand, Infineon is scaling up the industry's first **12-inch GaN manufacturing facility** in Villach, Europe, ensuring massive scale and cost-competitiveness.

## Ultra-High-Density Core Power Delivery
- **BBU and IBC Innovation:** High-density power storage and conversion units are scaling rapidly.
  - **Battery Backup Units (BBUs):** New pure-play 24 kW BBU solutions utilizing SiC join the existing portfolio (1.5 kW to 12 kW).
  - **Intermediate Bus Conversion (IBC):** High-voltage 10 kW GaN solutions are achieving power densities of 1.6W/mm².
- **Vertical Power Delivery Roadmap:** Moving beyond 2A/mm² vertical power modules currently in production, Infineon unveiled its ultra-high-density roadmap for GPUs, TPUs, and ASICs:
  - **Slim Vertical Power Module:** A 2.5A/mm², four-phase module featuring an incredibly thin Z-height of less than 3mm.
  - **Dual-Phase Solutions:** Advancing to 3A/mm² (substrate attached).
  - **Embedded Substrate Modules:** Target densities exceeding 4A/mm² for next-generation accelerators.

## Physical AI, Robotics, and Security
- **Humanoid Motor Control & Sensing:** Modern humanoids can feature up to 70 joint actuators, requiring complex motor control, sensing, and power management.
  - Infineon provides specialized **MOTIX** controllers, position/current sensors, and its industry-leading **Aurix** microcontrollers (featuring functional safety) to optimize battery life and miniaturize robotic joints.
- **High-Bandwidth Connectivity:** Utilizing recent acquisitions, Infineon is deploying automotive-grade, low-latency Ethernet solutions into robotics to handle the massive data loads generated by physical AI.
- **Nvidia Security Partnership:** Infineon announced a collaboration with Nvidia to secure physical AI platforms.
  - Infineon's **Optiga** security controllers will integrate with Nvidia’s **Jetson Thor** platform, delivering a quantum-resilient hardware root of trust to protect AI models and cryptographic keys in the field.
