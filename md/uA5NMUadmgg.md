## TL;DR
Dylan Patel explains that the global compute race is being dominated by OpenAI and Anthropic—who are buying up 25% to 40% of all newly added compute—causing rental prices for H100s to spike from a $1.70 trough to a $2.80 midpoint. Despite tightening debt markets and $11 trillion in projected AI capex by 2029, high inference profitability and NVIDIA’s cluster backstops are helping keep the open market alive.

## The Compute Crunch & The AI Labs
* **Explosive Growth in Global Compute:** 
  * The entire world had **60 gigawatts (GW)** of compute globally at the end of **2024**, which reached **over 100 GW** by the end of this year (nearly doubling in two years).
  * AI labs held **4 GW** total between Anthropic and OpenAI at the end of last year, scaling to **10 GW** this year, and **over 20 GW** the year after.
* **Lab Dominance:** 
  * In **2027**, Anthropic and OpenAI are adding about **12 GW** out of **40 GW** globally added that year, taking **25% to 40%** of all new compute.
  * In **2028**, AI labs are projected to add **on the order of 30 GW** based on signed commitments.
* **Training vs. Inference Spend:** 
  * Labs spend the vast majority of their compute budget on training rather than inference. 
  * All of CloudCode is served by **1 GW** of compute despite Anthropic holding **4 GW** today.
  * Anthropic spends north of **$100 million** serving Fable or Opus 5.5 (per IPO disclosure documents).

## Compute Pricing & Token Economics
* **Price Spikes:** 
  * H100 rental prices hit a trough in **October** at **$1.70/hour** (with some found as low as **$1.45/hour**).
  * Current transactions reach **$2.40 to $3.20/hour**, with a midpoint of **$2.80/hour** for a 1-year commitment.
* **High Inference Profitability:** 
  * Building a B300 cluster costs **$40 million** upfront plus an operational cost of **$9 million/year** over 5 years.
  * Renting from the cloud costs roughly **$14 million/year** per megawatt.
  * Serving open-source models (like DeepSeek V4.1 Flash) at **60% utilization** generates **$70 million/year** in revenue, creating a massive incentive to buy compute and sell tokens.
* **Market Scale:** 
  * Over **100 trillion tokens/day** are generated across just a few providers.

## Cloud Evaluations & NeoCloud Security (ClusterMAX)
* **ClusterMAX V3 Rankings:** 
  * SemiAnalysis tracked **323 providers**, reviewed **77**, and awarded medallions to **19**.
  * **Coreweave and Nebius** ranked as the best providers, though Coreweave is sold out for 6 months and Nebius requires full upfront prepayment for 6 to 12 months at high prices.
  * **Upgrades/Downgrades:** Google moved up (reaching bronze-to-top tiers over time), while Azure dropped to **Silver**, and Crusoe, Together, and AWS all dropped to **Bronze**. AWS was downgraded due to difficult user experiences, slow response times, and an unwieldy HyperPod offering.
* **Security Vulnerabilities:** 
  * Using open-source AI models to hack neo-clouds revealed severe open internet vulnerabilities, allowing models to access other customers' storage.
  * In one instance, an open-source model gained access to Slurm across an entire cluster, exposing a foreign country's defense industry and national security intelligence agency job names.
* **Failure Testing:** 
  * Injecting Nvidia error code `XID 719` showed that many clouds failed to detect the GPU fault internally, requiring manual user alerts and taking days to respond.

## Capital, Debt Markets, and NVIDIA Backstops
* **Massive CAPEX Projections:** 
  * By the end of **2029**, SemiAnalysis expects **$11 trillion** in cumulative global AI capex (**$5 trillion** from cash/equity, **$7 trillion** from debt).
  * Recent spikes in the US 10-year yield have caused market pandemonium for financing.
* **Financing NeoClouds:** 
  * Neo-clouds cannot get loans to scale from 100 MW to 1 GW unless they pre-sell capacity to a hyperscaler, which removes that compute from the open market.
* **NVIDIA Cluster Backstops:** 
  * **Softbank Energy (Ohio):** Land, power, and shell guaranteed for a 9 GW facility coming up in 2030 (earmarked entirely for OpenAI).
  * **Firmus (Australia/Singapore):** NVIDIA guaranteed they will rent the cluster at a breakeven price so Firmus can secure debt; any revenue over that baseline is split 50/50 with NVIDIA.
  * **Scale:** NVIDIA has backstopped **6.5 GW** of compute so far, with a maximum possible capacity of **45 GW** across these programs.
