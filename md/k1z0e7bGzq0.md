## The AI Productivity Revolution & the Rise of the "Builder"
* **The "AI Vampire" Phenomenon:** Marc Andreessen describes a new class of workers who are exhausted but euphoric because their marginal productivity has exploded.

* **Marginal Productivity vs. Job Loss:** Andreessen argues that increasing worker productivity does not lead to fewer jobs; historically, it leads to an expansion of work, higher demand for talent, and higher compensation.
* **AI-Native Success:** Contrary to "doomer" narratives that junior roles will vanish, Andreessen suggests companies will prioritize hiring "AI-native" youth (ages 15-25) who can outperform veteran "Luddite" peers.

* **AI as a Scapegoat:** Andreessen suggests many current tech layoffs are not caused by AI, but rather companies using AI as a "scapegoat" to finally trim decades of accumulated organizational bloat.
* **Non-Profit Inefficiency:** The bloat extends to NGOs and government-adjacent organizations, which often prioritize self-aggrandizement and power over their stated missions.
