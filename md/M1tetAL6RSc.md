## The Rise of Hybrid and Agentic AI
* **The Shift to Hybrid AI:** Enterprise AI workloads are rapidly moving out of the public cloud to maintain control over data, security, intellectual property, and costs. 
    * A recent Dell Technologies survey reveals that **67% of AI workloads already run outside the cloud** (on-prem, edge, or colocation), and **88% of respondents** run at least one AI workload on-prem.
    * AI infrastructure spending is projected to reach **$3 to $4 trillion globally by 2030**, driven by a massive shift from bits back to physical enterprise hardware.
* **From Assistants to Agentic AI:** Enterprise productivity is transitioning from simple digital assistants (delivering 20% to 30% gains) to autonomous "digital workers." These agents plan, reason, and execute workflows recursively, unlocking **20x to 30x productivity improvements**.
* **Model Choice and Open Ecosystems:** To avoid proprietary lock-in, the Dell Enterprise Hub on Hugging Face has added several major open-source and frontier models, including DeepSeek V4, GLM 5.1, Kimi K2.6, MiniMax M2.7, Gemini 3 (via Google Distributed Cloud on-prem), and SpaceX's Grok.

## Transforming Industry: Eli Lilly and Honeywell Case Studies
* **Eli Lilly (Pharmaceutical Innovation):** CIO Diogo Rau discussed how the 150-year-old pharmaceutical leader utilizes high-performance computing to solve massive scale challenges.
    * **AI-Driven Manufacturing:** Lilly uses high-speed computer vision on packaging lines, capturing 70 photos per vial within milliseconds to instantly detect defects. They also utilize digital twins to optimize manufacturing workflows beyond human capability.
    * **Drug Discovery Supercomputing:** Running the pharmaceutical industry's largest supercomputer with 1,016 GPUs, Lilly has moved beyond simple protein folding (AlphaFold) to model dynamic protein-protein interactions. This allows scientists to design highly selective cancer therapies that target specific diseased cells.
* **Honeywell (Industrial Automation to Autonomy):** CTO Suresh Venkatarayalu outlined Honeywell's evolution into a pure-play automation company powered by its "Forge Platform."
    * **Forge Cognition:** Developed in partnership with Dell and Nvidia, this initiative deploys physical AI servers at industrial sites (such as oil refineries and chemical plants). 
    * **Edge Intelligence:** Running AI models locally at the edge minimizes latency, allowing operators to run real-time simulations to predict manufacturing yield, throughput, and equipment downtime.
