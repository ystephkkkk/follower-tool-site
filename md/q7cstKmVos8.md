## TL;DR
Thomson Reuters President and CEO Steve Hasker outlines how the company leverages "fiduciary-grade AI" and proprietary data to drive growth across the legal, tax, and news sectors without sacrificing accuracy. He also addresses token cost management, internal employee adoption, and strategic restructuring.

## Thomson Reuters: Business Overview & Growth Drivers
* **Company Profile (2026 Overview):** Described by **Steve Hasker** as the largest provider of content and technology to the legal profession, the largest provider of tax calculation engines, a major risk/fraud/compliance business, and the owner of Reuters News (the world's largest wholesaler of news, representing about 10% of the overall business).
* **Core Growth Drivers:** 
  * **Coping with Complexity:** Providing content-driven technology that enables decision-makers to comply with growing legal and regulatory complexity without adding headcount.
  * **AI Product Expansion:** Moving from traditional search (like *Westlaw*) into work-product generation (first drafts of contracts, briefs, motions) and automating activities around tax return preparation.

## "Fiduciary-Grade" AI vs. Consumer AI
* **Definition of Fiduciary-Grade:** **Steve Hasker** coined this term for AI used by professionals (lawyers, tax, accounting, audit) who cannot afford hallucinations or incorrect citations due to risks to their professional licenses and client relationships. Outputs must be deterministic, not probabilistic.
* **Four Pillars of Accuracy:**
  * **Proprietary Content:** Decades- and centuries-old data sets (*Westlaw*, *Practical Law*, *Checkpoint*, *CLEAR*) maintained by thousands of experts.
  * **On-Site Domain Experts:** A couple of thousand reference attorneys and CPAs who produce content and train AI agents to behave like tenured professionals.
  * **Data Privacy Guarantees:** A strict promise that customer inputs will not become part of the AI output.
  * **Human Support Infrastructure:** Dedicated call centers where users can trace and walk through the exact logic, output, and citations of an AI query.
* **The Thompson Model:** **Steve Hasker** highlights the launch of *Thomson Model*, a large language model built specifically for legal (and expanding to tax/accounting/audit) that outperforms cutting-edge frontier models on specific professional tasks.

## Token Costs, Budgets, and the Customer ROI Gap
* **Managing Token Costs:** **Steve Hasker** acknowledges that token costs for frontier models have risen exponentially. Thomson Reuters covers these within Wall Street guidance, but the *Thomson Model* provides internal financial flexibility should external token costs become unmanageable.
* **The Customer ROI Lag:** 
  * While 74% of professionals use AI weekly, 91% feel organizations fall short on translating this into value, and 33% of legal/accounting professionals turn to unsanctioned tools creating unmanaged risks.
  * **Steve Hasker** notes an 18-to-24-month lag for firms to rewire workflows, prompting Thomson Reuters to deploy forward-deployed engineers and customer success consultants to drive change management.

## Human Capital and Newsroom Transformation
* **Engineering Retooling:** Clarified that recent engineering adjustments (such as 500 job reductions mentioned in reports) are a retooling for agentic AI talent rather than cost-cutting efficiencies.
* **Workforce AI Adoption:** Encouraging 27,000 employees to automate mundane, repetitive tasks. 
* **Reuters Newsroom Impact:** **Steve Hasker** projects that Reuters' news output will increase three-to-four-fold over the next few years with roughly the same headcount (around 2,700 journalists across 200 locations), driven by AI assisting long-term partners like the London Stock Exchange Group (LSEG).

## Strategic Transactions and Leadership Lessons
* **Global Print Joint Venture:** **Steve Hasker** discussed a transaction with KKR giving them a 51% stake in the 140-year-old global print business for $500 million in gross proceeds, allowing Thomson Reuters to focus entirely on its digital and AI business.
* **Daily Customer Engagement:** **Steve Hasker** maintains a commitment to speaking with a Thomson Reuters customer *every single day* to understand industry challenges and maintain effectiveness.
* **Career Philosophy:** Reflecting on his career across PwC (Melbourne), McKinsey (11 years), Nielsen, and CIA Global, **Steve Hasker** emphasizes that he never had a career plan, instead following a "North Star" of prioritizing continuous learning.
