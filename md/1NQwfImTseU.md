## TL;DR
Dwarkesh Patel spent $20,000 in API credits on *Blood on the Clocktower*—a social deception benchmark involving 368 games—revealing that newer OpenAI models (like GPT-6 Astra) drastically outperform Anthropic models in multi-agent coordination, deception, and holistic skepticism.

## Benchmark Methodology & Setup
- **Game Mechanics:** Matches pit a good team (seeking to execute the lead evil player, the Demon) against an evil team (trying to eliminate the good team via secret night kills and daytime bluffs), racing to control information flow.
- **Experimental Cost:** $20,000 in API credits was used to build the benchmark harness and fund matches where every player on a given team is a copy of the same AI model.
- **Public Platform:** The full benchmark, complete game logs, and an interface for humans to play against the AIs are available at `clocktowerbench.com`.

## Model Comparison: OpenAI vs. Anthropic
- **Overall Leaderboard Shifts:** OpenAI’s models made massive leaps, with GPT-6 Astra (released September 2026) sitting at the top, gaining 580 Elo points over GPT-5.4 in six months—enough for Astra to win 97% of games against 5.4.
- **Anthropic vs. OpenAI Timeline:** Claude Opus 4.6 launched as the premier 2026 model in February, but Astra now beats Opus 4.6 by about 430 Elo points, translating to a 92% win rate for Astra.
- **Skill Divergence:** OpenAI's advantage stems primarily from superior play on the *good* side, driven by deeper skepticism of reported evidence rather than taking claims at face value.

## Standout AI Game Strategies
- **The Expendable Minion Bluff:** In a match with GPT-6 Astra (evil) and Claude Opus 5.5 (good), Astra's Demon successfully bluffed as an Investigator. An expendable minion suggested the Demon accuse him, which the good team verified as genuinely evil via the Undertaker—making the Demon the most trusted player at the table for the rest of the game.
- **The Saint Trap:** In a game where Opus 5.5 (evil) played against Claude Opus 4.6 (good), an evil heiress realized that executing the real Demon would cause her to inherit the role via a secret power. She joined the mob to execute the Demon, forced a secondary deal to execute a claimed Saint if the game continued, and successfully won after the village took the bait.
- **Slayer Ability Sabotage:** When tested with a once-per-game Slayer shot that missed due to overnight evil poisoning, Claude models playing good immediately accepted the target as innocent. Conversely, GPT models on the good side refused to treat the miss as conclusive proof, deduced the evil team's sabotage, and successfully executed the correct Demon.
