## TL;DR
Driven by an AI-induced electricity demand projected to increase sixfold over the next 20 years, NextEra Energy CEO John Ketchum is scaling the world's most valuable power company through a massive $60 billion annual capital investment plan and a record-breaking merger with Dominion Energy.

## AI Boom, Grid Scale, and the Dominion Merger
* **Exploding Power Demand:** 
  * Power demand remained static for decades until the adoption of AI accelerated it exponentially over the past 24 months.
  * Broader demand drivers include the redomestication of manufacturing, semiconductor chip facilities, LNG terminals, and increased residential and transportation consumption.
* **The Dominion Energy Merger:** 
  * NextEra agreed to acquire Dominion Energy to expand its footprint into three key states: Virginia, North Carolina, and South Carolina.
  * Commitments for Virginia include bringing 1,000 new jobs (600 at NextEra, 400 from supplier letters of intent) and building a dual headquarters office tower.
* **Capital Intensity & Supply Chain Advantage:** 
  * NextEra plans to spend **$60 billion a year** ($1 billion a week) on power plants between 2027 and 2032 across a 50-state capital investment plan.
  * Supply chain bottlenecks for turbines, transformers, solar panels, inverters, and batteries are mitigated through massive bulk purchasing; NextEra is already buying equipment through 2030.

## Data Centers: Power Generation vs. Ownership
* **Strategic Focus on Power, Not Real Estate:** 
  * After testing the data center business model by buying a couple of smaller facilities three years ago, NextEra decided to leave data center operations to hyperscalers.
  * Hyperscalers want to own and control their own data centers for cybersecurity and the flexibility to upgrade chips over time.
* **Direct Contracting & Ratepayer Protection:** 
  * Hyperscalers contract directly with NextEra to bring their own dedicated generation rather than drawing down the public grid and driving up retail power prices.
  * NextEra and Dominion have signed ratepayer protection pledges, and Florida codified a protective large-load tariff into statute.
* **Community Relations:** Developers must use closed-loop cooling (reusing water from treatment facilities), pay property taxes, observe noise and setback requirements, and invest locally (e.g., Meta funding $50,000 teacher scholarships in Louisiana).

## Energy Mix: The Return of Gas and Nuclear
* **Fossil Fuels for Baseload:** 
  * While solar is two to three times cheaper than gas, data centers require round-the-clock baseload generation that 4-hour to 8-hour battery storage cannot yet fully supply on its own. NextEra relies on its extensive experience operating the largest US gas-fired generation fleet.
* **Nuclear Restarts:** 
  * NextEra is restarting the Dwayne Arnold nuclear plant, treating the restoration as a "super outage" to supply 25 years of power under an agreement with Google.
* **Small Modular Reactors (SMRs):** 
  * Out of 96 SMR original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), NextEra has narrowed its focus to 3 or 4 technically viable Gen 3 options.
  * SMR deployment requires shared risk among the federal government, the hyperscaler customer, the EPC contractor, and the OEM, as NextEra will not risk shareholder capital on unproven technology.

## Leadership, Culture, and International Strategy
* **Psychologically Safe Dissent:** Ketchum enforces a culture where anyone—from a 25-year-old newbie to a 20-year veteran—is expected to push back on his ideas if they spot a blind spot.
* **Internal Mobility & Career Growth:** Ketchum held 12 different jobs over 24 years, rising from line tax lawyer to CEO. NextEra continuously moves employees across roles to build well-rounded strategic thinkers.
* **Bottom-Up Innovation ("Rewire"):** Annual company programs like *Rewire* (formerly *Accelerate*, *Momentum*, and *Velocity*) rely on employees pitching hundreds of capital and O&M savings ideas directly to an executive steering committee.
* **International Caution:** A past venture into Spanish solar in 2012 resulted in retroactive feed-in tariff cuts during the European recession. Ketchum served as the workout lead for those projects, cementing NextEra's disciplined "toe in the water" approach of making small initial bets before committing significant capital abroad.
