**TL;DR**
Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent presented an $11.5 billion FY 2027 budget request, a 12% reduction from the prior year, focusing on technology-driven efficiency, the implementation of the "Working Families Tax Cuts Act," and establishing U.S. leadership in digital assets through the GENIUS Act. While the administration emphasized improved IRS performance and middle-class relief, minority members challenged the efficacy of cutting IRS enforcement and the impact of sanctions policy on rising domestic energy costs.

## FY 2027 Budget Priorities and IRS Modernization
* **Spending Reductions:** The Department of Treasury is requesting $11.5 billion, a $1.5 billion (12%) decrease from FY 2026.
    * **Efficiency vs. Funding:** Secretary Bessent argued that prior "bloat" is being replaced by technology, noting that enforcement recoveries rose 12% last year despite budget cuts.
* **IRS Workforce & Direct File:**
    * **Staffing Levels:** Ranking Member Jack Reed noted the IRS has lost 28,000 employees since the start of the Trump administration, with another 4,000-person reduction proposed.
    * **Program Cancellations:** The administration officially ended the "Direct File" program. Secretary Bessent defended this by citing high costs ($138 per filer) compared to the public-private "Free File" alternative.
* **Operational Modernization:**
    * **Digital-First Strategy:** 98% of taxpayers now file electronically, with 90% receiving refunds within 21 days.
    * **Vendor Accountability:** Chairman Bill Hagerty praised the cancellation of contracts with Booz Allen Hamilton following a massive data breach, citing a failure to properly vet personnel who leaked sensitive taxpayer information.

## Tax Policy and "Working Families" Initiatives
* **Signature Policy Implementation:** The Treasury is prioritizing the "Working Families Tax Cuts Act," which provides relief on tips, overtime, and Social Security.
    * **No Tax on Tips:** Over 7 million filers claimed an average deduction of $7,000.
    * **No Tax on Overtime:** 28 million filers claimed an average deduction of $3,100.
    * **Seniors’ Relief:** 34 million seniors claimed an enhanced deduction averaging $7,500.
* **Trump Accounts:** The budget supports a pilot program for "Trump Accounts," which has seen 5.5 million accounts opened and 1.3 million children eligible for a $1,000 contribution to foster long-term financial security.
* **Paid Family Leave:** Senator Deb Fischer highlighted the expansion and permanence of the Paid Family and Medical Leave tax credit; Bessent committed to issuing finalized guidance for employers by early summer 2026.

## Digital Assets and Global Economic Leadership
* **The GENIUS Act:** The Treasury is requesting $4.8 million across departmental offices and FinCEN to implement the regulatory framework for stablecoins.
    * **US Leadership:** Chairman Hagerty emphasized that regulatory certainty is required to ensure the U.S. remains the home of financial innovation and protects the U.S. dollar's status as the world reserve currency.
* **Trade and Reciprocity:** Secretary Bessent highlighted efforts to reset global trade by negotiating reciprocal agreements to reduce dependence on competitors like China, particularly in the shipping and pharmaceutical sectors.

## National Security, Sanctions, and Energy
* **Sanctions Policy:** Senator Chris Coons questioned the administration on "sanctions relief" for Russia and Iran during the ongoing conflict, alleging it provided Russia with $150 million in daily revenue.
    * **Global Supply Strategy:** Secretary Bessent countered that temporary relief was necessary to prevent global oil prices from reaching $150/barrel, which would have severely impacted the American consumer and vulnerable allies.
* **Financial Intelligence:** The budget seeks increased funding for the Office of Terrorism and Financial Intelligence to trace and sanction illicit financial networks.
* **Foreign Influence Concerns:** Senator Chris Van Hollen raised questions regarding potential "swap lines" for the UAE and alleged linkages to private Trump family crypto ventures; Secretary Bessent denied any knowledge of the private investments or specific linkages to Treasury policy.

## Community Development and Rural Outreach
* **CDFI Cuts:** The budget proposes a 60% cut to the Community Development Financial Institutions (CDFI) fund. 
    * **Rural Focus:** Bessent argued that the program had become "partisan" but highlighted a new $100 million allocation specifically for rural areas to support daycares and small businesses in states like Nebraska and Maine.
* **Rural Service Access:** Senator Susan Collins expressed concern over the closure of Taxpayer Assistance Centers in Maine, noting that some residents now face a 10-hour round trip for in-person help. Bessent committed to reviewing these closures to ensure rural accessibility.
