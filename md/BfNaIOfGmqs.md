## Canada-EU Strategic Rapprochement
* **Proposed Associate Membership:** 
  * European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen initially proposed that Canada become the EU's first associate partner during her annual speech on Wednesday.
  * Prime Minister Mark Carney embraced the concept during his address to the European Parliament in Strasbourg, though he preferred to brand the initiative as an "Alliance of the Future" rather than using the associate membership label.
* **Potential Areas of Integration:** 
  * **Educational and Research Exchanges:** Canada aims to participate in EU programs such as Erasmus Plus (student exchange) and Horizon (research and investment projects).
  * **Technology and Resources:** Continued cooperation on artificial intelligence (AI), semiconductors, critical raw materials, oil, and gas supply.
  * **Submarine Procurement:** Canada recently signed a major agreement with a German-Norwegian consortium to provide new submarines.
* **Legal and Structural Precedent:** 
  * DW’s Jack Parrock notes there is currently no legal provision or specific mention in EU treaties for this type of associate membership, making Canada a test case.
  * Questions remain regarding how this relationship will impact candidate countries like Ukraine or traditional strong-tie nations like Great Britain, Australia, and New Zealand.

## US-Canada Trade Tensions & US Reactions
* **Vulnerability of the Status Quo:** 
  * Political commentator Sean Mallen highlights that approximately three-quarters of Canadian trade goes to the United States, leaving Canada vulnerable to a volatile US administration.
  * The current trade war and US pressure have prompted Canada to expand economic ties globally, including outreaches to the Asia-Pacific region and Europe.
* **Donald Trump's Response:** 
  * US President Donald Trump dismissed the proposal as "laughable" and labeled Canada a "terrible trade partner."
  * Trump issued a stark warning that if he views the Canada-EU move as a "hostile act" with "bad intention," he will impose very heavy tariffs on the EU or halt trade altogether.
* **Historical Context and Public Sentiment:**
