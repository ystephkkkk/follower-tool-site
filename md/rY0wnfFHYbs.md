## TL;DR
Driven by coding agents, falling costs, and rapidly closing capability gaps with frontier labs, AI model consumption is experiencing a massive shift toward open-weight models, highlighted by a 150x increase in token usage on Ollama Cloud since the start of the year. Jeffrey Morgan, co-founder and CEO of Ollama, shares insights on this industry-wide transformation, the evolving balance between local and cloud-hosted open models, and the journey behind building a foundational tool used by 9 million developers and 85% of the Fortune 500.

## State of Open Models & Enterprise Adoption
* **Massive Token Explosion:** 
  * **Ollama Cloud Growth:** Token usage has surged 150x since the start of the year (Jeff Morgan).
  * **Individual Usage Inflection Points:** Average weekly token usage per developer saw a major run-up at the start of the year driven by coding agents (Kimmy, GLM, MiniMax), followed by an explosive wave in April driven by OpenClaw and the Hermes agent project, which allowed non-developers to automate complex multi-step workflows (Jeff Morgan).
* **Enterprise Shift:** 
  * **AT&T Benchmark:** AT&T has already shifted 40% of their token consumption to open models, largely utilizing US and European models while evaluating Chinese origin models (Jeff Morgan).
  * **Geographic Trends:** Cloud token consumption on Ollama Cloud is predominantly driven by Chinese-origin models accessed globally, with the US and Germany standing out as major access hubs (Jeff Morgan). Local models show a strong, even split between US, European, and Chinese-origin models (Jeff Morgan).
* **Primary Drivers Beyond Cost:** 
  * **Control & Customization:** While cost is the immediate pain point that opens the door, businesses view open models as a way to gain better control over AI and customize it for their unique use cases (Jeff Morgan).
  * **Security Testing:** Open models serve as a critical advantage for security testing and pentesting because closed frontier models typically refuse to execute malicious-looking tasks (Jeff Morgan).

## The Infrastructure & Ecosystem Stack
* **The OS for AI Models:** 
  * **Unified Runtime:** Ollama acts as a glue layer or operating system, packaging inference engines, hardware stacks (Nvidia, Apple Silicon, AMD, Intel), and application harnesses into a consistent day-zero experience (Jeff Morgan).
  * **Day-Zero Fire Drills:** Model release cycles are accelerating drastically (e.g., DeepSeek Flash releasing three iterations over the summer); coordination typically happens within the final 24 hours before public release to ensure inference speed, accuracy, and tool-calling spec compliance (Jeff Morgan).
* **The Rise of Best-of-Breed Layers:** 
  * **Unbundling the Stack:** Moving away from closed walled gardens, the open ecosystem is unbundling into best-of-breed services handling knowledge/context, coordination (sub-agents), and execution/sandboxes (Jeff Morgan).
  * **Stateful vs. Stateless:** Stateful elements like storage, security credentials, and safety memory will remain outside the model layer due to rapid model training cadences (Jeff Morgan).
* **Hardware & Local Workstations:** 
  * **Next-Gen Personal Desktops:** Hardware advancements like Nvidia's DGX Spark (featuring 128 GB of unified memory) and Apple Silicon (via the MLX project) allow developers to run 20B to 120B parameter models locally at high speeds (Jeff Morgan).
  * **Hybrid Execution Model:** Enterprises are adopting a hybrid approach where simpler, low-latency document processing runs locally for free on existing hardware, while heavy coding agents route to cloud models (Jeff Morgan).

## Economics, Flash Models, and Task Composition
* **The Era of "Unlimited" Tokens:** 
  * **Ultra-Low-Cost Workhorses:** A new class of models (like DeepSeek Flash and GPT Luna) provides ultra-low cost per task and per token, enabling widespread team adoption and high-volume token consumption without strict rationing (Jeff Morgan).
  * **Task Composition Over God Models:** Rather than relying on a single "god model," developers are successfully chaining cheaper flash models together via orchestration layers to handle complex workflows cost-effectively (Jeff Morgan & Panel).
* **Future Budget Balance:** 
  * **Token Volume vs. Spend:** While open models will handle 80% to 90% of a business's total token volume, budget allocation will be split, with the hardest reasoning tasks reserved for expensive frontier labs and the vast majority of line-item grunt work handled by cheap open models (Jeff Morgan).

## The Ollama Origin Story & Lessons Learned
* **Pivoting in the Wilderness:** 
  * **Early Missteps:** Founded by former Docker engineers (Jeff Morgan and co-founder Michael), the company spent its first two years (including post-YC Winter 2021 and Series A funding from Benchmark in 2022) overengineering ideas like Kubernetes SSO and developer security tools before finding product-market fit with Ollama in July 2023 (Jeff Morgan).
  * **The Zero-to-One Moment:** The pivot clicked instantly when running local Llama models revealed a genuine, highly resonant developer pain point, leading to rapid viral adoption and 100,000+ GitHub stars (Jeff Morgan).
* **Monetization & Enterprise Expansion:** 
  * **The Docker Parallel:** Much like Docker, Ollama spent years cultivating a massive open-source developer base before introducing monetization via Ollama Cloud earlier this year (Jeff Morgan).
  * **Frictionless Adoption:** Because open models are free and can be run anywhere without IT permission, hobbyist adoption translated seamlessly into widespread Fortune 500 enterprise usage (Jeff Morgan).
