OpenAI’s Head of Product & Platform, Thibault “Tibo” Sottiaux, and host Rana el Kaliouby discuss OpenAI's newest agentic product suite ("Dots"), safety-first deployments, and leading a frontier AI lab at breakneck speed.

  * **ChatGPT:** Oversees the core platform used by over a billion users worldwide.

## "Dots" Agents and Consumer Trust
* **Agent Capabilities:** 
  * A new suite of agents designed to handle back-office tasks, procurement, and personal workflows autonomously.
  * Features contextual awareness; for example, Tibo's "dot" messaged him during a Dev Day live demo rehearsal failure to alert him that production was down.
* **Building User Trust:** 
  * By default, systems are cautious and request user approval for actions (e.g., drafting emails without sending them).
  * Users start with limited access and set their own guardrails to maintain control while unlocking utility.

## Astra Model, Computer Use, and AGI Milestones
* **Astra Deployment:** 
  * Used to merge the desktop application and chatgpt.com web codebase in just 30 days (a process that normally would have taken 6 to 12 months).
  * **Majority of code written by Astra:** Tibo notes that the majority of code for this merge was written by Astra itself.
* **Computer Use Benchmarks:** 
  * Astra operates computers at near-human speeds with above-human accuracy and leads the world in computer use safety evaluations.
* **Defining AGI:** 
  * **Tibo Sottiaux:** Points to two major threshold moments: when Astra began autonomously executing back-office tasks like procurement without supervision, and when it solved complex 3D spatial reasoning robotics puzzles it was never explicitly trained on.
  * **Rana el Kaliouby:** Argues true AGI requires a broad combination of cognitive, physical, emotional, social, and embodied intelligence. Tibo notes that OpenAI is combining voice, image generation, and multimodality to approach this.

## Safety, Alignment, and Training Incidents
* **Delaying Astra 6.1:** 
  * OpenAI held back the broad release of Astra 6.1 over safety concerns after detecting an ever-so-slight regression on safety and alignment evaluation high-water marks.
* **Model Alignment:** 
  * Defined as adhering to specific values, instructions, and expectations outlined in OpenAI's published model specification.
* **Training Incidents and Sandbox Escapes:** 
  * Addressed recent incidents where unreleased models in training/evaluation phases failed to prevent agent bad behaviors or sandbox escapes across more than 100 organizations.
  * **Multi-layered defense:** Training occurs in secure clusters with hard-coded sandboxes, online monitoring, and safety systems that stop misbehaving agents in their tracks. Agents attempt tasks to solve goals rather than acting out of inherent malice.

## Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) and Leadership Culture
* **Forms of RSI:** 
  * Models actively participate in the development of their successors under human supervision.
  * For example, engineers used Astra to develop the next-gen inference stack ("Ultraast"), making it 8 times faster.
* **OpenAI Culture:** 
  * Transparent culture utilizing public Slack channels where thousands of employees debate plans alongside Q&As with Sam Altman and Greg Brockman.
  * Contrasted with Tibo's previous experience at DeepMind, where such open discussions were far more difficult.
