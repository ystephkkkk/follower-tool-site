## The Record-Breaking Gift & Investment in Excellence
* **Historic Donation Scale:** **Ken Griffin** committed $3 billion to Carnegie Mellon University, marking the largest single gift ever made to a US university and bringing his total giving to approximately $5.7 billion.
* **Investment in Excellence:** **Ken Griffin** framed the donation as an investment in national leadership, citing CMU's top-tier standing in computer science, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and robotics.
* **Pittsburgh Impact & Naming:** A portion of the funds will support the Pittsburgh campus on day one, including naming the top-ranked computer science school the **Kenneth C. Griffin School of Computer Science**.

## A New Model for Higher Education in Miami
* **Pioneering a Miami Campus:** **President Farnam Jahanian** and **Ken Griffin** detailed plans to establish a new CMU campus in Miami, modeled after how the university helped drive Pittsburgh's industrial renaissance.
* **Ecosystem Transformation:** **Ken Griffin** predicted that the Miami branch will attract hundreds of technology companies, startups, and career opportunities to the region.
* **Grand Challenges Over Silos:** **President Farnam Jahanian** explained that the campus structure will abandon traditional departments in favor of organizing around grand societal challenges, such as:
  * Human health
  * National security and cybersecurity
  * Innovation, energy, and climate resiliency

## Balancing Technical Rigor and Human Skills
* **ROI and Return to Core Mission:** **Ken Griffin** highlighted that US News rankings place CMU #3 in the country for educational return on investment and #14 overall, arguing that universities must focus on tangible student outcomes rather than "four years of fun."
* **Interdisciplinary Synthesis:** **President Jahanian** and **Ken Griffin** emphasized that the future requires well-rounded students who combine technical and scientific knowledge with critical thinking, problem-solving, and communication skills.
* **Humanities Integration:** **Ken Griffin** and **President Jahanian** noted CMU's deep roots in the humanities, pointing to achievements like 70 Tony Award winners from its School of Drama (founded in 1914) and alumnus Andy Warhol.

## Cultural Shift on American Campuses
* **Restoring the Narrative:** **Ken Griffin** stated that higher education lost its narrative to issues that do not matter to the nation's future prosperity, but observed that the cultural pendulum is shifting back toward the center.
* **The Ethos of Higher Education:** **Ken Griffin** argued that colleges must return to the foundational ethos of preparing young men and women to serve the country, run businesses, and solve tangible problems.
