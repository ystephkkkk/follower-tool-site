## The Price of Greatness & The 900
- **Obsession over Accolades:** Hawk notes his childhood was defined by a relentless, singular focus on learning new tricks rather than chasing contest wins or magazine covers.
- **Financial Reality Check:** During his early professional career, Hawk had **$640 in the bank** and bought a Honda scooter to get to the skate park independently.
- **The 1991 Slump:** Following a popularity boom driven by *Back to the Future* (1985), Hawk's income dropped by **half every month** in 1991; his Airwalk shoe sponsor cut his monthly payments from **$1,500 to $1,000, then $500, and eventually $300** within a single year, forcing him to pay mortgages by doing odd jobs and Hollywood stunt consulting.
- **The 900 Landmark (1999):** 
  - Landed the historic 900 on live ESPN during a best-trick event after **20 minutes** of attempts.
  - Had **never landed the trick** in practice prior to that moment; previous attempts resulted in severe injuries, including a broken rib in **1994** at around **2:00 PM** on a weekday in a warehouse.

## Video Game Empire: Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater (THPS)
- **Early Rejections:** Pitched a skateboarding game engine around **1997** to companies like Nintendo, where one executive dismissed it by stating, *"Skateboarding isn't even popular. Why would a skateboarding video game be popular?"*
- **Activision Partnership:** Connected with Activision (who had recently released *Apocalypse* starring Bruce Willis, where early test builds literally featured Bruce Willis on a skateboard doing ollies).
- **The Buyout Offer:** Right before the game's release, Activision offered Hawk a **$500,000 buyout** to surrender future royalties, which he declined to "let it ride."
- **Explosive Success:** 
  - By the release of the fourth game, **four out of the top 10 best-selling video games** were *Tony Hawk's Pro Skater* titles.
  - Culminated in a single royalty payout meeting where an Activision executive slid a check across the table for **$4.5 million**.

## Current Business Ventures & Investments
