## Honoring the Legacy and Scale of D-Day
* **The 82nd Anniversary:** Secretary Pete Hegseth commemorated 82 years since the historic landings, calling the Normandy American Cemetery a monument to the unyielding spirit of the American warrior and a testament to the supreme sacrifices made to liberate Europe from Nazi tyranny.
* **Unprecedented Scale and Engineering:** Hegseth highlighted the immense logistics and industrial might that fueled the invasion.
    * **The Armada:** Recalled the words of Sergeant William Ashbrook of the 101st Airborne, who remarked that there were so many ships in the English Channel "that it seemed as if you could step out of the plane and walk to France on top of them."
    * **Higgins Boats:** Praised the blue-collar grit of the 20,000 workers in New Orleans who manufactured the Higgins landing craft, enabling troops to storm Omaha and Utah beaches.
* **Reviving the Warrior Ethos:** Hegseth emphasized that the Department of War is actively working to revive the uncompromising warrior ethos embodied by the Greatest Generation.

## Redefining Alliances: Shared Sacrifice vs. Empty Slogans
* **True Alliances Require Action:** Hegseth stressed that the victory of Operation Overlord was achieved because every partner nation brought its full measure of industry, courage, and blood.
    * **The Critique of Modern Diplomacy:** He argued that effective alliances are built on "real allies doing real things, taking real losses" rather than "empty slogans, lavish summits, or communiques."
* **Reciprocal Defense Burden:** While affirming that America must and will lead, Hegseth stated that Western allies must be capable, ready, and willing to stand shoulder-to-shoulder in the breach.
* **Peace Through Strength:** He warned that some Western capitals have grown too comfortable, forgetting that peace is not merely wished into being, but bought with strength, readiness, and political will.

## Modern Threat Landscapes and the Defense of the West
* **The Modern "Invasion" of Europe:** Hegseth drew a direct parallel between the storming of Normandy's beaches and contemporary migration and ideological challenges facing Europe today.
    * **Vulnerable Borders:** He pointed out that beaches in Spain, Italy, Greece, and Bulgaria are being "stormed by different dangerous ideologies" via arriving boats and men.
    * **A Warning to European Leaders:** Hegseth openly questioned when European capitals will take action to stop this "invasion" before it is too late to maintain the freedom secured in 1944.
* **Defending Western Tradition:** Invoking President Ronald Reagan, Hegseth reminded the audience that freedom is never more than one generation away from extinction and must be unapologetically defended.
