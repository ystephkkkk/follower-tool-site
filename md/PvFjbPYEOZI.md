## TL;DR
Jensen Huang announced major European AI economy investments and unveiled next-generation hardware and software—including the Vera Rubin architecture, DSX POD turnkey AI factories, OpenShell security for autonomous agents, and the RTX Spark personal AI computer.

## European AI Economy & Infrastructure
* **33-Year European Roots:** **Jensen Huang** noted NVIDIA's deep history in Europe, manufacturing early chips (NV1-NV3) in Crolles, France, assembling them in Malta, and partnering with German workstation pioneer Elsa and automotive leaders Audi and Mercedes.
* **$10B European Investment:** **Jensen Huang** announced that NVIDIA has invested nearly $10 billion in Europe over the past year across startups, large partnerships, and 14 newly announced startups during his current tour of France, the UK, Sweden, and Germany.
* **AI Factory Economics & Buildout:** **Jensen Huang** highlighted that a single 1-gigawatt AI factory costs approximately $50 billion (land, power, shell, cooling, compute), generating tens of billions in annual revenue, where a 3-month deployment delay costs up to $10 billion in lost revenue.
* **NVIDIA DSX POD:** **Jensen Huang** introduced the DSX POD, a factory-built, integrated system containing seven liquid-cooled Vera Rubin NVL72 compute racks and 17 kilometers of Spectrum-X cables, reducing on-site installation time from 4–5 months down to about a month.
* **Energy, Cooling, and Efficiency:** **Jensen Huang** detailed that DSX features closed-loop liquid cooling handling up to 45°C coolant temperatures, saving up to 54 million liters of water daily at a 2-gigawatt scale.

## Hardware & Architecture: Vera Rubin & Networking
* **Six Generations of Extreme Co-Design:** **Jensen Huang** contrasted NVIDIA's architecture with Moore's Law (which has largely ended), stating that extreme co-design across chips, systems, software, and algorithms has increased AI performance by 1 million times over the last 10 years.
* **Vera Rubin Architecture:** **Jensen Huang** stated that Vera Rubin is in full production, delivering 100% liquid cooling (reducing assembly time from 2 hours to under 5 minutes per node), 20 petaflops per GPU, and a 30x performance leap over Blackwell in just two years.
* **Networking & Silicon Photonics:** **Jensen Huang** introduced Spectralmax, the world's first co-packaged optics (CPO) solution developed with TSMC using CoWoS technology, featuring a 512-port, 200 gigabits-per-second Ethernet switch that connects lasers directly to the chip without PCB transmission loss.

## Autonomous Agents & OpenShell Security
* **The Five Elements of AI Agents:** **Jensen Huang** defined an agentic system by five core building blocks: the **Model** (perception, reasoning, planning), the **Harness** (exoskeleton/orchestration), the **Sandbox** (isolation environment), **Knowledge** (structured/unstructured data), and **Tools** (CUDA-X simulation and execution).
* **NVIDIA OpenShell & Sentry:** **Jensen Huang** introduced OpenShell (an Apache 2.0 open-source secure runtime enforcing policies at system-call, file-access, and network levels) and NVIDIA Sentry (hardware-isolated monitoring running on BlueField-4 that can quarantine compromised agents in milliseconds).
* **Knowledge Foundation:** **Jensen Huang** outlined NVIDIA's knowledge libraries—including Nemotron Parse, Nemotron Embed, Nemotron Rerank, and Kumo—designed to bridge unstructured PDFs and structured SQL databases to power domain-specific enterprise agents.

## Personal AI & Physical Robotics
* **RTX Spark Personal Computer:** **Jensen Huang** announced the RTX Spark, a 4-year collaboration with Microsoft featuring the N1X chip, delivering 1 petaflops of compute and 128 gigabytes of memory in a laptop/desktop form factor starting at the end of the year.
* **DGX Station with RTX Spark:** **Jensen Huang** introduced the DGX Station workstation variant running Windows, offering 20 petaflops and 754 gigabytes of memory.
* **Robotic Ecosystem & Hyperion Standard:** **Jensen Huang** announced that Volkswagen Vans, JLR, Volvo, and next-generation Mercedes fleets will adopt the Hyperion autonomous vehicle architecture, and unveiled the NVIDIA Agile One reference robot (built with Agile Robotics) launching in Q1 of next year.
