## The $3 Billion CMU Investment & New Higher Ed Model
- **Historic Gift:** Ken Griffin announced a $3 billion investment in Carnegie Mellon University, developed over 2.5 to 3 years.
- **Campus Distribution:** The funds will support CMU's Pittsburgh campus and build a new, fully integrated campus in Miami.
- **Interdisciplinary Educational Model (President Farnam Jahanian):** 
  - Moves away from traditional departments, colleges, and majors.
  - **Societal Grand Challenges Focus:** Organizes the Miami campus around areas like human health, national security, economic competitiveness, cybersecurity, energy/climate resiliency, and advanced manufacturing/re-industrialization.
- **Institutional Alignment (Ken Griffin):** 
  - Chosen due to CMU's willingness to think creatively and step out of the box.
  - Ranked #1 nationally in computer science, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and world-renowned in robotics, alongside a strong fine arts/drama program (including the first degree-granting drama program in 1914, producing nearly 70 Tony Award winners).

## Higher Education, Return on Investment, and Economic Literacy
- **Ranking Improvements (President Farnam Jahanian):** 
  - CMU's national US News & World Report undergraduate ranking rose from #20 to #14 in one year.
  - **ROI Metrics:** CMU is in the top three nationally for graduate return on investment.
- **Holistic Skill Development:** Emphasizes teaching critical thinking, problem-solving, creativity, and communication alongside science and technology, maintaining high standards without grade inflation.
- **Critique of Economic Ideologies (Ken Griffin):** 
  - Expressed concern over rising socialism among young graduates, attributing it to "economic illiteracy."
  - Argued that higher education must return to academic rigor, common sense, and basic principles, noting historical failures of socialism (e.g., China).

## Market Events & AI Concentration
- **Situational Awareness Liquidation Event (Ken Griffin):** 
  - Explained behind-the-scenes actions regarding Situational Awareness, an AI-focused fund that faced valuation setbacks due to too much leverage and enthusiasm.
  - **Citadel's Rescue:** Citadel spent roughly 40 hours straight working on the liquidity challenges and bought most of the fund's portfolio to resolve issues for limited partners and Citadel fund investors.
- **National Debt & Productivity:** Stated that America's $40 trillion national debt requires economic growth driven by productivity, with AI, robotics, and computer science serving as vital tools.
- **AI Dualism & Guardrails (President Farnam Jahanian):** 
  - Students are finding strong employment and are not worried about job prospects.
  - Emphasized approaching AI with wide eyes, balancing technological advances (personalized therapeutics, infrastructure protection) with ethical implications, policy considerations, and regulatory frameworks.

## Talent, Hiring, and Regional Transformation
- **Hiring Criteria at Citadel (Ken Griffin):** Looks for candidates with serious educational backgrounds, grit, determination, work ethic, and a passion for lifetime learning.
- **Talent Demand:** Candidates with backgrounds in AI and computer science are the most sought-after and difficult to recruit in current job markets.
- **Urban Transformation (Ken Griffin):** 
  - Great universities anchor great cities.
  - Bringing CMU to Miami will be "transitive" for Miami, much like CMU has been transformative for Pittsburgh—which is currently surrounded by around 100 robotics companies driving the re-industrialization of America.
