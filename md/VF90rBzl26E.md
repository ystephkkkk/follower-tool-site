## California's Economic & Regulatory Collapse
* **Economic Paradox and the "Resource Curse":** Steve Hilton notes that California is ranked 50th out of 50 for business climate by Chief Executive Magazine, suffers from the highest poverty and unemployment rates in the country, and has a median house price near $1 million against a median salary of $65,000. 
  - Hilton attributes the state's GDP ranking to Silicon Valley's wealth generation and a doubled state budget over 10 years, noting that net private sector job creation since the pandemic is "basically zero."
  - Peter Thiel's prior observation describes Silicon Valley as a "resource curse" that generates enough tax revenue to insulate the state government from good governance.
* **Regulatory Bloat and Legislative Overload:** The Sacramento legislature passed 1,118 bills in the prior year, creating over 420,000 total state regulations.
  - Hilton points out that a 2018 state law legalizing street vending across California preempts local governments, ruining long-standing tax-paying small businesses (such as a 14-year-old Latino-owned shoe store in South Central LA) while allowing unregulated, non-taxpaying vendors to operate without licenses or health codes.
* **Hidden Taxes and Government Waste:** 
  - Hilton highlights that programs like the Low Carbon Fuel Standard and the cap-and-invest program add about $1.50 to gas prices and fund projects like high-speed rail.
  - Vehicle registrations in California cost hundreds of dollars ($800–$1,000+), whereas they are under $100 in most other states.

## The Path to Victory & Proposed Reforms
* **Polling and Persuadable Voters:** Hilton outlines his polling metrics, showing a race where 33% are solidly with him, 41% with opponent Javier Becerra, and 26% up for grabs.
  - While starting from a 30-point gap on primary night, recent polls show the gap closing down to 8 points.
  - The target demographic consists of non-college-educated, regular working people who are at a financial breaking point.
* **Hilton's 100-Day Reform Agenda:** 
  - **Tax Cuts:** Cancel high-speed rail, cut government headcount by 10% with a 5% efficiency target, stop state funding for nonprofits worsening homelessness, and use the savings to make the first $150,000 of income tax-free.
  - **Energy Policy:** Utilize California energy resources, eliminate outdated regulations and hidden gas taxes, and drive gas prices down toward $3.
  - **DMV Reform:** Abolish the DMV and cap flat vehicle registration at $73 per vehicle per year.
  - **Regulatory Target:** Cut the number of state regulations from over 420,000 down to under 200,000 by the end of a first term.
  - **New Cities Initiative:** Publish a plan to build 10 new cities in California to spur housing and business development.
* **The Case Against Javier Becerra:** Hilton cites Biden administration figures to criticize opponent Javier Becerra, noting that domestic policy head Susan Rice described Becerra as an "idiot" and an "ass," while another cabinet member stated that running a large executive agency is not his skill set.
