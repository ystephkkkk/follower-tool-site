**TL;DR**  
At WWDC 2026, Apple unveiled its next generation of Apple Intelligence, featuring "Siri AI"—a deeply conversational, privacy-first assistant built in collaboration with Google—alongside macOS Golden Gate, expanded family safety controls, and massive system performance upgrades.

## Apple Intelligence Architecture and the All-New Siri AI
* **Google Foundational Collaboration:** Apple partnered with Google to integrate technologies from the Gemini family of models, creating a next-generation foundation model optimized for on-device execution and Private Cloud Compute servers.
* **The "Siri AI" Upgrade:** Rebuilt with advanced AI at its core, Siri now utilizes personal context, on-screen awareness, and app actions to perform complex multi-step tasks. 
    * **Conversational Engine:** Users can engage in continuous, multi-turn dialogues, which are saved and synced privately across devices via a new dedicated Siri app.
    * **Expressive Voice & Dictation:** Supported on high-end Apple silicon, Siri features highly customizable vocal expressivity and a significant accuracy boost in system-wide keyboard dictation.
* **Strict Privacy Protocols:** Unlike competitors who retain data, Apple’s Private Cloud Compute ensures user data is never stored or accessible to Apple, with protocols open to continuous verification by outside experts.
* **Visual Intelligence:** A new "Siri mode" in the iPhone Camera app allows users to point their devices at objects to retrieve instant information, split dinner bills, get nutritional insights, or check if luggage meets flight requirements in visionOS.
* **System-wide Writing Tools:** Siri can now draft text from scratch, match specific communication styles, provide critical feedback, and automatically proofread text across native and third-party apps.
* **Global Rollout Constraints:** Siri AI will launch in beta in English later this year. It will be initially unavailable in the EU (due to privacy/security integration paths) and China (pending regulatory approval).

## System Performance and macOS Golden Gate
* **macOS Golden Gate:** Craig Federighi, Senior VP of Software Engineering, introduced the latest version of macOS, featuring key visual and functional updates.
* **Liquid Glass UI Refinements:** Apple’s UI design language now features a personalization slider for transparency (ranging from ultra-clear to fully tinted), uniform top-of-app toolbars, and edge-to-edge sidebars that restore color to inactive icons.
* **Core Performance Optimizations:** Stacey Lysik announced deep fundamental updates that make Apple platforms feel significantly more responsive:
    * **App Launches:** iPhone and iPad apps now launch up to 30% faster by preloading essential data.
    * **Data Transfer & Loading:** New photos render 70% faster, AirDrop transfers are 80% faster, and iPad-to-external drive transfers are up to 5x faster.
    * **Older Device Support:** An optimized CPU scheduler extends support for iOS 27 all the way back to the iPhone 11, matching the hardware compatibility list of iOS 26.
* **Rebuilt Search Index:** The infrastructure powering Spotlight, Photos, and Mail has been re-architected to index new content almost instantly and surface more accurate results using a new ranking system.

## Integrated App Intelligence and Generative Tools
* **Agentic Safari & Passwords:** Safari now automatically groups open tabs by topic and features a "Notify Me" tool to monitor pages (such as restock notifications) in the background. Working with the Passwords app, it can securely log into websites and automatically upgrade weak credentials in the background with a single tap.
* **Image Playground:** Reimagined with advanced generative models, this tool lets users generate high-quality, photorealistic images, apply custom styles to existing photos, and edit specific objects within an image using simple touch gestures.
* **Spatial Reframing in Photos:** Photographers can use spatial models to change the camera's original perspective after a photo is captured. Generative AI seamlessly fills in the missing frame borders, making the edits look entirely natural.
* **Natural Language Shortcuts:** The process of building automations has been simplified by allowing users to describe what they want (e.g., "When I leave work, text Pedro my ETA") in natural language, which Apple Intelligence then converts into active shortcuts.

## Safety, Health, and Family Features
* **Expert-Guided Health Frameworks:** Dr. Sumbul Desai outlined Apple's commitment to child development. In collaboration with the American Academy of Pediatrics, Apple is integrating healthy digital habit guides directly into its safety features.
* **Age-Based Recommendations:** 
    * **Time Allowances:** Screen Time now suggests daily time allowances for entertainment, gaming, and social media based on a child’s age.
    * **App & Web Access:** Parents can restrict child devices to select apps and require approval for new websites via the "Ask to Browse" feature (on by default for children under 13).
* **Communication Safety:** Built-in safeguards will now scan for and blur instances of gore and violence in shared media, expanding on existing nudity filters.
* **Expanded Health & Ecosystem Features:** 
    * **Cycle Tracking:** The Health app now offers dedicated tracking, pattern notifications, and symptom logging for perimenopause and menopause.
    * **Shared Albums:** iCloud Shared Albums now support full-resolution media sharing and allow Android and Windows users to contribute.
