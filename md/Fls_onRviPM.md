**TL;DR:** OpenAI announced over 20 new products and features at DevDay 2026—led by personal autonomous agents called "dots," collaborative workspace "ChatGPT spaces," new frontier models like GPT-6.1 Sol and UltraFast, and cloud-native developer tooling—aiming to transform AI from a conversational assistant into an active, 24/7 collaborative partner.

## New User Experiences: Dots & Spaces
* **Introduction of Dots:** **Dots** are always-on, highly capable agents integrated into ChatGPT designed to handle complex, multi-step delegated work (e.g., managing bug reports, monitoring dependencies, or prepping board decks).
  * **Omnichannel Access:** Beyond ChatGPT, users can interact with their dots via text messaging, direct phone calls, or voice interfaces.
  * **Enterprise Specialist Dots:** Virtual teammates configured with specific goals and context to scale intensive work across an entire enterprise team.
* **ChatGPT Spaces:** A dedicated collaborative environment where human team members and dots can work together on shared pages, live prototypes, interactive charts, and spreadsheets.
  * **Dynamic Multi-User Collaboration:** Users can tag teammates and dots (e.g., dotty) directly in comments or chat threads to execute tasks like updating bar charts or generating PR fixes.

## Models, Speed, & Research Breakthroughs
* **GPT-6.1 Sol:** A new model balancing high intelligence with lower cost, designed for reusing context, iterating on solutions, and handling longer tasks as an everyday driver.
  * **UltraFast Model:** Available across API, ChatGPT, and Codex, delivering 300 tokens per second (3x the speed of standard models).
* **New Subscription Tiers:** 
  * **Pro-500:** Offers 25x the speed and access to UltraFast.
  * **Pro-200:** Reopened subscription providing access to all frontier models.
* **Decisions API:** Enables models to respond in a fraction of a second by selecting from a predefined set of choices.
* **AI Research Intern:** **Tejal Patwardhan** shared that OpenAI has reached its milestone of deploying an AI research intern.
  * **Task Automation:** Models running in continuous loops now autonomously handle over a third of day-long research tasks without human intervention.
  * **Scientific & Mathematical Impact:** Models have helped solve over 100 math problems open for decades, accelerate antibiotic creation, and study ancient languages.

## Developer Platform & Codex in the Cloud
* **Codex in the Cloud:** **Romain Huet** demonstrated the new cloud-native Codex harness, allowing developers to start tasks on mobile and execute them in cloud environments (e.g., rewriting an entire backend in Rust or running secure cloud scans).
* **Computer Use & API Expansion:** Computer use is now available via API for building testing agents, alongside bedrock managed agents that integrate with existing applications and data.
* **OpenAI Private Tension (Z-EDR):** Delivers frontier model safety without storing customer content on OpenAI servers, establishing new enterprise privacy standards.
* **API Performance Metrics:** Reliability remains above 99%, with time-to-first-token reduced by 45%.

## Ecosystem, Distribution, & Business Tools
* **Ecosystem Integration:** 16 launch partners signed on for sign-in functionality utilizing existing ChatGPT subscription tokens, eliminating cost barriers for end users.
* **Plugin Extensions:** Developers can build editors, dashboards, and workspaces directly inside ChatGPT and Codex, with simplified human review submission processes.
* **Open Source Model Access:** Through a partnership with BaseTen, enterprise customers can access open-source models directly.
