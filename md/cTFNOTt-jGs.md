* **The Flawed AI CapEx Calculation (Gavin Baker, Founder of Atreides Management):** Public markets are discounting AI stocks by comparing ~$2T in CapEx against under $200B in visible frontier lab revenue (~$80B Anthropic, ~$40B OpenAI, plus Grok and Gemini), overlooking massive open-source and enterprise adoption.

## Autonomous Logistics, Defense & Hardware Scaling

* **Autonomous Delivery at National Scale (Keller Rinaudo Cliffton, CEO of Zipline):** Zipline's factory has quadrupled in size as it grows 10–15% week over week, shifting from a 1M deliveries/day plan to serving 3+ strategic partners each seeking >1M deliveries/day.
  * **Uber Eats & Walmart:** Zipline's Uber Eats partnership targets 10-minute delivery for 100 million US homes across 1.2 million merchants. Its Walmart partnership is expanding from 22 to 300 Supercenters in phase one, delivering 90,000 of 100,000 SKUs in 2–5 minutes after prep.
  * **Metro Rollout:** Zipline operates ~25 charging sites in Dallas, launched Houston a month ago, and just launched Austin.
  * **Global Tech Stack Race:** Developing nations want trade, not aid, and are choosing between US and Chinese AI/robotics stacks.
* **Rebuilding US Maritime Capacity (Vibhav Altekar, CTO of Saronic):** With China holding 50% of global shipbuilding capacity, Saronic is scaling autonomous naval vessels from its new Port Alpha shipyard in Brownsville, TX.

  * **Reliability Challenge:** Unmanned ships must survive 5–55 day missions under 10–20G slamming loads without human maintenance.
  * **Jones Act Waiver:** In the first 40 days after President Trump extended the Jones Act waiver, more petroleum products (LNG, diesel) moved from the Gulf to California than in all of the prior year.
* **Direct-to-Consumer Hardware Scale (Matteo Franceschetti, CEO of Eight Sleep):** Eight Sleep has crossed $1B in cumulative sales across 37 countries (expanding to 40 by end of Q1) entirely via DTC.
  * **Efficiency & Retention:** Eight Sleep's revenue productivity per employee is 3–5x higher than any other hardware company, with record-low return rates and higher retention than Oura following the Pod 6 launch.

## Agentic Finance, Cybersecurity & Open-Source Defense

* **Financial Rails for AI Agents (Brian Armstrong, CEO of Coinbase):** To prevent AI from being controlled by one or two overlords, Armstrong advocates for a decentralized marketplace of open-weight models.
  * Coinbase is building AI-enabled financial accounts and real-time payment rails so autonomous agents can assemble into teams, hire each other, and transact globally.
  * **Institutional Security:** Coinbase stores 99% of crypto offline (air-gapped) and scans every line of production code with cyber-expert AI models, serving 7 of the largest banks, 140 government agencies, 50 of the top 100 hedge funds, and most crypto ETFs.
  * **Personal Security:** Armstrong recommends password managers, app-based or YubiKey 2FA (avoiding SMS), and liveness checks to defeat AI deepfakes.
* **The New Cyber Threat Landscape (Guillermo Rauch, CEO of Vercel):** AI models have lowered the barrier for attackers while making AI tokens—fungible with US dollars—a prime target for theft and distillation attacks.
  * **Defensive AI:** Vercel deploys enterprises behind firewalls and inserts adversarial AI agents (combining proprietary and open-weight models) into Git commit pipelines to audit code. Vercel also open-sourced a cybersecurity harness called `deepsec`.
  * **Ecosystem Alignment:** Rauch praised Nvidia's open-source contributions (e.g., Nemotron) and warned that China's first-party data advantage in video (e.g., Seedance, TikTok) makes maintaining an open US AI ecosystem existential.
