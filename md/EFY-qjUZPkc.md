AI is a five-layer stack—from energy to data and applications—that democratizes computing and requires every country and enterprise to build local digital infrastructure to capture economic growth. Nvidia’s CEO Jensen Huang and U.S. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick emphasize that while AGI is nearly here, AI automates tasks rather than eliminating jobs, supercharging human ambition and productivity across a multi-trillion-dollar technological revolution.

## The Five-Layer AI Stack and Sovereign Infrastructure
* **The Five-Layer Architecture:** Jensen Huang breaks down artificial intelligence into a structured stack:
  * **Layer 1 (Energy):** Transforms electricity into mathematics.
  * **Layer 2 (Chips):** The GPU hardware tier led by Nvidia.
  * **Layer 3 (Infrastructure):** Large data centers incorporating land, power, and shell architecture.
  * **Layer 4 (AI Models):** Models spanning languages, biology, chemicals, physics, and robotics.
  * **Layer 5 (Data and Applications):** The most critical layer where actual value and user interaction occur.
* **Sovereign Infrastructure Investment:** Howard Lutnick and Jensen Huang emphasize that every country must build local digital infrastructure to support researchers, students, startups, and industries.
  * **Local Activation:** Deploying local infrastructure immediately activates domestic startup ecosystems and academic research.
  * **Fungible and Durable Hardware:** Nvidia operates on its 14th architectural generation, providing general-purpose GPUs that run every closed, open, physical, and biological model across clouds, on-premise setups, and edge devices.
  * **Capital Scaling:** Infrastructure buildouts are capital-intensive, with a single gigawatt costing approximately $50 billion to $60 billion.

## Democratization of Intelligence and Workforce Impact
* **Democratization via Human Language:** Anyone can now program a computer using natural human language, elevating everyone as a universal equalizer.
* **Agentic Systems:** AI has evolved from static large language models (LLMs) into "agentic systems" surrounded by digital exoskeletons that retrieve knowledge, maintain working memory, use digital tools, and operate physical machinery.
* **Physical AI Integration:** Agentic systems are deployed into physical machinery, including self-driving cars, manufacturing robotic arms, logistics vehicles, surgical robots, and autonomous drug discovery laboratories.
* **Job Evolution over Elimination:** Jensen Huang rejects the notion that AI will eliminate jobs, comparing onboarding an AI agent to onboarding a PhD graduate from MIT, Stanford, or Harvard:
  * **Context and Purpose:** Companies must surround AI agents with context, purpose, relevance, and environmental harnessing.
  * **Task vs. Job:** Routine typing and talking tasks are automated, but human jobs—defined by purpose and meaning—remain intact while supercharging overall productivity.

## Regulatory Balance and Economic Growth

* **Avoiding Fear-Driven Stagnation:** The worst outcome for any country is failing to adopt AI due to fear, uncertainty, and excessive focus on risk rather than prosperity and opportunity.
* **Economic Scale and Job Creation:**
