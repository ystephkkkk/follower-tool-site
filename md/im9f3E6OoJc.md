## Supply Chain Disruptions & Inflationary Pressures

- **Slow-Moving Shock:** Because tankers are slow-moving vessels (e.g., taking **40 days** to reach Fiji), supply disruptions will deepen over the coming weeks, representing a 20th-century type of shock in the 21st century.

- **Short-Term Inflation Reversal:** Inflation projections for **2026** have been upgraded due to rising short-term expectations in the US and euro area, though long-term inflation expectations remain well-anchored.

## Policy Advice for Governments & Central Banks
- **Fiscal Policy Balance:** Policymakers must strike a balance between safeguarding fiscal sustainability and protecting those hit hardest without using untargeted export controls or broad-based tax cuts.
- **Energy Conservation Measures:** Countries should implement demand-reducing incentives, such as free public transport, remote work options, or switching to less energy-intensive activities over time.
- **Central Bank Guidance:** Central banks with high credibility should signal price stability commitments without rushing to raise rates preemptively, while central banks lacking credibility may need stronger signals.
