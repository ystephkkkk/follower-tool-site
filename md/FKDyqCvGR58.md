## The Rise of Agentic AI and Distributed Computing

* **The Integration of Cloud and Edge:** AI workloads are transitioning to a unified system where cloud and edge computing merge seamlessly, eliminating the distinction between the two. 
    * To support this, edge devices require highly powerful Neural Processing Units (NPUs) and GPUs to handle sensor data and maintain situational context.
* **The "Year of the Agents":** Autonomous AI agents are transitioning from abstract concepts to scaled deployments on commercial devices.
    * Orchestrators like OpenClaw and Hermes, alongside agentic assistants like Claude Desktop, are running natively on Snapdragon PCs.
    * Major players are building integration layers, including Google's Gemini Intelligence for Android, Microsoft's OS-level agents, and Humane's agentic operating system.
* **Dual Device Personalities:** Future personal devices (such as smartphones and laptops) will have two distinct operational modes:
    * **Human-operated:** The traditional user interface designed for manual interaction.
    * **Agent-operated:** The AI agent autonomously executing workflows, using software and services at machine speed rather than human speed.

## Physical AI: Automotive, Robotics, and Enterprise

* **AI-Defined Vehicles:** The automotive industry is evolving from software-defined to AI-defined vehicles, powered by two integrated layers of intelligence:
    * **Cockpit Personalization:** A personal AI agent that travels with the user across devices, interacting with them inside the vehicle context.
    * **Physical AI:** A safety-critical navigation system that perceives, plans, and acts in real time using cameras, radars, and maps.
* **Hierarchical Computing in Robotics:** Qualcomm is designing comprehensive hardware and software platforms for autonomous mobile robots (AMRs), industrial arms, humanoids, and drones. Succeeding in this space requires a three-tiered compute hierarchy:
    * **Instant Execution:** Low-latency reflexes (e.g., maintaining balance or adjusting grip).
    * **Action and Grounding:** Mid-level tasks mapping the environment.
    * **Reasoning:** High-level logical decision-making.
    * *Partnerships:* Qualcomm is collaborating with robotics innovators including VinFast, Neura Robotics, Suncar, and Figure AI.
* **Enterprise and Industrial Vision AI:** Locally run computer vision systems will transform retail, warehousing, energy, and smart cities. Local devices will dynamically access larger on-premises or cloud models only when highly complex reasoning is required.

## 6G as the First AI-Native Wireless Generation

* **Three Pillars of 6G:** Qualcomm defines 6G through three core pillars: connectivity, distributed computing, and sensing.
* **Continuous Context Exchange:** 6G is designed for high-speed uplink capabilities. Amon noted that 6G will essentially turn users and devices into "walking cameras," sending continuous high-definition video and contextual data to the cloud.
* **Network-Wide Computing:** The 6G telecom infrastructure itself acts as a massive distributed AI network, running real-time inference at radio base stations and central offices.
* **RF Sensing and Digital Twins:** Radio frequency (RF) signals will serve as physical inputs for AI models. By treating hundreds of millions of radio connections like radar, the network can triangulate data to create real-time "digital twins" of neighborhoods, cities, and entire countries to manage traffic, pedestrian flow, and drone corridors.

## The Economics of Token Consumption and Hybrid Routing

* **Exponential Token Growth:** The transition to agentic AI is creating an unprecedented demand for tokens, growing by orders of magnitude:
    * **Level 1 (Conversational):** ~10,000 tokens per prompt/response.
    * **Level 2 (Reasoning):** ~100,000 tokens per task.
    * **Level 3 (Agentic/Autonomous):** 1,000,000+ tokens per multi-step task.
    * *Projections:* Global token demand is expected to surge from 31.7 billion tokens every 10 seconds in 2026 to 1.27 trillion tokens every 10 seconds by 2030—reaching the "quintillions" annually.
* **Distributed Routing for Cost Reduction:** Running 100% of token-heavy workloads in the cloud is economically unsustainable. Qualcomm demonstrated that hybrid routing workloads between local Snapdragon NPUs and the cloud dramatically reduces costs:
    * **Coding Workloads:** Running Claude Code with local edge processing saved 1.4 million tokens (a **60% cost reduction**).
    * **Webpage Creation:** A distributed orchestrator achieved the same generation results with 30% fewer tokens and a **4x lower cost**.
