## The Cost of "Token-Maxxing" and AI Business Model Risks

* **The Shift to Token-Based Pricing:** Tech companies are realizing that subscription models have drastically underpriced actual AI token usage.
    * **GitHub Copilot Pricing Changes:** On June 1st, Microsoft moved GitHub Copilot to token-based pricing to combat underpriced subscriptions, resulting in significant customer pushback. If usage declines due to pricing, the broader AI growth narrative could weaken.
    * **Unchecked Corporate Spending:** The era of "token-maxxing"—where companies force AI adoption regardless of cost—has led to massive budget overruns. Uber burned through its entire annual token budget in four months, and an unnamed company reportedly spent $500 million in a single month on Anthropic models because they failed to set spend limits.
* **Google's Massive Stock Dilution:** To fund its ballooning AI CapEx, Google is raising $80 billion in capital through a stock sale rather than debt. Google's AI CapEx is projected to double from $90 billion in 2025 to $180 billion this year, a level that cannot be sustained solely by organic cash flow.
* **Nvidia's PC Market Entrance:** Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang announced the N1X processor built in partnership with Microsoft. Nvidia plans to launch over 30 laptops and 10 desktops featuring the chip across major OEMs (Dell, HP, Lenovo) to run agentic AI locally.
