## Honoring Naval Air Station Oceana and Carrier Air Wing 8
* **A Legacy of Service:** Vice President Vance recognized active-duty service members from all six branches of the Armed Forces gathered at NAS Oceana, praising their high operational tempo over the past 18 months.
* **Carrier Air Wing 8 Achievements:** Vance offered a special welcome home to Carrier Air Wing 8, which returned in May from an intense 11-month deployment.
    * **Operational Milestones:** The wing logged nearly 12,000 launches and over 5,500 flight hours in support of Operation Epic Fury.
* **Historical Connection:** Drawing a parallel to General George Washington’s call for "gratitude of heart" the day after the Revolutionary War victory at Yorktown, Vance urged service members to take pride in safeguarding American liberty as the nation approaches its 250th birthday.

## The Administration's Military Doctrine: Clear Objectives and Strength
* **Defined Mission Parameters:** Drawing on his own experience as an enlisted Marine corporal (2003–2007), Vance contrasted past open-ended conflicts with the current administration's foreign policy. He pledged that the administration will never send troops to war without a clearly defined objective and the proper tools to win and return home safely.
* **Negotiating From Strength:** Vance defended the administration's use of diplomatic leverage, arguing negotiations are only effective because adversaries fear American military capabilities. 
    * **Countering Iran:** Vance credited the military with destroying Iran's conventional naval capabilities, degrading its defense industrial base, and pushing its nuclear development program further back than it has been in 20 to 30 years.
    * **Targeted Use of Force:** He emphasized that military action—such as recent strikes to deter Iranian attacks on commercial shipping—must only be used to secure specific, vital national interests rather than "dropping bombs just for the sake of dropping bombs."

## Prioritizing Veteran Care and VA Reform
* **The Vietnam Veterans Legacy:** Vance recalled his law school work with the Vietnam Veterans of America, noting that their advocacy ensured subsequent generations of service members from Iraq and Afghanistan would return to a supportive, grateful nation.
* **Historic Drop in Backlogs:** Vance highlighted the joint efforts of Veterans Affairs Secretary Doug Collins and Representative Jen Kiggans in improving VA infrastructure.
    * **Systemic Progress:** VA backlogs have been reduced to their lowest level in 30 years.
    * **Service Delivery:** The administration has prioritized responsive communication and reliable medical and legal care for transitioning veterans.

## Shifting Military Culture to Readiness and Merit
* **Elimination of DEI Programs:** Vance highlighted the removal of Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) initiatives from the military, stating that the administration aims to foster unit cohesion and individual success based strictly on merit and capability.
* **Equipment Superiority:** He reiterated the administration's commitment to ensuring American troops always possess superior technology and equipment, eliminating any risk of being outmatched on the battlefield.
