Economist Nouriel Roubini argues that the U.S. must choose decisive military escalation to force an Iranian regime surrender, as a "half-baked" blockade will spike oil prices without removing the strategic threat. Despite these severe geopolitical risks, Roubini maintains a long-term "Dr. Boom" outlook, predicting a massive technology-driven secular boom that will drive U.S. potential growth toward 4% by the end of the decade.

## The Iran Conflict: The Case for Decisive Escalation
* **Strategic Dilemma:** Roubini asserts that while starting the conflict may have been a mistake, de-escalation now would be a "strategic disaster," leaving Iran as a permanent threat to global energy corridors and emboldening adversaries like China.
* **Escalation Tactics:** Effective escalation requires more than a naval blockade.
    * **Troops on the Ground:** Roubini suggests the U.S. needs to take over Kharg Island and other islands near the Strait of Hormuz to dismantle the regime’s revenue and unblock shipping lanes.
    * **Regime Surrender:** The goal should be "regime surrender" through total economic, financial, and military strangulation rather than a slow-motion collapse which Iran has historically withstood.
* **The Blockade Risk:** A "half-baked" blockade (strangling revenues without reopening the Strait) is a "game of chicken" that Iran could win.
    * It likely leads to a "tit-for-tat" scenario with sustained oil price spikes and no political resolution.

## Global Economic Fallout and Energy Risks
* **Oil Price Projections:**
    * **Ceasefire/Stalemate Floor:** Oil is unlikely to return to $60; a floor of $80+ is expected due to a permanent risk premium.
    * **Worst-Case Scenario:** Severe escalation without reopening the Strait could push oil to $150–$200, mirroring 1970s-style stagflationary recessions.
* **Regional Impact Disparity:**
    * **Asia:** The primary victim due to both price shocks and "quantity shocks," as 20% of Gulf energy exports are destined for the region.
    * **Europe:** Significant impact on growth and inflation as a net energy importer.
    * **United States:** Least affected as a net energy exporter, though consumer spending will suffer from higher fuel costs.

## Geopolitical Friction: The US-China Relationship
* **Diplomatic Deadlines:** The conflict threatens to derail high-level summits between Trump and Xi Jinping. A "weakened" Trump, distracted by the Middle East, may lose the ability to negotiate "dealer-to-dealer" with China.
* **Chinese Perspective:** China views the conflict as a U.S. "own-goal" that depletes American soft and hard power. However, they fear a "wildcard" U.S. administration and the potential rise of more hawkish advisors like Marco Rubio or J.D. Vance.
* **Defense Tech Superiority:** The war has demonstrated U.S. and Israeli dominance in "defense tech" (precision-guided munitions and drones), which may force China to rethink the military viability of a confrontation over Taiwan.

## The "Dr. Boom" Outlook: AI and the Secular Growth Story
* **Technological Explosion:** Roubini identifies a "Cambrian explosion" across 15 industries, including AI, robotics, fusion, and quantum computing.
    * **Growth Targets:** He predicts U.S. potential growth will rise from 2% to 4% by 2030, potentially reaching 10% by 2050.
* **"Tech Trumps Trump":** Innovation is a "positive aggregate supply shock" that outweighs the negative "stagflationary shocks" of war and populism.
    * **The 4-to-1 Ratio:** Roubini estimates the positive impact of tech at a 200-basis-point increase in potential growth, compared to only a 50-basis-point drag from trade and migration issues.
* **Market Resilience:** Despite the war, the AI boom is viewed as a fundamental secular trend, not a bubble, providing a massive tailwind for both the U.S. and Chinese economies.
