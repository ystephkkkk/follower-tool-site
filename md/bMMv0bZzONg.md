## TL;DR
Companies are increasingly choosing to own their AI intelligence ("sovereign AI") down to the weights rather than renting external APIs, driven by cost, speed, performance, and control. With open-weight models now near the frontier, owning your stack is no longer a performance sacrifice, but a key competitive edge.

## Sovereign AI & The Shift to Decentralized Intelligence
* **Definition of Sovereign AI:** Refers to companies owning their own intelligence without external dependencies down to the weights.
* **Scope of the Shift:** Does not mean abandoning frontier APIs like Opus or GPT for coding or desktop work, but vertically integrating AI capabilities into parts of products.
* **Ecosystem Momentum:** 
  * Highlighted by leaders like Alex Karp, Satya Nadella, and Jensen Huang (who led support for open-weight model availability in the US).
* **Centralized vs. Decentralized Intelligence:**
  * **Centralized Intelligence:** A single all-powerful black-box AI powers the world's GDP and swallows data exhaust and flywheels.
  * **Decentralized Intelligence:** The world builds on a solid core, but every company builds bespoke intelligence tailored to their own data, industry, and working style, allowing individuality to triumph.

## Four Key Drivers for Owning Models
* **Cost:** **Must-have for successful products:** Especially for low-, zero-, or negative-margin companies where high AI COGS scale with product success.
* **Speed:** **Domain-specific velocity:** In domains like coding and security, small distilled custom models can beat large general models because speed is critical.
* **Performance:** **Domain-specific outperformance:** Open models can now outperform closed models when tuned on proprietary domain data.
* **Controlling Your Own Destiny:** **Independent standing:** Companies want their own independent legs to stand on rather than relying entirely on Anthropic or OpenAI.
* **The New Mantra:** *"Not your weights, not your product"* (adapted from the crypto DeFi meme *"Not your keys, not your crypto"*).

## The AI Stack: Production & Development
* **Closed Model Stack:** Simple setup with foundation models (Opus, GPT) and out-of-the-box harnesses. Higher floor, but lower ceiling (no ability to use online production data to improve intelligence).
* **Sovereign Stack (Pandora's Box):**
  * **Production Stack (Harness + Model):** Requires choosing an open-source base, extensive post-training, and configuring open harnesses (logic, tools, context).
  * **Context Drivers:** Vector databases (**TurboPuffer**), enterprise knowledge graphs (**Glean**), open-source connectors via **MCP**, and novel memory approaches (e.g., **Dan Beerman / Engram** encoding context directly into weights).
  * **Development Stack:** Elevated importance for monitoring evals, tracking production drift, gathering domain data (expert trajectories, synthetic data, RL environments), and setting up online learning.
