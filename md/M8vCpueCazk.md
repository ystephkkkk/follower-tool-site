## Federal Reserve Independence and Kevin Warsh
* **The "Sock Puppet" Critique:** Senator Warren argues that nominee Kevin Warsh failed to demonstrate the independence required for the Federal Reserve Chair. 
    * **Refusal to Disagree:** Warren noted that during her meetings and public hearings, Warsh would not cite a single economic or factual point of disagreement with Donald Trump, including the results of the 2020 election.
    * **Monetary vs. Regulatory Independence:** Warren criticized Warsh’s reported stance that he only needs to be independent regarding monetary policy, while leaving "the door open" for the President to influence other Fed activities like bank mergers or crypto regulation.
* **Presidential Influence:** Warren expressed concern that Trump is attempting to "terrify" Fed officials through threats of firing and criminal investigations to gain control over the economy ahead of the November elections.
    * **The "Tough Guy" Defense:** While Warsh claimed in private meetings to be a "tough guy" who could stand up to Trump, Warren contends his public performance proved otherwise.

## Financial Regulation and Private Credit
* **Response to Scott Bessent:** Warren rebutted Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent’s claim that her "overregulation" killed community banks and forced lending into the unregulated private credit market.
    * **Underregulation Argument:** She noted that the recent failures of the second, third, and fourth largest banks in U.S. history occurred because they were *underregulated*, not overregulated.
    * **Community Bank Consolidation:** Warren attributed the decline of community banks to "too-big-to-fail" giants buying them up, rather than regulatory pressure.
* **Private Credit Risks:** Warren warned that the growth of private credit creates a "regulatory arbitrage" similar to the conditions before the 2008 financial crisis.
    * **Legislative Goals:** She is advocating for better regulation of the private credit market to gain visibility into systemic risks, though she expressed skepticism about gaining Republican support for such measures.

## Housing and Domestic Priorities

* **Economic Stance:** Warren emphasized her focus on "transforming" an economy she views as "hip-deep in corruption," aiming to deliver lower costs for hardworking people rather than billionaires.
