## Launch of America.gov and Citizen Experience
* **Single Digital Front Door:** **America.gov** replaces tens of thousands of separate government websites, rules, and over 10,000 forms with one unified portal.
* **Ease of Use:** President Trump noted that anyone who has used Grock, Gemini, or ChatGPT will instantly know how to use the platform.
* **Streamlined Services:**

  * **Name Changes & Benefits:** A woman changing her name after marriage will be able to ask the platform to fill out forms, track progress, and have new social security, passport, and insurance cards mailed to her; similar simplifications apply to reserving national park campsites, choosing Medicare plans, and securing veteran benefits.
