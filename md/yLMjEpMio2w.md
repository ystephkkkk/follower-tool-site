## TL;DR
Ronnie Vasishta (SVP of Telecom at NVIDIA) outlines how telecom infrastructure's 100 gigawatts of distributed power and connectivity are fueling the AI revolution through three main pillars: AI infrastructure buildouts, AI-RAN (Software-Defined Radio Access Networks), and agentic network operations.

## AI Infrastructure & Distributed Telco Clouds
- **Distributed Power Assets:**
  - Leverage **100,000 existing mobile switching offices, mobile exchanges, and points of presence**—representing roughly **100 gigawatts of power** in current telecommunications infrastructure—to solve land and power shortages for compute.
- **Deutsche Telekom's Distributed Cloud:**
  - DT is extending its industrial cloud into a distributed cloud in partnership with NVIDIA, and piloting AI-RAN with Nokia, achieving **10-15% uplink performance improvements** in labs using Nokia software.
- **European AI Factories:**
  - The evolution from GPU-as-a-service to models-as-a-service and inference-as-a-service is sweeping Europe, with **9 telcos in Europe** announcing new builds, enhancements, and commercial go-to-market strategies.

## AI-RAN & Software-Defined Radio Access Networks
- **Crossing the Chasm:**
  - AI-RAN has moved from early adopters to the early majority, backed by **19 leading telcos** and **147 AI-RAN Alliance members**.
- **New NVIDIA Aerial RAN Computers:**
  - **Super Micro MGX 4U (CRAN)** and **Dell MGX 2U** systems join the Aerial RAN family.
  - Existing hardware includes the **Quanta MGX 2U short** and the **DGX Spark** (capable of delivering **6 cells, 100 megahertz, 4T4R** at a base station).
- **Elisa & BT Deployments:**
  - Elisa demonstrated an MGX 2U short (with an **RTX Pro 4500**) running both RAN and a video-to-text AI application simultaneously at just **30% capacity utilization**.
  - BT will run Nokia software on these systems in live trials in **Birmingham, UK**.
  - T-Mobile (US) completed its first commercial call on an AI-RAN network using the **MGX 2U short with Nokia software**.

## Edge AI, Robotics, and Real-Time Applications
- **Edge AI Robotics (Agile Robots):**
  - Compared cloud-trained robots running models entirely on-device against robots connected to an **edge RTX Pro 6000**, showing significantly smoother pick-and-place server tasks and faster cycle times at the edge.
- **Orange Real-Time AI Translation:**
  - Piloted a **Nemotron speech-to-speech full duplex model** (with tone and semantic recognition) for emergency services via Orange’s live intelligence platform, demonstrating low-latency edge performance.
