**TL;DR**
Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent outlined an "America First" economic strategy centered on three pillars—industrial dominance, domestic investment, and preparedness—to ensure economic sovereignty while introducing "Trump Accounts" to broaden citizen participation in the U.S. equity market.

## The Three Pillars of Economic Security
*   **Industrial and Technological Dominance:** The administration is prioritizing the reshoring of critical industries to eliminate "single points of failure" exposed by the COVID-19 pandemic and the "China shock."
    *   **AI Infrastructure:** Bessent describes AI and quantum computing as existential battlegrounds for national security, noting that Treasury is working via the Financial Stability Oversight Council to promote responsible AI adoption in finance.
    *   **Digital Assets:** The administration supports a dollar-based stablecoin market to reinforce the U.S. dollar’s global role, supported by oversight tools provided by the GENIUS Act.
*   **Investment in America:** Maintaining the liquidity and credibility of the U.S. Treasury market is viewed as the foundation of global finance.
    *   **Strategic Capital:** Treasury intends to evaluate supervisory frameworks over the next six months to better mobilize capital toward semiconductors, energy infrastructure, and defense innovation.
*   **Preparedness and Deterrence:** Economic security requires proactive measures to prevent geopolitical shocks from causing domestic instability.
    *   **Supply Chain Vulnerability:** Bessent highlighted that 90% of advanced chips are still manufactured in Taiwan, representing a significant risk frontier alongside potential cyberattacks on financial infrastructure.

## Financial Inclusion via "Trump Accounts"
*   **Direct Seed Investment:** To address the fact that 40% of Americans have no equity market exposure, the administration is launching "Trump Accounts"—a $1,000 Treasury-funded seed investment for every eligible American child at birth.
*   **Philanthropic Support:** Texas natives Michael and Susan Dell have committed a $6.25 billion donation to "top up" these accounts for 25 million children in lower-income zip codes.
*   **Long-term Growth:** The funds are invested in a diversified index tied to the U.S. economy and remain locked for 18 years, though they can be rolled into retirement accounts or used for business startups and home down payments.
*   **Legislative Roots:** Bessent credited Texas Senator Ted Cruz with authoring the precursor to this provision.

## Banking Reform and Private Credit
*   **Small and Community Banks:** Bessent aims to address the "too small to succeed" trend by supporting legislation for enhanced non-interest-bearing accounts with deposit insurance up to $10 million to help local banks retain payroll and business clients.
*   **Private Credit Oversight:** While acknowledging that private credit provided a necessary "bridge" during tight regulatory periods, Treasury is focused on preventing "contagion" between unregulated capital and the regulated banking system.
*   **Retail Protection:** The administration is committed to ensuring that 401(k) plans do not become a "dumping ground" for bad assets from private equity "continuation funds."

## Tariff Strategy and Judicial Rulings
*   **Supreme Court Response:** Regarding the recent 6-3 Supreme Court ruling on IEEPA (International Emergency Economic Powers Act) authorities, Bessent clarified that while the court limited revenue collection under IEEPA, it did not strike down the tariffs themselves.
*   **Alternative Authorities:** The administration will pivot to using Section 232 (national security), Section 301 (unfair trade practices), and Section 122 (global tariffs) to maintain revenue levels.
*   **Revenue Outlook:** Treasury estimates that total tariff revenue for 2026 will remain virtually unchanged despite the shift in legal authorities.

## National Debt and Macro Outlook
*   **Deficit Reduction:** Bessent set a target to reduce the deficit-to-GDP ratio from the current ~7% down to 3% by the end of the President’s term through a combination of spending restraint and economic growth.
*   **Labor Market and AI:** Bessent expressed optimism regarding the labor market, citing a 12-14% CAPEX boom forecast for 2025. He views AI more as a tool for labor reallocation than pure displacement.
