## TL;DR
Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnik and OSTP Director Michael Kratsios announced a unanimous G20 consensus on the Carolina principles for emerging technology during a press conference in Chapel Hill, North Carolina, emphasizing heavy private sector AI infrastructure investments and the global reach of the American AI stack.

## G20 Innovation Ministerial & Consensus
* **Consensus Achievement:** All 20 G20 nations agreed on the G20 innovation ministerial statement after extensive staff-level negotiations.
* **Carolina Principles:** The agreed principles for emerging technology call for governments to:
  * Invest in foundational research to accelerate discovery.
  * Strengthen development and commercialization pathways.
  * Drive trusted adoption and deployment by applying existing sector-specific rules and reserving new regulations strictly for novel considerations.
* **Featured Speakers & Interventions:** Day-one and day-two discussions included contributions from SpaceX CEO Elon Musk, PCAST Co-Chair David Sachs, Commonwealth Fusion Systems CEO Bob Mumgard, Facebook CEO Mark Zuckerberg, ASU President Michael Crowe, Nobel laureate and Google DeepMind founder Demis Hassabis, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, Anthropic Co-Founder Tom Brown, and OpenAI CEO Sam Altman.

## Data Centers & Local Pushback
* **Data Center Narrative:** Secretary Lutnik dismissed local opposition and utility concerns over data centers as misinformation driven by geopolitical adversaries trying to slow U.S. progress.
* **Power and Prosperity:** Lutnik argued that data center operators will build their own power sources—lowering local energy costs rather than raising them—while dramatically expanding the local tax base.
* **Moratorium Warnings:** Communities enacting data center moratoriums (such as Orange County, North Carolina) risk losing economic opportunity to rival regions that roll out the red carpet.

## International Relations & Export Controls
* **China Relations:** President Trump offered to sell H200 Nvidia chips and AMD equivalents to China, but Chinese officials have not taken up the offer due to internal concerns; the administration considers the matter closed.
* **Export Controls & Remote Access:** Secretary Lutnik stated the administration is combating remote-access loopholes using a "whack-a-mole" approach bolstered by AI-driven monitoring of data center usage and token sales.
* **American AI Exports Program:** Led by Secretary Lutnik, the program assists international partners in acquiring U.S. chips, hyperscalers, and models through a "jam-packed" export center.

## AI Models, Safety, and Regulation
