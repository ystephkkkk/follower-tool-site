## TL;DR
Jensen Huang delivered the Nvidia GTC Berlin 2026 keynote, announcing a nearly $10 billion investment in Europe over the past year to build out the region's AI economy. He introduced major technological advancements, including the Vera Rubin architecture, the DSX POD supply-chain integration, and the RTX Spark personal AI workstation line.

## Europe's AI Economy and Investments
* **Capital Deployment:** **Jensen Huang** noted that Nvidia has invested nearly $10 billion across European startups and big and small partnerships over the past last year alone.
* **Partnership Ecosystem:** **Jensen Huang** stated that Nvidia now counts over 12,000 partnerships across Europe, working toward the new industrial revolution.
* **Regional Infrastructure:** **Jensen Huang** highlighted European cloud and infrastructure builders, including Mistral in France, Nebius, Schneider Electric, Siemens Energy, and Firebird (a cloud provider in the Central Asia region).

## Vera Rubin Architecture and Full-Stack Hardware
* **Generational Leap:** **Jensen Huang** presented the Vera Rubin architecture in full production, noting it achieves performance 30 times greater than Blackwell in large language model processing in just two years.
* **Vera Computer & CPU:** **Jensen Huang** introduced the Vera CPU featuring eight instructions per clock, the world's first LPDDR memory system, and the fastest on-chip fabric in the world.
* **Spectrum-X Co-Packaged Optics (CPO):** **Jensen Huang** detailed the world's first CPO switch—a 512-port at 200 gigabits per second Ethernet switch—utilizing CoWoS technology created with TSMC to attach silicon photonics directly onto the chip.
* **DSX POD Supply-Chain Revolution:** **Jensen Huang** announced that entire rack-scale systems (like NVLink 72) will now be fully assembled, qualified, and burned in within the supply chain as a "DSX POD," cutting data center on-site installation and assembly time from 4 to 5 months down to about a month.

## AI Agents and the OpenShell Security Runtime
* **Five-Layer Agent Architecture:** **Jensen Huang** defined autonomous agents by five core components: Models (perception/reasoning/planning), a Harness (the exoskeleton orchestrating work), a Sandbox, Knowledge, and Tools.
* **OpenShell Secure Runtime:** **Jensen Huang** introduced OpenShell, an open-source (Apache 2.0) runtime that enforces security policies at the granularity of system calls, file access, and network connections via Linux kernel isolation.
* **Nvidia Sentry & BlueField-4:** **Jensen Huang** explained that Nvidia Sentry runs on the BlueField-4 DPU as a hardware-isolated trust domain to monitor agent activity and quarantine rogue agents in milliseconds.
* **Industry Adoption:** **Jensen Huang** noted that Anthropic and Nvidia are partnering to create Claude OpenShell agents, with frontier labs, OS vendors, and cybersecurity companies adopting OpenShell.

## Custom Models, Knowledge, and CUDA-X Tools
* **Nemotron & Open Models:** **Jensen Huang** highlighted Nvidia's open models including Nemotron (languages), Cosmos (world foundation model), Groot (human and robotics), Alpamayo (autonomous navigation), Ising (quantum/classical computing), and Earth-2 (physics AI), along with open recipes and training data.
* **Sovereign Intelligence Fine-Tuning:** **Jensen Huang** showcased ecosystem partners—such as A21, BaseTen, CodeRabbit, Trajectory, Harvey, Scale, Skydance, and Palantir with Tampa General Hospital—fine-tuning Nemotron on enterprise data to achieve frontier capabilities.
* **Agent Knowledge Foundation:** **Jensen Huang** presented libraries for enterprise data extraction, including Nemotron Parse, Nemotron Embed, Nemotron Rerank, and Kumo for structured database predictions.
* **CUDA-X Tool Acceleration:** **Jensen Huang** emphasized that agents rely on CUDA-X libraries (running domain tools for fluids, particles, quantum chemistry, and ray tracing) to perform real work, with partners like Lila Sciences, Siemens, and Dassault Systèmes.
