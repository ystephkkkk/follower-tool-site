## Historical Foundations and New York's Legacy
- **Revolutionary Roots:**

  - On July 9, 1776, General George Washington had the Declaration of Independence read aloud to brigades and citizens at the Commons (now City Hall Park), within range of British guns from over 100 ships offshore.
  - Early New Yorkers melted down the statue of King George III at Bowling Green to cast bullets for the Continental Army.
- **Immigrant Arrivals:** 
  - New York Harbor has long served as a gateway for arrivals, from Lenape dugouts and early European explorers (Verrazano, Hudson) to millions seeking a new start.
  - Historic communities reflect waves of arrivals: James Weeks founded Weeksville in Brooklyn in 1838 as a haven for newly emancipated Black Americans; subsequent waves brought Irish famine survivors, Chinese sailors, Jewish refugees escaping pogroms, Italians, Syrians, West Indians, South Asians, and West Africans.
